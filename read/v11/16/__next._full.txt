<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 10:<br/>The Line Between Winner and Loser</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HE SIXTH EVENT</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> that was chosen was archery, a two-on-one event. Another one of ours. Class C had won thanks to Akito’s efforts, making our score now three wins and three losses. Sakayanagi made no particular comments about this, but quietly watched the event play out. It was almost like she</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> wanted </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">us to get to three wins and three losses.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Up next was the long-awaited seventh and final event. And, through a mischievous twist of fate, that event was…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Chess</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Required Participants: 1 Person</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Allotted Time: 1 Hour (Going over time results in a loss)</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Rules: Standard chess rules apply. However, the allotted time will not increase, even after the forty-first move.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Commander: The commander can give instructions to their respective player for up to a maximum of thirty minutes at any time. Time spent by the commander in this way will use up the allotted time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There were no provisions to add time, such as Fischer Rules. This was probably a concession Sakayanagi had made to get the school to approve adding the event to the roster, as chess tends to take a while. A single match could take two hours or more, so I assumed the time limit was set to one hour for this same reason. That made sense.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Three wins and three losses each, and now we’re going into the seventh and final event. I couldn’t possibly be any happier. I can’t believe that </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">this</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> event was chosen as the final one… My, it seems that good things do come to those who wait, after all,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She probably intended to intervene at a critical moment in the game and provide instructions to her player. Actually, we were most likely going to intervene at roughly the same time. Considering the rules for commander intervention, I didn’t see myself beating Sakayanagi without giving it my best effort.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Doesn’t the fact we drove you this far into a corner mean that Class A miscalculated?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would seem so. I must admit you put quite a bit of pressure on us in the sports events,” she replied, thinking back to the six we’d had so far. “However, the seventh event will be a little different. The commander’s abilities will have a significant impact on how this battle plays out.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m afraid I happen to be pretty good at chess.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">From this point onward, Sakagami-sensei and Hoshinomiya-sensei would witness our match. It was probably smart to take some precautions. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My, oh, my… What a coincidence! Perhaps choosing chess was a mistake on my part, then,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But first, the preliminary match was about to begin. A&#160;battle between the students we’d picked from our classes. I chose Horikita Suzune from the list of students who hadn’t yet participated in any events. Sakayanagi, on the other hand, chose…Hashimoto Masayoshi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh ho, so Horikita-san makes an appearance, after all. Even though she’s an exceptional student, you didn’t use her until now because you wanted to hold her in reserve until the final event, hm?” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s no need for me to hold onto my trump card anymore.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We informed the teachers of our selections so that they could get the match started.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you sure you don’t need to get a drink of water or anything?” said Hoshinomiya-sensei, worried that we hadn’t left our seats once during the entire exam.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you for your concern. However, there’s no need to worry.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m all right, too,” I added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that so? Well, all right then…” replied Hoshinomiya-sensei, letting out a sigh. The tense, strained atmosphere must be getting to her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seems like preparations are complete. Let us now begin the seventh event: chess.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At Sakagami-sensei’s instruction, Sakayanagi and I stopped our idle chatter at once. The stage they had prepared seemed to be in a corner of the lecture halls. A chessboard had been set there.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Hello.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Both Horikita and Hashimoto slowly bowed to one another. The final battle was about to begin.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">10.1</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">C</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HESSBOARD SAT</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> in front of me. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A week ago, I didn’t even know the rules. And here I was, right now, touching an actual chess piece for the first time in my life. Training with </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">him</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> online had made me come to understand the fascination and complexity of chess. If Ayanokouji-kun or Sakayanagi-san were my opponent, then my chances of winning would probably be one in a million.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, the student I was squaring off against wasn’t either of those two people. I had no idea how skilled Hashimoto-kun was, of course, but I couldn’t imagine he held a candle to either of those two.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good luck, Horikita.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My opponent addressed me casually. Based on what I’d heard, even the students in Class A considered him something of a charlatan. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, come now, don’t make such a grim face. Why not try to enjoy this?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Someone who spent the last year in Class A couldn’t understand how important this battle is to us, in Class&#160;C,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it sure would suck to have a ton of class points deducted if we lose this. That much is the same for us as it is for you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The class that won this chess game would get one hundred and thirty points. This really was a monumental battle. One that would decide whether we could secure those points to finish out our first year.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“By the way, do you remember my name?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve never spoken with you before, but it’s Hashimoto-kun, I believe.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh ho, what an honor. I mean, they say Horikita from Class C is a bit of a celebrity, y’know? If I remember right, I think I first heard your name back when you really pulled the rug out from under Ryuuen during the uninhabited island exam.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I hadn’t done anything back then. It was all part of Ayanokouji-kun’s strategy, which he had carried out from behind the scenes. Well, no…I supposed he might not have considered that a strategy at all.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, I’ve only been playing chess for a couple months. Go easy on me, okay?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, I should be asking you that. I’ve only been playing for about a week.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh ho…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The battle had already begun. Even though my experience with the game consisted of just practice matches, I knew anything said during this match could be a mixture of truth and lies. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chess was a game where you and your opponent tried to keep each other in check while simultaneously exploiting any cracks you found in their mentality. The school was quite lenient about students talking to one another during this exam, with the exception of the written tests, where answers could be said out loud. Ayanokouji-kun and Sakayanagi-san, the commanders, had almost certainly been sparring verbally just like this. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And now that the score was three wins and three losses, everything came down to the seventh event. All thanks to Hirata-kun’s return, Sudou-kun keeping his wits about him, and the collective efforts of a number of our classmates. The issue of Kouenji-kun was certainly something I needed to reflect on, but that could wait for another day. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I absolutely could not afford to squander this chance and lose this battle. I recalled what Ayanokouji-kun had said to me before the exam this morning. A statement so arrogant it left me stunned. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“No matter who I’m up against, there’s no one out there stronger than me, even when I’m slacking off.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While it was certainly irritating, for some reason, I felt like I could put faith in what he said. If Hashimoto-kun was no match for him at all, then I had a chance to win. For some reason, I felt like there was no way I could lose. I wonder why?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even before the battle started, all I could think about was how it felt like I had the upper hand.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We will now begin the seventh event, Chess. Both players, please take your seats.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Following the teacher’s instructions, I took my seat. The smile never left Hashimoto’s face, but it didn’t reach his eyes. The outcome of this game would directly decide the fate of our classes. It seemed that Hashimoto-kun was feeling the pressure in this situation, too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Welp, let’s get started, yeah?” said Hashimoto-kun, picking up two pawns, one black and one white. “You know how to decide who goes first, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After I told him I understood, he closed his hands, the pieces hidden, and presented both hands to me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Left,” I told him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Upon hearing my answer, he opened his hand, revealing the white piece.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That meant that I was going first.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m lookin’ forward to seeing what kinda opening move you’ll make.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not sure I can match your expectations.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I took the white chess piece. This was the first time I’d touched one, and it felt nice and cool. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And so, the seventh event—the battle between Hashimoto-kun and I—had begun.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My first move: pawn to E4. Just as the game officially started, Hashimoto-kun’s smile had briefly faded. He moved a black piece: pawn to E5. I quickly moved my knight, planning to take his pawn. Through all of the games I had played with Ayanokouji-kun, this was the playstyle that I had the most confidence in. I would control the flow of the game based on how my opponent reacted as they tried to defend their black pawn. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y’know, I learned a lot from Sakayanagi, too. You think I’m gonna let you start the game off by putting black at a disadvantage?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After those first moves, we both proceeded to make our next ones without spending too long thinking about them. The time limit was one hour. But Ayanokouji-kun had said to use thirty minutes, meaning that was all I had. I had no time to waste.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As we played, I realized something. My opponent was refusing to play defensively. Sakayanagi-san must have taught Hashimoto-kun to play this way, as it didn’t seem like a conventional playstyle at all. He was making offensive move after offensive move.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Pretty screwed-up playstyle, huh?” said Hashimoto-kun.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. Did you inherit this playstyle from your instructor?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yep. Sakayanagi played this way against me. I guess it’s like, when she taught me, this one just clicked for me the most, y’know? But, well, yours is different from mine. Seems pretty solid… You self-taught?”</span></p>

I had no choice but to use somewhat </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">forceful</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> methods in order to take that away from him, didn’t I? This is still a ‘school,’ more or less, no?” he replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So this had been nothing more than a pointless diversion. All for the purpose of purging the current administration. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh, for crying out loud. If everything had gone according to plan, we would’ve gotten Ayanokouji-kun expelled this time around. But it seems there’re quite a few overzealous teachers at this school, and they’re giving me quite a bit of trouble.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">During our game, there had been a number of instructions I’d issued to Horikita after a lengthy period of consideration. But even after inputting everything on the keyboard and pressing enter, there was almost a thirty-second delay before the instructions were received. Up until that point, the time lag before my instructions were received had only been about ten seconds. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This was because my instructions were tampered with. They were manipulated in the computer, so what I inputted and what came out were different. The automated voice played the tampered instructions over the intercom to Horikita instead.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He was planning to use a different move, at the time. An even better move than the best one we could’ve come up with. I had a significant number of personnel and even a dedicated machine in place, and we were still forced into making an extremely difficult decision.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If they’d made an overtly careless move, it would’ve been obvious to anyone watching that something was off. To avoid that happening, he’d been forced to come up with a complex move.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that sense, Sakayanagi-san did a marvelous job seeing through the move we came up with,” he added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was hardly praise, at this point.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why didn’t you say anything, Ayanokouji-kun?” asked Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if he did, it would’ve been pointless. No—it’s more that he </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">couldn’t</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> say anything at all,” Tsukishiro explained. “It’s simple, really. As a former inhabitant of the White Room, and moreover, as someone who forcibly inserted himself into this school, he doesn’t want to draw attention to himself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If word got out that Tsukishiro had interfered with me, it would cause me some major headaches down the line. Frustrating as it was, I had no choice but to grin and bear it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Pathetic as it might be, a victory is still a victory. Shouldn’t you be happy?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…You are quite good at provocation, Acting Director. However…you do realize that you’ll pay dearly for this, yes?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Acting Director Tsukishiro merely applauded once more at the sight of upon seeing Sakayanagi’s anger-filled smile.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You do say some </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">very</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> amusing things for a mere child, someone in her first year of high school. Have you gotten a big head simply because you’re, as they say, the queen bee here?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A student sharing a ring with Sakayanagi really wouldn’t want to make an enemy of her. But to this man, she probably just looked like a child who talked big. Nothing more. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you wish to make me pay dearly, then please go ahead and do something about it now. Quickly now, come on.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was no way that was happening. A brief silence had passed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, then, I suppose it’s time I make my exit. Adults do have quite a few responsibilities to keep us busy, after&#160;all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Acting Director Tsukishiro then walked forward, deliberately wedging his way between Sakayanagi and me as he went past.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, and if possible, please do choose to drop out voluntarily. That way, we can settle things without getting any other students getting caught up in this,” he added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He kept going down the hall, leaving me with that parting shot. Sakayanagi slowly started to walk away, too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now everything’s been completely ruined. How incredibly unpleasant.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You have nothing to apologize for, Ayanokouji-kun. I’m just disappointed by what happened when an adult interfered with the affairs of children, is all. He just trampled on my most wonderful memory.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She didn’t seem to care that her victory had been tainted, only that our competition had been interfered with.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But…don’t you think it unreasonable to expect me to be satisfied with this?” she asked, stopping in her tracks and looking up at me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose so. Yeah, you’re probably right about that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had intended to be quiet about Acting Director Tsukishiro’s interference, but in the end, it might actually be a good thing that Sakayanagi had found out. Deep down, I was also bothered by what had happened. Even if only a little. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So please, let us continue our competition. Let us pick back up from before the acting director screwed everything up,” said Sakayanagi. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It would be easy for me to refuse her request. But I felt like it would break something inside her if I did that. And maybe something in me, too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have no reason to refuse, I guess. But where should we do this?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you know that there’s a chessboard in the library?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nope… First time I’ve heard about it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I occasionally play on it. Let’s use that for our game.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We headed over to the library. There was no one there today, most likely because the special exam had ended and all classes were done for the day. Inside that entirely still, quiet library, I took the chessboard in my hands and placed it on a small table that two people could fit at. Sakayanagi adeptly arranged the pieces to match how they’d been near the end of our game.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now, then, we’re in the same position as we were then. Please show me the move that you were really going to make.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I picked up my piece and placed it where it was supposed to have gone.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">10.3</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">O</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">UR GAME RESUMED</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> and time marched on, with neither of us saying a word to the other. It was evening now, and only the clacking sounds of the white and black chess pieces could be heard in the library. It didn’t last long, though. There was no need to spend too much time on a game we’d picked up from a point when it was nearly over. Before long, the match came to an end. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi let out a quiet sigh while looking at the board. There was no longer any way for her to avoid being put in check.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just as I’d expect of you, Ayanokouji-kun. It seems I’ve lost this game.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was the kind of game where every single move we made was the difference between life and death. Sakayanagi didn’t seem displeased at all. In fact, she acknowledged her defeat with an air of satisfaction.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re pretty upfront about how you feel.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do I look like a woman too proud to admit her own defeat?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Honestly, I’d probably be lying if I said I couldn’t see it…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wanted to know which of us stood on top. I would never do something like whine and complain about the result.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But even though I might have won, this is just a replay of that game, at best. There’s no guarantee it would have gone the same way back then.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t dismiss the possibility that the moves I’d ultimately come up with were things I’d thought of in the extra time I had been given. But more importantly…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This outcome was a result of the advantage Horikita created when she was playing against Hashimoto. The way I see it, that means I held that advantage when I took over for her. So I don’t think we were really playing on even ground.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The game had unfolded the way it did precisely because Horikita had pulled our side ahead and then passed that advantage to me. Sakayanagi’s ability to turn the tide when handed a disadvantageous situation was testament to her skill. If we were to play again, there was no guarantee that I’d win. She was skilled enough that if she </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">did</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> propose we play again, I’d be tempted to turn her down and run away for real.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you trying to console me?” Sakayanagi giggled. She must have found what I said strange.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, it’s not like that. I was just stating the facts, objectively.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m satisfied with these results. Isn’t that enough?” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If she was satisfied, then I supposed it was fine. But it didn’t make me feel better.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When the special exam was announced, you could’ve focused on getting me to compete directly with you in a one-on-one event. If you’d suggested something like that, I would have had no choice but to accept. But you didn’t. Why not?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, the seven events that were chosen from the ten possibilities were randomly selected. There was no guarantee such an event would have been chosen. But even so, if we’d both come to an agreement about a one-on-one event, then such an event would have had a higher chance of coming to fruition. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The reason is simple,” she replied. “As I’m sure you’ve already surmised, Ayanokouji-kun, there was no guarantee such an event would be drawn. And if you carelessly competed against me one-on-one, it would undoubtedly cause the people around you to grow suspicious. I wanted to avoid that. Though the acting director took advantage of it all, in the end…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi had done her best to consider and accommodate my circumstances when she made her plans for this special exam. That was probably exactly why she was so irritated by Tsukishiro’s interference. I could go so far as to guess that the seven events chosen today, and the order in which they were chosen, were most likely not random at all. There was no way we could’ve had a fair fight.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Besides, Hashimoto-kun is regarded as the most talented chess player in Class A, and he was losing to Horikita-san, who was taught by you. Which means I lost in that regard, as well.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi slowly bowed to me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun. I’m glad I was able to do battle with you. The answer is clear to me now. You are, without a doubt, a genius. You are by no means a fake.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re not thinking about a rematch?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you want me to?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…No, I don’t.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Fu fu</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, you’re an honest one.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We’d only been able to have this quiet game because we were in the midst of an extremely rare moment. It was precisely because the special exam was over now and the long vacation was starting tomorrow that we could find an empty space with no one else around. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“As for the reason why I’m not going to seek a rematch… To be honest, it’s because I’ve determined that our chess skills are about even. If we played ten games for fun, it wouldn’t be surprising if we each ended up with five wins and five losses. Am I wrong in making this assessment?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nah, it’s accurate.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If we were to compete against each other again and again, things would play out just as Sakayanagi said they would. We were evenly matched in ability, which was interesting.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“However, I have a feeling you had the upper hand in our first game. Now that I think back to it…I might have been losing. Well, I suppose you’ve been playing chess a little bit longer than me, Ayanokouji-kun. I’m sure that made a difference.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Judging from the look on her face, she had a bit of a competitive streak. It sounded like winning was important to her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Besides, if I sought a chess rematch to try and win against you, I’d no longer be playing for the fun of it. Chess is a fun pastime for me. I wish to keep it that way,” she added, picking up a knight.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You mentioned my history with the game. I’m guessing that means you did see me before, after all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I was watching you, Ayanokouji-kun, while you overwhelmed your opponents in the White Room. Ever since then, I became fond of playing chess. I believed that a day would come when I would get to play against you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So the feeling I had when I saw the list of proposed events from Class A had been right. It wasn’t mere coincidence that chess had been chosen as one of their events.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now then… I suppose it’s about time for us to be going, yes?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll tidy things up. Sit tight for a minute.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you very much. I will gladly accept your kind offer.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I put the board and pieces back where they originally belonged.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, I will be keeping some distance from you from now on, Ayanokouji-kun. Our classmates may become suspicious if I continue to fixate on you forever. But more importantly…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“More importantly?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve been dying to get to know you. I feel like you’re a childhood friend whom I’ve been chasing for the longest time but haven’t had the chance to actually meet. If we can compete against one another so easily, then I feel like the value of doing so will be diminished,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She looked at me and smiled sweetly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Though when you factor in Acting Director Tsukishiro, I suppose we don’t have time for students to be fighting with one another.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Talk about getting your priorities backward. Normally, this school </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">wanted</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> students to compete with each other. But even if Sakayanagi and I did compete in a similar fashion again, we couldn’t say for certain that he wouldn’t interfere once more. In fact, he’d probably do anything he could to sabotage me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In that respect, I supposed I was grateful I didn’t have to be on alert for other things, aside from him. It would be incredibly exhausting to be surrounded by enemies on all sides. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi and I left the library.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come to think of it, this is the first time the two of us have walked back to the dormitories together like this, isn’t it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now that you mention it, yeah.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi always had people with her. On top of that, the thought of us walking side by side was normally unimaginable. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry, I’m quite a slow walker.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t have to apologize for that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was certainly slow. It was because of her disability, though. And oddly enough, I was grateful for that today. If I walked at my usual pace, I would’ve reached the dorms in no time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What will you do now?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait and see what Tsukishiro does, I guess. Even if he’s just the ‘acting’ director, I probably can’t slip just anything by him.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose you’re right. Given the current state of things, it doesn’t seem like getting my father reinstated will be easy.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What about you? What are you planning to do?” I&#160;asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I asked her that question, she stopped to think for a moment.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose I’ll continue to enjoy myself for now, as I’ve been doing so far. If Katsuragi-kun tries to rebel against me, I will take care of him. And if Ichinose tries to close in on Class A, it’ll be fun to crush her and toy with her. If she got expelled, I could watch Class B collapse.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She smiled innocently, like a girl playing with dolls.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I honestly didn’t foresee Ryuuen-kun making a move, but…if he does come seeking a fight, then I’d like to do battle with him, too. Surprisingly enough, I might not be bored at this school after all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s nice.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you going to do, Ayanokouji-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If possible, I’d like to avoid anything that might make me stand out. I’ll just let Horikita continue to work hard.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And I’m sure she’ll astonish everyone with how much she grows. I am quite looking forward to it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Someday, Sakayanagi might be able to list Horikita’s name alongside Ichinose and Ryuuen’s as an opponent she would be wary of. If that day came, I was sure Sakayanagi would enjoy her time at this school all the more.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…There is one thing I’d like to apologize for.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Apologize?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Earlier, when I talked about the reason why I avoided a one-on-one match with you, I lied.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She’d said she avoided it out of consideration for me, to keep attention from being focused on me. Now, she retracted that answer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To be honest, it’s because I wanted to be with you, Ayanokouji-kun, even if for just one second longer,” said Sakayanagi, offering her right hand to me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I took it, thinking that she was going for a handshake. But she put her left hand over mine, enveloping it. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“People can come to know warmth by touch. And that is precious. The warmth of another’s skin is by no means a bad thing. Please remember that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s a belated message from me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I stood there, still unable to understand what she meant. Sakayanagi slowly let go of my hand and started walking away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now, then, shall we be going?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It seemed she didn’t intent to tell me anything else. As the two of us walked back to the dorms, we gazed at the setting sun.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That reminds me, have you heard? Yoshida-kun from Class A, he…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We didn’t have the kind of relationship where you might reminisce about the old days. But we had no objectives in this moment, either. We were just talking about ordinary, everyday things. Just until the moment we arrived at the dorms.</span></p>
