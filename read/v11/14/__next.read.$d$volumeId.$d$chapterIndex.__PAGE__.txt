<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 8:<br/>Ayanokouji VS. Sakayanagi</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">FTER A LONG PERIOD</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> of preparation, the day of the final special exam for the first-year students was here. The commanders of the two losing classes would be expelled—or, more accurately, stripped of their Protection Points. While no one would be getting expelled, you might say the most important aspect of this exam was that class points could fluctuate significantly. Depending on the outcome, there was a significant possibility that the entire class hierarchy would be flipped.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Forget about all the data from the notebook I showed you yesterday and forget about everything I told you,” Horikita, my neighbor, said while we waited for our morning homeroom to begin. “Choose whatever five events you like and then select who will compete.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If I take over and it messes with your plans, won’t the other students be unable to adapt, though?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I made no promises to anyone about what events we were going with, or if they’d be put in play or not. I&#160;simply said that I would be playing things by ear, making decisions and reacting logically to our needs at the moment, depending on the ten events that come up and the order. So, it should be fine,” she answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Meaning she’d set everything up perfectly, and in such a way that I could fight without anything to bother me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not taking responsibility even if something goes wrong, though,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is a class-wide competition. Even if the commanders are able to intervene, this exam is basically testing Class C’s overall abilities. Our enemy is Class A, led by Sakayanagi-san. The most formidable opponent in our grade. Even if you lose, no one will blame you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Giving Horikita a sidelong glance, I checked the last message that she had sent me. It was a record of everything the students in Class C had done for the past two weeks to prepare for the special exam. Stuff like what everyone had discussed, what events students had practiced for, and how they’d practiced for those events.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll put your efforts to good use. All your efforts,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I got up from my seat and was about to walk away, Horikita gave me a few parting words. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The chances of chess being chosen are seven out of ten. That’s not low,” she told me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Over the past several days, Horikita and I had played several games of chess.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In the end, I hardly ever won against you, even when you were going easy on me,” she added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was certainly true that she had only beat me a few times. Few enough to count on my fingers. But there was no need to count her losses, really. The chess skills Horikita had picked up in such a short period of time were quite impressive.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No matter who I’m up against, there’s no one out there stronger than me, even when I’m slacking off. Don’t forget that,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You sure have a great deal of confidence in yourself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Having finished my conversation with Horikita, I left to go fulfill my duties as commander. The remaining students were basically on standby, waiting for me to issue them instructions from the multi-purpose room. After the events were announced, the students would then go to the designated locations, get changed, and so on. Since finer details wouldn’t be shown on the monitors, information would probably be shared when they returned.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">8.1</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ENTERED THE SPECIAL BUILDING</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> and headed toward my destination. When I got there, I saw that Sakayanagi and Ichinose, who had arrived before me, were chatting. Apparently, the multi-purpose room hadn’t been opened to us yet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good morning to you, Ayanokouji-kun,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good morning, Ayanokouji-kun!” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They both greeted me at the same time. I raised my hand to give them a relaxed wave in response.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems like we’re not allowed in yet,” I remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“They told us to let them know once all four of us had arrived here,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was probably to ensure that the exam was carried out as fairly as possible. If someone were allowed to go in the multi-purpose room before everyone else, they’d be able to get a feel for the test location, perhaps even use that to calm their mind. Considering this was a particularly unique special exam, I supposed there was no limit to how far they’d go to ensure it was fair.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems like we’re only missing Kaneda then,” I observed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yep,” replied Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I looked around. While I still didn’t see any sign of Kaneda, I supposed it wasn’t like he would be late.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I must say, you really are quite lucky, aren’t you, Ichinose-san?” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Lucky?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“As they are right now, Class D is essentially a bunch of infants. It’s not like they could beat Class B, even in a million years. I suppose the only question that remains is how many wins you can accumulate, hm? If you manage to win all seven events in a row, then it’s even possible that you’ll change places with us, depending on how Class A performs,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I don’t know about that. I don’t know what’s going to happen, but I’m sure they’ll fight us tooth and nail. We can’t be too careful,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Upon seeing Ichinose’s renewed determination, an amused smile appeared on Sakayanagi’s lips.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, did I say something weird or something?” said Ichinose, puzzled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not at all. It’s just that you spoke as if you’re already on top, just waiting for a challenger to appear. If nothing else, it seems you don’t see Class D as an equal opponent. But I suppose that’s only to be expected from the leader who defended Class B for the entire year,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her words were a touch mean-spirited. But Ichinose didn’t let it get to her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We came here with a strategy to win this, too. We’re not going down that easily, especially in an exam where unity plays such a big part,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Please pardon my rudeness. You are most certainly correct, Ichinose-san,” replied Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I looked through the window while I listened to their conversation. April was almost here, and the weather was clear, with not a single cloud in the wide-open sky. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Five minutes passed, and we started to wonder if Class&#160;D might be late. Then, finally, we heard the faint sound of approaching footsteps from the hallway. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems he’s not late or he’s abstaining from the contest due to nerves, after all.” Sakayanagi sounded amused that Kaneda would be showing up at the last possible minute.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose seemed to be trying to regain her composure and focus, now that the test was finally about to begin. We’d meet up with Kaneda and all enter the multi-purpose room together. That was what the three of us had imagined would happen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Someone unexpected showed up instead. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose looked the most shocked of everyone present when this newcomer entered our field of vision. While Sakayanagi was surprised as well, her eyes quickly changed to show amusement.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Ryuuen-kun? Why…are you here…?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A wave of uneasiness seemed to wash over Ichinose. Well, actually, neither Sakayanagi nor I had expected this to happen. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s the matter? What are you all upset for?” Ryuuen, the former leader of Class D, deliberately drew attention to the fact that Ichinose was upset.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see… Well, I hadn’t expected this. I had thoroughly convinced myself that the commanders for this special exam would be the students with Protection Points,” said Sakayanagi, the first of us to figure out what was going on. Kaneda was nowhere to be seen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The special exam can’t start without the commanders. Meaning, if the commander is absent, then obviously someone has to take their place. Isn’t that right?” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I supposed an unexpected absence on the day of the exam was certainly a possibility. There was probably some kind of system the school had in place for such an event, like having one or two people ready as replacements for the commander. And of course, if that happened, the substitute commander would be the one to accept responsibility in the event of a loss.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Still, I never imagined that this would happen and that you’d be here, Ryuuen-kun,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, yeah, I s’pose not. Especially you, Ichinose. Even if you sprung a fever or got injured on the day of the event, I guess you’d still come crawling along the floor to avoid letting other students run the risk of gettin’ expelled, huh,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was no way to prevent the commander’s expulsion other than a Protection Point, in the event of a loss. Just as Sakayanagi had said, we’d all assumed that the students who had Protection Points would be the commanders for this special exam.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose cleared her throat. I was sure she’d been wary of this back when the special exam was first, but the possibility had vanished from her mind once Kaneda was determined to be the commander for Class D. If I had to guess, Ichinose had unconsciously gone through the options in her head and eliminated Ryuuen as a potential commander, without even realizing it. She’d assumed we’d all be facing off against students who had Protection Points.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, I’m guessing that there must be some kind of penalty for someone to participate as a substitute, right?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yep. Kaneda isn’t allowed to participate in any events. I mean, that’s pretty reasonable, all things considered,” answered Ryuuen, apparently having already factored in the penalty into his decision.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you do this to shock me? Even if you did, won’t it be bad for you guys if Kaneda-kun can’t participate?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I didn’t know everything there was to know about Kaneda, he was at least an asset to Class D. So what was the point of this bizarre strategy, which required losing him? That part gnawed at me. When had it been decided that Ryuuen would be the commander? If it had been decided right away, did that mean that this was all part of the plan? </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The same questions were undoubtedly racing through Ichinose’s mind right now, making her feel terribly confused.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, come on, no need to keep your guard up around me. I’m just a human sacrifice. The commander from the losing class gets expelled, remember? This just means that those Class D knuckleheads can officially get rid of me. That’s all there is to it, right?” sneered Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, does that mean that you’ll take it easy on me?” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Ku ku.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Sure, I’ll ease up on ya. So, just relax and bring it,” said Ryuuen, spreading his arms out wide, like he was welcoming her to come at him. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But there was no way Ichinose was going to let her guard down.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When you want to win, you do so by any means necessary. That’s the way you do things, isn’t it?” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When I’ve decided that I’m gonna win, then yeah, it&#160;is.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I wish you wouldn’t. You don’t have a Protection Point, Ryuuen-kun. You’re fighting with your back to the wall, making your last stand. I can’t help but have a sinking feeling Class B might end up losing this,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose was the kind of person who worked hard to build confidence, trust, and security from the ground up. She wasn’t good at dealing with sudden, unexpected developments. She would’ve been just fine up against a normal opponent, but Ryuuen was anything but normal. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The shock might not stop at Ichinose, either, but soon spread to the rest of the students in Class B. Everyone in her class would inevitably notice that Ryuuen had become the commander. And even if they didn’t, Ishizaki and the others would let them know. And if that happened, the Class B students wouldn’t be able to hide how flustered they were, just like Ichinose. For Ryuuen, who was supposed to have been neutralized, to have become the commander… The students of Class B couldn’t possibly predict what he’d instruct his classmates to do, and that would scare them.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, well, it seems the showdown between Class B and Class D is…shaping up to be quite interesting,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This wasn’t exactly a laughing matter for Ichinose, though. She should have taken action back when Class D students were repeatedly stalking and harassing her classmates. If she’d been able to detect Ryuuen’s presence in the background at the time, then she might not have been so flustered right now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, now that everyone is here, let’s be going, shall we?” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With Sakayanagi leading the way, we entered the multi-purpose room. When we got inside, we saw a newly constructed wall that hadn’t been there on the first&#160;day. It&#160;divided the room into two perfect halves. Though it was a temporary construction, it looked quite sturdy, and seemed to be quite soundproof, too. The four instructors in charge of the first-year classes were lined up, waiting for us. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Class B and Class D representatives, please move over to the other side,” said Mashima-sensei. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As per his instructions, Ichinose and Ryuuen went over to the other side of the wall, disappearing from view. Chabashira followed after them. We, the commanders of Class A and Class C, were guided by Class D’s Sakagami-sensei and Class B’s Hoshinomiya-sensei. It seemed the teachers had been put in charge of classes other than their&#160;own.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The exam will begin in five minutes, so take this time to mentally prepare yourselves, okay?” Hoshinomiya-sensei advised us, before going off to have what looked like a final talk with Sakagami-sensei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi and I had a little bit of time alone before the exam started. Just the two of us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Finally… The day has finally come. To be honest, I wasn’t able to sleep last night, and I almost overslept this morning,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t recall making you wait that long for this. Besides, it was just a coincidence that you and I met in the first place.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you saying that if you hadn’t come to this school, we wouldn’t have met?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I nodded in response. She laughed, rejecting my answer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s certainly true that our reunion at this school was mere coincidence, yes. However, I was absolutely sure the day would come when we would meet again. It was fated to happen,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Fate? Okay, now you’re saying some pretty abstract stuff,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I am but a young maiden, after all,” she answered, smiling, approaching me slowly while holding onto her cane. “If you hadn’t enrolled at this school, I suppose I would have postponed our meeting for another three years. I was confident I could rein in my excitement, hide it deep down, and avoid rushing things. But that quickly proved to be quite impossible. From the moment I knew you were this close to me, the days just seemed to get longer and longer. I wanted to fight you right away. It took an unbelievable degree of effort to restrain myself. That’s how much I dreamed of this day.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The words poured eloquently from her mouth. So, her wish was being granted, huh?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Aren’t you afraid of waking up from this dream?” I&#160;asked her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once we actually competed against each other, there’d be no going back from it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose everyone has to wake up at some point,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She didn’t mind if she woke up. Or maybe, that was just how she felt today. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Normally, I would…ask you to please be gentle with me, but…” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her eyes didn’t look like that of a young maiden’s. They had the sharpness of a hunter stalking its prey.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please come at me with all your might,” she said.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If I pulled my punches, she wouldn’t be happy. I wasn’t doing this to make her happy…but it would get annoying to stay involved with her any further. At the same time, I had my doubts about whether this special exam would really satisfy her. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As if she had sensed what I was thinking, Sakayanagi spoke up once more. “I’d be lying if I said I didn’t feel conflicted. The contents of this special exam are far too inadequate for both of us to sufficiently demonstrate our abilities. And even though we are the commanders, there is a limit to how much we can intervene.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The school would never implement an exam where the outcome depended on the efforts of a single person—the commander. Even so, Sakayanagi was saying that as long as the two of us could have our showdown, everything else was trivial. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That being said, I suppose it would give rise to other problems if the commanders had </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">too</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> much involvement in the exam. I’m trying to be considerate of you, Ayanokouji-kun. You don’t want your classmates to find out about your abilities, after all. Right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was grateful for her consideration. If this test had been set up to let the commander’s involvement have a significant impact on the outcome of every event, then I probably wouldn’t have been able to use my abilities to their fullest. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right! The exam is just about to begin. Please take your seats!” said Hoshinomiya-sensei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In response to her instructions, Sakayanagi and I sat across from one another, with the computers and equipment between us. Naturally, this meant we couldn’t see each other’s faces. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Displayed on my computer screen were pictures of the faces of everyone in Class C. Thirty-eight people, excluding me. These were the people I would be assigning as participants to the events that were selected. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Next, the ten events that we had prepared were displayed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I am Sakagami, and I will be in charge of proctoring this special exam for you. So, without further delay, let us begin the final special exam for the first-year students. We ask that the representatives from each class press select five events and then press the confirm button.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I selected the five events Horikita had her mind set on and pressed the button without hesitation.
