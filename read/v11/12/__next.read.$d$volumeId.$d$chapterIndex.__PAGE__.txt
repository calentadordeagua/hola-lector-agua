<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 6:<br/>Traps, Home Cooking, and a Favor</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">RATHER UNUSUAL INCIDENT</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> occurred the same day. It happened at the start of lunch break, when the Ayanokouji Group made their way to the cafeteria. As I walked with Akito and the others, we heard someone shout, somewhat aggressively.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, Ichinose. We should definitely say something about this! We gotta protest!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The person who shouted those words was none other than Shibata, from first-year Class B. He was accompanied by two other students form Class B: Ichinose and Kanzaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh, now </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">there’s</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> something you don’t see every day. It’s pretty unusual for Shibata-kun to get that angry,” said Haruka.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, that’s definitely not something I’d expect,” replied Akito.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Their surprise was understandable.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really?” asked Airi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She didn’t seem to know anything about it at all, since she didn’t really involve herself with the other classes. Shibata was part of the soccer club. He was bright, cheerful, popular, though a little different from Hirata. As far as I knew, he wasn’t the kind of person to raise his voice like that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But isn’t it possible that it was just a simple coincidence?” replied Ichinose calmly, trying to reason with the upset Shibata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, Shibata seemed to be convinced otherwise, because he immediately denied the possibility.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not. That was the third time just today. Get it? They’re definitely trying to pick a fight!” he asserted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki noticed us on our way to lunch, and gently gestured to Shibata, who looked over at us with an embarrassed expression on his face. He was trying to play it cool, but it was already too late. There was an awkward silence.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, were you guys just on your way to lunch?” asked Ichinose, calling out to us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She wasn’t addressing a specific person, but rather our group as a whole. My friends hadn’t really interacted much with the leader of Class B, so they felt flustered, unsure of how to respond. Haruka, standing next to me, jabbed me in the side with her elbow. I decided to speak up on behalf of the group.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Yeah. We’re headed to the café. What’s up?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After hearing my answer, Ichinose clapped her hands together happily.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, hey, what a coincidence. We’re headed there too,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I noticed something a little strange. Normally, Ichinose always made eye contact with me when we talked. But today, she wasn’t.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, if you like, how about we all have lunch together?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Everyone in the Ayanokouji group exchanged glances with one another, a bit bewildered at this unexpected invitation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you doing, Ichinose?” asked Kanzaki. He sounded a little flustered and confused, probably because he hadn’t expected Ichinose to offer us something like that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What am I doing…? We’re not competing against Class C or anything, so it’s not a problem, is it?” she replied?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that’s true, it’s not, but…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki didn’t seem that open to the idea of inviting us to join them for lunch. But, if Ichinose had already settled on it, there was no way he could refuse her. We, on the other hand, were left a little unsure of what to do or how to answer… </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, time’s a-wastin’! Let’s go!” said Ichinose cheerfully.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When she smiled like that, there was no way anyone could refuse her.</span></p>

<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">6.1</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">W</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">E JOINED TWO TABLES</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> in a corner of the café and ate lunch together. Not only was this group made up of Class B and Class C students, but it was just a generally odd combination of folks.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry for inviting you to join us out of the blue like that. It’s my treat though, so please, eat up!” Ichinose apologized, then announced happily.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you sure about that, Ichinose?” said Kanzaki, reacting strongly to her offering to treat us. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In the last special exam, Ichinose had made a deal with Class D. She’d had her class cast their praise votes for Ryuuen to keep him from being expelled, and in return, she’d saved a Class B student from the same fate. Doing so should have used up all her private points. I&#160;was sure she’d come up with some way to get by and make ends meet, but it wasn’t like she could afford to live lavishly. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We were already planning to eat here, like we usually do, so we can cover ourselves,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Everyone in the group nodded in agreement.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I kind of strong-armed you into coming, though, so you don’t have to force yourself…” said Ichinose quietly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nah, it’s all right. This way, we can all eat without feeling guilty.” I declined her offer, using the pretense of us then being able to enjoy our lunch as equals.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So…why </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">did</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> you invite us to join you, anyway?” asked Keisei, apparently unable to hold himself back any longer. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I guess it’s because you all just seemed so shocked at how Shibata-kun was acting earlier. I figured it might be less confusing for everyone if I told you the whole story up front, rather than letting wild speculation get around,” reasoned Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her judgment might be correct. If Ichinose hadn’t approached us, we’d probably have discussed what happened with Shibata among ourselves for a while, wondering why he was so angry. We might have talked to a third party and unintentionally gotten the word out,&#160;too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you sure it’s okay to tell them?” asked Kanzaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you really think this is something we need to keep on the down-low?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We can’t rule out the possibility that someone in Class C might be involved.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if there is, it wouldn’t really make much difference, would it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, Ichinose’s got a point there. Honestly, we’re just grumbling,” said Shibata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As soon as Shibata spoke up, Kanzaki shot him a sharp glare.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what, Kanzaki?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nothing…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Shibata didn’t seem to understand the true meaning of what Kanzaki was trying to tell him with that look. He was probably trying to rebuke Shibata for so carelessly using the word “grumbling,” but the other students didn’t seem to notice anyway, so it didn’t matter.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Anyway, now that they’ve heard this much, don’t you think it’d be best to just tell them?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I suppose.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Shibata’s thoughtless comment had apparently been the deciding factor, causing Kanzaki to back down.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To put it simply, it seems like Class D has been, well, kind of harassing us lately,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Kind of?”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> replied Shibata, incredulous. “They’ve been messing with me, Nakanishi, and even Beppu. It’s like, I dunno. They’re messing with us for no reason at all or like, they’re just </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">following</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> us around. I heard Beppu was nearly scared to death when Albert silently cornered him against a wall!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki must have decided it wouldn’t really matter at this point if he spoke up or not, since we’d already heard this much. So, he joined the conversation, too. “I&#160;interviewed both Nakanishi and Beppu. Their stories check out.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Which meant Class D had been targeting some Class B students since the start of the special exam. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But it’s not like anyone’s actually gotten in a fistfight, have they?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For now, no.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So Class D students had just been intimidating kids and following them around. They hadn’t caused any physical damage so far. Of course, if they </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">did </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">end up crossing that line and resorting to physical violence, there’d be trouble.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess this is just their way of trying to put pressure on us. I suspect their plan is to keep this kind of harassment going until the big exam day, in order to wear us down and dull our judgment.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, gimme a break, man. Class D is just flat out scary, that’s who they are. I mean, even Class C has gotten caught up in their shenanigans before. I’m sure they know what we’re talking about, y’know?” said Shibata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Shibata was probably referring to the time Sudou got in a fight with Ishizaki and Komiya. Keisei, who had been quietly listening to the conversation so far, now opened his mouth to speak.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know it’s probably weird to get advice from another class, but come to think of it, this isn’t unusual. It’s certainly true that Class D has a reputation for being the bad guys, but I think a certain degree of pressure is only to be expected from this exam. In fact, we’ve had people from Class A trying to spy on us, apparently,” said Keisei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh, really?” asked Shibata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Keisei responded with a nod before proceeding to tell them how a student from Class A hung around near our classroom and tried to eavesdrop on our conversations.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Class D is desperate, too. I wonder if they’re also trying to get their hands on whatever information they can get?” said Shibata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He seemed totally convinced, just based off of the meager information he’d gotten from Class C. That being said, it certainly seemed like Class B was the one suffering the most here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, there’s no denying the fact that if we played it straight, our class would definitely have the advantage in this exam. We should expect the possibility that they’ll continue to harass us like this until they get to the very limit of what’s considered acceptable without violating school rules,” reasoned Kanzaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The only part of this theory that stuck out to me was the fact Class D was only harassing </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">some</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> students. Was it possible they’d decided it was too risky to go after Ichinose and Kanzaki…? Or did they have another goal in mind?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t imagine this is a strategy that Kaneda-kun thought of, though. Maybe Ishizaki-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, maybe.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know it worries you, but we need to just do the best we can. We’ll pick events that make good use of our class’s unity, so that we don’t let our teamwork get disrupted, and try our hardest on the day of the actual exam. Right?” asked Ichinose. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki and Shibata both nodded in agreement.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Meaning you’re not going to do anything in response to Class D, Ichinose-san? Not even reconnaissance?” asked Keisei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s right. We’re not going to do that. We’re going to focus on dealing with the ten events that come out next week,” said Ichinose. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Basically, that meant they intended to continue fighting as themselves, focusing on bettering themselves. They wouldn’t be sidetracked by the hunt for information, but would face things head-on, relying on the truth. A solid and reliable approach.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow. I don’t know what to say, except that you Class B folks really are incredible?” said Keisei, astonished. He continued, “But wouldn’t you normally do whatever it takes to beat a class above you? I mean, if things like spying and silent intimidation are effective, then you should probably use them. To be honest, focusing on yourself, not doing anything to your opponent, taking the high road—that’s not something our class could possibly do.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I don’t know if I’d put it like that. Maybe it’s just that we’re not clever enough to do that kind of stuff?” replied Ichinose, with a smile. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Anyway, I think I understand what you wanted to tell us. If we went around casually telling people about Shibata being all angry earlier, we’d basically be signaling to everyone that Class D’s strategy is working,” said Keisei, having unraveled the meaning behind why Ichinose had invited us all out to lunch.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If Class D found out what kind of damage their harassment was doing to Class B, it would just encourage them to keep it up, meaning Class B would have even more to deal with than they already did. Class B wanted to hold their ground instead and maintain that Class D’s strategy was pointless.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Exactly. Which is why I’d like to ask you to please not spread it around too much, if at all possible,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It wouldn’t benefit us at all to do so. And we don’t want to make an enemy of Class B,” answered Keisei, accepting her request.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Haruka, Akito, and then Airi all nodded in agreement, without hesitation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you so much, everyone!” exclaimed Ichinose. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As she did so, our eyes met for the first and only time that day. In that moment, she casually brushed her hair away from her face. As if carried on the wind, a faint citrusy scent tickled my nose. She quickly looked away, returning her gaze to our group as a whole. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose really was acting a little strange today. Not that I was going to point that out right now.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, Ichinose-san sure is cute, isn’t she? Oh, and that last smile she gave us at the end there? Ugh, it’s like, not fair. Don’t you think so?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Me? Not really…” replied Keisei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Yukimuu, your face is turning red just thinking about it!” she teased.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is not!” he protested.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, there’s no point in denying it. I’m a girl, and even I think she’s cute. I bet guys are totally head over heels for her,” said Haruka.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Airi must have agreed, because she was nodding along vigorously.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You think so too, right? Miyachi? Ayanokouji-kun?” asked Haruka.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since Akito and I didn’t want to be targeted like Keisei, we just wore wry smiles and dodged the question.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know, I might have just been imagining it, but… Has Ichinose-san ever used perfume before?” asked Airi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, hey, I picked up on that too. She was wearing some kind of citrus-scented perfume, right?” said Haruka.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. That might have been what surprised me the most. Maybe she’s got something on her mind or something?” said Airi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, what do the three of you think?” Haruka asked, posing us guys a question we couldn’t possibly know the answer to.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, was she wearing perfume? I mean, even if she was, maybe it’s just because she felt like it or something?” replied Keisei, matter-of-factly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">His offhanded comment prompted an openly exasperated sigh from Haruka.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh, boys, really… They never really notice the small changes, do they?” sighed Haruka.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…More importantly, it looks like we’re not the only ones having trouble. It seems Class B has a tough road ahead of them, too.” Akito changed the subject, probably not wanting to deal with any more of Haruka’s teasing.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Class D probably can’t afford to care about appearances if they’re going to win against a higher-ranked class. It’s possible they might escalate their harassment even further, going forward,” said Keisei, taking this chance to bail on the previous conversation and hopping onto the topic Akito presented.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">His prediction was probably accurate. It seemed there were only three people being harassed so far, but it wouldn’t be surprising if the number of victims crept up. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And Ryuuen’s nowhere to be found, either. So they probably won’t stand a chance of winning if they </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">don’t</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> do at least that much.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even so, it feels to me like they’re pretty much following Ryuuen-kun’s example, anyway, and doing what he would’ve done.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">True enough. Applying pressure like this was definitely the kind of strategy Ryuuen would come up with.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But it’s pointless, isn’t it? That’s not going to be enough to break Class B’s defenses. After talking with them today, I thought, maybe it’s a </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">good </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">thing that we’re up against Class A this time. Because I really don’t want to be fighting Class B,” said Keisei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? What makes you think that, Yukimuu?” asked Haruka.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I mean, the depth of their solidarity and the fact that they approach things honestly, without overestimating themselves, is incredible. I don’t think any other class can even touch them in that area. It allows them to consistently produce good results, no matter the test. I don’t think we could beat them,” said Keisei.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HE WEEKEND WAS OVER</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> and before any of us knew it, it was Sunday, March 14th. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Prepared?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“O-Oh, it’s nothing, don’t worry about it.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, see ya later.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-Y-Y-Yeah! S-See you, later!” she stammered.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come to think of it, I didn’t catch a whiff of that citrus scent today,” I mused to myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, it was early in the morning on a holiday. There was no reason for her to go out with perfume on.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T WAS MONDAY MORNING</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">—the day our opponent’s ten events would be announced. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While on my way to school, I just so happened to bump into Horikita’s brother and Tachibana. It didn’t seem like they were waiting for me. If anything, it really seemed to just be a coincidence this time. </span></p>

<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">6.9</span></p>

But as soon as I set foot inside her room, I noticed something strange.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">For some reason, places had been set for two people at her small table. I wondered if she was going to have dinner with someone after we were done talking?</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Chop</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. She got done chopping something with her kitchen knife and then moved to finish the miso soup. </span></p>
