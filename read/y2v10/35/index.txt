<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.94.1">“Of course it can happen. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.94.2">If you had begged me to give you a Class Transfer Ticket, saying that you wished to transfer classes, should the opportunity have arisen, I would have gladly given it to you.”</span></span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.97.1">“Be serious. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.97.2">I am sure you would not have accepted the ticket.”</span></span></p>
