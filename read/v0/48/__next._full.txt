I don&apos;t understand the politics, but it seems that a politician named Naoe has been working to prevent Ayanokouji-sensei&apos;s return. He&apos;s caused us some trouble. However, I decided to think positively. An extended vacation after a long time without one&#x2014;that&apos;s not too bad.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Taking a breather, Suzukake powered off the computer monitor.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;People are very interesting. Just as they can breathe without being taught how to breathe, the children are seeking communication even in a closed environment. While it generated emotions, it decreased the rate at which their abilities improved. Nevertheless, the curriculum&apos;s difficulty was slightly increased, and starting from the fifth generation, they achieved abilities superior to the third-generation students.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left"><em>Punishment should be executed, and emotions are only to be kept at a minimum</em>&#x2014;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Suzukake still hadn&apos;t changed this way of thinking.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;The curriculum was prepared in ten different levels; in the end, the fourth level of difficulty was applied to the fifth generation and the fifth level to the sixth generation. Eventually, these children will become the ideal adults.
