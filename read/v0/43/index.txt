Naoe-sensei wasn&apos;t raising you to be at the top. He hoped that when his son becomes the country&apos;s leader, you would support him as his right-hand man. Naoe-sensei&apos;s only miscalculation, despite being someone who could see through anything, was your boundless ambition&#x2014;he failed to perceive it.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">In another ten years, even Naoe wouldn&apos;t be able to completely crush me.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">He took action to prevent that in advance.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Whether&#xa0;it was a parting gift to his son or a bomb for me, who might threaten his son.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Are you satisfied with the answer?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Why are you speaking so openly?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;If you were the type of person who would meet their downfall here, I wouldn&apos;t have spoken.
