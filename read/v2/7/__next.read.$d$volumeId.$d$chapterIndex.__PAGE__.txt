<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 1 - Prologue:<br/>Sakura Airi’s<br/>Soliloquy</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> don’t know how to interact with other people. I’m bad at talking while making eye contact. I’m horrible with crowds. I don’t remember how or when I became so hopeless.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, one thing I do know for certain is that a person cannot live entirely in isolation. No matter how much I may love my solitude, I can’t remain totally by myself. So, I came up with a solution. I would adopt a false face and hide my true self. Then, I wouldn’t be completely honest, but I could be a version of myself. I could continue to live in this dark, lonely world.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The world isn’t entirely beautiful. Everyone knows this, but in their hearts they still wish for that perfect, idyllic place. A bit of a contradiction.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I don’t care who answers me, but I need to know. Is&#160;everyone else putting on a façade, just like me? Or do most people show their true selves to the outside world? Since I can’t connect with people, I suppose there’s no way for me to find out the answer. Therefore, I remain alone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’m all right by myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’m all right with being alone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I want to connect with someone from the bottom of my heart.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And so I will continue to live quietly, with my eyes downcast. Alone.</span></p>
