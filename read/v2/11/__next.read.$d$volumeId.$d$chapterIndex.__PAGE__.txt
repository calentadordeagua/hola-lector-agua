<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 5:<br/>Each and Every<br/>Prediction</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">t was just one day until the meeting between Sudou and Class C. With Horikita’s cooperation and Sakura’s testimony, as well as Kushida and Hirata’s actions, our entire class felt spirited and courageous. You could say that we were united. However, it was obvious that we lacked firm, irrefutable evidence, and it would still be difficult to prove Sudou’s innocence. Our deliberation would decide the outcome.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Man, it really is hot today…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I never thought about global warming more than when I exited a building with working air conditioning. Considering I’d likely suffer every day until August, my spirits remained low. The moment I left my dorm’s lobby, hot and humid air assaulted me. While I endured the pain of my burning skin, I walked the path to school lined with green, leafy trees.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Something was different today, though. There was something on the bulletin board by the stairway’s landing, a little in front of the shoe cubbies. A paper on the board said that they were looking for students with information related to Sudou and Class C.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Clearly, somebody was trying to help. It was sincerely appreciated, because we hadn’t even considered taking such measures ourselves. This mystery person was action-oriented. Furthermore, while the sign itself might have appeared a weak effort, the author also wrote that they’d be willing to give points to helpful informants. In that case, even apathetic students would pay attention.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I scanned the message, I was quite impressed…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good morning, Ayanokouji-kun!” Ichinose called out from behind me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She must have just arrived.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I saw the paper on the bulletin board. Did you post it, Ichinose?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose joined me in looking at the board. She appeared deeply interested.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm. I see, I see. So there’s this method, too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? This wasn’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d thought that surely this was her strategy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This was probably— Ah, he’s here! Good morning, Kanzaki-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose raised her hand up and signaled to a lone male student. The boy noticed Ichinose, and approached us with quiet steps.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you put this up, Kanzaki-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. I made and posted it on Friday. Is there something wrong?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, no. Not at all. My friend here just wanted to know who did it. Ah, I’ll introduce you two. Kanzaki-kun from Class B, this is Ayanokouji-kun from Class D.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nice to meet you, Kanzaki.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">His demeanor was stiff, but he appeared to be a serious student. He was tall and slender. A pretty boy, but in a different way from Hirata. I took his outstretched hand.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How’s it going, Kanzaki-kun? Did you get anything reliable?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, I haven’t received any useful information.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Well, how about we look at the bulletin board.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At the bulletin board? Did you put up another poster?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose wore a thin smile, indicating it was something else.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Have you ever checked out the school homepage? There’s a message board. I requested that people come forward with information there. I said that if there anyone witnessed a violent incident at our school, I’d like to hear about it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After she said that, Ichinose showed us her phone’s screen. It showed a message seeking out witnesses, as well as a count for the number of people who’d viewed it. The displayed number was still only in the dozens, but it was much more efficient than asking people directly. Also, the message posted to the homepage offered compensation to witnesses and people with helpful information.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, regarding the stuff about points, don’t worry. We just decided to do that on our own. Besides, it will probably be difficult for us to get new information now. Ah!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s the matter?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It looks like I just got two messages regarding the post. There might be a little information.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose checked her phone for confirmation. After she’d read the messages, a slight smile crept onto her face. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She showed me her phone so I could read the message for myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It looks like one of the boys from Class C, Ishizaki-kun, was a bad seed in junior high. He was good at fighting, and terrorized the locals. A kid from his hometown probably leaked this.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Interesting,” Kanzaki muttered, also reading the message.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Like Kanzaki, I found the information quite interesting. Everyone had assumed that the three students Sudou fought were normal kids. However, if they were troublemakers too, then that was a different story. As for the other two, being basketball players meant they probably had good motor skills. Yet Sudou had turned the tables, and beat them all without once being hit himself. I couldn’t help but feel there was something unnatural about that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kanzaki-kun, what do you think?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Perhaps they let Sudou beat them up on purpose. If the three of them had wanted to set a trap for Sudou, the story would make perfect sense. The connection seems natural.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I think so too. I knew you’d figure it out, Kanzaki-kun. Great work. If we can verify this information, then we could be one step closer to proving Sudou-kun’s innocence. But what we have is still pretty weak, isn’t it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. Even if we managed to convince people with this new evidence, we’re really only halfway there. The fact that it was such a one-sided fight definitely puts a lot of pressure on us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou probably wouldn’t want to shoulder some of the guilt alongside the others. Neither party wanted to be liable for this situation. If we got testimony from Class D, though, our chances would probably be 6 to 4, or maybe 7 to 3.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, we can’t say anything yet.” I concealed Sakura’s name since we were still negotiating.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Is there some reason why?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since the whole Sakura situation was rather delicate, I&#160;avoided explaining in detail. After all, she might decide to quit after all, even on the day of the decision. I wanted to have an escape route.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’ve been no reports of another witness, just as I thought. It would’ve been interesting if one had stepped forward, but I suppose it was tough. We’re out of time. Our only choice now is to wait for more information from either the Internet or the bulletin board, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is it okay to wait that long, though? I mean, those Class C guys might zero in on us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’ll be okay. Besides, both Class C and Class A originally targeted us, anyway.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ichinose is right. Besides, even if you wanted to stick by the rules, everyone is acting out of bounds. I think it’s okay to ask for forgiveness this time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose and Kanzaki made it pretty clear that they wanted to be fair and square when competing against the school and the other students. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Anyway, we’ll have to transfer points over to anyone who provides us with information. Ah, but what if someone does so anonymously? In that case, how would we transfer over the points?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We can tell you, if you’d like. Do you want me to?” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you know something, Ayanokouji-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I just remembered something when I was messing around on my phone. Do you know the person’s number?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s a toll-free number, but I remember it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose drew close and pointed at her phone. Being this near to somebody usually made people feel defenseless. I’d thought that a girl wouldn’t want a man to be in her intimate space…and I didn’t know exactly what it was, but Ichinose had a pleasant scent about her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Here, open up the points remittance screen. You should see your </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ID</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> number in the upper left corner.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I instructed her, my heart rate began to skyrocket.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Umm…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose’s fingers were nimble. She pressed the button to open up her own points page. After the page loaded, it was displayed on the screen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yep, yep. There it is. So what should I do now that I can see the </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ID</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> number?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“From your </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ID</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> number, you can issue a temporary token key. If you open that, and send the key, you should get a request for payment.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Thank you!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. Let’s go, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose started walking.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…………”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just then, for a split second, I had seen something on Ichinose’s phone. The fragment of screen I’d noticed had burned itself into my mind and wouldn’t go away. What should I do? Was what I’d seen even possible? Ichinose might prove a huge obstacle for Horikita in her quest to reach Class A.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“G</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">ood morning!</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ayanokouji-kun!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“O-oh, hey. Good morning.” Kushida greeted me looking incredibly bright and energetic. I was taken aback by her radiance.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thanks for yesterday. You really saved me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, I suppose her dazzling face made me happy, but something else was bothering me that I couldn’t remember. I’d gone on an outing for the first time, and it had been with girls like Kushida and Sakura. Ah, it was almost too much. Well, I guess for the time being, things were great…until Ike and Yamauchi got to school, that was. If they heard about this, they’d definitely hold a serious grudge over it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s hang out again sometime, okay?” Kushida said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“S-sure.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if she’d only said it to be polite, my heart thumped a little faster. Well, that wasn’t a bad thing.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you spend your day off with Kushida-san?” an icy voice asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I did,” I answered quietly. “Kushida wanted Sakura’s cooperation, so she asked for my help. I didn’t have much of a choice.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Was there something wrong with…that?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I looked over at my neighbor, and saw an expression on Horikita’s face that I’d never glimpsed before.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what’s the matter?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, you just had this really weird look on your face.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really? I didn’t intend to make any kind of face. I should look the same as I always do. However, I will say that I admire how freely you’ve been moving. When I ask you for help, you’re often reluctant, but when Kushida-san asks you, you accept readily. I was calmly and discretely analyzing the difference between us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She did not remotely look calm and discreet. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just then, someone lightly tapped my shoulder, and told me to go over and see Kushida. Horikita wore a tremendously puzzled expression as I went over to the hallway, where Kushida briefly peeked into the classroom.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have a feeling I just saw something really incredible!” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Did Kushida understand the meaning behind Horikita’s expression? She seemed both delighted and surprised.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Something incredible? That’s creepy… I think that Horikita was a little angry.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not it. I think she feels alienated and lonely over not being invited.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She probably doesn’t know how she feels… I’m sure she’s probably noticed how much fun it is to spend time with friends and talk to them, though. That’s a good thing, a good thing.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What a bizarre thought. Horikita didn’t have a good opinion of Kushida. Even so, it was strange for Kushida to say that Horikita felt alienated over not being invited.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Perhaps you’re realizing something fundamental, Ayanokouji-kun. Horikita-san is upset that you didn’t invite her to come along.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No, no, that couldn’t be it… I mean, Horikita was a girl who loved solitude, after all. She wasn’t supposed to enjoy going out, certainly not with a guy like me. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In that moment, I had come to a rather baffling realization. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">fter homeroom finished for the day, we asked Chabashira-sensei to see us in the faculty room. We did this out of consideration for Sakura. Because I hadn’t been able to discuss it with her fully over the phone yesterday, I waited towards the back of the room for an opportune moment. Kushida was probably going to be able to tell Chabashira-sensei everything that had happened.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A witness? For Sudou’s case?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. Sakura-san saw everything from start to finish.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kushida called for Sakura, who stood quietly behind her. She stepped forward, looking slightly nervous.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So according to Kushida, you saw the fight between Sudou and the others.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I saw it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It’s not that our teacher didn’t believe us, but I felt ill at ease before her skeptical gaze. Sakura, true to her word, slowly gave us the truth. This was the first time that we had heard the full story. None of us, not even the teacher, spoke a single word or even moved until the very end.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand what you’re said. However, I can’t simply accept what you’re telling me,” Chabashira-sensei said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I would have thought that, as Class D’s homeroom teacher, she would’ve been delighted to discover a witness from Class D. Kushida, betrayed by this turn of events, was flustered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-What do you mean, sensei?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sakura, why are you testifying now? When I reported the issue during homeroom, you didn’t step forward. It’s not as though you were absent that day, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well… That’s… I’m just not good at talking with other people…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re not good at talking with other people, and yet you’ve decided to testify now? Isn’t that strange?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira-sensei started hounding Sakura, as was typical. If Sakura had stepped forward back when she’d first called for witnesses, though, I wondered if Chabashira-sensei would honestly have welcomed it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sensei, Sakura-san is…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m speaking to Sakura right now.” Chabashira-sensei cut Kushida off sharply.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Umm… Well, it’s because our…class is in trouble now, and…I thought if…if I testify, I can help…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura hunched and shrank away, much like a frog cornered by a snake. As our teacher, Chabashira-sensei should have understood what kind of girl Sakura was. She should have realized that merely by speaking up, Sakura had made great progress.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. So, then you mustered up the courage to come forward?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes…”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you have any plans for tonight? For right now?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Right now? No, I don’t have anything planned. Or rather, I didn’t make any plans.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hmm. Even I felt a little sad when I heard someone say that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, why not go out with me for a while? If it’s not a bother, of course.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I decided to be daring and invite Sakura. Then, without any hesitation, she shot up out of her seat.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?!” As she leapt up, she banged her knees against the table and doubled over in agony. Her glasses flew off her face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That looked like it really hurt just now. Are you okay?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I…I’m perfectly fine!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She wasn’t very convincing; the pain was so intense that she was on the verge of tears. I picked up her glasses. Just as I’d thought, there were no lenses. I handed her glasses back. Her hands trembled when she took them, and she thanked me. Sakura wrestled with her pain for about a minute before she finally calmed down and quieted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-where do you want to go?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was on her guard, but I didn’t understand why. Maybe she believed I was some kind of pick-up artist trying to smooth talk her. If that were the case, it was bad.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura responded cautiously, as if worried about what to say. “If you don’t mind…there is someplace I’d like to go. Is that all right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Yeah, sure, I don’t mind. Please lead the way.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t really care about the location; I just wanted to get a change of scenery and talk. If Sakura had a place she preferred, then everything would be going according to plan.</span></p>

I’m so pathetic.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If you were good at making conversation with other people, you’d have an easier time of it. I was once again impressed by Horikita’s ability to make fun of Ike and Yamauchi while also dealing naturally with complete strangers. That was a splendid talent.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t intend to give her empty encouragement like “Do your best.” Sakura should face tomorrow entirely as herself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For Sudou. For Kushida. For your classmates. Throw all of those thoughts away.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Throw them…all away?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When you testify tomorrow, speak for yourself. As someone who tells the truth of what she saw, as a witness.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was good for a self-reliant person to try doing something for other people. However, Sakura still couldn’t properly take care of herself. She had a tendency to wrap herself up and endure pain, sorrow, and suffering alone. If you weren’t happy yourself, then you couldn’t make others happy, either.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tell the truth for your own sake. Do that, and Sudou will be saved. That’s enough.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t know how effective my advice would be. It probably was meaningless blather, actually. But perhaps it was right to encourage Sakura to speak for herself. Maybe I did it because I understood how it felt to be wanted. Because I needed someone to know I understood the pain and anguish of battling loneliness.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hopefully, my words had found purchase somewhere in Sakura’s heart.</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">5.6</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">hat night, under Kushida’s orders, everyone except for Sudou gathered in my room. Apparently Kushida had even invited Horikita, but it seemed she didn’t want to join us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So. Has there been any progress, Kushida-chan?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">has</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> been progress, yes, but I’ve also noticed something incredible. Ayanokouji-kun, can I borrow your computer for a minute?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure,” I answered with a nod. Kushida went to my desktop computer, booted it up, and opened the Internet browser.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. Have a look at this!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kushida accessed what appeared to be somebody’s blog. It was rather elaborate, too. Unlike some normal person’s website, it had the sheen and polish of a fully-fledged business.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, is that a picture of Shizuku?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Shizuku?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She’s a gravure idol. She was just featured in a young men’s magazine.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There were many pictures of her. I certainly couldn’t complain about her looks or proportions.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you recognize her?” Kushida asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Am I supposed to recognize her?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Look closely.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kushida clicked on a picture of Shizuku’s face. Ike took a long hard look at her, and then…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She’s cute.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, not that! This is Sakura-san, isn’t it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kushida-chan, who are you talking about?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sakura-san, from our class.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? No way, no way. Sakura-san? No, no, no, there’s no way that’s true.” Ike laughed, but Yamauchi’s expression stiffened.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Ike… You know, when I actually get a good look at her, I…think she probably does look a little like Sakura.”</span></p>

After such an undignified reaction, I imagined Kushida would want to pull away from him. Although she was kind to an almost reckless degree, I couldn’t sense that kind of acceptance from her now.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She lived a double life as an idol and a quiet, inconspicuous student at our school. Why had she wanted to create another life? It was like a coin with two very different sides. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh yeah. So, about Sakura-san?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was surprised when I found out that she was an idol, but I also kind of understood it. I wonder if that’s her real personality?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though I’d avoided just stating this idea outright, I also thought that Sakura had a hidden side, just like Kushida. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. I think with the right pretenses in place, Sakura-san could even make herself smile in front of people.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, if things with Sudou turn out okay, could you tell me then?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, that would be fine.”</span></p>

I&#160;couldn’t tell whether Kushida’s smile was genuine or bitter as she whispered in my ear. The only thing that I knew for certain was that Kushida was no angel. <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">5.7</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">fter everyone left, I sat in front of my computer and looked at Sakura Airi’s – I mean, the gravure idol Shizuku’s – blog. Her hopes and aspirations for the future were spelled out in writing. I didn’t see anything that especially stuck out, no red flags. I checked out other idol blogs just for reference, but they seemed similar.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had to wonder, how would it have felt for a second-year junior high student to debut in the entertainment world? <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She had strictly adhered to the rule about contacting anyone outside the school. Among those many comments, a post from three months ago snagged my attention.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you believe in fate? I do. I believe we will be together forever.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“I can always feel you close to me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“You were even cuter today, huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you notice when our eyes met? I noticed.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If Sakura saw this, those words would probably frighten her. It was almost as if the poster wanted to be physically close to Shizuku so he could whisper these words into her ear. Were they just delusions? Within this heavily restricted school, only a very limited number of people could encounter Sakura. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Students, teachers…or anyone who did business with the school. I flashed on a memory of the man who worked at the campus electronic store. Then, a post from last Sunday caused my hair to stand on end. I had a crushing realization.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Look, God is real after all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura had bought a digital camera after she enrolled in school. Of course, she’d probably disguised herself that day, just like any celebrity would. But while a disguise like that would make sense for her to use with fans, that clerk had recognized who Sakura was. Of course, there were only a few ways that they could have contact at that point.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, after Sakura’s camera broke, he saw her. Since she loved it so, she had to fix it. Given our circumstances it was nearly impossible for someone in Class D to buy a new one. However, because she took it in for repair, there was the possibility that she’d encounter the store clerk.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She’d been hesitant to go get her camera repaired because of the clerk. The clerk, on the other hand, had been in incredibly high spirits. After all, here was his chance to get his favorite idol’s real name and phone number on the form she had to fill out. It also might explain why she’d called me that evening and asked some rather significant questions. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I thought about it, the answer seemed obvious. I combed through the comments, searching for more he might have written.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s so mean for you to ignore me! Or perhaps you just didn’t notice me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you doing now? I want to meet you, I want to meet you, I want to meet you!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Frightening comments were posted one after another. Of course, other fans had simply been disgusted by these comments, but it was different for Sakura. I wondered if knowing that he was so close pushed her to the point of almost unimaginable terror? But Sakura had concealed that from us, and now she was desperately trying to fight Class C for us as a witness. She was probably hesitant to leave her dorm, considering how much this man’s existence terrified her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If they were on the same campus, her fear wasn’t surprising. However, there was hardly anything we could do, no plan we could implement to solve the issue of this stalker by tomorrow. In the end, the only option was to wait on an </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">SOS</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> from the girl in question.</span></p>
