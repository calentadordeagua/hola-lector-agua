<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 6:<br/>Truth and Lies</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">he day our fate would be decided was here. Before anything else, I wanted to confirm that Sakura was coming to school. When I entered the classroom, I saw the same thing as always. Sakura sat quietly alone, so as not to get roped into other students’ conversations. Her expression seemed even gloomier than usual. But in any case, she’d still come to school.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you okay?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, yeah. I’m fine.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered if she was nervous. She seemed composed, if not completely calm.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I thought that things would be tough if I were absent today, so…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She understood that the entire class would be upset if she were absent, so she’d made the painful decision to come to school. I imagined it would be impossible to tell her not to think about Sudou and the others.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t forget what I said yesterday. Testify for your own sake, more than anyone else’s.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. I’ll be okay.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike and Yamauchi looked over at Sakura with great curiosity. Of course, that was because they now knew of her idol identity. Sakura was probably sensitive enough to notice this. She looked like she’d guessed that Ike and Yamauchi had discovered her identity. Oh no… </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But Sakura just wore a thin smile and quietly mouthed the words, “It’s all right.” She was aware that we knew of her double life. Maybe working as an idol had made her sensitive to subtle changes in a room.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.1</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">s the bell rang for the end of class, Horikita and I stood up. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Have you prepared for this, Sudou-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah… I’m good. I was born ready.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As if mentally preparing himself for what lay ahead, Sudou closed his eyes and folded his arms. But then he slowly opened his eyes again.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You might call me a complete idiot and make fun of me, but I’m me. If you have something you want to say, say it now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t do anything selfish. Actually listening right now would be the clever thing to do, yes?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh, you always act so high and mighty, lady.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When you saw them like this, it did seem as though they fought like cats and dogs. But at the very least, Sudou didn’t hate Horikita. If he did he would’ve absolutely refused her help, no matter how advantageous her offer. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do your best, Horikita-san. Sudou-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita didn’t respond at all, but Sudou pumped his fist to show resolve. I turned to check on Sakura who was still sitting down, her body rigid. She stood, her lips trembling slightly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah… I’m okay. Thank you…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura was far tenser than I’d expected. If she was in this psychological state before the meeting had even begun, she might not be able to speak satisfactorily. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s go. We’ll make a bad impression if we’re late.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The discussion was scheduled to begin at 4:00 </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">PM</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. It was already 3:50 </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">PM</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. We couldn’t afford to take it slow. When the four of us got to the faculty room, a teacher waved at us to come on inside.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yahoo! Hello, Class D students!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The homeroom teacher for Class B, Hoshinomiya-sensei, gave us this happy-go-lucky greeting. “It seems like something rather incredible has happened, hmm?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her eyes sparkled, as if she enjoyed sticking her nose into other people’s business. (Well, she did).</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you doing this time?” Chabashira-sensei muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no. I’ve been found out already, huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira-sensei glared over at Hoshinomiya-sensei as she exited the faculty room. “Whenever you sneak out, that’s around the time I start to feel suspicious.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hoshinomiya gave a cute wink, as if saying, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Teehee, you got me! </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So I guess I can’t join in, huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course you can’t. You know that outsiders cannot participate.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Aw, that’s too bad. Well, that’s all right. The results should be out within the hour, I guess.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira-sensei forcefully pushed Hoshinomiya-sensei back into the faculty room.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, shall we be going?” she asked us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’re not going to do this in the faculty room, are we?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course not. This school does have rather complicated rules, but in cases such as these a settlement is reached between the homeroom teacher of the class in question, the concerned parties, and the student council.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita froze the very moment she heard the words “student council.” Chabashira-sensei turned and glanced sharply at Horikita’s face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you want to stop, now’s the time, Horikita.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou, who didn’t understand why Horikita would react that way, looked confused. It was almost as if a giant question mark was floating above his head. Our teacher, as usual, had revealed an important detail right at the last minute.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll go. I’m fine.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita quickly glanced at me. Her look probably meant something like, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Don’t worry on my account.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> We left the first-floor faculty room and walked up three floors to the fourth. A placard that read “Student Council Room” was affixed to the wall near the doorway. Chabashira-sensei knocked, and we went inside.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though Horikita had winced, she immediately followed us. Inside, long tables had been arranged in rectangular formation. The three students from Class C had already arrived and were seated. Beside them sat a bespectacled male teacher in his 30s. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry we’re late,” our sensei said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s before the scheduled start time. There’s no need for apologies.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Have you already met?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou, Horikita, and I did not know the teacher.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is Sakagami-sensei, the homeroom teacher for Class C. Now then.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One lone male student seated in the back of the room attracted everyone’s attention.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is the student council president.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita’s older brother, without so much as even a glance at his sister, pored over the documents on his desk. Horikita directed her gaze at her brother for a short time, but when she realized that she wasn’t his focus, she lowered her eyes and sat down in front of the Class C students.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, I would now like to discuss the violent incident that occurred last Tuesday with the members of the student council, the involved parties, and their homeroom teachers. You may begin the proceedings, student council secretary Tachibana.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Secretary Tachibana, a woman with short hair, gave a slight bow.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course, considering the magnitude of this dispute, there are times when the student council president will take over. There are several unusual things about this incident. Other than that, the bulk of the proceedings will be handled solely by Tachibana, as usual.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Because I am quite busy, there are certain agenda topics upon which I will defer. As a general rule, though, I would prefer to attend to these issues, as I’m entrusted to lead this student council.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, this is all by chance?” Chabashira-sensei smiled as she said that, but Horikita’s older brother never wavered. On the contrary though, Horikita—Horikita the younger sister, I mean—couldn’t hide her trembling. Considering they were brother and sister, the odds were not in our favor. In fact, I couldn’t help but think that this situation was extremely disadvantageous, as Horikita couldn’t demonstrate her usual prowess here. Our expectations had been thoroughly shattered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If the student council president acted, there’d be nothing we could do, even if we didn’t like it. He’d enrolled in Class A and immediately taken office as secretary for the student council. In December of his first year, he became the student council president after receiving an overwhelming amount of support in the election. Although some senior students had naturally voiced their displeasure, our current, hopeless situation spoke to his incredible abilities. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Secretary Tachibana summarized the situation from both sides in an easy-to-understand manner. There wasn’t any need for further explanation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Based on the aforementioned facts, we would like for you to identify which version of events is true.” After completing her explanation and the preface to the proceedings, Secretary Tachibana turned her eyes toward us in Class D. “Komiya-kun and two other members of the basketball club went to the special building after Sudou-kun called them there. There, they claim to have been beaten up in a one-sided brawl. Is this true?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What those guys said was a lie. I was the one that got called over to the special building,” Sudou said. “That day, after practice, Komiya and Kondou asked me to go to the special building. Honestly, I thought it was kind of annoying, but I also thought it might’ve been because they were always hostile towards me. So, I went to meet them.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou wasn’t one to mince words. Normally, Horikita would have been disgusted by his casual way of speaking, but judging from her trembling she didn’t hear him at all. Sakagami-sensei, Class C’s homeroom teacher, stared in wide-eyed amazement.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s a lie. Sudou-kun called </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">us</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> over to the special building.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t screw with me, Komiya! You were the one who called me, you jerk!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t seem to remember your position here.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou, irritated, impulsively struck his desk. Immediate silence followed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please calm down, Sudou-kun. Right now, we’re merely listening to what both parties have to say. Komiya-kun, we ask that you too please show some restraint, and not interrupt.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Puh, fine…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Both parties insist that the other called them over, so the accounts conflict. However, the stories share some commonality. There </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">was</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> a dispute between Sudou-kun, Komiya-kun, and Kondou-kun, correct?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wouldn’t call it a dispute. Sudou-kun’s always picking fights with us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“‘Picking fights’?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sudou’s better at basketball than we are, so he’s always bragging about it. We practice with everything we’ve got, but it doesn’t exactly feel good when he makes fools out of us. So we often butt heads.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t really know the details of Sudou’s club activities, but when I saw the veins popping on his forehead, it was rather obvious that they were lying. Next, Secretary Tachibana spoke to Sudou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not one bit of what Komiya said was true. Those guys are just jealous of my talent. When I practice, they constantly get in my way. That’s the truth.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Naturally, both sides claimed that the other party was at fault.</span></p>

Did her brother’s mere presence truly have this much of an effect?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I flashed back to those two talking behind the dormitory. Even Sudou, Horikita, and I felt like this was impossible for Sakura, and mentally resigned herself.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In other words, the compromise had been a trap. A sweet trap set to lure us to our defeat. The two of them didn’t seem convinced, though. Kanzaki in particular insisted that we’d made the wrong choice.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The fact remains that he hit the other students. Your opponents made a concession because of corroboration from the witness and her evidence. You should’ve accepted the compromise.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But like Ayanokouji-kun says, Sudou’s suspension would be a loss for Class D. If Sudou were suspended due to his bad behavior, then his chances of being a team regular would probably vanish. He’d be back to square one.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He might not just be sent back to square one. It might be worse, actually. If the school knew both sides shared the responsibility, they’d take that into account when assigning punishments. However, if Sudou’s share of the blame increases tomorrow, it’ll be bad news.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Neither of them were wrong. Either we appealed for his innocence, or we accepted the deal. One of those was the correct answer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. I think so, too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you think so, shouldn’t you have stopped it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you’re brought back into a retrial, you’ll inevitably lose. Just like Kanzaki says, obtaining a not-guilty verdict is virtually impossible.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No matter our testimony, no matter how passionately we made our claims, we couldn’t win on that point. It wasn’t about just winning or losing anymore. We’d come to an impasse on the battlefield.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you still going to fight? Even without new evidence or testimony?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Our leader gave us her decision. We’ll fight to the bitter end.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita wasn’t stupid. She already knew well enough that this extension wasn’t a victory. Yet she’d still made the choice to press forward, intending to fight on. That Class D was prepared to face the difficulties ahead was proof of our readiness.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm. Well, I don’t think we’ll be able to obtain any more leads, but I’ll go check what information I can gather from the Internet.” Though it wouldn’t have been odd for her to wash her hands off us at this point, Ichinose laughed and still offered her cooperation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll do my absolute best to look for more evidence or another witness.” Even though Kanzaki would have opted for compromise, his cooperation also remained unwavering.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re still willing to help us?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’re in too deep now. Besides, it’s like we said before. We can’t forgive liars.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki nodded. These were really good people.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I sincerely appreciate the offer, but it’s not necessary.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita, who I thought had returned to the dorms, suddenly stood alongside us. Had she been waiting for me?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not necessary? What do you mean, Horikita-san?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We cannot get Sudou acquitted. Even if a new witness were to come from Class A or B, it would be impossible. However…there is something I would like you to prepare for us. It is the only possible solution.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Preparing something?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita proceeded to explain to us what she wanted. The previously calm Ichinose’s expression now stiffened.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh… That’s going to be a </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">really</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> hard request.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If Ichinose was this hesitant, perhaps it truly was unreasonable. Kanzaki fell silent and appeared deep in thought.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand that I’m in no position to ask this,” Horikita said. “The burden I’d be placing upon you is exceptionally great. But—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, no. Well, this should be within the range of what we can do, I think. That’s because I plan to figure out what’s happening to Class D. I’ve got tons and tons of things I want to know, but… Well, maybe it would be better for you not to tell us why?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re certainly right about that. Well then, if I can convince you, will you cooperate with us?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita continued to explain the details of her solution to Ichinose, Kanzaki, and me. Why was it necessary? What would we use it for? What was its purpose? After Horikita had finished, Kanzaki and Ichinose stood lost in silent thought.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You should understand the risks as well as the usefulness of this strategy,” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When did you come up with this?” Ichinose asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just before the deliberation ended. Only by chance, though.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That…an incredible move. I went to the scene of the crime and even I didn’t think of that. Or, I guess I should say, I was completely in the dark about it. It wasn’t even close to what I could imagine.” Ichinose appeared to understand the plan and its intended effect. However, her expression was still distant, and she still seemed to be thinking.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“An unusual idea. You can probably anticipate the results, too. But does such a thing even exist?” she asked Kanzaki, who seemed a little shocked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It might be contrary to your ethics and morals, Ichinose.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ha ha, yeah. You might be right. It’s a change for me. But…it’s certainly one way of doing things.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. That’s what I thought as well. It’s something that never should have been done.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Were they going to lend us a hand? There were lies baked into this strategy. For someone like Ichinose, who hated lying, it was a harsh request.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, since a lie started all this trouble, maybe it’ll take another lie to close the books on this incident. That’s what I think, anyway.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Mmmhmm, I see. An eye for an eye, a lie for a lie, yeah? But is that even possible, I wonder? I can’t imagine such a thing would be easy to obtain.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry about that part. I’ve confirmed it,” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Had she left the student council room immediately to ascertain whether or not it was possible to collect the proof she needed?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you ask the Professor to assist us, it should be good. I’ll talk to him.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita gave a slight nod. She apparently didn’t have any objections.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Kanzaki-kun. Did you help us so that we would outpace Class C?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. That’s right.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But I was just thinking, perhaps what we’re doing right now might end up coming back to bite you in the ass later?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That could happen.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Man. I completely neglected to calculate the fact that Class D has a girl like you.” Ichinose, after complimenting Horikita, took out her cell phone with a look of slight amazement. “I’ll lend this to you. Please return it later.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that, she affirmed her willingness to help.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure. I promise.” Horikita, grateful for the assistance, accepted it without any hesitation. “Well then, Ayanokouji-kun. There’s something I’d like you to help me with.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If it’s not something really bothersome, sure. I’ll help.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Helping others is fundamentally bothersome and time-consuming.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In other words, I needed to prepare myself. I didn’t see a way out of this, so I hesitantly decided to yield to Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, let’s g—?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I received a shocking blow to my side. The pain was sudden and intense. I tumbled into the corner as if blown by a stiff breeze.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll forgive you for touching me this time. However, next time I’ll pay you back double.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wha— Ah, ah!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The pain stole my voice, as if I weren’t allowed to argue. Wait, when she said she’d pay me back “double,” she meant her blows would be twice as hard as now? That was unimaginable! </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Dumbfounded, Ichinose watched the whole spectacle. She looked at Horikita as if the girl were something terrifying. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Remember that well, Ichinose.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Horikita was a woman without mercy… </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Gulp.</span></p>
