<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 5:<br/>Battle of the First-Years and Third-Years</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T HAD BEEN ALMOST</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> three months since the new students first started here at the Advanced Nurturing High School, and they had begun to understand the state of things at this institution. The first-year students were also right in the midst of forming groups for big upcoming special exam. However, it wasn’t long after the school gave the students permission to start forming groups that things already started to not go so smoothly. The students in Class 1-D, led by Housen Kazuomi, stubbornly refused to join any groups and rejected offers to trade cards. If a student from another class came to them wanting them to be in a group, the Class D students demanded points in exchange, or otherwise they wouldn’t join.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Because of that, the new students were put into a situation where they couldn’t freely form groups. Although representatives from each of the other three classes had been hoping that Housen would have changed his mind sometime in June, even today, on the first of July, the situation still hadn’t changed. There were classes within their grade level who tried to ignore Class D, but Yagami Takuya from Class 1-B put a stop to them. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While it was easy for the other students to ignore Class D and to create groups just from their own three classes, competition with the other grade levels was an important part of this special exam. Yagami had voiced the need that, in the event that issue was to be prioritized, they would absolutely need to handpick the best of the best from each and every class to make optimal groups. At about the exact same time, with the support of students who shared Yagami’s views, the three other classes had agreed to take a wait-and-see approach until&#160;July.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, Housen’s continued deliberate ignoring of the situation meant that those previous talks wound up becoming meaningless. And today, now that the deadline had arrived, the representatives of the four classes from their grade were supposed to gather to resolve the situation. Yagami had proposed that the gathering be done in a simple manner so as not to intentionally raise the level of confidentiality of the meeting. It was agreed that the leader of the class or someone close to the leader would attend. But as the time came after class ended that day, there was no response from Class D, the class that was at the center of all these issues.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In a hallway lined with rather unsurprising, nondescript first-year classrooms, the first person to show up to the meeting was Yagami from Class 1-B. That was because he thought it necessary that he show up before anyone else as the original advocate of the proposal. Not long afterward, Utomiya Riku from Class 1-C had shown up as well.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems like it’s still just you here, Yagami.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, hey Utomiya-kun. I was kind of expecting it would’ve been you participating in this talk,” replied Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Although it’s not really like me to be a leader or anything, I came because no one else wanted to. While the kids in my class kinda like to go ahead and say whatever they want, they seem to not like these kinds of headache-inducing things,” said Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think it’s because they know that you’re such a dependable person that they asked you to come. I saw the updated OAA values this month. It says that your Societal Contribution score got bumped up to a B, didn’t it?” said Yagami, with a bright and chipper smile.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya, despite the fact he was being complimented, responded by wrinkling his brow. The person he was talking with, Yagami, had a C in Physical Ability, but an A in Academic Ability. In addition to that, his Adaptability and Societal Contribution scores had gone up to A thanks to his repeated contributions to Class B. In terms of overall ability, he was certainly a cut above. More importantly though, Class C wasn’t exactly in a situation that they could be happy about. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ve lost somebody. To be honest, I think it’s a pretty big loss,” said Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I didn’t think that Hatano-kun would’ve gotten expelled either. It is really unfortunate,” said Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Yeah.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hatano was a male student from Class 1-C. He was a valuable student too, with an A in Academic Ability. However, he had been dealt a fatal blow as a result of him being involved in actions that, once discovered, resulted in a penalty of immediate expulsion. The somewhat relaxed first-year students were once again reminded of this school’s harshness. Though at this point, it had already been a month since Hatano had been expelled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya, his classmate, didn’t even have the time to regret his loss, unfortunately. Now that he had lost one of his best students, he needed to get definite results on the upcoming special exam.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seemed like you and Hatano were pretty close,” said Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He said he wanted to join the student council together. He wanted to make the school a more fun place to be,” replied Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya nodded lightly and then directed his gaze towards the Class 1-D classroom.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you think that Housen’s gonna come?” asked Utomiya, wondering about the very person about which this discussion was taking place over.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess I’d say it’s about a fifty-fifty chance,” replied Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Fifty-fifty? Wow, you sure put a lot of faith in Housen, huh. I’m betting he won’t show,” said Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If he doesn’t show up to this meeting, then the matter is settled. We’ll go ahead and form groups with students from our three classes. If that happens, then Class D and Class D alone, which has been trying to push for getting a lot of points out of us, will be left out in the cold. Then any chance they have to win will disappear,” said Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If he thinks that he can make us fork over our Private Points, then he really has a massive ego. He objected to the idea of trying to smoothly form groups with people from all classes together. Since the second- and third-years are our enemies here, we really ought to work together. Yet Housen still refused anyway,” said Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though they were all in the same grade level, Housen wanted to compete in areas where they didn’t even have to. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seems that way, at least on the surface. But I can’t imagine that’s what Housen-kun actually planning, deep down.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can understand that it’s a bargaining tactic on his part. But it’s one that’s not going to pay off for him.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If he really intends on playing that game though, then I have to wonder if that might actually be a blessing in disguise for us. Because that means that Housen-kun isn’t that much of a threat to us then,” said Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Yeah, you’re right,” replied Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yagami explained that he had arranged this meeting to gauge what Housen was thinking. As the two of them were in the midst of their debate, a third person showed&#160;up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, hey! Riku, Takuya. I figured it’d be you two!” shouted Takahashi Osamu, from Class 1-A, greeting them in a loud voice and waving as he came closer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though he had a low Academic Ability ranking, just a C+, he was particularly good at getting to know people no matter who they were. He was often called up to participate in discussions. He had many friends in other classes and other grade levels too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Osamu-kun, did you come because you were forced into handling things for your class again?” asked Takuya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, the leader of our class is the kind of person who hates dealing with this sort of hassle, y’know? So that’s where I come in,” replied Takahashi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, this discussion is going to move a lot more smoothly now that you’re here, Osamu,” added Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just like in Utomiya’s case, the leader of the class didn’t have to come to this discussion. On the contrary, it would have been more appreciated by the other classes if the students who came for these talks were the ones who excelled in conversation. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All that’s left is Kazuomi, huh?” said Takahashi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was only about three minutes left until the appointed meeting time. If he didn’t show up, the three other speakers would proceed with their meeting without hesitation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t you all think it’d be better if we teamed up right at this stage? Because honestly, I really want to isolate Class D so we can crush them as soon as possible,” said Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ve been told that the exam on the uninhabited island is going to assess more than our academic abilities though. If you’re looking at academic ability alone, then Class D is at the bottom of the rankings, but in terms of physical ability, they’re a close second. It’s possible that they could play a key role in group-building,” said Takuya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Look, I get what you’re tryin’ to say here Riku. My class is feeling pretty darn frustrated with the situation too. But don’t you think it’s a little too early to just give up on Class D? I mean, we can’t say for sure that there aren’t going to be more exams like this in the future where all the classes in our grade have gotta work together, right?” said Takahashi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While Utomiya had advocated for ostracizing Class D, Yagami had continued to maintain that they needed to support Class D. Takahashi, meanwhile, didn’t really take a firm stance on either side, remaining in the middle. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If we just need to work together to tackle this exam, then we’ll be fine if it’s just us our three classes. I’ll admit that yes, there certainly are some capable people in Class D that we could use, but they’re not worth enough to the point where I want to make a request to Housen and try to get in his good graces. Besides, it’s just about time for our meeting to start. I’d like our three classes to start talking about reaching an agreement between ourselves,” said Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just then, almost as if he had anticipated how their conversation was going to go, the person in question appeared, with a relaxed attitude.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“’Fraid that things aren’t gonna work out that way, Riku,” said Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seems like you came after all then, eh, Housen-kun?” replied Yagami, greeting Housen with a smile on his face. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">On the other hand, Housen approached the three of them while bearing his white teeth in an unnerving manner, like he usually did. Utomiya shot a quick glance over at Housen, and then averted his eyes, looking out the window.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey Kazuomi, who showed up at a great time!” exclaimed Takahashi in a friendly manner, without fear of Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Takahashi’s feeling was that he wanted everyone to get along. That’s all that he wanted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t say my name like we’re all buddy-buddy. I’ll kill you,” snapped Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Housen, after trying to intimidate Takahashi, turned his eyes back to Yagami and Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, decided to pay up?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t make lame jokes. We’re not paying you a single point,” replied Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now, now, let’s calm down, okay? We can’t hold a discussion if you’re fixing for a fight right from the get-go,” said Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right, now that everyone’s here, I guess we can get this thing started. For the groups, we—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t just start yammerin’ outta nowhere,” snapped Housen, shoving Takahashi by his shoulder, causing him to fall down hard on his bottom.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya, displeased by Housen’s actions, shot him an intense glare.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Housen. Don’t bring your violent attitude here.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh? What, you plannin’ on gettin’ in my way?” replied Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If necessary, yeah,” said Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmph, ain’t that interestin’. If you feel like you can bring it, then go ahead and try,” said Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When Housen raised his left hand in the air, Takahashi scrambled back to his feet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, wait, hold on! Look, I just slipped and fell, that’s all, man. Come on, calm down, Riku,” shouted Takahashi, in a panic.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“See, that’s all it was, yeah?” said Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately for you, I’m not as nice as Takahashi,” replied Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, why don’t ya show me what ya got?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just when Housen made a fist, Utomiya reached out and grabbed his arm.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Would you have not chosen to come here, if you had known beforehand that this school’s curriculum is designed around the assumption that you’d cooperate with other students?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I wouldn’t say that. I like this school. In fact, I’ve never once felt dissatisfied about anything here, even once. The point system is also very pleasant,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kouenji seemed to be enjoying himself fully too, so I’m guessing he must also like this school, as well. Students who could manage to do anything on their own after graduation didn’t need to cling to Class A.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seems like you don’t mind being disliked by other people,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Other people’s evaluations don’t mean anything to me,” she answered, boldly and confidently, followed by a strange smile.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I had wanted to as you some questions, but it seems like you’re the one asking me all the questions,” she added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kiryuuin, as if she were switching from being on the defensive to going on the offensive, immediately went ahead and prompted me to speak.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think it’s about time you tell me about yourself</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why me, though? There are lots of academically gifted students here,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A hunch. My intuition is telling me that this student who was passing by is no ordinary person,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, she was asking because of her instincts, with no real basis for anything whatsoever. I had already thought she and Kouenji might be similar, but now they seemed like the same exact person.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you planning to get first place on the uninhabited island survival exam?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There aren’t any students who don’t want to be first, are there? Except for people like you, Kiryuuin-senpai, I suppose,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Putting aside first place, I am one of the people shooting for one of the top spots. If I get a high score, I can get Private Points. I’m the type of person who spends whatever she has on hand, so I’m always short on money,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, Class Points and Protection Points were secondary to her. In the end, it seemed like Kiryuuin was only participating in this exam to get Private Points.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nagumo and Kiriyama and the others are naturally hoping to get first. And I’m sure there are some quite competent underclassmen lined up as well, right? This upcoming special exam will be a battle to determine who gets first place in the school,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It certainly might be, yes,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The abilities needed in this exam weren’t limited strictly to academic ability or physical ability. If this was a battle of overall ability, then you could say that was exactly what you needed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whether or not my interest in you fades will depend on what you do on the island,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If I had to say anything in response to that, it’s that I’d rather that you lose interest in me, senpai,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. You say some intriguing things. I’m looking forward to fighting you, Ayanokouji,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And with that, Kiriyama waved me away like she was trying to shoo away some small animal, signaling that I should leave.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you’ll please excuse me,” I stated, before walking away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although I had a meeting with a rather bizarre third-year student just now, one thing was for certain. If I wanted to get one of the top spots in this upcoming exam, I was going to have to defeat Kiryuuin as well. And it looked as though she’d be just as troublesome as Nagumo and Kiriyama, or perhaps even more so.</span></p>

<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">5.6</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">E</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">VEN AFTER</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Ayanokouji had left, Kiryuuin continued to stay put. Her daily routine was to simply relax and enjoy the day at her leisure, according to her own whims. In her line of sight, she saw a familiar head of blonde hair swaying about. Next to the owner of that blonde hair was Student Council Vice President Kiriyama, who had walked off just moments ago.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, well, well! It looks the faithful dog has returned, and he’s brought his master with him,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What…?” huffed Kiriyama.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, if what I’ve said there is making you angry, it seems that it’s because you had interpreted it a certain way, Kiriyama. I don’t remember stating exactly who the faithful dog and master are between the two of you right here. I’m just saying this as a third-party, someone who doesn’t know your situation. Why do I say this? Because you’re the one who walked away earlier Kiriyama, and thus, you’re the only one who fits the description of a faithful dog,” she replied, as Kiriyama approached her with Nagumo standing beside him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What an irritating chick…” mumbled Kiriyama.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s a rather unpleasant thing to say, Kiriyama. It certainly doesn’t sound like something a serious vice president should say,” replied Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nagumo, engaging with her is a waste of time. You already know this quite well,” said Kiriyama.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I’m of the same opinion. Can the both of you please get out of my sight, immediately? This is just a waste of my precious time,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who do you think you are? First of all, you—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo cut in, gently tapping Kiriyama on the shoulder to cut him off.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kiryuuin, please don’t wound my precious colleague like that,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo then forced Kiriyama to back off and he stood in front of Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, a precious colleague, hm? I didn’t feel any kind of emotion from you when you said that, though,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s just your imagination,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now then, I wonder what business the student council president has with me? I had thought we wouldn’t ever be speaking with one another again,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t want to stay around you for too long, if at all possible,” said Nagumo, aggressively taking a seat on the bench right next to Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re beautiful, but you’re utterly lacking in charm. I’m only interested in women with charm, after all,” added Nagumo, expressing that as the reason he didn’t want to stick around.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I do have charm. It’s just that I haven’t found the right gentleman that can draw it out,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If there’s a man out there who can bring out your charm, I’d like to see him,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Me too. However, your tastes aside, why is it that I am not popular, hm?” asked Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s because a woman who is too capable and too talented is difficult to handle. Unfortunately, I can’t bring myself to like that kind of woman either,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Well, in that case, I suppose that I’ll never pass your standards. If the reason I haven’t gotten a boyfriend yet at my age is because I’m just too good, then yes, I guess that does make sense,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After enjoying a bit of meaningless conversation with Kiryuuin, Nagumo cut to the chase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I heard something from Kiriyama. I would have never imagined that you, who didn’t show any admiration for neither Horikita-senpai nor myself, would have been interested in in Ayanokouji. I was shocked when I heard about this,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And that’s why you’ve come all the way over to talk to me? You must have a lot of free time on your hands, President,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve finished my administrative work. I have some extra time right now,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would appear that you’ve misunderstood something here. I’m not entirely indifferent to people, Nagumo. I&#160;make it a point to talk to people who rouse my interest at least once. You and Horikita Manabu had caught my interest before,” said Kiryuuin, gently stroking Nagumo’s bangs.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seems like you never slack off when it comes to taking excellent care of your hair. I can tell that you take better care of it than I, a woman, do mine. It’s no wonder you’re popular, President. Say, has your love life been fruitful over these past three years?” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ve never been with a man before. How would you know anything about love?” countered Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“While it’s certainly true that I don’t have any experience in that arena, it’s nothing to be ashamed of. In fact, some could say that it actually makes me more valuable, don’t you agree?” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems like you still have absurd ideas, as always,” replied Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kiryuuin and Nagumo once again veered off into a bizarre tangent, but Nagumo proceeded to steer the conversation back towards the topic at hand.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, what did you think of Ayanokouji? Is he someone deserving of holding your attention?” asked Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I just paid him some lip service because he’s a cute underclassman. But that was all,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That was all? Does that mean that you don’t feel impressed with him?” asked Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m putting that question on hold for the time being. We did have a brief face-to-face conversation, but I didn’t get a grasp on his true abilities. Though you could say that in itself is a type of skill, I suppose. Anyway, he is more entertaining to me than you, President, who I’ve lost interest in,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re the only person in our grade level who can talk to me with that much sass,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo then drew in close, with his mouth near Kiryuuin’s ear, and lowered his voice to a whisper.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you think that you’re better than me, I’m more than happy to correct that vain idea you seem to have in your head. Understand?” he threatened quietly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo issued her a challenge, effectively issuing a challenge for the upcoming special exam on the island.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When you lose, what you lose is incalculable, President. It seems like you’re misunderstanding something here. But it’s not as though I’m underestimating you. I don’t have the outstanding leadership skills that you and Horikita Manabu possess, nor do I have any talent for making friends. In fact, I’ve never even had anyone that I could truly call a friend. Right?” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo moved his face away from Kiryuuin’s ear, seeming somewhat bored.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“However, in other matters, it’s different,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although Nagumo had started to pull back from Kiryuuin, their faces were less than forty centimeters apart. Nagumo shot Kiryuuin a sharp glare.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you saying that I’m inferior to you in some respects?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well now, can you state with absolute certainty that there are no areas in which you are inferior?” countered Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have given you several chances to test that, and you have done nothing. And the result is that you’ve ended up in B,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Up until this point, Nagumo had competed against Kiriyama and Kiryuuin’s class via the numerous special exams. But never once did Kiryuuin actually take part and cooperate with her class. And as a result, her class was demoted from A to B.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s certainly true that if you only look at those results, I have been thoroughly crushed,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kiriyama continued to glare at Kiryuuin, who was pleasantly enjoying the conversation, but Kiriyama didn’t interrupt at all.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I know that you’re not the kind of person who really cares about A or B though,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo then got up from the bench, signaling that this was the end of their conversation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry for bothering you, Kiryuuin. Enjoy the remainder of your time here at this school,” said Nagumo, preparing himself to leave.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I had said earlier that I was going to put the question of whether or not Ayanokouji was someone I’m impressed with on hold. I do think he’s an interesting student,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What?” asked Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That was the answer you were hoping to hear from me about Ayanokouji, wasn’t it?” asked Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One of the reasons why Nagumo had come over to talk with Kiryuuin was because he wanted to find out what she thought about Ayanokouji.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Interesting? I would think that this personality is far from interesting. Don’t you think?” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kiryuuin smiled, as if she were saying, “See, you’ve taken the bait, after all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, you know how the proverb goes, ‘a skilled hawk hides its talons.’ Isn’t that right? I had heard he had gotten a perfect score on a rather difficult mathematics test,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure, there are people out there who hide their talents because they don’t want to stand out. I’ve beaten all of them though. And I don’t think that they were all that interesting,” said Nagumo, then directing his gaze over at Kiriyama, who had been waiting for him while standing some distance away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If I had to say anything, it’s his vibe, I suppose. He has a different vibe that you and Horikita Manabu,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that’s rather abstract,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, why don’t you test him and see?” asked Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course, I intend to. Maybe I’ll get to see what he’s capable of during the uninhabited island survival exam,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You seem like you’ve been bored ever since Horikita Manabu graduated. Will this underclassman be a good playmate for you? If you’re serious about this challenge, you’ll most definitely get first place in the island text, Nagumo,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Obviously, I’m going to take first. Or perhaps Kiriyama will, since he’s been dying to compete with me. But we’re going to need one more group if we’re going to take all three top spots in the exam, aren’t we? You are going to take on that role, Kiryuuin. I will provide you with some useful companions, if necessary,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And then, Nagumo touched on the main reason he had reached out to Kiryuuin today. Kiryuuin smiled, as if to say that she understood what he was asking.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. So, you met up with me because you’re seeking my help,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You might think that I’d let the younger students at least take third place, but I’m not that nice,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You have countless pawns at your disposal that you can move about the board, don’t you? You don’t need to rely on me,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Meaning that you’re not willing to do this?” asked Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Somewhere in the top fifty percent is fine for now. Sorry for wasting your time,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo, as if he had already known that she was going to answer like that, had turned his head to look off far into the distance.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You really are that kind of person, I suppose. I thought I’d try talking to you, as someone from my grade, but it seems like it was a waste of my time,” said Nagumo, his statement suggesting that he was going to leave now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He walked over to where Kiriyama stood.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Since you came all the way here to talk to me, I’ll give you one piece of advice,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re going to give me advice? Sorry, but I don’t need that from someone beneath me,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“By that logic, you wouldn’t be able to get advice from anyone,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo, with his back turned to Kiryuuin, let out a scornful chuckle. Kiryuuin continued speaking.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, just thinking of what I’m about to say as me speaking to myself. You should be looking ahead, not paying attention to the younger students. If you focus on the younger students behind you, you’ll going to be in for a lot of pain,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What a boring monologue,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo seemed to have decided that standing around any longer would’ve been a waste, so he left.</span></p>
