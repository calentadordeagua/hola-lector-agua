However, with a faculty meeting in session, I changed my direction and headed toward the chairman&apos;s office to submit my notice. The chairman did seem a bit surprised, but perhaps that man had already informed him&#x2014;he decided against asking any further questions. Afterward, the various arrangements swiftly proceeded; which included the verification of the twenty million points and their source.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Having heard about this right before the homeroom period started, I was sure that it&apos;d take some time for Mashima-sensei to understand that this was in fact real.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">With a hint of bewilderment still remaining on his face, Mashima-sensei, the homeroom teacher, cleared his throat and looked at me.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;For what it&apos;s worth, I think I should introduce myself here. What do you think?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Of course, it wasn&apos;t my first time at this school.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">From the time I&apos;d spent in this school up until now, even though we might have been in different classes, I remembered everyone&apos;s names and faces.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">And all the Class C students were aware of me.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">However, I still had to go through the formalities.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;The opening ceremony just ended, but I, Ayanokouji Kiyotaka, have transferred to this class using twenty million Private Points. deteriorated situation.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I kept it brief, but I firmly conveyed what was necessary.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Reflecting on my past failure during my self-introduction as a first-year student, I chose my words carefully.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left"><em>I think it was a passing mark, but I wonder if my intentions were properly conveyed to the students here.</em></p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">As everyone silently watched, one student broke the silence and clapped.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Glad to have ya, Ayanokouji.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">It was the biggest investor in my transfer to Class C&#x2014;Hashimoto Masayoshi.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Starting from there, a few others began to clap sporadically.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">However, I could see that not everybody was welcoming.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">The looks directed at me weren&apos;t all warm.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Rather, the majority of their gazes were cold, and many of them weren&apos;t truly welcoming.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Of course, I didn&apos;t expect everyone to accept me from the beginning.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Rather, it would speak poorly of the class&apos;s quality if that were the case..</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">It would be like them introducing themselves as a feeble gathering of a class&#x2014;one that, upon losing Sakayanagi, had now lost all of its ability to judge situations, leaving everything to a helper<sup class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">In terms of the seating arrangement alone, securing enough space for four seats wouldn&apos;t be a problem.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: the seating arrangement?</em></p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Before Mashima-sensei could decide, a female student sitting by the window in the very back raised her hand swiftly enough for it to make a noise.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;For now, I believe the seat in front of me would be suitable.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: Placing him in the center of the class right away would make him shrink back like a loner.
