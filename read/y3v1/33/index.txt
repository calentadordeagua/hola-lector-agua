If they were chosen and defeated, no one knew what kind of treatment they&apos;d receive from Ryuuen.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">The participants chosen for the small-group battle were usually communicated to the parties involved beforehand.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">However, Ryuuen kept completely silent, not revealing even a single person.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Until the start of the exam, everyone was a candidate.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">In the face of such an aggressive approach, everyone felt like they couldn&apos;t afford to hold back.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Katsuragi had a grasp of the entire class&apos;s academic abilities, and he could feel the improvements the class had made in less than two weeks. Such was the selection.
