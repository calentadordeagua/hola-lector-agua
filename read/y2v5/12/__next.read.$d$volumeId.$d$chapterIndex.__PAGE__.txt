<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 6:<br/>Ryuuen Kakeru’s Choice</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HE UNANIMOUS SPECIAL EXAM</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> began at one o’clock in the afternoon. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Class D was another class of forty students. The classroom was becoming enveloped by a thick, heavy atmosphere, and this was, of course, due to the intense nature of the final issue they had finally arrived at.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em; text-transform: uppercase;">ISSUE #5:</span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;"> In exchange for expelling one of your classmates, gain 100 Class Points.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">(In the event the class is unanimously in favor, a vote will be held to choose the student to be expelled.)</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Round 1 Voting Results:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> For: 14 Votes, Against: 26 Votes</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This was the moment when their voting results were revealed. Like in Horikita’s and Ichinose’s classes, the majority of voters were opposed to the issue. However, in contrast to those two, there were more than a few students in favor of expelling someone. In other words, it meant that one in every three students felt, at least at first, that they ought to prioritize Class Points, even if they had to expel someone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what do we do, Ryuuen-san?” grumbled Ishizaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After seeing the results, the very first person that Ishizaki turned to for guidance was Ryuuen Kakeru, the leader of the class. Ishizaki had done the very same thing after every other issue. Since the probability of coming to a unanimous decision in the first round of voting was low, the class used the first interval period to hear the leader’s policy on it, and they aimed for unanimity in the second round of voting and beyond. The sequence of events in this class was similar to that of other classes, but the degree of precision in this class was extremely high.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In the first issue of selecting another class to face off against, the third issue where they voted on the matter of Protect Points, and in the fourth issue where they voted on test conditions, they had come to a unanimous decision on the matter after only one interval period. In those cases, they chose the answer that Ryuuen ordered them to. The only issue where Ryuuen let his classmates vote as they pleased was the second one, regarding where they wanted to go for the school trip.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen let his classmates debate for roughly thirty minutes about where they wanted to go, and in the end, they voted unanimously in favor of the destination that had the highest number of votes by that time. It was obvious now to everyone that while the content of the fifth issue was different from that one in essence, it would still work the same way. Every issue that seemed to need direction was settled by just a few words from Ryuuen. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The students were only deeply conscious of how Ryuuen voted on issues. If Ryuuen was in favor, that meant it was an absolute certainty that someone was going to be expelled. His decisions were absolute. That was the peculiarity of this class—students here were held together by a dictatorship. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen stood up from his chair and stared at the results with a smile on his face. “Well, it’s been boring as hell so far, but guess the school wasn’t gonna just let things end on an easy note after all. If they did, things wouldn’t be so interesting now, would they?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though he muttered those words to himself, he said it loud enough for all of his classmates to hear. He then proceeded to the podium. Sakagami, the teacher in charge of watching over the class, sensed Ryuuen approaching and moved away. He was well aware of the fact that this was where Ryuuen’s showboating act was set to begin. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen sat atop the podium as if it were a seat specially reserved for him. He leaned forward so that he could look out over all of his classmates. “Raise your hand if you voted in favor.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">His order didn’t show a hint of consideration for anyone. There was incredible tension in the air for everyone, regardless of how they voted. Ryuuen hadn’t directly asked everyone what they voted for in any of the previous issues. After a few seconds of hesitation, scattered hands began to rise. Among them were Nishino and Kaneda, who raised their hands while staring out the window, as if they were somewhat unwilling.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Five of you, huh,” said Ryuuen. “Well, that ain’t bad for the first time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The truth was that there were nine other students who had voted in favor of the issue who didn’t obey Ryuuen’s order and weren’t coming forward. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Students like Ishizaki and Komiya were surprised to see this.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, come on now. You know no good’s gonna come from tryin’ to hide it?” Komiya appealed to his silent classmates. “It’s not like people are gonna be mad at you for voting in favor for this one time or anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After all, if they spoke up now, there wouldn’t be any trouble.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Besides, it’s not like we were ordered to vote one way or another,” he added. “We’re each free to vote either </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> or </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This was also Komiya’s way of checking with Ryuuen that he had his facts correct though, just in case. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When Ryuuen didn’t answer right away, Komiya immediately started feeling nervous. If there was a difference in their interpretation of the situation, it was possible it could lead to a reprimand. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki, not liking this change in the air in the classroom, immediately panicked and followed up on what Komiya said. “Come on!” he said, flustered. “Get your hands up in the air before you get us in trouble!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One more student raised their hand, fearfully and apologetically, bringing the total to six. That meant that the remaining eight students hadn’t budged and left their hands down.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s all right, Ishizaki,” said Ryuuen. “If they don’t wanna raise their hands, they don’t gotta. For now, at least.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? R-really?” asked Ishizaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Komiya said it himself. Everyone’s free to vote </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> or </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. So, first of all, each one of you should think about what you yourself are gonna do. We got a little over eight minutes left. That’s plenty of time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen checked the clock leisurely. He didn’t relax his posture, and the smile never once left his face. He had only vaguely told the other students to think about it, that was it. He remained completely silent for more than two minutes, not doing anything in that time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Listen up now,” he said at last. “Don’t waste this time. Think about what choice is the right one.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was another period of silence. Ten seconds. Thirty seconds. Even after another minute passed, he didn’t say another word. In all of the issues so far, Ryuuen had forced them to come to a decision after the first interval period. Because of that, the only thought going through the students’ minds right now was, “</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Why isn’t Ryuuen giving us instructions?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, there weren’t many students who could just speak up and voice their opinions like that, and the more time that passed, the more their mouths felt like they were sealed. Ishizaki and others like him were among the first you’d expect to say something like, “</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Please give us an order!” </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, even they kept their heads down. Their lips were pressed together as if they had been fixed in place with glue.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The seconds ticked by and the more they felt like they were almost losing the desire to speak at all anymore. Eventually, those who wished to say something would stop speaking up, shifting instead to hoping that someone else would say something on their behalf. And when even more time passed, students began hoping that it would be time to vote again soon, even though they had a lot of time remaining. The first interval felt incredibly long and drawn out, and it ended with the majority of time spent in total silence. Even Sakagami hadn’t expected that, and he ended up forgetting to transition to the next round, when they were a few seconds over the scheduled interval time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sakagami,” Ryuuen said suddenly as he hopped off the podium and headed back to his seat. “It’s time, ain’t it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Yes, you’re right,” said Sakagami. “We’ll now begin the second round of voting. You’ll have sixty seconds.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As soon as everyone finished putting in their votes for the second round, the results were displayed up on the monitor.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Round 2 Voting Results:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> For: 10 Votes, Against: 30 Votes</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Four of the fourteen votes in favor of the issue in the&#160;first round had been swayed to the other side. For the majority of students in the class who didn’t want anyone to be expelled, this result was, generally speaking, not a bad one. One or two more sternly worded warnings from Ryuuen would reduce the number of votes in favor. And, in the not-too-distant future, they would see the class vote unanimously against the issue. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The results of the second round indicated that to those students, but at the same time, Ryuuen didn’t seem satisfied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is this the answer you came up with?” he said. “I don’t believe it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Because of the small reduction in votes </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, you mean?” asked Kaneda, adjusting the position of his glasses.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen immediately denied that supposition.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, does that mean…that you’re voting in favor of the issue, Ryuuen-kun?” asked Kaneda.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen denied that as well, letting out an exasperated but amused snort. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what in the heck is up with you Ryuuen-san?” Ishizaki wailed. “I just don’t get it!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did the votes in the first and second round really reflect what you truly think?” said Ryuuen. “Your will? This last issue is obviously unique, it’s the only one like this. And that’s exactly why I wanna know your true </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">intentions</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> here. Don’t worry about what I voted for. Vote how you honestly feel.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After saying that, Ryuuen got up from his seat once again and slowly walked around the classroom.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For these next ten minutes, talk about it,” he ordered. “Intensely. Whether you want to vote in favor of it or vote against it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With those instructions, the class was unavoidably forced to debate the issue. The students began talking as much as they pleased, and the classroom was filled with a hurried, panicked commotion. As Ryuuen listened in on what the others had to say, he would occasionally lean in close to a student and whisper something in a quiet voice. He said something to Nishino and Shiina, then Yoshimoto and Nomura—he didn’t seem to be especially choosy about which students he talked to. Then, he approached Suzuki, and whispered in a similar manner as he had before.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re free to vote for or against. Vote according to whatever you think.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When Ryuuen said those words to Suzuki, however, Tokitou overheard him from two seats away. Although some students wondered why Ryuuen deliberately went out of his way to say things to some students, the discussion continued for as long as time allowed. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, it came to be time for the third round of voting.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Round 3 Voting Results:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> For: 9 Votes, Against: 31 Votes</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The results on the monitor were hardly any different from those of the second round. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen took a seat upon the podium desk again and decided to offer his thoughts during the third interval period.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Raise your hands if you voted in favor,” he ordered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once more, after seeing the results, Ryuuen ordered everyone to raise their hands. Only two people raised their hands this time: Nishino and Kaneda. The remaining seven refused to step forward, keeping their presence hidden. Ishizaki was visibly annoyed by these unseen supporters of the issue, but Ryuuen didn’t pay them any mind. Instead, he turned his attention to Nishino and Kaneda.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You two voted in favor three times now,” he said. “What’s the reason, Kaneda?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To win,” Kaneda replied. “While it’s not good by any means to have students be expelled, I think it’s important to get those 100 Class Points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Didn’t you think that if you raise your hand, you’d be a candidate for expulsion?” asked Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s a stupid question, Ryuuen-kun. You would dispose of people you can’t use—people who are unnecessary. But you wouldn’t dispose of someone you need. At the very least, in this class, 100 points doesn’t match my worth.” Kaneda had measured his own value and confidently determined that he was in no danger of being thrown aside.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, looks aside, you’re a lot of use to me,” replied Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you very much.” Kaneda nodded in satisfaction, conveniently paying no attention to the remark about his physical appearance.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nishino, you the same as Kaneda?” Ryuuen asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? No way. I’m just voting yes because it’s a quick way to get more Class Points, that’s all. I raised my hand because I don’t like being all sneaky. There’s nothing wrong with voting in favor.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki was even more nervous than Nishino herself because he knew that Ryuuen was likely to glower at anyone who talked to him like that if they weren’t careful.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right, I figure it’s about time I tell you all what you’ve been wonderin’ about. What </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> voted for,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“P-please tell us!” Ishizaki loudly voiced his wishes and pitched forward as he spoke.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Things couldn’t officially start until the class heard what Ryuuen’s vote was. Or, in other words, what the class’s policy would be.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right. For this issue…I voted in favor of it. All three times.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As the results currently stood, that meant that three of the nine votes in favor had come from Ryuuen, Nishino, and Kaneda.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“S-so, basically, this means…someone’s gonna get expelled from class, right?” Ishizaki asked meekly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen smiled ominously. “Don’t jump to conclusions,” he said. “I’m just tellin’ ya how I voted, that’s all. I&#160;already decided that in this issue, you all gotta figure out what you want to do yourselves.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“U-us…you mean?” Ishizaki stammered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s right. I voted in favor three times now without hesitation.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If Ryuuen had voted in favor three times, then it was safe to assume that his plan was for one of their classmates to be expelled. However, since Ishizaki didn’t approve of this plan of action, he didn’t understand what this all meant. He was at a loss for words.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The reason for voting in favor is simple,” Ryuuen told the class. “If we get rid of one person, we get a hundred points. Or, putting it another way, this is a golden opportunity to take out the trash and get Class Points for it. It’s the best possible choice. It’s one that’s gonna help us and not hold us back. But, even after votin’ three times now, there’re still more votes </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> than </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. Meanin’ that over half of the class voted against the issue, basically. If&#160;that’s the case, then I’m gonna respect their wishes, and change my vote to </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen was giving up on the Class Points and setting a plan of action where they’d hold onto their classmates.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki looked relieved after hearing this easy-to-understand plan. He appealed to his classmates to follow along. “I-it’s settled! Everybody, don’t vote in favor! Vote </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">! Those’re Ryuuen-san’s orders!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hold on a minute,” said Ibuki, sounding dissatisfied. She had looked bored through this entire special exam so far. “This isn’t like you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean?” asked Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re in favor of the idea, aren’t you? In that case, you could just force things ahead and make everybody vote </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> like how you always do. But </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">now</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> you’re trying to act like the good guy and say something like how you’re gonna protect your friends?” Ibuki was implying that Ryuuen would always go for the Class Points in front of him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What, so you voted in favor too?” asked Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I voted </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">,” Ibuki replied. “But my intentions are none of your business.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If this thing weren’t anonymous, then yeah, I might’ve let it come to a unanimous decision in favor, without a second thought,” Ryuuen said. “It would’ve been quicker if I just drove one of the people who defied my opinions toward expulsion. But unfortunately, this time they’re doin’ this exam by way of an anonymous vote. As long as I can’t be sure who voted for what exactly, it’d just be faster to get everyone to vote </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> since that’s already got over half the votes.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you’re saying that you’re not confident that you could get a unanimous decision in favor?” asked Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Ku ku</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, you can think whatever you want,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-you shouldn’t go overboard with what you say, Ibuki,” Ishizaki cut in. “Ryuuen-san said to vote </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, so isn’t that good enough? If we’d be losin’ Class Points, that’d be one thing, but we’re not. We’re just gonna finish the exam.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whatever,” huffed Ibuki. “It’s just that this is all a little out of character for him. I was curious, that’s all. Do&#160;whatever you want.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Now that a plan had been set in place, a great deal of the interval period was spent in silence. And when the fourth round came, the results were…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please, Ryuuen,” said Katsuragi. “Give Tokitou a chance.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen heard what Katsuragi said and interpreted it to be solely to his advantage.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He snapped his fingers. “All right. Tokitou, I’m gonna give you a chance. Everything’s gonna come down to your one vote in the next round. If you vote in favor, then I’m gonna have you expelled.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hah… Big talk. You think you can get me expelled?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I do.” Ryuuen said. “In the next round, every other vote except for yours will be against the issue. Which means it’ll be one vote </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, and thirty-nine </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. That’s the situation. So, if you vote against the issue, it’ll be unanimous, and we’ll be done here.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, hold on. When did the four other people who voted in favor besides me disappear?” said Tokitou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Ku ku</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">… Well, I flipped those four votes during the interval.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Enough with the lame jokes. There’s no way you could’ve done that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Aside from the fact that Ryuuen himself had stubbornly been in favor of the idea up until this point, he had spent most of this interval period talking with Tokitou. He hadn’t even tried to talk to any others to get them to change their votes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Then let’s put this to the test,” he said. “You vote in favor as you’ve done all this time. Do that, and you’ll find&#160;out.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Time continued to tick by during this interval. There was now less than a minute remaining. Although the air-conditioned room was kept at a comfortable temperature, sweat was slowly starting to trickle down Tokitou’s back. Ryuuen was merely threatening him; it was just a bluff. It was hard to imagine that anything had actually changed during the interval. But…what if all the other voters in favor really had changed their votes? That would indicate that those four other students were following Ryuuen. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tokitou could have employed the same defensive measure that Ibuki mentioned—he could vote against the issue when the rest of the class was going to vote in favor before they could come to a unanimous decision. But Tokitou couldn’t go that route now. It would be disgraceful. At any rate, a deciding vote between him and Ryuuen was likely inevitable. And if that happened, it was a certainty that Tokitou would be defeated.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re prepared for expulsion, aren’t you?” said Ryuuen. “Don’t hold back. Vote in favor.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…You don’t have to tell me twice,” said Tokitou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Soon, the time would come for them to vote. Tokitou cast his vote in favor of the issue, without looking back.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, I’ll show you the results,” announced Sakagami, displaying the voting results on the monitor.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Round 13 Voting Results:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> For: 2 Votes, Against: 38 Votes</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wha—?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When Tokitou saw those results, he was sure his heart was racing more quickly than anyone else’s. It was almost just like Ryuuen had said. Nearly all of the other people who were voting in favor had changed their votes. Only one person hadn’t.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hah. Now that’s certainly a shock… But this just means that there’s still one other student here who has got a strong will like me! Someone else who didn’t give in, no matter how much you threatened ’em!” bellowed Tokitou, as though he were declaring victory. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, Ryuuen wasn’t looking at Tokitou. Instead, he was looking at someone completely different.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s the meanin’ of this, huh? You voted in favor, didn’t you, Katsuragi?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What…?” asked Tokitou, shocked to hear Katsuragi’s name.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s right,” Katsuragi replied. “If I had voted </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, then it would’ve been one vote </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> and thirty-nine </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, as you declared. That means it would’ve gone on to a deciding vote. If that happened, it would’ve been impossible for us to get through this exam without one of the two of you being expelled.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s the way it should have gone, yeah,” said Ryuuen. “And depending on the explanation you give me right now, you might not get off with just an apology.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have one reason. I think that Tokitou is a student this class needs. Well, actually, not just Tokitou. Yes, I am an outsider and came to this class from Class A. However, that is exactly why I’ve been able to look at this class with an objective eye. As a result, I can tell quite clearly that there isn’t a single student here who isn’t needed.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re sayin’ that Tokitou is a student we need? Somebody who doesn’t follow directions?” asked Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s right. Actually, I would consider him a valuable asset. He’s someone who can disagree with your opinions without hesitation, like I do. No, actually, even more than I can. Of course, that being said, the way he did it in this particular case, in a special exam, was wrong. I wasn’t impressed by the fact that he put the class in danger just to take you down, Ryuuen.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Katsuragi wasn’t only going to address Ryuuen. He turned to speak to Tokitou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you don’t like the fact that Ryuuen is the leader, make your case fairly and honestly in a way that doesn’t involve anyone else. If your argument is correct, then I will not hesitate to take your side.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Katsuragi, you…” huffed Tokitou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you fall for Ryuuen’s tricks here, you’ll be expelled from school having accomplished nothing. We’d all move forward and Ryuuen won’t even remember that a student named Tokitou Hiroya ever existed.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“B-but, what about the four other people who voted—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He was referring to his invisible reinforcements who had helped push Tokitou to this point. The foundation for his courage.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There were no such people from the very beginning,” Katsuragi told him. “It was an illusion.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“An illusion…?” repeated Tokitou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To be precise, I suppose I should say that they were weeded out through repeated voting. Five votes in favor remained after Yajima’s was called out. That included you, Tokitou, and…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Katsuragi paused and slowly turned around, directing his gaze elsewhere. He pointed at other students one by one. “Shiina, Yamada, myself, and…Ryuuen. The four of&#160;us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Katsuragi gave Tokitou an answer that neither he nor any of their other classmates could understand. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh… Wait, what are you talking about? Ryuuen was…voting in favor too?” asked Tokitou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When there were only five votes </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, that meant there was really only one anonymous vote remaining. However, everything came to light when you stepped forward,” said Katsuragi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So during this entire interval, Ryuuen was just mocking me in his head, huh… That figures,” huffed Tokitou, dejected.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not true,” said Katsuragi. “While our intention </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">was</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> to smoke out whoever was voting in favor, when you came forward, that matter was settled. You could have simply stayed silent and kept voting, not challenging anything. If that happened, the vote would’ve naturally resulted in a unanimous decision </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, and the next vote would have ended with an expulsion.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So you were playing word games just to insult me?!” snapped Tokitou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, we weren’t. We were giving you the possibility of </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">not</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> being expelled,” said Katsuragi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wha…?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“However, you pushed on ahead, not noticing that chance. I suppose that was because you didn’t think that Ryuuen would give you one, even if it was in a roundabout way.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I…!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Still, no matter how much I try to convince you, if you won’t listen to me, that’ll be the end of it. I’m sorry that this has taken up so much time, but please give Tokitou one final chance, Ryuuen,” Katsuragi said. “I would like you to give him one more opportunity to vote </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, before having everyone else vote in favor of the issue.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“One more chance?” asked Ryuuen. “You think that I’m that nice?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You also share some fault here. You were overly argumentative and completely overlooked the idea of saving someone. Now that everything has been revealed, you can finally give Tokitou a choice.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And if he doesn’t cooperate, you ain’t gonna object to me expellin’ him, will you?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I won’t. You can do whatever you like.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Katsuragi closed his eyes and crossed his arms. Now, the matter of deciding Tokitou’s future was in Tokitou’s own hands. If he voted in favor of the idea, there was a 100 percent chance he would be expelled. On the other hand, if he voted against the issue, it would be a unanimous decision and he would avoid expulsion. However, voting against the issue would still mean submitting to Ryuuen in some form. It would be a serious blow to Tokitou’s pride.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now then, we’ll begin the sixty-second voting period,” announced Sakagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At the same time he made that announcement, the countdown began. All thirty-nine students except for Tokitou finished entering their votes within the sixty-second time limit…and the counter continued to run. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakagami looked up and glanced over at Tokitou. “As was explained to you in advance, if you exceed sixty seconds, you will accrue penalty time,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tokitou stared downward, alternating glances at the two words displayed on the tablet: </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> and </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Damn it… Damn it!” he groaned.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This should have been the signal fire marking the start of a counterattack, he realized. However, it had turned out that Tokitou had been alone the entire time. He had been dancing solo in the palm of Ryuuen’s hand through all of it. He felt frustrated, embarrassed, and pathetic. All sorts of negative emotions took hold of Tokitou’s heart and wouldn’t let go. For a moment, his pride was clear on his face, like he was saying he was not about to give in to Ryuuen here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He could go out in a blaze of glory. Or he could intentionally vote yes instead, in order to buy himself some time. If he continued to vote opposite the thirty-nine other students, he might also be able to make the class fail the issue in the end. The exam would end not with his expulsion, but by him making the entire class fail the special exam… </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Those thoughts flashed through his mind, but Tokitou quickly shook his head to dispel them.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was nothing for him to gain, not even if he did those things to try and resist Ryuuen. It would only cause more trouble for his classmates and make them hate him more than they did Ryuuen. That was not what Tokitou wanted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“God… DAMMIT!!!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tokitou lifted his arm up high in an exaggerated motion and slammed his finger down on a button to cast his vote. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…All votes are now in,” said Sakagami. “I’ll show you the results now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The teacher paused to take a breath, fiddled with his tablet, and displayed the results on the monitor.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Round 14 Voting Results:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> For: 0 Votes, Against: 40 Votes</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s unanimous—the class has voted against the issue. This concludes the special exam.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Everyone had thought that chances were high someone would’ve been expelled from Ryuuen’s class. But the exam ended with all students remaining. Ishizaki turned to look back at Tokitou, about to say something to the downcast Tokitou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tokitou, you—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Don’t get the wrong idea here, Ryuuen,” Tokitou spoke over him, addressing Ryuuen instead. “I don’t approve of the way you do things. If I decide that your way is gonna prevent us from moving up to Class A, I will take you out, no matter how many times I gotta come at you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Then come at me any time,” Ryuuen replied. “I’ll take you on without mercy.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmph…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It would have been awkward for Tokitou to remain in the classroom any longer, so he left as quickly as he could. After watching him leave, Katsuragi walked over to Ryuuen’s side.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You overstepped, Katsuragi,” Ryuuen told him. “I&#160;was welcoming the idea of someone gettin’ expelled, remember?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sure half of you was. But the other half of you was open to exploring other possibilities, yes?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The hell are you on about? Do I look that nice to you?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know whether you’re nice or not,” said Katsuragi, “but if your objective is complete control over the vote, it’s important to keep the students loyal to you on your side without pushing things too far. However, after the second round of voting, even though you walked around and said things to some other random students as well, you gave your true orders to Shiina. If you go around whispering only to specific students, people will think that you’re planning some kind of strategy. Then, through Shiina, you gathered together a group of people to falsely pose as supporters of the issue and vote </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. I was included among that group. The reason you did so was that you knew I would protect Tokitou, isn’t&#160;it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’d protect Tokitou?” Ryuuen repeated. “And where exactly would I have gotten that information?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Shiina overheard Tokitou and I talking about you. It wouldn’t be surprising if you found out about it from her reports.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was just bein’ selective in lookin’ for people who would vote in favor so he’d be misled by fake votes. And that was so I could get him expelled, of course, so I could get Class Points. Disappointing.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen left the classroom sometime later. After that happened, Katsuragi turned to look at the person who was watching him. He was honestly moved to see Shiina smiling warmly at him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, it’s possible that it was Shiina’s judgment call, one she made on her own, that brought me into it…” he muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In any case, the fact remained that Ryuuen laid the foundation for Tokitou to be saved and gave him a chance. As Katsuragi looked around at the other students, feeling relieved that no one had been expelled, he felt convinced of something. He felt like this class actually had the potential to defeat Sakayanagi and become Class A. And he wanted to pursue that path together with them.</span></p>
