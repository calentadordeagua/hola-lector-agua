<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 4:<br/>Dark Clouds</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">B</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 0.80em;">RRRR-ING!</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The same alarm clock I’ve had for the last ten years was ringing in my ears. I quickly and silently reached for it, and violently slammed the button to stop the alarm without a single care. The excess force caused the alarm clock to fall off the nightstand, letting out one final </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">ping!</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My alarm clock wasn’t so weak a partner that this level of abuse would break it though, not after all the training it had been through at my hands.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s already six o’clock…?” I muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ultimately, I had only gotten about two hours of sleep before morning came. I took off my pajamas, wondering why I’d even bothered putting them on in the first place, and walked over to the washbasin in my underwear with heavy, plodding steps. As I crossed the room, I picked up my alarm clock off the floor and realized the cover had come off. Specifically, the latch that kept the cover&#160;in place had popped out. It had broken before and I had patched up with tape. On top of that, one of its batteries was loose on the floor.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Looks like I was a little too rough with you,” I said to my alarm clock. “I’ll be more careful tomorrow, so forgive me, okay?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I walked over to the mirror.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh, I look awful…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I supposed it wasn’t </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">too</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> bad, but there was no way I could show my face to my students the way I looked right now. The dark circles under my eyes were even more pronounced because I’d been having a particularly hard time sleeping the past few nights. After carefully washing my face, I lined up my cosmetics, which I typically didn’t use at all. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I really am a mess… </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, I supposed it was just because I didn’t want my students to know how stressed out I was.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I reached for the container of lotion and took it in my hands, but I suddenly caught sight of myself in the mirror.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I certainly look terrible.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Without really thinking, I reached up and touched my cheek. The feel of my skin under my fingertips probably couldn’t begin to compare to what it was like during my school days.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess I’m getting old too,” I muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">You could say it’s only been a little over ten years since then, but still, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">ten years</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. Just feeling my face was enough to remind me that so much time had passed whether I liked it or not.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I guess that’s probably a trivial problem now…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It wasn’t as though this was the first time I was confronted with the passage of time. I always had a firm grasp on the concept. I resumed my previously halted movements, opened the lid, and silently began applying lotion to my skin. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I knew this would be coming someday. I knew it from the moment I decided to become a teacher. But even so…I wasn’t really prepared for it. I couldn’t be.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Calm down,” I told myself. “This is not my fight. The situation now is different from the way it was then too. The way the class is now, I bet they’ll make it through without incident. Yes, they should be able to do just that. There’s no point in me being nervous.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I felt my heart beating faster and faster, I tried to tell myself that this was essentially not my problem. Such shallow thinking was useless though, and my heart started to pound in my chest. At this rate, I wouldn’t be able to keep myself together before the special exam ended. I was worried about the future.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I pressed both of my palms against the mirror and glared at my reflection. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You have to be prepared…” </span></p>

<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">4.1</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">M</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ORNINGS WERE SURPRISINGLY</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> busy for teachers. We were already at our place of work without commuting since we lived in the dormitories on campus, close to the school building. Even so, there was a mountain of things for us to do: preparing for our classes, checking for new messages, sometimes checking the water quality of the pool, and so on. However, the start of our actual workday was when homeroom started, so essentially, taking care of those things amounted to unpaid overtime. They were things we had to take care of on our own.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After our respective morning preparations were finished, we teachers had a meeting as well for morning assembly. The rush of activity in the mornings was twice or even three times as intense on days like today when special exams were being administered. Any mistake on the school’s part would be absolutely unforgivable as it would affect some of the students’ lives.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The most important thing for you to keep in mind during this special exam is that as instructors, you are not to intervene,” warned Ikari-sensei. He wore a stern look on his face. “Please make absolutely certain that you avoid any kind of situation where you inadvertently offer help to your students because you want to protect your class.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ikari-sensei was in charge of we four homeroom instructors, and he was also responsible for proctoring this particular special exam.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, excuse me,” said Chie, “I know it might be too late for me to speak up now, but may I ask a question?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What is it, Hoshinomiya-sensei?” he answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The last time this… Eleven years ago, when this exam was last administered, the school implemented a rule to shuffle the classes around for this exam, so each class wasn’t led by its usual homeroom instructor, correct? So, why is it that this time, we’re watching over our usual classes for this exam? I would think that, for the sake of fairness, this aspect should be changed.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was clear from the school’s warning to the teachers that the administration wished to prevent teachers from intervening in the exam. However, shuffling the classes so that teachers would be overseeing different students would certainly be a much more reliable method. It was unlikely any of the teachers would go so far as to take on such risks themselves for the sake of helping a rival class.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Isn’t it simply because the school believes that fairness will be upheld in any case?” Sakagami-sensei said this calmly, having come to that conclusion after listening to the conversation thus far.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that really so?” remarked Chie.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I cannot provide you with any reasoning for it,” Ikari-sensei replied. “I can only state that things are the way they are because it’s been decided that way.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Meaning it’s a decision that came from the top?” asked Chie.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was not a single thing we teachers could decide ourselves for any of the special exams. Everything was decided upon by the higher-ups, meaning Chancellor Sakayanagi and those involved in the administration and management of this institution. All we could do was follow the rules and enforce them accordingly. However, something about this decision didn’t seem to add up, and Chie made no attempt to hide her dissatisfaction. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Seeing this, Ikari-sensei spoke once more, in a low, strained voice.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is only my own personal opinion here, but I believe this exam could potentially offer insight into the hidden workings of the students’ minds,” he said. “There is, essentially, a great deal of information to be learned. I suspect the school administrators felt that allowing teachers from other classes to access that information could impact subsequent special exams.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That sounds like they don’t have faith in the teachers then,” said Chie.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s nothing that can be done about it,” said Ikari-sensei. “Besides, three of the homeroom instructors present here have experienced something similar to this special exam in the past, and… Well, perhaps it has something to do with the fact that each of you were assigned to your usual classes during last year’s In-Class Voting special exam as well?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose that’s true,” said Chie. She sounded convinced at that, as though this was something she’d understood from the beginning.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hoshinomiya-sensei… May I continue now?” asked Ikari-sensei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, yes, please do! I understand, so please go on ahead!” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though Ikari-sensei was clearly annoyed, he resumed his explanation, sounding like he had given up on trying to argue.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If the person on monitoring duty deems it necessary, you will be issued a warning,” he said. “If there are repeated attempts to intervene, pay cuts will be involved. While we aren’t worried about you doing this, please keep in mind that, in the worst-case scenario, if it is found that any teacher intervenes in an underhanded manner and intentionally tries to coax their students into making a certain choice, that teacher will be demoted.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The Unanimous Special Exam was all about choice. If&#160;a teacher were to try anything that guided their students toward a particular choice the teacher favored, it would naturally undermine the very purpose of the special exam itself. Of course, neither myself nor any of the teachers from the other classes had any intention of doing that. As usual, I wouldn’t let myself get too emotionally involved with my students—I would simply press on ahead silently. That was all. Even if this special exam was full of bitter memories for me, I wasn’t going to do things any differently.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That is all. Now then, I wish you all the best of luck in today’s special exam,” said Ikari-sensei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After the morning meeting ended, I simply tried to get through my morning classes the way I usually would. Well, I was probably the only one who felt like I was being my usual self; in reality, I might have seemed different. I&#160;felt like I had no sense of time and before I knew it, it was already time for lunch. I sat in the faculty office with my lunch on the desk in front of me. After I had gotten about a third of the food down my throat, my chopsticks just suddenly stopped moving altogether. Not wanting myself to be seen like this, I decided to put my remaining lunch into a bag and put it away. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then came the sound that signaled the start of afternoon classes. As I left the faculty office with my eyes on the ground, I heard the sound of footsteps approaching from behind me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Guess it’s finally time. Huh, Sae-chan,” said Chie.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Chie?” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ve been like this since this morning,” she said. “I take it you couldn’t sleep last night because you were thinking about the special exam?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I dismissed her obvious and cheap provocation, letting it wash over me like water off a duck’s back. Actually, it would probably be more correct to say that I simply couldn’t muster an answer to it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My current class has nothing to do with me,” I told her. “It doesn’t matter if my students pass this exam easily or not.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm? It doesn’t look like you really believe that yourself though.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I started to walk away. As I turned my back to her, Chie said something else. She made no effort to hide the resentment in her voice.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, whatever. Don’t forget that you don’t </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">deserve</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> to aim for Class A, Sae-chan.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Throughout that whole exchange, I was unable to lift my face at all.</span></p>

<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.09em;">4.2</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T WAS SEPTEMBER 17</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> shortly after our lunch break. Less than three weeks after our summer vacation ended, the next special exam was here. When I came back to the classroom roughly five minutes before the exam was due to start, there was already an adult standing by, quietly watching the students from the back of the room. I was a little surprised to find that I wasn’t assigned to my usual seat. I had been placed in another seat just for the duration of this specific exam. Perhaps that was to ensure even stricter adherence to the rules.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Interestingly enough, I was seated by the window at the very back of the classroom, where I used to sit in my first year here. As for the rest of the students, they…appeared to be spread about randomly, with no relation to where they were seated this year or last. I guess that meant it was only a coincidence that I was assigned in the same spot I used to sit in. When I looked over at Horikita, already in her seat, I saw that she was still placed in the front row. She was only one seat over from her usual spot.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Satou was seated to my right, and Onizuka was seated in front of me. The students then began to arrive, one after another.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Starting now, we would be undertaking the “Unanimous Special Exam.” We would simply choose from the options available for the five issues presented to us and repeat the voting process over and over until we reached a unanimous decision on each issue. Nothing more and nothing less. Simple. While there was little of anything of note for this particular special exam, it was also true that there was little we </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">could</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> do in order to prepare for&#160;it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There were only a few preparations we could make. We promised to split our votes in the first round regardless of the contents of the issues we were voting on, because communication wasn’t allowed prior to the first round of voting. We would also pay careful attention to the time limit when it was time to vote. Thirdly, we decided in advance whom we would follow in the event that a dispute arose over the choices and votes were split. That was about it for us, and about all that any class could do to manage the situation. That was probably the reason that there wasn’t really any kind of heavy, oppressive air hanging over our class.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">On top of that, this was an exam all participants could manage quite easily. All we had to do was make a choice and press a button for whatever we wanted to vote for. As&#160;a result, the class was more relaxed. Of course, there were still some feelings of nervousness over this particular special exam… </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Our tablets were covered with a protective film to prevent any prying eyes from getting a look at what was on the screen. Even if a person in a neighboring seat did try to sneak a peek, they wouldn’t be able to spy on you. We weren’t allowed to get up from our seats during voting either, so it was impossible for us to figure out exactly who was voting for what based on sight. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if you could see someone else’s voting results through some other means, or even just by accident, whether anyone would actually believe you if you tried to say anything was another matter entirely. It was impossible to make a fuss about who voted for what since we were forbidden from stealing glances in the first place. We had no other choice but to take this special exam as it was, head-on. It also looked like the tablets placed on our desks had been turned off, and we were forbidden from turning them on without permission. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heya!” Ike called to Shinohara. “So, like, if we pass this entire thing in an hour or two, let’s head over to Keyaki Mall.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, sure, I’d like to go,” she said, “but we’re supposed to be doing self-study in the dorms after, right? So, how about we go in the evening?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike and Shinohara, now dating and much closer with each other than they were before, were talking about what they were going to do after class. Would we really be able to pass this special exam so easily? Maybe. I&#160;wondered, though, just how many of the students here right now understood that there was a chance this exam could become fraught with difficulties, depending on the conditions. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The issue was that voting was anonymous. It was impossible to know who voted for what during the exam. Or ever, actually. Complete anonymity. In this particular exam, everything came down to how big an impact that factor would have. At any rate, the time limit for this special exam was a long five hours, from one o’clock to six o’clock in the afternoon. Put simply, that meant we could spend no more than one hour on each issue. It wouldn’t be surprising if we completed this special exam in one or two hours then, like Ike had said earlier.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If we managed to finish within the time limit, we would get a quick and easy fifty Class Points. On the other hand, in the event that we failed to pass the exam within those five hours, we would be penalized by 300 Class Points. That meant coming to a unanimous decision on all five issues was an absolute necessity. Still, in light of the mechanics of this exam, you could say that the scant rewards and heavy penalties were acceptable. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I sat in my spot in the corner of the classroom, I noticed that about half of the seats were filled now. Chabashira, the facilitator of this special exam, stood over by the podium, while the teacher assigned to monitor both us and her was stationed in the back of the classroom.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“As you have already been informed, we are now going to collect all communication devices,” announced Chabashira.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There were restrictions on the bags that we could bring into the room, and the school had arranged to monitor us from both the front and the back of the classroom in order to prevent anyone from stealing glances at other students’ tablets. They were being much more thorough than was strictly necessary. To me, that was proof positive of how much they were trying to prevent people from finding out who had voted for what. It might have seemed harsh, but it was the right call for them to make. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In order to ensure that the students’ true, genuine feelings were being reflected in the voting, total anonymity had to be assured. If there were opportunities for students to get a look at what others were doing, then the chance that they would succumb to peer pressure would increase. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">For example, let’s say everyone went with option alpha. Even though you really wanted to go with option beta, maybe you voted for option alpha, because that’s what everyone else did. That’s what the school wanted to avoid happening.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They were placing a lot of importance on students’ respective wills, which was the purpose of this special exam. But still, from the perspective of the students, we wanted the vote to come to a unanimous decision anyway, whether it was coming from peer pressure or not. In that sense, the measures the school was taking weren’t to our advantage. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In any case, there wasn’t any room for fraud. Whatever issues we were going to face, we had to come to a unanimous decision on it. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, Airi. You decided that you were going to say something, didn’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Hm?</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> When I drew my gaze back from the window to see what was happening inside the classroom, I saw Haruka pushing Airi from behind.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“U-um, excuse me, Kiyotaka-kun…!” Airi stammered. “I… If you don’t mind, would you… Would you mind, um, give me a minute, after school today? Maybe?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Haruka, standing beside her, nodded several times. She then looked at me with an appeal in her eyes, as if to say, “</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">You understand that you’ve gotta respond to her efforts, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, I wanted to…to talk to you a bit. About the cultural festival,” added Airi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, that’s what this is about,” I said. “I figured I’d need to speak to you about that in person anyway, so I don’t mind. Sure.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Th-thank you!” she squeaked. “W-well, okay then, later!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Airi quickly shot away from me like she was trying to run away. She proceeded to take her seat, far away from mine, with her back facing me. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She managed to get a grip on things, somehow,” said Haruka. “She’s still not over her heartbreak yet, but she is trying her best to face forward.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t mention it when Airi was standing in front of me earlier, but she was really struggling to make eye contact with me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Still, now we’ll have to see if she really wants to go for the idea. It’s all going to come down to how much effort you put in, Kiyopon.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yousuke, I know you don’t want to suspect anyone, but since you have such a wide circle of friends, both guys and girls, surely there’s </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">someone</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> who comes to mind, right?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There isn’t,” he replied after a pause. “That’s not a lie. I&#160;really can’t think of anyone.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see… In that case, what about you, Kushida?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though I was suddenly addressing her, Kushida didn’t appear out of sorts at all. Rather, Horikita turned to me, somewhat shaken and upset, almost as if she were silently asking me, “</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">What are you going to say?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who do you think is voting in favor?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm… I’m sorry, Ayanokouji-kun,” she replied. “Like Hirata-kun, there isn’t anyone who comes to mind for me either.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know the class best out of anyone, Kushida,” I told her. “I thought you might know a little about which students are feeling dissatisfied. Everyone knows that you care about the class more than anyone, and you’re always there to offer friendly advice. I want you to try and think hard.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After I had said those words, the class’s eyes turned to Kushida with looks full of anticipation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“U-um… I don’t think…anyone is really coming to mind,” she said. “But if I think of anything, I’ll be sure to let you know.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thanks. We’re counting on you. I have a feeling that people like you and Yousuke are indispensable for this special exam.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Without everyone’s combined efforts, it would be difficult to break through the opposition in this issue. However, those discussions proved to be in vain, as the results of the sixth round of voting showed…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Round 6 Voting Results:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> For: 1 Vote, Against: 38 Votes</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The results hadn’t changed. We discussed it again and again.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Round 7 Voting Results:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> For: 1 Vote, Against: 38 Votes</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Round 8 Voting Results:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> For: 1 Vote, Against: 38 Votes</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There still wasn’t any change in the results. Our conversations were marked by more and more silence. Now, we were about to enter into the eighth interval period. A&#160;little over an hour had passed since we started this issue. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">CLACK!</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With a loud rattling sound, Chabashira suddenly toppled over, losing her balance. She managed to prevent herself from falling over completely by holding her arms out against the podium. In the position she was in, it was like she was bowing before us. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Huff… Huff</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">…” As our discussion continued, Chabashira, who had been standing up at the podium this whole time, started breathing heavily. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“S-Sensei?!” shouted Yousuke, worried.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I’m all right…” she replied, adjusting her posture, as though she were trying to rouse herself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The students stared at Chabashira with wide, vacant eyes, wondering what she was thinking. Eventually, she exhaled deeply, sounding as though she had finally come to a decision about something.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We teachers are not allowed to guide students toward any particular choice,” she said. “So, naturally, I won’t be doing anything of the sort. However, may I tell you an old story? Of course, by doing so, it means I’ll be taking away your precious time. But if you don’t mind, I’ll share it with you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Chabashira-sensei,” the monitor interjected. “While teachers are not banned from making statements, if you violate the rules, you will not resolve this matter with a simple apology. If it’s determined that you are trying to induce a decision, to protect the class, then…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, I understand. I am prepared to receive punishment should the school see what I say as an intentional effort to guide the class into making a certain choice.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Now that Chabashira had said she understood, the monitor had no choice but to fall silent. It was a totally unexpected proposal from Chabashira, someone who never meddled in special exams as a matter of course. This could be seen as a single ray of light being shone upon us while we were stuck in this standstill.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We are really struggling with this current situation,” said Horikita. “As long as what you say doesn’t influence what option we’ll choose, please share your story with us, Sensei.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita figured that if what Chabashira said could offer some way of breaking through this situation, we should welcome the opportunity. Of course, if I was going to be honest, I would’ve liked to see momentum driving us to oppose the issue, but while we were under the watchful eyes of the monitor, direct expressions had to be avoided.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I also attended the Advanced Nurturing High School,” Chabashira told us. “And when I was a student here, I took this same special exam.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita and the rest of her classmates were surprised. This was the first time they had heard about this.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sensei, you took the Unanimous Special Exam too…?” asked Yousuke.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s right. There were five issues. Some of the issues back then were slightly different from the ones you’ve voted on, but the final issue, the one you’re facing now, is the exact same as the one I experienced. Word for word. Either you can get Class Points in exchange for expelling a student, or you can protect your classmates and not gain any Class Points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The students all turned their attention to Chabashira’s story. They hung onto her words as she told them how she experienced this same special exam.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s one thing for certain,” she said. “You have to give it your all with no regrets. Whatever choice you decide to make, whether </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> or </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, or to let time run out… Seek a way forward that will leave you with no regrets about the outcome. There’s still time left.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">All of the students listened intently as Chabashira spoke to them with real, genuine feeling for the very first time. She wasn’t guiding the students toward a particular option, nor was she presenting us with a concrete solution. It was simply clear advice, just barely within the lines of what she was allowed to give us, as a teacher. The other teacher listening from the back of the classroom didn’t announce that Chabashira had violated any rules and simply listened quietly until the end. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t know whether this would change the outcome or not, but those were most definitely words that would help students face this special exam. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even with the support from Chabashira, though, it wouldn’t be a good idea to waste the time we had left in this interval period. Horikita kept struggling to increase her chances, even if only by a single percent.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The time when we’ll need to make up our mind is coming… But before we get to that point, please let me say something to you, just one more time. I am not your enemy… I am on your side,” said Horikita, addressing the person who voted in favor.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was sure that the name of this person had likely crossed Horikita’s mind many times over. That person’s face, their voice, their eyes, their breathing. Horikita was trying her best to persuade herself into not revealing to everyone else who this specific person was. I was sure she must have been telling herself that over and over. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I personally thought she should just come out with it and say that person’s name, but the reason Horikita didn’t do that was because she truly, genuinely wanted to be on this person’s side. Horikita’s appeal was something akin to a tragic, grief-stricken cry. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But in response to that, the ninth round of voting had come. And the results were…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Round 9 Voting Results:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> For: 1 Vote, Against: 38 Votes</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That one vote in favor hadn’t been swayed after all. It&#160;was just one person. One, single student who seemed to be clinging to those 100 points for as long as it took. Well, it was more like…they were clinging to the right to have someone expelled. That was the real truth that only I, or perhaps just Horikita and I, knew.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was safe to assume that this certain someone was going to continue voting in favor of the issue without exception. At the same time, there was no objective method to confirm whether that person was opposed to what was happening right now or not. Horikita said that if time were to run out, she would have no choice but to give a name. However, in reality, no matter how many times the vote was repeated, Horikita still hadn’t said it. She was likely thinking, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you actually against the idea of this?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita knew that there wasn’t actually any use in asking such a question. Rather, the moment that Horikita uttered that person’s name, she would lose everything going forward. Even though we still had a bit of a grace period, the time limit was approaching us. We had about two hours remaining until our deadline to make the choice.</span></p>
