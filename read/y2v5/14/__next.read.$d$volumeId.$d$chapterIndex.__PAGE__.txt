<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It wasn’t any wonder why he would want to voice his doubts. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I won’t be raising my hand. But do not worry—I am voting against the issue,” Kouenji said in response to Yousuke’s anxiety-filled look.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even for Sudou, there was no other option but to keep going back to Kouenji. Kushida, Onodera, Okiya, and Mori as well. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Eventually, Horikita came to me, the very last person, seated at the far back of the classroom near the door. There was a mixture of impatience and anxiety in her eyes, but they still held a fiery passion.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And what about you, Ayanokouji-kun?” she asked.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ten minutes are almost up,” said Chabashira. “Return to your seat, Horikita. The vote is about to begin.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Round 11 Voting Results:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> For: 1 Vote, Against: 38 Votes</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The results hadn’t changed at all. There wasn’t anything else to say about it. The same results were being shown to us over, and over, and over.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Up until this point, the students had been operating on the assumption that the person persistently voting in favor would eventually back down. Anyway, the person voting </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">would ultimately be afraid of letting time run out. Horikita had been fighting hard to overcome this final issue for the past three hours. It wasn’t like her strategies were overly optimistic. What was the fundamental reason?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Because persuasion, negotiation, and every other kind of action would have been meaningless. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> ≥ </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> &gt; Time Out</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> &gt; Time Out &gt; </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This special exam had devolved into chaos because a student who wasn’t aligned with the rest of the class was thrown into the mix. If we continued to stubbornly press on ahead, all that awaited us was timing out. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If that was the case, what should we do? There was only one thing that we could do in the two hours that we had remaining.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Unanimous approval.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita, I voted in favor just now, in the fourteenth round,” I told her.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There is no certain future anywhere. So, we have to choose the best option we can.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No matter how many arguments I strung together, Haruka wasn’t hearing it, even if it seemed like she did. However, Airi </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">was</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, and that was the important part.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’ll be okay. It will all be okay, Airi. All right? I will keep voting opposed to your expulsion, definitely. No matter who else votes in favor, I’ll—!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Everyone… Please…vote for me…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Airi spoke so softly, it was like her voice would fade into the background, but it was loud enough that everyone could hear her. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Haruka grabbed both of Airi’s arms, in a frantic display of resistance. “I hate this,” she said. “I absolutely hate this… We were having so much fun together just until yesterday, and yet…! Even this morning was just a typical morning. I met up with Airi, and we came to school. We chatted about stupid stuff, talked about things like the cultural festival, and… And today, after class, we were going to call Kiyopon, and Airi was going to unveil her surprise! You’re taking that away from me!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We had less than ten minutes remaining in the exam. This was, essentially, the final vote. No one could easily vote </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> this time, no matter who was going to be expelled. That was the weight this final voting session had. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Airi shook her head from side to side and did not grab hold of Haruka’s outstretched hand. She was rejecting her friend’s offer to save her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No! No, no, no!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Haruka shouted and screamed, denying what was happening, rejecting it like a child. Every time Haruka protested, Airi tried to express her gratitude to her, but was still trying to persuade her to accept what was happening.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The situation could no longer be changed. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Haruka, understanding everything at last, sat on the ground as if she collapsed right on the spot.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Someone who doesn’t have any ability accepted the situation and came forward,” I said. “We have an obligation to respond to her wishes. It would be easy for you to vote </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Against </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">in the next vote, but even if you did that, Airi likely won’t stay at this school anyway. She’d be torn apart by strong feelings of self-condemnation for having dragged her classmates into this situation, and would leave the school, unable to face forward. Haruka, the only way to save your best friend Airi is for you to vote </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> and make her face forward.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I…!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Airi hugged Haruka while she was still collapsed on the floor. “Thank you, Haruka-chan… Thank you for helping me so, so much, all this time. I couldn’t give you anything in return, but… Please, listen to my final selfish request.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I hate this, Airi… I can’t…” muttered Haruka.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Vote in favor for me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Airi thanked Haruka, gently stroking her hair as Haruka sobbed. Then, she addressed Chabashira, speaking up loud and clear.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m nominating myself. Please vote for me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, after making Haruka get up and return to her seat, Airi returned to her own seat to accept everything that was about to happen. However, even after Chabashira had declared it was time, the vote didn’t end as normal. Even sixty seconds, seventy seconds after the vote started, the period hadn’t ended. Students had a total of ninety seconds of extra time after the sixty-second voting period. That meant that in over seventy seconds, Haruka’s expulsion would be set in stone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The thought, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">if my best friend Airi is going to disappear, then I’ll disappear too,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> may have crossed her mind. If she chose that weak option here, then that would be that. Although the class would suffer from losing another person, we’d still come to a unanimous decision without any issues, because that would mean Haruka’s vote would be eliminated. Now that 100 seconds had passed, Haruka had forty-something seconds remaining. Haruka just kept crying and showed no signs of reaching for her tablet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“HARUKA-CHAN!!!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was anger in Airi’s voice, something I had never heard from her before. It was also the loudest she had ever shouted. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Haruka quickly looked up in surprise, like someone had slapped her on the back. Airi looked at her crying face, smiled, and nodded. If Haruka didn’t come to a decision and vote right now, then it would be denying everything Airi was doing.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Voting has finished,” said Chabashira. “I will now show you the results.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Round 22 Voting Results:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> For: 38 Votes, Against: 0 Votes</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira, who had been watching the entire spectacle, forgot to report the voting results aloud. She simply stared at Airi and Haruka. Airi, with her expulsion now confirmed, just looked straight ahead as though she had accepted what had happened. On the other hand, Haruka was struggling desperately to hold back her tears. She couldn’t protect her friend Airi. Despite her&#160;attempts, she couldn’t hide her sobbing from the now speechless class.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um… Um, Chabashira-sensei,” said the monitor. </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Ahem</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. Please continue.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even the monitor, who had been silent and composed throughout, only uttering the bare minimum necessary to give reminders and warnings, seemed to have forgotten to prompt Chabashira to signal to the students that it was the end of the special exam.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Regarding the matter of the expulsion of Sakura Airi…the final issue is now complete with the class having come to a unanimous decision in favor,” Chabashira said. “The decision has been validated and you will be awarded 100 Class Points. Just as a reminder, there is only one way for you to cancel her expulsion. If you had 20 million Private Points at this moment in time and you used them to…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira was about to continue providing us with the explanation as she was obligated to do, but she suddenly stopped mid-sentence.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I suppose no further explanation is necessary,” she admitted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hypothetically, even if we were to gather up all the Private Points everyone had, there was no way we’d come close to 20 million.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The other three classes have already finished their special exams, so you will be heading back to the dormitories immediately. As for Sakura, you will remain in the classroom for now, but you will come with me to the faculty room afterward.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although Airi responded to Chabashira quietly, unlike her shouts from earlier, she still answered without hesitation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That is all. Everyone, please get up from your seats and leave the room as instructed,” said Chabashira.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After that announcement, we all got up from our seats, although not all at once. Airi, of course, remained where she was as instructed. Haruka couldn’t seem to stand up. She struggled to get to her feet, but her knees shook, and she couldn’t seem to find her balance well. Her breathing was labored, and she was starting to show symptoms of hyperventilation. Akito, unable to bear the sight, rushed over to her and helped her to her feet, forcing her to stand upright. Nothing good would have come from just leaving her here, after all. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I stepped out into the hallway right away, and my phone was immediately returned to me. Keisei came out after me and called out to me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kiyotaka… I’m not going to tell you that you did the wrong thing. It’s just… Still, I… I don’t know if I can say what </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> did was right, I guess. No, there’s no point in me asking you this anyway… Just forget it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though he still had plenty of things on his mind that he wanted to get out, Keisei turned his back to me and started walking down the hall. Even if I waited around for Haruka and Akito, it would likely be pointless. Things like justifiability were irrelevant. There was no way that they </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">wouldn’t</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> feel something after I had gone ahead and thrown away a precious member of their friend group.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kei approached me. I noticed that she seemed upset and on edge, so I stopped her with a glance. It would probably be better to have Kei settle down quietly right now as well since she was probably grieving over the loss too. There was no need for me to incur hate from others by doing something necessary. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If I remembered correctly, Chabashira mentioned wanting to meet with me after the special exam. I looked at my phone and saw that I had received a message, telling me that we were supposed to meet at six o’clock. That was a while off, so I decided that I should leave, figuring that it would be best if I didn’t stick around for too long. Still, I knew if I headed straight for the entrance, I would likely bump into Keisei and other students… In any case, since I had an appointment with Chabashira, I supposed I’d just wander around the less-populated parts of campus.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had known there was someone following me, but they didn’t call out to me until there was no one else around.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s up?” I asked. “I would’ve thought you’d be talking with Kushida.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, not really. I just urged her not to do anything desperate, that’s all,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kushida had many friends before the exam, but no one went to talk to her after it. It made sense—as people would’ve found her difficult to approach right after witnessing her intense true nature.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry.” Horikita’s hair was a little longer now than it used to be, and it swayed when she bowed deeply. “This special exam… I… I wasn’t good enough…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not good enough? You did the best you could though, didn’t you?” I asked. “Besides, this year’s exam was a much, much more difficult battle than last year’s In-Class Vote.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Still, no matter how difficult it was, you ended up with such a big burden on your shoulders… You took on all the responsibility, which should have been broken up and dispersed.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was inevitable that someone would be expelled. That was precisely why Horikita wanted to illustrate her intentions.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was the one who told you to be quiet,” I assured her. “It’s fine.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, it’s not fine. It left a huge scar on your treasured friend group. It’s very… I can’t imagine that it’s ever going to recover.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s all right. If anything, there might come a day when they’ll see this as a good thing.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If Horikita had gotten involved, then the blame certainly would have been divided equally between the two of us. I hadn’t wanted that to happen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A good thing…? Whatever do you mean by that?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nothing, don’t worry about it,” I replied. “It’s trivial.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, I didn’t expect Horikita to accept that and immediately change her mind about things, but I also didn’t want this special exam to become something that would drag her down in the future.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Think positively,” I added. “We earned 100 precious Class Points, which will help us move up to Class A. We cannot foolishly dismiss those points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But…we lost Sakura-san,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s a positive, because it raised the average value of the class as a result. It was the perfect conclusion.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Stop. You don’t need to force it, trying to act cruel.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Force it?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I thought about denying it, but I ended up getting caught on one thing she said, deliberately repeating it back to her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s right. Maybe you’re trying to push away painful feelings,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kiyotaka-kun!” called a kind, familiar voice, from far down at the end of the hallway.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita, startled by the voice, turned around. She was surprised to see who was approaching us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait… Sakura…-san?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Airi, who had no stamina to speak of, was completely out of breath. She walked over to us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I’ll be going now…” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, that’s probably a good idea,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just as Horikita walked past Airi, she tried to say something to her, but she hesitated, and ultimately said nothing. She probably couldn’t think of the right words for when someone was about to leave.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I really want to show you while I have this last chance, Kiyotaka-kun… What do you think?” said Airi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just before we took the final vote, Haruka mentioned that Airi wanted to unveil something. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">This was it, huh?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You look completely different,” I said. “No wonder Horikita didn’t recognize you for a minute.” “I guess… I was a little late in working up the courage to do it though… He</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">he.” Airi stood there with her glasses off and her hair done up in a fashionable style. She smiled shyly. “I know this probably isn’t for me to say, but… Please take care of Haruka-chan.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Bye-bye… Kiyotaka-kun.” Airi showed me an extraordinary smile, the likes of which I had never seen before.</span></p>

<div style="page-break-after: always;"></div>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She then turned away from me and started to walk away. However, her steps gradually slowed town, and it seemed like she was about to come to a full stop. Even so, she desperately kept putting one foot in front of the other, pushing ahead without looking back. I could hear her wordless voice carrying down the hallway—the sounds of her sniffling and desperately fighting back tears.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The sight reminded me of something I often saw in the past. The failures would always look back on their miserable situation and be filled with regret, even though it was too late for them. It was just as true here in this school as it was in the White Room.</span></p>
