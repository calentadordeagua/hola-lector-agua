  <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_10"><span>Chapter 1: Hoshinomiya Chie’s Soliloquy</span></p>

  <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break"><span>&#160;</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">O ME, SAE-CHAN </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">is my best friend.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">To me, Sae-chan is my rival.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">It might seem like a contradiction on the face of it, but, surprisingly enough, that’s the kind of relationship we have. Besides, I don’t think it’s that unusual to have two emotions running in parallel. Despite how it looks, I have quite a few friends. Friends from elementary school and junior high, friends I met in high school and college, and friends I came to know when I became a working adult. However, Sae-chan’s the only person with whom I have an ongoing relationship, with whom I can talk honestly, about how I really feel. I don’t know exactly what she thinks about it, though.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Even if I lose to someone else, I cannot lose to Sae-chan. The days that we spent together in the same class, aiming for Class A together, instilled such feelings in me. Originally, Sae-chan did not want to be a teacher. But that day, the day that we were made to understand that we wouldn’t be able to graduate from Class A, I knew for certain that Sae-chan wanted to become a teacher to try to go for Class A in a different way. That’s why I decided to aim to become a teacher too.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Frankly, this profession is a far cry from what I wanted to do. Day after day, I get made fun of and treated with contempt by arrogant students, and I can’t expect much of a paycheck either. Even so, I still became a teacher.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I have only one goal: to keep Sae-chan from getting any hope of achieving her goal of graduating from Class A.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I mean, it’s obvious why, right?</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">That day, we couldn’t graduate from Class A anymore because of Sae-chan’s trivial romance. Had it not been for that, I wouldn’t have become a teacher and would have led a more glamorous life. And yet, Sae-chan is the only one to get her students to graduate from Class A?</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">And like that, she just squares up her past, with a sense of self-satisfaction?</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I can’t allow that.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Not when I’m still held prisoner by the past, even now.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">So, as long as my eyes are on her, I will never let her win.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">If my class loses in the End-of-Year Special Exam…</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">If Sae-chan’s class ends up becoming Class A, then…</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">In the worst-case scenario, I will have to do whatever it takes to put a stop to it.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I don’t care if I’m branded as an unfit teacher.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I don’t even mind if I’m ousted from my teaching position.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I am definitely going to put a stop to it, even if I have to force my way through, to take her down with me.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">That’s what I had vowed to myself, deep down.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">The special exam for the end of the second year will begin soon.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Depending on whether we win or lose this time, the fate of my class, which has been pushed to the brink, will be decided.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">For both me and my students, an important battle that we absolutely cannot lose is about to begin.</span></p>
