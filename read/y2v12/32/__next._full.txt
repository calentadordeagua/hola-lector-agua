  <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_31"><span><span style=" font-size: 0.86em;">9.1</span></span></p>

  <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break"><span>&#160;</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Discussion No. 1</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Participants</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Class 2-A</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yanagibashi Motofumi, Ishida Yuusuke, Shimazaki Ikkei, Toba Shigeru, Tamiya Emi, Morishita Ai, Takanashi Kou</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Class 2-C</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki Daiichi, Kaneda Satoru, Komiya Kyougo, Nakaizumi Shouhei, Suminokura Mami, Takarajima Miko, Hatate Kaoru</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">The curtains rose on the first discussion, which would be an important match between generals. Moreover, the right to send in a traitor had been exercised immediately. At this point, one traitor was mixed in among the students from Ryuuen’s Class C. In a situation where everyone seemed hesitant to speak up first, it was none other than Morishita Ai from Class A who made the first move.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Ishizaki Daiichi. First, could I ask you to prove, with certainty, that you are not a model student?” asked Morishita.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Huh? Wha—m-me?! Why me, all of the sudden?!” he wailed.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Because suspicious people are called upon,” replied Morishita. “It is what police officers and private detectives do as well.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">The traitor who had been sent in should have been a concern as well, but Morishita didn’t seem to make any mention of that whatsoever. As if to go with the flow, all the eyes in the room followed Morishita to Ishizaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“No way, dude… Uh, I mean, I ain’t the model student!” exclaimed Ishizaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Then, could I ask you to prove it?” replied Morishita.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Aww dude, that’s legit impossible though! How the hell could I even prove it?!” </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“How about this: if you are the model student, you promise that you’ll bite your tongue and die later. Well?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“H-huh?! Stop sayin’ stuff I can’t understand!”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Ishizaki was flummoxed by Morishita’s continued questioning, but Kaneda quickly intervened, without a moment’s delay.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Please wait, Morishita-shi. Ishizaki-shi, you don’t need to answer her. I do not approve of such heavy-handed methods. Let’s talk about something that can actually be resolved in this discussion. Now, if we follow the established practices of police officers and private detectives, then I would suspect the person who spoke first. I would ask that </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">you</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> prove with certainty that </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">you</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> are not the model student,” said Kaneda.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Saying this, Kaneda shifted his glasses, and all of the attention that had been focused on Ishizaki shifted onto Morishita.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“There is no way to prove anything with certainty under the rules of this discussion, though,” replied Morishita with a look of exasperation on her face, like she was dealing with an idiot, even though she herself had been pressing Ishizaki for definitive proof just moments ago.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“In that case, why were you trying to force an unreasonable demand onto Ishizaki-shi?” asked Kaneda.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Because he had a stupid look on his face and seemed like he would give himself up.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Who’s gotta stupid look on his face?!” shouted Ishizaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Please calm yourself,” Kaneda said. “To defeat Sakayanagi-shi, it is necessary for us to use the appropriate means. Conversely, defeating Ryuuen-shi will be similarly difficult. Morishita-shi is provoking you in order to make sure her class leader wins. If you want to assist Ryuuen-shi, who I imagine is fighting on the other side of that camera, then you must remain calm here. If you find yourself swayed by her words, then you’ll be playing right into our opponent’s hands.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Kaneda had succeeded in pacifying the indignant Ishizaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Y’know, I’ve been watching other people’s discussions for a while now, and I’ve noticed something. It seems like the model students tend to look each other in the eyes. Komiya-kun and Takanashi-san were looking at each other right before the discussion started, weren’t they?” said Class A’s Tamiya, openly suspecting the other students with a skeptical gaze.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Oh, yeah, you’re right. I felt like that was suspicious too,” replied Nakaizumi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Perhaps Nakaizumi was on the same page as Tamiya, because he nodded several times in affirmation as he said that. Even though Class A and Class C were enemies, their back-and-forth didn’t give that impression.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Right? And didn’t Komiya-kun look especially relieved when everyone’s attention was focused on Ishizaki-kun?” added Tamiya.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Tamiya was making a strong case to those around her that those two, Komiya and Takanashi, were the model students. Was that genuinely what she thought, or was it something to divert attention away from herself? Even though she was new to the discussion, she had learned how to handle things by watching as a bystander thus far. The superior students made full use of the skills they had developed through those experiences to advance the conversation. While the presence of the traitor was a disadvantageous element in the battle between representatives, it was a great boon to the traitor and their classmates. That was precisely why neither class’s participants forcefully pursued the issue.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>

  <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break"><span>9.2</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HE DISCUSSION BEGAN </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">with a surprise attack from Morishita, and then the time came for nominations by the representatives as the five-minute mark approached.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“This debate’s gotten pretty wild right from the start, huh?” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It would seem so,” replied Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">They both casually offered their impressions of the discussion.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Now then…what do you think, Ryuuen-kun? There were a few things that appeared to be clues,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Just as she had exercised her right to send in a traitor preemptively, Sakayanagi had made the first move here as well. Some bits of information that seemed like they could be used as a stepping stone had quickly popped up in the earlier discussion. There was Komiya and Takanashi, who Tamiya had claimed exchanged looks with one another. Then there was Tamiya, who had spoken up about the exchange of glances, and Nakaizumi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">It was quite possible that one of those two pairs could have been a pair of model students. However, there was no guarantee of that. Without further clues, it would be quite risky to start attacking in the first round. However, if Ryuuen chose to pass at this point, information would actually be disclosed to Sakayanagi by the end of the round, thanks to the traitor’s effect. To prevent that, he needed to exercise his right to a dialogue to get rid of the traitor. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">In addition, Ryuuen absolutely needed to avoid picking the traitor if he did push ahead and make a nomination. If he made that mistake in nominating, he would lose two Life Points, and on top of that, he would restore Sakayanagi’s right to send in a traitor. If that happened, it would inevitably become more difficult for him to nominate students from his own class. Without uttering a word, Ryuuen calmly thought back on the discussion that had just taken place. Who was lying, and who was telling the truth? Though he had temporarily stepped down from his position, for the past two years, he had reigned as king of his class. Now, his true worth was being tested. After using the entire time and entering his nomination, it was finally time for the answer to that question.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">“Because Ryuuen-kun has nominated erroneously, he will lose one Life Point.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">While Sakayanagi had chosen to pass, Ryuuen had gone ahead and made a nomination. Ryuuen, knowing the risks, had determined that Tamiya was a model student. However, he didn’t think of this result as a serious blow, but as a necessary expense. As long as his opponent was coming at him while using the traitor, he had to keep moving forward.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Unfortunately, it would appear that you chose incorrectly,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Ain’t a big problem. But y’know, for all the big talk you’ve been makin’, you’re kinda slow in makin’ a move, huh? Goin’ second, then?” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Heh heh. Yes, that might be so,” she replied. “Slow and steady wins the race, as they say.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Not denying it, Sakayanagi nodded her head honestly. For her part, there was no need to rush. In fact, she thought that it would be a waste for her to name a student with a position so quickly. In that sense, she didn’t really want the discussion to progress prematurely. However, in the first round, two pairs of students unexpectedly emerged as possible model students. Supposing even if one of the model students were to be nominated at this time, the other would inevitably be targeted in the next round.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">If that happened, then a tie would be probable. Considering that she would end up losing her right to send in a traitor, she decided that a nomination at an early stage wouldn’t strike a good balance. On the other hand, Ryuuen wanted to either find the traitor or push through the discussion fast and reset it. Also, he was going to be strongly tested on who he’d engage in dialogue and how he would exercise it. It was only natural that Sakayanagi would choose to wait, with the intention of making Ryuuen go through more instances of having to think, to make him suffer.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">“Ryuuen-kun will now be leaving the room temporarily to engage in dialogue.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Ryuuen, who had chosen to engage in dialogue in no time at all, immediately moved to take action to smoke out the traitor.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I hope your war efforts prove fruitful,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">If Ryuuen made an error in his judgment here, it was possible he’d lose even more Life Points. The person that Sakayanagi had chosen to be a traitor was Takarajima Miko. And the person that Ryuuen chose to engage in dialogue was Nakaizumi. Which meant that, at this point, no matter how Ryuuen tried to suss out the traitor in this round, he would not be successful. Only about two minutes after getting up out of his seat and leaving the room, Ryuuen returned.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Ryuuen had asked one simple question: “</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">Are you the traitor?”</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> That direct question was all he needed. If you lied to Ryuuen, you would face the risk of immediate expulsion. There were no Private Points or Class Points worth taking risks to protect. Since Ryuuen had determined that he wasn’t the traitor, he didn’t lose any Life Points and remained at nine. One person was supposed to have been ejected from the room thanks to a model student’s nomination, but it was reported that the teacher had successfully blocked the nomination, and so none of the students left.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">After that, Sakayanagi was given the casting information for just one person, thanks to the traitor still being in the discussion. The information displayed on Sakayanagi’s tablet showed that Morishita Ai was a graduate. The discussion seemed to be progressing well, and her identity being revealed to Sakayanagi was a positive. If the capable Morishita continued to wield her power carelessly, the discussion could develop quickly, so a prompt nomination would be required. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">When the second round began, Morishita began questioning once again. Even though it was impossible to tell over the monitor who was being investigated, the search for a new position holder had begun. When the time came for the representatives to make their nominations, Sakayanagi did not hesitate to nominate Morishita as a graduate. Eliminating her on this turn was a definite. Ryuuen had also nominated Morishita, but he had only determined that she wasn’t a model student and avoided unnecessary risks.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">“Morishita-san has been nominated as a position holder by Ryuuen-kun and identified as the graduate by Sakayanagi-san, so Ryuuen-kun loses one Life Point.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Although they had both answered correctly, Ryuuen’s points had slowly but surely been whittled down to eight because he hadn’t been able to figure out Morishita’s exact role. Sakayanagi remained untouched by Class C, the class in which a traitor lurked, and Ryuuen was unable to touch her. This time, Ryuuen had called up Kaneda for dialogue, and he returned as quickly as before. Thanks to the traitor, whom Ryuuen could not eliminate, the fact that Shimazaki was an underclassman was conveyed to Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">At this moment, Sakayanagi briefly adjusted her plans in her mind.   <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">An early declaration of defeat, brought out by his true feelings. He had resisted voicing it at first, but once he finally put it into words, he felt refreshed. It was unmistakable proof that he felt that Sakayanagi really was a cut above the rest.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It would appear that you too have seen the checkmate in this battle,” remarked Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN"><span><img alt="" height="100%" id="INTERIORIMAGES_09" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/y2v12/OEBPS/Images/INTERIORIMAGES_09.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting" id="auto_bookmark_toc_35"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Yeah. I admit it,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You fought well,” Sakayanagi said. “I will honestly admit that you are a capable individual worthy of praise.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">In fact, Sakayanagi was also sorry to see this match ending. She wanted to watch Ryuuen fight a little longer, harboring a feeling that was similar to a parental love. Despite receiving such a declaration of defeat from Ryuuen, Sakayanagi wasn’t careless or proud. That was because she was taking into consideration the fact that he might be playing possum; or, in other words, that he was trying to turn the situation around by feigning inevitable defeat. After seeing the sharp glean in Sakayanagi’s eyes as she shot him a sideways glance, Ryuuen involuntarily blurted something out.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You’re a shrewd chick, all right, who won’t even relax her guard after a surrender,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“But of course. I will not relent in the slightest until I see the results from the school,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">There were about three minutes left until the next discussion, which would be the last. Ryuuen had but one Life Point. The right to send in a traitor also remained.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Tch…” Suddenly, Ryuuen clicked his tongue.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“What are you clicking your tongue for?” asked Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Nah, nothin’,” Ryuuen had unconsciously clicked his tongue when he recalled the day’s events. “Maybe he had predicted that I would lose like this.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You mean Ayanokouji-kun?” asked Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Yeah. He called me up this mornin’, after they explained how the special exam works,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I know that Ayanokouji-kun had been giving you a look, Ryuuen-kun. You were going to the restroom, if I recall,” Sakayanagi said. Naturally, she had gotten a grasp on what had happened, nodding as she thought back to it. “I imagined that there was some exchange between you two.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“That’s when he said somethin’ to me. He said that, if I lose, he wanted me to give you a message, Sakayanagi,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I see. So that was why he called upon you, then,” she replied.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Just as Ayanokouji had foretold, Ryuuen had gotten pushed to the verge of defeat.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Well, let us hear it. What message did he have for me?” asked Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Whether the message was the truth or a lie could be determined by listening to what Ryuuen had to say. That was precisely why Sakayanagi was interested. However, Ryuuen had come back with some unexpected words.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I dunno. All I know for sure is that it’s Hashimoto who has the message,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Hashimoto-kun?” repeated Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“On top of that, he said it’s a message that you can only understand from this special exam,” Ryuuen said. “I dunno whether it’s true or not, though.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">If someone said something like that, there was no way that Sakayanagi </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">wouldn’t</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> be interested.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“If you choose the group that Hashimoto is in for the final discussion, I’ll exercise my right to send in a traitor,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">When this battle had begun, the group that Hashimoto belonged to was the first one that Sakayanagi had eliminated. At this moment, when the battle was about to be decided, Ayanokouji’s message was to lead to a development where she would use Hashimoto. She couldn’t help but smell something fishy going on.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Does this mean you haven’t given up on the match yet?” asked Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“If that’s what you wanna think, go ahead,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Sakayanagi’s instincts were telling her, “</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">Don’t accept this offer.”</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> In this situation, where the odds of her winning were as close to 100 percent as possible, it would be foolish to lower that probability even slightly. However, she didn’t think Ryuuen was lying. It was precisely because she felt that was Ayanokouji’s message that she had grown subconsciously wary. However, at the same time, she desired to know what Ayanokouji’s message was.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“If you had been deliberately holding onto the right to send in a traitor until this moment, then that means you were fighting against me while putting yourself at a disadvantage. I do not like this one bit,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It was a complete failure on my part,” said Ryuuen. “I should’ve used it as soon as I could.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Sakayanagi was sure that Ryuuen had tried to win without using the traitor, but there were moves he could have used before trying to force his way forward. And yet, Sakayanagi was curious about Ayanokouji’s message and the fact that Ryuuen had not exercised his right. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Sakayanagi was never going to use Hashimoto. That was why Ryuuen had seriously regretted what happened. Ryuuen gave a self-deprecating chuckle and flicked his wrist.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“However, I do wonder what he is thinking, sending a message through Hashimoto-kun,” mused Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I ain’t got a clue either. But if I were to speculate, I’d say it’s a setup. The only risk for the traitor is gettin’ expelled, but that’s a possibility if Hashimoto tries to plead innocent,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Ryuuen would nominate Hashimoto as the traitor, and Sakayanagi would call him up in dialogue. And, in the event that Hashimoto went undetected, he could gain a lot of money. On the other hand, if Sakayanagi knew what he was, she could make a decision without hesitation and get Hashimoto expelled.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“He’ll make light of the situation; he’ll think that there is no possible way he’d get chosen as the traitor. He’ll probably keep his mouth shut and pretend not to know anythin’, even if you try to rattle him,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It is certainly true that he will risk expulsion if he goes that route, but that would be difficult, wouldn’t it?” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">The role of the traitor was extremely unfavorable. Once their identity was discovered, there was a high probability that they would confess during the nomination stage. Of course, if the student was being declared as the traitor, but they actually weren’t, the representative would be penalized. But that wouldn’t be a major setback, as the representative would only lose one Life Point. Hashimoto himself would likely consider it highly probable that he would not be chosen as the traitor, but if Ryuuen exercised his right to choose one, he could choose Hashimoto to be sneaky, to try to outwit Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Even if that were not the case, it wouldn’t change the fact that if there was a traitor in the mix, Sakayanagi would consider Hashimoto a target of suspicion and, at the very least, would probably call him up for dialogue.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“He would most definitely confess when I call him up. Since there is nothing for me to fear on my part about making that decision, Hashimoto not admitting to being the traitor would mean he would be expelled from school,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">If Sakayanagi had one Life Point remaining and was backed into a corner, then things might have been different. However, as long as she had five or more points, there was essentially no choice but for her to rule him as the traitor, regardless of whether he was innocent or guilty.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“If you have some faint hopes, it is futile. Regardless of whether Hashimoto-kun is innocent or guilty, I will judge him as such,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I know that. In that case, show me the skills you’re so proud of. If you sweet-talk Hashimoto into thinking that you won’t rule him guilty, then you might be able to take him down together with me,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">At times like this, Sakayanagi wondered, </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">“What if?” </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">What if Ryuuen had entered into a contract with Hashimoto in advance? What if this was a trap set by Ryuuen at an early stage? However, the details of the special exam were only revealed this morning. At that stage, the representatives and participants were completely isolated, and they didn’t even have a small window of opportunity to exchange information. Which meant that Ryuuen had no way to draw up a contract with Hashimoto, like a promise to save him in the unlikely event that he was expelled for stubbornly lying through his teeth about not being the traitor while he was, in fact, the traitor.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">“Actually, wait,”</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> thought Sakayanagi. Deliberately choosing not to exclude such a possibility from her mind, Sakayanagi considered what the real “what if” might be. What if all of the rules of the special exam had been leaked in advance? Putting aside whether Ryuuen had offered such a contract to Hashimoto. No, that was absolutely impossible. Regardless of whether Hashimoto were to confess or not, Sakayanagi could simply make the judgment that he was the traitor.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">If she thought nothing of it, and simply chose to go with that option, there wouldn’t be any surprise victory from behind. After all, this was not caused by Ryuuen, but by Ayanokouji, after all.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Let us see if there really is a message from Ayanokouji-kun, shall we?” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Sakayanagi chose Hashimoto’s group. Then, Ryuuen picked a group at random and exercised his right to send in a traitor. Both of them were acting in accordance with Ayanokouji’s message, which was not a lie at all. However, when the discussion began, Ryuuen closed his eyes and didn’t even look at the monitor.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It is rather kind of you to go out of your way to make it so clear that you are not playing a trick,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Heh, I don’t exactly hate the idea of putting up useless resistance, but I can’t forgive myself for fallin’ for Ayanokouji’s coaxing,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">This time specifically, Ryuuen had personally declared to Ayanokouji that he was going to screw Sakayanagi over using his own talents. If that didn’t happen, then this match was already over. Sakayanagi watched the discussion, just to be safe. Although the information that could be gained in one round was limited, there were several people whom she sensed may be a position holder. Then, while at the top of her game, she entered the time for dialogue. With cane in hand, Sakayanagi slowly exited the classroom. Watching Sakayanagi go, Ryuuen looked up at the ceiling and brought his fist down on his knee with all of his strength. He regretted that he had let the lead slip away from him, from start to finish, and that he was unable to catch up to Sakayanagi.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Damn it…” he huffed.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">He didn’t want it to end here. That meant that his growth would end up stopping here. But that wish would no longer come true.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Ryuuen Kakeru lost.</span></p>
