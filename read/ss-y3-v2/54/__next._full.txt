A duty as an observer.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">To meet the expectations of Shirogane-sensei, I had continued to do the same thing every single day.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">That day, when I was called into that room just before graduation.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">My mission began ever since the day I learnt about you, and yet&#x2026;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;&apos;Won&apos;t do&apos;?
