To think he&#x2019;d grasp the true principle of this special move in an instant...</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Now that you mention it, that is true indeed. I shall praise you, Ayanokouji Kiyotaka.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">However, I did not reveal this special move merely for the sake of showing it off.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">It was to give a push to the back of Yamamura Miki, who was too nervous to move forward.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">As though to add the finishing touch, I gave her cheek a little pinch.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Wh-What!?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;How about it, Yamamura Miki? Don&apos;t you feel a little less tense now?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">She should now be able to reflect on her own situation for a moment, and with this, move forward.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">And so, today once again, I have helped those around me.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">What an outstanding person.
