I&apos;m hesitant to ask her directly, so I was in the middle of trying to find some kind of lead.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Why is the student council president investigating the one who betrayed her class?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;While it isn&apos;t a frequent occurrence at this school, now that a class transfer happened, it must be dealt with. I would like you to resolve this as quickly as possible.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">It wasn&apos;t as if I wanted to take my time either, but if it were that simple, I wouldn&apos;t be having such a hard time.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;For now, I don&apos;t have any information regarding Ayanokouji-senpai, but I have someone in mind who might know about his past.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Really? It&apos;s to be expected that they&apos;ll try to figure out how you found your way to them. You can handle that with pride befitting Class A and the student council president, correct?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">He asked for confirmation, deliberately applying pressure as if to make a point.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Perhaps, from Ishigami-kun&apos;s perspective, this person was just that troublesome to deal with.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">No, that was something I&apos;d known from the very beginning.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">If this was a person who knew Ayanokouji-kun&apos;s past, it stood to reason that they would be someone of considerable ability.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;I&apos;ll do my best. For now, all I can do is ask you to trust my resolve.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I answered him that way, fully intending to refuse if he asked me to wager something or enter a contract.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Very well. On the chance that Nanase is unrelated, I&apos;ll also check if Amasawa or anyone else seems to know about Ayanokouji-senpai. Student Council President Horikita, you just have to keep your promise to me and move forward with your own investigation as you please.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Yes, thank you. Even if I&apos;m pressed for information, I&apos;ll protect you at all costs.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;I am hoping for positive results. Well then.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">With those words, Ishigami-kun turned his back on me and started walking in the opposite direction of the entrance.
