<h1 id="prologue" class="chapter-title">Prologue: Miki Yamamura’s Monologue</h1>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">I REALIZED THAT I was alone. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">It wasn't that anyone particularly disliked me. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">I just didn't catch anyone's eye. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">I was inconspicuous, lacking presence. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Before anyone could like me, they couldn't even hate me. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">That was why I was always alone. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">It was the same in kindergarten, elementary school, and junior high. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">I didn't have any friends, and I spent a lot of time by myself. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">I couldn't develop the ability to talk to people, so I remained invisible. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Even after becoming a high school student, nothing changed. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">But I thought that was okay. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">I forced myself to believe that it was my strength… </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">I thought it would be okay to live quietly alone even as an adult. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Even so, I am certainly here. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“. .After all..  I don’t think losing suits Sakayanagi-san...” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Why don't you try telling her? No one has the right to criticize your actions.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> <i class="calibre3">What is this sensation, this emotion that is seeping into my heart? </i> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">I didn't know. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">I just didn't know. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">—Until this day. </p>
