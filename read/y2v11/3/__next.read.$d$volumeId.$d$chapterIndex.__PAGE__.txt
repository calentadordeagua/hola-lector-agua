<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">However, it was hard to believe that Hashimoto was completely unaware of that detail. He spoke without showing any signs of deception, mixing truth and lies in similar proportions. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Even the current situation, the three of us walking together, might not be a mere coincidence. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Hashimoto might’ve wanted to make Sakayanagi indirectly realize that he’d contacted me. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">It might be safe to assume that he had such a plan. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">If he didn't want to be found out, he wouldn't have waited in front of my room where he could’ve been noticed. We both knew each other's contact information, so we could discretely communicate as much as we wanted. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Hashimoto, the traitor, had a purpose in making Sakayanagi aware of the fact that he contacted me, either directly or indirectly. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Of course, only Hashimoto knew the truth, but there were other things that I could deduce. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">The truth and lies that Hashimoto revealed in my room. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">All his actions were tied to his own benefit. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">He wanted to be the only one to benefit. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">He wanted to be the only one to survive. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">He wanted to be the only one to win. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">If a pacifist knew of this, Hashimoto would probably be despised as an evil entity. The more I knew about Hashimoto, the more I sympathized and agreed with him. </p>



<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><a id="p30"></a><img src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/y2v11/index-30_1.png" loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="Image 39" /></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Because he lived according to his nature. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Normally, to carry out such evil, one needed undeniable power. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">But Hashimoto didn't have that power. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">So, like a chameleon, he learned to change colors to match his environment. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Trying to blend into the environment to survive. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">That was exactly what he was doing at that moment and what he had been doing up until then. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">We left the lobby and the three of us walked to the convenience store. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Then we went inside, picked up a basket, and I contacted Kei on my phone. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">While listening to what she wanted, I decided on dinner for myself as well. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">The convenience store's microwavable side dishes were quite delicious. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">While shopping, I ran into someone who came in after us in the beverage section. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Ah... H-hello. .” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">The one who greeted me was Yamamura Miki, a girl from the same class as Hashimoto. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“I didn't expect to see you here.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Yes, I suppose so.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Yamamura agreed, looking somewhat uncomfortable at my response. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">It seemed that it was indeed Yamamura who had been watching Hashimoto on the emergency stairs. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Even after leaving the dormitory, I’d hardly noticed her presence and didn't know who she was. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">That was why I thought it might be Yamamura, and it seemed I was right. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">I didn't know whether she was acting alone or if Sakayanagi was hiding behind her, but considering that she’d been on standby before I returned to my room by elevator, it was more likely that she’d been watching Hashimoto. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Moreover, I couldn't find any particular reason why Yamamura would be secretly watching me. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Oh, it's Yamamura. What a coincidence.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Hashimoto, who’d noticed us talking, approached us with a curry-flavored cup noodle in his hand. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Good evening..  Hashimoto-kun.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“This is the first time I've seen you use a convenience store.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">I wondered if this way of speaking was just a habit or if he’d sensed something. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">While voicing information that he didn't know the credibility to, he observed Yamamura's reaction. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Um, I. . I use the convenience store quite a bit..  about once or twice a week..  but I don't stand out..  I'm sorry.” </p>



<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><a id="p31"></a><img src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/y2v11/index-31_1.png" loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="Image 40" /></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Oh, no, I'm sorry if I did something wrong. .” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Hashimoto seemed to have tried to probe her but ended up apologizing in a hurry since he pointed out Yamamura's lack of presence. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“It's rare to see Yamamura Miki talking to a boy.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Are you really one to say that, Morishita?” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“I'm just a little curious about Hashimoto Masayoshi. Is it love?” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Don't deliberately throw me under the bus like that..  Well, I guess Yamamura is suspicious of me too.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> <i class="calibre3">Is that so? </i> In response to such a probing gaze, Yamamura looked down and averted her eyes. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">The heavy silence didn't match the convenience store's atmosphere or the upbeat music, creating a discordant sound. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">The one who broke the silence was not Hashimoto nor Yamamura, but Morishita. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Let's shop together since we're all here. You don't mind, do you?” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Eh, um, yes..  if that’s okay with you..  I mean.. ” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Her not reading the room from the get-go seemed to have paid off here. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Without waiting for her answer, Yamamura ended up shopping with us. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Well, after all, a convenience store was a place to shop. It wasn’t a strange thing for us to do. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">I didn't often see Yamamura talking to other students, but she even seemed to struggle talking to her own classmates. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">She was being led around by Morishita and forced to pick up items she recommended. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">And without being able to refuse any of them, she put three or four items in her basket. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“You shouldn't push her around too much.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Why not? Yamamura Miki is happily accepting my recommendations.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“I don't think she's happy at all. She looks troubled to me.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Is that so?” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Um, uh...” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Not knowing how to respond to either side, Yamamura was at a loss for words. “Am I forcing you to buy anything?” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“No, that's not.. ” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Yamamura backed off at the mere pressure of words, and any words of defiance were swallowed up. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Do you dislike this? Well, let me give you my next recommendation. It's a secret from everyone else.” </p>



<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><a id="p32"></a><img src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/y2v11/index-32_1.png" loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="Image 41" /></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Even though she wasn't a convenience store employee, Morishita was trying to get her to buy the next product. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">She tried to take out a juice box from one of the reach-in coolers. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Sorry to interrupt your friendly chat, but could you move a bit?” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">While they were talking amongst each other, a new customer stopped by the beverage corner. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Morishita seemed to have noticed her, but Yamamura, who was nearby, seemed to have overlooked her, and their shoulders slightly collided. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Oh, I'm sorry.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">The convenience store wasn’t very spacious, so if several people gathered, it could get in the way of other customers when they were choosing items. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">It wasn't a big shock, but Yamamura apologized and made way. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“No, I'm the one who didn’t notice. I'm sorry.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">She gently fluttered her long silver hair and took out a bottle of green tea. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“I like this brand's tea. You can feel the umami and aroma as if it were brewed in a teapot, right Ayanokōji?” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">The one who turned her gaze to me, speaking like a beverage brand's promoter, was Kiryūin Fūka from class 3-B. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“I've never had that brand before, so I can't answer.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“That's a shame. You should try it if you have a chance.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Are you going home now, Kiryūin-senpai?” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Yes. It's getting a bit late. I thought I'd just stop by the convenience store today. Is this female student… your new girlfriend?” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“No, she's not.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Ah, um... I'm Yamamura...” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“I'm Morishita Ai.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Yamamura and Morishita, huh? Are you in the same class as Ayanokōji?” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“No, they are in Class A.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Oh? It's good to have a wide circle. You should cherish your friends.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Are you one to say that, Kiryūin-senpai?” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">That was unexpected advice from someone who stood out among the third-year students for being aloof. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Nice to meet you, Kiryūin-senpai. I'm Hashimoto, also from Class A.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Hashimoto greeted her, interrupting Kiryūin who was looking at Yamamura, and extended his hand. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Kiryūin nodded, lightly brushing off his hand. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“I'll remember all three of you.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">After a brief exchange, Kiryūin finished paying first and left the convenience store. </p>



<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><a id="p33"></a><img src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/y2v11/index-33_1.png" loading="lazy" decoding="async" alt="Image 42" /></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">It was a bit surprising that Kiryūin, who didn't seem to be interested in others, said she would remember the three of them, even if it was just a formality. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">It might not have meant much. </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“Are you close with Kiryūin-senpai? She's famous for not hanging out with anyone.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">“I wouldn't say we're close.” </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">Hashimoto continued to watch Kiryūin's back as she headed towards the dormitory for a while. </p>
