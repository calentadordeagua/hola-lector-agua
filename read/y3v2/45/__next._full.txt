Or some other kind of consideration? It wasn&apos;t particularly difficult to imagine the real reason.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;If you&apos;d like, how about we walk home together? Ah... saying it like that makes it hard for you to say no, doesn&apos;t it?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;No, it&apos;s fine.
