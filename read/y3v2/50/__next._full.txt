He found me out.<sup id="fnref-1-fbd73bf2-885b-5850-984b-29ea3fc3d540"><a class="footnote-ref" href="#fn-1-fbd73bf2-885b-5850-984b-29ea3fc3d540" data-ref="1" epub:type="noteref">[1]</a></sup></p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Conflicting feelings kept flooding my mind simultaneously.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">It would be easy to line up excuses, but that would be unforgivable.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Ahh... that just won&apos;t do.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I have a noble cause.
