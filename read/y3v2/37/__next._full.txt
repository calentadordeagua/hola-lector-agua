Most students had already gone home and the majority of those remaining were in the middle of their club activities.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Shouts could be heard from the fields and the sound of squeaking shoes from the gymnasium.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I was heading to the library to meet Hiyori, something I&apos;d been putting off for a while.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">We exchanged greetings on Friday, but didn&#x2019;t get to talk beyond that.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">The invitation to transfer to Class B.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I refused it and, for my own purposes, transferred to the unrelated Class C.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">It wasn&apos;t that I regretted it.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">But the reason I still felt a lingering pull was probably because I, too, had wanted to transfer to Class B, where Hiyori was waiting.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">As an ordinary male student of the Advanced Nurturing High School.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">A year spent left">A future I could&apos;ve chosen.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I recalled the end of our second year, when Ishizaki led me to hold hands with Hiyori.</p><p class="paragraph" Of course not. The answer was already in my head. The act of asking myself questions while pretending not to know... it felt unsettling somehow.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I decided to move to Class C and rejected Hiyori&apos;s invitation.
