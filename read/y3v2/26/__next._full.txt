I have something I&apos;d like to talk about.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;S-S-S-Suzune!? Wh-what are you doing here? I mean, uh, it&apos;s not a problem or anything!&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">A mechanical pencil was clutched in the hand of the flustered, gesticulating Sudou-kun.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Oh? Even I had my suspicions, however slight.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Was I interrupting something?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;N-No, you&apos;re not interrupting anything at all! More importantly, both of them were still in their school uniforms.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">As the student council president, there seemed to be no problems to point out.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;...Well, y&apos;see, about this special exam. They said we don&apos;t gotta study yet, but it&apos;d be bad if there was a written test, right? So I figured we should prepare, even just a little.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Even though I hadn&apos;t yet told them our plan for tomorrow, he&apos;d already begun to prepare countermeasures. I doubt anyone could have predicted seeing him like this back when we first enrolled.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;That&apos;s admirable, but make sure you get Onodera-san back to her room safely before curfew, all right?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;O-Obviously! So what do you want, showing up without any notice?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">He tried to shoo Onodera-san toward the living room with his hand, but I stopped him. Since there was a guest, it would be faster to explain it to both of them at once.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;May I come in for just a moment? This is something I&apos;d rather people from other classes not overhear.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Yeah... of course you can.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I stepped into the entryway and closed the door.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Then I beckoned Onodera-san, who was watching me with curiosity, over to the entryway.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;The special exam that starts tomorrow. On purpose? What&apos;s the point of doing that? She&apos;ll just make people hate her, right?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;I interpreted that attitude as the only hint the teacher could give us... Yeah, she was pretty damn cold back when we enrolled.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Sudou-kun glanced back to meet Onodera-san&apos;s eyes, recalling the past, and they both nodded in understanding.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Do you remember the first special exam we took as first-year Class D?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Hmm?
