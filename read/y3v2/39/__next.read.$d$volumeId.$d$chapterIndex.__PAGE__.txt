It was almost the end of the fifth day since the exam was announced, but not a single major change had occurred. As far as my brief investigation showed, every class maintained a perfect record of no tardiness or absences and the student body was leading a quiet, more serious school life than usual. I also heard no rumors of any trouble over the weekend, a time when things tended to get lax. It seemed the students hadn&apos;t forgotten to maintain a sense of tension.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">As was evident from their diligence in their studies and sports, their avoidance of tardiness and absences, and their strict adherence to order and politeness, all four classes were acting with the consideration that their daily conduct would be key to the exam. It seemed Ryuuen&apos;s class hadn&apos;t been able to grasp that on the first day, but Kondou and the others&apos; actions, while probing, had clearly taken daily conduct into account from the very beginning. I didn&apos;t know which student in Ryuuen&apos;s class proposed the idea, but in that class, the leader&apos;s decision was everything.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">The fact that he adopted it meant that, at the very least, Ryuuen himself came to the same conclusion.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left"><em>Thwip, thwip-thwip!</em></p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Just as I was about to get up from my seat, a sharp pain shot through the back of my neck again and again.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Ow, ow, that hurts.
