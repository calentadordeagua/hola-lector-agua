As I told you during the lunch break, you have a matter in the Student Council room in one hour. I don&apos;t mind you having a discussion, but don&apos;t forget.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Yes, I understand.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">And just as I&apos;d expected, Sensei left the classroom after coming over to me and saying just that, without ever touching on the details of the special exam during the afternoon homeroom. After seeing her off, I made my move. I wanted to avoid a repeat of the morning&apos;s complaint session.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">As I quickly stepped up to the teacher&#x2019;s podium, another student began to move.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">He was the one person I&apos;d identified as a potential problem but hadn&apos;t spoken to.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;That guy...&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Sudou-kun, who muttered quietly nearby, clicked his tongue and turned around.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">The student who drew a conspicuous amount of attention, Koenji-kun, stood up from his seat alone and quickly left the classroom.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Hey, Koenji&#x2014;&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Sudou-kun immediately called out to him in a reprimanding tone, but he walked away without turning back or even stopping for a moment.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">That was normal. He wouldn&apos;t interfere with the discussion, but at the same time, he wouldn&apos;t cooperate either.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I knew that, so I didn&apos;t even try to talk to him before school let out.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I resigned myself to the fact that asking for his cooperation would be more trouble than it was worth and would likely just lead to a fight.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Essentially, Sudou-kun, the only one who called out to him, must have felt the same way.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">As if to prove it, he too lost interest in Koenji-kun&apos;s disappearing form.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">That was fine. We could just start the discussion as usual without him.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">The discussion would proceed more smoothly that way, and problems were less likely to arise.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">However...</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I wondered if it was really alright to keep letting this slide, dismissing it as <em>normal</em>.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Hirata-kun, I&apos;m sorry, but could you get started without me? I&apos;ll be right back.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Yeah, I understand.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I entrusted the room to Hirata-kun, who readily agreed.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Even though I knew it was pointless to plead with him, I hurried out into the hallway to chase after Koenji-kun.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I followed him at a light jog down the hallway, where he was presumably heading for the shoe lockers.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">And when I spotted the large back of the person I was after, I quickly closed the distance and fell in step beside him.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Would you mind waiting a moment?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I called out, looking up at his tall figure from close by.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&#xa0;&quot;Well, well, Horikita Girl. Is there something I can do for you?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Koenji-kun glanced at me once, but he didn&apos;t so much as turn his head, his face remaining pointed straight toward his destination.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I struggled to match my steps to his long strides as I continued to follow.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;I just want you to at least hear me out.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;I don&apos;t mind listening a bit, as long as we&apos;re walking.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;That&apos;s not it. I want you to return to the classroom right now and listen to the discussion.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">It was a modest request.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Koenji-kun didn&apos;t react extravagantly, just flashed a smile that showed his white teeth.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Wouldn&apos;t that simply be pointless? My time is quite priceless, you see. Besides, you and I have an established contract. I&apos;m under no obligation to lend you a hand, am I?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Yes, I am aware of that. I won&apos;t even ask you to say anything.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">If I were to ask him to use his potential to contribute to our victory, he would obviously refuse. I recognized the fact that he had every right to do so.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;All you have to do is sit there until we&apos;re dismissed. It&apos;s simple, isn&apos;t it?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;You seem to be making a passionate case, but I&apos;m afraid I don&apos;t follow.
