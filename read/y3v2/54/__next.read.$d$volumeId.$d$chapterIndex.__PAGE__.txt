3:T40d8,<h1 class="heading_1"><a class="heading-link" href="/read/y3v2/2">Shiraishi Asuka&#x2019;s Short Story:<br/>The Voice Hidden Within</a></h1><hr/><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left"><em>(Original translation provided by </em><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.reddit.com/user/AdventurousArt8711/&amp;sa=D&amp;source=editors&amp;ust=1756121329617835&amp;usg=AOvVaw1qgXx__99UemRNsrjLVeEk"><em>Drago</em></a><em>, edited by LegacyEMTLs)</em></p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">OVER THE COURSE of more than two years of my school life.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I cannot deny that the feeling of <em>I mustn&#x2019;t hear those words</em>, and the feeling of <em>I want to hear those words</em>, had jumbled up into a confusing mess inside me.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;It may be conceited of me to say, but hadn&apos;t you been watching me, to some extent, before I transferred classes?&#x201d;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">His gaze and his voice mercilessly pierced into me.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Ah...&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I was found out. He found me out.<sup id="fnref-1-fbd73bf2-885b-5850-984b-29ea3fc3d540"><a class="footnote-ref" href="#fn-1-fbd73bf2-885b-5850-984b-29ea3fc3d540" data-ref="1" epub:type="noteref">[1]</a></sup></p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Conflicting feelings kept flooding my mind simultaneously.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">It would be easy to line up excuses, but that would be unforgivable.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Ahh... that just won&apos;t do.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">I have a noble cause. A duty as an observer.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">To meet the expectations of Shirogane-sensei, I had continued to do the same thing every single day.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">That day, when I was called into that room just before graduation.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">My mission began ever since the day I learnt about you, and yet&#x2026;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;&apos;Won&apos;t do&apos;? What&apos;s wrong?&#x201d;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">There was nothing different from usual in Ayanokouji-kun&apos;s voice.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">It wasn&apos;t that he had arrived at a firm conclusion; rather, my own irrepressible gazes and mannerisms must have given birth to his suspicion, I suppose.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Please, forgive me.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Please, just for this moment, allow me to forget that duty.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;I am Shiraishi Asuka.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Our conversations up until now had been a lie.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">That&apos;s why, for my self-introduction at the very least, I wanted to speak from the heart, you know...</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;And you&#x2014;who are you?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Would he answer me, I wonder.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Would he answer this question of mine&#x2014;the voice of my first true introduction, which I&apos;d kept hidden deep within&#x2014;.</p><hr class="footnote-rule"/><ol class="footnotes"><li data-id="fbd73bf2-885b-5850-984b-29ea3fc3d540" data-ref="1" class="footnote first-footnote" id="fn-1-fbd73bf2-885b-5850-984b-29ea3fc3d540"><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left"><a class="footnote-backref" href="#fnref-1-fbd73bf2-885b-5850-984b-29ea3fc3d540">[1]: </a>When Shiraishi says &quot;He found me out&quot; (&#x77e5;&#x3063;&#x3066;&#x3082;&#x3089;&#x3048;&#x305f;, shittemoraeta), she expresses it with a sense of gratitude, as if he did her a favor by noticing or understanding her. It&apos;s a nuance that&apos;s quite difficult to fully capture in English, so I&#x2019;m leaving this note for anyone reading to be aware of it.</p></li></ol><h1 class="heading_1"><a class="heading-link" href="/read/y3v2/2">Special Short Story:<br/>The Six First-Year Students</a></h1><hr/><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">TSUBAKI GAZED OUT the cafe window. Her expression is somewhat serious, somewhat delicate.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">In her line of sight, the second-year Ayanokoji Kiyotaka was walking.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Tsubaki, do you want to move up to Class A?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;...Not really. They say that&apos;s what the goal of the school is, then isn&apos;t it something like a mission for the enrolled students to give it their all?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Not much time has passed since I met you, but you don&apos;t seem like the type to act like she&apos;s carrying a mission.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Maybe. But aren&apos;t you the same, Utomiya-kun? You don&apos;t seem interested in things like Class A or special privileges.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Averting her gaze from Ayanokouji, Tsubaki turns her eyes to Utomiya, who is sitting next to her.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Seeing this, Utomiya looks somewhat awkward and turns his face away, towards the outside window.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;It&apos;s Housen and Nanase.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Hm...?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">In the line of sight of Utomiya, who was muttering to change the subject, were two first-year Class D students who were about to pass by Ayanokouji.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">It looked like there might be some kind of contact, but there was no exchange of words, only Nanase giving a polite nod.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Coincidence? Or not?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;I can&apos;t say anything just from that. But the bandage on Ayanokouji&apos;s left hand&#x2014;it piques my interest a bit.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;He was wearing something like that?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Tsubaki, who hadn&apos;t noticed, shifted her gaze to Utomiya once again.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Maybe something had happened. Knowing Housen&apos;s personality, it wouldn&apos;t be surprising if he started it.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;You mean he injured his hand? Housen-kun looked unharmed, though.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;If they clashed somewhere, Ayanokouji probably chose not to fight back. There&apos;s a risk in carelessly injuring your opponent.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Oh? But for that to happen, wouldn&apos;t Ayanokouji-senpai also need a certain level of skill?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;His actual fighting ability is unknown, but I can&apos;t imagine him losing to someone at Housen&apos;s level. I can tell that much just by watching his usual demeanor and presence.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;&apos;Housen-kun&apos;s level&apos;... Are you serious? He seems to just rampage around as he pleases, though.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">What surprised Tsubaki was not the high evaluation of Ayanokouji, but the low estimation of Housen.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;That&apos;s just because everyone around him is weak. &#x2015;&#x2015;Probably.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Feeling he had said too much, Utomiya trailed off.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Men are creatures who bluff. Most of them inwardly believe they are stronger than whoever they face.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">There was a good chance that Utomiya&apos;s remark was just a bluff, or that he simply had an abnormally high self-assessment, but he wasn&apos;t usually one to behave like that.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">For the first time, Tsubaki felt a slight interest in her classmate, Utomiya.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;I think I&apos;ve become a little interested in getting to know you, Utomiya-kun.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Stop it. I don&apos;t pry into anyone&apos;s business, and I don&apos;t want anyone prying into mine.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Ahh~. What a nice atmosphere~. Could it be that you two are dating?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">A shadow fell between the two, and a girl appeared as if to interrupt them. It was Amasawa from first-year Class A.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;We are not dating.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Utomiya denied it instantly. Tsubaki also nodded in agreement, not seeming particularly interested.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;You think so? For that, your sense of distance seemed pretty close, though~&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;It&apos;s your imagination. Did you approach us just to say something so stupid? Or did you come over because you wanted to extract some information from us?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">When Utomiya retorted in a harsh&#xa0;tone, Amasawa smiled, her lips curling in delight.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Oh, come on, I didn&apos;t mean it like that. Even if our classes are different, we&apos;re still first-years, so let&apos;s get along. Right, Tsubaki-chan?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Sorry, but I&apos;ll pass. I&apos;m not exactly looking for friends.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;So harsh, meow~&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Without looking particularly shocked, Amasawa gave a cute reply.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Well then, I guess I&apos;ll save deepening our friendship for next time. See you later, Tsubaki-chan, Utomiya-kun.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Saying that, Amasawa left behind a blown kiss, turned her back, and walked away.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Amasawa doesn&apos;t know about the special exam, right?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;She shouldn&apos;t. In Class A, only Takahashi and Ishigami know. However, I can&apos;t deny the possibility that they asked her to investigate even without knowing the details.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;I see.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;But in any case, it&apos;s a baffling exam. To think it&apos;s targeting a specific individual, only Ayanokouji.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;It might be something that&apos;s normal at this school, and we just don&apos;t know about it.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;...If that&apos;s the case, it makes the situation&#xa0;even more unsettling.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Hello, Utomiya-kun. And Tsubaki-san as well.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">A voice calls out from a short distance away.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Yagami from first-year Class B, who had come to the cafe, stopped and approached the two.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Now it&apos;s Yagami... He&apos;s the type to show up if you talk about the exam.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Can you blame him? The amount of private points you can get is on a whole other level.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;If you don&apos;t mind, could I join your conversation as well?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;We were just having a boring small talk, that&apos;s all.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;It didn&apos;t look that way to me. It seemed you were watching Ayanokouji-senpai and Housen-kun&apos;s group.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Oh... So you&apos;ve been watching us from quite an early stage.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;It was just a coincidence, though.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;Either way, we&apos;re enemies.&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">&quot;It&apos;s precisely because we are enemies that there is meaning in talking to each other, don&apos;t you think?&quot;</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Utomiya, Tsubaki, Housen, Nanase, Yagami.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">What they all had in common, Yagami included, was that they were all targeting Ayanokouji Kiyotaka.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">Tsubaki and Utomiya had no way of knowing, but Amasawa was also involved in a certain special exam for first-year students.</p><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left"/><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">From this gathering, the story connects to the upcoming special exam on the uninhabited island&#x2015;.</p><h1 class="heading_1"><a class="heading-link" href="/read/y3v2/2">LegacyEMTLs Afterword</a></h1><hr/><p class="paragraph" style="text-align: left">First off I would like to apologize for all the delays with releasing this volume.
