<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The main focus of the story in this volume was the November Cultural Festival. At any rate, the story is progressing smoothly without stopping. Soon, the second semester will end, the students will have their winter vacation, and they’ll be thrust into the turbulent third semester. Although there have been a few more volumes than I initially expected in this story, the second-year arc has finally passed its turning point. I feel like, step by step, we’re getting closer to the conclusion of the story.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Will Ayanokouji be able to graduate from school safely? Where will each class end up when everything’s said and done? <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Oh, and also! Also, also! The second season of the anime will finally air this July! I’ve been waiting for this for a long time. I’ve been eagerly awaiting this so much that I even asked myself how much longer </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">could possibly wait! I’m so excited to see Ayanokouji and the rest in action again for the first time in many years. And also, since a third season is also planned… Yeah, hmm… How can I put it? I’m filled with deep emotions.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, to all of you who like </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Classroom</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, and those of you who don’t, those of you who are interested in the story, and those of you who aren’t, it would make me immensely happy if you could tune in and watch. I humbly ask that you consider checking it out.</span></p>
