<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 8:<br/>Unseen Characters</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">M</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">Y TURN AT THE CULTURAL FESTIVAL</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> had come to an end at three o’clock in the afternoon. Now that our hidden gem had made its exciting appearance, I&#160;exited the classroom, leaving things to Ayanokouji-kun to handle.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At any rate… I never imagined that we’d really, honestly turn Chabashira-sensei into a maid,” I thought aloud.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ayanokouji-kun and I had discussed all of the preparations for the festival in advance. He told me that Chabashira-sensei was going to be featured in the final hour, but I was only half-convinced that it would&#160;actually happen. But he had just shown me that it was actually&#160;playing out, and he was working to get incredible results out of it. When I walked out into the hallway, I&#160;clearly witnessed when talk about Chabashira-sensei dressed in a maid outfit began to spread like wildfire.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At any rate, Chabashira-sensei’s involvement was a favorable event for me, personally. The large amount of attention that was drawing people to the special building would inevitably pull people away from other exhibits. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After sending a message to a certain someone on my phone and making sure that it had been read, I decided to head to the student council office. I wanted to check the meeting minutes once more. I could ask Yagami-kun to let me see on the day of the student council meeting, but he wouldn’t just let me calmly observe him writing.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The culprit behind this was someone indirectly working at getting Ayanokouji-kun expelled. This person seemed to have some connection to Amasawa-san too. They also were extremely dangerous and had a very high level of physical ability. If Yagami-kun was the culprit, and if I asked him to show me the meeting minutes again, he’d realize that I was suspicious of him. Well, actually… If I was operating on the assumption that Yagami-kun was the culprit, it would be best for me to also assume that he already realized as much.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Anyway, in order for me to make sure of things without him realizing it, it was necessary for me to choose a time when no one was around. The student council had been shut down for a while thanks to President Nagumo’s circumstances, so while opportunities for me to sneak a peek at Yagami-kun taking meeting minutes were limited, I also didn’t have to bother with clearing people out of the room. That had already been done for me, and I figured that my chance had come with the arrival of the Cultural Festival.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had told Chabashira-sensei that I had most likely forgotten my notebook in the student council office this morning, and she gave me permission to go to the faculty office during my break to get the key and pick it up. Even if someone were to see me entering the student council office, I had a just cause for being there. After quickly changing out of the maid outfit and into my school uniform, I strode over to the faculty office, alone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“About fifty minutes left…” I muttered to myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I walked to the student council office, I looked up at the clock in the hallway and exhaled. At any rate, today had certainly been busy. It wasn’t over quite yet, but I was finished with my role. Right now, I needed to take my hour-long break, but as soon as my break was over, the Cultural Festival would be over too. I had really been busy ever since I put on my maid outfit in the morning, and I worked without stopping since then. Now, dressed in my regular uniform, I walked up to the student council office and quietly inserted the key into the lock on the&#160;door. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Everyone was busy with the Cultural Festival, so no one was in the student council office. It wouldn’t be difficult for me to review the meeting minutes again and to take pictures with my phone. Or that’s what I had thought, anyway. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just then, my phone started vibrating in my pocket, and I realized I was receiving a call. My heart practically jumped out of my chest when I saw the caller ID: Yagami Takuya. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Why am I getting a call from him now…? </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though I felt like this was a terrifying coincidence, I answered the call anyway.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hello?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita-senpai.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was expecting to hear Yagami-kun’s voice from over the phone, but the voice I heard also came from just a short distance away. He was right here. The very last person I wanted to see right now was facing me, waving to me with a smile on his face. I felt chills all throughout my body, as if someone had dumped ice cold water directly onto my heart.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did I startle you?” He hung up his phone and took a step toward me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why are you here, Yagami-kun?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why am I here…?” he repeated. “That’s what you’re asking? Are you not bothered by the fact I called you when I was standing nearby?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was so focused on other things that I completely forgot to point that out. It was as if Yagami-kun was trying to determine just how upset and panicked I was.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“By the way, why are </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> here, senpai? This is such a deserted place,” Yagami-kun asked. “Isn’t this the time to be making the final push for the Cultural Festival, as the event enters its climax?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m on my break now, so my role in the Cultural Festival is over,” I responded. “I just wanted to be alone for a little while.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You took your break starting at three in the afternoon? That’s a rather unusual choice, isn’t it?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Was it that unusual?</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I didn’t have any criteria to make a judgment like that because I had never experienced a festival like this before. However, since the rules stated that all participants were required to take an hour-long break, there must have been at least a certain percentage of students who chose to take their break starting at three like I did. But as soon as my train of thought led me to that answer, I fell silent for a few seconds. Then, I realized something.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What Yagami-kun had just said, about it being unusual, was neither the truth nor a lie. It was nothing more than a statement that he made to try to find out whether I simply chose three o’clock as my break time unintentionally, or if I had chosen this time with a specific purpose in mind. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The fact was, I wasn’t able to answer him immediately because what he was doing was rattling me. No matter how I responded to him now, I might have already fallen into his trap. Since I was already late in commenting on what he said, I had the option of ignoring it and moving on. I had no other choice but to just let his off-putting choice of words slide off me for the time being.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why are you here, Yagami-kun?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I saw you with this rather grim look on your face, Horikita-senpai,” he said. “I got curious, so I followed you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“From when? Whatever your reason, following a girl around isn’t very admirable behavior.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I tried to call out to you, but I suppose you didn’t hear me over all of the hustle and bustle.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was certainly true that I had been lost in thought as I was walking here, but that didn’t mean that I wouldn’t have noticed if he had called out to me. I couldn’t help but feel that he was trying to fluster me, like before. But maybe there was some kind of real meaning to this sequence of events. Besides, he could have called out to me any number of times before I had gotten here. Or&#160;perhaps he hadn’t been following me at all, but rather had been waiting in this area from the beginning…?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">All of that was based on the assumption that Yagami-kun was indeed the person I had been trying to track down—the person with the neat handwriting. If he had nothing to do with that after all, then I supposed I was being suspicious of him enough to the point where I would need to earnestly and profusely apologize to him&#160;later.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is it okay for you to be away from the festival?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m in the same position as you,” he said. “I’ve finished what I needed to do; my role is finished. It’s not my ‘break,’ technically, but more like free time, I suppose. There is no rule that states that you cannot be on break for more than an hour.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So it was really a simple coincidence after all, then? </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">No, I shouldn’t operate on that assumption now.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> If I found out later that this was all a coincidence, then it wouldn’t be a problem. But if it wasn’t actually a coincidence, and I assumed it was, I’d be in trouble.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you have business in the student council office? I&#160;believe it’s locked, and I don’t think anyone is in either,” he added, looking over at the door, almost as though he anticipated what was going on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m looking for something,” I replied. “I borrowed the key from the faculty office, so there isn’t any problem.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re looking for something, hm? In that case, I’ll give you a hand.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Feelings of calmness and impatience were fighting with one another in my mind, competing for dominance. I couldn’t make a clear judgment as to whether his statement was made simply out of good intentions or if there was some malicious intent behind it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not something I really need help with though,” I&#160;told him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But it’s something important enough that you’d go through the trouble of searching for it while the Cultural Festival is going on, isn’t it?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that remark, it was like he had stripped my thoughts bare and could see right through me. There wasn’t any point in wasting any more time standing here. I figured I’d just go ahead and tell Yagami-kun the same lie that I told the teachers.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s my notebook,” I said. “I bought it a little while ago, but I can’t find it anywhere. I worry that someone might have picked it up and read it, which isn’t good for my mental health. I was about to give up on finding it, but it was still bothering me. The student council office is pretty much the only place I haven’t looked.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, I’ll help you search. I’m sure that things will be hectic once the festival is over. And besides, simply put, it would be twice as efficient to search with two people rather than one.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-yes, you’re right,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I slowly unlocked the door and opened it. Yagami-kun was standing directly next to me. I took a step ahead of him, moving to enter the room, but I suddenly stopped.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita-senpai?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do we really need two people to search for a missing item in the student council office?” I asked. “Or is there something else you’re after?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh…?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Under these circumstances, I made the deliberate choice to fight back.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I refused your offer to help before because honestly, I&#160;felt a little scared,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re scared of me…? Why?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t know?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nothing comes to mind…” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s no one in the student council room right now. You said that you called out to me, but I didn’t notice you. From what you said, you followed me here, and now we’d be all alone, just the two of us. Do you know what that means for a girl?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was attacking him not from my personal position as Horikita Suzune, but instead, I was using the angle of gender differences in society. Regardless of whether his intentions were good or bad, this would surely get him to go away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I see. I’m sorry, I hadn’t considered that at all… I see…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And with that, he wouldn’t be able to thoughtlessly come into the student council office. He would have no other choice but to wait outside in the hall. If he did come into the office now, then it would be considered creepy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m terribly sorry. I acted incorrectly.” Yagami-kun bowed deeply. “However, might I ask you just one more thing, at the risk of sounding rude?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What is it?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He still had his head down. I wondered what he was going to say to me now, at this stage.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He began talking, lifting his face up. “Horikita-senpai, is your real intention in coming to the student council office—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In that same instant, he suddenly collapsed right in front of my eyes, as though his upper body suddenly broke and bent. A second later, I realized that someone else was responsible for that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Caught you!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That voice belonged to none other than Ibuki-san, still dressed in her Japanese-style outfit.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t just stand around like an idiot!” she hissed. “Quick, let us in, Horikita! It’ll be bad news if someone sees!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It certainly </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">would</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> be a big problem if we were seen, because what she was doing would appear to be an obvious act of violence to anyone. I opened the door to the student council office and Ibuki-san forcefully pushed Yagami-kun inside. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The first person to speak was, of course, Yagami-kun, the victim in this.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what are you doing…?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I could see Ibuki-san standing behind Yagami-kun. By detaining him, she was making a mess of the situation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, well, you’ve been saved again thanks to my handiwork, eh, Horikita?” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You saved me? He wasn’t doing anything, though…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you’re</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> the one who told me to pay close attention to this guy. And besides, he was hounding you. Anyone would think that there was something going on.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She blurted out all sorts of things that didn’t need to be said all at once. Her single-minded behavior rendered the entire conversation I had with Yagami-kun up until that point totally useless. Seriously, her declaring that outright in front of the person I had </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">told</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> her to be wary of was complete and utter nonsense.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, excuse me, but why are you paying close attention to me, exactly?” asked Yagami-kun. It was an entirely natural question to ask as he remained in Ibuki-san’s grasp, unable to move.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Now that things had come to this, I figured that I had no other choice but to just tell him everything.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I apologize for Ibuki-san assaulting you like this,” I said. “However, there has been something bothering me about you. I wonder if you remember when you showed me your meeting minutes the other day.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re referring to the remarks that Student Council President made, yes?” he replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I wanted to see what you wrote back then one more time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What I wrote? I don’t quite understand, but are you saying that what you’re </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">really</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> looking for is the meeting minutes notebook?” Yagami-kun looked puzzled, but he continued speaking. “You say that you wanted to see what I wrote, but what’s your true purpose in doing so?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though I was still wondering what Yagami-kun was about to say before Ibuki-san appeared, I continued my own explanation. I told him how a piece of paper had been slipped inside my tent during the Uninhabited Island Special Exam, and about how I was trying to find out who the sender of that note was. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yagami-kun, still restrained, listened silently for a while. “And that’s because my handwriting, which you saw in the meeting minutes, is similar to the handwriting on that note, then? Is that what you’re saying?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, exactly.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If what you say is true, then I can certainly understand your wariness of me,” he said. “And I suppose that, yes, it might have been best that you elected to come here now, at this time of day, if you were trying to secretly confirm your suspicions.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In the days leading up to the festival, everyone had been busily preparing for it, so people were constantly coming and going on Saturdays and Sundays. Students were constantly walking all over campus looking for stall locations, so I couldn’t come to the student council office to confirm my suspicions then.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“However, I’m not the one who sent you that letter,” stated Yagami-kun. He denied it categorically. Part of me wanted to believe him…but I noticed that in his response, when he was unable to come out and admit that it was him, he started speaking a little more emphatically. “Do you have any reason to doubt me, perchance?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I’m afraid I don’t have any grounds for doubting you,” I said. “But I suppose it’s not like I’d expect you’d honestly admit to it either.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Would you mind letting me take a look at the piece of paper in question?” he asked. “That way, we could compare the writing on the paper to my meeting minutes, and I should be able to prove my innocence.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m afraid that it’s impossible, unfortunately. There was a little incident, and I ended up losing the paper.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It had actually been torn to shreds by Amasawa-san, whom Ibuki-san and I fought with on the island.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that’s certainly a pickle,” said Yagami-kun. “Does that mean that there’s no way I can prove my innocence?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s why I wanted to double-check the meeting minutes,” I told him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But when you say that, you’d still just be seeing if it’s consistent with what you picture in your memory, right? If anything, I’d say that you suspect me rather strongly right now, Horikita-senpai. That being the case, I would think that there’s a not insignificant possibility of you misremembering it to better suit your case and deem me the culprit. This is clearly a very disadvantageous situation for me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…You might be right about that, yes,” I admitted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t want Yagami-kun to be the one, but I had to admit that my desire to identify a culprit was very strong. I could understand why he’d be concerned about what was going to happen next.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“While it is upsetting and regrettable that you suspect me, could I at least ask that you let go of my arm for now?” he said. “In any case, I can’t imagine that you two carrying on in this position would be a very welcome development. How would you excuse your behavior if Student Council President Nagumo saw this situation?” </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you think’s going to happen next if you cause more chaos?” Amasawa-san asked him. “Do you think that you’ll get their approval? Do you think that they’ll accept you? It’s… It’s already over.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">not</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> over!” he yelled back. “The instructors are waiting! I… I’m going to become the best!!!” </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You? You’ve never beaten me before, not even once. Don’t make me laugh.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Maybe I can’t beat you by force,” she said. “But…I’ll just have to try.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Pft,” Yagami-kun scoffed. “I knew you were devoted to Ayanokouji, but I didn’t realize it was to such a stupid degree.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, just die already,” he retorted. “There’s no point in you being alive any longer.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, just as Amasawa-san readied herself for a fight, the footsteps of multiple people could be heard in the hallway. I didn’t recognize all of them, but I did notice that two of the five were guests of the Cultural Festival who came to our maid café&#160;earlier. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We received a call to come and pick you up at the student council office,” one of the newcomers said. “Even though things are a little different than we had planned.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yagami-kun was on a total killing spree just minutes ago, but now, before we even realized what was happening, he shriveled back like a child. To me, it looked like someone terrified of being found and chastised by his parents. Surrounded by the adults, Yagami-kun was taken away without resistance. Amasawa-san walked with them, as though she were accompanying them. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As they left, Mashima-sensei stood back up, bearing the pain he was in.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You people,” he began, looking to them for confirmation. “You’re…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We are people with a connection to Yagami and Amasawa,” said one of them. “We will settle matters here, so please, go ahead and seek medical attention for your injuries. Also, please do not tell anyone—not other teachers, not students—about what transpired here. Rest assured that we will discuss everything with Chairman Sakayanagi.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I understand,” replied Mashima-sensei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With Sakagami-sensei’s help, Mashima-sensei left the student council office. The room, which had seen such chaos, was suddenly enveloped in silence.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Talk about a letdown,” Ryuuen-kun sighed. “Just when it was gettin’ fun too. Get up, Ibuki. We’re leavin’.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh… Give me a hand, at least,” she huffed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki-san still couldn’t stand up on her own. Ryuuen-kun gestured with his chin to Komiya-kun, instructing him to help Ibuki-san up, and they all left the room. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Finally, it was just President Nagumo and me alone in the student council office.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, there we have it,” said President Nagumo. “A&#160;lot of things went totally sideways there, but I guess the issue’s been settled for the time being.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How much did you know about what happened today?” I asked him. “Ayanokouji-kun was involved, wasn’t he?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you talking about? I already told you earlier that I only came to the office because I was supposed to talk to Ryuuen.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But if that was true, it wouldn’t have been necessary for you to bring that letter.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The love letter sat crumpled on the floor.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To echo what Yagami said earlier, it was just a coincidence. It just so happened to be in my pocket still.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was an obvious lie. This was essentially President Nagumo’s way of telling me that there was nothing further to say on the matter—an order from him as student council president.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And this crazy Cultural Festival is over too,” he added. “Leave now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…All right.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Four o’clock would come soon, and the Cultural Festival, which triggered this completely unexpected event, was going to officially be over.</span></p>
