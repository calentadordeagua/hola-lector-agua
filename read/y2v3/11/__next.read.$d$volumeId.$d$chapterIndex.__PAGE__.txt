<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t help but feel like Ike-senpai is in somewhat of a precarious state right now,” she said. “I’m worried that what was said yesterday might backfire and cause him to do something reckless… That is why I’m feeling uneasy about whether we should split up now.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I agreed with her that Ike’s emotional state was a point of concern, but traveling together with them also carried significant risk. Depending on what those were, we might have to head off in completely different directions. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had to wonder, then: was Nanase’s proposal born from a real worry? Or did she have some kind of ulterior motive? <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I certainly couldn’t say that sticking with them was a great strategy, but it wasn’t like it was impossible to do something. In that case, if the designated area after this happened to be randomly assigned and was somewhere off to the west on the other side of the mountains, then we’d never be able to reach it and we’d suffer for it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would be quite troubling if the area assigned to us at nine happened to be the random selection, wouldn’t it?” said Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If we continued walking for two hours straight, starting now, even supposing everything went well, we would probably only end up being able to reach I8 or I7 at best. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We also have the option of missing the designated area for a second time, so we don’t push our luck,” said Nanase.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though our destinations were different, it sounded like we were basically taking the same route on our respective journeys then. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I figured it’d be better to just go with Sudou and his friends after all since I probably wasn’t going to be able to arrive at my destination in time anyway. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Full of vigor, Ike rolled his shoulders and stretched both arms out before taking up the position at the head of our party and leading the way forward. Putting on a false show of bravado wasn’t that different from a real show of bravado.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t seem to be enjoying yourself very much, Ayanokouji-senpai,” said Nanase. “You have a very stern look.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is just how I normally look,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, really?” replied Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was true that I was worried about what was going to happen with our designated areas, but I didn’t remember ever letting it show on my face at all.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No point in worryin’ ’bout it. Ayanokouji’s face just always looks like that,” said Sudou over his shoulder, backing me up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wasn’t sure if I should have been grateful or not for his agreement on that point.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There you go,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had some mixed feelings, but I decided to roll with it. I saw that Sudou had a mischievous grin on his face as he headed towards the front of the group and started talking with Ike.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re thinking that something is up with Ike-senpai too, after all, aren’t you, Ayanokouji-senpai?” asked Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re being paranoid,” I replied. “I’m happy to see that Ike is maturing. I’m honestly not sure what you’re implying.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that so…” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since I had determined that there was a chance Ike and Sudou might overhear us if we weren’t careful, I decided to put a stop to that conversation right there. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At any rate, for the time being, if I was going to find out her true identity, I didn’t have any other choice but to keep working with Nanase for as long as I could.</span></p>
