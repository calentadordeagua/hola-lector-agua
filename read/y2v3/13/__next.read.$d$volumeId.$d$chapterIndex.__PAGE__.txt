<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 7:<br/>The Aloof Child Prodigy of Class 2-D</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T SEVEN O’CLOCK</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> in the morning on the following day, the fifth day of the exam, we were walking along the riverside from area D4 to area D5. After we had reached G3 yesterday on the fourth day of the exam, we decided to ignore the next designated area, H4, and instead traveled west, back towards the starting area. As a result of that, we had missed out not only on reaching H4, but on the areas that came after it, which were H6 and I7. Which meant that we failed to reach designated areas three times consecutively.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Unless by sheer coincidence a randomly assigned designated area was to appear somewhere along our route, we were inevitably going to miss four designated areas in a row. Ultimately, that lucky scenario didn’t end up happening. Once seven o’clock came around this morning, we saw that our first designated area of the day was in I8. Well, since I8 was so far away from us, I supposed that it made it easier for us to accept it emotionally without feeling too bitter about it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Perhaps it was because it was so early in the morning, but the gentle murmuring of the river was quite pleasant. It would’ve been a great morning if I hadn’t gotten several bits of bad news.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At any rate… Things certainly look dire for Shinohara-senpai, don’t they?” said Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Shinohara had been left all alone after her teammates Komiya and Kinoshita had been eliminated the other day. Though Ike and Sudou were doing what they could to support her, there was a limit to how many points she could really get all on her own. As of yesterday, her group didn’t appear in the bottom ten, but when I checked this morning, she had already fallen to the bottom eight. On top of that, when you considered the fact that the groups behind her in the rankings would be picking up more and more points than she would, Shinohara would probably be in last place tomorrow or the day after. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And thanks to that, in an ironic twist of fate, Akito’s group was no longer in the bottom ten—for the time being at least. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, on the other hand, we had the rankings for the top groups, which I hadn’t checked yesterday. In first place was Nagumo’s group, which was composed of students from Class 3-A. In second place was Kiriyama’s group, made up of students from Class 3-B. The representatives from the third-years were accounted for in the top two.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, senpai,” said Nanase. “There’s someone fishing.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A lone student came into view in the distance. They were sitting on the rocky shore, leisurely passing the time by fishing. I immediately recognized who it was, thanks to this person’s distinctive physical characteristics. He was a member of a group that I’d now been wanting to meet up with more than anyone else. I just never imagined that I’d get my chance to do this so easily. After all, searching for a specific person you wanted to meet was quite difficult to begin with. I had already been considering using the GPS function to find him after it had been made available for us yesterday. I wanted to take advantage of this opportunity to talk to him while I had this chance.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you mind if we make a stop here, Nanase?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though several rather tempting Tasks had appeared in the area, I’d most likely have to give up on them.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I am simply your traveling companion, Ayanokouji-senpai,” said Nanase. “Please do not pay me any mind.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was grateful to hear that, so I took her up on it and decided to approach the person in question. It didn’t seem as though he had noticed me yet, but I decided not to call out to him, so that I wouldn’t disturb him while he was fishing. Instead, I approached quietly, walking along the gravelly path. Eventually, I had gotten close enough for him to take notice of my presence, and he slowly turned to look in my direction.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I had heard that you started this exam on your own, but it appears that you haven’t fallen into the bottom ten,” said Katsuragi, of Class 2-B. He didn’t seem to be turning me away, but rather, welcoming me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Somehow, more or less,” I agreed. “But if I took it easy for even just a day, I’m sure that I’d fall fast.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen, having overheard the conversation, emerged from his tent and shot me a somewhat exasperated glance.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you’re casually walkin’ around this island with a chick in tow, eh?” he said. “What, you get tired of Karuizawa and kick her to the curb or somethin’?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Karuizawa?” Katsuragi turned to look back at Ryuuen, confused. “Why are you bringing up her?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Ku ku</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. Ain’t nothin’, don’t worry ’bout it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems like you two are doing pretty well,” I observed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">You could check who was at the top of the rankings by looking at your tablet. As of this morning, I had a total of fifty-two points, putting me in seventy-fourth place overall. If you took the fact that I was going it alone into consideration, that rank was fairly good. That being said, Katsuragi and Ryuuen’s group was in a better position, being in tenth place with a total score of ninety-two points. Twenty-nine of those points had come from Arrival Bonuses from reaching their designated areas, forty-one of the points came from Early Bird Bonus, and twenty-two from Tasks.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Shut the hell up,” said Ryuuen. “Hey, isn’t that freakshow with a whole bunch of screws loose one of your guys?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, that’s right. He is,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The ‘freakshow,’ as Ryuuen put it, was none other than Kouenji. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though he was going it alone like me, he was currently sitting in fourth place in the rankings. Out of all the groups in the top ten, he had earned the most points from Early Bird Bonuses. He had really racked up a lot of points via Tasks as well, bringing his total score to 126 points. He was getting excellent results and had apparently been making no mistakes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, there were still ten days left before the exam was over, if you included today. If he were to have some kind of accident, for example if he became overcome with fatigue, or if he got injured, then he’d likely drop in the rankings in a flash. During this two-week-long uninhabited island exam, we didn’t even have a single day where we could just kick back and rest our bodies. No matter who you were, if you overworked yourself day after day, you’d damage your body more and more. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It would start with obvious symptoms, such as muscle pains, and then, gradually, your legs would feel like they were getting heavier, and your walking pace would slow. That, coupled with the fact that we could only get the bare minimum amount of nutrition and hydration that our bodies needed, meant that we’d suffer from feelings of fatigue and exhaustion, both physically and mentally.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s your next designated area?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s already past seven,” I said. “You seem to be pretty laid-back, though.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was the one who wanted to stop,” said Katsuragi as he cast his line into the river, flicking his fishing rod with a </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">fwip</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. “We’ve been moving at a faster and faster pace over these past four days, as we continued to challenge ourselves to reach designated areas and complete Tasks. When they announced our first designated area of the day a little earlier, it was randomly assigned. It’s E10. We&#160;would need to push ourselves quite hard if we were to arrive there in time. I concluded that it wasn’t worth using up that much energy just to get a point.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen wore a thin smile as he shrugged his shoulders in response. Ryuuen was the person who always tried to push things to the absolute limit whenever and wherever, but Katsuragi had gotten him to stop, eh? There was no way that Ishizaki or Kanedra could have managed to make Ryuuen take a break if they were here. It seemed like Katsuragi was quickly becoming an important part of Class 2-B.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Have you caught anything?” asked Nanase, looking at the bobber in the river. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, I haven’t gotten many bites. If I wanted to catch a lot of fish, I’d have to go out to the sea,” said Katsuragi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So he was casting his fishing rod here simply to kill time, from the sounds of it.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I had no idea how much they’d tell me about that, I decided to ask anyway.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We can easily take care of our food needs by gathering from the sea, the river, or the forest,” Katsuragi said. “It’s the same with water. All we need to do is boil river water, and it’s fine.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But aren’t there risks in drinking river water?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, there are,” Katsuragi agreed. “No matter how much you boil it to sanitize the water, you can’t state with absolute certainty that it’s perfectly safe to drink. That’s why it’s my role to drink river water. Ryuuen is drinking the water we purchased at the start of the exam and any water we obtain from Tasks.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, they were managing the risks perfectly. By this point, I was sure that some groups out there were struggling, completely dead in the water. But these two seemed like they could carry on safely for quite a while longer yet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Actually, it just so happens that I was looking for you, Ryuuen,” I said, turning to him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For me, eh?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sure you must be aware of what groups are in the bottom ten right now, right?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, yeah, sure. Dunno what those morons from my class are thinkin’, though, sitting down there in the bottom eight,” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With two members of that particular group gone, the rate at which they collected points had declined sharply, creating a growing disparity between them and other groups in the bottom of the rankings.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Komiya and Kinoshita got eliminated,” I informed him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen stopped smiling. He now had a stern look on his face. Katsuragi turned his attention over to me as well, pulling himself away from his fishing.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Eliminated? What happened?” asked Katsuragi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since Katsuragi had now become a member of Class 2-B, Komiya and Kinoshita were allies that he needed to protect. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“They were both seriously injured,” said Nanase. “It’s unlikely that either of them will be able to walk for a while.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Was it an accident?” asked Katsuragi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that’s—” said Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“According to the remaining member of their group, Shinohara, they were attacked by someone,” I replied, interrupting Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, I guess in that case, that certain ‘someone’ or whatever got eliminated too then, right?” asked Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, Shinohara’s testimony was the only evidence they had,” I replied. “Neither Komiya nor Kinoshita could seem to remember if they had really been attacked or not. I think the school is going to be looking into it, but I wouldn’t count on anything coming of it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The school seems to be operating under the assumption that it was a lie on Shinohara-senpai’s part because she didn’t want her teammates to get eliminated,” added Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What should we do then, Ryuuen? Even if we manage to break into the top three, it’ll be meaningless if Komiya and Kinoshita are expelled.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If Shinohara’s group came in last place, then both my class and Ryuuen’s class would suffer severe damage.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You said you were lookin’ for me, right?” Ryuuen said. “Shinohara’s your classmate. So, am I good in assumin’ that you’ve already got a plan to stop ’em all from gettin’ expelled?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I expected as much of Ryuuen. Even though he couldn’t know exactly what my plan was, he had intuited that I had something up my sleeve.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry, Nanase, but I can’t let you hear the rest of this conversation,” I told her. “The survival of the second-years is at stake in this battle.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand,” Nanase replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After I had confirmed that Nanase was far enough away from us, I drew closer to Ryuuen and laid out the details of my strategy. He could share that information with Katsuragi himself later.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Ku ku</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, all right, all right. Well, with somethin’ like that, then Shinohara definitely has a way to survive. Still…is it all gonna go smoothly?” asked Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Our chances will be even better if you work together with us. The rest will steadily fall into place,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You got some balls, man, changin’ things up,” Ryuuen said. “If the others realize somethin’ is up, they’re gonna make a move.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I nodded. That was the reason I didn’t want Nanase to overhear our conversation. If the first-years were to find out, it would likely lead to a direct showdown between the second-years and the other grade levels.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There are some pretty perceptive first-years out there too,” I told him. “There’s a possibility they might catch on faster than we expect.” It was also impossible for us to predict what the third-years would do if they caught wind of the situation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If they’re small fries, then I don’t really give a crap about lettin’ them get tossed out,” said Ryuuen. “But Komiya and Kinoshita still have their uses.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, can I take that to mean that…you’ll work with me?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Our interests align on this one, so ain’t no way I’m not gonna make use of this plan.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Shinohara’s group was composed of students from both of our classes, after all. If we couldn’t work together here and now, then there wouldn’t be any way we could save Shinohara, Komiya, or Kinoshita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you run into Ichinose, can you tell her the plan too?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ichinose’s a goody-two-shoes, so she’s not gonna be&#160;a problem. But I can’t imagine Sakayanagi’s gonna lend a&#160;hand so easily,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She isn’t the type who’d just let herself get done in by first-years,” I argued.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Ku ku</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, yeah, guess yer right.”</span></p>

It had a short time frame, at just forty minutes, and the number of participants was limited to two women and two men. However, you could earn five points just for participating, and if you won, you would earn an additional ten points on top of that for a total of fifteen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since it would only take us a short time to reach the summit, any rivals outside of the area were unlikely to get there before we did. After considering that I’d be missing my fourth designated area in a row soon and losing ten points as a result, I decided that I wanted to go for it. Plus, there was a good chance that no one else would show and I wouldn’t have anyone to compete against, so I could end up getting a free fifteen points.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Despite the mountain’s high elevation, we continued walking on at a quick pace and had arrived at the Task location with about five minutes to spare. I had thought we would’ve been the first to arrive, but apparently someone else had gotten there before we did. They seemed to notice our presence, but there wasn’t any sign that they were even going to try to make eye contact with us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He got here rather quickly, didn’t he? He must have been rather close by, don’t you suppose?” said Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have to wonder about that,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if he had been on the south side of C5, it should have still taken him quite a bit of time to have gotten here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not sure if this will help clear things up for you, but that’s Kouenji Rokusuke,” I told Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kouenji?” she repeated. “…As in, the person from your class who is currently in fourth place, Ayanokouji-senpai? Well, he certainly…radiates an aura of greatness, I suppose.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Getting here faster than we did was one thing, but what was really strange was the fact that he didn’t seem to have any bags or supplies. There was nothing on him at all aside from the single bottle of mineral water that he was holding. I supposed that if he were traveling light, that would certainly explain why he was able to climb up the mountain faster than we did, but… That would also mean that he was moving about without a tablet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, I supposed that was par for the course, for someone like Kouenji. I’m guessing he must have been satisfied with himself, having just finished climbing to the summit. After taking a single sip of his water, he proceeded to dump the rest of it over his head, like he was bathing in it.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She cocked her head to the side, puzzled, unable to fully understand Kouenji’s behavior. Nanase and I signed up to register, securing our spots for the Task. However,&#160;because this Task was a one-on-one event, with the sexes divided into different competitions, there was no avoiding the fact that I would be going up against Kouenji. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Yes, you’re right. I did plot against you, senpai,” said Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But that being said, there are also some things that make it impossible for me to think of you as an enemy,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even though I have acted with such blatant hostility…?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Strange, huh?” I agreed. “Well, at any rate, the other reason I told you all that was because I figured that you would’ve figured it out to a certain extent anyway, even if I didn’t say anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though she had repeatedly looked surprised throughout this conversation, I was sure that she had picked up what my true intentions were. She was aware of my plans, but she was pretending not to have noticed anything. There was probably something else that she was trying to draw out of me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That being said, all that was just a hunch on my part, though,” I added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Afterward, Nanase fell silent. I wasn’t going to press the matter any further either, so we simply continued walking quietly through the woods. Right now, I thought, my top priority was reaching the next designated area.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">7.4</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">“P</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HEW… </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We managed to make it our final designated area in time, somehow.” Nanase slumped down onto the ground right on the spot and let out a heavy sigh, as if she were expelling all the exhaustion out of her body. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Our fourth designated area for the day had been B5, the area directly above B6. I was sure that even though we only traveled a short distance, that trip had been quite a burden for Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems like you’re pushing yourself pretty hard,” I remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was fine for a while when we set out from the starting area, but Nanase’s pace had gradually slowed as time went on. I’d been considering the option of leaving Nanase behind depending on how things played out and going for this designated area alone. In the end, though, </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">she had managed to stick with me all the way through sheer willpower.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To be completely honest, that swimming Task really took quite a lot out of me,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t have any doubt that it had taken all the remaining energy that she had left.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I think we’ll call it right here for today,” I told her. “We can take our time and look around for a place to pitch our tents.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We rested for a little while, waiting for Nanase to feel ready to walk, and then set out to find a suitable place to camp. After looking around in the immediate area, we came across a clearing. It looked like another group had already set up camp there. They must have been getting ready to eat dinner since they had various cooking utensils laid out in front of their tents.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There seemed to be plenty of space, enough for another group to set up. But it was a difficult ask to just go ahead and make camp when you would be right next to people who you didn’t know particularly well. Just as I was about to pass the campers by, one of them called out to us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey there!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was Hamaguchi Tetsuya, from Class 2-C. I gently waved in response, and then gestured for Nanase to come along.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You guys in a hurry to get somewhere?” asked Hamaguchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nah,” I said. “I was thinking of heading down closer to the ocean, but we’ve already reached our designated areas for today.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, why not hang around here for a bit?” he offered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had last spoken with Hamaguchi during the uninhabited island exam last year, and then during the exam on the cruise ship that was held while we were on our way back afterward. We only interacted for a brief period during those times, and we didn’t typically interact with each other at school. Our relationship wasn’t deep enough for us to call each other friends, and yet… What in the world was he thinking by trying to strike up a conversation with me?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was a prolonged period of silence. He looked at me, and then spoke up once more, sounding somewhat apologetic and sheepish. “If it’s a bother, though, you don’t have to force yourself to stay or anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nanase had been following along without voicing a single complaint, but I was sure that her fatigue must have been reaching its peak.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose we can take a short break,” I agreed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, man, come on over,” he replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hamaguchi ushered us over to his campsite as if he were inviting some close friends into his room. Just as you’d expect from one of Ichinose’s classmates, he really managed to create a kind, welcoming atmosphere here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, what interested me wasn’t Hamaguchi, but rather, his two companions. The sound of Hamaguchi and I talking had brought Andou Sayo and Minamikata Kozue out of their tents at almost the exact same time. They were whispering things to each other, and occasionally glanced over in my direction.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you’re not comfortable inviting me to stay, I can leave,” I stated.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since I was a student from a different class, if me hanging around here made them feel uncomfortable, it would probably be best that I just go. At least, that’s what I had thought was going on here, but Andou and Minamikata hurriedly stopped me from leaving. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no, no, it’s not like that. We were talking ’bout something else. Actually, we kind of wanted to talk to you, Ayanokouji-kun. You don’t have to go at all—it’s fine with us if you camp here tonight. Right, Kozue?” Andou looked to her friend Minamikata, who quickly nodded several times in agreement.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, since you’ve decided to stick around and kick back a bit after all, how about we have ourselves a welcoming party?” said Hamaguchi, bringing a backpack out from one of the tents.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When he unfastened the zipper of the bag, revealing the contents hidden within, we saw a bunch of canned foods peeking out.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s a heck of a lot of stuff</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">” I remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even with just the amount of food that I could see right now, I reckoned that they had enough to make it through a week quite easily.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To tell you the truth, all three of us actually had those cards that gave us one point five times the number of points to buy stuff with, at the start,” said Hamaguchi. “That’s why we have way more food than other groups.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although I had already looked into this matter and figured that out for myself, I figured that I’d just pretend like I was genuinely impressed with what he just told me. Under normal circumstances, a three-person group would have a total of 15,000 points to start. However, Hamaguchi and his teammates had 22,500 points. Even if they’d gone ahead and bought a whole lot of meat and a barbeque grill, they would still have plenty of change to spare. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, items like those wouldn’t be a good fit for travel. Plus, the weight would pile up. One of Class 2-C’s strengths was that their students rarely acted for their own selfish interest. Despite that, though, after taking a glance at this situation, you might think that Hamaguchi’s group had been spending their points wastefully. But that probably wasn’t the case here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Most likely, this was Ichinose’s idea. It would be extremely tough to move about with an abundance of food supplies, especially if you were lugging around cooking utensils such as grills since those things would generally get in the way. However, it’s a different story when someone is specifically tasked with holding onto the items. Ichinose had probably thought of a plan where students would share convenient items that would let them prepare meat and fish.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">According to the special exam’s rules, we were allowed to share food with one another. So, I supposed it was appropriate to think of these three as kind of like the kitchen staff for Class 2-C. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hamaguchi pulled a bunch of skewers out from the backpack.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Quite an interesting strategy, isn’t it?” mumbled Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She had most likely gone through the same process that I had.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Maybe so,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We first-year students are greatly lacking in terms of solidarity,” said Nanase. “I would have to say that there are few students in our grade level who would have any desire to be there for another.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Still, a different problem emerged when you considered a strategy like this. It was important that someone take on the role of guarding the food supplies, sure. But when a group stepped up to do that, they would run into issues trying to score points during the exam. In the worst-case scenario, I supposed that the penalty that came from failing to reach designated areas could be mitigated by one person. However, that group would still be gradually outpaced by their rivals around them, in which case that group would inevitably face the threat of expulsion.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You two okay with yakiniku?” asked Hamaguchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? What do you mean by that?” asked Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, we figured we should at least treat you to some dinner tonight. Right, guys?” said Hamaguchi, turning to his teammates for their approval.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Both girls immediately nodded in agreement, without a single shred of reluctance.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, wait, hold on.” I said. “I really appreciate the gesture, but I can’t accept.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, that’s right. Food is so valuable, after all,” added Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although Nanase was incredibly grateful for their goodwill, we both declined their offer. Apparently Hamaguchi wasn’t having it though, because he started preparing food for us anyway. He was far too good-natured. He should be using his resources to support his own classmates in need, rather than wasting them on helping people from other classes and grade levels. But without so much as a second’s thought, Hamaguchi took out a bunch of meat from the cooler that was stored inside the backpack.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really, don’t worry about it, it’s cool,” he said. “We just so happened to get some beef today as a reward from a Task is all. And it’s not going to last awfully long anyway, so we would’ve had to eat it up ourselves.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">From the looks of things, Hamaguchi was going to put together a real, honest-to-goodness meal, with cuts of meat on skewers. His group had even brought out mosquito repellant to make the atmosphere even more comfortable.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is it really okay for…you to treat people like us to a meal?” asked Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“’Course it is! Don’t be shy,” said Hamaguchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No matter how much people from their class tended to enjoy caring for others, I still had to ask myself, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Why me? </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’m sure they wouldn’t go around inviting every single student that passed them by.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you wondering why I called you over?” asked Hamaguchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, considering that you’re going as far as treating me to a meal, yeah, I’m definitely curious,” I answered. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After stopping to think for a short while, Hamaguchi started to speak once more to explain why he invited us over.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I guess it’s because we’ve been hearing about you a lot lately, Ayanokouji-kun. We just wanted to talk to you ourselves. Right guys?” said Hamaguchi, turning to his teammates.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, definitely,” said Andou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Both she and Minamikata seemed to be on board with the explanation that Hamaguchi gave.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it’s like… You know?” said Andou. She shot me an inquisitive glance, as if to say, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">You get it, right?</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I didn’t answer. Once Andou and Minamikata realized that I wasn’t responding, their expressions gradually changed to surprise.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Wait, hold on, then there really hasn’t been </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">any</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> kind of progress yet?” said Andou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No way!” said Minamikita. “I thought for sure they would’ve gotten to that ‘more than friends but not quite lovers’ yet stage.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know, right? I mean, Honami-chan has been saying Ayanokouji-kun’s name like all the time lately,” said Andou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really?” said Minamikata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I mean, I know it’s probably not our place to say anything, but… Is there any reason why they wouldn’t be going out?” said Andou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I had heard that girls liked talking about these kinds of things, I had no idea they could talk about it right in front of the person in question. Nanase must have just wrapped her mind around the situation here, because she turned to look at me with rapt attention.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Okay, I don’t really get what you’re talking about exactly,” I interrupted, “but I don’t think we’ll go out with each other.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, wait, wait, hold on,” said Andou, “You don’t think so? I’m just gonna say this once more to be sure, but you know we’re talking about Honami-chan, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t really speak for the guys or anything since they don’t really say anything, but you know like, probably 80 or 90 percent of the second-years like her, right?” said Minamikata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m pretty positive that’s true, yeah,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While there was certainly no denying the fact that Ichinose was quite popular, 90 percent was an exaggeration. I mean, the truth was that Sudou liked Horikita, Ike liked Shinohara, and so on. I’m sure there were many other cases of romantic attraction aside from them too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I mean, sure, you’re in different classes, but I don’t think you’ve got to worry about that stuff when it comes to love,” Andou said. “There are lots of people out there who are dating others regardless of class and grade level.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, but isn’t the biggest issue here that Ichinose isn’t even interested in someone like me to begin with?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh ho, is that modesty I hear? Ayanokouji-kun, you do know that you were kind of a hot topic among the girls when you first started school here, right?” said Andou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Thinking back on it now, I remembered that Kushida had said something like that too. I had simply chosen to not take what Kushida had said at face value though. More accurately, I just didn’t think too deeply about it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seems you’re quite popular, Ayanokouji-senpai,” said Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I’m really not, at all,” I insisted. “I’ve never had a girl say anything remotely like that to me before.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really?” said Minamikita. “Oh, yeah, I definitely remember his name going around a lot before, but I think people kinda moved on pretty quickly.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I guess there’s no helping that,” said Andou. “You can’t really know if you could love somebody unless you talked to them face-to-face, I think. And I remember that a year ago, Ayanokouji-kun wasn’t the type to really go around talking to people.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I kind of get the feeling he hasn’t changed all </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> much then,” said Minamikata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The two girls smiled and shared a laugh as they got strangely excited talking about me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you mean that Ayanokouji-senpai has changed somewhat since back then?” asked Nanase, asking the two girls a question of her own after having listened to the conversation so far.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I guess I kinda get the impression he’s sort of softer now?” Hamaguchi cut into the conversation to offer his thoughts, having just gotten back from using the restroom.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I hadn’t spoken with Andou or Minamikata before, I had spent some time together with Hamaguchi during the exam on the cruise ship, so it was reasonable to say he was someone who could make a fair comparison to what I was like a year ago versus now. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At any rate…I didn’t get the sense that these three were afraid of the possibility that they might get expelled. It&#160;wasn’t clear to me what their scores were exactly, of course, but I was sure that they couldn’t be near the top of the rankings. If that was the case, then…</span></p>
