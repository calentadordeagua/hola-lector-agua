<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 8:<br/>The Arrival Of Autumn</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">M</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">IYAKE HAD BEEN WAITING</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> for her near the school entrance. Hasebe approached him and lightly tapped him on the shoulder.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry to keep ya waiting,” said Hasebe.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nah, no big deal, I didn’t wait long,” Miyake said. “If&#160;anything, I was just kinda bored.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hasebe had been absent from school for a week, but since she came back, she had been showing up to class every day.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re sure you’re okay quitting the archery club?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s more like I was sticking with it out of force of habit anyway, or just inertia.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you quit because of me?” asked Hasebe.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not like that. I quit because I wanted to. That’s all there is to it. Anyway, I don’t really care about that, I’m glad you came back to school.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hasebe only participated in five competitions—the minimum number required. Although she didn’t see much in terms of results, she had at least contributed the bare minimum to the class. Still, she rarely spoke to anyone other than Miyake, and she was even somewhat estranged with Yukimura as well, who had agreed with Sakura’s expulsion. Miyake continued to stay by her side without a word, as though he had no other choice in the matter now. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At first, I thought I wanted to destroy everything,” she said. “I thought it’d be fine if I could just get back not just at Kiyopon, but at all the rest of our classmates who abandoned Airi too. They’re bad people. And I’m one of those bad people.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I… I understand how you feel,” said Miyake.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Someone had to be expelled in that situation. But it should have been Kushida-san. That was what they originally promised. That was the correct decision. Right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Yeah,” replied Miyake.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I won’t forgive Kiyopon. I won’t forgive our classmates. But I thought it’d be wrong to drag our feet forever to hold them back, to make them suffer for it.” Miyake responded with complete silence as Hasebe continued to confess everything that she was thinking. “Hey, Miyacchi. Just this once, would you…be with me and help me get revenge?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Judging from the look in her eyes, Hasebe wasn’t joking. But Miyake didn’t have the courage to ask her if she was being serious.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Haruka…” said Miyake.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What? Come on, I was just kidding.” She brushed it off with a laugh and then started walking. “I’ll get revenge on my own.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hasebe had reached out her hand to Miyake as she spoke but pulled it back. Now, she turned her back and left. Miyake, although hesitant, started walking after her without a word.</span></p>
