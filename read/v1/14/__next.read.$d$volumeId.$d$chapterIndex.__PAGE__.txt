<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 8:<br/>The Association of Failures</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">M</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">ay 1 came and went, and before we knew it, the school week was over. Ike and the others had started listening to the teacher. Only Sudou kept unashamedly falling asleep in class, but no one tried to reprimand him. Because we hadn’t yet found a method to increase our points, he’d apparently decided not to fix his habits. However, many of our classmates grew to disdain him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was a little sleepy, myself. It was hard to stay awake just before lunchtime. Also, I’d stayed up late last night watching an online video. Ah, sleep would feel so nice…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just as my head started to bob, sudden pain shot through my right arm.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s the matter, Ayanokouji? You cried out. Have you started your rebellious phase or something?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“N-no. Sorry, Chabashira-sensei. I got some dirt in my eye.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Normally, the other students would have started whispering. But, wary of potentially losing points, they instead shot me pained glances. As I rubbed the sting on my arm, I glared at my neighbor. Horikita brandished her mathematical compass. This was insane. Why did she even have a compass ready in the first place? You didn’t even really need one of those for this school. After class, I immediately went over to her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Certain things are off limits! It’s dangerous to stab someone!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you angry with me?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You put a hole in my arm! A hole!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What? When did I stab with you a compass needle, Ayanokouji-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re holding a dangerous weapon right now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, just because I’m holding something means I stabbed you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d spent most of the class wide-eyed, not because of the lecture, but the pain.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Be careful. If you get caught sleeping, that would undoubtedly lead to a loss in points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita had started to take action within Class D. Her protests to the school had gone nowhere. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Ah, that hurt!</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Damn it, if Horikita ever fell asleep in class, I’d return the favor. When everyone got up for lunch, Hirata spoke.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Chabashira-sensei mentioned that the midterm is coming up soon. Remember that if you fail, you’ll be expelled. Therefore, I think it would be a good idea to form a study group.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, the hero of Class D had started another project.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you neglect your studies, you’ll get a failing grade and be expelled on the spot. I want to avoid that. However, studying won’t just prevent expulsion; it may also help earn points. If we receive high marks, our class’s assessment should improve as a result. I asked some of the students who scored high on the test to help prepare a study plan. So, I would like people who are anxious to come join our group. Everyone is welcome, of course.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata stared directly at Sudou while making his grand speech.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tch.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou averted his gaze, crossed his arms, and closed his eyes. Ever since Sudou had stomped all over Hirata’s introduction game, their relationship had been rocky. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Starting today at five o’clock, we will plan to study in this classroom for two hours a day until the test. If you’d like to join us, please come whenever you like. Of course, I don’t mind if you need to leave partway. That’s all I have to say.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Immediately after he’d finished speaking, several of the failing students got up and went over. There were three people with failing grades who didn’t rush to Hirata, though: Sudou, Ike, and Yamauchi. Ike and Yamauchi seemed unsure of what to do for a moment, but in the end, they stayed in their seats. I couldn’t tell whether they were afraid Sudou might lose his temper, or because they were jealous of Hirata’s popularity.</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">8.1</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“A</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">re you free for lunch?</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Do you want to eat together?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">During our break, Horikita came up and invited me out.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s unusual to get an invitation from you. I feel nervous.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s no reason to be. I can treat you to the vegetable meal set, if that’s okay with you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Wait, wasn’t that the free meal?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m joking. Seriously, whatever you want to eat is on me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now I’m </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">definitely</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> scared. Is there some kind of catch?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">An invitation from Horikita was suspicious enough. The suddenness of the request also gave me pause. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If people can’t honestly accept kindness, then mankind will meet its end, won’t it?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I suppose so, but…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With no other plans, I decided to follow Horikita to the cafeteria, where I chose one of the most expensive special meal sets. Together, we sat down.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, shall we eat?” she asked. Horikita started at me intently, as if she were waiting for me to begin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s the matter, Ayanokouji-kun? Aren’t you going to eat?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was definitely a catch, no doubt about it. Nevertheless, I couldn’t just sit here and not eat. Letting the food get cold would be a waste. I hesitantly bit into my croquette.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know this is rather sudden, but I want to talk to you about something.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have a bad feeling about this…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just as I was preparing to flee, she grabbed my hand. “Ayanokouji-kun, I’ll ask once again. Will you listen to me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ever since Chabashira-sensei’s warning, fewer people have arrived late or talked in class. When I say we eliminated more than half of the reasons our class got into trouble, I’m not exaggerating.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, that’s true. It wasn’t really a difficult issue to begin with, though.” There was no guarantee things would continue in this vein, but at least these last few days had been considerably better than before.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The next step is to improve our chances of scoring well on the midterm. Hirata-kun started taking action toward that end earlier.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The study groups, huh? Well, I suppose that a study group certainly could help. Only…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Only what? It sounds like you’re implying something. What’s the problem?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nothing. Don’t worry about it. I have to say, it’s unusual to see you so worried for others.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t truly imagine failing a test. However, it’s true that some students in this world can do just that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sudou and the others, you mean? You’re vicious as ever, I see.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m just telling the truth.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Because students couldn’t leave campus, contact anyone on the outside, or even attend cram schools, their only option was to help one another.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m relieved that Hirata-kun set up a study group. However, Sudou-kun, Ike-kun, and Yamauchi-kun didn’t join, did they? That worries me,” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, those guys. I wouldn’t say they’re Hirata’s enemies, but they aren’t on good terms with him. They wouldn’t join.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, in other words, there remains a high probability that those three will fail. In order to reach Class A, we need to avoid demerits and build toward a positive evaluation, correct? I think it highly likely that good test scores will help with that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I suppose it’s only natural that a student would expect their grade to reflect how much effort they put into the test.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What if you also held a study group like Hirata’s, specifically for helping Sudou and Ike?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure. I wouldn’t have any objections to that. You probably find that rather surprising, though, don’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, everything about your behavior up until now has been surprising.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wasn’t actually surprised, though. Horikita was doing this all for her own benefit. Personally, I’d never thought that Horikita was that cold of a person.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I understand that you want to move up to Class A. However, I didn’t think that you’d opt for ordinary methods like tutoring them. Typically, failing students tend not to like studying. Besides, you’ve kept your distance from the other students since the first day, right? I doubt someone who considers friends unnecessary would be able to bring people together easily.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s why I’m asking you. Fortunately, you’re already friendly with these people, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Hey, wait. You couldn’t mean…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’ll be quicker if </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> try to convince them. It shouldn’t be a problem; they’re happy to say that you’re friends, right? Bring them to the library, and I’ll tutor them.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is insane. Do you honestly think someone who does his best to lead an utterly harmless and inoffensive life would be able to do something that requires actual social skills?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not a matter of can or can’t. Just do it,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Was I her pet dog or something?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You can aim for Class A, but don’t involve me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You ate the food I treated you to, right? Lunch. The special set. A wonderful, delicious meal.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I simply received the honest goodwill of another human being.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, that wasn’t out of goodwill. I had an ulterior motive.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry, I didn’t hear a word you said. Here, have some points, my </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">treat</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. Now we’re even.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I refuse to stoop so low as to accept handouts from others,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think this might be the first time I’m actually angry at you…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So what will you do? Cooperate? Or make me your enemy?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It almost feels like you’re holding a gun to my head.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, not ‘almost.’ I really </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">am </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">threatening you,” replied Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The power of violence certainly was effective. Well…if all I did was gather them together, there wasn’t anything particularly wrong with cooperating. After all, due to Horikita’s stance against friendship, she wouldn’t be effective at diplomacy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Furthermore, it’d taken a lot of time and trouble to become friends with Sudou and Ike. I’d hate for them to have to drop out so quickly. Sensing my hesitation, Horikita pressed me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t think I’ve forgiven you for conspiring with Kushida-san and inviting me out under false pretenses, do you?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You said you wouldn’t blame me. Bringing that up is unfair.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I said that to Kushida-san. I don’t remember saying that to </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow. You play dirty.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you want my forgiveness, cooperate with me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It looked as though I’d never had an escape route in the first place. At this juncture, the only way to avoid a hassle would be to help her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t guarantee anything. Are you okay with that?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I believe you’ll find a way. Oh, here’s my phone number and email. If something happens, contact me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although the circumstances were unusual, I’d gotten a girl’s contact information for the first time in my high school life. It was Horikita’s, though, so I wasn’t particularly happy.</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">8.2</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 3.00em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">glanced around the classroom. What was I supposed to do now? If I said, “Hey, want to study with me after class?” would anyone even come?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou and I were close enough that he might, but I wasn’t sure about the others. Well, with nothing to lose, I decided to try.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Sudou. Have a minute?” I called as he headed back to the classroom after lunch. He was sweating and a bit short of breath. Probably played some basketball during his lunch break.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you going to do about the midterm?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, that. I don’t know. I’ve never really studied seriously before,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, yeah? Well, I have just the thing. I wanted to form a study group to meet every day after class, starting today. Want to join?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou stared at me, his mouth slightly agape. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You serious? If the lessons are a pain in my ass, why would studying </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">after</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> class be any better? Besides, I have club activities, so it’s pointless. Plus, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you’re</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> going to be tutoring? Your scores weren’t great, either.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry about that part. Horikita’s the tutor.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita? I don’t really know anything about her. Sounds fishy; I’ll pass. I’ll be fine cramming for the test the night before.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou had refused to join, just as I’d guessed. If I persisted, it’d fall on deaf ears. Damn, was it really useless? If I tried to press him further, he might punch me. Perhaps there was no helping it. Maybe I should start with someone more manageable. I called out to Ike, who was playing on his phone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Ike, h—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Pass! I heard you talking with Sudou. Study group? No way. Not my thing.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You do know you’ll be expelled if you fail, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, yeah. I might have gotten failing grades before, but I’m doing much better now. I’ll just cram the night before with Sudou.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Was he really fine with that? He didn’t seem to grasp the danger of this situation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If the last test hadn’t been such a surprise sucker punch, I probably could’ve gotten, like, forty points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know what you mean, but wouldn’t it be better to stick together on this?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A high school student’s free time is precious, you know? I don’t want to waste it studying.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He waved me off, completely focused on texting with some girl. Ever since Hirata had managed to get a girlfriend, Ike was desperate to find a girl of his own. My shoulders slumped as I returned to my seat. Maybe if I told Horikita I’d tried my best, she’d forgive me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No good,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Uh, what do you mean by that?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I said ‘no good.’ You really didn’t think it would be that simple, did you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Damn it. She’d completely ignored my appeal. How shameless.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, of course not. I still have 425 plans remaining,” I grumbled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I looked around the room. Contrary to the tension of class, lunch had a friendlier, albeit noisier, atmosphere. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I needed a method to get reluctant students to buckle down and work. Also, I needed a way to get them to study during free time, not during class. Normally I wouldn’t involve myself, but they were in danger of expulsion. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was sure that Sudou would participate if given the chance. Now I had no choice but to find some kind of incentive. I needed him to think there’d be a juicy bonus he’d get by studying. I’d require something concrete and easy to understand. Something effective.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And then it hit me!</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Blessed with a divine revelation, I turned, wide-eyed, to Horikita.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I could see that the elevator had stopped on the seventh floor. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Looking wary of her surroundings, she exited the building. After she’d vanished into the night, I decided to follow after her. However, I instinctively hid again after I turned the corner.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s… You’re wrong about that. I’ll show you. I’ll reach Class A right away, then—”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I will definitely, definitely reach—”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He moved forward, as if about to act. The situation looked fraught with danger. Resigned to facing Horikita’s anger, I leapt out from my hiding space and went after her brother.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A-Ayanokouji-kun?!” Horikita cried.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Reluctantly, I released her brother. Instantly, he tried to backhand me in the face. I instinctively took a step back to avoid it. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Watch it!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He had enough power to knock me out with one blow. Looking slightly confused, he exhaled deeply, extended his right arm, and opened his hand.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re rather talkative today, too. It’s odd for someone who likes to avoid trouble to talk so much.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If someone lacks persuasiveness, he or she won’t be able to make others believe in their cunning theories.” She clearly wanted to know why I was so desperate to persuade her that Sudou and the others’ expulsion was bad. “Cut the crap.
