<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 12:<br/>Victory Celebration</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“C</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">heers!” Ike shouted joyfully and toasted with a can of juice. That evening, the former association of failures had convened once again. Liberated from our studies, we were all overjoyed that no one had been expelled. Everyone smiled… Well, except for Horikita. We’d shared our burdens, and together, we’d overcome the challenge. Perhaps that was the point of being young. I guess if you ignored the one dark spot, this wasn’t terrible.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s with the long face, Ayanokouji? Sudou wasn’t expelled. Everything’s fine now, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t particularly mind that you’re holding a celebration party, but why are you holding it in </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">my room</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Mine’s a mess. So are Sudou’s and Yamauchi’s. And we can’t go to a girl’s room, right? I mean, yeah, I would’ve </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">loved</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> going to Kushida-chan’s room. But your spectacularly plain and empty room is the best option.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s only been two months since school started. I think it’d be weird to have a lot of stuff.” Aside from daily necessities, I didn’t really need anything.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you think, Kushida-chan?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think it’s fine here. It’s simple, but it feels nice and clean.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Right? Man, it must be nice to have Kushida-chan praise you. Ha ha ha ha!” Ike grudgingly pushed me. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All things considered, though, that midterm was dangerous. If we hadn’t put the study group together, I would’ve been fine, but Ike and Sudou would have definitely gotten kicked out.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? You were close to getting expelled, too, you know.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, no, I could have gotten a perfect score if I were serious about it. Really.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Everything was thanks to Horikita-san’s efforts. She tutored Ike, Yamauchi, and Sudou.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita sat outside the circle, quietly reading a novel. When we said her name, she bookmarked her page and looked up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I did it for my own sake. If someone had been expelled, Class D’s evaluation would’ve worsened.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just say that you didn’t want us to get expelled, even if it’s a lie. We’d like you better.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’d be fine with me if you didn’t.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, her attitude remained unchanged, but simply participating in this gathering was a sign of her progress. The old Horikita most definitely would not have come.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I guess, but…you’re a surprisingly good person, Horikita,” replied Sudou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since Horikita had apologized to Sudou, he’d completely stopped antagonizing her. Before, he’d said she was a bad person. But people could change.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Anyway, why did Chabashira-sensei change her mind about expelling Sudou-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wondered about that, too. What kind of sorcery did you use, Horikita-chan?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm, I don’t really remember.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whoa, it’s a secret?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike tumbled over in an exaggerated fashion.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even though we managed to make it through the midterm, we shouldn’t lose our heads. Our next challenge is the final exam. We should expect those questions to be even more difficult than the ones today. In addition, we still need to find a way to increase our points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do we really need to start this hellish cramming all over again? This sucks.” Still on the floor, Ike buried his head in his hands.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t you think that if we start right now, it won’t be hellish?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No!” He sounded sure about that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t understand this school at all. I don’t get the class divisions, the point system, anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, points. I want points! Living in poverty really sucks.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After Ike and Yamauchi had used up all of their points, they’d had to resort to living off the school’s free offerings.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Horikita-san. Do you think it’ll be really difficult to get more points?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We tried so hard on the midterm that they’ll definitely give us some points, right?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you not see Class D’s average score? We were the lowest of all of the classes by far. If you think that we’ll get points for that, then I think you need to open your eyes.” Horikita spoke the truth without mercy, without sugarcoating anything.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Then we’re not getting any points next month, either. Boo.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think you should learn to live a more modest life and give up on points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry, Ike-kun. We may not get any points right now, but surely we’ll get some soon. Right, Horikita-san?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder about that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Can I say something? We’re all friends here, after all. Horikita-san, Ayanokouji-kun, and I are all working together to get into Class A. If you’re okay with it, I want you three to help us,” Kushida said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Get into…Class A? A-are you serious?” Ike said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I absolutely am. Increasing our points is an inevitable part of getting to the top as well.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“B-but, isn’t the idea of reaching Class A kind of ridiculous? They’re all smart, right? It’d be impossible for us to win against those guys by studying.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When you considered their average test scores, everyone in that class was probably on Horikita’s level.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Studying isn’t the sole factor in deciding who goes into what class, though. Right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, but if you can’t study at </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">all</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, then moving up is out of the question.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The three least-academically gifted people averted their eyes and whistled nonchalantly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’re still far from our goal, but we can do it if we all work together. I know it.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What makes you so sure?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What makes me sure, hmm? Well, you know what they say, ‘A single arrow is easily broken, but not ten in a bundle,’ I suppose.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think that even if you bundled these ten together, they’d still break,” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-well how about this? Three heads are better than one! Or something like that,” Kushida said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess if you combined all three of their test scores, you’d get one normal person’s grade.” Every time Kushida tried to raise the three up, Horikita would knock them back down. What an amazing pair.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If we keep going back and forth like this, though, we won’t get anywhere. It’s definitely better for us to get along.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose that, logically, you’re right.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita didn’t try to argue further. At any rate, if we wanted to move up, we’d need to get along with as many of our classmates as possible. We gained nothing by fighting one another.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So that’s why I’d like to ask the three of you to help us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Gladly!” replied Ike and Yamauchi in unison.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, if Horikita asked me to help, I would. I guess.” Sudou tried to hide his embarrassment when he spoke.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I never wanted your help, Sudou-kun. Besides, I have a hard time imagining how you’ll be useful in the first place.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Grr. I just thought I’d try to be nice, is all, and—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You were trying to be nice? I’m surprised.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Unsurprisingly, Sudou looked angry, but it didn’t seem like he was going to raise his fist. Wow, he was making progress, too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re a really annoying girl,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you. I appreciate your words of praise.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You are not cute at all, lady.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You say that, but how do you </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">really</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> feel?” teased Ike.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou instantly glared at Ike and put him in a headlock.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ow! Ow, ow! S-stop!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you say anything else, I’m going to strangle you!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-you already </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">are</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> strangling me, jeez! I give, I give!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita sighed deeply. Her eyes seemed to ask, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Is this male bonding?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In this school, ability is paramount. I’m positive that our competition will grow even more severe moving forward. If you say you’ll work with us, know that you can’t do so half-heartedly. Otherwise you’ll be a burden.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, if it comes down to physical ability, leave it to me. I have serious basketball and fighting skills.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I really can’t expect anything from you at all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ability was paramount, huh? I felt my chest tighten. We’d been isolated from the world and now thrust into this situation. Perhaps we were cursed. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita seriously planned on getting into Class A. Her will was unshakeable. However, our path out of Class D would not be an easy one. Considering our current performance, it was hard to imagine us even reaching Class C. What should we do from here?
