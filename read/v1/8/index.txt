<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 2:<br/>Welcome to the School<br/>Life of Your Dreams</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“A</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">yanokouji-kun, do you have a moment?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She came. She was here. It was terrifying. I’d been feigning sleep during class, pondering society’s true purpose while I pretended to nap, when the devil approached me. Shostakovich’s Symphony No. 11 played in my head, music that captured the sense of people fleeing from pursuing demons and the desperation that comes at the end of the world. Right then, it was the perfect accompaniment. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though my eyes were closed, I understood. I could feel the devil’s presence as she waited for her slave to awaken. So, as a slave, how exactly could I get out of this situation?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My computer-like brain instantly executed all the calculations to arrive at the answer I most needed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Conclusion: I’d pretend not to hear her. I had dubbed this the “Sleeping Strategy.” If she were a kind girl, then she would say something like, “Aw, well, there’s nothing to be done. I’d feel bad to wake you, so I’ll forgive you.&#160;★” “If you don’t get up, I’ll kiss you!” would also be okay.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you do not awaken within three seconds, I will bestow additional punishment upon you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean, ‘punishment’?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In an instant, I’d abandoned my “Sleeping Strategy” and yielded to her threats of force. Well, at least I offered some resistance by not meeting her gaze.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“See, you </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">are</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> awake after all, aren’t you?” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know enough that I’m afraid to make you angry.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Glad to hear it. Well then, may I have a little of your time?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If I refuse?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, even though you have no right to veto such a decision, I suppose I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">would</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> be exceptionally displeased.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She continued with, “And when I am displeased, then I will prove a major obstacle to your school life, Ayanokouji-kun. For example, I might set a great number of thumbtacks on your chair. Or, when you go to the toilet, I might splash water on you from above. Or stab you with the needle of my mathematical compass. Those kinds of obstacles, I suppose.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s nothing but harassment, or rather, bullying! And besides, that last one sounds strangely familiar, because you’ve already stabbed me before!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I reluctantly sat up at my desk. A girl with beautiful, sharp eyes and long black hair that framed her face stared down at me. Her name was Horikita Suzune, a student of the Tokyo Metropolitan Advanced Nurturing High School, Class D, and my classmate.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry. That was only a joke. I wouldn’t splash water on you from above.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s more pressing are the thumbtacks and the compass needle! Look at this! There are still marks from when you stabbed me the last time! Will you take responsibility if it scars me for life?” I rolled up my right sleeve and displayed my forearm to Horikita, so she could see the scars she’d left behind.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Evidence?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What about the evidence? Did you decide I’m the culprit without evidence?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was right; there was no evidence. Even though Horikita was the only one in class close enough to stab me with a needle, I’d be hard-pressed to call that definitive proof…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, I needed to confirm something first anyway.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, I’m required to help you? I’ve thought on it again, and, after all, I—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun. Would you prefer to regret while you suffer or regret while you despair? Which would you like more? Because if you refuse me and force my hand, it will be your responsibility.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was stuck with Horikita’s two completely absurd choices. It appeared she would not accept any delays. Though it was a mistake to make a deal with this devil, I gave up and obeyed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right, then. What am I supposed to do?” I asked, filled with trepidation. Her requests no longer surprised me. I certainly didn’t like how this situation had turned out, but… I thought back to when I’d met this girl two months ago, on the day of the entrance ceremony.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">2.1</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">pril. The school entrance ceremony. I rode the bus to school, bobbing and shaking in my seat. While I looked idly out my window, watching the city’s scenery change, the bus picked up more and more passengers.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Most of them were young people wearing high school uniforms.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was also a frustrated salary worker, who looked like the type to have once mistakenly groped someone aboard a crowded bus. An unsteady elderly lady stood in front of me, wobbling so badly I thought she was in danger of falling. Considering I’d known how packed this bus would likely be, I supposed I was simply reaping what I’d sown by getting on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was fortunate to have found a seat, but it was still crowded. I forgot about the unfortunate elderly woman and patiently waited to arrive at my destination, my mind clear as a passing stream. The weather was especially fine today, not a single cloud in the sky. It was so refreshing that I nearly fell asleep then and there.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, my gentle respite was promptly obliterated.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Excuse me, but shouldn’t you offer up your seat?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My eyes, which were about to close, snapped back open. Huh? Could this person be angry with me? But I realized that it was someone else being scolded. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A young, well-built blond man of high school age had sat down in one of the priority seats. The elderly woman stood right next to him, and another woman stood beside her. This second, younger lady appeared to be an office worker.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, you there. Can’t you see that this elderly woman is having trouble?” the office lady said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She seemed to want the young man to offer up his seat.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her voice carried quite well throughout the quiet bus, attracting several people’s attention.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s a really crazy question, lady,” the boy said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered whether the boy was angry, unobservant, or just painfully honest. In any case, he grinned broadly and crossed his legs. “Why should I offer up my seat? There’s no reason for me to do so.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re sitting in a priority seat. It’s natural to offer up those seats to the elderly.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t understand. Priority seats are just that: priority seats. I have no legal obligation to move. Since I’m currently occupying this seat, I should be the one who determines whether or not I move. Am I supposed to give up my seat just because I’m young? Ha! That reasoning is nonsense.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He didn’t speak like a normal high school student. His hair was dyed blond, which made him stand out.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m a healthy young person who certainly wouldn’t find standing inconvenient. However, I’d obviously expend more energy by standing than I would by sitting. I have no intention of doing such a pointless thing. Or are you suggesting I should act a bit livelier, I wonder?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what kind of attitude is that to take with your superiors?” she demanded.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Superiors? Well, it’s obvious that both you and the old woman there been alive longer than I have. There can be no doubt about that. However, the word ‘superior’ ­implies that you’re referring to someone of a higher position. In addition, we have another problem. Even though our ages are different, wouldn’t you agree that you have an impertinent attitude and are being extremely rude?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wha— You’re a high schooler, aren’t you?! You should be quiet and listen to what adults tell you!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s f-fine, whatever…” the elderly woman mumbled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She apparently didn’t want any further commotion and tried to calm the office lady. But after being insulted by the high school student, the younger woman still seemed very upset.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Apparently, this elderly woman is more perceptive than you, which is nice. Also, I haven’t given up on Japanese society yet. Please enjoy your remaining years.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After flashing a pointlessly vigorous smile, the boy slid his earphones in and began listening to rather cacophonous music. The office lady now clenched her teeth in frustration. Though she tried needling the boy by arguing further, his smug, self-important attitude remained fixed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At any rate, I had to at least partly agree with the boy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If you ignored the question of a moral imperative, it was true that he wasn’t legally obligated to give up his seat.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry…” Desperately fighting back her tears, the office lady apologized to the elderly woman.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, it was all just a minor incident on the bus. I was relieved that I hadn’t been caught up in the situation. Honestly, I couldn’t care less about giving up my seat for an elderly person.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Clearly, the egotistical boy had won. At least, everyone secretly thought so.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um… I think that the lady is right.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The woman received unexpected support from someone standing alongside her. The helper, a girl wearing my high school’s uniform, gave her brave and frank opinion to the boy. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And the new challenger is a pretty girl, eh? It would seem that I’m rather lucky with the fairer sex,” the boy said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This poor woman appears to have been suffering for quite some time now. Won’t you offer up your seat? While you might consider such courtesy unnecessary, I think it would contribute greatly to society.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Crack!</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> The boy snapped his fingers. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A contribution to society, you say? Well, that </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">is</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> a rather interesting opinion. It’s certainly true that offering up one’s seat to the elderly could be viewed in such a positive light. Unfortunately, I have no interest in contributing to society. I care only for my own satisfaction. Oh, and one more thing. You’re asking me, the one in the priority seat, to give up his spot, but couldn’t you simply ask one of the other people seated on this crowded bus? If you truly cared for the elderly, then something like priority seating would be a rather trivial concern, wouldn’t you agree?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The boy’s haughty attitude remained unchanged. Both the office lady and the elderly woman simply wore bitter smiles in response. However, the girl didn’t back down.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Everyone, please listen to me for just a moment. Won’t someone give up their seat for this woman? It doesn’t matter who. Please.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Text_body__And__Centre_Alignment__And__Page_Break"><span><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol1_INTERIORIMAGES_" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v1/OEBPS/Images/ClassroomElite-Vol1_INTERIORIMAGES_.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top;"/></span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">How could someone pour so much courage, determination, and compassion into so few words? That was no simple feat. The girl might have seemed like a nuisance to those around her, but she appealed to the other passengers earnestly and without fear.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though not in a priority seat, I was near the elderly woman. I imagined if I raised my hand and offered my spot, then the matter would be settled. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, like everyone else, I didn’t move. None of us had thought it necessary to move. The boy’s attitude and remarks aside, everyone on the bus had, for the most part, agreed with him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Now, of course, the elderly have undeniable worth to Japan. But we, the youth, will continue to support Japan into the future. Also, considering that our society ages more and more every year, you could say that our youthful value only increases. So, if you were to examine both the elderly and the young and ask yourself which group is more valuable, the answer should be obvious. That’s really the perfect argument, wouldn’t you say?</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 3.00em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">disliked the entrance ceremony and imagined that many first-year students probably felt the same. Failure to do so signals the beginning of a rather tragic three years.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As someone who dislikes trouble, I decided I’d like to establish proper relationships. Unfamiliar with the notion, I’d spent the day before in preparation, running through different scenarios. </span></p>

I had ventured alone into a battlefield, and it was do or die. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Looking around the classroom, I walked toward the seat that bore my nameplate. It was at the back of the room, near the window. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Not good. I was trapped in a downward spiral.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In the end, I couldn’t talk to anyone at all. At the rate things were going, I’d be completely alone. Do people become friends after sharing a meal together? Can you become friends with someone after you walk to the bathroom together for the first time? The more I thought about it, the more I wondered: What is friendship? Is it something deep and meaningful? I tried to piece it together.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Trying to make friends is incredibly bothersome. Besides, don’t human relationships tend to form naturally? My thoughts were in utter disarray, as though a raucously loud festival was being staged inside my head. While I sat lost in a haze, the classroom quickly filled. Fine. Whatever. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Before I knew it, the rotund, bespectacled boy in front of me had started to talk with another classmate.</span></p>

Some of the girls started giggling. Inogashira was paralyzed with fear. She couldn’t move a muscle. Another girl spoke up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s okay to go slowly. Don’t rush.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although it might seem kind, saying, “Do your best!” and, “It’s okay!” it actually conveys a completely different meaning. To someone who is extremely nervous, “Do your best!” and, “It’s okay!” can actually seem forceful, as if indicating she needs to match her classmates. On the other hand, saying, “Just take things slowly. Don’t rush,” allows her to take things at her own pace.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After that, the girl calmed down and regained her composure. She took a few small breaths and tried again. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My name is Inogashira…Kokoro. Um, my hobby is sewing. I’m pretty good at knitting. I-It’s nice to meet you all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was able to finish without stopping. Looking alternately relieved, delighted, and embarrassed, Inogashira sat down. Other introductions followed hers.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m Yamauchi Haruki. I competed in table tennis during elementary school, and in junior high I was the ace player on our baseball team. I was number four. I got hurt during the inter-high school championships, though, and I’m undergoing rehab now. Nice to meet you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t think that the number of his baseball uniform was essential information…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Besides, I’d thought the inter-high championship was a national sports competition for high school students. Junior high school kids were supposed to be ineligible.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Was he trying to crack a joke? He seemed like a talkative guy who got carried away pretty easily.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, I’m next, aren’t I?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The cheerful girl who stood up was the same one who’d told Inogashira to go slowly and calm down. She was also the same girl who’d helped out the elderly woman on the bus that morning.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My name is Kushida Kikyou. None of my friends from junior high made it to this school, so I’m alone here. I’d like to get to know all of your names and faces right away and become friends as soon as possible!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While most of the students had only said a few words of introduction, Kushida continued to talk. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My first goal is to become friends with everyone. So, after we’re finished with introductions, I’d love for you to share your contact information with me!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She wasn’t just saying that. I could tell right away that this girl was the type to open up her heart to anyone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her encouraging words to Inogashira hadn’t been platitudes, but a genuine reflection of her feelings.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, after school or during vacations, I want to make all sorts of memories with lots of people. Please feel free to invite me to lots and lots of events! Anyway, I’ve talked for a long time, so I’ll end my introduction here.”</span></p>

<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She said it as though she knew I’d been critiquing everyone’s introductions. I felt strangely uncomfortable, and I wasn’t sure why.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What should I say when my turn came? Should I make a joke? Should I go into it with really high energy in order to get some laughs? No, that wouldn’t work. Going out of control would just ruin the atmosphere. Besides, that didn’t really fit my personality anyway.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The introductions continued while I wrestled with my anxiety.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, next up is…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As Hirata looked encouragingly toward the next student, that student glared back. His hair was dyed a fiery red. He both looked and sounded like a delinquent.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What, are we a bunch of little kids or something? I don’t need to introduce myself. People who want to do that can go ahead. Just leave me out of it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The red-haired guy scowled at Hirata. He had quite a presence, his attitude intense and overpowering.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t force you to introduce yourself, of course. However, I don’t think that getting along with your classmates is a bad thing. If I’ve made you uncomfortable, I apologize.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When Hirata bowed his head, some of the girls glared at the guy with red hair.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Isn’t it fine to introduce yourself?” one of them snapped.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, yeah!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I’d expected, the pretty-boy soccer star had captured most of the girls’ hearts in the blink of an eye. However, half of the male students started to look angry, probably out of jealousy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Shut it. I don’t care. I didn’t come here to make friends.” The guy with red hair got up from his seat. It seemed he had no intention of getting to know anyone. Several other students followed suit and left the classroom together. Horikita got up and briefly glanced my way. When she realized I wasn’t moving, she started to walk out the door. Hirata looked a little lonely when he saw Horikita head out.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“They’re not a bad bunch. It’s my fault. I was being selfish and made people do this.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No way. You didn’t do anything wrong, Hirata-kun. Let’s just leave those guys be, okay?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although some people had rebelled at the idea of introductions, the students who remained were happy to continue. In the end, things wrapped up in a rather ordinary fashion.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m Ike Kanji. I love girls, and I hate pretty boys. I’m currently in the market for a new girlfriend. It’s nice to meet you! All the better if you’re a cutie or a beauty!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was difficult to tell if he was joking or not. At the very least, the girls looked at him with revulsion.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow. You are </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">so</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> cool, Ike-kun,” one girl said, in a completely emotionless voice. Of course, her statement was 1000 percent false.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seriously? Seriously? Oh, man. I mean, I thought that I wasn’t bad or anything, but…heh heh.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, Ike thought that she was being serious. He blushed. Instantly, the girls started to laugh.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, wow. He’s </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">cute</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, huh, everyone? He’s looking for a girlfriend!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Dude, they’re making fun of you.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Ike continued to jovially go along with the teasing. He didn’t seem like a bad guy, though.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Next up was the combative boy from the bus, Kouenji. While inspecting his bangs in a hand mirror, he combed his hair.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Excuse me, can you introduce yourself?” Hirata asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmph. Fine.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He smirked like an aristocrat, displaying his impudent attitude. As he shifted in his seat, I thought he might leave, but Kouenji placed both of his legs on his desk and introduced himself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My name is Kouenji Rokusuke. As the sole male heir to the Kouenji conglomerate group, I will soon be tasked with carrying Japan into the future. I sincerely look forward to making your acquaintance, ladies.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He aimed his introduction solely at the opposite sex, rather than the entire class. After hearing that he was rich, some of the girls looked at him with sparkling eyes, while others regarded Kouenji as if he were nothing more than a weirdo. That was only natural.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Starting today, I will mercilessly punish anyone who makes me uncomfortable. Please exercise proper precaution so that you may avoid that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, Kouenji-kun. What exactly do you mean when you say, ‘anyone who makes me uncomfortable’?” asked Hirata, who looked uneasy at the word “punish.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I meant exactly what I said. If asked to give an example, well… I would say I hate ugly things, for instance. So, if I saw something ugly, I would do just as I said.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Fwish!</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> He flipped his long, flowing bangs.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, thank you. I will be careful then.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was the guy with red hair, Horikita, Kouenji, Yamauchi, and Ike. Apparently, this class was full of people with bizarre idiosyncrasies. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I, too, was especially peculiar, in that there was nothing peculiar about me. I had wanted to be free, free as a bird, but prior to this I’d languished in a cage. I had wanted to fly into the expansive open skies. If you looked out the window, you could watch birds gracefully soaring… Well, not right now, but in general. Anyway, that’s the kind of guy I was.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, time for the next person. Can you please introduce yourself?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Oh, shoot. My turn had come while I’d been daydreaming. Students turned, waiting for my introduction. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Hey, hey! Don’t look at me with so much anticipation. Oh well, I might as well try my best</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Clack!</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> The chair rattled as I stood.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um. Well, my name is Ayanokouji Kiyotaka. And, uh, I don’t really have any special skills or anything. I’ll do my best to get along with all of you. It’s, uh, nice to meet you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well? Was </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> my introduction?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d failed!</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I instinctively buried my head in my hands. I hadn’t had time to construct a proper introduction because I’d been too busy daydreaming. It was the worst possible intro. It didn’t attract attention, and absolutely no one would remember it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s nice to meet you, Ayanokouji-kun. I always want to be friends with everyone, just like you. Let’s both do our best, okay?” Hirata responded with a refreshing smile.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Everyone clapped. Their applause felt somewhat like pity, which strangely pained me. Despite that, however, I felt kind of glad.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">2.3</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">E</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">ven though people said this place was tough, the entrance ceremony was the same as any other school’s. Some important people offered words of thanks, and the ceremony concluded without incident. Then, it was noon. After we received some general information about the campus, the crowd dispersed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">70–80 percent of the students headed toward the dormitories. The remaining students quickly formed into groups. Some made their way to cafés, while the louder ones went out for karaoke. The hustle and bustle quickly died down. On a whim, I decided to swing by the convenience store on my way back to the dormitory. Of course, I went alone. I didn’t have a chaperone, or acquaintance, or anyone like that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My, what an unpleasant coincidence.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Entering the convenience store, I ran into Horikita once again. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, there’s no need to be so hostile. Anyway, did you need to buy something?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, just a few things. I came to get some necessities.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was no shortage of things you needed when starting life in a dorm, especially if you were a girl. Horikita took various necessities like shampoo off the shelves and promptly threw them into the basket she was carrying. I’d thought she would choose higher quality items, but she only took the cheapest options.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I thought girls usually made a fuss over what kind of shampoo they bought.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that depends on the person, doesn’t it? I’m the sort who doesn’t know when you might need money,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She shot me an icy glare that seemed to say, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Could you please not inspect other people’s purchases without their permission?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At any rate, I was terribly surprised that you stayed for introductions,” she said. “You didn’t look like the type to hang out with a circle of classmates.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I decided to participate precisely because I don’t like trouble. Why didn’t you introduce yourself to them, Horikita? You could have gotten to know several other students, and it would have been a chance to make friends.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Quite a few of the students had exchanged cell numbers, too. If Horikita had participated, she would probably have become quite popular. What a waste.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There are several reasons why I objected, but I suppose it might be better if I simply explain, hmm? My introduction might have sown discord, depending on how things went. Thus, doing nothing avoided creating more problems. Am I wrong?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But, statistically speaking, there was a high probability that you could have hit it off with everyone after introducing yourself,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How did you arrive at that conclusion? Actually, if I argue this with you now, we’ll just end up in an endless debate. Let’s say that the probability of making friends was high, like you said. So, how many people did you get to know?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She gazed at me. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was a rather splendid argument. The fact that I hadn’t yet exchanged contact information with anyone worked in Horikita’s favor. It proved there was no guarantee that introductions led to friendship. I instinctively averted my eyes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In other words, you have no evidence to support your claim that self-introductions lead to making friends, do you?” she asked. “Besides, I never intended to make friends in the first place. If I have no need to introduce myself, then I also have no reason to listen to anyone else’s introductions. Have I convinced you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That reminded me of the disastrous first time I’d tried to introduce myself to Horikita. Come to think of it, it might have been a miracle that I’d managed to get her name.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I asked her if I shouldn’t have introduced myself to her, she shook her head. People tended to have hidden depths, no doubt about it. Horikita might have been a more solitary, more aloof person than I’d imagined. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We roamed around the convenience store without looking at each other. Even though she was somewhat uptight, being with her didn’t feel uncomfortable.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whoa! There’s even an amazing selection of noodle cups here! This school is super convenient!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Two rather noisy male students stood before the instant foods. They tossed a veritable mountain of noodle cups into their basket and made their way to the cash register. Besides noodles, they’d stocked up on snacks and juice. Hey, it’d be nearly impossible to go through all your points; better to spend them. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Noodle cups. They have so many kinds.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">These were definitely one reason I’d come to the convenience store.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, do boys really like this kind of stuff? I can’t imagine that it’s healthy,” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I like them just fine, I guess.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I picked up a noodle cup and examined the price tag. It said 156 yen, but I couldn’t tell whether that was expensive or cheap. Even though the school referred to its credit system as points, the prices were all listed in yen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, what do you think? Is this price high or low?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm. I’m not sure. Why, is there something curious about it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I was just wondering.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The store’s prices seemed reasonable. One point appeared to really equal one yen. Given that the average freshman’s allowance was around 5,000 yen, the amount of money we’d received seemed impossibly large. Horikita, noting my odd behavior, gave me a quizzical look. I grabbed a noodle cup to avoid suspicion.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, this is enormous. It’s a G Cup, huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, that stood for “Giga Cup.” Just looking at it made me feel full. On an unrelated note, Horikita’s breasts were neither small nor huge. They exquisitely straddled the line between the two. The perfect size.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun. Were you thinking about something stupid just now?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Er. No?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I felt like you were acting strangely.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She could sense my inappropriate thoughts just by looking at me. She was a sharp one.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was just wondering whether or not I should buy this. What do you think?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh. Well, I suppose that’s fine. Anyway, do </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> really think you should buy that? This school offers far healthier food options. Don’t you think it’s better to avoid eating junk?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Like Horikita said, I had no reason to eat junk. However, since I had an irresistible urge, I took one package of regular-sized instant noodles with “FOO Yakisoba” written on it and tossed it into my cart. Her attention wandering, Horikita moved away from the food and began hunting for daily essentials. I planned to use witty jokes to score more points with her next.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you’re looking for something </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">a cut above the rest</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, how about this razor with five blades? I bet it’d do the job.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why in the world would I want to shave with </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I grinned smugly and pretended to shave an imaginary beard, but she didn’t laugh. Far from it. Instead, she looked at me like I was dirt.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Look at me,” she said. “I don’t have anything to shave. Not on my chin, not under my armpits, and not down </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">there</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I mumbled hesitantly, my spirit crushed. It looked like my jokes failed colossally with women.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have to say, I’m a little envious of your ability to babble inanely to someone you’ve only just met.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I feel like you’ve been saying stupid crap, too, and you only just met </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">me</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that so? I’ve merely stated facts. Unlike you.” She calmly tossed my words back at me, shutting me up. To be fair, I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">had</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> said some random nonsense. The smooth, eloquent Horikita, on the other hand, was always well-spoken, no matter how you sliced it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita chose the cheapest face wash. I would have thought girls cared more about that kind of thing, too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t you think that this one is better?” I took an expensive cream off the shelf and showed it to her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unnecessary.” She refused it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, but—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I already said it was unnecessary, didn’t I?” she snapped.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I gently returned the face wash as she glared at me. I thought I could carry on a conversation without making her angry, but I’d failed. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t seem adept at socializing. You’re terrible at conversation.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, if it’s coming from you, then it’s definitely true,” I grumbled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s right. I consider myself, at the very least, to have a good eye for people. Normally, I wouldn’t want to hear you talk anymore, but I will put in a painful level of effort to listen to you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d said that I wanted to be her friend, but, apparently, she didn’t feel the same. With that, our conversation abruptly stopped. Two new girls entered the convenience store. It was a little strange, but I became aware of something crucial: Horikita really was cute.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey. What’s up with this?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While looking around the store, desperate for a new topic, I’d found something strange. Some toiletries and food had been tucked away in the corner of the convenience store. At first glance, they appeared to be the same as the other items, but there was one big difference.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Free?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita apparently also thought it strange, so she picked up one of the items. Daily necessities like toothbrushes and bandages had been stuffed into a clearance bin and labeled “Free.” The bin was also marked with the proviso “three items per month.” These were obviously different from the store’s other goods.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“They must be emergency relief supplies for students who use up their points. This school is so incredibly lenient,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had to wonder how far their leniency extended, though.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, shut it! Just wait a sec! I’m looking for it right now!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A sudden, loud voice drowned out the store’s peaceful background music.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, hurry up. You have a line of people waiting on you!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, yeah? Well, if they have any complaints, they can take it up with me!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, trouble was brewing by the register. A dispute had broken out between two young men who were glaring at each other. I recognized the one with the thoroughly ill-tempered look on his face. It was the student from my class, the guy with the red hair. He had his hands full of noodle cups.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s going on here?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Who are </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had meant to appear amicable, but the guy with red hair scowled at me. Apparently, he was under the mistaken impression that I was an enemy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My name’s Ayanokouji. I’m from your class. I just asked because it sounded like there was trouble.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At my explanation, the red-haired guy looked somewhat mollified and lowered his voice a little. “Oh. Yeah, I remember you. I forgot my student </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ID</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> card. Forgot that it pretty much acts as our money from now on, too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I looked at his empty hands. He’d put the noodle cups away. He started to leave, probably heading back to the dorms, where he’d likely forgotten his card. To be honest, the fact that the student </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ID</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> was necessary for payment hadn’t yet sunk in for me, either.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can pay for you. I mean, it’d be annoying if you had to head all the way back to the dorms. I don’t mind.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s true. You’re right, it’d be absolutely annoying. Thanks.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The store wasn’t particularly far from the dorms, but by the time he got back there would be a long line of students buying lunch.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My name’s Sudou,” he said. “Thanks for helping me out. I owe you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nice to meet you, Sudou.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou handed me his noodle cup, and I walked over to the hot water dispenser. After watching our short exchange, Horikita sighed, aghast.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re acting like a pushover right from the start. Do you intend to become his servant? Or are you doing this to make friends?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I didn’t care about making friends. I just wanted to help. No big deal.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t seem to be afraid.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Afraid? Why? Because he looks like a delinquent?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A normal person would try to keep someone like him at a distance.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess, but he doesn’t seem like a bad person to me. And you don’t appear to be scared either, Horikita.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s mostly defenseless people who stay away from those types. If he acted violently, I could rebuff him. That’s why I don’t withdraw.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita’s words were always a little difficult to understand. To begin with, what did she mean by “rebuff”? Did she carry pepper spray to keep off perverts or something?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s finish our shopping. We’ll be a bother to the other students if we dawdle,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Wrapping things up, we presented our student </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ID</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> cards to the machine by the register. Since we didn’t have to deal with small change, our transaction was speedy. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You really can use it like money…” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My receipt showed the price of each item and the remaining amount of points. The payment had gone through without any problems. I poured hot water into my noodle cup while waiting for Horikita. I’d thought it might be tricky, but opening the lid and pouring hot water up to the line was simple enough.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Anyway, this school was eerie.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What merit could every student possibly have that would warrant such a massive allowance? Considering that there were about 160 people enrolled in my grade, simple calculation suggested that there were 480 people total in this school. That alone would mean 48 million yen each month. Annually, that would equal 560 million yen. Even for a government-supported school, that seemed like overkill.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How does the school benefit from giving us this much money?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder. The campus has more than enough facilities for the number of students, and I wouldn’t think it necessary to hand out so much. Students who should be studying might slack off.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Perhaps it was some kind of reward for working hard and passing a test or something. Indeed, student motivation might increase if offered an incentive. However, the school had just handed out 100,000 yen to everyone, with no strings attached.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I won’t tell you what to do, but I think it would be best to avoid wasting your money. It’s difficult to fix frivolous spending habits. Once a person gets used to an easy life, they find they need more and more. When you lose it, the shock can be great,” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll keep that in mind.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t really intend to waste money on miscellaneous junk, but she had a point. After paying and exiting the store, I found Sudou seated outside, waiting for me. When I saw him, he gently waved me over. I waved in return, feeling somewhat embarrassed, yet happy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you really going to eat here?” I asked him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course. It’s just common sense.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou perplexed me with his matter-of-fact reply. Horikita sighed in exasperation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m going back. I’ll be stripped of my dignity if I spend more time here,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean, ‘dignity’? We’re just high school students. We’re ordinary. Or, what, are you the high-born daughter of some noble family or something?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita didn’t flinch at Sudou’s harsh tone. Seemingly irritated, Sudou set his noodle cup on the ground and stood.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Hey, listen to people when they’re talking to you! Hey!” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s his problem? He just suddenly got angry.” Horikita said this to me, ignoring Sudou. This was apparently too much for Sudou, who started to shout.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, get over here! I’ll smack that smug look off your face!” he yelled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Look, I’ll admit that Horikita has a bad attitude, but you’re taking this too far.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was apparent that Sudou’s patience had run out. “Huh? What was that? She has a bratty, obnoxious attitude. That’s bad, especially for a girl!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For a girl? That’s rather outdated thinking. Ayanokouji, I would advise you not to become his friend,” Horikita said. With that, she turned her back on Sudou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, wait! You shitty girl!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Calm down.” I held Sudou back as he actually tried to grab Horikita. She made her way in the direction of the dorms without stopping or glancing back.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What the hell is her deal? Goddamn it!” he shouted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There are many different types of people, you know.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Shut it. I hate those stuffy, too-serious types.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He continued to glare at me. Sudou grabbed his noodle cup once again, ripped off the cover, and began eating. Just a little while ago, he’d fought in front of the register, too. He probably had a short fuse.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, you guys first years? This is our spot.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As Sudou slurped his ramen, three boys called out to us. They seemed to have come out of the same store and were carrying the same brand of noodle cups.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who are </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">? I was already here. You’re in the way. Get lost,” Sudou barked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You hear this guy? ‘Get lost,’ he says. What a cocky little first-year punk.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The three laughed in Sudou’s face. Sudou shot up, slamming his noodle cup against the ground. The broth and noodles splashed everywhere.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“‘First-year punk,’ huh? You tryin’ to make fun of me, huh?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou had an </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">extremely</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> short fuse. If I had to judge, he seemed like the type to immediately threaten anyone or anything that crossed him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re awfully mouthy, considering we’re second-year students. We already put our bags here, see?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Plop</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">! With those words, the second-year upperclassmen students put down their bags and guffawed loudly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“See, our stuff’s here. Now, beat it,” one of them said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You got a lot of guts, asshole.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou didn’t back down, unfazed by being outnumbered. It looked like fists were going to fly at any moment. I, of course, didn’t want any part of it myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, wow, scary. What class are you in? Wait, never mind. I think I know. You’re in Class D, aren’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, so what?” Sudou snapped.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The upperclassmen students exchanged glances and burst into laughter.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You hear that? He’s in Class D! I knew it! It was a dead giveaway!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? What’s that supposed to mean? Hey!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As Sudou barked at them, the boys grinned and stepped backwards.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Aw, you poor things. Since you’re ‘defective,’ we’ll let you off the hook, just for today. Let’s get going, <br/>guys.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, don’t run away! Hey!” Sudou shouted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, yeah, keep on yapping. You guys’ll be in hell soon enough anyway.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Be in hell?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They appeared calm and composed. I wondered what they’d meant. Previously, I’d been certain this school would be filled with upper-class young men and women, but there seemed to be plenty of rowdy, combative people like Sudou or those upperclassmen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, damn it! If those had been nice second-year students, or cute girls, that would’ve been great. Instead, we had to deal with those annoying morons.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou didn’t bother to clean up his mess. He thrust his hands into his pockets before heading back. I looked at the wall outside the convenience store, discovering two surveillance cameras.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This might lead to problems later,” I muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Reluctantly, I bent down, picked up the cup, and started cleaning up the mess. Come to think of it, as soon as those second-year students found out Sudou was in Class D, their attitudes had changed. Although it ate at me, I couldn’t explain it.</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">2.4</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">round one pm, I made my way back to the dormitory, my home from that day onward. At the reception desk, I received a keycard for Room 401 and a handbook containing information about the dorm’s rules, then boarded the elevator. I quickly flipped through the handbook, which only detailed the most basic things that we needed for our daily routines. The dates and times for garbage disposal were listed, as well as a notice about avoiding excessive noise. I also saw notes about not wasting water or electricity, and so on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, they don’t place restrictions on electricity or gas usage?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d assumed that the school would deduct the cost from our points. This school really went to great lengths to have a perfect system for its students. However, I was a little surprised that they’d implemented co-ed dormitories. After all, this was a high school, so the rules stated that unsuitable romantic relationships were frowned upon. In short, sex was strictly forbidden…obviously. I mean, a member of the clergy wouldn’t say that engaging in illicit sexual activity was okay.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I privately doubted that such pampered students could develop into fine, upstanding adults, it would be wise to make the best of the situation for now. My room was about eight tatami mats wide. Also, though this was a dorm, it was the first time I’d lived alone. I refused to have any contact with the outside world until graduation. Considering my situation, I unintentionally cracked a smile.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This school boasted a high employment rate upon graduation, and its facilities and student services were unmatched throughout the country, making it the preeminent high school in Japan. I found such things trivial, however. I’d chosen this school for one fundamental reason.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At this high school, people were not allowed to contact students without permission, even if they were friends or immediate family. I greatly appreciated that. I was free. In English, they would call it, “freedom.” In French, they would call it, “liberté.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Isn’t freedom simply the best? When I wanted to eat something, I could eat it. I almost didn’t want to graduate. Before being accepted, I’d honestly thought I would be been fine either way, that the difference between passing and failing would have been trivial. But my true feelings finally welled up. I was glad to have been admitted here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No one else’s eyes or words would ever reach me. I could start ag—no. I could begin anew entirely. A new life. I resolved to enjoy my time here to the fullest, but without drawing attention to myself. Still in my uniform, I dived into my already-made bed. I felt far from tired, however. I was so incredibly excited about my new life that I was unable to calm down. My eyes remained wide open.</span></p>
