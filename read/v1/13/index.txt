<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 7:<br/>Classroom of the Elite</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">O</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">n May 1, the morning bell rang for our very first day of class. Soon afterward, Chabashira-sensei strode into the room, holding a rolled-up poster. Her expression today was even more stern than usual. Had she started menopause, I wondered? If I made that joke out loud, I think she would have swung an iron bat at my face with full force.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, sensei, did you start menopause or something?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Unbelievably, Ike actually let that joke fly. Honestly, it was more shocking that I’d thought the same thing as Ike.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right, your morning homeroom is about to begin. Before we get started, does anyone have any questions? If so, now is the time to speak.” Chabashira-sensei completely ignored Ike’s sexual harassment. She appeared totally convinced that the students had questions they wanted answered. Immediately, several students raised their hands.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, I checked my point balance this morning, but I didn’t see any deposits. Points are given on the first day of every month, aren’t they? I couldn’t buy juice this morning.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hondou, I already explained this before, didn’t I? Points are deposited on the first day of the month. I’ve confirmed that points were wired this month without any issues.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, but…nothing was deposited into my account, though.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hondou and Yamauchi exchanged glances. Ike appeared too shocked to notice them looking at each other. I’d checked my point balance that morning as well, but saw that it had remained unchanged from the day before. No more points had been deposited into my account. I’d simply thought that the points would be wired later.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you kids really that dumb?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Was she angry or delighted? I was getting an ominous vibe from Chabashira-sensei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Dumb? What?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As Hondou stupidly repeated her words, Chabashira-sensei looked at him sharply.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sit down, Hondou. I’ll explain once more,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“S-Sae-chan-sensei?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hondou, taken aback by her unusually strict tone, slumped in his seat.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Points were deposited. That much I know for certain. There is absolutely no chance that we forgot about anyone in this class. To think so is ludicrous. Understood?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, even if I tell you that we understand, we haven’t received any points…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hondou, still perplexed, began to look dissatisfied. Supposing that what Chabashira-sensei said was true and that points had been wired to us, then that meant…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Had there been a discrepancy, then? Did that mean that zero points had been deposited into our accounts? My vague doubts quickly grew.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ha ha ha! I see. So, it’s like that then, teacher? I think I’ve solved the mystery,” Kouenji boomed, laughing.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He propped his feet up on his desk and smugly pointed toward Hondou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s simple. We’re in Class D, so we didn’t receive a single point.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? What are you talking about? They said that we’d get 100,000 points every month—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t remember hearing that, though. Do you?” Chuckling, Kouenji boldly pointed at Chabashira-­sensei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“While he certainly has an attitude problem, Kouenji is exactly right. For crying out loud, barely anyone seems to have noticed the hint I gave you. How deplorable.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In response to this sudden turn of events, the classroom exploded in an uproar.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sensei, may I please ask you a question? I’m afraid I still don’t understand.” Hirata raised his hand. He appeared to ask on behalf of his classmates rather than out of selfish concern. Just as I’d expect of the de facto class leader. Even now, he took the initiative.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Can you please tell us why we didn’t receive any points? We won’t completely understand otherwise.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was certainly true.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A total of ninety-eight absences and late arrivals. Three hundred ninety-one incidences of talking or using a cell phone in class. That is quite a few infractions over one month. In this school, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">your class’s results are reflected in the points that you receive</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. As a result, you wasted all of the 100,000 points that you should have received. That’s what happened.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I should have explained this all to you on the day of the entrance ceremony. This school measures its students’ true abilities. This time, you were evaluated as being worth nothing. That’s all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira-sensei spoke in a robotic fashion, devoid of any emotion. The doubts I’d had since coming to this school were finally confirmed, though, in the worst way possible. Even though we’d started with the huge advantage of 100,000 points, Class D had lost it in just a single month. </span></p>

<p class="P_Text_body__And__Centre_Alignment__And__Page_Break"><span><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol1_INTERIORIMAGES_5" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v1/OEBPS/Images/ClassroomElite-Vol1_INTERIORIMAGES_5.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top;"/></span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I heard a pencil moving against paper. Horikita seemed to be tallying the number of absences, tardy arrivals, and instances of talking in class down in her notebook, perhaps trying to make sense of the situation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Chabashira-sensei. I do not recall hearing you explain that to us before—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What? Are you incapable of understanding something unless it’s explained in detail?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course. There was never any talk about reducing our points. Had that been explained beforehand, I’m sure we would have avoided being late or talking during class.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That is a rather bizarre argument, Hirata. It is certainly true that I don’t recall explaining the rules of point distribution. However, didn’t you all learn in elementary school not to be late or talk in class? Was that not taught throughout your elementary and junior high schools?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that’s—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sure that in nine years of compulsory education, you learned that being late and talking in class are bad things. And now you say that you can’t understand this because I haven’t explained it to you? I’m afraid your reasoning is flimsy. If you had simply acted properly, then your points would not have dropped all the way to zero. This comes down to you taking personal responsibility.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was no way for anyone to refute her perfectly sound argument. Everyone knew that bad behavior didn’t pay.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Having just entered your first year of high school, did you honestly think you’d receive 100,000 points every month with no strings attached? At a school established by the Japanese government for the express purposes of training gifted people? That’s unthinkable. Try using some common sense. Why would you leave it to chance?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although Hirata appeared to be frustrated, he looked the teacher straight in the eye. “Well then, could you at least explain in detail how points are added or deducted? We can keep that in mind for future reference.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I cannot tell you. We cannot disclose the methods behind our student evaluation. It’s the same as any other organization. When you enter a company, it is the company’s choice whether or not to tell you how it evaluates its employees. However, I’m not cruel, and I’m not trying to be cold. In fact, this situation is so pathetic that I will give you one bit of guidance.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">For the first time, I saw a faint sliver of a smile on Chabashira-sensei’s lips.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s say that you stop being late to class and have no more absences… Even though zero points will be deducted from you this month, that doesn’t mean that your points will increase, either. That means next month you will still receive zero points. From another perspective, you could say no matter how times you’re late or absent to class, it doesn’t matter. So, you’re not really at a loss, are you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tch…” Hirata’s expression darkened. Her explanation was so counterproductive that it had the opposite effect; some students seemed incapable of understanding what she meant. The students who thought they could improve their situation by remedying bad behavior had their hopes dashed. That was probably Chabashira-sensei’s, or rather, this school’s, intention.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The bell rang, signaling the end of homeroom.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It looks like we spent too much time yammering. I hope that you understood the gist of it. Well, it’s about time that we switch to our main topic.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">From the tube she carried, she removed a white rolled-up poster and spread it out. She stuck the poster to the blackboard with some magnets. The still-confused students stared blankly at the poster.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are these…the results for each class?” Horikita tentatively took a guess. She was probably right. Class A through Class D were listed. To the side was a row of numbers that went up to a maximum of four digits. Class D had zero. Class C had 490. Class B had 650. And at the top was Class A, with a total of 940. In this case, 1000 points would mean 100,000 yen, wouldn’t it? Every class had apparently lost points.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Isn’t something about this odd?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. The numbers look too even.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita and I had both noticed something strange.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ve all been doing whatever you pleased this past month. The school has no intention of preventing you from doing what you want. Your actions, such as being late or talking during class, only affect the points you receive. The same goes for how you use your points. How you choose to spend is entirely up to you. We have not put any restrictions on point usage.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This isn’t fair, though! We can’t enjoy our student lives like this!” shouted Ike, who’d stayed quiet until now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi wailed in incredible agony. He’d already used up all of his points…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Look here, morons. Every other class got points. The amount of points we gave you for the first month should be plenty for you to live on.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“B-but, how do the other classes still have points left? That’s weird…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve already told you, there’s nothing unfair about it. All of the classes were scored using the same rules. Despite that, they didn’t lose as many as you. That’s the truth.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But…why is there such a difference in our point values?” Hirata also seemed to have noticed that the numbers were too tidy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you finally understand now? Do you see why you were placed in Class D?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The reason why we were placed in Class D? Weren’t we simply accepted into this school?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? But classes are </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">normally</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> divided up like this, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Students exchanged glances.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In this school, students are sorted by their level of excellence. The superior students are sorted into Class A, the least capable in Class D. It’s the same system you’d find in the major cram schools. In other words, Class D is akin to the last bastion for failures. You are the worst of the worst. You’re defective. This is just the result of you being defective.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita’s face stiffened. She appeared shocked by this line of reasoning. It certainly made sense to sort the superior students with the other superior students and the failures with the failures. If you mixed rotten oranges with good ones, the rotten would quickly spoil the good. Inevitably, the superior Horikita would find this revolting.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I, on the other hand, was glad. This meant I couldn’t go any lower.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“However, I have to say, this year’s Class D was the first to ever spend all of their points in a single month. I am impressed by how much you indulged yourselves. Wonderful, just wonderful.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira-sensei’s false applause echoed throughout the classroom.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You still haven’t given me an explanation. Was I honestly sorted into Class D? Did anything go wrong with the grading? Please double-check,” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry, but you weren’t sorted by mistake. You are definitely in Class D. You are at that level.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that so? Then I will ask the school again, at another time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, she wasn’t going to give up. Horikita had merely determined that her homeroom teacher was the wrong person to ask.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ll get the same answer from anyone in a higher position. Besides, there’s no need to be disappointed. As I told you this morning, it’s possible for one class to overtake another. You could conceivably reach Class A before you graduate.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t imagine it will be easy, though. Forget overtaking Class A; how in the world could those immature Class D misfits gain more points? I can’t see how it’s possible.” Horikita spoke the truth. The difference in points was overwhelming.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know. You alone get to decide how you head down that path. At any rate, Horikita, do you need to be in Class A for any special reason?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well…I suppose that’s enough for now. Excuse me. But know that I’m not yet convinced I was sorted correctly.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Understood. I will keep that in mind.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A chair squeaked against the floor, signaling that the discussion was over.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, that reminds me. I’ve summoned another person to the guidance room. It’s someone relevant to you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Relevant to me? No, you can’t mean… bro—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on out, Ayanokouji,” the teacher said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This was a bad time to reveal myself. Maybe I just wouldn’t go.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you don’t come out, I’ll have you expelled.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Jeez. A teacher shouldn’t casually wield expulsion like a weapon.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How long do you intend to keep me waiting?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With a sigh, I entered the room. Naturally, Horikita appeared surprised and perplexed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Were you listening to our conversation?” she asked me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Listening? I know you guys were talking, but I didn’t really hear anything. The walls are surprisingly thick.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not true. Voices carry pretty well into the kitchen.” Apparently, Chabashira-sensei wanted to drag me into the action.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sensei, why would you do this?” Horikita noticed that this had all been planned and was clearly angry.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Because I deemed it necessary. Now then, Ayanokouji, I’ll explain why I called you here.” Chabashira-sensei dismissed Horikita’s concerns and shifted her attention to me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, if you’ll excuse me…” Horikita muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, Horikita. It would be in your best interest to stay and listen. It may provide you with a hint on how to reach Class A.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita stopped dead in her tracks and sat back down.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please keep it brief,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira-sensei chuckled as she glanced over her clipboard. “You’re an interesting student, Ayanokouji.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not at all. I’m certainly not as interesting as a teacher with a strange surname like Chabashira.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Would you speak like that to every Chabashira in the nation? Hmm?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If you looked all over the country for another person with the surname of Chabashira, you probably wouldn’t find one.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, when I read over the entrance exam’s results, your scores piqued my interest. I was shocked.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">On her clipboard, I saw a rather familiar answer sheet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Fifty points in Japanese. Fifty points in mathematics. Fifty points in English. Fifty points in social studies. Fifty points in science. You even scored Fifty points on the recent short test. Do you know what this means?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A stunned Horikita looked over my test paper and then shifted her focus to me. “This is a rather frightening coincidence,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh? You believe that getting 50s all across the board was a coincidence? He did it intentionally.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s a coincidence. There’s no evidence that it’s not. Besides, what would I gain by manipulating my scores in the first place? If I were smart enough to do that, I would’ve tried for perfect scores.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I feigned innocence, Chabashira-sensei sighed in exasperation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You really do seem like an odious student. Listen. Only 3 percent of students solved the fifth math problem successfully. However, you solved it perfectly, and used a complex formula to do so. However, the tenth problem on the test had a completion rate of 76 percent. Did you make a mistake on it? Is that normal?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know what normal is. It was a coincidence, I tell you. A coincidence.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For crying out loud! I respect your frank attitude, but it’ll cause problems for you in the future,” the teacher said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll think about that when the time comes.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira-sensei shot Horikita a glance that seemed to say, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">What do </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> think?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why do you pretend not to know?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Like I said, it was a coincidence. It’s not like I’m hiding that I’m a genius or anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder. He may be even more intelligent than </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, Horikita.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita flinched. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Please don’t say anything unnecessary, Chabashira-sensei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t like studying, and I don’t plan to try hard. That’s why I get those scores.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A student who chose this school wouldn’t say something like that. However, some students may have different reasons for getting in. You, for example, and Kouenji as well. I think you’re fine with being in either D or A.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It wasn't just this school that was abnormal—the teachers were weird, too. Moments earlier, Chabashira-sensei had upset Horikita with just her words. It was almost as if the teachers knew every student’s secrets.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What other reasons do you have?” Horikita asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You want me to explain it to you in detail?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I noticed the sharp gleam in Chabashira-sensei’s eyes. It was almost as if she wanted to provoke Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, we’d best stop here. Any more, and I might go mad and destroy all the furniture in here,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you did that, Ayanokouji, I would demote you to E Class.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, there’s an E Class?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Certainly. Of course, the ‘E’ stands for ‘expelled.’ As in, you’d be kicked out of school. Well, I suppose our conversation has ended. Enjoy your lives.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What incredible sarcasm.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m leaving, too. It’s almost time for the faculty meeting. I’m going to close the door, so please step outside.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She pushed us into the hallway. Why had Chabashira-­sensei called both of us in together? She didn’t seem like the type to do meaningless things.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well. Should we head back?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita didn’t respond, and I walked away. Probably best if we weren’t together right now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait.” Horikita called out, but I didn’t stop. If I managed to keep away from her until I got to the dorms, I’d be home free.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Was your score…really just a coincidence?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I already said so, didn’t I? Or do you have any proof that I got that score on purpose?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t, but…I also don’t understand, Ayanokouji-kun. You said that you like to avoid trouble, but you don’t seem to be interested in Class A.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You have an extraordinary fixation on Class A.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Should I not? I’m simply striving to improve my future prospects.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, absolutely. You should. It’s perfectly natural.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When I entered this school, I thought graduation was my only goal. But the reality is different. I’m not even at the starting line.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita sped up and started walking next to me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, why are you aiming for Class A?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“First, I want to ascertain this school’s true motives. Why was </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> put into Class D? Chabashira-sensei said I’d been deemed a Class D student, but why? When I discover the answer, I’ll aim for A. No, I will definitely make it to A.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s going to be difficult. You’ll have to rehabilitate the problem children. You have Sudou’s continued tardiness and class cutting, everyone else talking in class, and, of course, the test scores. Even if you manage all of that, you’re still at zero points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know that. I still think the school made a mistake with my placement.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Anxiety had replaced Horikita’s previously overflowing confidence. Did she really know that was the case? The only conclusion I could draw from today was that “despair” was a two-syllable word. If you followed the fundamental school rules, you could avoid losing points. However, it was still unclear how to turn those losses into gains. Class A had had only had a small number of points subtracted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if we somehow found an efficient way to increase our points, the other classes might also find a way to do the same. Since we’d started out with such a substantial difference in points, we’d have to compete hard against the other classes in a limited amount of time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can understand your thoughts, but I don’t think the school will continue supervising us so carefully. If they did, there’d be no meaning in competition,” Horikita said.</span></p>
    
    
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. I suppose you could think that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had heard somewhere that the school didn't allow students in Class A to drop out in the first month after being admitted, among other things. In other words, Horikita believed this precise moment in time represented an opportunity to see a huge increase or decrease in points.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you’ll try to take care of this situation by yourself?” I asked.</span></p>
    
    
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t act so proud.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A hand chopped my side. Horikita ignored my pained expression.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ow. Look, I understand how you feel, but you can’t solve this by yourself. Think about Sudou. Even if you improve, the rest of the class will drag you down.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No. You’re right that no lone individual can solve this problem. We won’t even make it to the starting line without everyone’s help.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it sounds like we’ve got a huge problem on our hands.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We have three major, immediate issues. Tardiness and talking during class are the first two. Third, we must make sure no one fails the midterm exam.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think we’ll manage those first two issues, but the midterms…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The short test we’d taken had contained a few difficult questions, but overall it had been pretty easy. Even at that level, some students had failed. Honestly, their chances of passing the midterm exam were slim.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I need your help, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Help?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita glared at me. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What if I refuse? Like how you refused Hirata this morning.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you want to refuse?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What if I said I’d gladly help?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I never would’ve thought you’d do it gladly, but I doubt you’d refuse. If you </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">did</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> refuse to work with me, then that would be the end of it. No matter what I said about our future, I’d be powerless if you refused. So, will you help me or not?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wanted to say what she’d said before, when she’d silenced Hirata… What was it, again? Well, it wasn’t as though I would just bluntly refuse someone who asked for my help. Then again, if I told her I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">would</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> help, she’d probably run me into the ground until graduation. I needed the heart of a demon.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I refuse,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I always knew you’d help, Ayanokouji-kun. I’m grateful.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I didn’t say that! I turned you down!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I heard the voice inside your head. You said you’d help.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Terrifying! It was like she could read my thoughts.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t even know how I could help you, though.” Besides being an exemplary student, Horikita was incredibly quick-witted. She probably didn’t need my skills.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry. I don’t require your brain power, Ayanokouji-kun. Leave the planning to me, and act as I tell you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? What do you mean by act?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Doesn’t our lack of points trouble you, Ayanokouji-kun? If you follow my instructions, I promise you we’ll see a point increase. I would never lie.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know what you’re cooking up, but there are other people you could count on. If you made friends, they’d cooperate with you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, no one else in Class D is nearly as easy to manipulate as you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, there are several people. Hirata, for example. He’s popular and smart, so he’d be perfect. Besides, he’s worried that you’re all alone, Horikita.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If she reached out to him, they’d probably become good friends.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He’s no good. Even if he has some talent and ability, I can’t use him. To use an analogy, think about the pieces in shogi. Right now, I don’t need a gold or silver general. I want a pawn.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">So, you just called me a pawn? </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That’s</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> what you called me?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, if a pawn were to cooperate, he could become a gold general?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“An interesting answer, but you don’t seem like the type to make that effort, Ayanokouji-kun. Besides, haven’t you been thinking, ‘I’ve always been a pawn, I don’t want to advance,’ all along?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She’d shot me down with precisely the right brand of ammunition. If I were a normal person, my feelings would’ve been hurt.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry, but I can’t help you. I’m not suitable for this,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, contact me once you’ve given it some thought. I look forward to hearing from you.”</span></p>
