<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">9.3</span></span></p>

Well, that was </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">something</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, at least. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Fine, but if you do anything to stab us in the back, I won’t forgive you. Even if you’re an upperclassman, I’ll do anything and everything I can to make sure you’re expelled.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right. I’ll toss that in, too. I think that your concerns are pointless, though.” It seemed he understood what I was after.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun, what you did was unexpectedly daring.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun, you really are an incredibly observant person, aren’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m just cunning. Besides, I didn’t believe that I could pass the midterm without some help. I was just looking to make things easier for myself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm.” Kushida grinned like some gears were turning in her mind.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have one more favor to ask. Could you please tell everyone that </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> got the old test papers, Kushida? <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s fine, but…are you okay with that, Ayanokouji-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I like to avoid trouble. I don’t want to stand out. Besides, our classmates trust you, Kushida. I think it would be better if you told them.”</span></p>
