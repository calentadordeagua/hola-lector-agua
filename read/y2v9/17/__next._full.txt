<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, I’ll be eagerly awaiting your call!” </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, think I’ll be headin’ on back myself. See you later, you two!” </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita and I watched her go.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I was accompanying you, but in the end, I really didn’t do anything. You made it easy for me, so thanks.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not true. At the very least, in Kushida-san’s case, it seemed to me like your words had an effect on&#160;her. I’ll&#160;be sure to report that you did your part properly.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, what? Oh—that reminds me, there’s a study group session happening at Keyaki Mall later. Want to go see? Your girlfriend is supposed to be participating, too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A study group session? Sure. I can pop my head in for a bit, I guess.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s just, I was sure you were going to refuse. Is Karuizawa-san’s presence that significant after all?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That wasn’t it, but I supposed it was the sort of scenario where it was inevitable someone would perceive it that way. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that, I headed to the café along with Horikita.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">“S</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ORRY TO HAVE KEPT</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> you waiting, everyone,” said Horikita as the two of us arrived at the meeting place for the study group, whose members got together in the café after class. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She naturally went over and joined her classmates. I was curious about how she was doing when it came to interacting with the other members of her class, and I had to say, I was impressed with how much she’d improved.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh! Kiyotaka, you came too?!” Kei had been looking down at her notebook with a perplexed expression on her face, but she smiled when she noticed me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry, but I just came to check up on things,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Aww, really?” She pouted, obviously displeased, but she didn’t continue complaining beyond that. I was sure that was because I had told her well in advance, up until the day before, that she needed to proactively show up to study sessions, and that I wasn’t going to help her with studying. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Shortly after Horikita and I had arrived, Sudou appeared.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whoa, sorry I’m late!” His voice was strained; he was out of breath from running to the café.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It must be difficult for you, Sudou-kun, doing this on top of your club activities,” remarked Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nah, it ain’t no big deal,” he replied. “I do this stuff all the time anyway.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou’s attention had been momentarily captivated by the sight of Horikita, but then he went ahead and sat down in an empty chair nearby. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Many students, Kei included, seemed shocked to see Sudou in study mode. That was probably because he hadn’t really attended many of these study sessions until now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What the hell are y’all starin’ at?” asked Sudou.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s like, at first, I hated studyin’. It put me to sleep, and I couldn’t solve the problems right away. But y’know, the more you learn, the more you realize how useful this stuff is in school.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While there wasn’t any need to name specialized professions, it was true that his choices would be more limited than the average person’s in that case.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, even if my wish of goin’ pro doesn’t come true, I’ll get more options if I study, right? <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">You didn’t have to have only one dream. </span></p>
