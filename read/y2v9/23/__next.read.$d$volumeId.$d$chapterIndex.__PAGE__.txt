<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_22"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Chapter 5: How to Spend a Day Off</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T WAS SUNDAY</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> the day after I’d had my discussion with Kanzaki and the others, and experienced a little friction with Kei. The time had come for Ichinose and me to meet, as we had promised to do the day before. I headed down to the lobby a little early, but I didn’t see Ichinose in the area. I thought there might’ve been a possibility that we’d bump into one another by coincidence, but there was no sign of that happening. I turned around and looked back at the elevator, but it didn’t seem to be moving.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess Kei isn’t following me after all,” I mused.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if Kei was worried about me meeting Ichinose, she wouldn’t necessarily do something like that. Well, actually, no, it might’ve been premature for me to assume that Kei wouldn’t do anything just yet. Kei could always head over later, or she could have already gone ahead to where Ichinose and I planned to meet. Or, I supposed, I couldn’t rule out the possibility that Kei might just audaciously barge in on our meeting when we were already in the middle of talking.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Analyzing Kei’s behavioral patterns thus far, I couldn’t reduce the probability of something like that happening to zero. If it </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">did</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> happen, I figured I’d just let it happen, but… Judging from how she was acting yesterday, I doubted she’d do anything too reckless. It took a certain amount of courage to stand by and watch something you didn’t want to see, after all. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I exited the dormitory building. The sky was completely clear at the moment, but unfortunately, the weather forecast called for rain starting in the afternoon, so I’d brought an umbrella with me just in case. I wondered how Ichinose felt as she greeted the morning. The things she desired, the things she wanted. It seemed clear to me that it wasn’t just one thing. To have the skills of an outstanding leader, to have a successful romantic relationship, to become strong in spirit… If I were to count the wishes on my fingers, I might not be able to do it on one hand. Scratch that, I might not even have enough fingers if I did it with both hands.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just that one night on the school trip hadn’t been enough to bring about any tangible change in my relationship with her. I had no way of knowing what Ichinose, who was still feeling anxious and unstable, was thinking, except by meeting her in person. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Wrapped up in thoughts about the future, I ended up arriving at the meeting spot a little before the appointed time—and found that Ichinose was already there, waiting for me, holding an umbrella behind her back. She noticed me before I called out to her, and she slowly waved her free hand hello.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“G-good morning, Ayanokouji-kun!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t get the sense she was depressed. If I had to say, I felt like she had a kind of youthful, vibrant nervousness, innocent and pure. Unlike that night when I had caught her off guard with a surprise visit, it seemed like Ichinose had prepared herself well for this, both in terms of her outward appearance and her emotions. She made eye contact with me at first, but when I continued to gaze into her eyes try to discern her true feelings, she quickly averted them. I could tell that she had lowered her gaze, looking at my mouth, nose, or neck, to make it more difficult for me to pick up on her deeper emotions.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry that I made you clear your schedule for me like this,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, no, no, it wasn’t any trouble at all,” she insisted. “I didn’t have any plans to begin with, anyway. Really.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if she was only saying that to be polite, I still appreciated it, as the person who had invited her. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There were still a few minutes before the mall opened, and since we couldn’t go inside, the two of us lined up outside the entrance. We were next to each other, but we weren’t too close, nor were we too far apart. From an uninformed onlooker’s perspective, it’d probably be difficult to judge whether we were waiting separately for the mall to open, or waiting together.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I haven’t had that many opportunities to come to the mall before it opens, but still, I’m surprised no one’s here yet,” I observed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It is especially cold today,” Ichinose said. “I wonder if maybe everyone is still relaxing in their rooms.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She had a point there. I supposed it wasn’t necessary for anyone to wait in line early in the morning for the mall to open unless there was a special sale going on that day or something. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It really is cold, isn’t it?” she added in a very tiny voice, almost like a mutter under her breath.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The conversation stopped there for the time being, since I figured we’d probably have to wait until after we got inside before we could really talk. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In my daily life, I’d been spending more and more time with my lover, Kei, and it wasn’t like even that daily routine was always filled with conversation. There were some points when Kei and I shared our time together talking, but it also wasn’t unusual for us to spend ten or twenty minutes at a time in companionable silence. At first, I’d felt the same kind of awkwardness that I was feeling right now with Ichinose, but before I knew it, that awkwardness with Kei had disappeared, and I had begun to feel comfortable with the silence sometimes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It wasn’t so much an issue of whether or not I was used to the silence. It was more that slight moments of silence could feel strangely heavy when you were with someone who wasn’t close enough that you could say you were really intimate—where there was still that distance between you and them. It wasn’t like I couldn’t stand the continued silence, but I wondered if it would be better for me to break the ice and say something, as the person who had invited her out. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose might have been thinking the same thing by chance. However, neither one of us was able to take that first step, because we couldn’t really speak casually with each other. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">A trait we have in common, </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I thought to myself. If I threw something out there, we could go back and forth two or three times and get an actual conversation going. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just as I was thinking about that, a certain male student popped into my mind.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, by the way, on the school trip we just went on, I had Watanabe in my group,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, I heard that,” replied Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I hadn’t ever talked to him before, so I didn’t know him, but Watanabe’s friendly and easy to talk to. He’s a good guy.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I told Ichinose what I had honestly thought about him, she looked really happy, as though my complimenting him made her feel good, too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah!” she said. “I think all our classmates like him, guys and girls alike.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He wasn’t the type to get as carried away, like Ike, and he could read situations well, though not as well as someone like Yousuke. I’d only seen part of Watanabe, but I was sure that he was the same way in his class.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ve all been at the same school for close to two years now, but just because we’re in different classes from one another, there’s so much about other students that I don’t know,” I remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I feel the same way,” said Ichinose. “I don’t know much at all about the other classes. It’s totally different from how things were in elementary school and junior high. Or rather… I guess this is what happens when we have to seriously compete against one another.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If we had normal friendships, we could show each other our weaknesses, and help one another. However, at this school, those normal concepts didn’t apply. That was the commonly held impression that most students here seemed to have, Ichinose included.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Socializing is hard, too,” I said. “Even with my own classmates, I can’t really say that we’re actually opening up to one another yet. By comparison, I think it’s amazing how you can become friends with anyone so quickly, Ichinose.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Oh no, I’m not that amazing at all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t get the impression she was being humble about it. It seemed more like she really didn’t realize just how highly skilled she was.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is there, like, a special trick to it?” I asked. “Like a method for opening up to anyone and becoming friends?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No matter how much I learned about building friendships, I was still an extremely long way from standing level with those at the top. I had yet to master the sort of skills that people like Ichinose or Kushida had. I already understood what I needed to do. I knew what to say, and I knew the words to use. But I still wasn’t as good as Ichinose or Kushida. The slight differences between our cumulative experiences, our vibes, and even our body language and gestures led to an enormous difference in the outcomes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm… I have to wonder if there is one,” Ichinose mused. “If there is, I don’t think I know, either.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She couldn’t break it down and explain the theory behind it precisely because, for her, it was a skill that came naturally. Even if I were to observe her and try to learn it, it wouldn’t be so easy for me to understand it, absorb it, and then put it into practice. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Somehow, we managed to keep the conversation going for a little while longer. Shortly afterward, when ten o’clock rolled around, the automatic doors that had been closed finally opened.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Shall we?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And so, we were the first to enter Keyaki Mall, and we were enveloped by the central heating circulating through the building.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How long can you stay out today?” I asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, until whenever is fine. I don’t have any other plans today.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There were several questions I wanted to ask her today, so this was a good opportunity. If I had a time limit, after all, then I’d need to plan what to talk about within that time, and maneuver accordingly. I wanted to know more about her reasons for resigning from the student council, in particular, as that was a critical issue Kanzaki and his team had asked me to look into. So, I supposed it was a good thing that I was able to make sure I had enough time to take care of Kanzaki’s request. But…on the other hand, I couldn’t help feeling a sense of unease.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Putting the question of romantic issues aside for a moment, Ichinose was, fundamentally speaking, not a&#160;thickheaded person. Even if she didn’t necessarily have&#160;a first-rate sense of deduction, she definitely had excellent perception skills, better than the average student. If she wasn’t the sort of person who was reasonably perceptive, after all, she wouldn’t have been her class’s leader in the first place. I had to assume that, even in her current mental and emotional state, chances were high that she already had some idea of how her classmates viewed her from the way they looked at her, the words they used, the emotions they expressed, and so on. If that was the case, I could not assume that I’d been blessed with this opportunity to meet her purely by chance. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was likely she had at least guessed what my intentions were when I invited her, and made some inferences. Depending on certain factors, she might even be aware that her classmates had something to do with my intentions here. It’d be best for me to go into our conversation today with that kind of mindset.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, so, what are we going to do now?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My ulterior motive was to extract information, but I still hadn’t told her what our ostensible purpose was yet. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After thinking about what kind of plan I could come up with to share time with Ichinose today, I arrived at my conclusion.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I haven’t really decided on a clear goal,” I said, “but… Okay, I think I’ve got it. Could I ask you to tell me how you spend your days off, Ichinose?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How I spend my days off?” she repeated.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. I wanted to find out tips on how best to spend my time on an everyday basis in a way that’ll increase my ability to make friends with people. That kind of thing.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Is that really something you can understand this way?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess I just said what popped into my head… Is that okay?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I thought about proposing a second plan if she refused, but Ichinose nodded, not looking bothered in the slightest.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not sure if I can be of any help to you at all, but if you’re okay with me, sure, why don’t we give it a try?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She readily agreed to my request, having apparently taken it in a positive way. It looked like the first round of negotiations was a success.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm, well… So, are you really sure it’s okay for us to just do what I do on my days off?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course,” I replied. “Whether it’s shopping, going to the movies, going to a café, whatever, I’ll go with you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Perhaps none of those examples applied in her case, though, because Ichinose chuckled. “I’m afraid I might not really meet your expectations,” she said. “I hope that’s okay.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She seemed like she had been feeling somewhat awkward ever since we met up this morning, but now there was a natural smile on her face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, let’s get to it, then!” she declared.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And with that, Ichinose started walking, getting on the escalator to the second floor without any hesitation.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HERE WERE A VARIETY</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> of retail facilities within Keyaki Mall, most of which even I had set foot in by now. However, there were still several facilities that I hadn’t experienced yet. One among them was the fitness center on the second floor.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I try and come here on weekends and holidays,” Ichinose told me. “I’m not very athletic, so I was hoping to improve a little, if possible.” She took out her student ID card as we arrived in front of the gym. “You haven’t been to this gym before, have you, Ayanokouji-kun?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Right, I haven’t,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, then this was a great choice!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have to say, though, I’m pretty surprised you go to the gym, Ichinose. How long have you been coming here?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I tried the free trial sometime around mid-September, and I became a full member at the start of October, if I remember right,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Which meant she had been going to the gym for over two months now, huh? I’d had no idea.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you start out all by yourself?” I asked. “I’m not particularly good at just jumping into things, I guess…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I supposed I wouldn’t mind coming alone if I had joined and already started, but doing it by yourself the first time or two was a high hurdle to clear. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s tough for me too,” she replied. “That’s why I started with friends. I mean, even if you don’t have the courage to do something all alone, if there’s two of you, you can do things pretty confidently. So, it’s a good thing we’re together today, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I nodded in response, and then I followed Ichinose as she led me inside the facility. Ichinose greeted the affable female staff member standing at the reception, and after presenting her student ID, turned to where I was standing behind her to explain things.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you have your student ID?” she asked me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, if you presented your student ID, you could easily access the free trial without having to fill out detailed forms. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, Ayanokouji-kun, I’ll see you in a little bit,” she said. “The staff will explain what you need to do from here.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One of the male trainers took over showing me around. He provided me with the full explanation, going over things like how to use the lockers, the changing rooms and showers, and so on. Then, I was asked to change. It&#160;seemed as though you didn’t need to bring stuff with you to the gym; you could even arrive completely empty-handed. After removing my personal clothes, putting them in the locker, and putting on rental gym clothes, I headed for the workout room toward the back.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It looked like Ichinose wasn’t finished changing yet, because there was no one else in sight. Well, the mall had just opened for the day, so that was only natural, I guessed. Still, it was a little awkward for me to be the first one here, since I was only on a free trial. A male trainer seemed willing to teach me various things, but I declined his offer. I thought it would be better for me to learn from Ichinose, since we were spending the day together and this was a good opportunity. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Still, since I had no idea what I was supposed to be doing, I just randomly looked around at the machines. I didn’t feel particularly uncomfortable, as I was already extremely familiar with the workout equipment itself—when I was in the White Room, we had all the latest equipment for developing our bodies. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although the manufacturers and model years of the equipment here differed slightly, it didn’t seem like I’d have any problems using any of it. As I was thinking about those things, surprisingly enough, gym-goers started appearing one after another. I had thought the place would be more inactive, but it was quite popular.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry to have made you wait, Ayanokouji-kun,” Ichinose said. “Oh, it looks like some of the boys have already started.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though I was a little surprised when I saw Ichinose dressed in her workout clothes after she finished changing, she was right.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think two or three people just came into the women’s locker room, too,” she added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I noticed there are adults here, so I guess even non-students can use this gym, huh?” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It wasn’t like everything was exclusively meant for the students, after all, whether you were talking about the movie theater or the supermarket or whatever else; this&#160;gym was no exception.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose nodded. “I often see Mashima-sensei and others here too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I see.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I supposed even teachers were no exception. Places to work out were important to people who lived on school grounds too. I’d shied away from these sorts of facilities all this time, finding them difficult to approach, but if students with whom I was very well acquainted, like Ichinose, were coming here, then maybe I could come here, too. As I was beginning to think about that, Ichinose began to thoroughly and politely explain the machines to me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She gave a bit of a demonstration of what the machines were like in motion. I deliberately refrained from saying that an explanation was unnecessary and decided that I’d just quietly listen to her instructions, pretending that I didn’t know anything. Ichinose had acquired a fair amount of knowledge, but perhaps because it still hadn’t been that long since she started going to the gym, she only seemed to have practical experience with a few of the machines. After Ichinose had been instructing me on how to use the equipment for about ten minutes, the number of students in the gym started to gradually increase. Now, excluding Ichinose and I, there were about seven guys and girls working up a sweat.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, I suppose it’s probably time we start on someth—oh, Mako-chan, good morning!” Just as we were about to get started on our workout, Ichinose spotted a familiar face, and called out to her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hu—wha—Honami-chan?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The person who had just emerged from the locker room in her gym clothes was Amikura. She’d known that I was going to be out with Ichinose today, so she seemed genuinely surprised to see her here at the gym.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-why are you here at the gym?” asked Amikura. The question in her mind must have just slipped out of her mouth unintentionally because she was flustered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I started coming to the gym on holidays and weekends, remember?” Ichinose said. “Well, I figured I’d show Ayanokouji-kun around a bit, too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, I see,” replied Amikura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Amikura never would have guessed that we’d come here together, apparently. Since there was no way that Ichinose could know what Amikura was feeling right now, though, I played it cool, a nonchalant look on my face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yep, there you have it,” I said. “Sorry to barge in like this.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know, Ayanokouji-kun, you really, really don’t seem like the type of guy you see at the gym,” Amikura added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I guess it’s like, it’s hard to imagine you doing this sort of thing. Or, like, you seem like the sort of person who doesn’t like places where people gather.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I wanted to say that was simple prejudice on her part, she was correct. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, um, can you come here for a sec, Honami-chan?” Amikura must have noticed something, because she pulled Ichinose away by her arm, putting some distance between me and them. Then she whispered something into Ichinose’s ear. The two of them looked over to me for some reason.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I thought Ichinose must have been shocked by whatever she was whispering about, since she reacted with a start, but then she ducked down and hid behind Amikura for some reason.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You didn’t realize, Honami-chan…?” said Amikura. She looked somewhat embarrassed about something, just as Ichinose did.</span></p>

You know?” said Amikura. She gave me another pointed look, as if to say, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Read between the lines. Whether you felt embarrassed or not, it was better not to bring the concept of shyness into a place like this. Apparently, Ichinose herself hadn’t realized this fact, but Amikura had brought it to the forefront of her mind.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, at times like this, the best thing to do is to work up a sweat, right?” I said. “Teach me a bunch of different things. I’d like to try.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once you started worrying about how you looked in front of the opposite sex, you’d lose sight of things, so I tried to get her to think about something else. After what I said, it seemed like Ichinose made up her mind about something.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“O-okay, let’s see,” she said. “Hmm… W-well… What should we do, Mako-chan?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why are you asking me?!” sputtered Amikura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, Ichinose’s feelings of panic hadn’t yet subsided, and so she turned to Amikura for help. The two of them discussed between themselves in very hushed tones, almost like they were whispering to each other…and then nodded almost simultaneously, having reached a mutual understanding.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’re still beginners, so would it be okay if we started with the treadmill, since we’re used to using that?” Ichinose asked me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course. I don’t mind at all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The two girls got on a pair of treadmills, which seemed like the standard thing you’d find in fitness centers, and they each started running at the individual settings that suited them. These machines were different from what I was used to, of course, in terms of the manufacturer and so on. But since I’d used these sorts of things time and time again ever since I was little, I wasn’t at a loss here. A treadmill was a standard aerobic exercise machine that was indispensable for working out indoors. Ichinose and Amikura’s settings were almost exactly the same, so I figured I’d set mine to around their level, too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is your first time coming to the gym, right?” Amikura showed concern for me. “Make sure you don’t push yourself too hard, okay, Ayanokouji-kun?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I responded with a gentle wave of my hand, telling her that I was okay. Then, I got on the treadmill and started running for a while, silently. At first, it seemed like Ichinose was unable to push past her nervousness and shyness, but gradually, those feelings must have faded a bit. After about thirty minutes had passed, she seemed to have gotten used to it, to some extent. Once the thirty-minute time that Ichinose set had passed, her treadmill stopped, and she looked up.</span></p>

Since it was just Amikura and me here now, I decided to chat with her a bit.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You and Ichinose were at it for a long while. You’re both pretty good at this,” I remarked.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. But you know, Honami-chan seems like she’s really having fun, so that’s good.” As Amikura toweled off her sweat, her eyes creased happily, as though Ichinose’s happiness was her own.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess you can understand those kinds of things when you’re best friends with someone, huh?” I remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh yeah, you can tell. She usually has a smile on her face, but today, it feels like there’s this outburst of purity to it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Now that Ichinose wasn’t around, and I was all alone with Amikura, I thought I’d challenge myself to try and casually extract information from Amikura, in order to fulfill my promise to Watanabe.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s almost Christmas,” I remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. You’re going to be spending it with Karuizawa-san, right, Ayanokouji-kun?” Amikura answered with a question, turning things around on me before I could get anything out of her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm? Well, that’s the plan, more or less.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, um… I’m just going to come right out and ask… What are you going to do about Honami-chan?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What am I going to do?” I repeated.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you think I should do, Amikura, from your perspective?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? You’re asking me?!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, since you’re asking me this, you must have at least some idea of what you want me to do, right?” </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I… What I want most is for Honami-chan to smile, I guess,” she said. “That’d be best, speaking as her friend. But you have Karuizawa-san right now, Ayanokouji-kun. I think the situation is a little different if it means you have to break up with someone, you know? I guess I think what would be best is if she could fall in love with someone else, and be happy with that person.” She made revisions to her response as she thought about her ideal scenario.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just like Amikura said, Ichinose’s current affections for me were indeed troublesome. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re right,” I agreed. “I don’t really know very many guys myself or anything, but someone like Watanabe seems like a good fit for Ichinose. He’s easy to get along with.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I tried throwing out Watanabe’s name, going along with Amikura’s train of thought. Amikura appreciated Watanabe enough to go shopping with him on a day off—that alone might be enough for me to explore that possibility.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“By Watanabe, you mean Watanabe-kun, right? From our class?” said Amikura.</span></p>

If I took too long with this, Ichinose would be back before I could get anywhere.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Me?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You seem like you know a lot about love.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh? So, you do have someone you like.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, yeah, but that’s all it is. I mean, I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">am</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> in high school.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Who was it? It’d be best if I could find that out.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve had this unrequited crush for almost five years now. When will I move on to my next love, I wonder?” she muttered to herself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Five years. That meant she’d had those feelings since before she came to this school. On the plus side, it at least meant he didn’t have any rivals for her affection at this school, but…</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, no—no worries. You feeling okay now?” replied Amikura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If I hounded her with questions now, it would only make me look suspicious. I decided that I’d try questioning Ichinose a little later, when I could get a bit more information out of her.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">F</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">OR ABOUT ANOTHER HOUR</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I continued to experience what the gym had to offer alongside Ichinose and Amikura. However, that wasn’t because Horikita was somehow superior to Ichinose in terms of ability; it was nothing more than Ichinose losing on the mental level.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s probably better that I resigned after all, Ayanokouji-kun, since that way, I won’t embarrass you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You never know how a competition ends until it’s over, though,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just hearing you say that makes me happy,” said Ichinose. “Thank you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But even before the election came up, you had already decided to resign from the student council, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s right.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did that one incident from the school trip have something to do with it? If so, then—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not it,” said Ichinose firmly, interrupting me. She was holding on to the paper cup in her hand so tightly that it looked like it was about to crumple. “I was already thinking about it before that happened. I’m not qualified for the student council. I don’t have the ability, I’m not popular, and, more importantly…I have a past that I can’t erase.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">For an instant, as she talked, Ichinose’s face in profile closely resembled what I’d seen during the school trip. But she didn’t start crying, like she had back then. Apparently, she had no intention of remaining weak.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But…but I haven’t given up on everything,” she went on. “I know that some people in my class are worried that I’ve even given up on trying to get to Class A, but it’s not&#160;true.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Meaning you plan to keep trying for Class A?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“On that night during the school trip, you said, ‘If you don’t have the courage to take that first step, I can lend you a hand.’ When you told me that, it helped me come to a decision.” Ichinose made eye contact with me and smiled. “I can still fight. But I realized then that I couldn’t win that fight wearing two different hats at once, you know? I decided that continuing to serve on the council was a luxury I couldn’t afford—or really, I guess, it would just be a distraction.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, that was what led her to resign from the student council, then.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh… But I guess maybe the reason I quit actually </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">was</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> because of what happened during the school trip, after all, then,” she said, a little sheepishly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s what it sounds like,” I said jokingly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose chuckled in response to my lighthearted reply, the line of her eyes curving happily. “I’m planning to tell everyone in my class the things I just told you about, Ayanokouji-kun—about me quitting the student council, and what I was thinking up until I resigned—at the beginning of the week. It wouldn’t be good if things were misunderstood, after all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s good,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If her classmates still didn’t know what Ichinose was really thinking and feeling, and continued to fumble around looking for answers, that would hinder them in their showdown with Ryuuen’s class. After that time of instability throughout the school trip, Ichinose’s ability to digest what happened and process it quickly was a major strength of hers. She had lost her position in the student council, which was one of the weapons in her arsenal, but what she’d gained was greater than that. I figured it was safe to say that the situation that I had some apprehension over had been resolved, at least temporarily. I felt like I could probably give Kanzaki a good report.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, that reminds me,” I said. “This is completely unrelated, but there’s something I wanted to ask you. Do&#160;you mind?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not at all. What’s up?” she asked in return.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wanted to push a little harder and do some more legwork for Watanabe. “Do you know what type of guy Amikura likes?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?” Ichinose had been lifting her cup to her mouth, but her hand froze when I asked that question. Up until moments ago, Ichinose had often averted her eyes from me in our conversation, but now, she looked straight into my eyes and wasn’t looking away. If anything, I had to say I felt like I’d been struck with an urge to flee.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why are you asking me that?” asked Ichinose. Her voice hadn’t changed. She didn’t sound angry or anything. But still, I wondered why the feeling I was getting from Ichinose as she sat in front of me, which shouldn’t have&#160;varied from how it was moments ago, was now different.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well… I mean, if you’re asking why, it’s just that I was kind of curious for some reason, is all,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For some reason?” she repeated. “You want to know Mako-chan’s type for ‘some reason’? That really doesn’t seem like you, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As she spoke, the already heavy air seemed to get even heavier. I found myself stumbling over my words, whether I liked it or not. However, I couldn’t let her sniff out Watanabe’s part in this so easily.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I mean, it crossed my mind since we were on the school trip together and everything, but Amikura is cute, and she seems popular.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I think so too. Mako-chan is cute. So, how does that tie into asking her type?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It…doesn’t,” I admitted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Mm-hmm. This isn’t like you, Ayanokouji-kun, you know that?” She told me once again that I was acting out of character. More importantly, though, there was no sign of her taking her eyes off me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, it’s… Well, yeah, you’re probably right about that,” I conceded.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered where in the world the peaceful vibe we’d had until moments ago had gone. Ichinose, still holding her cup near her mouth, was pressing me for answers, her expression no different from usual.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why do you want to know Mako-chan’s type?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No reason in—” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“—in particular?” she said, finishing my thought.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I mean, it’s not like there’s ‘no’ reason at all whatsoever, I guess. That’s why I’m asking.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I abandoned trying to maintain eye contact and averted my eyes, looking over at the café employees. Oh, it looked like they’d just gotten an order or something, because they were making a drink with chocolate in it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Without paying any mind to the fact that my eyes were wandering, Ichinose continued to question me. “Did you meet up with Mako-chan somewhere before you met up with me?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Wait, what?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When we bumped into her at the gym today by coincidence, you two shared a strange look. Like what they call having a conversation with your eyes.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If Ichinose was this sure of the truth, then denying anything would only make things worse.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You noticed,” I admitted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I could tell. Because I…I’m always looking your way, Ayanokouji-kun, and thinking about you. So…” At that point, Ichinose finally averted her gaze from me. She must have realized that she had worked herself up into saying something really embarrassing. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She started over. “This is just a theory on my part. Mako-chan and everyone else in my class must have been really worried after hearing talk of me resigning from the student council. So, they came to talk to you for help, didn’t they? They asked you to come check on me, if at all possible. Right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose had just recovered mentally and emotionally, and showed it by proving to me that she had a firm understanding of what was going on. She really was paying attention to what was happening around her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Bravo. Exactly correct.” She was so good that I honestly wanted to give her a round of applause, but I&#160;decided to refrain from doing so.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But still, I don’t get it. Why would you want to know what type of person Mako-chan likes, then?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if Ichinose was able to deduce that I’d had a discussion with Amikura shortly before, it was completely understandable why she wouldn’t see how that would lead to me asking what type of man Amikura liked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why do you think?” I decided to try asking Ichinose, to see if she could think it through and guess correctly. Or rather, this was the best way I had left to conceal Watanabe’s presence. It would probably be better to work backward starting from Ichinose’s suspicions, and then just fabricate a suitable answer based on those.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm, it could be that…Ayanokouji-kun is interested in Mako-chan, so… No, that’s probably not it,” said Ichinose, thinking aloud. “Yeah, I’m not fond of that answer, so I’m not going to think about it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although she had thrown that idea out as an option, she quickly shot it down herself. More importantly, though… That was a really bold thing of her to say, even though it was just the two of us here. Ichinose still had feelings for me. She wasn’t even trying to hide her intentions in that regard. Or perhaps she wasn’t actually thinking too deeply about it, and she had muttered those words unconsciously? Even though I was observing her closely, I couldn’t tell what Ichinose’s true intentions were, because the fog was too thick; I couldn’t see through.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, if it’s something other than that, then… There’s a boy who likes Mako-chan, and he asked you to find out. Yeah, that might fit. He probably figured that I’d know her type,” reasoned Ichinose. She was getting so many bullseyes that it was almost a little scary. “So, in other words, it’s a boy who is aware of the relationship I have with Mako-chan. And, on top of that, it’s a student from my class who has contact with you, Ayanokouji-kun. So—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right. I’ll come clean,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Sorry, Watanabe.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> No careless attempt at deception was going to work against Ichinose today, when she was so sharp. If I hadn’t stopped her right then, she was going to start naming names any second.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s a guy who likes Amikura, and he asked me to find out,” I admitted. “I thought that you wouldn’t be able to figure out who that guy is, but I’m seeing now that might’ve been a little presumptuous on my part.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wasn’t saying that the act of indirectly exploring what someone liked in a member of the opposite sex was bad. However, whether it would please Amikura was another matter.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry. Just forget about this,” I added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no, it’s okay,” said Ichinose. “It’s natural for people to want to know about the person they like, and I know it takes a lot of courage to ask someone directly. Mako-chan is a really, really good girl. I honestly don’t know what her type is, though. I’ve never asked. But, at least from what I’ve heard, I don’t think that there’s anyone at this school that she likes.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If there wasn’t “anyone at this school,” that implied that there was someone, just not someone here. That tied back to what Amikura was saying earlier.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Apparently, there was a classmate she liked back in junior high,” Ichinose went on. “I don’t think they ever dated or anything, but I think she’s always cared about this person, and I don’t think she’s fallen for anybody else yet.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, that was the state of Amikura’s love life. Most likely, not even Watanabe had envisioned this situation. The fact that she’d been thinking about this boy for such a long time might prove a surprisingly high hurdle for Watanabe. Even so, that didn’t necessarily mean he had no chance at all. If he could build a good, close relationship with her over the year starting now, I figured he’d probably have a fairly good chance. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m afraid that’s all I can tell you, but I hope it’s helpful,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Plenty. Thanks, Ichinose,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Watanabe-kun really has come to depend on you a lot, hasn’t he, Ayanokouji-kun?” she said happily.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I never once mentioned Watanabe.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, that’s right. Sorry, sorry.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I supposed that the biggest reason I had failed to keep Watanabe’s name hidden was because I had too few friendly acquaintances in their class, rather than because I had mentioned his name earlier in the morning.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">FTER THAT</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> we enjoyed ourselves around Keyaki Mall for a while. Then, when it was time for lunch, we both left the mall.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s already started to rain, huh?” I remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I wouldn’t say that it was pouring or anything, it looked like it had been sprinkling for a while now. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Looks like it,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since we had both brought umbrellas with us, we each put them up and started walking.</span></p>

Now, I wasn’t being conceited here, but after calmly looking at Ichinose, I could tell that she still had strong feelings for me.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, I supposed that my cold war with Kei might continue for the foreseeable future. There was another period of silence between Ichinose and me, and things stayed that way until we parted ways. We eventually reached the dormitory lobby, and once the elevator came down, we both got onto it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose waved goodbye to me when we reached the fourth floor. “I had a lot of fun today. Thank you, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“See you later, Ichinose.”</span></p>

I told him that Ichinose had not given up hope of reaching Class A, and that she had resigned from the student council so that she could concentrate more on that fight in the future. As an addendum, I also included a note that Ichinose intended to tell people about her resignation from the student council tomorrow on Monday, and so on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The message I received back from Kanzaki was a question. He wondered whether, from my point of view, those things seemed to be true. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At the very least, I hadn’t sensed any falsehoods. More importantly, though, I was able to catch a glimpse of an unusual assertiveness from Ichinose that I hadn’t seen before. Whether that would lead to good luck or bad luck was unclear at this point, but I had a feeling that from now on, I was going to see a different side of Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I told Kanzaki to watch over and support her, while at the same time looking for friends who could express their opinions to her. Perhaps he felt relieved for the time being, because he sent me back a message conveying deep gratitude.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Still no word from Kei, though, huh?” I thought aloud.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I could have told her that I was done with my plans for the day now, but I’d meet her at school tomorrow anyway.
