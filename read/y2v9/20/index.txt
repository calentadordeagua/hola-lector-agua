<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_19"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Chapter 4: How to Spend Time with People in Ichinose’s Class</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">E</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ARLY DECEMBER</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> It was just before 2 P.M. on Saturday, the start of the weekend. I had received a message from Kanzaki two days earlier, and, as promised, I headed over to Keyaki Mall to meet him. Though we technically hadn’t decided on a specific meeting place within the mall, I was able to find Kanzaki and the other person without any issues. Kanzaki had been watching the mall entrance, and he immediately noticed me coming and gently waved.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry for calling you out on your day off,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I usually just sit around doing nothing on my days off. I welcomed the invitation,” I replied, politely telling him that it wasn’t necessary for him to feel bad. I noticed that along with Kanzaki, who’d invited me here, Himeno and Watanabe were here too, as well as Amikura. “I was told it was just Himeno coming with you, but there’s more of you, huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My apologies,” said Kanzaki. “Some things happened.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki was about to explain the details of how things had changed since he contacted me, but Watanabe and Amikura spoke up first.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“’Sup, Ayanokouji!” said Watanabe. “It’s pretty cold out today too, huh?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hello, Ayanokouji-kun,” said Amikura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Watanabe and Amikura treated me no differently than they had back when we were on the school trip, greeting me with smiles on their faces. I nodded in response, expressing that the feeling was mutual. When Kanzaki contacted me beforehand, he’d said that only Himeno would be accompanying us on the day of our meeting. For that reason, I’d thought for sure that it would be a discussion about </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> issue. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But the combination of these four was a little unexpected—or rather, I couldn’t clearly discern the goal of this meeting. Were Watanabe and Amikura the first two key players for Kanzaki and Himeno, perhaps? But surely it would be an absurd coincidence for Kanzaki and Himeno to pull in two people who just so happened to be the ones I was grouped with during the school trip?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s understandable that you’d be puzzled by this,” Kanzaki said. “I hadn’t initially expected these two to be here myself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Himeno also seemed somewhat unsettled, and she nodded in agreement, albeit only slightly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Meaning?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I felt like I had more and more questions by the moment, but Kanzaki seemed more concerned about us being seen by others in the area. I’d assumed there would only be a few people around for at least a while, but students kept on coming to shop, one after another.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Christmas sales have started.” Amikura pointed to a store, looking at the hustle and bustle inside the mall. She was right; the stores were completely decorated with decorations that screamed Christmas, and the various store shelves had the words “Christmas Sale” displayed prominently.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’d like to move to a less conspicuous location for the time being, if at all possible,” said Kanzaki. “I want to make sure our group isn’t noticed by anyone else… I’d especially like to avoid people from Sakayanagi and Ryuuen’s classes finding out.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since I could guess the circumstances without asking for details, there wasn’t any reason for me to refuse his request. If it had just been the four of them alone, it probably wouldn’t have been a problem, but with me added to the mix, we wouldn’t really be able to avoid looking like we were holding some sort of mysterious meeting. Besides, I’d much prefer to discuss things in a calm and quiet place, rather than in the middle of traffic.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, why don’t we just go with the go-to choice—karaoke?” proposed Amikura. The karaoke rooms were often used for things like studying and strategy meetings. They were one of the few places on campus where it was possible to meet in an enclosed space. That floor was about a three-minute walk from where we were now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s a safe choice. Let’s move quickly.” Kanzaki, taking the initiative, walked on ahead, in the lead, and I followed slightly behind. Amikura quickly came over to walk in step with me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess this is going to be a kind of serious discussion, then,” she apologized in a whisper. “I’m sorry. I didn’t think it was going to be like that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It sounded like she had just suddenly decided to join the group. Watanabe, who was standing beside her, spoke up next to explain the events that led to this.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, me and Amikura just happened to overhear Kanzaki and Himeno talking earlier completely by chance, basically. It sounded to us like they were gonna meet with you, Ayanokouji, so we asked them if we could come along.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, that’s right,” said Amikura. “I originally came here to go shopping with Watanabe-kun—at his request, actually.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At that, Watanabe looked both a bit embarrassed and happy at the same time. Then, though, he looked like he was sad about something, and glanced away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you sure you don’t want to go shopping instead?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Both were empty-handed, so it didn’t look like they had bought anything yet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s okay. I guess it’s like… It’s not really that big a deal,” Watanabe said. “Besides, I can just go shopping later.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki was walking just in front of me, and was able to hear what we were saying, so he turned around and explained things. “Originally, I thought only Himeno and I needed to meet with you, Ayanokouji. However, I changed my mind after I heard that you took such good care of them during the school trip.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Wait, </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> took good care of them? I should be saying they took care of me.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Over the course of the school trip, Watanabe and Amikura had helped me out in lots of different ways. I was grateful to them, but I hadn’t done anything for them to thank me about.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you decided that you ought to take another step forward, then, eh?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki nodded, a humble look on his face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, what?” asked Watanabe. “Ought to take a step forward?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll explain later,” said Kanzaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I could guess how eager Kanzaki must be feeling, judging from how quickly he was walking.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">4.1</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">FTER CONFIRMING THINGS</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> at the karaoke reception desk, the five of us headed to our assigned room. Since I was invited to join them as their guest, I was ushered to the back, where the other guys, Watanabe and Kanzaki, were seated together. It wasn’t like we could just not order anything, so we went ahead and got drinks for everyone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, guess we’ll start singing songs right away…won’t we?” said Watanabe as he picked the microphone off the table. He jokingly pointed the microphone toward Kanzaki, as though he were conducting an interview.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Much like me, Kanzaki wasn’t good at playing along with playful joking like that, so he brushed away the microphone with his hand, looking disgruntled. “I’m sorry, but that can wait.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Yeah, you’re right.” Watanabe sheepishly retracted the microphone and slumped back.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“First things first,” Kanzaki said. “I’ve explained to Himeno what today’s discussion is about, but you two haven’t heard anything about this yet. I know I already asked you this before Ayanokouji arrived, but can you promise to keep everything we talk about here strictly confidential?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, before granting them permission to accompany us, Kanzaki had told them in advance that today’s discussion was private. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ll keep it secret, no matter what we talk about. Right?” said Watanabe, turning to Amikura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes,” she agreed. “Don’t worry about us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They both seemed to take pride in how tight-lipped they were, but it looked like Kanzaki still didn’t fully trust the two of them yet. Almost as if to prove my hunch was correct, Kanzaki conveyed his thoughts openly. “I’m sorry, but I still have my doubts.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey now… Okay, what should we do, then?” asked Watanabe.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Watanabe seemed to have his own thoughts about the situation—about still being held under suspicion, even after having been made to promise not to tell anyone. However, considering my assumptions about the subject matter of the conversation we were about to have, Kanzaki’s actions were justified. If Kanzaki simply wanted to ensure that he managed this situation safely, then he could have refused Watanabe and Amikura, who’d asked to follow along because they were curious. Or he could have tried to leave this meeting for another time. His choice not to do those things, and how thoroughly he was checking with Watanabe and Amikura, was probably a gamble on his part. He was doubting the two of them precisely because he wanted to trust them and rely on them.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How about we just have them sign, like, a written contract or something?” Himeno suggested. “Something that says they won’t talk to anybody.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see,” said Kanzaki. “A written contract. That’s not a bad idea. We could even take a record right here with our phones.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki was going to make them swear on camera that they wouldn’t tell anyone else, and punish them if they broke their promise. A protocol like that would be one way to make sure that Watanabe and Amikura kept their mouths shut. Without any hesitation, Kanzaki took out his cell phone and placed it on the table to show them that was the plan.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, are you being serious? I dunno, this kinda makes me feel awkward,” Amikura looked like she was somewhat disgusted by the idea, like she couldn’t imagine a proposal like this coming from a classmate.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I already told you,” Kanzaki said. “We’re going to have an important discussion with Ayanokouji today. I believe that if what we talk about here were to be leaked, even if the chances of that are unlikely, the impact it could have on our future would be immeasurable.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re…not exaggerating, are you?” said Watanabe.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki wasn’t the only one staring at Watanabe. Himeno was also looking straight at him with an intense gaze.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll ask you just one last time. Can you promise you won’t tell anyone?” Kanzaki, fully prepared to be disliked as an individual, placed his hand on top of his phone as he asked the two of them for confirmation once again. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">If you don’t want to bear responsibility for this, you should probably leave right now</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. That was the kind of determination and spirit radiating from Kanzaki, and it surely must have penetrated Amikura and Watanabe deeply.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I promise,” said Watanabe. “I absolutely, positively will not tell anyone.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Me neither,” Amikura agreed. “Besides, saying ‘I&#160;don’t think I can keep a secret’ and walking away because of it is totally uncool. You can even record this on your phone if you need it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If the two of them went back on their word and told people, they’d certainly disappoint Kanzaki and&#160;Himeno, at the very least. Although they didn’t appear to be close friends with Kanzaki or Himeno, Watanabe and Amikura had the stance that they should keep their word, just as fellow human beings. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki, now convinced, put his phone away, looked away from the two of them, and turned his gaze toward me. “And there you have it. Once again, I’d like to ask that Watanabe and Amikura join us today.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I didn’t have any objection to it in the first place. This is a problem that Ichinose’s whole class is facing, after all,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If a foreign body were to get mixed in, then it would be Kanzaki’s fault for making an error in judgment. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That reminds me,” Kanzaki said. “Ayanokouji, there’s one thing I want to ask before we get down to business. I’m sure that most of the class, including you, Watanabe, and you, Amikura, have heard about this, but there’s a rumor that Ichinose is leaving the student council.”</span></p>

To close that gap, the entire class will have to be prepared to take on an appropriate amount of pain and resolve.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Pain and resolve…?” she repeated. “What does that mean, specifically?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry, but I can’t answer that right now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You can’t answer, huh? I wasn’t expecting that kind of response. I thought you’d say, like, you hadn’t given it any thought at all, or that this was just something you came up with at random, or something.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That would be the normal thing to think, yeah.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Because you’re talking about another class’s problems, or like, our distress—I mean, the more our class suffers, the more your class will benefit relative to it, Ayanokouji-kun,” she said. “Right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, you’re right,” I agreed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And yet you’re being kind to us, and helping us. Why?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose that’s because, regardless of whether we’re friends or foes, I have this strong desire to see what’s going to happen to Ichinose’s class in the end.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In the end…? You’re talking like you can see what’s coming in the future, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No one could see into the future, but we could predict and prepare for it. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s why, for the time being, I’m going to lend you a hand in times of trouble,” I said. “If you don’t mind someone like me, that is.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Himeno interpreted the situation favorably and pumped both her arms in a small victory pose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sure Kanzaki-kun would be really pleased. I feel really reassured by that myself.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I hope you’ll be able to show that pose off openly someday,” I remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? I-I’m suddenly feeling kinda embarrassed…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that, Himeno thrust both her hands into her pockets and averted her gaze for good measure.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">4.5</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">S I WAS WALKING BACK</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> to the dormitory with Himeno, I saw Kei sitting on a bench, fiddling with her phone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, see ya,” said Himeno. Having already sensed what was going on, she quickly left my side and started walking away hurriedly. Before leaving, she gave a slight bow to Kei, then went inside the dormitory building.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you doing out here?” I asked Kei. “I thought you went back to your room.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What am I doing? What does it look like I’m doing?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Waiting for someone.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Correct. Okay then, who am I waiting for?” Kei held up a finger as she suddenly hit me with a pop quiz. “One, Ike-kun. Two, Minami-kun. Three, Kiyotaka.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s an extremely puzzling question,” I said. “I mean, I think it’s highly likely that the answer could be number one, but…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you get it wrong, there’s going to be a penaaalty,” Kei singsonged. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Before I give my answer, can I ask what the penalty is going to be?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm, let’s see. I think I’ll write the words ‘I wuv Kei-chan’ on your forehead in marker and have you go to class with it on.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, the answer is number three.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So quick! You wanted to avoid the penalty that badly?!” Kei shot up from her spot on the bench, looking a little bit angry, and then sidled up beside me. “So? That girl you were with a minute ago, that was Himeno-san, wasn’t it? Why was she walking with you, Kiyotaka?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kei was smiling, but I felt a fierce pressure emanating from her, demanding to hear an explanation right now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I told you I was meeting Kanzaki today,” I said. “Himeno was one of the people in that group I met up with.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm? But Kanzaki-kun and the others weren’t with you just now, just her.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We split up earlier. Then, while I was on my way back, I happened to bump into Himeno, and we were casually chatting. That’s all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmmm? Hmmmm? Well, I’m your girlfriend, so I suppose I’ll believe my boyfriend’s statements for the time being. Hm?” Though that’s what she said, it didn’t seem like I was entirely free from doubt. “It looked to me like you two were kinda friendly, though.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I doubt that. You wouldn’t have been able to see that much in this darkness,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Uh… Y-yes, I suppose you’re right. Well, it’s like I was able to sense it somehow! Whatever. It doesn’t matter!” Kei wrapped herself around my arm, as though trying to claim the spot next to me as hers. “Let’s talk about something fun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, how about we go shopping together tomorrow at Keyaki Mall? Christmas is close,” said Kei, inviting me out with a meaningful grin. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“You understand what I’m trying to say, right?” </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">the look on her face was asking me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You mean, because Sudou’s romantic confession of love ended in failure,” I realized. “So, we’re shopping for your Christmas present, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s right. Getting a surprise gift isn’t bad, but going shopping with my boyfriend to pick out what I want isn’t bad either.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It would be a good thing for me too, actually, because I was sure that she’d be more pleased with something that we decided on together than something I struggled to pick out myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’d like to say yes to that, but unfortunately, I can’t tomorrow,” I said. “It’ll have to be next week.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Do you have other plans again, by any chance?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had informed Kei in advance that I was meeting Kanzaki and the others today. Kei didn’t have any connection to Kanzaki and his crew, and she didn’t know why I was meeting them, so while she did look puzzled when I told her about it, she didn’t seem particularly bothered. But…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yep, that’s right,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Can’t you make just a little time for me? What kind of thing do you have going on tomorrow?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Spending time with Ichinose. It would’ve been easy for me not to tell Kei about that, and deceive her instead. But the disadvantages of keeping that secret were immense, just as Kanzaki and the others had discussed in our conversation earlier. Ichinose was the sort of person who drew so much attention that if I were next to her, disquieting rumors were bound to start circulating. And on top of that, Kei had lots of friends, and those students would be her eyes and ears.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m meeting Ichinose,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Ichinose-san?” Kei’s reaction was clearly different from the time when I told her I was going to meet Kanzaki. She stopped dead in her tracks. “Who else is going to be there? Like, Kanzaki-kun, Himeno-san, those people?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“As of right now, we’re not planning on anyone else being there. Just Ichinose.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, what? I’m a little confused. You’re meeting up with a girl, alone, just the two of you, on your day off?” I could tell that she was clearly in a bad mood, but I supposed that was understandable. If the situation were reversed, a normal guy would’ve reacted similarly. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s right,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I looked at Kei to see how she was taking this, she met my eyes with a glare, as though she were trying to make me feel guilty.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And what?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Normally, in this kind of situation, you’d explain your reasons,” Kei lectured me. “Like, you’d say, ‘Yeah, we’re going to meet up, just the two of us, but don’t get the wrong idea. It’s just such-and-such,’ or something. You absolutely do not want to make your girlfriend feel anxious, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s certainly true,” I said. “There are several reasons why I’m meeting up with Ichinose, and one of them is that Kanzaki and the others asked me to.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Kanzaki-kun and the others asked you to? Really?” Kei was a little relieved at the mention of Kanzaki’s name.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not public knowledge yet, but Ichinose has resigned from the student council,” I added. “There’s a lot of confusion over that right now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-wait, hold on a minute. Is that true? I don’t really get this. Why did she?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s strange, isn’t it? Kanzaki and the others want to know the truth behind it. Being a member of the student council is in itself a positive thing for one’s class, after all. So, it’s not surprising that her classmates are upset that she’s leaving the student council at a time when they’re trying to get as many points as possible, since they’ve dropped down to Class D.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even from that brief explanation, Kei could probably imagine some of the anxiety that Kanzaki and the others were feeling.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But they’re afraid to ask Ichinose the reason directly,” I went on. “They can’t bear to hear their leader tell them something like she’s giving up on trying to get to Class A.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So…they want you to ask her the reason why in their place, then, Kiyotaka?” asked Kei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s exactly it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand that situation, but… Why would you get involved with Ichinose-san’s class, Kiyotaka? Isn’t it better to just let things be? I mean, if you help them out, they might become our rivals again.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her questions were entirely reasonable. This wasn’t the sort of thing that I should let Horikita and the others from her class hear about.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There are reasons to help an enemy when they’re in trouble,” I replied. “But I can’t tell you about those yet.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You can’t tell me…? You think I might tell someone?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not like that. I’m aware of how tight-lipped you are. I just don’t feel like telling anyone what I’m trying to do at this stage, is all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I deliberately chose to speak those words in a harsh, cold tone, causing Kei’s expression to stiffen slightly. But Kei was Kei, after all; it was only natural that she couldn’t just take what I had said in stride. She seemed to hold herself back for a moment, but then she immediately started to hit me with her feelings.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Look, I know you have a lot on your mind, Kiyotaka,” she said. “I’m sure that you’re helping the class in ways I don’t know about, and I’m trying to understand that, yes, it makes sense for you to do what Kanzaki-kun and the others are asking, to find out about what’s going on with Ichinose-san. But…but… It’s… I don’t like it. You saying you’re meeting up with a girl on your day off, just the two of you… I just don’t like it. Can’t you just do this some other way, like when we’re at school, or during lunch, or something?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kei pursed her lips and looked off into the distance, sulking. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It would have been easy for me to say that I was sorry, and that Kei was the only thing that mattered to me. I had already learned that it was important in romantic relationships to tell the other person not to worry. In that case, what would the opposite be like? Even if I could assume what the answer to that might be, I couldn’t actually say that I understood unless I tried to find that answer for real.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, are you going to get in the way? You can just barge in when I’m meeting Ichinose on our day off,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Th-that’s…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You won’t, will you? There’d be no benefit in it, even if you did try something like that. In that case, this conversation is over. We’ll go shopping for your Christmas present together next week. So, there shouldn’t be a problem.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Simply by not speaking kind words, I had caused the feeling in the air to change and turn extremely depressing in an instant. The happy Kei who had been waiting for me under the cold sky had vanished.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I guess not,” said Kei. “You have your own thoughts on the matter after all, right, Kiyotaka? I’m not qualified to say anything.” It wasn’t just her facial expression that was off. It was even like her emotions were escaping somewhere far away. “I’m going to stop by the convenience store for a minute before going back. You head on inside first.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that, she ran off in the direction of the store without looking back at me. However, while her pace looked fast as she left, it also seemed slower than it could be, and I could tell from looking at her back that she was expecting me to run after her. All I had to do was hurry, after all, tell her that I was sorry, and say that I’d think of some other way to meet up with Ichinose. If I just did that, she would probably go back to the way she’d been feeling moments ago. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But I decided to take my eyes off her back and instead head back inside the dormitory. Thanks to this, the rift between us would grow deeper. How would Kei react, and what kind of behavior would she exhibit? And how would I feel? And what would I do? </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I figured that this would be a good opportunity for me to experience those things.</span></p>
