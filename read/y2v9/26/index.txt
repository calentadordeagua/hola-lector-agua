<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_25"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">Chapter 6: Approaching the Special Exam</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> FEW DAYS HAD PASSED</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> since things settled down with Kanzaki and the others over the matter of Ichinose’s resignation from the student council. Day by day, the second-years were studying continuously for the upcoming special exam. Perhaps it was because the students with a lower level of Academic Ability bore a heavier responsibility this time, but it certainly felt like there had been a substantial change in the environment compared to previous written exams. As soon as lunchtime came, while many students headed to the cafeteria as usual, more than half remained in the classroom, taking out lunchboxes they’d brought themselves or lunches they’d purchased from the convenience store. There was a litany of groans and complaints from the students around me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ughh, ugh… I’m so incredibly sleepy. I wanna go to bed…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wanna do something fun, I wanna do something fun, I wanna do something fun, I wanna do something fun…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh, aren’t people being kinda noisy in the hallway? They’re really messing with my ability to concentrate. Can someone please go make them quiet down?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">…And so on. Apparently, some of the students even had concerns about what was going on outside the classroom, and more and more people couldn’t help but moan to themselves over the various things that they desired, but lacked. In particular, many of the students seemed short on sleep, and Sonoda was one of those students.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She held her head in her hands, and then gave herself a shake, trying desperately hard to stave off the feeling of drowsiness. “I’m sooo sleepy.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, let’s just hang in there a little bit more, okay?” Mii-chan, who was sitting next to Sonoda and had been tutoring her, tried to give her a pep talk. “We’ll take a break after we get through this…!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">On the other hand, though, there were some students who were showing surprising progress.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Satsuki, you’re finished already?!” exclaimed Ike.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I dunno, I guess I just suddenly felt really motivated, or like I was just going with the flow. I’ve been feeling good,” said Shinohara.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike and Shinohara were seated next to each other, studying together as a couple. Between the two of them, it seemed like Shinohara was the one who was seeing an unprecedented improvement.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I guess that’s probably because I’ve been going to all these study sessions lately,” she said. “I really feel like I’m paying for all the times I skipped out on doing that before now, though…” She kept on letting out sleepy yawns, but even so, she looked like she had a positive attitude. “I really feel like I’m getting better, though, little by little.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I’m still nowhere close…” sighed Ike.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, let’s just do our best together then, okay?” Shinohara said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re so dependable. That’s my girlfriend for you!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When he tried to give her a hug, Shinohara brought her textbook down on top of his head. “After you finish.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, you can’t just keep doing stupid stuff over and over forever. Look at the problems and solve them.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, you’re pretty motivated, Shinohara-san,” remarked Yousuke, who had been watching from close by.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I’ve pretty much just been a burden to the class all this time,” she said, “but in this special exam, I get a chance to take advantage of the fact that I’ve been a burden, right? I need to contribute to the class at least a&#160;little. Besides, I don’t want to get expelled.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The real problem was that if students didn’t improve their abilities, they would go down in the rankings of who was needed in class. It had already been proven that, when push came to shove, those who didn’t put in hard work saw repercussions for it. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It looks like you’re really doing your best too, Ike-kun,” Yousuke said. “But don’t push yourself too hard, okay? If&#160;you wear yourself out and collapse before the exam, it’ll all be for nothing.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“O-okay,” said Ike.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was obvious, but I figured that students who weren’t eager to study certainly wouldn’t want to do something like study too hard. However, it was important to be able to make an effort in accordance with the importance of the situation. You could find whatever reason suited you; it didn’t matter if it was a boyfriend or a girlfriend. That was a shortcut to unlocking that effort, just like how Sudou’s driving force had been Horikita. Up until now, many students had had difficulty making that effort, but thanks to the entire class coming together like this, it was possible they could make it happen slowly but surely.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Still, though…there really is a lot of noise in the hallway, isn’t there?” someone complained.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">During this time when the students wanted to concentrate on their studies, there must have been a lot of people coming and going in the hallway, because the sounds of voices and running footsteps were constant. When your concentration was heightened, that kind of&#160;hustle and bustle was like an uninvited guest.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m going to go check it out,” I said. “I’m sure a lot of students are wondering what’s going on.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if I couldn’t stop the noise, I could at least investigate what was causing it. If the restless students understood the cause of the situation, that should produce a slightly stabilizing effect on them.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. I was thinking about asking you to do that,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had figured it would be best for me to go and check it out, so the students who were studying wouldn’t be bothered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.00em;">6.1</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">O</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">NCE I STEPPED OUT</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> into the hallway, I saw students from Ichinose’s class running down the hall, their faces red with anger. Not only that, people from Ryuuen’s class were headed in the same direction. With that, the cause of the commotion became immediately apparent. A crowd had formed in front of a certain classroom, and Ishizaki and Albert were trying to forcefully create a path through, scattering the crowd.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on out, Ichinose!” Ishizaki shouted into the classroom. “Ryuuen-san’s comin’!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Shibata, who was already out in the hallway, went to stop him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, what the hell, man?” he yelled. “What do you think you’re doing, just barging in? We’re in the middle of somethin’ right now!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In the middle of somethin’? Don’t care. Come on, bring out Ichinose, right now.” Ishizaki aggressively grabbed Shibata by the shoulder and tried to force him out of the way, but Shibata resisted. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The person giving Ishizaki orders was none other than Ryuuen, standing behind him with a fearless grin on his face. Still, it probably wasn’t a good idea for anyone to haul off and just attack someone else right out in the open. It was lunch now, a time of day when lots of people were coming and going, and there were a lot of surveillance cameras in the hallway, so the school administration would soon get wind of any problematic behavior. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Could it be that students from Ichinose’s class had sensed what Ryuuen and his people were doing and hidden Ichinose in a classroom? The situation seemed like it had been locked at a standstill for a while, but it was quickly beginning to change. The door to the classroom opened and Ichinose emerged, accompanied by several girls who seemed to be trying to stop her, telling her not to do this. Not only that, but other major players, like Kanzaki and Hamaguchi, showed up as well.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, well,” Ryuuen called out with his usual attitude. “You finally came out. It’s the dumbass leader who quit the student council.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The announcement about the student council’s new composition had just been made today. Ichinose’s resignation wasn’t surprising at all in itself, as everyone already knew about that. The reason that was provided for her resignation was, at least ostensibly, so that she could give her undivided attention to her classwork. But whether that was the truth or a lie didn’t matter to Ryuuen at all. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen had decided that he needed to use this information, this weakness, as a means to shake Ichinose up, so he’d come over as quickly as possible to do so. Apparently, even the timing was deliberate. He had decided it would be more effective if he could get people’s attention. In fact, there were many other students who had come over to check out the commotion and see what was going on, even students from other classes. I clearly met the eyes of Hashimoto from Class A, and then he quickly blended into the crowd of students.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seems like this has turned into quite the commotion,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, yeah,” sneered Ryuuen. “You weaseled your way into the student council real early on to try and earn yourself some points. So, it’s only natural that these fine folks here want to hear about how you’re feelin’ now, since you couldn’t even manage to keep that up anymore. Right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Mm-hmm,” Ishizaki grunted in agreement, casually unfolding his arms.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Looking a little distressed, Ichinose reiterated the reason for her resignation. “I’ve already said that it’s so I can focus on my classwork.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But, just as whether something was the truth or lie didn’t matter to Ryuuen, he also didn’t care at all what response she had actually given to his question. “You were really kicked out, weren’t ya? I bet they said that they can’t let incompetents be on the council.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It if seems that way, then maybe you’re right.” Ichinose, realizing that trying to offer Ryuuen a serious reply would be pointless, instead just gave his words back to him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen chuckled, continuing his verbal onslaught. From the beginning, he’d had no intention whatsoever of letting anyone show her sympathy. “Heh heh. Or maybe it’s that they’ve realized your past crimes are gonna be a problem, is that it? I mean, the student council’s gotta be pretty damn sloppy to let a cunning little shoplifter on board. Hell, I can understand why you’d wanna run away.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Some people might have had their own thoughts in response to the word “shoplifting,” but it was something that Ichinose had now come to grips with after hearing it again in the recent past. She didn’t show any signs of being upset, because she had, at least temporarily, built up a tolerance thanks to what happened with the student council.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know what people might be saying about me, but it’s not good to cause trouble for other people,” she told Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nah, it ain’t like that. A lot of people wanna know the truth about why you quit the council, don’t they?” Ryuuen kept on trying to provoke her, as if saying, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, expose the truth right here and now.”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki, as a friend, couldn’t just stand by silently and listen any longer, and he wedged himself in between the two of them. “That’s enough, Ryuuen. Ichinose’s reason for resigning is just as you heard from the student council’s official notice.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t give a crap about the ‘official’ reason or whatever,” Ryuuen scoffed. “Come on, since she’s quittin’ now, it just makes her look all suspicious. Anyway, when you lose to me in the next special exam, yer finally gonna be fallin’ over that cliff, after all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was a statement that was very typical of Ryuuen, as he couldn’t imagine anything like losing to Ichinose. Ichinose’s class, which was in decline, would no longer have the opportunity to rise back to the top. Furthermore, the gap between Ichinose and Class A would be doubled, which would further increase their hopelessness. Even the students from Ichinose’s class who weren’t feeling much of a sense of crisis at the moment were probably soon going to realize those facts.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s too much of a pain in the ass to beat you in every single exam, so I’mma go ahead and suggest that your class withdraw,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And I’m going to ask that you stop making these ridiculous comments,” Kanzaki replied. “We have no intention of giving up on Class A. We are making every effort not to lose, and that includes this special exam.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Effort, huh?” Ryuuen snorted. “Yeah, it’s def true that the only thing yer class has got goin’ for you is how stupidly serious you all are. No wonder you can’t give up hope for this special exam, then, since I guess it’s possible you could win—if you could talk to your textbooks about their feelings.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was entirely unthinkable that Ichinose’s class would decide to withdraw just because of this altercation. Most likely, Ryuuen figured that if he could just shake them up a little more, that would be plenty. According to what Kanzaki and the others had said, there had already been numerous attempts at sabotage during their studies. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since Kanzaki stepped into the conversation, Ichinose had remained silent throughout. It seemed like she just didn’t have anything to say back, but the expression on her face wasn’t one of gloom.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You could have contacted me ahead of time,” I told her. Even if we couldn’t meet up right away, I could have at least told her when I was going to be heading back to the dormitory.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no, it’s all right,” she said. “I didn’t want to bother you, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t think it was a problem of “all right” or not, really… Anyway, if she herself wasn’t bothered by waiting, then there was nothing more for me to say.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, hey, um… It’s not like I had anything in particular that I needed to come tell you or anything, but, uh…” Ichinose spoke apologetically, essentially asking for my permission to visit me. “I was wondering, have you patched things up with Karuizawa-san?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, not yet,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I told her that, Ichinose nodded in understanding, replying with “I see.” Was she happy? Sad? Or something else? The look on her face could have been either of those feelings, but at the same time, I couldn’t tell how she truly felt.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case… Um, could I be a little selfish for a moment?” she asked. “If you don’t mind, I was thinking that I’d like to have a little chat with you, Ayanokouji-kun. I mean, if it’s really okay with you, I mean. But…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I figured that, since she had taken the time to wait, she hadn’t come here simply to say hello.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you don’t mind, Ichinose, then I’m fine with it. Do you want to come inside my room?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that okay?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t really have any reason to deny her. Kei hadn’t contacted me, and I didn’t have anything I really needed to spend my time on for the rest of the day. Besides, this wasn’t the sort of situation where I could make her stand outside and talk. More importantly, there was no way I could let Ichinose get any colder standing out here, so I&#160;turned the key and then opened the door.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m feeling kind of nervous. Sorry to barge in like this,” said Ichinose as she came inside.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once she stepped through the door, she must have immediately noticed what was different from the last time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The last time you came to my room was on that rainy day,” I remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you for everything back then,” she said. “I was completely soaked, and I still just barged into your room and everything…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I took off my shoes first, and then Ichinose took off hers and neatly lined them up next to mine. After I turned on the light and the entire room brightened, allowing us to see what we were doing, Ichinose spoke up once more.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh! You know, it’s like it turned into a really cute room.” She was transfixed by the changes to my bed and everything around it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It wasn’t like there were any especially major changes. I hadn’t bought new furniture or remodeled. It was just that there were plushies, a hand mirror, and cushions around the place, which looked a little out of place in a guy’s room. There were a lot more accessories around the room compared to before. Those were all things that had been brought here and left behind by Kei, who often came in and out of my room.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If someone unfamiliar with this school were to see that, it would be easy for them to get the mistaken idea that Kei and I were living together. If you looked in the kitchen, you could easily spot things such as matching cups and chopsticks in assorted colors. Ichinose understood full well that I was dating Kei, and she must have assumed that the state of my room would have changed. In fact, there wasn’t any sign of confusion on Ichinose’s face at all.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sit down wherever you want,” I told her. “I’ll fix you a hot drink. Cocoa okay?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was the same drink I’d made her before, on that day, and she smiled warmly and happily. “Yes. Thank you.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The best way to warm up a cold body was from the inside out. That being said, it was getting rather cold in my room, so I turned on the heater and dehumidifier.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think the room should warm up real soon,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nodding, Ichinose took off her coat and placed it by her feet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Girls really are amazing,” I said. “You’re always wearing skirts like that going to and from class. Aren’t you cold?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, definitely, it is cold. But I guess maybe I’m just so used to wearing skirts that I don’t really pay much attention to it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Noticing a framed picture of me and Kei, she approached it, took it in her hands, and stared at it for a long time. “Can I ask what it was that made you fall in love with Karuizawa-san?” she asked me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you interested?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I am. I haven’t had much contact with her. I only know what I heard—that she was dating Hirata-kun in our first year of school. I never thought that the two of you would end up going out, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Many students, even those in Horikita’s class, were still puzzled by me being with Kei. I was sure that, for students in other classes, it was probably difficult to figure out how it happened.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not like I don’t want to answer the question, but it’s difficult to,” I answered. “It was the first time I’ve ever been in love, and even if I wanted to talk about it in detail, I can’t. It could just be the natural progression of us learning together in class, I guess.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since I was unable to say anything specific, I weaseled my way out of the question using words that sounded like they made sense.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Karuizawa-san really is a cutie, isn’t she?” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I won’t deny that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once the water in the pot came to a boil, I poured the hot water into a cup and mixed the powder in with a spoon. Thus, the cocoa was ready.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Here you go,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s nice and warm.” Ichinose’s hands must have been&#160;cold. She wrapped them around the cup and let out a deep breath—</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">phew</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. “I selfishly dragged you all over the other day, like to the gym and stuff. Didn’t you hate it?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was the one who originally came to you, Ichinose,” I pointed out. “I wanted to know what you did on your days off. And besides—” I opened my desk drawer and took out a sheet of paper. “It was such a good experience that I’m actually thinking of turning this in on my next day off.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, a gym membership form…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had already filled the form out, entering my name, student ID number, monthly plan selection, and so on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve always led a pretty self-indulgent life,” I said. “I thought I’d start getting my body moving a bit.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. That makes me kind of happy to hear.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had often seen a depressed look on Ichinose’s face up until, and throughout, the school trip. However, ever since we’d spent that day off together, I felt like she smiled much more than before, noticeably so.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sure we’ll have more opportunities to see each other at the gym moving forward, so I’ll be looking forward to it,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah! Me too, I’m looking forward to it… So, we can hang out at the gym now.” Ichinose took a sip of her cocoa and smiled, her eyes curving into pleased crescents. “Hey, um, to tell you the truth…” She trailed off shyly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There must have been something on her mind, because Ichinose was looking into my eyes. “Actually, I wasn’t waiting outside your room just because I wanted to see you, Ayanokouji-kun. There is something I felt that I really had to tell you… Um, if you’d like, you can sit next to me.” She gently patted the open space on the bed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since I could tell that this was a serious matter, I sat next to Ichinose to fulfill her wishes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The reason I decided to meet you this past Sunday, Ayanokouji-kun, was so that I could give myself closure,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Closure?” I repeated.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To bring an end to my feelings for you, Ayanokouji-kun.” Ichinose, determined, didn’t even make a pretense of averting her eyes from me. “You have someone you love, Ayanokouji-kun. You’ve got Karuizawa-san. I told myself, ‘I can’t destroy the relationship these two have.’ That’s why I thought that day would be our first and last date.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was no hint of sadness on her face as she spoke; nothing like a sense of tragic resolve. Was this what Ichinose had been thinking about during that time we spent together at the gym?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, that’s the end of it?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose nodded firmly. “I thought it was the right thing to do. That we shouldn’t see each other in our private time anymore.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If so, that meant this moment we were having right now was a contradiction. Even if it wasn’t a day off, this undeniably fell into the category of private time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But I was wrong. That thinking wasn’t right. I knew that nothing had changed,” said Ichinose. I still didn’t know what conclusion she had reached. However, this change in thinking of hers was probably the reason that the bright, cheerful Ichinose I saw today had come back. “I thought about what I should do, I suppose. What I should do from here on…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her smile looked the same as it always did, but at the same time, somehow different. Until now, I had thought of Ichinose as being easy to read, as someone whose emotions always showed on her face, and as someone relatively easy to understand. I had seen instances where she skillfully put on a poker face during exams and such, of course, but even so—that’s what I had thought of her, in her private life, at least. Now, however, Ichinose was often wearing a face that made it impossible for me to read her true intentions.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you know what?” she said. “On that day, there was one thing I decided, in the back of my mind. That I wouldn’t ask you about your girlfriend Karuizawa-san when I was with you, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why’s that?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Because it made my heart hurt,” she said softly. “It made my chest tighten. I knew that it would hurt if I asked you.” She was choosing her words carefully, trying to convey what she wanted to say to me. “But when we finished at the gym, I just couldn’t bear it anymore, and I asked you. I asked you about which one of you fell in love first.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She certainly had asked me that. I knew what Ichinose was feeling at the time, her state of mind.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did it hurt?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Strangely enough, it didn’t. It was that moment when I realized that my thinking wasn’t right.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, what did you realize? What was the conclusion you came to?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You want to know? I’ll tell you.” Ichinose slowly took a deep breath, and then looked into my eyes as I sat beside her. “That I still love you after all, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose wasn’t running away. She didn’t even want to just catch me and let me go. That’s what I saw in her eyes as she looked at me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that moment, I was able to reaffirm that I love you, Ayanokouji-kun,” Ichinose repeated.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her thinking had been that she should give up, move out of the way, and treat what had happened as our first and last date. She had accepted that. However, she had then arrived at the opposite conclusion.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At the same time, I thought about something else,” she said. “I can’t just stay in the dark. I need to change from the ground up.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, that moment had been what changed Ichinose, who before had kept her head in the sand.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey… May I touch your face, Ayanokouji-kun?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not like a prize is going to come out, you know.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose laughed softly at my joking response and nodded. Then, she reached out with her right hand and&#160;touched my cheek. She oh-so-gently pulled me toward her, so that I was now face-to-face with her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve never done this kind of thing with anyone,” she breathed. “I’ve never felt like this about anyone before. It&#160;feels like my heart is always racing, I’m so nervous, and somewhere inside my heart, there’s this pain… But right now, I’m so incredibly happy. Just having the person I love right next to me fills my heart with joy.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose was telling me the truth, completely vulnerable and unvarnished. There was something I wanted to ask her about.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I remember I asked you about something on the school trip,” I said. “I asked you if there was something you wanted.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I wanted… First, to be in Class A,” she said. “That was a goal I wanted to reach with my friends. I had lost sight of that then, and I was about to lose heart. I was telling myself, ‘It’s impossible now.’ No, that’s not quite right—I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">did</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> lose heart. I even thought, ‘Well, it’s inevitable, I’m going to get expelled, it’s understandable.’”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And now it’s different?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now it’s different. I want to stay here. I want to aim for Class A. I want to achieve that goal.” The hand stroking my cheek was filled with strength. “Then, there’s one more thing I want. The person I love… Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know you know this, but—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. You have Karuizawa-san. I understand, Ayanokouji-kun. I’m not going to ask for anything more right now. But…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’ll be different in the future. I’m going to become the kind of person who can make you turn your head and look at me, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose was looking straight at me, determined, refusing to avert her eyes even though her cheeks were turning red. She wasn’t going to take that last step, though; it would have been a violation of morality in this situation, where the other person already had a lover. If she had tried to take that step, I would have stopped her from doing so, but she kept herself firmly in check. That was likely due to her core sense of righteousness. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Watch me from here on, Ayanokouji-kun,” she told me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was already planning on watching where you went, even if you didn’t want me to,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So… The end of the school year,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah,” I agreed. “When that time comes, we’ll meet again, just the two of us, and I’ll tell you my conclusion.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My resolve was broken once before, back then, but that’s not true anymore,” said Ichinose. “I’ve recovered.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t need to question her on that. As I sat next to her, I could practically feel the passion and strength coming off her in waves. I didn’t know what the outcome would be, but Ichinose had most definitely accomplished a major emotional change. It was rooted in an intense dependence on another person, though it was very different from Karuizawa Kei’s. That dependence, which could end up being a double-edged sword, undeniably gave Ichinose a great deal of strength.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was only natural that people wanted the person they loved to respond to their feelings. Even if it was only fleeting, you wanted to hear that person quietly whisper, “I love you.” You wanted them to touch you, and to know what came after. But Ichinose wasn’t going to beg for that. It was clear that she was determined to win that commitment from me herself. Slowly, she pulled her hand away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m going to head back to my room,” she announced.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll walk you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no, it’s okay. I’m all right from here. Ayanokouji-kun, you need to patch things up with Karuizawa-san as soon as possible, okay?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll take care of it,” I promised her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Coat in hand, Ichinose put her shoes on, walked over to the front door with light, graceful steps, and opened it. Then, after giving me a gentle wave goodbye, she closed the door behind her. A still silence followed, along with the slight, subtle lingering scents of cocoa and citrus. What kind of world would Ichinose go on to create from here, I wondered? How would that world affect the people around us—and would it bring about a change in my own thinking? </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I found I was looking forward to life at this school even more.</span></p>
