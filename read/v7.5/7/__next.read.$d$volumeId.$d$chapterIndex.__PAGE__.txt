<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T WAS STILL SNOWING HARD </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">on the morning of December twenty-fifth, a day of Christmas celebrations. People all over the world would be spending time with families or lovers, while some fortunate students here would spend it with their boyfriends and girlfriends. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Speaking of, it was almost time for the date I’d agreed to go on. I started getting ready, taking care to groom myself properly.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I should probably get going.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was already past eleven o’clock, and I’d promised to go out with Satou Maya today. I had no idea what, if anything, was going to happen—but I had a feeling that this day would be significant. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Falling in love with someone. Considering them precious. Sharing happiness merely by being together. Elation. I wondered whether I’d ever be able to experience such strong feelings.</span></p>
