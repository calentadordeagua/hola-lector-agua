<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 2:<br/>Love’s Arrow</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">D</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ECEMBER TWENTY-THIRD.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Clear skies. I woke up feeling great, so refreshed that I almost couldn’t believe it. Even though I’d just gotten out of bed, I felt as though I was still dreaming. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I felt different. What’d changed, exactly? If someone asked me, I’d have said nothing at all. But that wouldn’t really have been true. There </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">had</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> been a change. A dramatic one. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I, Karuizawa Kei, was now free of my horrible past. No, that wasn’t it—it was more like I’d gained enough power to overcome that past. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It’d happened yesterday, after the second semester’s closing ceremony. Ryuuen Kakeru summoned me to the roof and bullied me viciously. It sounds lame when I say it like that, but it’s the truth. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Faced with his cruelty, I hit rock bottom. I thought I was cursed, doomed to hell once more. I’d run to this school in search of salvation, only to be trapped all over again. I learned shocking things that day on the roof. I&#160;learned that Kiyotaka had goaded Manabe and her friends into bullying me. At first, I felt despair. Then&#160;anger. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But, in the end, I was saved. By Kiyotaka.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I left the roof, the former student council president and Chabashira-sensei were waiting for me. They didn’t say much, but they were considerate, taking care to avoid attracting attention. If it weren’t for them, I probably wouldn’t have made it back to the dorms. They told me that they were acting on Kiyotaka’s instructions. Maybe they knew that was the only way to put me at&#160;ease. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I thought of what’d happened on that rooftop. If I’d had the power to shake off my past, I wouldn’t have been afraid. No one would’ve discovered who I was in junior&#160;high. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But that wasn’t really true, was it? This was my fault, too. I’d acted arrogant to make myself look big and strong. No wonder Manabe and her friends had hated me. My attempts to save myself from bullying just got me bullied again.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ahh,” I sighed. Not a bad sigh, exactly. A sigh filled with emotion? I didn’t know how to describe it. I was certain of only one thing—whether I was asleep or awake, Kiyotaka was always on my mind. Since yesterday, I hadn’t been able to stop thinking about him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though I didn’t have a fever, I felt hot. I closed my eyes, trying to suppress that heat. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ayanokouji Kiyotaka. First year, Class D.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At first, I hadn’t noticed he even existed. He was just a background classmate, that’s all. Some classmates thought he was cool or whatever, but I wasn’t interested. Besides, they forgot Kiyotaka soon after. You had to be able to talk to people to be popular, and Kiyotaka lacked that skill. It didn’t matter how good you were at school or sports—if you couldn’t make others </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">want</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> to follow you, you’d never have a lot of influence. That was why Yousuke-kun, Class A’s Tsukasaki-kun, and Class B’s Shibata-kun were way more popular than Kiyotaka.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As it turned out, though, the real Kiyotaka </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">was </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">a good talker. He was smart, mature, calm, and so good at sports that he even held his own against seniors. He was also unbelievably strong.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He could be cruel and coldhearted, too, but…well…he’d still saved me in the end.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?!” Wait a minute. Had I…? </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Don’t tell me that I… For Kiyotaka…</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> “No! No, no, no! No way!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I held my hands over my beet-red face and shook my head. Blushing like this, I was acting like a fairy-tale princess in love for the first time. I mean, I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">would’ve </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">liked to fall in love. But…part of me just didn’t want to admit that I thought about Kiyotaka that way.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. There’s just no way. I mean, it’s because of him that all those bad things happened to me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If anything, Kiyotaka should’ve been grateful that I wasn’t holding a grudge. Stealing my heart, on top of everything else? I couldn’t forgive such selfishness. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I brushed my messy hair in front of my mirror, wondering whether a normal person could’ve forgiven Kiyotaka for everything he did. Probably not. Actually, it’d be impossible. They’d </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">definitely </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">bear him a grudge. Only a person with a generous heart, like me, could forgive such a thing. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Just be satisfied with that, Kiyotaka</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I talked to myself, I tried to shake off my wild fancies. I didn’t know what to say when I was actually in front of him, though. I couldn’t tell Kiyotaka that I already forgave him. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Maybe I should give him a hard time? Let him know I was angry that he used me?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I thought about that, a message popped up on my phone. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Today at eleven. I’m counting on you, Karuizawa-san.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, yeah. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">That</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> thing.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The text was from my classmate Satou Maya. She’d told me that she wanted to meet up and get some advice from me. Satou-san and I belonged to different friend groups, so we didn’t usually interact all that much. Of course, it wasn’t that we didn’t get along. We did. But this was the first time she’d invited me to hang out alone. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Despite everything, I’m feeling pretty good,” I said to myself. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Given that I’d had buckets of water dumped over my head in the freezing outdoors yesterday, I was proud of how well I was holding up. I’d taken a hot bath right after, but a normal girl probably would’ve caught a cold. She might’ve slept for three days and nights after torture like&#160;that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m just used to that kind of treatment, I guess.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The self-deprecating joke slipped out almost too easily. I thought I’d changed who I was, but really, I hadn’t. I’d been terrified of bullying ever since I started at this school, and that darkness had always been there, deep in my heart. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Now, however, I saw everything clearly. Maybe I could change. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I took off my pajamas and stood in my underwear. I&#160;couldn’t stop looking at the scars on my body, but they didn’t bother me as severely anymore. It was unbelievable how much I could change in one day. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Still, I absolutely couldn’t let a boy glimpse those scars, since girls should have soft, smooth, beautiful skin. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I’m sure the sight would cool even the hottest love. Although…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kiyotaka was different. He’d looked at my scars without disgust. Maybe he hadn’t wanted to say how repulsive they were. Or maybe it’d been, like, really dark in that room on the ship. Or maybe he was too busy intimidating me to be grossed out.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Maybe, deep down in his heart, he thought I was disgusting. Or…maybe he didn’t.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I went over the memory again and again, I realized something. “Wait. He touched me, didn’t he? He put his hands on me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It hadn’t really sunk in back then, but that’d happened, hadn’t it? He touched my thighs. I’d never even held hands with a boy before. What in the world had he done to me? </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Augh! Jeez! I’m thinking about it again! I’m such an idiot!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Enough with this!</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> For the time being, I banished all thought of Kiyotaka and got dressed.</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">2.1</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> WAS RUNNING LATE </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">at this point, so I had to hustle. Students on winter vacation crammed Keyaki Mall, making it way more crowded than usual.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess that makes sense. There isn’t anywhere else to go,” I muttered to myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I made it to the café in the nick of time, waving to Satou-san, who waited by the entrance with her cell phone in her hand.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good morning, Satou-san,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, Karuizawa-san! Good morning!” Her eyes sparkled as she waved back. She must’ve just gone to the salon or something, because her hair was styled beautifully. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I hid my exhaustion. No one could know about yesterday’s torture on the rooftop. I had to be my usual cheery self, which was why I’d agreed to meet Satou, even though I could’ve turned down her invitation. Besides, I’d been curious about what she was up to for a while.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry for inviting you so last minute,” said Satou-san.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nah, it’s no big deal.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whew! I’m so glad to hear that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Satou-san looked delighted as we entered the café. It was completely packed, but a group got up to leave, allowing us to take their spot. Perfect timing.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, it sure is a full house,” I remarked. It was absolutely crazy in here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder if any classes have exams over winter break,” said Satou-san. It sounded like she was thinking the same thing I was. We first years had gone to sea on a luxury cruise ship over summer break, but this time around, there didn’t seem to be any special exams in the works. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered whether the school was being merciful by giving us winter break off. Or maybe there’d be some kind of exam as soon as the new year started. I hoped not.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you haven’t eaten breakfast, go ahead and order anything you want. My treat,” said Satou-san with a smile. Taking her up on that kind offer, I ordered an American scone and a café au lait. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Satou-san and I sat down at our small table for two. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, what did you wanna talk to me about?” I asked. If she was paying me in food, it had to be a pretty big ask. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-yeah. Here’s the thing. To tell you the truth, I…I’m gonna go on a date soon,” said Satou-san.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A date?” I was surprised, but I kept my shock and minor anxiety in check.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah,” said Satou-san, her face turning red. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had a bad feeling about this. If I wasn’t misreading Satou-san, her date was… “Uh, so, with who?” I replied. It seemed like she was waiting for me to ask.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, with Ayanokouji-kun, actually. It’s…pretty surprising, huh?” She sounded embarrassed, but happy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I heard a slight ringing in my ears, but tried to pretend I was completely calm. I picked up the scone and took a large bite. Some crumbs landed on the tray. The inside of my mouth was all dry from the flaky pastry, so I washed the scone down with the café au lait.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh. So, you’re going after Ayanokouji-kun, huh? <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thanks, Karuizawa-san. You see, um, I’m really happy to be going on a date, but…this is the first date I’ve ever been on. I have no idea what to do.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ve never gone out with a boy before?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, based on what she said earlier, it made sense. Still, I’d assumed that a trendy girl like her had at least gone on a date.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Satou-san shook her head, looking embarrassed. “I’m telling you this because you’re, well, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, Karuizawa-san. I’ve never gone on a date, and I’m starting my second year. If I told anyone else that, they’d laugh at me. They’d say I’m slow or something. You think so too, don’t you,&#160;Karuizawa-san?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-well, no. You might be a late bloomer, but that just means it took a while to find someone you really like, yeah? It means you respect yourself too much to settle for less.” Even as I lied, I supported her. Not for Satou-san’s sake, but for my own.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you for saying that,” she replied. “Well, um, here’s the thing. I think I’m going to be so nervous that it might mess up the date. So, I was wondering if maybe you and Hirata-kun could come along and make it a double date? I want you to be my wingwoman!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t believe what I’d just heard. “D-double date?! W-wingwoman?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I really should’ve asked sooner, right? I had you help with all the reservations and stuff, too,” said Satou-san sheepishly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The reservations hadn’t really been a big deal. What mattered was that she wanted me, someone totally inexperienced in romance, to play Cupid. What could be more hilariously wrong?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s probably a bad idea,” Satou-san added. “Right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that’s—” </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I should say no.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I was sure to mess up, exposing my inexperience for all to see. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Augh! </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since this was Satou-san’s first date, though, maybe she wouldn’t notice? </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Should I act cool and composed, and cheerfully accept?</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> “Well, I was thinking I’d like to spend Christmas alone with Hirata-kun. Just the two of us, you know?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Normal</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> partners would probably also spend tomorrow and the day after together. That should’ve been an obvious conclusion, but my mind was still racing. I agonized over what to do.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh.” Satou-san looked anxious. “It’s just…you and Hirata-kun are so perfect together. I want to have a relationship like yours.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">From Satou-san’s perspective, I smoothly sailed through life. That wasn’t true, of course, but…something kept bothering me, and it wasn’t Kiyotaka. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t actually like Yousuke-kun in that way, and we weren’t actually dating. We were a fake couple. As long as we continued, neither of us could find true love. The thought nagged at me. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even Kiyotaka wasn’t attracted to me. How could my lies help Satou-san?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I don’t know.” I thought I might decline, but I stopped myself. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Constantly thinking of Kiyotaka wasn’t good for me. I needed to get him off my mind. If I could encourage him and Satou-san to get together, then… Well, it’d eliminate the possibility of Kiyotaka dating me, unlikely as it was.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“L-Leave it to me. I’ll handle it,” I said.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol7-5_INTERIORIMAGES_" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v7.5/OEBPS/Images/ClassroomElite-Vol7-5_INTERIORIMAGES_.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really?! Thank you, Karuizawa-san!” Satou-san took my hands, hopping up and down. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">She really does like him that much, huh? In that case, I should support her first love. </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I scooped up some snow and pressed it against my forehead to cool off.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I did, I reflected.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wasn’t the person I’d been in junior high. I wasn’t the same despair-filled girl of three years ago. I wasn’t even the same student who started school here eight months earlier. Playing a part for my classmates hadn’t set me free; it’d been a way to protect myself. That was no good. If someone needed your help, you had to be honest. You couldn’t be a true friend otherwise.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But, if I went on the double date, other issues would come up. First, I had to know whether Yousuke-kun was available. Since the whole school knew we were together, we didn’t need to appear in public much, so we’d meant to relax and take it easy this Christmas. If someone asked what we did, we’d decided we would just tell them we had a date in one of our dorm rooms. Even if someone saw us outside alone during the day, we could say we had plans in the evening. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh…um…one more thing,” said Satou-san. “I kind of hoped you could act like you and Hirata-kun met up with Ayanokouji-kun and me by accident.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Jeez, another request. </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t want it to be, like, a pre-established double date?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that all right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, it’s fine.” Of course she wouldn’t want that. I&#160;thought about it, then advised, “Let’s not go that route. I think it’s probably better to say up front that you want this to be a double date.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. What if Ayanokouji-kun doesn’t like that, though?” asked Satou-san.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Won’t he be more upset if he finds out later that we manipulated him?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, you’re probably right.” She looked troubled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s up to you, Satou-san.” I couldn’t force her one way or the other, but I thought lying to Kiyotaka was a bad idea. Sooner or later, he’d figure it out…not that I could tell Satou-san that. Saying something like, “Kiyotaka’s surprisingly perceptive. Don’t try to trick him,” would be weird. As far as anyone knew, Kiyotaka and I never hung out. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, I couldn’t definitively say that a double date was a </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">bad</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> thing. Maybe I’d go online and find an article to help. Something like </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Why Double Dates Are Ideal for Beginners</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Could you just join up with us naturally on the day of our date? I think that’d be good,” said Satou-san. My suggestion of honesty clearly hadn’t found purchase. She&#160;wanted to keep our double date covert.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you’re fine with that, sure. I don’t mind.” Now, all that was left was to keep Kiyotaka from noticing our deception. Maybe this was a good time to test how much I could sneak past him. “If Yousuke-kun doesn’t want to go on a double date for whatever reason, I’m sorry,” I added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that disclaimer, we headed back to the dormitory. </span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">2.3</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">W</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HEN I GOT TO MY ROOM,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I lay on the bed with my phone in my hand and stared at the ceiling. A bunch of weird emotions welled up inside me. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My discussion with Satou-san, the fact that she loved Kiyotaka, her asking for my help to make them an official couple… That stuff pissed me off, but I also felt restless. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">If this were just about romance, that’d be one thing. I think I’d be able to help Satou-san out. But…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Did Kiyotaka plan to go on a date with Satou-san just to “study” what girls were like on dates? What if he wasn’t interested in her romantically at all—but, instead, planned to use her like he did me? I might’ve been overthinking it, but this </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">was</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Kiyotaka. I could never tell what he really thought. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Maybe he was dating her to see whether she could be useful to him. I was afraid that he saw Satou-san as the key to making his life at this school easier, the same way he’d once used me. What if Kiyotaka decided Satou-san was better than me? He might stop protecting me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I picked up the phone and dialed, muttering, “I haven’t even memorized my </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">own</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> phone number, and yet…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">All I had to do now was touch the call icon. What did I even plan on asking him? </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Do you really think Satou-san will be more useful than me? </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No, that was stupid. It sounded as if I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">wanted</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> him to use me, and that wasn’t true. I just, well, wanted to protect myself. I wanted my Kiyotaka protection policy. Yeah. That was obviously it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Maybe I’ll try asking him directly.” I told my thumb to hit “call,” but my thumb didn’t budge. It wouldn’t even touch the screen. In the end, I couldn’t make myself do it. “I’m such an idiot,” I sighed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What I really wanted to ask was, “Are you done using&#160;me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then my phone rang. “Huh?!” It was Kiyotaka’s number. “H-hello?” I stammered, answering in a panic.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I want to ask you something.” His tone was the same as always, flat and apathetic.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What? What do you want to ask?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is anyone there right now?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No. I’m in my room.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Maybe he wondered whether I was sick, and called to check. Even so, it was too late in the evening for him to call me like this. Still, my heart fluttered to think he&#160;cared.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But Kiyotaka crushed my hopes in a second. “I need you to investigate something for me, Karuizawa.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What? Didn’t you say you weren’t going to rely on me anymore? You told me to erase your contact information,” I snapped. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">After what happened yesterday, shouldn’t he check on me? I didn’t expect something caring, like “You didn’t catch a cold, did you?” Still, it’d be nice to at least get an “I’m sorry,” or something like that. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kiyotaka had manipulated Ryuuen and the others into bullying me. I should’ve hated him. Anyone else would’ve reported him to the school. The least he could do was apologize. Yet the first thing he said was “I need you to investigate something.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Listen up, Kiyotaka. Who do you think you are? I don’t owe you anything anymore. Actually, </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">you </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">owe </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">me</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> protection. For free.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I ought to have said something bold like that, but those words remained stuck in my throat. I was afraid that if I actually did, Kiyotaka would walk away from me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you want me to investigate?” I asked instead.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Satou.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Satou-san?” This situation was getting weirder and weirder. I said nothing about my meeting with her today. “What about her?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I want to know who she usually hangs out with. What her behavior patterns are. I’d also appreciate it if you could fill me in on her personality, hobbies, and likes and dislikes. If you already know all those things, our conversation will be brief.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I don’t know any of that stuff</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. “Unfortunately, Satou-san and I hang out in different groups. I don’t know much about her.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not very well-informed, eh? So, there’re lots of things that even </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> the girls’ de facto leader,</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">don’t know.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, did</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> know you’re being a jerk right now?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you don’t have the answers, then do some digging for me. I’d prefer that Satou remain in the dark about this,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well…if I ask Shinohara-san, I might be able to find out some things.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do whatever you think would be best, please. I leave everything in your hands,” Kiyotaka told me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right, fine. I’ll try asking around…but tell me the reason why.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Send me the details via e-mail.” Kiyotaka hung up without even responding to my question. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What the hell is his deal? Ugh. God, I was an idiot to expect anything else from him.” </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I should’ve coughed during the call or something to hint that I didn’t feel well.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Grumbling, I sent a chat message to Shinohara-san. If nothing else, I could be proud of myself for acting professionally, even while Kiyotaka was being a jerk. Shinohara-san responded, and soon, I effortlessly turned the topic of conversation to Satou-san. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Shinohara-san and I chatted for a while, and once I’d gotten everything I could, I sent the information to Kiyotaka’s other e-mail address. I didn’t get a reply, as usual, but I was sure he got the message. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, Kiyotaka was actually interested in Satou-san? He was clearly collecting information before the date to make sure it went well. If it did, would the two of them start going out for real? Or maybe…maybe he was trying to turn Satou-san into his pawn? I just couldn’t figure it&#160;out. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Argh! Come on! What the hell’s up with that guy?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t sleep that night. Tomorrow would be a long day.</span></p>
