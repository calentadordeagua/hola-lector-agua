<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 4:<br/>The Meaning of<br/>Freedom</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">K</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">ouenji’s questions weighed on my mind for quite some time. On the third day, I left the base camp before noon and made my way toward the forest entrance.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ahh, ahh, ahh… Wh-where are you planning to go now, Ayanokouji-kun?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura was out of breath, her breasts heaving as she breathed. She must’ve been looking for me and given chase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Didn’t I tie a handkerchief around a tree? Anyway, I thought I’d just go and check something out.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wanted to confirm something much earlier, but didn’t had the time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-It’s probably not okay for me…to come along, is it? I’d only slow you down, probably, but…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Isn’t it better for you to stay back? Won’t it bother you if people start spreading rumors?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That doesn’t bother me at all. Besides, I….”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura mumbled so quietly that I couldn’t have heard anything even if I’d put my ear close.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not going to be particularly easy, right? I mean, after we went through all the trouble of coming to this island, I think you should have a little fun… Though I’m not one to talk about fun, myself.” I thought I’d turn Sakura’s offer down with an appropriate excuse. However…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Th-this </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">is</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> fun, though!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura offered more resistance than I would’ve imagined. After her surprisingly forceful reaction, our eyes met in shock. Then, Sakura crouched and hid her face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ahhhhh! N-no, that’s not what I meant! Ahh! I mean!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had no idea what Sakura was trying to say. All I knew was that she was an interesting girl. It’d be great if she could just show that part of herself to others.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, how about we go together? But I have a condition. If we get into trouble later, you won’t blame yourself. Okay?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really? It’s okay?!” she cried, still hiding her face with her hands.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What kind of conversation was this? It felt kind of odd to just stay silent as we walked, so I killed some time by bringing up a familiar topic. Anything was less awkward than listening to the crunch of our footsteps on the path.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you getting along well with the other girls? I&#160;mean, you can’t really do things alone in this test, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, not at all… We don’t really talk or anything,” Sakura muttered, twirling her hair with her index finger. It was like she felt embarrassed about herself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m just worthless, really. I can’t do sports, or study. I’m not really growing as a person in any area.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not true. You’re making progress, Sakura-chan.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Me, progress? Ah ha ha… That’s not true, though.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">is</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> true. Maybe you can’t see it, but you’re definitely making progress little by little.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I tried to convey that with both words and my attitude. It was really effective for people like Sakura, who lacked confidence in themselves. It was the first time I’d appealed to someone from the bottom of my heart like that, and I hoped it would resonate with her. Sakura stopped walking and looked at me, her eyes trembling. She was trying to discern the true meaning of my words.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s okay. You’ll make some friends really soon, Sakura. Then school will become much more fun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When our eyes met, Sakura panicked and averted her gaze downward. Even the fact that she was able to look at people for just one moment was a huge difference from when we’d first met.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That reminds me… That guy, from the incident before…it seems like he resigned.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The man who’d worked at the electronics store on campus had been an extremely passionate fan of Sakura from her gravure idol days… No, in truth, he was just a stalker. He hadn’t been satisfied with hanging around Sakura’s homepage, and had schemed to meet up with her personally.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you for everything… Everything worked out thanks to you, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I didn’t really do anything. You were saved because you got close to Kushida, and because Horikita and Ichinose helped out. I was just a bystander. Anyway, more importantly, nothing else weird has happened, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though the stalker had left the school premises, there was the possibility that he’d try and reach out online.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, everything’s been fine. Actually, I’ve been taking a little break from the bulletin board, too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was for the sake of precaution, most likely. Probably a wise decision.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Besides, even though you usually act nervous, you always had this really confident expression when you were an idol.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that’s…that’s because I did the photography myself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A long time ago? But when you were featured in magazines you probably didn’t take the pictures yourself, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura answered with a wry smile, looking sheepish.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I didn’t do well, and I also took way more time than the others. A cameraman would take pictures of the girls to make sure there were as few people involved as possible. Besides…back then, I was able to bear it because I made myself empty. It was like I erased myself, eliminated my feelings. But I eventually reached my limit, so I had to take a break.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura said this all in one go, stopping at the very end to take a deep breath. The incident with the stalker seemed to have left her scarred, but now things were headed in a better direction. A mass of trees waited right ahead of us. I went ahead of Sakura, proceeding to the opening of the path just ahead. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The way forward began to get rather steep. Since we’d walked for a while, and it would be terrible if Sakura scratched herself on a branch, I decided it was time for a break. I looked back. Sakura was trembling, her shoulders shaking. Maybe she hadn’t thought I was going to look back.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How about we take a short break? It’ll probably take a while longer to reach our destination.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura was probably exhausted after walking such a rough trail for half an hour. She looked relieved. I&#160;searched for a big tree that could provide shade against the extreme heat, and the two of us sat between the roots, where there was enough space for two people. However, Sakura decided to sit a little further away, perhaps due to her reserved nature. But the ground was all rugged and uneven, so it probably hurt her to sit there.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sit down over here.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that okay?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, of course. You aren’t comfortable over there, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-yeah…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura, still reserved, plopped down next to me. She sat at such a distance that the sleeves of our gym clothes barely touched.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nature sure is amazing, isn’t it? I like to spend a lot of my free time out in nature, even if it’s just walking around,” Sakura said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Considering even Kouenji’s satisfaction, the school has probably been really good about keeping up the place. Normally, an overseas jungle would be more dangerous.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When we left for the trip, I felt depressed at first. I&#160;didn’t have any friends, and I don’t really like traveling. I thought it would be fine if I could just stay in my room. If I did that, my life would have been the same as always. But then, all this happened. We were told this was some kind of test…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura looked up at the sky, her back against the tree.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But now I’m kind of glad that we came here. That’s because I never really had the chance to talk with you like this before, Ayanokouji-kun…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Together, deep in the forest, we were embraced by nature and a feeling of serenity.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wish we could stay like this forever…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In the three days since we’d come to the deserted island, this was the longest time I had been alone with Sakura. I wondered if this was some kind of karmic meeting between two friendless people. It didn’t feel like it was strange or pointless. Like Sakura said, I felt like the distance between us had shortened a bit. I wouldn’t say we were falling in love or anything, but it was like we’d become friends. This was probably the first time I’d felt a relationship tilt toward friendship. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh… It’s too bad. If I had my digital camera, I probably could have taken the best photos, but…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura used her thumb and forefingers to make a frame, and placed both herself and me in the frame several times. Then she made an embarrassed face, like she regretted doing so. It was certainly true that a camera was essential for preserving memories. Since Sakura was always carrying her digital camera around at school, I imagined that this moment was probably the perfect photo opportunity.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Preserving memories, huh? So that was why Ibuki had a digital camera.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But if I’m in the picture, wouldn’t that spoil the scenery?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, if </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you’re</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> in the picture, Ayanokouji-kun, it will be the best photo… Ah! No! I mean, that is, I’ve never taken a picture with a friend before!” cried Sakura, shaking her head.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura was absolutely genuine. While we sat side by side, I suddenly stared at her. Sakura didn’t notice my gaze at first, but after a long period of silence, she finally did. Our eyes met.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what?! What is it?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Calm down. Be quiet.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura had fallen into a state of panic. I placed my hands on both of her shoulders and held her firmly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Eek!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I brought my body closer to Sakura’s, sidling up next to her. When I did so, Sakura froze like a frog trapped by a snake’s glare. My eyes focused on her hair, where a bug was crawling around. Even someone like me, who didn’t know a lot about insects, could identify it. People commonly referred to it as a “hairy caterpillar.” Honestly, it grossed me out. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The way its body squirmed and the fact that it had countless legs sent a shiver down my spine. It had probably fallen off the tree we were resting against. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">What should I do?</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I thought. If I told Sakura she had a caterpillar in her hair, she might panic and start screaming. If the caterpillar got into her clothes, that’d be an even worse disaster.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sakura, there’s something I want to ask you…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what is it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You…are you okay with bugs?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“B-bugs?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. Bugs. Like grasshoppers and dragonflies, those kinds of things”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“N-not at all. I absolutely can’t stand them. Ants either.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Well, all right then.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t point it out here, then. I needed to think of another way. I wished I could just quickly snatch it away, but being a city boy, I hated insects, too. Even if I tried to remove the bug with a branch or something, Sakura would undoubtedly notice.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm. Anyway, don’t move for the time being, okay?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“O-okay. Sure…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that careful warning, I let go of Sakura’s shoulders. Meanwhile, the caterpillar squirmed like it was starting to go somewhere. I’m sure it just wanted to escape, and I had to think of a way to remove it safely. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s the matter?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I tried to come up with a plan, Sakura tilted her head with a bewildered look. Perhaps sensing that Sakura’s movement meant danger, the caterpillar frantically tried to escape. Ah, this wasn’t good! Don’t be reckless, bug!</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t delay any longer. I had to save Sakura even if it meant sacrificing myself. With my trembling hand, I courageously reached for Sakura’s hair. There! I touched the caterpillar, grabbed it quickly, and threw it into the bushes. Sakura probably didn’t understand the situation, but I’d managed to protect her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh…I feel like there was something gross on me…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After our break, we chatted amiably and arrived at our destination with the help of my handkerchief acting as a marker. It had taken less time than I thought to get there, only about twenty minutes in total. At any rate, I carefully retrieved the handkerchief and returned it to Sakura, then observed the surroundings from where I thought Kouenji had been standing before. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nothing in these woods stood out at first glance. There wasn’t anything else here. What in the world had he been looking at?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you notice something?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Umm… What’s different?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If your sense of sight did not work, you had to rely on your other senses.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For the time being, let’s investigate the area, but don’t go too far. Then let’s check on each other regularly. If we don’t concentrate, it’s likely we’ll get distracted.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We searched the soil and the big tree’s roots on our knees, and tried touching the lush green leaves and branches above our heads. Our sense of smell was sharp as we breathed warm air through our noses. We also tried to open our ears and listen. We made use of all five of our senses and checked the area thoroughly, careful not to overlook even the slightest change.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wha—?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura, who’d been searching in the bushes away from me, shrieked in surprise. The bushes were so thick that I could only see part of her body, and wondered if she’d fallen over again.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, look! I found something amazing!” Sakura’s voice was full of eager excitement.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I tried to figure out what she was talking about, I&#160;saw a different variety of green leaves sprouting from the bushes. Some yellow parts peeked through as well.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is this…corn?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seems like it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered if the corn only grew in this area. I didn’t know much about plants, but it was obvious that this was unnatural. The soil here was a different color from the surrounding forest soil, proof that the corn had been cultivated artificially. It was also strange because bushes surrounded the corn on all sides, making it difficult to find due to the weeds.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, this is what Kouenji saw…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He’d noticed the corn at first glance, and said nothing due to his awful pride. In any case, I was absolutely certain that school officials had been coming and going on this deserted island. This cultivated spot made that clear. I inspected the corn, which was rather beautiful thanks to careful management and cultivation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would’ve been nice if we brought bags… We probably can’t, but I wonder if we should try taking it all back with us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There were about fifty ears of corn, but carrying them unaided was impossible. We would inevitably need to make several trips if we wanted to bring them all back. I took off my shirt.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?! Wh-wh-wh-what are you doing, Ayanokouji-kun?! It’s way too soon for that!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura dropped the corn as she covered her eyes with her hands.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry, sorry. I thought it would be okay. Wait, what did you mean by ‘it’s too soon’?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t think a girl her age would mind seeing a topless guy, but maybe I hadn’t showed enough consideration.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If we tie up the openings of my shirt, we can use it as a bag. That way we could carry more at once.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was afraid that if we left and other classes found this place, they’d harvest the corn. I wanted to minimize that&#160;risk.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ll tell everyone when we get back, and then come harvest more.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The unexpected harvest had made both of us gleeful, until we spotted some unexpected visitors.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow. Look, Katsuragi-san! There’s an incredible amount of food here!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura, her attention focused on the corn, jumped in surprise. She hid behind my back. When Katsuragi saw this, he apologized.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry. We didn’t mean to surprise you. He had no bad intentions, either. Please forgive us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He shot Yahiko a stern look, compelling him to apologize. Yahiko, in low spirits, apologized. I’d encountered these two before. Katsuragi showed no reaction, but Yahiko immediately noticed me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, you’re the spy from yesterday!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yahiko raised his voice, shouting angrily. Sakura once again jumped in surprise and curled into a ball. Seeing this, Katsuragi dropped his tightly clenched fist onto Yahiko’s head. We could hear the dull, painful thud from where we stood.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My name’s Katsuragi, from Class A. He’s Yahiko. Since this is the second time we’ve met, a self-introduction should be fine, yes?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m Ayanokouji, from Class D. This is Sakura.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After we’d exchanged a brief greeting, Katsuragi glanced at the large amount of corn.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You found this. Don’t worry, we have no intention of snatching it away from you. But if someone else finds this spot, they’ll likely take it away.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s nothing we can do. There’s only the two of us here.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We had no choice but to pray that no one else found this place. One idea was to hide it all, but that didn’t lessen the potential danger.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You idiots. One of you two can stay behind and watch the stuff! Isn’t that right, Katsuragi-san?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re the one who doesn’t seem to understand the situation, Yahiko. Don’t dismiss the danger of moving through the forest alone. It’s difficult enough in groups of men, but when men and women are together, no matter what, there will be certain limitations to what you can do.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was why Katsuragi didn’t go alone into the forest.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ll help you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A-are you serious, Katsuragi-san? But, working together with Class D is—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yahiko obviously wanted to refuse, but he stayed quiet after Katsuragi shot him a sharp look.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We appreciate the offer, but our class told us to be careful. They’ll get mad if they find out we relied on Class A for help. I’m sorry, but we have to decline.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was a lie, but Katsuragi had no other choice but to withdraw.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. We can’t force you. However, can you trust us? After you leave here, there’s a chance we’ll make off with all of this, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, I’d have no choice but to give up what I’m carrying right now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Katsuragi quietly cleared a path. Sakura still looked worried as we sped away. Afterward, when Sakura and I returned to base camp, we reported to everyone about the corn. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What an amazing find, Ayanokouji! You too, Sakura! Let’s go get it, Yamauchi!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike called out to Yamauchi, who was nearby. After spotting Sakura and me talking with Ike, Yamauchi dashed over with incredible intensity. He grabbed my arm and shoved me away from Sakura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“H-h-hey, you! Why were you all alone and shirtless with Sakura?! Why?! Huh?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Calm down. This is a misunderstanding, that’s all. We didn’t do anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t know what kind of delusions Yamauchi was having, but now wasn’t the time to get into it with him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have to go talk with Hirata. Sorry.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I trusted you, Ayanokouji!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi ranted and raved while I went to report to Hirata. Soon afterward, we organized students into a team, which departed from the camp to bring back the corn. In addition, we set the goal of exploring other places and looking for more food. Around one o’clock in the afternoon, everyone returned from harvesting the&#160;corn.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ve got lots of food!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The bag appeared to be absolutely filled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It was pretty dangerous, though. That guy from Class A, Katsuragi, was nearby.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently Katsuragi hadn’t made off with the corn after we left. Instead, he stayed there so that he could watch us. Whether Katsuragi had good or bad intentions, we didn’t know for sure.</span></p>
