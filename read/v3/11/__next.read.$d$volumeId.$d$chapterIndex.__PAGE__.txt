Thanks to the fruit our classmates had found, we’d saved more points than anticipated, and were making it through this test.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Currently, we’d spent a total of about 100 points, including setbacks like Kouenji’s retirement. Even Yukimura, who’d resisted the most at the start, had no complaints. Every single person was satisfied with our results.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I started to get a prickly feeling in my head, which was throbbing. I borrowed a ballpoint pen, put it in my pocket along with the folded-up piece of paper from before, and left base camp. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d started to figure out more about this test. If I were to break it down, eighty percent of it was defensive, determining whether your class could form cooperative relationships. However, this 8:2 proportion wasn’t reflected in the test results. Rather, that twenty percent influenced the outcome even more.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We already understood each class’ plans. In that case, we knew what we had to do, which was attack the other classes. The true appeal of that spot wasn’t just that it offered shelter from the elements; the place itself had meaning. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I wandered through the forest, I heard the faint sound of ocean waves. I picked up the pace, and successfully pushed through the trees and toward the coast.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whoa…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I applied the brakes and came to a full stop before a cliff’s edge.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I spotted several facilities located rather close to the cave. It didn’t seem like there was any kind of detour route, but as I walked along the cliff, I noticed a ladder set up in a blind spot. Anyone would’ve missed it upon first glance. I grabbed onto the ladder with all of my strength. It seemed sturdy, so I used it to climb down to the bottom of the cliff. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Shortly after getting down, I found a small hut. Near the entrance, I saw a device—proof that this was a spot. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I checked to see whether the spot had been occupied, I saw the words Class A displayed on the device. It seemed they had four hours remaining. I didn’t doubt that Katsuragi and the others had come here and taken possession of the area after they’d held down the cave. You wouldn’t have known about the spot’s existence unless you discovered it back when we were on the boat. Since the small hut was located right under the cliff, you didn’t have to worry about anyone in the surrounding area seeing you.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The tools inside looked unused. Dust had accumulated on them. Measuring it accurately would’ve taken an insane amount of time. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After I finished marking it on my map, I refolded the piece of paper and put it back in my pocket. Since there didn’t seem to be anything else, I climbed the ladder back up the cliff.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When we circled around the island, I saw a tower over there…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While scanning the area, I relied on my memory. I&#160;shifted my gaze toward the ground that had been trodden on by people. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although it seemed possible to look out over the whole beach by going up the installed ladder, the facility didn’t seem that useful. It seemed like some spots were better than others.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I drew closer to confirm the equipment on the wall of the facility. Unlike the last place I’d checked, this place didn’t seem to be occupied. The facility itself was rather large, so even though the place was well in the backwoods, it would’ve been easy to find. In other words, that meant I didn’t know who had been monitoring the area. The fact that no one was occupying the tower showed that holding it ran the risk of being observed by the enemy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He wouldn’t carelessly take the bait, even bait this sweet and this close.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In other words, they intended to sign a contract now, and transfer private points after the test.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although I wanted to see Sudou’s efforts bear fruit with Horikita, Chabashira-sensei had called for me. It was best to get unpleasant things over with as soon as possible.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey! Where are you going?” Sudou called, stopping me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Where do you think? I got called over by the teacher, so I don’t really have a choice.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Can you wait just a little? Just a bit?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That expression grossed me out. He grabbed my wrists with his thick, meaty hands and didn’t let go.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re gonna watch me do battle. Be my wingman.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t say such nonsense—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Bye.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While we bickered, Horikita finished getting ready to go and stood up from her seat. She left the classroom without any hesitation. Sudou simply watched her go, completely dumbfounded.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Damn. Guess it was useless after all. Well, guess I’ll go do club stuff.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita’s absence meant I wasn’t needed, so I left. As I arrived at the faculty room, I saw Chabashira-sensei waiting in the doorway.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come in.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t understand why you called me over.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ll talk inside.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My “incoming depression” meter rose steadily as she met my questions with such short answers. I’d hoped she’d called me here as a joke.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You might expect bad things when you’re asked to come to the faculty room, but contrary to your expectations, it’s a fine place. There aren’t eyes all around in here. Many things are better said with some personal privacy.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I noticed that the security camera, which should’ve been installed in a room like this, was missing.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So what did you want to talk to me about? I’m busy planning my summer vacation right now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s funny. I thought you didn’t have any friends?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, no, I was exaggerating when I said that. I have at least a few friends.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although I could count the number of friends on two hands, the quantity wasn’t important. Or at least that’s what they say. Besides, wouldn’t it have been just fine if I spent summer vacation by myself?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve called you here today because I wanted to tell you my personal story.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira-sensei’s story? This was headed in a very different direction. I didn’t understand why she’d called me over by name and wanted to tell me her story. Nor did I have any interest.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s something I haven’t talked to anyone about since I had become a homeroom teacher. It’s silly, but please listen.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Before that, should we have tea? You must be thirsty,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I stood up from my pipe chair and opened the kitchenette’s door. No one was inside, right?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t tell this story to anyone else. If you can do that, please return to your seat.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I closed the door and returned to sit with Chabashira-sensei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How do I appear to you as Class D’s homeroom teacher?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Another abstract question, I see. Is it okay if I say I think you’re beautiful?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She didn’t even twitch an eyebrow when I made that joke. But I could feel her bloodlust rising.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Umm… Well, if you don’t mind being compared to other teachers, I think you don’t care at all about Class D’s future, and that you’re a cold teacher with no interest in her students. That’s my answer.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She wasn’t as friendly as Class B’s homeroom teacher, Hoshinomiya-sensei, nor was she as willing to help out her students like Class C’s homeroom teacher, Sakagami-sensei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Am I wrong?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, it’s as you say. I won’t deny it. However, the truth is different.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira-sensei paused and looked up to the ceiling, as if she’d just remembered something.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was once a student at this school. I was in Class D, just like you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I must say that’s surprising. I would’ve thought you were more capable, Chabashira-sensei.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh… Well, in my time, the class difference wasn’t as extreme. You could say that we were in a four-way battle, not a three-way one. Up until we approached graduation in the third semester of our third year, the difference between A and D wasn’t even 100 points. It was a close battle, where even one trivial mistake could knock you off balance.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t feel as though she were bragging. Rather, her story felt like one of regret.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m guessing someone made a trivial mistake, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. It happened rather unexpectedly. Class C went to hell because of my mistake. In the end, my goal of reaching Class A and my dreams were shattered.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I felt really sorry for her, but having her bring up her past was really troublesome. If anything, it felt uncomfortable.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not catching your drift. What does this have to do with me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I feel that your presence will be vital to reach Class A.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What am I supposed to say to that? You’re joking, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I felt happy to have been praised so unexpectedly, but I didn’t know how to answer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A few days ago, a certain man contacted the school directly. He said ‘expel Ayanokouji Kiyotaka.’”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira-sensei made a complete change of topic. She was getting to the true issue.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He said to expel me? Well, that’s nonsense. I don’t know who it was, but you ignored his request and won’t have me expelled. Right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course. We can’t just expel someone on a third party’s whim. As long as you are a student at this school, you are protected by the rules. However…if you cause any problems, that’s a different story. Smoking, bullying, stealing, cheating… If you cause any scandal, expulsion is unavoidable.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry, but I don’t intend to do anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It has nothing to do with your intentions. If I determine that something seems like an issue, it will become reality.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you threatening me?” I found her wording suspicious.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Here’s the deal, Ayanokouji. You are going to aim for Class A for me. I will follow up as extensively as I can in order to protect you. Don’t you think that sounds like a good offer?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d thought she had changed a lot from when I first met her, but I never could’ve imagined she would blackmail a student. I laughed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Can I go now? I won’t listen to any more of this.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s too bad, Ayanokouji. You will be expelled, and once again, Class D won’t reach Class A.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her speech and behavior weren’t just for show. She seriously intended to cut me. She was placing her unachieved dreams on my shoulders.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let me ask you once more. Will you aim for Class A? Or be expelled? You choose.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With my left hand, I reached over the desk and grabbed Chabashira-sensei’s collar.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I remember when Horikita said you made her feel uncomfortable. I wonder if she felt like this. It’s like entering someone’s home with shoes on.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Right.” Chabashira-sensei, who’d behaved with confidence until then, laughed in self-deprecation. “I surprised myself. I realize now that I still haven’t given up on reaching Class A.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her eyes had gotten slightly misty. Her usual cold indifference was gone. When she grabbed my left arm, my hand still grasping her collar, I saw the forceful determination had returned to her eyes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’d thought you would lead Class D voluntarily, but we don’t have any time to waste. You have to decide here and now. Will you help me or not?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Luke, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Star Wars</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">’ protagonist, originally chose to return to his uncle’s farm and reject the call to adventure. However, he was eventually dragged into the horrors of war. That was his destiny. You might say I should’ve taken this woman’s story with a grain of salt. I didn’t know how much of it was true.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ll probably regret trying to use me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Relax. My life is already full of regrets.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was the troublesome event that launched my summer vacation. It was something I didn’t like to think about. At any rate, I couldn’t lose my current school life. Throwing away your freedom in order to protect it… How ridiculous.</span></p>
