<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 6:<br/>False Teamwork</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">W</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">hile I was sleeping, I could hear girls’ voices from outside the tent. They sounded like they were in a bad mood.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, boys. Can you all gather around?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The voice sounded rough, like she’d meant to say, “Hurry and wake already!” I’d just gotten to sleep at dawn, so I rose slowly and rubbed my eyes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What the hell? God, I’m so goddamn tired…” An irritated Sudou appeared from the tent and looked around.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s wrong?” Hirata asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, Hirata-kun. I’m sorry, but can you please wake all of the boys? It’s serious,” said Shinohara, sounding apologetic.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Whether she was flustered or angry, the issue didn’t seem to be hers alone. A little further away, the girls glared at us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand. I think if I shout, they’ll come.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Within two minutes, the boys came out of the tent rubbing their sleepy eyes. When the half-asleep boys looked around, they inferred that this situation was especially alarming. The girls all looked unusually scared.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s going on? Why did you wake us up so early?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry, Hirata-kun. This doesn’t involve you, but…we’ve gathered everyone to confirm something.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Shinohara gave everyone except Hirata a look of complete contempt.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, this morning…Karuizawa-san’s underwear went missing. Do you know what this means?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“U-underwear?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even Hirata, usually calm and collected, appeared visibly shaken. Speaking of Karuizawa, she was missing, along with some of her friends.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Karuizawa-san is crying inside the tent right now. Kushida-san and others are comforting her right now, but…” Shinohara looked over to the girls’ tent.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Huh? What? Why are you glaring at </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">us</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> over her underwear being missing?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Isn’t that obvious? Someone went through her bag in the middle of the night and stole them. Our luggage was outside the tent, so if someone wanted to steal something, they easily could have!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The boys, still in a state of drowsiness, all exchanged looks.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, no, no, no! Huh?! Huh?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike, in a complete panic, looked back and forth between the boys and girls. One of the boys who’d been observing all this grumbled calmly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come to think of it, Ike, you were pretty late getting back from the toilet yesterday. You took a really long time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, no, no! That was just, well…I was struggling because it was dark!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that so? You stole Karuizawa’s underwear, didn’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-you’re wrong! I didn’t do it!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The boys started blaming one another for this particularly nasty crime.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Anyway. This is a huge problem, don’t you agree? It’s impossible for us to camp alongside a bunch of underwear thieves,” said Shinohara, her arms crossed. She looked as though she was going to lose her temper.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hirata-kun, can you find the culprit?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, there’s no evidence that the boys stole it. Maybe Karuizawa lost it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, that’s right! We have nothing to do with this!” The boys shouted behind Hirata, proclaiming their innocence.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t want to think there’s a criminal among us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Doubting our classmates seemed wrong.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know that you’re not the culprit, Hirata-kun. But for the time being, let’s check the boys’ luggage.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, the girls weren’t changing their minds on this. They had decided that the culprit was on the boys’ side. Well, I supposed that it was only natural to think that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Don’t give us that crap. We don’t need to do that. Hirata, tell them no.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For now, we’ll try gathering the guys together and talking it out. Can you please give us a little time?” Hirata asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you say so, Hirata-kun. I understand. I’ll try talking to Karuizawa-san. But if the culprit can’t be found, we have some ideas.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that, everyone scattered. Hirata quickly gathered all the boys in front of the tent. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s just ignore what the girls said. I hate being treated like a suspect. I’ll fight it!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike had managed to get some degree of trust from the girls on the first day, but apparently it wasn’t meant to last. It was only natural that the boys would be unhappy about being unjustly accused. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Exactly. It’s not like we stole Karuizawa’s underwear or anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi exchanged looks with everyone individually. It wasn’t as though Karuizawa wasn’t cute or anything, but since Karuizawa was Hirata’s girlfriend, it would’ve been a much better idea to go after Kushida and Sakura. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t doubt you guys, but we won’t solve the problem like this.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The girls, who were talking together in their cliques, looked like they were about to jump on us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It might be better to just accept the baggage inspection with dignity and prove your innocence.” With that, Hirata brought out his own bag.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“As miserable as this might be, I think you guys’ll have to do it. Are you okay with that?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“B-but…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course. I’ll open up my luggage first,” Hirata said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In order to get us all to move, he had no choice but to take action. Still, there probably wasn’t even one person here who thought Hirata was the culprit. Besides, you could say that stealing his own girlfriend’s underwear made no sense. However, if one person opened his luggage, then the rest of us couldn’t help but follow. Inevitably, the students who wouldn’t open their bags would be suspected. Hirata’s bag naturally didn’t have the underwear.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Guess we don’t have any choice…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">All the other boys began pulling bags out, one after another. Ike and Yamauchi hated it, but couldn’t resist being pulled in. The three of us were the last to go. I reluctantly headed into the tent, following after Ike and Yamauchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Damn, I’m so pissed. Men are always being suspected of stuff. It’s way too unreasonable.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, let’s prove our innocence.” Ike grabbed his bag, but suddenly froze.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s wrong?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, nothing…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He turned his back on Hirata and the others, checked the inside of his bag, and frantically closed it back up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kanji?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike’s face was pale, his body stiff. He was completely paralyzed. “Hey, come on. Let’s hurry and get going.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What, you’re the one that really stole them?” said Yamauchi, half-jokingly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Th-that’s crazy talk!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike frantically denied it, shaking his head while clutching his bag. What a blatant overreaction. We weren’t dumb enough to believe nothing was wrong.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, don’t tell me…” Yamauchi said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What? You don’t believe me?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I’m not saying that. Show me what’s in your bag.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, wait!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi snatched Ike’s bag to check inside. When he did, he saw…white underwear, definitely not a man’s, balled up and hidden.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Th-that’s not mine! Someone put it in my bag or something, somehow!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, don’t give me that excuse…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi looked at Ike with pity.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m telling you, I don’t know how it got there! Why would there be underwear in my bag?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is shameful. Let’s go explain things to Hirata and the others.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?! But if I do that, they’ll make me out to be the culprit!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There was no culprit…right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Why was Yamauchi asking Ike? Ike had Karuizawa’s underwear in his bag, making Ike the culprit, right? Putting aside when and how he stole the underwear, the thief likely wouldn’t have hidden the stolen goods in his own bag. It was obvious that in the case of an uproar, a search for the criminal would begin. If Ike was really guilty, he should’ve been panicking when he was told to open his luggage. But I hadn’t seen the slightest hint of that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I concluded that someone other than Ike was the culprit, and that person had planted evidence to frame Ike. Unless Ike really was that stupid and simple…but he couldn’t be, right?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji, you believe me, right? That I didn’t steal them?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, if I think calmly, no solid evidence suggests you’re the culprit, Ike.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji!” he cried.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s unlikely that Ike is the culprit. If he were, this would be far too stupid of him.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that sounds right, but… Wait, what? You mean someone put the underwear in Kanji’s bag?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We just have to figure out who!” Ike cried.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, hurry up!” one of the boys by Hirata cried.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-wh-wh-wh-what am I gonna do? I’m in serious trouble!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If the stolen item were found here, the girls would probably determine that Ike was the culprit.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We have no choice but to hide them. Now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hide them? Where?! We can’t hide them!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was certainly true that we currently lacked storage options. If the girls saw us hurry to the toilet or into the tents, they’d become suspicious and demand to search that area. Most importantly, we were spending too much time in here. It wouldn’t be surprising if we were already under suspicion. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We have no other choice. You have to put it in your pocket.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was the only advice I could give. There wasn’t any time to hide the underwear anyplace else, and we didn’t want to draw attention to ourselves.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I concluded that Ibuki had stolen the underwear from Karuizawa’s bag and hidden it in Ike’s luggage.</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.3</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">t was the end of the special test’s fifth day, and Class D was depressed. It was like we were holding an all-night vigil over a dead body. A whole day had passed with everyone jumping at shadows. Everyone was suspicious, and no one knew who the culprit was. Despite the oppressive atmosphere, it was my turn to get the fire going. As I watched the fire’s condition, I occasionally tossed in some branches. It was monotonous, easy work. We had other problems, though.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Ayanokouji-kun! Didn’t we tell you to move the tent properly?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I moved it like I was told.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose that’s true. I’m certainly not two-faced.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was an understatement. Horikita lived her life completely true to herself. She threw my criticism back at me rather skillfully. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Exactly. Trustworthy Hirata-kun has kept his mouth shut about it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come with me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered what in the world she wanted to show me so badly that she’d leave the fire unattended. On top of that was a battery-powered cordless fan.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The tent on the other side has the exact same things in it, too. A total of twelve points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">thought</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> the girls were putting up with the heat unusually well. So this is how you’re getting through it.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Karuizawa-san and the others asked for these.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I only found out after they’d already ordered everything. It’s difficult to do anything when the rules say anyone may buy an item and spend points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just like how Kouenji had withdrawn so early in the test.</span></p>

But </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> didn’t know, nor did he inform anyone else. I think he absolutely should have shared this information.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita had her arms crossed. She did have a point, but I doubted Hirata had stayed quiet out of malice. Maybe he’d just wanted to avoid unnecessary confusion? If Karuizawa had reported properly to Hirata, then the amount could have been evaluated.</span></p>

We can’t get spent points back, and there aren’t many days left before the test ends. Karuizawa and the others probably won’t spend any more points,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d thought she’d be angered by such a short, blunt response, but Horikita seemed to have anticipated my words. She promptly ignored me and kept talking.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If things stay as they are and nothing happens, people will probably stay quiet. But things could get bad if the case of the stolen underwear isn’t resolved. If the culprit is nearby, he or she might be trying to hinder us. That’s why I want to find the culprit as soon as possible.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. Now that there’s a divide between us and the boys, there are many things I can’t do by myself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The guys and girls were in the midst of a cold war, cut off from obtaining new information.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Our class is all messed up because of this. It’s a shame, because we were doing really well.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder what the culprit’s goal is, whoever that may be. Did they intend to steal Karuizawa’s underwear, or did they want to throw our teamwork out the window? I&#160;feel like there’s a hidden agenda.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s not get ahead of ourselves… Sorry, but I’m going back into the tent.” Horikita turned away, sweeping her hair aside. Her breathing was shallow.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Confessed? Confessed what?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although Horikita pretended to be calm, she was sweaty. Enough was enough.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She’d looked ill even before we started traveling, but it hadn’t been that noticeable. Due to Horikita’s loner personality, she’d likely planned keep to her room and avoid socializing.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When…did you notice?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“On the deck of the ship, when I asked you what you’d been doing.”</span></p>

If you reported illness, the class would be hit with a bigger penalty. The test’s timing was rotten luck on her part.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So she intended to soldier through to the bitter end. She had an iron will.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> felt something strange on my cheek, warm and stiff. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Suzune…I can’t hold back anymore…” he moaned.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s wrong? Did something happen?” I asked Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Follow me. We can’t talk here.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that, Horikita walked toward the forest. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s wrong? Are you planning to search for more food?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita walked without answering me. She stopped once we’d gotten far enough away that we couldn’t see the camp anymore. Horikita turned around and looked ready to speak, but then hesitated as though having second thoughts.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This happened because of my carelessness. I am aware that I made a mistake. Okay?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A mistake?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It was stolen.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-wait, your underwear was stolen, too?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No. This is much, much worse. The…key card. A complete blunder on my part.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita looked completely disgusted with herself, a look I had never seen her make before.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wanted to talk to you because I trust you. I absolutely couldn’t consult with someone who might be the culprit. This is so humiliating. I want to die…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I felt honored that she trusted me, but I couldn’t exactly rejoice in front of someone so depressed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Such a huge failure.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, the one who stole it is to blame. Right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even so, this is an issue of responsibility. It has nothing to do with me being sick or covered in mud.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita hung her head. If this got out, it could cause us massive damage.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wasn’t supposed to let go of the card for a single second. But I…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t blame yourself. I doubt this’ll be any consolation, but I think you did your absolute best.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t know if she heard me. She merely bit her lower lip, as if overwhelmed with regret.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s probably better if we don’t make this information public. We should get to the truth first.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I think so, too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Everyone would panic if they knew. I wanted to avoid that at least.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suspect two people. Either Karuizawa-san, or Ibuki-san.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The former might have done it simply out of hatred. Karuizawa could have stolen it because she wanted to see Horikita panic after losing the card.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, the chances of that are low. Karuizawa was in front of the shower the whole time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re sure about that?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. Same goes for her flunkies, too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If that’s the case, it’s highly likely Ibuki-san is the culprit. It’s possible she came to know about the card this morning, and the timing is just far too good. But, wouldn’t stealing it be an extremely dangerous gamble? Since the leader’s name is engraved on the card itself, just looking at it would be enough. Maybe she carried out the crime so she’d get a penalty.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She looked at me, her eyes full of anxiety, as if searching for an answer from me. I placed my hand on Horikita’s shoulder.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If we examine the timing and talk with Ibuki, we can understand what happened. If we suspect Ibuki, we shouldn’t take our eyes off of her. Her escape would probably be the worst-case scenario, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s right. I’m sorry, but can you go back to camp first? That way you can start following her immediately.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure. I understand. I’ll keep an eye on her.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I felt like Horikita probably wanted to be alone to throw up. I left her and returned to the base camp.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.7</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">H</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">orikita returned about ten minutes later, rejoining the campsite’s unsettling atmosphere. The cause was dark smoke coming from behind the temporary toilet. It was far too early for the bonfire to be lit, and the location was rather strange.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s that smoke? What in the world happened?” Ike cried.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I joined up with Horikita, I also caught up with Ike, who was clearly in a panic. I asked what was up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is serious. There’s a fire! Fire! Something is burning behind the toilet!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">All of the girls who’d been lined up in front of the shower room were gone now. They must have left as soon as they heard the uproar.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t see Ibuki. The fire might have been her handiwork. Where is she?” Horikita asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She noticed the fire, and now she’s just walking around.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I rushed toward the area behind the temporary toilet and saw Hirata and some of the others. Ibuki was also there. Horikita looked ready to call out to Ibuki, but hesitated when she saw her. Ibuki’s expression was just so genuine. She couldn’t hide her confusion about the fire.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Does this mean she didn’t do it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita was overwhelmed by doubt. If the keycard had indeed been stolen, Ibuki must’ve done it. If a fire had started, then Ibuki must’ve caused it. Even so, Ibuki remained at the scene, and appeared surprised by the fire. When I examined more closely, the source looked like a bundle of paper. Some of it was still legible, but most of it had turned into soot. I didn’t know what it was for a moment.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, looking at the legible parts, I finally understood.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did the manual get burned?” Horikita asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. It looks like it. Who would do this?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s just one thing after another…” Horikita muttered in a low voice, lowering her eyes. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m responsible for this. The manual was in my bag. We piled the bags up in front of the tent and I didn’t think that someone would steal anything in the daytime. But first, we need to extinguish this fire properly…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Rather than searching for the culprit, Hirata headed to the river to douse the fire. While he drew water in our plastic bottles, he muttered to himself, his expression dark.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why? Who could do something like this? Why can’t we all just get along?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata spontaneously crushed the plastic bottle with all his might. The shift in his personality was rather terrifying. Hirata, the eternal leader of our class, the one who toiled tirelessly to play peacemaker, was carrying a terrible burden.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t think you need to take so much on yourself.” I tried comforting him. He stood and responded with a quiet “Thank you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We need…to discuss this incident properly.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Right. Most of Class D witnessed the fire. I’m sure they’ll want to know the truth.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Depressed, Hirata took the water he’d scooped up and returned to the campsite.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, who did this? Is there a traitor in our class?” Karuizawa asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When we returned, we found her leading the confrontation between the guys and girls, who glared at one another.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why do you suspect us? Isn’t this a completely separate issue from the underwear incident?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know about that. Isn’t it possible you burned something to mislead us?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Stop jerking us around. As if we’d do something like this!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait a minute, everyone. Please, calm down. Let’s talk about this,” Hirata cried.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He gave me the water and I took his place, extinguishing the remnants of the fire. Hirata immediately went to the center of the circle and tried to play mediator. This might have been residual stress from yesterday’s underwear theft incident, but both sides were heated up and showed no signs of settling down. It seemed like quite a few people in Class D wanted to start hunting the culprit right here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At any rate, we don’t have to worry about the fire spreading.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I shook the empty plastic bottles twice, then three times. A few water droplets plopped onto the smoldering remains of the fire. I looked up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Rain, huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Raindrops dripped onto my cheeks. The clouds were even darker than they’d been before, proof that heavy rain was about to start. Once, we’d have all come together as one to make it through this last pinch. Now, the guys and girls were locked in a tense confrontation. They stood, glaring at each other.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is pointless. Seriously, this is the worst. We have underwear thieves and now arsonists in our class. This really is the worst.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We keep telling you it wasn’t us! How long are you going to keep suspecting us?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The fight would never be resolved. It would just keep going on forever. Hirata should’ve stepped in and stopped this, but for some reason he just stood there in a daze. Was he wondering who the culprit could have been?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey Kanji, I can’t see Ibuki anywhere.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi had noticed Ibuki was gone. I noticed that one of the bags was also gone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Perhaps the person who started the fire…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s rather suspicious. If a fire broke out then, that would mean…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The guys directed their suspicions at Ibuki, and even the girls began to voice their doubts. However, before we could reach a resolution, the rain started to fall, and fall hard.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no, this isn’t good. Let’s discuss this later. It’ll be awful if we all get soaked.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike and the others, panicking, began shoving food and luggage inside the tents. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hirata, tell us what to do!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike called for Hirata, but he just stood in the same place. Hirata continued staring off into nothingness and didn’t budge an inch. Meanwhile, the sound of rain kept growing louder and louder. I was a little worried about the situation. I approached Hirata, but there wasn’t any sign that he noticed me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why…why is this happening? It’s just like back then…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He muttered something in a low voice. I couldn’t understand what it meant, but it certainly wasn’t trivial. This wasn’t like the calm and composed Hirata at all.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol3_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_13" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v3/OEBPS/Images/ClassroomElite-Vol3_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_13.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why was I doing this? Why was I doing all this until now?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Hirata! What are you doing?!” Ike shouted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It wasn’t clear if Hirata heard him at all. I gently placed my hand on his shoulder. He looked surprised, but slowly turned around.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ike’s calling for you,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata’s face was drained of life. He was pale. The second time Ike called, Hirata slowly started to regain his sanity. He finally noticed that it had begun raining.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Rain…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’d be good for you to help Ike and the others. We have to keep our stuff dry.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-yeah. We need to take care of everything quickly.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji. Is Hirata okay?” Sudou asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Looks like he’s in shock. I suppose it’s probably because of all these things going wrong one after the other.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know, in junior high there was this rich kid honor student. He had a lot of really heavy responsibilities, y’know? Anyway, he took so much on himself that one day, he ended up having a breakdown. After that, his class became a complete mess.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You think there are signs of that with Hirata?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, saying he’s gonna have a breakdown would be an exaggeration, but I think there’s danger there.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered if this was just Sudou’s wild imagination at work, but it seemed surprisingly accurate. Since this special test had started, Hirata has taken on a lot of responsibilities. These issues made the troubles we’d faced at school seem easy. Hirata’s carefully maintained environment had definitely begun to change. The theft of Karuizawa’s underwear and the uproar over the fire had turned Hirata as unhinged and stormy as the weather.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For the time being, let’s take care of the luggage.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We joined up and helped the students put things away. Thankfully, everything got secured pretty fast.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. All of the preparations are made.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It didn’t surprise me that Ibuki disappeared, but Horikita had vanished as well. By my calculations, the possibility had been fifty-fifty, but it seemed things were progressing favorably. I fixed my eyes on the road that led straight to the beach, and stepped onto the path.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.8</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> forced my heavy, sluggish body to chase after Ibuki-san while the hard rain fell. The sky was covered with rain clouds that blocked out the sun, so visibility was poor. Even though I couldn’t see Ibuki-san, she’d left footprints in the muddy ground. If I just followed them, they’d lead me right to her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She’d walked about a hundred meters from the base camp, sometimes veering off to the right or left. Rather unexpectedly, I found her waiting, as if expecting me. I instinctively hid myself, though there probably wasn’t any point.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you doing, Horikita?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki spoke without even turning around. Her calm voice cut right through the sounds of the falling rain.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I noticed you following me. Why don’t you just come on out?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When did you notice me?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Right from the start.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her short answer felt ominous. My impression of her as being quiet and taciturn didn’t change, but something was different.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why were you following me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you really not know?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I don’t know.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was almost like I was the villain here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You clearly know why I was following you, don’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I really have no idea what you’re talking about.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki-san faced me, looking me straight in the eye. I&#160;saw no deception in her eyes whatsoever. It almost made me want to apologize. After all, I had no proof. I only had my intuition. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why should I lie?” she pressed further, as if she noticed my hesitation. “I’d at least like to hear why you followed me, from your own lips.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The underwear theft and the fire. Misfortune continues to befall Class D.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So what?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you realize that some people suspect you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah. I suppose since I’m an outsider, there’s not much I can do about that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s what I’m talking about.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re saying I’m the culprit? Do you have any proof?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, I don’t have a single shred of evidence related to the underwear theft. But I think it was you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s a pretty terrible thing to say. You don’t have any evidence, yet you suspect me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had to admit, I was impressed by how she was handling this. She’d laid low until day five, and she’d kept her distance from Class D. Contrary to normal expectations, she wasn’t suspected. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suspect you because of today. You don’t need me to explain that, do you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wanted to hear it from Ibuki-san herself. If I explained all of the reasons for my doubts, that would almost be the same as admitting my identity as the leader. Even if I was 99% confident, so long as there was a 1% chance of her being innocent, I needed to avoid being direct.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let me cut to the chase. I want you to return something you took from me,” I said to Ibuki-san, while I stood and stared her in the eye.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know what you’re talking about.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After giving that short answer, she walked away quickly. I followed, matching her speed. Ibuki-san changed course and headed toward the center of the forest.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Where are you going?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who knows?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was difficult to walk straight. I’d realized this over the past several days. That was even truer in this weather, which impaired visibility. However, Ibuki-san didn’t seem to care. I couldn’t pull back, though, not after coming this far to find the truth. Since I’d made the mistake, I had to take responsibility and solve the problem.</span></p>

I hated him so much that I wanted to die, but I supposed he was probably the man with abilities closest to those of Class A, after all. I had to acknowledge it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But…is there any guarantee Katsuragi will keep his promise? He might renege.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have that covered, of course. He will have no choice but to honor his promise.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I walked toward Horikita and, after carefully wiping my fingerprints from the keycard, stuck it back in her hand. There was nothing this girl could have done. All she could do now was endure and keep silent until the end of the test, all while knowing that Class C had discovered she was the leader. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This girl didn’t trust anyone. Even after she knew the keycard had been stolen, she hadn’t reported it to her classmates. Even though she’d opened up her heart to Ayanokouji alone, he was a loner, too. If we factored in her current incompetence, she wasn’t any threat. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Besides, if she had the keycard, then her mistake might not have been leaked to Class D yet. I understood her nature, to a certain extent. She was patient and stubborn, the type of person who didn’t listen to other people’s opinions. In other words, no matter how painful something was, she would endure it silently. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Use your smarts to protect yourself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then we disappeared quietly into the dark woods.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.10</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> kicked off from the wet ground and chased Ibuki. The weather was a bothersome problem. If it got much worse, I might get stuck or have an accident. Also, the fact that the sun was going down earlier than I’d anticipated made it difficult to push forward without a flashlight. The passing showers grew even stronger, and the winds started to howl more violently. The weather was just negative all around, no favorable points at all.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I could only see a few meters ahead because of the pouring rain. Also, if I wandered onto any of the side roads, I’d probably get lost. Thankfully, two pairs of footprints remained in the muddy ground and made it easy for me to follow after them. The footprints just suddenly stopped, though. No, on second glance, they didn’t stop; they continued deeper into the forest. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The fact that the footprints suddenly took a sharp turn meant they hadn’t gotten lost, but rather that they’d intentionally ventured deeper into the forest. When I pointed my flashlight toward the depths of the forest, I&#160;saw the two pairs of footprints going deeper and deeper. There was no reason for them to intentionally enter such a dangerous place.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just to be sure, I tried shining the flashlight on the route that led to the beach, but there weren’t any footprints. The ground was clear. I wiped away the rain dripping from my bangs, and followed the footprints deeper into the forest. Naturally, my visibility got even worse. It was like night had already fallen. The atmosphere was creepy and dark, but I pushed forward, relying only on the footprints.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I continued forward for about thirty meters. Suddenly, a bright light entered my field of vision. I immediately turned the flashlight off and held my breath. Looking in the direction of the light, I saw it shine once, then twice more. A flashlight. It was almost as if someone were sending a signal. Was it Ibuki and Horikita? No, that wasn’t it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Neither Ibuki nor Horikita should have had a light source on them. I quietly turned in the direction of the light and drew closer to the source. I heard people’s voices, muffled by the rain, and hid myself. Their conversation sounded trivial. So as long as they didn’t find me, getting a grasp on the situation was secondary.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Soon, the light moved further away. It was over, apparently. Just to be sure, I approached cautiously. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Near a large tree lay a muddy Horikita. She’d collapsed, unconscious. A single keycard lay on the ground near her hand. On her injured body were traces of excavated soil. After examining the situation, I confirmed that more people than Ibuki had discovered Horikita’s position as leader. After picking up the keycard, I lifted Horikita into my arms.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol3_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_14" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v3/OEBPS/Images/ClassroomElite-Vol3_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_14.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ngh…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Horikita let out a small sound, as if expressing discomfort at being held in someone else's arms. Slowly but surely, her eyes fluttered open.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you awake?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayano…kouji-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She sounded dazed, as if she couldn’t understand the situation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Agh… My head…hurts…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You have a high fever. Don’t push yourself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see… I-Ibuki-san… But, why are </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> here?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if I told her to sleep, Horikita wouldn’t listen, all while her fever grew worse. She started to understand the situation little by little.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I knew it…Ibuki-san stole my card.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t be dumber than Sudou-kun and the others.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She chastised herself and closed her eyes, as if lamenting a situation in which she was powerless.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This isn’t a test where you can just hide for twenty-four hours a day, right? No matter what you do, you can be open to attack.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d intended to keep going, but it seemed like anything more would just make the heartbroken Horikita even more depressed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I could’ve avoided this if I knew how to rely on someone…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">To seriously protect the leader’s identity, it was necessary to depend on allies that you trusted from the bottom of your heart. If you did that, you could protect the card twenty-four hours a day. However, Horikita hadn’t made a single friend.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She kept muttering, “I’m so pathetic” to herself quietly over and over.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When I was losing consciousness, I felt like I could hear Ryuuen’s voice… It’s strange, I thought he’d already retired…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You were losing consciousness. Maybe you had a dream?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If it was a dream, it was a nightmare…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered if she really had heard Ryuuen’s voice. Even if she were asleep or losing consciousness, her brain had probably heard something. It wouldn’t have been strange if she picked up Ryuuen’s voice when unconscious.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I was silently lost in thought, Horikita apologized.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why are you apologizing to me?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s no one I can apologize to except for you…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hmm. That made me think pretty hard.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you think things are bad, then make some reliable friends. Start there first.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s difficult advice… No one would want to be with me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It sounded like she’d resigned herself to unhappiness. Perhaps there was a trace of masochism in her. I laughed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s unpleasant to be made fun of, though…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, no, that’s not it,” I said. “It’s just that you’ve started to sound like you need allies.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nobody would say that…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Normally, Horikita would have been insulting me, but right now her words carried a different weight. She was blaming herself, or she wouldn’t have said something like that. Still, it wasn’t going to be easy. Horikita’s hollow eyes seemed to look through rather than at me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I should have understood this a long time ago…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">You can’t live alone in the world. School and society are composed of a great many people.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t talk. You’re sick.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I tried to convince her to be quiet, but Horikita didn’t stop. For Horikita, there had never been any choice but to rely on herself. She could have chosen no other option.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m going to try to get up to Class A using my own abilities. I’ll definitely recover from this failure…” Horikita weakly grabbed my sleeve as she appealed to me. “I’m prepared to be hated by everyone else… This was all my mistake.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“According to this school’s system, if you fight by yourself, you won’t reach Class A. We need to cooperate with our classmates. It’s unavoidable.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita closed her eyes, as if lacking the strength to keep them open. Her grasp might have been weak, but I still felt it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t accept that. No matter how hard it is, I’m still…alone.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, shut up already! Stop talking. Right now, you won’t be able to convince anyone.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I embraced Horikita tightly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You can’t bear every responsibility. You’re not that strong, unfortunately.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So you’re telling me to give up? I have a dream to reach Class A, a dream for my brother to acknowledge me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No one said you have to give up.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I looked down at Horikita, who lightly groaned against my chest.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you can’t fight by yourself, it’s better to fight with a partner. I’ll lend you a hand.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why? You’re not the kind of person who would say such things…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, why then? I wonder.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Shortly afterward, her energy was spent, and Horikita lost consciousness again. I had to carry her without anyone noticing. It would be easy to have her retire, but I didn’t know which button on the wristwatch was for emergencies. Besides, if the helicopter were suddenly dispatched, the sound would reverberate in the area.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm… Did I pick the wrong path? Oh no, oh no!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My route had ended in a steep, sheer slope. If I took one step further, I would fall. I tried shining a light below to see what it looked like about ten meters down. Unfortunately, I’d been walking in the wrong direction. Should I go back to the original route?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I tried to change direction slowly, so as not to burden Horikita, but then right after…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The soil underneath me collapsed, and I lost my balance. Alone, I could have braced my legs and grasped the tree, but unfortunately, both of my hands were occupied. I couldn’t avoid falling. I curled up into a ball so I could protect Horikita as we went tumbling down the slope. For several seconds, it was like flying. I don’t remember very clearly what happened afterward.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At least Horikita hadn’t been hurt, somehow. I looked up the slope, but with the way things were now, it didn’t seem like I could crawl back up while carrying Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I sure messed up.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, this wasn’t the time to accept defeat and die. Carrying the unconscious Horikita on my back, I ventured into the pitch-dark forest with a single flashlight. The rain poured down on us, mercilessly robbing me of my physical strength. More than anything else, the heat radiating from Horikita wasn’t normal. If she were exposed to the rain for any longer, it would become dangerous.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, we were deep in the forest. There were no caves or man-made shelters. We had no choice but to rely on the power of nature. Fortunately, the trees were lush and overgrown, and their branches might keep our bodies relatively dry. I looked around the area, found a remarkably big tree, and moved right under it. Of course, it didn’t block the rain completely, but the overgrown leaves did stop a lot of the rainfall.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I gently laid Horikita down. Her jersey was probably going to get dirty, but we had bigger problems now. I sat there, with Horikita’s head in my lap. If only the area were cool… But the temperature was so high, hot and humid. Horikita occasionally trembled, like she was trying to curl into a ball. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Trying to lessen her burden even a little, I held Horikita close to my chest. After some time had passed, Horikita woke up, sputtering out ragged breaths. Still in a daze, Horikita wasn’t able to understand our situation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why are you? I…?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She didn’t seem to remember what had happened. I explained the whole sequence of events. I had some doubts about whether she understood everything.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see… I remember.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s good.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I remember my mistake, so it’s probably awful.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, if she was able to crack self-deprecating jokes, then I could probably relax.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s already almost six o’clock, Horikita. You might think this will sound harsh, but you should retire. Your body is probably at its limit.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She had made it this far by pretending to be okay, but it wasn’t possible for her to carry on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t do that. We can’t afford to lose thirty points because of me… I was the one who confronted Karuizawa-san and the others about using points, right? It would make me look like such an idiot…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The penalty for poor physical condition was severe. In points alone, it was more than what Karuizawa had used herself. Horikita covered her eyes with her arm, probably to hide her tears.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not just that… The keycard was stolen from me, too. You understand what that means?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Class D will lose another fifty points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita gave a slight nod. Class D would only be left with a few points.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just leave me here and head back. If you do, I’ll be the only one absent from roll call.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you planning to do?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“By tomorrow morning, I’ll…try to return by myself, somehow. If I can deal with my poor health during roll call, then I’ll do something about retirement.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That way, we would be left with a five-point loss.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Things aren’t that easy. You’re feeling really weak now, and our teacher isn’t kind enough to let you act your way past her. It will be impossible for you to get back to camp on your own.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Still, there’s nothing else I can do… This is so Class D will have some points left.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Putting aside the keycard incident, we might retain some points for roll call and retirement. That certainly wasn’t a small number.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Go.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although Horikita was weak, I felt her indomitable will behind her words. She could bear whatever burden she placed on herself, but couldn’t seem to bear involving others. I rose, and rested her head against the tree. She wanted me to leave her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, I’ll leave. But if things continue like this, our classmates will blame you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. That’s the correct decision. Everything was my responsibility.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita praised my cold, calculating decision. She was ashamed of herself for being weak. Trembling, she forced herself to endure the cold. This was the kind of hardship that solitary people faced. The weather was still stormy, with no signs that the rains and winds would stop.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Can you really make it back alone by tomorrow morning?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah… I’ll be fine.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita. Do you really think that not retiring is the right decision?” I couldn’t help blurting those words out.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course I do. Retiring isn’t an option for me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was free to exercise her indomitable will as much as she liked, but it didn’t mean anything if she lost in the&#160;end.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey. Why do you think that we’ve been backed into such a corner?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I failed due to my negligence. That’s all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re wrong. You’re completely wrong.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita Suzune had fought as hard as she could, and had tried to get to the end of the test without making mistakes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Go… Because I think of you as my friend, please listen to my request…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After Horikita said that, she covered her mouth in surprise.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m going to make this right… As if it didn’t happen at all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, this is the wrong choice.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s fine. I can… By myself… Ugh…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita suddenly stood, but the burden was too much. She closed her eyes in pain.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Go, please…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She lost consciousness again. I gently took Horikita into my arms and shifted my position to make her a little more comfortable. Standing, I looked up at the boundless darkness and let out a sigh.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would have been easier if you’d just retired of your own free will.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The stubborn princess didn’t seem like she was going to throw in the towel. Wonderful. Yes, I thought it was wonderful. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">You were almost right. But unfortunately, Horikita, you were wrong about one thing. Right now, just for this moment, I’ll tell you.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I have never thought of you as my friend. I’ve never cared about you as a classmate. In this world, winning is everything. Your methods don’t matter. I don’t care what I have to sacrifice. As long as I have my victory in the end, I’ll be&#160;fine.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">You, Hirata—no, all other people are nothing more than tools</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I was complicit in what drove you to this. So, don’t blame yourself, Horikita. You were useful to me.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I walked down the muddy road, shining my flashlight on the path. My shoes were already covered in mud and filled with water. But I didn’t care about that. First, I needed to get an understanding of the location. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I went down the slope, I’d undoubtedly gotten further away from Class D’s base camp. But I was sure that if I turned the other way, the beach would be near. I could press on and walk through the woods for several days, relying on the map in my head.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It was close, after all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Eventually, I arrived at the beach. The ship was floating in the water, and the lights were on. It took a few minutes, but I returned to the place I’d left. Horikita had collapsed. She remained unconscious as I picked her up in my arms. Her beautiful face was splashed with mud. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I started walking toward the beach, rather than our base camp. Somehow, I had managed to make it on time. It was just around seven o’clock in the evening. The teachers’ tents had been taken down to avoid being blown away by the wind. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I went up the ramp to the pier and reached the ship’s deck. One of the teachers noticed and raced up to me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re prohibited from entering here. You’ll be disqualified.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is an emergency. She’s got a high fever and has lost consciousness. Please let her rest right away.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once I explained the situation, the teacher skipped the instructions and brought out a stretcher. I laid Horikita down.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is she okay with retiring?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Without question. However, allow me to confirm one thing. Because it isn’t eight o’clock yet, this shouldn’t have any effect on roll call, correct?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was 7:58. I was cutting it close, but we should be safe. I needed to get the teacher’s promise, though.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You are certainly right. It is quite close. However, you’re out.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand. Oh, one more thing. I’d like to return this keycard.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I took the keycard from my pocket and handed it over.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, I’ll be heading back.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t stay any longer, so I went back to the beach while the rain was still falling. With this, Class D would lose thirty points due to Horikita’s retirement, and an additional five points due to my absence during roll call.</span></p>
