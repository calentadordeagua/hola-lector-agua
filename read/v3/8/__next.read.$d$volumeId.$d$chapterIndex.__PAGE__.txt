<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 2:<br/>The Boundary Between<br/>Heaven and Hell</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">he endless summer sea.</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The infinite blue skies. The perfectly clear air. Here, in the midst of the Pacific Ocean, we didn’t feel the intense midsummer heat, and the gentle sea breeze kissed our bodies. Yes, this really was an oceanic paradise.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whoa! This is the beeeeeeesssssst!” shouted Ike Kenji, both hands raised high in the air. His voice echoed across the deck of the luxury liner.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Normally, someone would have grumbled or shouted “shut up” in response. But just for today, no one minded, instead enjoying this moment of bliss. The view from the “special seats” on the deck was exceptionally gorgeous.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This view is incredible! I’m honestly super moved right now!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A group of girls led by Karuizawa came out from the ship’s cabin. Karuizawa pointed out to the expansive ocean, wearing a radiant smile.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seriously, the scenery here is just amazing!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kushida Kikyou was also present among the group of girls. It looked like the extraordinary view had stolen her breath away. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After overcoming numerous hardships, midterms, and the final exam, we had welcomed summer vacation with open arms. The Advanced Nurturing High School had arranged for an extravagant two-week trip—a cruise on a luxury liner.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, Ken, you must be happy you didn’t get expelled. I mean, if this were a normal trip, it would’ve been impossible for us to go. Hey, how did it feel to be on the verge of expulsion, since you had the lowest score on the final? Come on, tell me. How did it feel?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though Yamauchi Haruki was insulting him, Sudou Ken was far from being in a foul mood. In fact, he howled with laughter in response, sounding more like the baying of a lone wolf than a high schooler. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“With my skills, there was nothing to worry about. Didn’t I tell you I’d prove myself, and it’d be easy?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We had faced oblivion just a short while ago, but this trip completely blew those feelings away. Perhaps the blue seas had washed away our everyday troubles.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I never dreamed that high schoolers could go on such a luxurious cruise. And it’s for two whole weeks. Two weeks! When my mom and dad hear about this, they’re gonna be so shocked they’ll wet themselves!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As Sudou had bluntly stated, this was certainly no ordinary trip. At our government-sponsored school, there was absolutely no need for us to pay for tuition or other miscellaneous expenses—which, of course, included this trip. We received the best of special treatment. The cruise liner and its facilities were of the highest possible quality. This ship was fully equipped with everything from prestigious restaurants to a theater, and even an upscale spa. On my own, this would probably have cost me about 100,000 yen, even in the off season.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Our trip, which promised the pinnacle of luxury, had finally started today. According to the schedule, we would spend our first week staying at a fancy summer lodge on a deserted island. After that, we would enjoy the cruise ship for another week.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At 5 </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">AM</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> today, the first-year students had boarded buses and departed for Tokyo Bay. The passenger ships departed from the port once the students arrived. After eating breakfast in the lounge, students were permitted to move freely throughout the entire vessel. Best of all, we were able to use any of the ship’s facilities free of charge. For those of us who’d suffered daily due to a lack of points, this ship was heaven-sent.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Suddenly, Kushida turned toward me. I could tell that something was on her mind. With the vast ocean and the endless blue sky behind her, Kushida looked even more radiant than usual. Even though I didn’t want it to, my heart started to pound. Could it be…?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Come to think of it, I wonder where Horikita-san is? Weren’t you two together?” Kushida asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently I wasn’t even allowed to indulge in a mere fantasy. Kushida had Horikita on her mind.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who knows? I’m not her keeper.” I didn’t recall seeing her after breakfast. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She probably doesn’t enjoy traveling, so maybe she’s in her room?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Probably.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Around noon, we can go onto the island’s private beach and swim around as much as we want. I can’t wait!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, the school owned a small island to the south. Our destination.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Attention, students. Please assemble on the deck. You will be able to see the island soon. This is a good time to take in some rather significant scenery.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This rather strange announcement issued from the ship’s </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">PA</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. Kushida and the others didn’t seem to mind, looking forward to what was to come. The island appeared on the horizon a few minutes after several students had gathered. Ike let out a gleeful cry.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Other students noticed, and began assembling on the deck. After a crowd had gathered, some particularly domineering boys showed up and began pushing us out of the way in order to get the best position.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, you’re in the way. Move it, you defects.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One of the boys tried to intimidate me, and shoved my shoulder. In a panic, I quickly grabbed onto the deck’s railing to keep from falling. The students laughed scornfully.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, what the hell are you guys doing?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou immediately responded in kind, trying to intimidate them right back. Kushida, looking worried, came to my side. I suppose men who require girls for backup probably look pretty pathetic.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You do understand how this school is structured, right? Class D doesn’t get any human rights. Defects like you are just that—defective—so you should submit. We’re all in Class A over here.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The students from Class D departed from the bow of the ship as if we’d been chased out. Sudou looked displeased, but managed to restrain himself. A fistfight didn’t break out, evidence that he was perhaps growing up. Or perhaps he simply understood Class D’s weak position here. Although the situation was unfair, we didn’t need any unnecessary trouble, so it was best to ignore it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, hey, you’re all here. Huh? What’s the matter?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata Yousuke, Class D’s leader, called out to me. It was the last day of the first semester. The cabin assignments for the trip were decided. I hadn’t expected to be called over to hang out with Ike and Sudou and the others; their group was already big enough as it was. Just when it seemed like I would be isolated, though, I was saved by the appearance of my hero, Hirata Man.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Hirata, how far have you gotten with Karuizawa?” Ike asked Hirata, who didn’t seem to be heading over to Karuizawa’s side. “Why don’t you try and be flirtier with her, since we’re on this long-awaited trip?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We just kind of take things at our own pace.” Hirata’s cell phone rang. “Oh, sorry, Miyake-kun looks like he’s having trouble. I’ll be going now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Fiddling with his phone, Hirata returned to the cabins. Popular people were the busiest of all.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s his deal? We’re on a trip, but he’s worried about his classmates?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Karuizawa is Karuizawa, though. I guess lately she and Hirata haven’t been very flirty with each other… Do you think that maybe they’ve broken up? If that’s the case, that really sucks. It’ll mean more rivals to get to Kushida-chan!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was certainly true that Hirata and his girlfriend seemed less close now than when they’d started dating. But they didn’t appear to have had a fight, and the situation hadn’t gotten tense. They seemed to get along well whenever I saw them talking.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve decided, Haruki. I…will confess my feelings to Kushida-chan on this trip!” Ike proclaimed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A-are you serious? If she rejects you, it’s gonna be crazy awkward, though. You gonna be okay?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is just my own selfish reasoning. Kushida-chan is really cute, right? That’s why most of the boys want to ask her out. But she’s just on a whole other level, so no one can get the courage to confess to her. That means she’s not used to being confessed to, right? I think Kushida-chan’s heart could be shaken by my declaration of love. It’s like, you know, not entirely hopeless.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. So, you’ve already made up your mind.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Normally Yamauchi would have gotten fired up and opposed Ike, but this time he didn’t. Instead, he looked out over the deck as if searching for something.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s the matter?” Ike asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, nothing really,” Yamauchi responded absent-mindedly. In the end, he never brought up the subject of Kushida.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, hey, Kushida-chan. Can I talk to you for a minute?” Ike asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm? What is it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike immediately approached Kushida, who had been looking at the sea. This was obviously a suspicious move.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, it’s like this… It’s been about four months since we met, right? So…I was wondering if it might be okay for me to call you by your first name now. I mean, it makes me feel like we’re strangers when I call you by your last name.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come to think of it, I guess you and Yamauchi have been calling each other by your first names for a while now, huh?” Kushida said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So… I can’t, huh? C-call you Kikyou-chan, I mean?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In response to Ike’s inquiry, Kushida simply smiled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course it’s okay for you to call me that. Should I call you Kanji-kun, then?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whooooaaaaa! Kikyou-chaaaaaaan!” Ike cried out and posed like he was reaching out to heaven, kind of like the guy on the poster for the movie </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Platoon</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. Kushida chuckled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“First names, huh? Hey, speaking of, I wonder what Horikita’s first name is? Huh?” Sudou asked me as if I was the one who’d know.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tomiko. Horikita Tomiko.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tomiko, huh? That’s a cute name. Just like I’d expect. It feels perfect for her.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, sorry, I was wrong. It’s Suzune.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, don’t make mistakes like that! Suzune, huh? It feels like Tomiko, but it’s 100 times better.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In the end, even if Horikita’s first name were Sadako or Sam or whatever, he probably would have felt it was perfect. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m going to call her by her first name during our summer vacation, too. Suzune. Suzune…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, it seemed the boys wanted to bridge the gap between themselves and the girls. It should be noted that none of the guys called me by my first name, and I didn’t call them by theirs.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, hey. Let me practice with you, Ayanokouji. Practice saying Suzune’s name, I mean.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Practice? What do you mean, practice? That’s not a normal thing to do.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was pointless to practice saying someone’s name unless you were saying it to the person in question. Sudou glared at me intently. He wasn’t planning to make me his imaginary Horikita, was he? It was probably because he was imagining me as someone of the opposite sex, but the look in his eyes really creeped me out. In my mind, I&#160;started breathing heavily.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Horikita, do you have a minute? There’s something I want to talk to you about…” Sudou murmured.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not Horikita.” Disgusted, I averted my eyes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You freakin’ moron! It’s practice! I don’t wanna do it, either, but I got to practice, you know? It’s like I have to practice basketball if I want to get good. In either case, I&#160;need to take my shot.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I really didn’t want to listen to this, but I didn’t have much of a choice. I had to grin and bear it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita. Isn’t it weird that we talk to each other like we’re strangers? We’ve known each other for a while now. Other people are calling each other by their first names. Isn’t it time we did, too?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wanted to hit Sudou on the head, but I tried to endure it like an adult.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Say something! Why aren’t you practicing?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you want me to say?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Answer like how Horikita would. You’ve known her for a long time, so you’d know how she’d answer, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We had only known each other for four months, so I wouldn’t necessarily know that. Even so, Sudou had asked me to play his imaginary Horikita. I clenched my fist in a somewhat threatening manner.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m one step ahead on the path to adulthood. You want me to practice this with you instead of Horikita? Feel free to practice by yourself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike jumped in to substitute for me instead. Sudou seemed kind of weird when he started talking.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita…is it okay for me to call you by your first name now?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Well, you’re not exactly a hottie, are you, Sudou-kun? And you don’t seem to have any money, so, like, you’re not my type at all, are you? Or it’s, like, sorry, sorry, but no thanks, you know?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Despite looking nothing like one, Ike was trying to play the part of a gyaru high school girl. Sudou put him in a chokehold until he writhed in agony on the deck. Those guys always seemed so energetic. I felt exhausted just looking at them. Still, they did look pretty funny. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A little while later, the crowd started getting riled up and making noise. The students’ enthusiasm increased by leaps and bounds as we sailed closer and the island became clearer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d thought that the ship would have gone directly to the island, but for some reason we passed the pier and started circling around. The island, on loan from the government, had a surface area of about 0.5 square kilometers. The highest point of the island reached 230 meters. In comparison to Japan’s total area, the island was tiny, but when seen by one hundred-odd people on a cruise ship, it looked unbelievably massive.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Eventually, the boat made a complete pass around the island. The ship continued to circle without changing speed, barely making a splash as it moved almost unnaturally fast through the water.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Such a mysterious sight! It’s so moving! Don’t you think so, Ayanokouji-kun?” Kushida gushed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“O-oh. Yeah, I guess.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I looked at Kushida, whose eyes sparkled as she looked at the deserted island, my heart started to pound. Kushida really was cute. I wanted to protect her smile, and her childlike mannerisms. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">An announcement came over the </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">PA</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> speakers. “We will disembark in thirty minutes. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, the private beach was near. Ike and the others went to change in high spirits. I started heading toward my group’s room, too. There, I put on the jersey that I used for gym class, returned to the ship’s deck, and waited until we reached the island. As the island drew closer and closer, the first-years’ enthusiasm reached its peak. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We will disembark now, beginning with the students from Class A. Cell phones are prohibited on the island. Please hand your phone to your homeroom teacher as you leave.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Following the loudspeaker’s command, the students went down the stairs in an orderly fashion.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on. Hurry up! Even though we’re wearing thin clothes, we’re all sweating!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was no place to hide from the sun on the ship’s deck. No surprise that people were complaining. The Class D students waited on standby in the heat. Horikita finally joined us. At first glance, it didn’t appear as though anything had changed, but there was a slight difference—something felt out of place. Even Horikita, normally so meticulous, was concerned about her appearance. Right now, though, her hair was disheveled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She seemed a bit cold, unconsciously rubbing her arms as we waited to disembark and set foot on the island.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What were you doing until now?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was just reading a book in my room. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">For Whom the Bell Tolls</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. You wouldn’t know it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Hey, come on now</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, I thought. That book was arguably one of Ernest Hemingway’s definitive works, an unquestioned masterpiece. I’d long been impressed by Horikita’s hobby of reading such acclaimed books. But I had to wonder what her priorities were, since she was reading while on a luxurious cruise. In such a case, I found it a little suspicious that she would shut herself off in her room to read.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She wasn’t saying anything, and it would be silly to investigate further. Best just to drop it. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m anxious about what’s coming, but since we’re prohibited from bringing personal items along there’s nothing I can really do,” she grumbled, seemingly dissatisfied. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Unusual for someone headed to the beach to say. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Disembarking took longer than I thought it would, probably because the teachers guarded the students on both sides as they got off the ship and checked their luggage.</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">2.7</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">W</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">e gathered up the branches and returned to camp. If she stayed within our sight, she probably wouldn’t cause trouble. Hirata was gone, unfortunately. That meant Yamauchi, Sakura, and I had to get the bonfire going. We wouldn’t be able to get a fire going in the dark, so we had to hurry.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Leave it to me. I’ll show you an easy way.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi produced a matchbox he’d received from Hirata, and crouched before the stacked branches. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Damn, this is pretty hard…”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He carefully lit the match and slowly brought it toward the branch. Though it seemed like the fire was desperately trying its best, it didn’t develop as desired. It ended up only smoldering a little, causing more smoke.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What the hell?! What am I doing wrong? I’m going to go ask the teacher.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi had wanted to look cool in front of Sakura, but that was over with now. He started frantically searching for Chabashira-sensei. He should have thought this through before trying it, obviously. I crouched down and jostled the branches.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura crouched next to me, looking at the burned branches with a puzzled expression.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since so few students in Class D had that kind of experience, Ike was becoming crucial to our success.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, damn it! The teacher didn’t tell me a damn thing. Whoa! Hey, how’d the bonfire get going?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi had returned, and was astonished to see such a splendid bonfire. Perhaps he felt frustrated that he hadn’t been able to show off, because he started complaining. I decided to leave the bonfire issues to Ike and Yamauchi, and left. </span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">2.8</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">O</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">nce five o’clock arrived, Kushida and her group returned. Hirata had apparently joined up with Kushida’s group. Since this was the return of our class’ central figures, nearly half the class began to gather together. They also seemed to have grapes and kiwis.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even so, I’m really thirsty… I’m hungry, too.”</span></p>

Kushida and several others realized this, and nodded in understanding. I would’ve thought that so much attention and respect would’ve given Ike a big head, but he wasn’t looking for praise from Kushida. Instead, he looked at Shinohara.</span></p>

If we added the twenty points we spent on a temporary toilet to that, as well as the cost for two tents for the guys, which was also twenty points, then we’d have 150 points. Sudou and Yamauchi both looked as though they agreed because they didn’t really have another choice. Yukimura still appeared reluctant, but seeing Ike join with Hirata made him fold.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry. Can you wait a little longer? I’m going to talk to him about you.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki pulled up a handful of grass, looking pissed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“After all, they’ll chase me out anyway. Am I wrong?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know. Hirata is an exceptionally good guy.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t imagine that Hirata would kick her out if he knew about her situation. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, I never introduced myself before. My name’s Ayanokouji.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, should I introduce myself once more then?”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata practically jumped for joy at Ike’s curt reply. Shinohara, who’d complained the most before, didn’t object. Hirata started gathering opinions about food.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it’ll be dark soon, so for now all we can do is order food. However, I ask you to think a little about tomorrow onward. There might be various foodstuffs nearby, so I wanted to explore.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean, nearby? Do you mean other than where Kushida-san and the others found the fruit?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. The river. It’d be great if we can catch and eat fish. It looks like there are quite a few freshwater fish in there. We’d be able to limit our point spending to some extent. Also, catching fish and cooking them over the bonfire sounds really tasty.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, putting aside whether or not they’d be tasty, how do you plan on catching fish?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll dive in the water. I haven’t done it before, though.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike made a swimming motion, but it probably wouldn’t be easy to catch fish by skin diving.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even though it might sound impossible to catch a fish with your bare hands, there are lots of tools,” said Hirata, pointing to an entry in the manual. “Fishing rods.” They had a variety, too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s one point for fishing rods with bait, and two points for fishing rods with lures.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It wouldn’t be difficult to recover the cost. It might even be a big win for us, if we could earn food for one or two days by spending just one point. And even if we didn’t happen to catch anything, the cost was so minimal that it couldn’t really hurt us. There were no objections.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I guess it’s decided. Let’s get ourselves a fishing rod and catch some fish! Of course, we’ll go with the cheaper one.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And so we settled on our goal of catching fish from the river and foraging for berries in the forest. If we were successful, we’d decide whether or not to purchase a set of cooking utensils for an additional five points. Also, we decided to spend another twenty points to install one shower. We expected opposition, but our health might worsen if we only used cold water. The guys were only allowed to use the shower in the middle of the night, though. All of the girls seemed to agree that they’d drink water from the river. So, with the opposition convinced, the motion passed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“By the way… That girl, Ibuki-san from Class C? I’ve seen her before.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A girl named Satou, who had finally noticed the interloper, eyed Ibuki with suspicion. Ibuki continued to quietly sit far away. Apparently there was no need for me to break the ice. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it sounds like there’s been some kind of trouble in her class…” Yamauchi, a little flustered, explained why Ibuki seemed to have been isolated from her classmates.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Your judgment was correct. We can’t just leave her.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But, Hirata-kun… Couldn’t she be a spy? I mean, if she can spot the leader…” Yamauchi, who'd only just realized this, held his head in his hands.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, that’s right. I suppose it’s possible. I’ll check on it. Is that okay, Yamauchi-kun? Ayanokouji-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata headed over to Ibuki. Had he excluded Sakura because of his pretty-boy thoughtfulness? Sakura seemed relieved to not be noticed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you have a moment, Ibuki-san? I wanted to talk to you,” Hirata said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m probably just getting in the way. You’ve already taken good care of me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She quickly stood, as if she wanted to run. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait a minute. I wanted to ask what happened. I want to help.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She stopped at Hirata’s words. After seeing her swollen face, Hirata had likely surmised that the issue wasn’t trivial.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nothing will change if I stay. I don’t want to waste time just sitting around.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is a test, so of course some students will doubt you. However, you’re hurt. I don’t want to chase you away if you can’t go back to your own class. I think that’s why Yamauchi-kun brought you here. So, tell me about your situation.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This isn’t something I can just talk about. Besides, I’ve heard all your plans. You’d hate it if more of your strategies were leaked, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki started walking away. Hirata stopped her in her tracks. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you really were a spy, you wouldn’t want to be chased out, would you? Am I wrong?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Enough. I’m just looking for a place to sleep.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I expected, she wasn’t going to return to Class C. The sun was setting, and it’d be night soon. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s crazy for a girl to sleep alone in the woods.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if it’s crazy, I don’t have a choice. Helping me won’t gain you anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This has nothing to do with losses or gains. We can’t just leave someone in trouble. We all think so.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her expression cleared, and she turned toward us without hesitation. Something like that was designed to melt even the toughest exterior. Ibuki seemed to believe Hirata, and it gave her confidence.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I had a fight with a boy in my class. He hit me and chased me away. That’s all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s horrible. Raising a hand against a girl, I mean.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I hadn’t expected it, either. I’d thought for sure she’d been in a fight with another girl.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I won’t say anything more on the subject. I didn’t think you’d take me in and give me shelter, anyway. See&#160;you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait. I understand that you’re really in trouble. Please give me a little time. If you can do that, I’ll tell the other students about your situation and see if we can find a place for you. Ayanokouji-kun, can you watch Ibuki-san? I’m going to go talk to everyone.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata left us and returned to the group. I wondered if Hirata had left me with her because he trusted me, or at least trusted me more than Yamauchi. I was a little curious.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He’s seriously a good-natured guy, isn’t he?” Ibuki asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think everyone here is, more or less. Are there not people like that in your class?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not at all… There isn’t really anyone in Class C like that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki sat down, bunched her knees against her chest, and lowered her head. Thanks to Hirata’s persuasiveness, Class D agreed to look after Ibuki. Though some students were strongly opposed, every time Class C held roll call, they’d hemorrhage points. Once everyone saw it as an opportunity, they were finally convinced. Hirata’s intentions had been pure, but the same wasn’t true for others. The incentive of potential profit motivated them to take the chance.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, maintaining exclusive possession of this place was an extremely delicate issue. We explained to Ibuki, and she promised not to wander near the device. If anyone realized that Horikita was the leader, the damage we’d suffer would be great. After that, we decided to purchase the necessary food and water sets for tonight, along with the guys’ tents. Thanks to Hirata and Ike, the tents were set up smoothly. Just before the sun set, we finished all of our preparations, and the students began eating their meals.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Ibuki-san. Eat this.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kushida approached Ibuki, who’d been quietly sitting some distance away by herself. Kushida offered her one of the nutritious meal seats and a bottle of water.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What? Why are you giving me this?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, you’re probably hungry, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Food is provided based on class numbers. There shouldn’t be any spare sets left.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. But don’t worry, we decided to share everything with our group.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Further away, the four other people from Kushida’s group waved and smiled at Ibuki. In other words, four people had shared three portions of food and water, and the leftover portion went to Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you guys stupid? You’re all being way too nice.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t be shy. Eat up. Let’s talk later, okay? I’ll be waiting in the tent.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that, Kushida returned to her group. It had seemed easy to help a girl from another class until we had to reduce our own food portions. Then it wasn’t so simple. But Kushida, who wished for everyone’s happiness, had charity to spare.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, when you look at them like this, the girls’ groups are kind of remarkable.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi, in the middle of eating, pointed to each group individually.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s the Empress Team, led by Karuizawa. Then there’s Kushida-chan’s Friendship Team and Shinohara’s Arrogant Team. Then you have Horikita and Sakura, who are by themselves.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">All of the guys huddled relatively close together as they ate, but the girls sat in their individual teams. There was a clear wall between them, like they were groups from other classes. Maybe Kushida’s team was the most neutral of all of them, or just held a lot of influence?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Poor Sakura, all alone. I wonder if I should eat with her,” Yamauchi said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re probably better off giving up, don’t you think? You’d just scare her.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Damn. I want to get to know her better, but she’s way too introverted…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In addition to being shy, Sakura probably found it difficult to deal with pushy types like Yamauchi. Despite being warned, Yamauchi seemed impatient to go over to her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What the hell, Haruki? That’s no fair, stalking a lone beautiful woman like that. Come on, let me join in!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike, seeing Yamauchi’s glances, misunderstood and approached him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have to say, Sakura’s breasts are really something else. You don’t often see breasts that big on a first-year high school student. Her clothes are just bulging. She’s way too sexy. Her boobs alone make her even more attractive than Kikyou-chan.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike stared intently at Sakura’s breasts, like he wanted to devour them. Yamauchi blocked Ike’s line of sight.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, what the hell, dude?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t just look at Sakura like a pervert. Besides, you’re aiming for Kushida-san, aren’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, yeah. But it’s all right, isn’t it? I mean, an idol belongs to everyone, right? Haruki, could you be… Ooh, are you Sakura’s—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-It’s not like that. Come on, let’s eat.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently Yamauchi wanted to hide the fact that he was changing targets and going after Sakura. Besides, it was night, with nothing left to do. It was only natural that the flow of the conversation would lead to the opposite sex. I noticed Hirata nearby, distributing food.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come to think of it, where’s Kouenji-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Everyone had gathered, but it looked like Kouenji was absent.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, Kouenji complained about being in poor physical condition and returned to the ship. Of course, that means that you’ve had thirty points deducted. Those are the rules, so there’s nothing to be done. Kouenji has retired, and he’s obligated to stay onboard the ship for one week for medical treatment,” said Chabashira-sensei.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whaaaaat?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Screams sounded throughout the night.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, you’ve gotta be kidding me! Kouenji, you jerk! What the hell were you thinking?!” The normally silent Yukimura shouted and kicked the ground. I knew Kouenji was a free spirit, but I never imagined he would just up and retire. Maybe he didn’t care about reaching Class A. If it made his life easier, losing thirty points didn’t matter at all.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Goddamn it! We lost thirty points! This sucks!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Both the boys and girls were furious over Kouenji’s actions, but could do nothing about it. Kouenji’s loud, haughty laughter reverberated in our minds.</span></p>
