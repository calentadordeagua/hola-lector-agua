<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 3:<br/>Rivals on the Move</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 3.00em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">woke up much earlier that morning than I’d anticipated. The heat and humidity had made me toss and turn in my sleep. It finally roused me, and I wasn’t very well rested. My bed felt warm, and I remembered that we’d kept the lamp on throughout the whole night. Our tent stank like sweat. Fortunately, we’d formed the tent with a mesh material because it allowed for the night breeze to blow through. But once the night had ended, the temperature rose significantly. I carefully slipped out of the tent so as to not wake anyone else, and approached our mountain of piled luggage. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We’d kept the luggage outside in order to make the tents as spacious as possible. After looking around to make sure I was alone, I found a singularly colored piece of luggage. It was Ibuki’s bag. Since her bag was different, it was easy to spot. I grabbed it without hesitation and checked inside. If someone saw me doing this, I’d be instantly labeled a pervert. Inside, I found the same kinds of things that everyone else had, like towels, a change of clothes, underwear, etc. However…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A digital camera, huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That explained the dull sound I’d heard yesterday when she dropped the bag against a tree. This item wasn’t appropriate for a deserted island. On the bottom of the camera, I found a rental sticker. Why did Ibuki have this? I considered the possible reasons, trying to think from her perspective. After conjuring up an image of her in my head, several possibilities suggested themselves. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I checked the camera’s power source. It had no data, and no sign that it’d been used. After I finished my inspection, I returned the item to the bag, and went back to the tent.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good morning, Ayanokouji-kun. Heading to the bathroom?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata was awake. He turned around as he greeted me. Maybe he noticed that I was sweatier than usual.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah. Did I wake you, by any chance?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no. I can’t exactly sleep soundly in this environment. Ouch, ouch… Ah, my back hurts. Well, I guess that’s only natural without a proper mattress.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It certainly wasn’t easy to sleep when we were so crowded together, and without real beds to boot. But somehow, everyone else was still asleep. They were probably tired out from yesterday’s activity.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you include Kouenji-kun’s penalty, we spent almost 100 points yesterday. I told everyone that in the worst possible outcome we’d be left with 120 points, but now I’m not even sure about that. I guess the anxiety woke me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata took out the manual to confirm his fears. Kouenji’s retirement had been a pretty serious blow. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is tough, even for the class peacemaker.” I&#160;couldn’t possibly fathom carrying this kind of responsibility. I peeked at the manual, and Hirata adjusted his position so that I could comfortably look at it. I was grateful for such small acts of consideration.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m only doing this because I like it. If my hard work makes everyone in class happy, then I’m satisfied. But it’s surprisingly difficult. How many points we have after this special test will have a huge impact on our lives. I think it’s wrong to freak everybody out.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Make everyone in class happy, huh? If such a thing were possible, it would be amazing. But it was probably close to impossible. The school’s system dictated as much.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, if we have students who want to aim for Class A, and students who want to stay in Class D, what do you do?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I knew that was a meaningless question, it accidentally came out sounding rather unkind. I guess I wanted Hirata’s opinion, since he was basically just a mass of good intentions.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s hard to answer. Aiming for the upper classes means forcing everyone… I’m sorry, I need to think on it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered how often he’d thought about it. Hirata smiled faintly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So Ayanokouji-kun, do you want to get into Class A? Or are you fine as long as life at school is fun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess if I had to pick, school life’s my priority. Besides, I don’t think it’s possible to reach Class A.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. I also don’t think it’s simple. Even if our class came together as one and aimed for Class A… I just think our first month’s losses did too much damage.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata was probably thinking about all the other students’ limits. If Class A didn’t fall in the rankings, making up the difference wouldn’t be easy. Even if Class D managed to live conservatively during this exam, we could only gain 100 to 150 points. Right now, even overtaking Class C seemed like a pipe dream.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t be impatient. First of all, Class D needs to come together and make it through this test. After that, we can focus on the next goal.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Most of us had decided to let Hirata take the lead. If we worked hard in the short term, we’d earn class points. Right now, ignoring the severe chasm between us and the other classes wasn’t a bad idea. Hirata politely excused himself and quietly headed to the toilet. I stretched out in the space left behind by his absence.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At the very least, Class A had the cave. It was likely that Classes B and C also had spots somewhere. Even though we held the river, that alone might not be enough. Once everyone was asleep, I neatly cut out one of the manual’s five blank pages. Then I took a ballpoint pen. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After I replicated a simple map of the island, I folded it into a small square and put it in my pocket. Soon after, Hirata peeked in the tent’s entrance.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Want to come wash your face together with me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I accepted his offer. The tent’s temperature was rising as the sun climbed higher in the sky. We took towels from our vinyl-wrapped luggage and headed to the river. Hirata put the manual inside his pack. The jingle of plastic accessories banging together sounded from Hirata’s bag.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Gifts or something from Karuizawa?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Spot on. How’d you guess?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Who else would give him little heart-bedazzled presents? As we headed to the river, we came across an unexpected person.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you doing here?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A student from Class B, Kanzaki, looked our way. Some other boys I didn’t know were with him, but they were probably Class B students, too. They appeared surprised, as if they hadn’t expected us at such an early hour, but quickly regained their composure. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“First day’s over, so I wondered how you were doing. I&#160;thought I’d check on you. Your location’s great.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He seemed legitimately impressed by our base camp. He didn’t appear to have a hidden agenda.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re Kanzaki-kun, from Class B, aren’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata seemed to remember him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did I surprise you? I’m sorry, I’m not here to cause trouble.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that apology, Kanzaki walked away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kanzaki. Where’s Class B camping?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Maybe he wouldn’t tell us, but I thought I’d try asking. Kanzaki answered without hesitation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There are large broken trees along the path from here to the beach. If you enter the forest to the southwest and walk a bit, you can find our campsite. You shouldn’t get lost if you enter by those large trees. If you need something, go ahead and tell her you can come by.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that, Kanzaki left. Hirata gave me a strange look. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m guessing he’s your friend, hmm? What’d he mean by ‘go ahead and tell her’?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm, I wonder.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki, Ichinose, and Horikita had recently worked together in a false accusation case. He probably thought they were still on good terms.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder if they came here to do some reconnaissance, see how we’ve spent our points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Doubtless that was one reason, after seeing Kanzaki’s slightly apologetic expression. You could estimate the number of points spent just by the number of toilets, showers, and tents. However, that was probably not the only thing Kanzaki and the others wanted to know. They must’ve wanted to discover our class leader. After all, the rights of exclusive spot possession expired every eight hours.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Perhaps they’d calculated the renewal time and hoped to see the spot updated. However, we’d planned for that. That’s why we postponed the second update yesterday, so the rights of ownership were adjusted to expire right after eight o’clock. That way, it was possible to use the big crowds from roll call as a kind of camouflage for the update. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata seemed to be more anxious. He muttered while drying off.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder if our strategy’s wrong. Even if we can’t beat the other classes, I thought it’d be good for us to unify on this test. That’s the real reason I don’t want them to discover our leader.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">His hair glistened with water. A splendidly handsome man faced such constant trouble. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry so much. You should relax a little.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you. Coming from you, that honestly makes me really happy.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After I washed my face, I scooped up some water for a drink. Even though the forest was insanely hot, the river water was cool and delicious. The water here was groundwater that flowed into the river as spring water, making it naturally resistant to warming or cooling. Since it came from upstream, the water’s temperature was resistant to heating. We’d been lucky to secure this place as our base.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“First, I think we need to adjust our sleeping arrangements. Since the ground’s so hard here, this week will be tough without proper back support. When everyone wakes up, I’m going to take a poll. We have to cooperate and do our best.”</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">3.1</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">fter morning roll call, we began to explore. Hirata provided roles to the more reliable students, starting his plan to save us points. Meanwhile, the less helpful students and the more independently-minded like Horikita and I did as we pleased.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What the hell, you guys?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ike’s angry voice boomed throughout the campsite. I looked in his direction, and saw two male students wearing wide, smug grins. A pained expression flashed across Ibuki’s face for a moment, but then she quickly ducked behind the tent to hide herself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Komiya and Kondou, huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Like Ibuki, I recognized those guys. They were from Class C. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, you Class D rejects are really living frugal, eh? I guess that’s what I’d expect from a class of defectives.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They were stuffing their faces with potato chips and drinking bottles of soda. Not water. Soda.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Looks like you’re living the high life, Class C.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you know Ryuuen?” Ibuki asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He’s a Class C student. I’ve heard various rumors about him. He’s pretty crazy, I hear.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not just ‘pretty’ crazy. Everything that guy </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">does</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> is crazy.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki sounded irritated, as if she were discussing a family enemy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Those two are Ryuuen’s friends. I’d say they’re more like his underlings, though.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I recalled the time those two had fought with Sudou. It’d seemed like they were acting on orders from Ryuuen, rather than just looking for a fight.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What did you even eat for breakfast? Grass? Or maybe bugs? Here, you can have some snacks.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They took a bag of potato chips and tossed it near Ike’s feet as he drew nearer. Though it was obvious that they were trying to start something, we couldn’t help getting irritated.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They didn’t leave, but continued snacking as if trying to harass us. Ike snapped at them, but they didn’t seem to care. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t think they were looking for me,” Ibuki said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. Guess they wanted to harass us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Their bizarre visit aside, we’d obtained valuable information about Class C: they had luxury items, snacks and soda and so on. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“They said something about sharing their luxuries. Any idea what they were talking about?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder if things are heading toward that worst-case scenario I imagined…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki said nothing more, but headed toward a tree on the camp’s periphery. The worst-case scenario she’d imagined, huh? Telling Horikita about this seemed like a good idea.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you hear those voices earlier?” I asked.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m a little worried. I want to check out the situation. Care to come?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s rather unusual to see you taking initiative. Are you feeling well?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d have loved to throw those words back in her face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I’m free for a week. I don’t have anything to do today, so I’ve just been killing time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t want to move too much. Since I’m the leader, if I stand out too much, we’ll be in a bad spot.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Since you've been appropriately designated the leader, that’s a definite risk.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if someone didn’t know a leader’s identity for certain, they might guess correctly if they noticed suspicious behavior. The more conspicuous a person, the more attention they would draw.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand how you feel, but even if you stay in here, the situation probably won’t change, right? No matter what you do, you’ll be a target.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At any rate, the penalty for a wrong guess was fifty points. When we’d appointed someone as leader, we’d taken a gamble, and precautions were necessary.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re right. I suppose I can’t say what’s correct. Fine. I’m rather concerned about the state of the other classes. Let’s go.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita and I set out for the beach, where Class C awaited. Her heavy footsteps seemed to belie her casual indifference.</span></p>

They had installed temporary toilets and shower rooms. But they also had a tarp to protect against sunlight, a barbecue, chairs, and a parasol. They had snacks and drinks. Everything required for a good, relaxing time was here. We smelled the smoke of cooking meat, and heard laughter. Jet skis whizzed past the shoreline. Students were enjoying themselves in the ocean, screaming with joy. Based on a rough calculation, they’d likely spent 150 points or more.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s check things out. I wonder what Class C is thinking?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We came out from the bushes and walked to the beach. One of the male students noticed us, and called out to another male student nearby. We couldn’t see his face too well, since he was leaning over in his chair. One of the boys rushed over to us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, Ryuuen-san has requested your presence,” he said. Judging from the timidity of his voice, he was either frightened or naturally listless.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He rules his classmates like a king. It’s like a royal welcome. What should we do?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s up to you, Horikita.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Fine. I’m interested in learning his intentions. Let’s go.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We accompanied the boy. As we approached the ocean, the delicious smell of cooked meat wafted by our noses.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is absolutely outrageous.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Our class didn’t seem to know how to have a vacation. We approached the man in command of this hedonistic paradise.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">thought </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">someone was sniffing around. It’s you, huh? What business do you have with me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You seem to be doing well for yourself. This looks like quite the extravagant party.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen, tanned and clad in his swimwear, laid back in his chair. He flashed his white teeth at us.</span></p>

<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol3_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_6" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v3/OEBPS/Images/ClassroomElite-Vol3_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_6.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen didn’t seem happy being told about his apparent ineptitude. Actually, he looked disappointed. “I’m shocked. Does that mean you’re offering help even to an enemy like me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If the person on top is incompetent, those below him will suffer. This is pitiful,” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen simply smiled, grabbing the bottle of water placed next to the radio.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How many points did you use? To be able to enjoy this level of entertainment, I mean.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm. Well, I didn’t make a precise calculation,” Ryuuen responded guilelessly. “Tch. Already getting warm. Hey, Ishizaki. Bring me some cold water, right away.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen poured out his remaining water on the sand, almost in provocation. Ishizaki, who’d been playing volleyball nearly, panicked and rushed to get Ryuuen another water. A mountain of cardboard boxes were piled up inside the tent, likely filled with food and water. Sakazaki peered into a cooler beside the boxes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“As you can see, we’re enjoying our summer vacation. We’re not your enemies. Do you understand?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita, finding his behavior incomprehensible, pressed her fingers against her forehead and wrinkled her brows as if she had a headache.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’re trying to </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">warn</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> you. You’re an idiot.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Which one of us is the idiot? Me? You?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen would accept no insults, and threw them back at Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You want to try surviving on this deserted island in this shitty heat? Don’t joke. Class D, the lowest of all, has to put up with starvation, heat, and futility just to save a measly 100 or 200 class points. It makes me laugh.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki ran over, dripping with sweat as he brought the water. He handed a cold bottle of water to Ryuuen. However, Ryuuen threw it back at Ishizaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I said to bring me </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">cold</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> water. This water’s warm.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I… B-but…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen’s pupils were just like those of a snake. Ishizaki’s body stiffened. He picked up the bottle and ran back toward the tent.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This test is about perseverance, ingenuity, and cooperation. It would likely have been impossible for you from the start. You can’t even establish a satisfactory plan.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They couldn’t possibly hold on for a week after having spent points so lavishly. Eventually, their lives would become hell. The tarps, parasols, chairs and other things would become obstacles.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Cooperation? Don’t make me laugh. People betray each other with ease. People lie. Relationships built on trust just aren’t viable. You can only trust yourself. If you’ve finished your reconnaissance, leave. But if you wish, we’d welcome you here. You’re free to enjoy yourself, whether it’s to eat meat or play on jet skis. Or perhaps you would prefer to have a different kind of fun with me? I&#160;can prepare a tent for personal use.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not the kind of answer I’d expect to hear from someone who declared war on us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I absolutely loathe hard work. Patience? Saving? You must be joking.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki returned once again, and handed over another bottle of water. Ryuuen opened the cap and chugged.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is the way I do things. No more, no less.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Right. Well then, do as you please. It’s convenient for us, anyway.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita had changed her mind. Class C wasn’t going to be our enemy here, so they weren’t a problem. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Working up a sweat in order to evaluate other classes is such a pain.” Horikita turned on her heel to walk away, but paused. “There is one more piece of business. You know Ibuki, of course?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. She’s a member of our class. What about her?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Her face is swollen. Who did that to her?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While Horikita was nearly convinced he was the culprit, she purposefully asked in a roundabout way. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah. She ran out of here rather suddenly. She went looking for help from another class in the end? Pathetic girl.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen snorted in disgust, then laid back in his chair.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There are helpless idiots in this world. A ruler doesn’t need subordinates who disobey orders. We determined that I would use our class points to my liking. That’s the fact of the matter. Besides, it’s pointless to raise the banner of revolution against the ruling class.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In other words, Ibuki-san clashed with you when you wanted to spend points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, you could say that. That’s why she got a light punishment.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He made a gesture like slapping someone’s cheek. Ryuuen had indeed hit her. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Another boy defied me as well, so I had him driven out. I heard he didn’t die, so he’s probably off somewhere eating grass and insects to survive.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t imagine that was something you could say about a friend. But now I fully understood. Even if Ibuki were absent during roll call, Class C wouldn’t care. That’s why Ryuuen didn’t care about his classmates or trying to find them.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You…used up all your points on the first day, didn’t you?” Horikita asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if you used all of the 300 points you were given, you'd suffer no penalties. There were no downsides.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, as you said. I used all of our points. Ibuki said she didn't care about points, anyhow. Do you understand how free that makes her?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can't believe you'd use the fact that you have zero points against her.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">His strategy was to be at zero points to negate negative elements. It was certainly unexpected, but it came at a high price. With no points, Class C would have the lowest rank. Even if they managed to guess every other class leader’s identity, they could only achieve a maximum of 150 points.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If Ibuki is with you, you’re better off chasing her away. If you shower her with your awkward sympathies, you’ll have one extra person to prepare water, food, and bedding for. Anyway, if you can’t deal with it, she can return here. If she grovels on the ground, I’ll forgive her. I’ve a tolerant heart.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He’d forgive her defiance if she returned to being under his control. He seemed pretty sure that she would. It would be difficult for Ibuki to live alone on a deserted island for one week.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What short-sighted thinking. You’re happy right now using your points, but what are you planning to do after the party’s over?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ha ha ha. What should I do, I wonder? Well, I suppose that plain, ordinary people can only engage in plain, ordinary thinking. You’re desperate to protect the points you were given. Looking around for the leaders, desperately holding spots, working up a sweat running through the forest. Absolutely worthless.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though we’d confronted him with facts, Ryuuen laughed and showed no sign of panic. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Fine. Let’s head back, Ayanokouji-kun. If we stay here any longer, I’m only going to start to feel ill.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“See you later, Suzune.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know where exactly you found that out, but do not call me by my first name so casually.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen had clearly done some investigating.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I rather like forceful women. I’ll make you submit to me eventually. When that time comes, it will be the ultimate pleasure.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When he said that, Ryuuen touched his crotch under his bathing suit, clearly to provoke Horikita. Horikita, eyes full of contempt, turned her back and walked away. As I started running after her, I stopped to look at a passenger boat anchored by the pier. I saw students swimming in the ocean, playing volleyball and beach flags on the shore, celebrating with barbecues. Also, I saw the tent where they were stockpiling food.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen seemed content to mock the school rules, apparently.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Class C is irrelevant. Their self-destruction will help&#160;us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems that way. They’ve used up all their points, anyway.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if they had some saved, it could only be a few dozen at most. The two students’ absence at roll call would swallow those.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t wait to see what they’ll do once trouble hits.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, Class C probably won’t face any trouble during this test.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why wouldn’t they? How can they endure this test without any points?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That was Ryuuen’s original goal. We were given 300 points as funds to enjoy our vacation for one week, which is not at all impossible. No matter how much we economize on our food, we have to give up on luxury items. The school made these rules accordingly.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita nodded.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, we should try to save where we can,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. But Ryuuen’s different. He can’t see past the end of his nose, much less a week.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He can’t see past what now?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Suppose the test ended today. What then? Do you think this trip would transform into a perfect vacation?”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s simple. He’d just do what Kouenji did.”</span></p>

If everyone did that, they could return to the passenger boat and go about their lives. That’s what they meant by fully enjoying their summer vacation: without hardship.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Granted, the school might turn us away if we feigned sickness. 300 points were enough to use freely for a one-night, two-day vacation. But sooner or later, the bill would arrive.</span></p>

Since he knew he’d be losing that many points every day no matter what, he came up with a drastic strategy.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I suppose that was true. It was certainly true that we couldn’t predict the actions of Ryuuen, whose plans were likely geared to a very unusual purpose, if his words earlier were true. Any rational person would regard Ryuuen’s bizarre schemes with some anxiety. After we passed the beach, I turned back and scanned the shore once more.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A zero-point strategy, huh? I see. That’s really interesting.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If we could simply shut down dissenting views from our classmates, it’d be a rather interesting method. After all, this test wasn’t just about saving points within our own group. We had to strategize if we wanted to win.</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">3.3</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">n order to make effective use of the extra time on our hands, we decided to check out how Classes A and B were doing. We went deeper into the forest, past the roots of the big, broken tree, just as Kanzaki had instructed. When I thought on it now, though, the tree looked as though the school had broken it intentionally, to use as a landmark. It hinted that there was a spot just further ahead. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The moment we set foot in the deep forest, I noticed a slight change. Quite a few students’ tracks stood out on this path, which made it easier to walk. If we simply followed the path, we’d probably arrive at Class B’s campsite. That might be why Kanzaki hadn’t provided a detailed explanation. So far, of all the ordeals on this island, the blood-sucking mosquitoes that went for our arms and legs were the most irritating. Soon, we arrived at Class B’s base camp.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I suppose it’s just what we’d expect from Class B…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Their way of living was entirely different from ours. Their class had made practical use of their spot, with many trees surrounding a well. They didn’t have enough space to spread out three or four tents, so they’d made good use of the space by putting up hammocks. Despite starting in much the same way, our class had chosen completely different items. I was rather curious about some unfamiliar equipment near the well, but what surprised me the most was the atmosphere.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Horikita-san? And Ayanokouji-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Someone called to us, almost as if she’d sensed the arrival of sudden visitors. Ichinose was trying to tie a string around a tree to put up a hammock. She wore a jersey, which really suited her, and appeared lively. Kanzaki sat a little further away from her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Your class seems to be functioning well, despite obstacles.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ha ha, yeah. It was really difficult at first! But we tried a bunch of different things, and it worked out. Though the list of chores just keeps on increasing. There’s still a ton of work left,” said Ichinose with a big smile.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry if we’re getting in the way.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, I’m sorry. That probably sounded like I was trying to drive you guys away. I think it’s all right if you hang around for a while. You probably came here to ask me something, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose welcomed us without any hint of protest. She invited us to sit on the hammock, but Horikita declined the offer, so Ichinose sat instead.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol3_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_7" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v3/OEBPS/Images/ClassroomElite-Vol3_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_7.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was wondering if it might behoove us to tentatively enter into a cooperative relationship, like last time,” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think so, too.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, how many points have you used so far? What did you purchase? Also, if you could tell us the value of your tools that would really help. We would disclose information in return, of course.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered if Kanzaki could have deduced that information about our class just from this morning. Ichinose, with a grin, took out a manual from a bag near her feet. She showed us a white sheet of paper, which itemized what they’d purchased, and read aloud.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hammocks. Cookware. Small tent, lantern, and temporary toilet. Fishing rod, a water shower… If we combine those items with the food we purchased, the total comes to exactly seventy points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Excluding the fact that Kouenji had retired, we’d applied our points with the same rigor as Class B.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s a water shower? I’m a little curious about that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">From the name, I deduced it had something to do with a bath, but since it was five points cheaper than the temporary shower we decided it probably wasn’t as effective and passed. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, let’s go over the situation bit by bit, shall we? Since there are various places in the woods where we can find fruits and vegetables, we can compensate for deficiencies in points by foraging for food. We can also head out to the sea and fish. That’s what I was thinking we’d do for food. We’re not worried about water, since we have&#160;a&#160;well.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Had Class B just naturally obtained those things, like when Kushida and her group found all that fruit? Since she’d said the word “vegetables”, they probably had better results than Class D. Ichinose took us to the well, and showed us the pulley system used to bring up a bucket of water.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At first we were worried whether the water was safe to drink, but when we looked around at the surrounding environment and the cultivated food items, we determined that the well must have been all right. To be on the safe side, I tried drinking the water yesterday. I waited for some time, but never got an upset stomach. Starting this morning, we had everyone use the well to get water.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They hadn’t jumped right in and used the well from the beginning. They’d started using it only after properly checking it out. Most people would rather drink from something convenient that could save points.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Also, there’s enough natural water that we can use it for showers. That’s what a water shower is.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She indicated some kind of large apparatus placed next to the well. That explained it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“After we put water in the tank here, we get hot water in only a few seconds. It’s really convenient. We use a gas can for the heat source. When we run out, I plan to request another.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita calmly listened to Ichinose’s explanation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you already know about them? Water showers, I&#160;mean,” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No. This is the first I’ve heard about or used them. The school’s rules are rather scary, aren’t they? There weren’t any details in the manual, and we can’t ask the teacher, either. Thankfully we have some kids who are familiar with the outdoors in our class.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Next to the water shower was a simple toilet set up with a one-touch tent. Nothing seemed to be inside.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We set up this temporary toilet instead of getting a shower room. We got it so that the people who dislike being seen by others when they shower could have privacy. The fabric is also waterproof.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So that’s why it was empty. I was convinced, even though the ground inside of the tent looked wet… </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you don’t struggle with sleeping on the hard ground?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, yeah. At first, I wondered what we were going to do, but then we took appropriate measures. Want a look?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The grass crunched underfoot as Ichinose made her way to the tent. After giving a heads-up to the girls inside, Ichinose lifted the bottom flap. Underneath the tent was a thick bunch of vinyl sheets, which looked to be about two centimeters thick.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When we paid for the temporary toilet, we were told we had access to an unlimited number of vinyl sheets. So I might have asked for too many, but we received a large number of them. Of course, I don’t want to waste resources, so I plan to put any unused vinyl sheets into one sheet, and return them in the end.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“By the way, what precautions have you taken against the heat? For some reason, it feels cooler around here…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder if it’s because we sprinkle water around. We’ve sprinkled water near our beds, because they’re close to the well. We put some water in the plastic bottles that people drank from, and then everyone carries them around so they can sprinkle water efficiently. The water soaks into the soil quite easily, and since it takes some time for it to evaporate, the effect lingers and removes the heat.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose and her class weren’t just relying on tools; they were making good use of their knowledge to improve the campsite. After receiving this information, Horikita carefully explained our situation. She left nothing out in the spirit of fairness.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see… Having someone retire sounds like it really hurt you guys.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. There’s a lot to make us uneasy right now, but we have to try and make it through.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Well, can we continue our cooperative relationship? I thought it would be a good idea to ignore the rule about trying to find out the leader’s identity. What do you think?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was thinking that we should talk about that, too. If we didn’t have to be on guard with even one class, we’d really appreciate it. As long as you don’t mind, Ichinose-san, I’d like you to accept the proposal.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course I’m okay with that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After we reaffirmed our mutual information exchange and resolved to stay in a cooperative relationship, Horikita sighed in deep admiration while looking around. There was a feeling of true solidarity here, with no disorder at all. Each individual student carried out his or her role. Additionally, everyone seemed to be fulfilling their duties happily. Normally, you’d find someone who hated their job or trying to skip out on it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This class has fallen in line far better than I could have imagined. I suppose that’s because you’re the one taking command, right?” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. For the time being, anyway.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose had managed to unify her class both in and out of the school.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is there anyone in Class D who can bring everyone together? Is that you, Horikita-san?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No. We do have a boy named Hirata, though. Everyone in class clusters around him.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah. From the soccer club! I know him, I know him! He’s really popular among the girls.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita seemed disinterested in talking about Hirata, so she changed the topic.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ichinose-san. I’m terribly sorry to keep asking you questions, but we want to confirm Class A’s status. Can you tell us anything that will help us capture their base camp? Even something you know about the area might really help.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, if you’re okay with something that </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">might </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">be useful, I can tell you something about their place. However, obtaining information is going to be tough.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just like I’d expected from Class B…or rather, what I’d expected from Ichinose. She’d already done her research on Class A. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just after you cut through this area, there’s an opening. Turn right and go straight until you see a cave. That’s probably where the Class A base camp is. I went there myself to investigate, but I don’t know for sure. It’s because they’re so thoroughly defensive…or rather, secretive.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Secretive? What kind of measures has Class A taken?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Honestly, seeing is believing. If you take a look yourselves, you’ll understand right away. Since you two are going to check on Class A, does that mean you already understand Class C’s situation?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. We just went there earlier. They’re doing some unbelievably stupid things.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“They seemingly have no intention of taking this test seriously. There are five days remaining, and they’ll run out of points well before the test is over. I can’t imagine they’ll be able to change their situation even if they enter into ‘point-saver mode’ right away. They’re not even looking for a spot. I can’t even begin to understand them.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose didn’t seem able to come up with the answer, either.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You can’t use any sly tricks in this test. Ryuuen has most definitely spent almost all of their points. They might be having fun right now, but they’re going to regret it later.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita deliberately didn’t tell Ichinose about the potential withdrawal plan I’d discussed earlier. I didn’t think she was hiding it; rather, Horikita had probably determined that Ichinose would come to the same conclusion herself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Pardon me, Ichinose-san? I’m sorry to interrupt. Do you know where Nakanishi-kun is?” asked a male student in a rather reserved voice.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think Nakanishi-kun headed down to the shore. Why do you ask?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I thought I’d offer to help. Is that unnecessary?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no, not at all. I’m really happy you feel that way, Kaneda-kun. Can you head over and follow Chihiro-chan’s group? If you tell them I told you to do so, it’ll be&#160;okay.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. Thank you very much!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita looked a little perplexed after watching that short exchange.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He sounded incredibly formal for a classmate, didn’t he?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, he’s—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A Class C student?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I spoke before Ichinose could finish answering. She confirmed with a nod.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you know him? It looks like he had some kind of dispute with Class C. He said he’d get by on his own, but I couldn’t just leave him. I haven’t asked him about his situation yet.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One male student had been estranged from Class C because he’d supposedly resisted Ryuuen. It looked as though Class B had taken him in. Perhaps he felt ashamed about his situation, hence his offer to cooperate?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We also picked up a student yesterday. Another student who escaped from Class C.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita filled Ichinose in on the details about her meeting with Ryuuen. She told Ichinose about Ibuki, one of the rebel students, who’d defied Ryuuen for just doing whatever he pleased. She also explained that Ibuki had been hit. After hearing that, Ichinose’s eyes hardened, as if strengthening her resolve to protect her class.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think it’s about time to get going, Ayanokouji-kun. We’ll be a bother to Class B if we linger too long.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We all said farewell, and Horikita and I left Class B’s camp.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In general, I think we’re all on the same page, but they’re further ahead. I can’t deny it,” said Horikita, after we’d left and there weren’t any other people around. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her words sounded like a declaration of defeat. My impression was the same as hers. There was a big difference between D and B, and not just in points.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I guess there’s nothing we can do about it. Class B just has those special qualities that Class D lacks.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s their teamwork, right? Class B is the superior class because they’re being led well, so when the time comes to make decisions, they don’t fight or break apart.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Class D had selfish students like Kouenji, who ran off on his own, and no one in our class had the power to step in. Meanwhile, Ichinose had brought Class B together, and there wasn’t a hint of disorder. They had a real sense of unity, probably the biggest difference between Classes D and B right now. The longer this conflict continued, the starker that difference would become.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">3.4</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">W</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">e saw a cave opening that looked like it cut deep into the mountainside, like a demon’s mouth. There were two temporary toilets and one shower room near the entrance. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t really see the inside from here…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Trying to confirm what was in the cave while keeping our distance was probably next to impossible. Neither Horikita nor I knew anyone in Class A. Even though we intended to sneak around, stay hidden, and gather information, we wouldn’t get anywhere. I stepped over Horikita and headed up the road to the cave.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-wait.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s go. I mean, it’s Class A, so of course we’re going to be scared. There’s nothing we can do about that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita and I both headed toward the base camp.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you planning? We gain nothing by carelessly exposing ourselves.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And what do we gain by trying to peek from our hiding spot? We can barely see, and there’s no one around. There’s not much we can see unless we enter the cave.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re awfully calm, aren’t you? Did you have something in mind?” Horikita asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I haven’t thought of anything. Come on, don’t worry.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh, what an unintelligible, half-assed answer.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She glared at me with cold and frightening eyes, but I pretended not to notice. Naturally, some Class A students hanging around the cave’s entrance discovered us. I thought I could salvage the situation so long as I could see the cave’s interior.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, inside the cave were several vinyl sheets joined together into one giant tarp, which blocked my view. I couldn’t see inside at all.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who are you guys? What class are you from?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This guy was definitely one of the two guys who’d found the cave quickly on the first day. Yahiko. The other guy, the sharp-witted Katsuragi, appeared to be absent. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We came to snoop. You have a problem?” Horikita responded, in an imposing manner. It was like a daring switch had been flipped in her brain. She continued, “I&#160;mean, I would’ve thought that since you’re in Class A you’d be clever, but…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She looked at the vinyl covering the cave’s entrance, and let out a rather forced sigh.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, rather than clever, I’d say you’re underhanded. What cowardly methods.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though it was plain to see she wanted to provoke him, Yahiko sounded irritated, as if she got on his nerves.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m Horikita, from Class D.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hah, it’s obvious you’re from Class D. You’re a bunch of stupid failures, after all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Stupid, huh? In that case, there’s no harm in us seeing what’s inside, right? Or does that make you feel uncomfortable?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not it at all!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So it’s not a problem if we look? You’re in the way.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-wait! Hey! Wait, I said! Don’t just do whatever you please!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yahiko moved in front of Horikita to block her, but Horikita’s words cut him off.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’re just going to look inside. That itself isn’t a violation of the rules, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Stop screwing around. Class A occupies this spot. Class D doesn’t have permission to use it!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh? So you occupy this place. I didn’t know that. Is there a device inside?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-yeah. So back off.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, there definitely aren’t any rules that say we can’t go into the cave. It’s certainly true that we cannot use the cave while it’s being occupied, but that’s not the same as the right to monopolize it or anything. We should have the right to look inside, or at least to verify if there’s equipment, right? If we couldn’t do that, then people could just forcibly monopolize every spot. That’s not what this test is about.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her sharp argument skewered Yahiko without any trouble. Horikita’s hair fluttered as she tried to peel off the veil that hid the cave entrance. However…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you doing? I don’t recall giving you permission to invite guests.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A particularly tall boy passed behind me and continued walking ahead to Horikita. This was definitely…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Katsuragi-san! These two came to snoop around our camp! They’re a bunch of filthy losers!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re exaggerating. It’s just vinyl. Show me around a little.” Horikita, turning to confront the two boys, didn’t seem scared in the slightest.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, it should be fine for you to look inside. However, prepare yourself. The moment you touch anything, I will notify the school and report your actions as obstruction toward another class. I can’t guarantee what will happen to Class D as a result.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Katsuragi was probably bluffing. There was a very low chance that we’d be disqualified for touching vinyl. And yet, there seemed to be some genuine danger hidden in his words.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re forcefully monopolizing control of the spot. The rules don’t protect such actions.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re right about that. I cannot argue the point. However, it’s something of an unspoken rule. You in Class D have a spot by the river. Class B has a well. You live in and surround the occupied space, so it’s halfway monopolized. Have you taken any forceful measures in dealing with trespassers to your area?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Katsuragi’s calm words stopped Horikita in her tracks. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“One class occupies one spot. Then, they continue to protect that spot so they may obtain points until the end of the test. If you violate this unspoken rule, it creates chaos. Naturally, Class A will trespass upon Class D’s base in retaliation. We should avoid trouble.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was possible to ignore what he said, but we couldn’t. Like Katsuragi said, the other classes had all unconsciously occupied one area each. If we broke that rule, our troubles would grow. Horikita turned on her heel and walked away from the cave.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, fine. I look forward to seeing the results, and Class A’s abilities.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We are quite capable. We also expect things from you, Class D. By which I mean, your futile struggling.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After that short exchange, Horikita had the wind knocked out of her sails. If Katsuragi hadn’t appeared, Horikita probably would have stepped through to the other side of the vinyl sheet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yahiko, don’t respond to cheap provocation. Her goal was to force her way in and look around. If you thrust your righteous superiority in their face, the other side will back off.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“S-sorry.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They’d stripped Horikita of all other options except retreat. Wonderful, wonderful.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seems like we have to leave Class A alone. We definitely can’t investigate them.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once they’d claimed the spot, they hid much of it away behind an impregnable wall. However, despite their efforts, we were able to glean something from the interaction.</span></p>
