<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 4:<br/>Double Question</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“Y</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">ou’re joking, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The first thing out of Horikita’s mouth sounded very accusatory.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, it’s the truth. Kouenji just ended the test for his group,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you an idiot? Why didn’t you stop him? Wasn’t that your responsibility as his roommate?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s an impossible task. Besides, there’s nothing I can do about it now. No use crying over spilt milk.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kouenji’s treasonous act had circulated around the ship, and naturally the classes were in an uproar. Even though we’d chatted yesterday, Horikita wanted to meet face-to-face today. She seemed unconvinced by my words, because she was still shaking her head.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When I see him next time, I’ll reprimand him myself. I’ll make him beg for mercy.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You already know that’s pointless, right? He won’t listen. He’ll just confuse you and waste your time. It’ll be a headache. For the time being, we should concentrate on our own groups.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She’d keep blaming me for Kouenji because he was my roommate. I decided to change the subject.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s true that my group is filled with troublesome opponents, but I’ve no intention of falling behind,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She certainly had an iron will. Well, I supposed I would have to leave the matter to her. I had problems of my own, specifically with Ichinose and the others who’d been secretly sent by Hoshinomiya-sensei to spy on me. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was thinking. You’re a girl, more or less. I wanted to ask you something,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I really don’t like the way you said that. ‘More or less’? I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">am</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> a girl.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita had misunderstood what I’d meant. She looked disgruntled, and turned away from me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, uh, that’s not really what I meant. I meant there was something I wanted to ask you, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">as</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> a girl.” Since she’d probably get angrier no matter what I did, I cut to the chase. “I want information on Karuizawa.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d planned to contact Karuizawa, but had never talked to her before. If Karuizawa ranked the boys in our class, I likely would’ve been last. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You want to talk to </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">me</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> about Karuizawa?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, exactly.” I nodded. “I’d like to know more about the people in my group, but it’s not easy. I should be able find out whatever I need to about the Professor and Yukimura, but I’m at a complete loss with Karuizawa. After the test on the island ended, Karuizawa invited you out to lunch. Right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I turned her down, though. You know that. I have no interest in Karuizawa-san. If you want information on her, why not talk to Hirata-kun? If he’s helping you, you’ll be able to contact her easily.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was certainly true. Unfortunately, prior to the test, I had </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">also</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> passed on the opportunity to have lunch with Karuizawa. Hirata probably remembered that, so I wanted to avoid asking him about this.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you think she’s the VIP? Is that what you’re worried about?” Horikita asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s that. But more than that, I can’t understand Karuizawa’s behavior. That’s why I’m worried.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, isn’t that none of your business? Besides, her behavior isn’t logical. I think it’s a waste of time,” she countered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita, I don’t think it’s good to write people off like that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Write them off? What are you talking about?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You and Karuizawa don’t get along at all because you’re both willful, so you see her as nothing more than a nuisance. You understand that she may also have strong points, too?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">She</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> has strong points? I can’t really imagine any. Isn’t she just flawed?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In terms of cooperativeness, Horikita was probably at the same level or even worse than Karuizawa.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When you first see someone, you make judgments based on their appearance. You might determine if someone is cool or cute, for instance. Basically, you read people. Call it your first impression; that’s simple enough. Next, you learn about their inner self through conversation and seeing them in action. You’ll see whether they’re sociable, belligerent, passive, etc.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita crossed her arms, acting like my words were obvious. She waited for me to continue.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But that’s still just as superficial as their outside appearance. Their innermost thoughts aren’t going to be immediately apparent from that. For example, take Kushida, Ibuki, or even me. There’s a difference between a person’s outer and inner selves.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So Karuizawa has some secret self?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Almost everyone does. They may not be aware of it themselves. You do, too.” Whenever she came face to face with her older brother, she exposed her fragility.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m still not entirely convinced. However, I imagine you’ll learn more about her by spending time together,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, that was easier said than done. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, what are Karuizawa-san’s good points?” Horikita asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I still can’t really describe it, but so far I’d say it’s her ability to assume control. She can take the initiative. In fact, her position in Class D is unshakeable.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, in the Rabbit group, that part of her stayed hidden, which exactly why I needed to uncover Karuizawa’s true nature as quickly as possible.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. Let’s say for the sake of argument that you’re right, and she has that ability. What do you intend to do? Are you thinking of making her your ally, too?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm, I have to think about that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I thought about how to answer, Ryuuen approached us. “Hey, you two. Having yourselves a nice date in the shade? Let me in on the fun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He wasn’t with Ibuki, apparently. He drew closer to us, wearing a creepy smile.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seems like you have an awful lot of free time. Not that I care, but there’s nothing for you here,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s for me to decide. Well then, have you decided how to find the VIP?” he asked. Ryuuen sat down without bothering to ask permission.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whatever my plans may be, I’ve no intention of telling you,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s too bad. I wanted to get your opinion. However, it looks like you haven’t made any progress at all in your search.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s an interesting thing to say. Are you saying that </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> know who the VIP is?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen smirked slightly at Horikita, as if expecting her to express disbelief. “I’ve already started to discover the VIP’s identity. Would you believe me if I told you that?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I wouldn’t. You don’t have any support like Ichinose-san and Katsuragi-kun do. You only have enemies. I can’t imagine you have reliable information,” she countered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, you’re right that I’m not a professional friend-collector, but making friends has nothing to do with whether or not I can gather information.” He spoke like a teacher rebuking his student for not getting the correct answer. “Unfortunately for you, I’ve already gotten a handle on the fundamentals of this test. Depending on how things go, Class C will win by an overwhelming majority.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, you can’t be…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No, what he was saying was probably true. The school always created tests with fundamental laws or rules at their core. That was true of our midterms, final exams, and even the test on the island. If you understood the logic behind those rules, it would be possible to win. This test was likely no different. He must have noticed that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s an extremely simple matter. All you need to do is find out what class the VIP is from. Then, your next move is to analyze the group,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Anyone could think of that. But are they going to answer honestly? If the school has a rule that guarantees your anonymity, all they need to do is lie and gain 500,000 points, right?” Horikita countered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen appeared calm in the face of Horikita’s doubts. “You just have to make sure it’s a situation where someone </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">can’t </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">lie,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Where someone can’t lie?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I take everyone’s cell phones. So if someone lies to me, all I have to do is check everyone’s email, one by one,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you insane? The school doesn’t allow that. If you were discovered, you’d be expelled,” snapped Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not really a problem at all, actually. I’m here </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">because</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> it’s not a problem. Do you get what I mean?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was a brute-force method that only he could pull off, because he was an absolute tyrant. If he forcibly looked at another student’s phone, then Ryuuen would be punished. However, even if Ryuuen rampaged through Class C, he believed no one would lodge a complaint against him. And if no one complained to the school, that meant they were practically giving him consent. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen’s calm proved that what he said was true—that he was operating within the rules. His strategy was to forcibly lay bare all of Class C’s secrets. At any rate, if what he was saying was true, Ryuuen had identified three of the VIPs. That was a huge step toward completing this&#160;test.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was comparable to a quiz where you could turn the page over and find the answer written on the back. If you didn’t flip the page, no one would know the answer, but if you flipped it just a little, you could spy the answer. In other words, Ryuuen probably knew the identities of every class’s VIP.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Looks like you finally understand.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. But you haven’t got the answer yet. If you had, you would’ve sent an email to the school immediately,” answered Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Maybe I’m just playing around?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t know when someone else is going to figure it out. You shouldn’t be so laid-back,” Horikita snapped. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She didn’t have any proof, but Horikita was probably correct. If he already knew the answer, there’d be no benefit to delaying the result. He should have ended it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now, then. Suppose I’m about to reach checkmate?” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ryuuen-kun. While you’re here, I’d like to ask you something. The test ended for the Monkey group yesterday. What do you think about that?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t think about it at all. I don’t really care about small fry. See you again, Suzune.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen left. His parting words made me wonder if he planned on reporting back regularly. I flipped him off, and Horikita made a face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know how much of what he said is true,” she&#160;said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While we kept silent, we peeked underneath the chair that Ryuuen had been sitting in. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">"Well, whatever. It’s not like this is the first time someone’s used force against me. So. Did you know about </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> too? How do you think I should act in an impossible situation?” She laughed coldly, then looked me right in the eyes with a thin smile. She was still trembling, and there was an intense darkness in her gaze.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“After a while, I gave up trying to resist. That’s right; I was everyone’s victim. I became cold, robotic. I could cry, scream, or thrash around, but it didn’t matter. I couldn’t do anything. All I could do was take it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa, as if accepting it all, lifted her skirt and touched her panties. I grabbed her slender arm and pushed her up against the wall.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What happened to you?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What happened? All sorts of things. Everything and anything. They’d put tacks in my shoes or stuff my desk full of roadkill. When I went to the bathroom, I’d get splashed with dirty water. They wrote words like ‘whore’ on my uniform. They pulled my hair and punched and kicked me. Anything you could imagine, basically. I was bullied in every way. Countless times. What I’ve just told you was just a fraction of what I’ve experienced. Those were the ‘gentler’ ways I’ve been bullied, too. It makes me want to laugh. So, why aren’t </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> laughing? Why don’t you laugh at the pitiful loser who’s been bullied all her life?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even after everything she’d suffered, she still got back up. She seemed ready to fight again. Her resilience had motivated her to enroll in this school. Still…the experiences she’d recounted weren’t enough to explain everything.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What else have you suffered?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Have you told me the whole truth?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I believed that something critical had shattered her spirit before this. There had to be some other reason behind her abnormal fearfulness, something that Karuizawa wanted to hide so much that it was worth giving up her body.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you hiding?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa turned her head from me and lowered her eyes to look down at her left side. I noticed that, of course. I reached over and touched that part of her body, over her uniform.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“S-stop!” she shouted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her shouts echoed within the enclosed walls of the room. I grabbed her uniform and pulled it up. There, on her beautiful skin, was an ugly scar. A deep scar, one that had been created by a sharp blade.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is it? This is your darkness?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“U-ugh!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This wasn’t the result of simple bullying. A serious scar like this had come from a life-threatening attack. Even though she was burdened with that horrible past, she remained tough. She got back on her feet. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Over the past few days, I’d keenly observed Karuizawa Kei. To protect herself, she forced people to become her allies. She protected her status, even if that meant being disliked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Despair comes in many forms. And you’ve experienced despair. Haven’t you?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa’s dark eyes met with mine. People who carry darkness within are attracted to one another. Slowly, they erode each other. Those who conceal a deep darkness will readily embrace the darkness of others. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what are you…you…” she stammered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If her past held her captive, then I had to forcefully free her from her shackles. Even if we weren’t close, I&#160;could feel the darkness from her, through her skin. Yes. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There were profoundly dark things left in this world that even Karuizawa didn’t yet know about.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I promise you one thing. From now on, I will protect you from bullying. I will be much more reliable than Hirata or Machida,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait. You meant that </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">can stop Manabe and her friends?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Right now, you should understand what I’m saying. If the wind blows, a tiny flame goes out. However, a larger flame only grows bigger. It becomes so strong that it won’t go out, not even in the face of wild winds or pouring rain. You will help me, and I will help you. Kindness has nothing to do with it. Do you have any problems with this arrangement?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“First things first. I’ll get rid of your anxieties for you,” I added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I pulled out my phone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have a way to stop Manabe and her friends.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I showed her phone. On the screen was a photo of Karuizawa being bullied in the emergency stairway.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s—” she began.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If I send this picture to them, they won’t get carried away anymore. If they still decide to harass you, perhaps by spreading rumors, then I will step in and put a stop to it. With this.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As far as Manabe and her friends were concerned, this incident should have been enough to satisfy them. If they got carried away and tried to hurt Karuizawa further, they would end up causing trouble for Ryuuen. Then they would be in jeopardy themselves. I let go of Karuizawa’s chin, and spoke in a flat, emotionless tone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All I want is for people to cooperate with me. I want you to assist me in the future, doing whatever I need.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What? Assist? What do you want me to do?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If things continue as they are, Class D will never overtake Class A. While the individual students within Class D certainly aren’t incapable, we lack a sense of unity. Our class is scattered. However, if you control the girls for me, the situation will gradually improve.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her social pull made her an even more valuable ally than Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you trying to…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Up until now, she’d only ever seen as me as a lowly, unobtrusive guy. Seeing my true self must’ve terrified her. But I was done explaining. Besides, the less I talked, the more terrifying I would appear. The less she would resist.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now, the first thing I need. We must guide our group to victory in this exam.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How am I supposed to help lead them to—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You can, because you’re… Right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though I didn’t articulate the key word in that sentence, Karuizawa seemed to know what it was. She looked me in the eyes. The truth resonated deep within her, in her heart. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She tried to look puzzled, but that was just an act. After all, a parasite couldn’t live without a host. By finding a new host, Karuizawa only had one way to go on living: with me.</span></p>
