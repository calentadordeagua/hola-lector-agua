<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 3:<br/>An Infinite Variety<br/>of Wishes</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">B</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">reakfast time. I avoided the buffet that was popular with a lot of the students, and made my way toward the ship’s deck. There was a café called Blue Ocean, which had hardly any students early in the morning. I took a table in the shaded back, where there weren’t many people around. It was 7:55 in the morning.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just one minute before the designated meeting time, the person I was waiting for appeared. She wore an emotionless expression, like always.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re rather early.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita sat down next to me. She was the one of the very few people at this school whom I could call a friend. She was unusually talented, and a thorn in my side, because she knew something about my hidden life.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was waiting for an hour.” I tried teasing her a little.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not my problem, as I’ve still arrived before the designated time. Besides, how would I know if you’d been waiting even ten hours or more?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It really wasn’t easy to tease her. Pointless, actually.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita wasn’t good at chit-chat. After I gave her my information yesterday, she hadn’t been able to reciprocate. All she did was propose we meet here. Was this some sort of trick? “So, did you get any more details?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just what you already told me. There are twelve groups, and four outcomes. Also, the school said they would send the names of the VIPs via email at 8:00 a.m. today. Any minor differences in the explanations they gave us could be attributed to differences in the way teachers communicate.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who’s in your group? How many people do you have?” I’d seen some of the lineup yesterday, but she knew that already.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The list is honestly surprising. It’s biased to the point where I can’t imagine it’s a coincidence.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita handed over a slip of paper, looking a little depressed. She’d written this down on her own notepad, and had memorized all the names. I took the list and looked it over. Her group name was Dragon. I understood what Horikita meant about bias when I looked at the names.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">CLASS A:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Katsuragi Kouhei, Nishikawa Ryouko, Matoba Shinji, Yano Koharu</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">CLASS B:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Andou Saya, Kanzaki Ryuuji, Tsube Hitomi</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">CLASS C:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Oda Takumi, Suzuki Hidetoshi, Sonoda Masashi, Ryuuen Kakeru</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">CLASS D:</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Kushida Kikyou, Hirata Yousuke, Horikita Suzune</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">First, the Class D students, Hirata and Kushida: They were both excellent students, and representatives of our class. Excluding the fact that Horikita was far too much of a loner, she definitely had outstanding talents that let her stand shoulder to shoulder with the other two. To be perfectly honest, it was the strongest hand Class D could have dealt. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d thought they’d have at least one more person, but that wasn’t the case. In terms of latent ability, Kouenji had an abundance, but he probably wouldn’t contribute any of it to the team. I didn’t know what group he was in.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. This really seems like an inevitable grouping,” I&#160;muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if I just limited it to the names I knew, they had Katsuragi from Class A, Kanzaki from Class B, and Ryuuen from Class C. All class representatives. If you thought of their group like players in the qualifying rounds of a soccer league, they were the dream team, and this was a death match.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But something feels unnatural,” I added. I didn’t really know many students, but it was somewhat unnatural for Ichinose to be in Rabbit and not Dragon.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You mean Ichinose-san being in your group, don’t you? I suppose only the Class B students know if she’s really excellent or not. A leader’s qualities and level of excellence aren’t necessarily directly proportional,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, are you talking about yourself?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She glared, so I averted my eyes. However, Horikita did have a point. We didn’t know the finer details of Ichinose’s abilities. Perhaps her academic scores were unexpectedly low.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“From what we can deduce, I wonder if there’s a certain method they’re using to sort us into the twelve groups? Ayanokouji-kun, your grades are very similar to Karuizawa-san’s. Are they grouping us based on our scores? Oh, but Yukimura-kun has high academic ability, and so does Kouenji-kun. They’re at the top of the class,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was taking our midterm grades and final exams into consideration. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But there’s probably a gap between me and the Professor, same as you and Hirata. There are too many details that don’t fit the score theory.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If students were divided into groups based purely on grades, then Kouenji should have been at the top. Of course, I agreed that our grades were taken into account, but another variable was likely involved in the process. If at all possible, I would’ve liked to see the member lists of the other groups.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At any rate, this is probably going to be difficult. Trying to lead the group and to outmaneuver everyone, I mean.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, when you had several high-ability people gathered together, the inclusion of the rather orthodox Horikita wasn’t necessarily advantageous. She and Ryuuen were especially incompatible, like fire and water. I didn’t like it; they were bound to clash.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But if I told Horikita that, she would probably be pissed. I decided to stay quiet. However, Horikita would probably work well with a straightforward person like Katsuragi. They both believed that intellect led to victory, so they’d get along.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it’s just about time,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once the clock struck 8:00, both of our phones beeped simultaneously. We immediately checked our phones. After we’d finished reading the contents of the message at almost the exact same time, Horikita turned her phone to show me the message. I did the same. We&#160;compared what was on each other’s phones, confirming the finer details.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“After thorough consideration, you have not been chosen to be the VIP. Please remember to be a team player as your group tackles the challenges of this test. The exam begins today, and will be held over a period of three days. Members of the Dragon group should gather in the Dragon room located on the second deck.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My message was almost the same as Horikita’s. Of course, the name of the group differed, but everything else matched up perfectly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose neither of us was chosen, then.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While putting our phones away, we shifted in our seats.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, we weren’t chosen. I’m not sure if we should be happy or sad.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. If you’re chosen, then you could lead your group to any of the outcomes,” I reasoned.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Being the VIP in this test placed you at an overwhelming advantage. If you kept up a good poker face, you could easily obtain 500,000 points. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even so, I don’t like how they wrote it. It’s like they’re saying I’m not qualified to be the VIP.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though she was on the dream team, Horikita still thought of herself as working alone. That was just like her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In this test, there’s a huge difference between the chosen students and the rest. Everyone who wasn’t chosen will need to struggle to find the VIP. The school said there were no disadvantages, but that was a lie. Unless the VIP is in your class, there’s a high probability that the point difference between classes will widen even further.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was certainly true. Depending on how things went, the gap we’d managed to close after the first test might widen again.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The leaders of each group should be coming up with a number of strategies already. If we don’t decide early on how we’re going to conduct ourselves, we probably won’t recover.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand,” Horikita responded.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She gave me a slightly frustrated look. I was trying to figure out how to fight this battle. As I considered the members of my group, and the underlying mechanics of this test, the goal began to take shape.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you thinking about the results?” asked Horikita, observing my expression. She sounded hesitant.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There are some things I can’t yet see, like how some of the students I don’t know will react. I won’t know until I meet with them directly. But I thought of a way to lead us to victory.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, we couldn’t behave recklessly. I’d need to time my move properly, and everything would need to be set up beforehand.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I look forward to your results,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Me too. Can’t wait to see what you do with your group,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But something about that message still nagged at me. “After thorough consideration,” it had said. That odd choice of words wasn’t by chance. Mashima-sensei had said something to the same effect. The VIP had been chosen based on certain considerations. There was something about the chosen that set them apart.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t get too hung up on phrasing right now. I&#160;knew that one person had been selected from each group. That meant there were twelve VIPs.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For the sake of reference, who are you most wary of? Based on how things have been going, I wanted your thoughts,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita’s attention had shifted slightly. Since she was assigned to the most intense group, that wasn’t surprising. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ryuuen,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That was fast.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s no one else I’d choose,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What about Katsuragi-kun? It’s because of him that Class A managed to quickly hold down that prime spot on the island. Isn’t he someone worth watching?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course. Considering he’s only a first-year student, he’s excellent. If you’d asked me who the most excellent student was, then Katsuragi would’ve been my answer. But if you’re asking who I’m wary of, then it’s Ryuuen, by a mile,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Class D had been victorious during the test on the island, without a doubt. Ryuuen had come up short in several ways. He’d telegraphed his intentions, so it ended up easy to get a read on him. However, it was highly likely that Ryuuen had read my intentions, too. I wanted to avoid him finding out that I was the one responsible for Horikita’s success on the island.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m curious about some things concerning the VIP’s identity. Even after reading the message and thinking about it, did you notice any unnatural-sounding phrases in the school’s email? And the strict—” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I silenced her by pressing my finger to her lips. Speak of the devil—a shadow appeared before us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nice weather, eh Suzune? You having breakfast with this clingy beta male?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Two people approached us, each wearing an unsettling grin. One of them was Ryuuen, the very subject of our discussion. The other person was…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve warned you not to call me by my first name, Ryuuen-kun. Also, considering you were a turncoat who only pretended to be our friend, it’s odd you’d show your face, Ibuki-san.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Beside Ryuuen stood a female student, Ibuki Mio. She&#160;had a somewhat cocksure gleam in her eyes. She also happened to be in the Rabbit group with me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki seemed slightly disapproving of Horikita’s light provocation, but didn’t snap back. Instead, she bit down lightly on her lower lip. Ryuuen, watching things unfold out of the corner of his eye, flashed a satisfied smile. During the test on the island, Ibuki had infiltrated Class D as a spy. Eventually, Horikita had caught Ibuki red-handed, but ended up catching Ibuki’s fist during their ensuing confrontation. Horikita firmly insisted that if she hadn’t been ill at the time, she wouldn’t have lost the fight, but right now, I wasn’t really concerned about who was stronger. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen silenced Ibuki. He appeared to be mocking us. “You should have received the message already. What were the results? Were you chosen as the VIP?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“As if I would tell you. Perhaps you’d like to tell us about </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">your</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> message?” Horikita responded.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you wish.” Ryuuen straddled one of the two vacant seats. “But before that, I want to ask you something. How did you wrangle those results on the island test?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have nothing to tell you,” Horikita responded.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita looked completely calm; she didn’t shake or waver in the slightest. There was nothing false about her attitude, either. She had incredible acting ability. She probably didn’t think she was acting, though. Even though she didn’t show any weakness, Ryuuen wasn’t convinced.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose you wouldn’t give anything away, but that doesn’t matter. According to my information, there’s no way that your farcical bumbling on the island should’ve led to victory,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not so stupid that I’d let someone like </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">her</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> see through me. Things were simply more difficult because of my fever.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In responsive to that more blatant provocation, Ibuki could no longer hide her irritation. “In that case, let’s have a rematch,” she challenged.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita kept her cool as Ibuki became more and more riled up. “Regrettably, I must decline your offer. Violent actions are considered a violation of the test rules. If you </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">do</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> happen to strike me, I will not hesitate to report it to the school. At any rate, please feel free to do whatever you please,” she said flatly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tch!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki closed the distance between herself and Horikita, looking ready to lunge, but she stopped herself. If you thoughtlessly went on a rampage here, you wouldn’t escape punishment. More than anything else, though, Ibuki was working beneath Ryuuen. She didn’t have the right to act freely. While Ibuki clearly detested Ryuuen, she was also exceptionally talented. That was likely the precise reason why Ryuuen had chosen her as the spy to infiltrate Class D. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Since we’re all together, how about we have some coffee? It seems a good time to enjoy a nice cup,” Horikita offered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She seemed in a strangely good mood as she ordered her morning coffee. I ordered the same thing. Ryuuen showed no signs of leaving, apparently wanting to continue the conversation. He continued to observe the silent Horikita, and opened his mouth once again when the coffee arrived. “Yesterday, it seemed Katsuragi was rather cautious of you,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that’s understandable. He couldn’t have guessed that someone from Class D would do so well. Isn’t that why you and Ibuki-san are here? You came to check on me. Am I wrong?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh. Well, I won’t deny that. I came here to measure your abilities for myself,” he answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course,” Horikita answered, sipping her coffee. She&#160;seemed at ease, which was unusual.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Katsuragi and I think differently. When I’m dealing with someone, I like to keep an eye on them.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re free to do as you please, but what exactly are you thinking?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m remembering the test on the island. The result. The process that led to it. There are only certain types of people who would’ve been able to conceptualize and execute a plan like that. A girl like you is far too serious-minded to have come up with it,” reasoned Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Think whatever you like. Though I wonder…how did you figure out my strategy? You only saw the results of the test. How do you know the way in which points were gained and lost? Those details are supposed to be unknown,” countered Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In response to Horikita’s calm demeanor, Ryuuen flashed his teeth in curious amusement. “Katsuragi probably didn’t know.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Based on how he’d said it, Ryuuen most likely </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">did</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> know.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, why don’t you explain it to me? If you’re correct, I’ll give you an answer. If you </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">can</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> answer, that is,” Horikita added. Ryuuen just laughed unnervingly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At the end of the test, I wrote down your name as leader, but that was wrong. There’s just one reason why that was wrong: Your class leader changed right before the test ended. The only explanation,” mused Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you think that was hard to deduce? Any idiot could have figured that out if they thought about it for a minute. Even Katsuragi-kun, who you’ve been making fun of,” answered Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah. However, Katsuragi thinks </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> planned the whole thing. But was that really the case? In my opinion, you becoming the leader and then retiring were both unexpected. Besides, I had my own strategy in place. I had Ibuki infiltrate your class to find out the leader’s identity. You did nothing to counter that strategy at first,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is it possible that I’d simply taken the proper precautions? Preparing for the unknown is absolutely basic logic. The moment Ibuki-san met Class D, I took all possibilities into account. You were so confident, but your argument ended up being flimsy. You’ve said nothing surprising,” responded Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The key question is who replaced you as leader. Personally, I think the second leader was pulling your strings from behind the scenes during the entire test.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was exactly as Ryuuen had said. Though he spoke to Horikita, he observed me quietly. If I appeared shaken for even an instant, he’d pounce.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m afraid I can’t understand you at all. I have no close friends. No ordinary high school student could pull that off. He really was exceptional in every way. Honestly, I wondered if Kouenji was even participating in this test. Hirata seemed to guess my thoughts.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kouenji </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">is </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">participating in a group. After all, students are required to participate, and points are taken away if a student fails to participate,” said Hirata. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Trust Hirata to read the rules thoroughly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To tell you the truth, I got word that two of our classmates have been selected as the VIPs in their groups,” he whispered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What? Who?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s… I can’t say. They only told me because they trust me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So you can’t trust us, Hirata? If you know, then I have the right to know, too. Besides, if you know who a VIP is, that might give us some kind of hint. We should all be sharing information with each other in the first place. It’s only natural, after all,” said Yukimura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, you’re right. That’s why I wanted to consult with you. It’s just that—” Hirata started.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Hirata. Wouldn’t it be better to give us that information by phone? We don’t know who’s listening in on our conversations,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, you’re right. Wait just a moment.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Two names showed up on Hirata’s phone. He turned the screen to us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Dragon Group, Kushida-san. Horse Group, Minami-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Immediately after I saw the names, Hirata erased them.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see,” murmured Yukimura. He was careful not to say too much. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So Kushida was the VIP. Having that position in the extremely well-stacked Dragon group meant they had an enormous advantage. However, being the VIP was terrifying. Once they knew her identity, she was at their mercy. If the VIP had been from another class, we wouldn’t have taken the brunt of the damage even in the worst-case scenario.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry. Everything is all right,” said Hirata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He’d noticed I was worried. The three Class D people in the Dragon group were the best we had to offer. They’d never be careless enough to reveal the VIP’s identity.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Speaking of Rabbit Group, it’s equally possible for the VIP to be from any of the four classes. So in Class D, there are two VIPs that we know about. There should be one more VIP keeping his or her identity secret,” said Yukimura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. I think your logic follows. They certainly haven’t talked to me about it, but they might’ve consulted someone else. After all, there’s a high risk of being discovered if you talk,” Hirata said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While we talked amongst ourselves, Kouenji started humming. Yukimura, who’d been patient up until that point, bolted out of his chair in frustration. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kouenji, stop the happy-go-lucky act already! I’m not going to tell you to take things seriously, but you need to at least participate. We don’t want you to screw everything up like you did back on the island,” he shouted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I couldn’t exactly help it. I was in awful shape. I couldn’t force myself to do the impossible,” answered Kouenji.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You just faked being ill to get out of it!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My, this test will be nothing but troublesome,&#160;wouldn’t you say?” Kouenji sniffed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He continued his push-ups, then stood gracefully. He grabbed the towel from his bed and started wiping his&#160;neck.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Troublesome? You’re not even thinking about this test at all!” countered Yukimura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, there isn’t any point to continuing a test that isn’t interesting, is there? Finding a liar is simple.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kouenji, phone in hand, started to fiddle around. Soon after, all four of us, including Kouenji, received a notification from the school on our phones.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What did you just do, Kouenji?!” shouted Yukimura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata and I read over the email we’d just received from the school. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“The test has now ended for the Monkey group. Those in the Monkey group are no longer required to participate any further. Please do not disturb the other students.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Monkey Group? Hey, that’s </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">your</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> group, Kouenji!” shouted Yukimura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course. And now, I finally have my freedom once again. Adieu.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He tossed his phone aside before slipping into the bathroom. The rest of us were simply dumbstruck.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-you’ve got to be kidding me! We’re desperately trying to think of a way out of this, and that guy, he just…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We still don’t know anything yet. He probably had his own ideas,” replied Hirata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re being way too generous! That guy will screw anyone over, so long as he’s able to enjoy himself and take it easy. This sucks!” shouted Yukimura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, Kouenji didn’t take the test seriously at all. That was true. However, he was remarkably perceptive and observant. He had boldly stated that the test was nothing more than a “simple quiz” to find the liar. If that were true, he’d probably hit the mark. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kouenji’s sudden actions soon became known to the rest of the students. Hirata’s phone started to beep continuously as new notifications poured in. Classmates were desperate to know what’d happened. Katsuragi, Ryuuen, and Ichinose would all be surprised by this. Probably no one had imagined that someone would turn traitor so soon. Horikita messaged me:</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I’m sorry. Things seem really confusing right now. I’m going to give you a call.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Damn it. Thanks to Kouenji, things have escalated,” said Yukimura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m stepping out for a bit,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yukimura seemed like he was so irritated he wouldn’t be able to sleep. I stepped out of the room. Even though the test had ended for Kouenji, I couldn’t dwell on that. To be honest, I was seeing my own limitations in this test. No matter how much I schemed, it would be extremely tough to lead the Class D students from all the remaining groups to victory. You could even say it was impossible.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If the students came together, we could do something. Otherwise, we were beyond help. We had no connection. You couldn’t interfere with another group’s answers using your own phone. There wasn’t enough time to find another method, and the risk was high. If I had some information that could decisively turn everything around, that would be another matter. The ringleaders were Hirata and Kushida. If I could use them…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s impossible,” I muttered. There were three days left. Even if I did manage to gain their cooperation, I still didn’t have enough eyes and ears. I needed to understand what was going on in each group’s discussions. Of course, I might still be able to use Horikita and Sakura, but…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well. Right now, I needed to get more eyes and ears on my side.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">3.6</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">he starry skies stretched out before me as far as the eye could see. I wandered around, and stumbled onto the deck.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, that’s amazing.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The view was more beautiful than anything I had seen in a movie or envisioned in a book. It was the kind of sky you couldn’t see in a large city. There were a few couples holding hands, looking up to the stars shoulder-to-shoulder. I felt a little lonely. Because there was almost no light, I couldn’t make out their faces, but I didn’t particularly care. I wasn’t interested in other people’s romances.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But amongst all the couples, there was one student looking up at the starry skies alone. A girl, judging by the silhouette.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t just walk up and say something like, “Why don’t we look at the stars together?” I’d probably come off sounding like one of those sleazy pick-up artists. Besides, if a boyfriend came along and joined her in the middle of me making a move, it would be bad. But part of me was interested in discovering her identity. I tried getting a little closer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The girl turned toward me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Oh, Ayanokouji…kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That voice… Kushida?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kushida stepped out of the shadows. She looked at me with an expression of shock.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you…alone?” I asked. Perhaps she was waiting to meet her boyfriend. Just imagining that made my chest tighten and ache.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I am. I just couldn’t seem to sleep.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I see,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, now I knew she wasn’t on a starlit date. In that case, I figured she wouldn’t mind, so I got closer. Kushida wore a jersey. She must have just gotten out of the bath, because she smelled nice. It should have been the scent from the complimentary shampoos in our rooms, but it wasn’t. How mysterious.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Aren’t you cold?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m fine. What about you, Ayanokouji-kun? Are you all alone?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I nodded. When I did so, Kushida laughed happily.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So we’re both alone? I admit, I felt a little ashamed being alone. This makes me feel better.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I should’ve spouted off a witty one-liner. But I couldn’t. Besides, being alone with Kushida in a place with a ton of couples spiked my heart rate. Deep down, though, Kushida must’ve been thinking how much she hated this.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I’m gonna head on back,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re already leaving?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m getting tired.” That was a total lie. I didn’t want to sleep in the slightest.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Well, I’ll see you tomorrow. Good night, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good night, Kushida.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I turned around and began to make a pathetic retreat.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Unexpectedly, Kushida wrapped her arms around my chest. Even in this cold, I felt the warmth of her body through her jersey.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“K-K-Ku…Kushida? Wh-what’s the matter?” I squeaked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, I was completely flustered. It was only natural that I’d panic. I couldn’t understand what was happening.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But Kushida didn’t answer right away. She finally responded in a small voice.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry. I…I just felt so lonely, I think,” she whispered. Her words hit me hard, like a fighter smacking me right in the jaw. I felt dizzy. Kushida buried her face in my chest for a few seconds longer, without saying a word. Then she pulled away, flustered, as if breaking from a spell.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol4_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_6" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v4/OEBPS/Images/ClassroomElite-Vol4_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_6.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"></div>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“S-sorry. I, um…I just hugged you so suddenly, Ayanokouji-kun. Good night!” she stammered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t make out Kushida’s face too well in the dark, but she might have been blushing slightly. Kushida quickly scurried away before I could speak. I stood there, hand on my chest, feeling the warmth that remained. I already couldn’t sleep, and after all that, I couldn’t just return to my room. I decided to wander around the boat before heading back.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now that I’ve calmed down, I’m feeling thirsty.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There should’ve been a few vending machines on the first level, so I decided to head there. But as I approached, I found an odd three-person group: Chabashira-sensei; the Class B homeroom teacher, Hoshinomiya-sensei; and Mashima-sensei from Class A.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The teachers were relaxing on the sofa. The area wasn’t technically off limits to students, but since things like izakaya and bars weren’t open to us, no one came around. I’d taken this route just for a change of scene, but had stumbled onto an opportunity. I hid myself and drew nearer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know, it’s been a long time since the three of us all got together.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It is what it is. Fate. After bouncing around from one thing to the next, we all chose the teacher’s life.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Enough. There’s no point talking about that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, that reminds me. You were on a date the other day, weren’t you? You’ve got a new girlfriend, huh? Mashima-kun, you’re quite the playboy. And here I’d thought you were the quiet, unsociable type.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Chie, what happened to the man you were with?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah ha ha! We broke up two weeks ago. I’m the type of girl who breaks it off once the relationship starts getting serious. It’s just like, see ya later!” Hoshinomiya-sensei said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s what you’d usually hear from the guy.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, but I’d never do that to </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, Mashima-kun. You’re my best friend, after all. I’d hate to ruin our friendship.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Relax. I’m not worried about that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What a shocker.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hoshinomiya-sensei poured whiskey into an empty glass. She downed it in one gulp. Must’ve been a drinker. On the other hand, Chabashira-sensei sipped her drink like a cocktail.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you planning, Chie?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? What are you talking about? What’d I do?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s customary to place all of the class representatives into the Dragon group, isn’t it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not screwing around or anything. It’s certainly true that as far as grades and attitude are concerned, Ichinose-san is number one. However, someone’s true place in society cannot be measured by numbers alone. I determined that she needed a challenge. And besides, rabbits are just so cute, aren’t they? The way they hop around. Doesn’t that suit Ichinose-san?” Hoshinomiya-sensei asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I hope you’re right.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think what you’re saying makes sense, Hoshinomiya, but what are you getting at?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We wouldn’t want you to base your judgment on personal grudges.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, are you still talking about what happened ten years ago? I thought that was all water under the bridge.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder. I mean, you can’t seem to stop yourself from running your mouth. You’re not satisfied unless you’re one move ahead. That’s why you put Ichinose in the Rabbit group, isn’t it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I genuinely thought that Ichinose needed to learn a lesson, so I removed her from the Dragon group. You know? By the way, Sae-chan, I couldn’t help but notice you’ve been focusing on Ayanokouji-kun. Anyway, it’s just a coincidence. Coincidence, coincidence. When the island test ended and Ayanokouji-kun became a kind of leader, you weren’t surprised at all, were you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see.” Mashima-sensei nodded as though convinced of something. However, he then strictly addressed Hoshinomiya-sensei. “There’s no rule against this, but I want to be clear. Stop spying on other colleagues’ classes.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Agh, it’s like you don’t trust me at all. Well, I’m not the only one to blame. Sakagami-sensei is part of the problem, too. If we evaluated the Class C students properly, another student should’ve been placed into Dragon. But they threw Ryuuen in there.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s certainly true. This isn’t your average year. The students seem rather special.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With this newfound information, it was about time for me to head back. If I stayed any longer, I might be seen. Just knowing that Ichinose had been sent to spy on me was enough for this little reconnaissance. Apparently I was under heavy scrutiny.</span></p>
