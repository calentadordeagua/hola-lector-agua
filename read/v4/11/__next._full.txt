<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">About twenty minutes before the discussion period was supposed to begin, some cute-sounding music played throughout the room. It came from Ichinose’s phone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Mmm…” she mumbled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose, eyes still closed, grabbed her phone. She unlocked the screen and stopped the music, which apparently was her phone’s alarm. Ichinose, still looking rather sleepy, started to sit up. Almost immediately, she noticed me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d wondered if she would be disgusted by my presence, but she wasn’t concerned in the slightest.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, good…</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">yawn</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">…to see you, Ayanokouji-kun. Sorry, did my alarm startle you?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, no. Looks like you slept well.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ha ha ha, sorry about that. I just passed out; I slept like a log. You’re here early, though. Don’t we have twenty minutes until the discussion?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I should be asking you the same thing. When did you get here?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“An hour ago, I think. I wanted some peace and quiet. Since my friends are going in and out of my room, it’s kinda noisy.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, this was the best place for a nap.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Besides, I wanted to gather my thoughts,” she added. Rather than looking refreshed by her nap, she seemed struck by sudden inspiration.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Any results?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“More or less.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She stood up. Then, for some reason, Ichinose walked over and sat down next to me. The two of us were alone in the room. The space between us was shrinking. Even though I couldn’t hide my nervousness, Ichinose didn’t seem to notice. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s still some time left until the discussion starts. Why don’t we have a little chat? If it’s not a bother, that is,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no, it’s not a bother. I don’t mind.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. To tell you the truth, Ayanokouji-kun, I want to ask you something. I’ve asked all my classmates this question, including the boys. But I’ve been thinking about asking the other classes for a while now. I’ve just been kind of curious. Ayanokouji-kun, do you want to ascend to Class A?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d been wondering what she would ask me, but the question was something surprisingly ordinary. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, yeah. Of course I do. I’ve thought about getting up to Class A. No, wait…I guess it’d probably be more accurate to say that I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">must</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> aim for Class A.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Because of the guarantee of college or a career?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In our school, students from Classes A through D competed against each other. The greatest privilege— being guaranteed to advance into any school or career path—was only limited to those in Class A. Many might think that sounded like a trick. The school pamphlet was rather ambiguous, so the details were murky.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nowadays, you can’t just enter college or get a job that easily. Jobs, especially,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think so, too. But don’t you think placing too must trust in the system could be dangerous? There’s something about that 99.9% percent they’re not telling us. Something dangerous,” Ichinose said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose was referring to the “99.9 % college and job placement” rate that the school touted. She had a point about hidden pitfalls. Let’s say I wanted to be a professional baseball player, but had no experience playing the game. How would the school go about making me a pro? Even with their professional connections, they were limited. And even if you did play regularly in school, that didn’t guarantee you’d go pro. Even if you graduated from college or went on to graduate school, that didn’t guarantee you any sort of future. Really, only a fraction of people really managed to accomplish what they set out to achieve. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Statistically speaking, only one of every six students can achieve their dreams. At first, you might think that’s a high percentage, but the data’s ambiguous and the statistics are fuzzy. Being a professional baseball player isn’t the same thing as becoming a top-ranked player. If you gathered up everyone who qualified as a professional baseball player, including trainees, you’d have about 900 to 1,000 people. However, if the dream is to play as a regular on the team and take down your opponents on your first attempt, then maybe a hundred people max could do it. Finally, even if you secured a spot as a regular player, you must continue playing against your rivals, always a linchpin for the team.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In other words, no matter what you chose, it’s unlikely you’d achieve your dream. And it’s an extremely difficult thing to do. Many students just continue boring, tedious lives, with vague lip service paid to dreams as the years pass. To actually achieve a dream requires a lot of effort and luck.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But this school… Well, it does have an incredible amount of influence, right? Most people who get anywhere in life succeed because someone influential helps them. Or are you not interested in that, Ichinose?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I’m not saying that. I want to graduate from Class A. I have a dream I want to make real,” she answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though she was smiling, her eyes were unwavering and serious. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The school system’s all well and good, but if you can’t graduate from Class A, it’s a mark of failure. This school is all about ability, and if your abilities can’t carry you here, it’s unlikely you’ll ever be labeled an elite. Students are ranked based on perceived superiority or inferiority. Right now, between the two of us, Ayanokouji-kun, only one can achieve their dream. Ah, but then again, we could both lose out on our goals.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though we were chatting like friends, only one of us could win. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ve heard there are exceptions to the rules, though.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm? You mean when someone manages to accumulate twenty million points?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. There hasn’t been a single student in the school’s history who’s successfully pulled that off, but it is theoretically possible.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh yeah, for sure. I suppose if we factor </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> in, it’s possible for both of us to graduate from Class A,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Be that as it may, whether or not you can really earn twenty million points is another problem. Even if you score well on the exams and save your points, it probably wouldn’t be enough,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Going by this test alone, it seemed possible to earn a great number of points, depending on how hard you worked, but we’d only had two such exams. From this point forward, it was possible that the number of such tests would be reduced, and the chances of being penalized could increase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s true. Even if you’re extremely economical, it’s unlikely someone could manage to save even half that amount,” Ichinose mused.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. Class D’s financial situation is especially awful. Even though Horikita’s trying her hardest, the points from the island test have yet to be deposited. Actually, it’s entirely possible we’ll lose them in this test,” I said. “Are you a thrifty person, Ichinose? You don’t strike me as someone struggling to get by.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm, I wonder about that. Personally, I’ll use points sometimes and save them sometimes, just like anyone else would. Even though I’m in Class B, I don’t really have that many points saved.” Ichinose responded to my question with ease. I didn’t see any indication she was hiding something from me, but…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose suddenly turned toward me. She looked me right in the face. “It seems like you saw it, a while back,” she remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t look away from her beautiful eyes. It was almost as if they were drawing me in. Ichinose was even cleverer than I’d imagined. I guess she’d seen right through my plans.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry. When you were using your phone earlier, I just happened to look at the screen. I was a little curious, so I was thinking of asking you about it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ha ha, you don’t need to feel guilty or anything. It’s not like I’m blaming you. I mean, it certainly </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">is</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> a lot of points, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yeah, it was. Before the first semester had ended, Ichinose had already amassed an insane amount of points. Even if I saved all the class points that were doled out on the first of every month and didn’t spend a single one, I still wouldn’t have been able to save that many.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But I can’t really give you any details. Sorry,” she added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No need to apologize.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course, if you </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">did</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> manage to obtain that information, Ayanokouji-kun, and even if you shared it with Horikita-san, you wouldn’t go blabbing to everyone, would you? I mean, even though you saw my phone, if&#160;someone else decided to ask about it, you wouldn’t tell, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t plan on telling anyone else. Besides, I might’ve been mistaken. I won’t pry.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">did</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> pry, it wasn’t like I’d get a satisfactory answer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Have you found a way to win this thing?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm, I think so. At least, I think I’ve found a hint.” I didn’t think she would answer that honestly, but Ichinose sounded relaxed and confident. She seemed the type of person to act on her own initiative and not waste time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, this contest will come down to a battle between A and B, I suppose.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We won’t know that until the end. My path to victory is—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Right before she finished her thought, members of our group poured into the room, one after another. The Class A students were first to arrive, but they took their seats without giving us so much as a greeting.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, what’s this? You’re here already, Ayanokouji?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All alone with Ichinose-dono? My word, how suspicious. A clandestine rendezvous, wouldn’t you say?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yukimura and the Professor bombarded me with questions as they walked into the room together. I couldn’t especially tell if they were impatient or depressed, but it seemed they’d given up on winning. On the other hand, the Class B students seemed rather relaxed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is the end, huh? So, have you found any hints?” asked Hamaguchi. He spoke gently to me as we waited for the final discussion to begin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To be honest, I have no idea. We haven’t really been able to talk, which means we haven’t been able to engage,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was my official answer, but I’d already executed the strategy I’d been plotting since the test began. My plan involved the phones we all received from the school. I had switched the VIP’s phones as a means of camouflage. Kushida was the Dragon group’s VIP, but what if Kushida and Horikita switched phones? If someone spied on her phone, they’d suspect that Horikita was the VIP.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, if a traitor submitted Horikita’s name as their answer, they’d be making a mistake. We would win.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good evening. Nice to see you all,” Ichinose said warmly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was smiling, just like always. I set the trap immediately. We didn’t know who else had a hidden agenda, after all. I’d been waiting for Ichinose to talk, and decided to cut in before she spoke up again.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, excuse me. If everyone’s okay with it—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have something I’d like to ask—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Both Hamaguchi and I started talking at the same time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, sorry. Go ahead, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, no. You can go first. I don’t mind,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">How annoying. Well, this didn’t get in the way of my plan, but any unexpected trouble could make things unstable. I decided to let Hamaguchi speak first. I’d chime in after thinking things through. Then, Hamaguchi shattered my plans in an unexpected way.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For the past three days, I’ve been thinking of how we could achieve Outcome #1,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hamaguchi launched into an explanation of his plan which, surprisingly, was quite like mine.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There is a way for everyone here to achieve Outcome #1,” he continued.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A faint glimmer of hope shone in everyone’s eyes. “Is that really true, Hamaguchi?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I came up with this idea after listening to everyone here, including Ichinose-san and Machida-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t believe it. There’s no way we can arrive at Outcome #1 without discussion,” Machida huffed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s hear him out first. Hamaguchi-kun isn’t the sort to speak without thinking,” offered Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m going to show you all my phone. Of course, the school sent us all an email. I think you all understand what I’m getting at? Because we’re prohibited from tampering with or misrepresenting the emails we’ve received in any way, there’s no way to deceive one another. That’s why the answer is simple. We show each other our emails, and then we find out who the VIP is. That’s how we’ll discover the truth.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is dumb. Why would anyone show their phone just because you told us to? Someone could betray us all the moment we show our emails. No one would do this,” answered Machida flatly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was a hopeless plan. Naturally, Machida-kun was flabbergasted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s certainly true that if the VIP knows they could be betrayed, they wouldn’t show their phone. However, from the perspective of someone who isn’t the VIP, there’s no risk in exposing your identity. The test will be over soon. If we don’t make a move, we lose our chance of winning. Suppose there’s a class working together to cover for the VIP. It’s true that none of them would show their phones. But this way, it’s possible to narrow down the list of candidates.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if you find out the VIP’s identity or the class they belong to, the moment someone decides to betray you, it’s over. The problem hasn’t been resolved. Or are you suggesting that the first to betray us wins?” countered Machida.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Through Hamaguchi’s strategy, it was possible to successfully weed out the VIP. But that was it. In the end, people weren’t going to play nice.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, please be quiet and just watch. If you don’t participate, Machida-kun, it’ll still be fine,” Hamaguchi answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hamaguchi showed everyone the email that he’d received.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I agree with Hamaguchi-kun. I’ll show mine, too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After Hamaguchi showed his phone, Beppu from Class B followed suit. This didn’t seem like some spur-of-the-moment idea. This seemed like a strategy Ichinose had come up with. Strangely enough, her plan was exactly the same as mine. However, I didn’t know how far she’d thought this through, or what her moves were. If she simply believed everyone would go along with this, then it was quite simply reckless.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think it’s a surprisingly good strategy. I don’t have any objections,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Smiling, Ichinose reached for her cell phone in her skirt’s left pocket. “I’ve been agonizing over this for a long time now, but after hearing Hamaguchi’s plan, I get&#160;it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose pulled out her phone. I decided to step in and interrupt before she could execute her strategy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re serious about this, huh? Well, if you’re all going to bet on that, I think I’ll join in, too,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Before Ichinose could show everyone the contents of her email, I took out my own phone and offered it up. But it wasn’t actually my phone; I’d switched it with someone else’s.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun…are you okay with this?” Ichinose asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure. After hearing Hamaguchi out, I honestly don’t think we have another option. I’m really bad at communicating, so all I can do is show you the truth,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, Ayanokouji. There’s no way this kind of strategy is going to work,” said Yukimura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He tried to stop me, but I showed everyone the email on my phone. Everyone saw that I wasn’t the VIP. An incredible amount of water was building behind this unseen dam. If even the tiniest hole opened up, the dam would collapse, and we’d be left with a muddy stream of water. My actions opened that hole.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Among the large number of people still scoffing at Hamaguchi’s strategy, a lone girl agreed. It was the person I’d least expected: Ibuki Mio.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you nuts? We don’t get anything out of this!” shouted Manabe.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki didn’t talk to Manabe as if she were an ally. She spoke like addressing an enemy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“N-no. I…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, show everyone your phone.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki’s words threatened her classmates. Manabe and her friends, as if accepting Ibuki’s order, showed everyone their phones. The hunt for the VIP had begun. Karuizawa took out her phone, which had a strap attached, and handed it over.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m doing this for my own sake. I want those private points,” said Karuizawa.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose proved that she was not the VIP.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was wrong to go along with your plan. This sucks,” he fumed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One by one, everyone left the room, leaving me alone with Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now all we can do is to trust everyone,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. Guess so,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re really calm, Ayanokouji-kun. Aren’t you worried?” </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not especially. I can’t do anything but believe, anyway. I’m heading to my room.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Class B: No Change cl; Plus 2.5 Million pr</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Class C: Plus 150 cl; Plus 5.5 Million pr</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Class D: Plus 50 cl; Plus 3 Million pr</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Class C…came out on top,” muttered Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Everyone looked stunned by the results.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Isn’t this great, Suzune? Thanks to your blunder, the Dragon group managed to clear this test with Outcome #1. Because of that, all the classes should receive a boost in points,” Ryuuen crowed. He clapped his hands and wore a satisfied grin. “If you beg for it, I’ll tell you the answer. How about it?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who would—” Horikita started to snap, but quickly shut her mouth.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ooh, that look you have. It’s pretty sexy.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen took out his phone and placed it on the table. On the screen was a list, and on that list were the names of the VIPs from Class A in the Rat, Rooster, and Pig groups.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I made some adjustments and arrived at the root of the test. Then, I just focused on the Class A students,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen had managed to clear the test without targeting Classes B or D at all. No one should have been able to pull off something like that, but Ryuuen did.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry to tell you, Suzune, but you’re my next target. I’m going after you with everything I’ve got. I won’t stop until I tear you to shreds, both in mind and body.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita, unable to attempt a comeback, simply kept staring at the results. After obtaining such a huge number of points, Class C had gained an overwhelming lead. Looking over the results, it became clear that Kouenji saved our asses, even though we thought he’d been screwing around. If he hadn’t done what he did, this victory would’ve belonged exclusively to Class C. Of course, Kouenji’s actions had ended up causing stray bullets to go flying at other VIPs.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m looking forward to the second semester,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen, his payback for the island administered in full, walked away with satisfaction. The rest of us weren’t exactly in a celebratory mood. If someone saw the stern looks on our faces, they’d imagine we’d suffered a crushing defeat.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand that Ryuuen-kun managed to discover the VIPs in Class A, sure, but I’m not convinced he has some kind of preternatural talent. Still, how did Dragon Group end up with that result?” asked Hirata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No one answered, perhaps because no one could figure it out. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I mean, it’s not really a difficult problem. If you just put your mind to it, it’s relatively simple,” I told everyone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Putting aside how Ryuuen found out the VIPs’ identities, all he had to do was tell everyone ‘Kushida is the VIP’ before the end of the test, right? Of course, no one would believe what someone like Ryuuen says. Especially a group of such intelligent, gifted people. However, the final half hour was different. Even if you answered incorrectly, there wasn’t any risk to class points. As such, even someone who’d been playing defensively like Katsuragi could vote, right? If there was even a 1% chance that Kushida was really the VIP, then they would’ve gotten Outcome #1, which was the most convenient for everyone involved.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If he’d just planted the seed earlier, it would’ve been extremely simple. But something like that would’ve normally been impossible. It was a tightrope act; something that couldn’t have happened unless every single person trusted that Kushida was the answer. Was it even possible? As I thought about it, I still had doubts. I never would’ve imagined he could succeed. How had he gained everyone else’s—excluding Class D, of course—trust? I&#160;was genuinely curious. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Perhaps if he had absolute proof?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita. I think that we might be in trouble,” I said. There was no quick fix to our problem. Depending on how things went, Class D might be stuck with these obstacles for the long haul.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“By trouble, you mean Ryuuen-kun? It’s true he did very well in this test, but there’s no guarantee he’ll be dangerous in the future. Your group won their test, after all. Didn’t they?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ve got a point. I’m probably overthinking things. Don’t worry.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Perhaps my feelings were nothing more than a premonition. But what if they came true? These might be our first faltering steps toward despair. But I also felt some alien emotion growing inside of me. It was something like excitement.</span></p>
