  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> FEW DAYS HAD ALREADY</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> passed since the start of the spring vacation. The zeal brought on by the End-of-Year Special Exam was nowhere to be seen, and many of the students, regardless of grade level or class, were likely enjoying their spring vacation to the fullest right about now. However, some classes had less to celebrate: Ichinose’s class, which had lost to Horikita’s class, and Sakayanagi’s class, which had lost to Ryuuen’s class.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">With only one year remaining, the students from both of those classes would need to carefully prepare for the battles ahead, steeling themselves for the challenges awaiting them rather than spending their spring vacation aimlessly. If they didn’t, their future prospects would likely be bleak. This was a particularly big problem for Sakayanagi’s class, who had only just learned that their leader was going to be withdrawing from school voluntarily. Once that fact had become public knowledge, I decided to call on certain people to meet up so that I could get a grasp of the situation in Class A.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Those certain two people were Morishita and Yamamura. The appointed time was ten o’clock in the morning, and the meeting place was near the dormitory.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">In other words, none other than this place, where I was standing right now. However, even after five, and then ten minutes of waiting, there was no sign of either of the people I was waiting for. I was sure that they hadn’t forgotten, as they had definitely read the message that I had sent them just after eight o’clock this morning. Even supposing that, hypothetically, something had happened, like one of them fell back asleep, it was hard to imagine that it would happen to both of them at the same time. I waited for another five minutes or so, but when neither showed, I tried calling Morishita. The phone rang, but she didn’t pick up.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“All right then. Guess I’ll try Yamamura,” I thought aloud.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I didn’t want to put too much mental strain on Yamamura, who was extremely shy, but I had no choice. I called her number even though I felt sorry for her. It started to ring, then I heard a panicked voice through the phone.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">“U-um sorry we’re late!”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Before I could say anything, I could hear her talking to someone else.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">“Huh? H-hang…the phone? B-but…time, it’s…late, so…um, I…”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Yamamura sounded flustered, her words choppy and scattered when I could hear them at all.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Is Morishita with you, by any chance?” I asked.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">“Y-yes. Um, well… Wait, come to the back side of the dormitory? Ah! My phone, don’t—”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">The call suddenly cut off what sounded like a bit of a struggle.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“What in the world?” I wondered aloud.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I didn’t really understand what that was all about, but she said something about behind the dormitory. Since I had at least heard that, I figured I’d head over there. It was only a two- or three-minute walk away.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> ARRIVED AT THE BACK</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> of the dormitory like Yamamura had mentioned. And when I did, I immediately spotted Yamamura looking flustered and panicking. Morishita was there too, down on the ground on all fours for some reason. The things she did were inexplicable, like that time at camp when she put her hand to a large tree and said something about the voice of the forest. Although that time, it was a lie, meant to tease me, so I couldn’t deny the possibility that this was just an extension of that kind of behavior. However, it was also true that Morishita was not a normal student…</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I figured that the only way to find out for sure was to talk to them directly.   <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“What are you doing here?” I asked.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">At my question, Morishita whipped her head back toward me and glared fiercely.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Shh! You’re too loud,” Morishita shushed me. “Please quiet down immediately. I am in the middle of observing insects.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I told you, observing insects,” she replied. “Have you become hard of hearing in the brief time that we have been apart from each other? Perhaps, Ayanokouji Kiyotaka, you are—”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">What she said after that was so vile that it’s probably best only heard by myself and Yamamura, who was holding her head in her hands.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It’s not that I didn’t hear you,” I corrected her. “It’s just that I couldn’t understand you.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">As for Morishita, she didn’t seem to care at all about things like our scheduled appointment.   <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Phew! Okay. However, if someone were to bring that topic up, it would only expose the fact that we had been piggybacking off of her,” said Morishita, acknowledging the undeniable fact that Class A’s status had been maintained by Sakayanagi carrying them. “We have no choice but to accept it.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I don’t know,” Yamamura answered with a shake of her head, as if to say she had absolutely no idea what the future might hold. “I think that we…we’ll probably have to give up on Class A.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“That is correct,” said Morishita. “Now that things have come to this, we will have to steer the class toward a policy of accumulating Private Points and send a few individuals to the official Class A by the end of the third year to finish things there. Because even students such as Yamamura Miki can be rescued if they are blessed with positive growth.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“S-students like me, wha…” Yamamura stammered, then went quiet as she thought it over. “No, I suppose I can’t deny that.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Although Yamamura accepted that comment, because of her low self-esteem, it sounded like it still hurt her a bit. Class A had maintained a large number of Class Points thus far, so they’d all probably be reasonably well-off financially. Besides, while the loss they suffered was significant, it was still possible for them to accumulate Private Points moving forward. It was a practical, businesslike decision, but one of the best moves they could make.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“But I’m sure that some students won’t give up so easily,” I remarked.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“That is a problem,” Morishita said. “I am sure there is a cheap pride over having maintained our position as Class A thus far, but we cannot keep dreaming forever. The Class Points that we lost due to our class’s defeat and then Sakayanagi Arisu’s voluntary withdrawal were far too painful. We must understand that.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Oh, that reminds me. What’s the penalty for voluntary withdrawal?” I asked. “We in the other classes haven’t been given that information.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Perhaps Sakayanagi’s classmates were deliberately withholding that information. Once April arrived, everyone would know regardless, but there was nothing to be gained by letting that information get out now.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Apparently, it varies depending on the circumstances,” Morishita replied. “But in this instance, Sakayanagi Arisu’s withdrawal was treated totally as a self-inflicted act, and so we suffered the heaviest penalty. We have lost three hundred Class Points.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">There was no guarantee that their penalty would be as I imagined, but it sounded like my expectations were spot-on. Morishita and her class were left with approximately eight hundred points, then. While it would appear that they could just barely stay in the fight, they had lost their leader. The emotional wound inflicted was more painful than the Class Points lost. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“The chances we have of being able to turn things around in the time we have left are—” said Morishita, before stopping abruptly.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Th-the chances are?” repeated Yamamura.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">Brrrrrrrrrrrrr</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">,” Morishita trilled in reply.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Huh?” </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“What? What is that face you’re making?” asked Morishita.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Well, uh…” Yamamura stammered. “I mean, did you just, uh, go ‘brrr’?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It is the sound of the Ai-chan Computer making calculations. Can’t you even understand that much, Yamamura Miki?” Morishita asked. “This is why people around you call you a pessimistic downer, or a gloomy person, or say that your presence is more transparent than a sheet of tracing paper.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“P-people say those things about me?” asked Yamamura.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“At the very least, I certainly do,” said Morishita.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I think that was completely Morishita’s personal opinion, not the voices of those around Yamamura, but that itself was only my personal opinion.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“At any rate, please do not disturb me. </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">Brrrrrrrr—DING</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">!”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">It seemed she had finished her calculations, because Morishita nodded once, with a “Yes,” and her eyes widened.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“The probability of our class becoming Class A with our own strength is approximately 10 percent,” reported Morishita.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I figured that some students might be surprised by those odds, not thinking that could be the case for a class that had been defending its position of Class A tooth and nail until the End-of-Year Special Exam, but in truth, even 10 percent was probably a naively optimistic estimate. That just showed how small a chance Morishita and her classmates had at present.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“As Paper-Thin Yamamura Miki herself has said earlier, everyone is in a dark place now, as though they were attending a vigil,” said Morishita.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I bet,” I replied. “It would be a pretty strange problem if the mood was light right now.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“U-um, excuse me, but Paper-Thin…?” sputtered Yamamura.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It’s cool, isn’t it? Like a street name,” said Morishita.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“H-huh…? B-but it doesn’t feel like that at all…” said Yamamura.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You wish for me to stop?” asked Morishita.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“If you could, then yes, please,” she replied. Surprisingly, even Yamamura seemed to care about such things. “B-but, even though Ichinose-san’s class has the same amount of Class Points, everyone has a smile on their face. It’s like, they’re all so positive, but is their situation really so different from ours?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I see. You are saying that things are much the same for them regardless of whether Ichinose Honami is even there. You spewed venom in the middle of your statement,” said Morishita, nodding with admiration and giving Yamamura a thumbs-up.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">As for Yamamura, she probably didn’t intend to imply such a thing, given that she hastily denied it, flustered.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Supposing we are hypothetically in the same situation, so what?” Morishita posed. “Are you saying that because they have a positive attitude, there is a possibility they’ll reach Class A? Let me be clear. An incompetent ally is more terrifying than a competent adversary. If you ask me, there is no tomorrow for those who are still thinking in such a flippant manner in this situation.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Yamamura recoiled in the face of Morishita’s unceasingly, aggressive verbal assault, but Morishita was speaking the truth. Right now, you could say that those who could make normal judgments were indispensable to the class.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“What are you going to do now?” I asked.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I thought that as long as Sakayanagi Arisu stood at the helm and gave the orders, I wouldn’t need to meaninglessly stand out, but…” Morishita trailed off with a somber expression, looking like she was brooding, lost in thought. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“By any chance, are you saying that you’ll lead the class from now on, Morishita-san?” murmured Yamamura, bracing herself for the answer.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“That is impossible,” said Morishita.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I-it’s impossible?” repeated Yamamura.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Unfortunately, I am not the type to lead people from the forefront,” answered Morishita. “Which is why, though it will be bothersome, we will need to select a new leader during the spring vacation. If possible, I would have liked for that to have all been completed before Sakayanagi Arisu leaves the school. All I can do now is offer guidance in that process and, afterward, guide the conversation toward saving Private Points.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">It was certainly true that someone needed to be cold and get the others to give up on Class A. It sounded like Morishita was willing to handle that much. Just then, Morishita glanced at me disapprovingly.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I think that is enough idle chitchat,” she said. “I think it is about time that you tell me the reason you have called us here today.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">She said that like I was the one who had wasted a lot of time here, even though we had spent most of the conversation talking about her insect observation and the decline of her shield shrimp.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You didn’t call us here to mock us over our present situation, did you? Ayanokouji Kiyotaka, of the new Class A,” said Morishita.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I’m not trying to mock you. I was just curious about that situation is all,” I replied.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“If that is the case, then you could simply ask the traitor,” Morishita said. “I’m sure he will tell you everything. He might even blab about the things you do not need to know.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“U-um, Morishita-san? Uh, isn’t ‘traitor’ a little harsh?” asked Yamamura.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Forgive my rudeness. It was a mere slip of the tongue. At any rate, our current situation is just as I told you moments ago,” said Morishita.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Seems that way,” I answered.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Although Morishita marched to the beat of her own drum, she seemed to be giving her class due consideration. However, it sounded as though Sakayanagi hadn’t taken any kind of action for her classmates, who were currently lost. Although most of the conversation we just had was irrelevant, I could call it successful since I had fulfilled my minimum required objective of gathering information.</span></p>
