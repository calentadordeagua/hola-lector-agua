  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> FEW DAYS AFTER</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> that promised day, when Ayanokouji and Ichinose had bridged the gap between them, their spring vacation was coming to an end. It was early April at Keyaki Mall, and six students from Ichinose’s class were gathered near the entrance to the café with confused expressions. A sudden message from Ichinose, who had been holed up in her room and hadn’t met with or talked to anyone, had announced that she would be coming back.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">It was sent to the whole class’s group chat, and the message was immediately received rather enthusiastically by the peers who were hoping for her recovery. Ichinose apologized first and foremost for not returning their calls and messages, and she said that she wanted them to hear something she had to say, as she had formulated her thoughts on their future plans. A number of students had wanted to participate in that meeting, but it was none other than Kanzaki who immediately shot them down.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">He suggested that the situation ought to be discussed by a small number of people, considering that they didn’t even have a grasp on the current situation and the responsibility implied. Kanzaki then selected the participants, and no one raised any objections, not even Ichinose. For better or for worse, the class was still gladly willing to accept positive and thoughtful suggestions. The members called by Kanzaki included Shibata, Hamaguchi, Amikura, Himeno, Shiranami, and Watanabe. Among the seven members, there were some that made classmates wonder at their selection, and feel like something might be amiss.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Some of the classmates included were central figures in the class, but others were not. This was because, even though Kanzaki included people who were close to Ichinose, he did so as a way to deliberately throw her off the scent of including Hamaguchi and Himeno, who would agree with his opinions. Even if, hypothetically, it came to a situation where a majority vote was to be taken during the discussion, Kanzaki believed that he would be able to secure at least three votes for his own views by doing this. Since Kanzaki didn’t know Ichinose’s current condition, he intended to be fully prepared to throw down the gauntlet. Kanzaki met up with Shibata on the way there, and they arrived at the café together.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Shibata-kun, Kanzaki-kun, good morning,” said Amikura.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Amikura, a female student who often spent her weekdays and days off together with Ichinose, and was close enough to her that you could describe her as Ichinose’s best friend, had been the first to arrive. She greeted Kanzaki and Shibata as soon as they appeared.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Oh, hey, Amikura, mornin’. Man, sure am glad though, right, Amikura?” asked Shibata. “About getting a message from Ichinose.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Yeah, seriously!” Amikura replied. “I literally almost cried a little when I got the message.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Ichinose hadn’t met with anyone since their class had lost the End-of-Year Special Exam. Even when her best friend Amikura went to pay Ichinose a visit in person, she was turned away at the door and with the explanation that it was hard to talk to anyone at the moment. Other people in class continued to encourage Ichinose with messages, but they wondered if perhaps their texts were actually having the opposite effect. Everyone was wondering if they could come up with a better way to help get Ichinose back on her feet somehow, but no one came up with a solution.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Still, I haven’t actually seen her face yet though, so I can’t really say that I’m relieved or anything,” Amikura said. “It sounds like she definitely went to her parent–teacher meeting, but I wonder if she’ll really be able to appear in front of everybody. Is Honami-chan really going to be okay?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Amikura feared the possibility that this meeting was going to wind up amounting to nothing. The fact that Ichinose carried the responsibility for her class losing the End-of-Year Special Exam solely on her own shoulders was still fresh in Amikura’s mind. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Nah. If she said she’ll come, she’ll come,” Shibata said without hesitation, having unshakable confidence in his classmate. “Ichinose is </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">not</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> an irresponsible person.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“That’s true, but…she’s hardly set foot outside her room,” said Amikura.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">She felt that it would be understandable if Ichinose couldn’t muster the courage to appear in front of everyone.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Well, supposin’ that does happen, we can just overlook it. Be forgiving and all, yeah,” said Shibata.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“That won’t solve anything,” snapped Kanzaki, breaking his silence to speak out angrily.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“What do you mean?” said Shibata. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Our defeat in the End-of-Year Special Exam spelled the difference between light and dark for our class.   <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Wait, hold on though. Isn’t…” Shibata hesitated as he tried to line his words up. “Uh…doesn’t Ayanokouji already got a girlfriend?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Shibata-kun… Look, um, it’s kinda hard for me to tell you this, but…” intervened Amikura, not wanting to dash his hopes, but deciding that it would be best to come out and say it immediately. “I heard that Ayanokouji-kun and Karuizawa-san broke up recently.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Shibata flopped down on the desk and started twitching slightly.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“At any rate, you should all understand things well enough now. That there’s no chance for us to win anymore,” declared Kanzaki. He’d been quietly listening until this point, even when the comments turned more toward gossip, but now it felt like he had a chance to make his classmates accept a different strategy. He had to change the course of the conversation to make them comply with an overall change of course for the class. “Now is precisely the time we should be moving in the direction of collecting Private Points.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">He wanted them to give up on Class A as a whole, and shift completely toward accumulating Private Points. Ichinose had once released the funds that she had been pooling on behalf of her classmates, but they had all begun saving again. If they were to increase the percentage of how much they put into their savings to the uppermost limit and all exhibited self-restraint, they could afford a few tickets to Class A.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“So you really think it’d be better for us to just throw away the idea of winning, Kanzaki?” asked Watanabe.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Sorry, but I wasn’t asking you for your opinion right now, Watanabe.” said Kanzaki, having asserted that an optimistic strategy would only delay the inevitable here. “Answer me, Ichinose.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It’s certainly true that if it’s difficult to reach Class A, relying on Private Points is the proper way to do it,” Ichinose replied. “I don’t think it’s a bad idea. In fact, I would even bet that some of the former Class A students have been considering that option several times now.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">When we become third-years, it’ll be a one-on-one battle between Horikita’s class and Ryuuen’s class.</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> More and more students were beginning to accept this situation.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“However, I think that this class of ours can still fight. We can still aim for Class A,” added Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“That’s impossible,” argued Kanzaki. “I cannot imagine that you’ve actually done the job of analyzing the competition, in any way.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Hey, come on, Kanzaki. Even if it’ll be tough to catch up like you’ve been saying, it’s not a terrible thing for us to shoot for it,” Watanabe piped up again despite Kanzaki shutting him out, perhaps inspired by Ichinose’s apparent certainty. “It can make all the difference for your motivation if you got a goal that you can really go for with everything you’ve got, you know?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“That would be inefficient,” countered Kanzaki, unable to accept an argument without evidence. “You’re certainly free to aim, and you’re free to aspire, but that freedom naturally comes at a price.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I don’t get it. What price is there?” asked Watanabe.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I can think of many ways in which there would be a price,” Kanzaki answered. “For example, if we needed to increase our assets in order to win an exam, spending Private Points would be the key to victory in that scenario. It’s not just limited to that kind of thing either. It could be to gather information or to avoid some kind of penalty. Or, what if a student in our class was in danger of being expelled? Would you have another twenty million points ready to go every time something like that happened?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">He was proposing a strategy of not wasting any Private Points, of reducing their consumption of points by ignoring the majority of the competition.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Wait, not only are you giving up on Class A, but you’re even abandoning your friends?!” exclaimed Watanabe.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It’s necessary for everyone in the class to have that approach, not just me,” said Kanzaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Hold on, everyone, that’s…” sputtered Watanabe.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Kanzaki, after having gotten Watanabe to quiet down, took the opportunity to stay on the attack.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Himeno, Hamaguchi,” Kanzaki addressed them directly, hoping to gain allies. “What do you think?” </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Well…” said Hamaguchi, hesitating. “To be honest, I also feel that everyone should give the idea some consideration, yeah.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Me too, I think…saving Private Points isn’t a bad idea at all,” Himeno added.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“So, how about that?” said Kanzaki. “I’m not the only one. There are at least some students in our class who feel the same way.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Wait, hold up. Are you guys all insane or something?” Shibata pulled his face up off of the desk and looked around. His eyes were wet at the corners, but he just couldn’t let Kanzaki’s suggested approach win out without any resistance. “Don’t talk stupid. You sayin’ that we sacrifice our friends so that one or two people can transfer to Class A and reach the goal? No way, bruh. Absolutely not. Our plan for the whole class winnin’ or losin’ together should </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">not</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> be changed. No way.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I think so too,” said Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Y-yeah…Ichinose…” replied Shibata.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Even though she was speaking up in agreement, seeing her warm smile made Shibata feel like his heart was being gouged out all over again, and so he flopped back down on his desk.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Enough already,” spat Kanzaki. “That’s all just wishful thinking.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You’re right, it certainly is wishful thinking,” said Ichinose without hesitation. “We have been able to fight our way to this point without anyone getting expelled. That is undeniably a strength, but we can’t deny the fact that it’s come at a cost as well, as we’ve continued to lose Class Points in exchange. Regardless, that wishful thinking may also bear fruit.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Ichinose answered with confidence, but of course, Kanzaki saw absolutely nothing of her clear vision. To him, it sounded simply like she was talking hypothetically, about her dreams and ideals.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Once again, I want us to graduate from Class A together, without losing anyone,” said Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You do realize that’s impossible, don’t you?” said Kanzaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“No,” Ichinose replied firmly. “It’s not impossible.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I’m sorry, but I cannot trust you whatsoever,” said Kanzaki. “Yes, I may recognize that you have recovered mentally from the exam, but there is absolutely no way I could be convinced by such a frivolous, careless statement, that we can make it to Class A from here.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Yes, I suppose not. I need to stop thinking that I’ll just resolve it with words, just by saying things like ‘Hey, it’s okay.’ It is true that over these past two years, we’ve fallen from Class B to Class D, after all,” said Ichinose with a nod at Kanzaki to acknowledge that his pointed remark still had a reasonable point. “Regardless, I was hoping that I could ask you to hold on until spring vacation is over.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Can I interpret that as meaning that you’ll prove it to me then?” said Kanzaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Yes. If you’re not satisfied with the answer at that time, then I will proactively consider your proposal, Kanzaki-kun,” said Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">If Ichinose agreed to lean toward accumulating Private Points, rather than trying to win in the class rankings, then even the classmates that disagreed with the method would likely follow. Just a few more days. Time was precious, but Kanzaki decided that it was worth it just for her to accept his conditions.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You will stand by that statement, right?” he asked.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I will. I have neither the qualifications nor the right to say that I will absolutely make us graduate from Class A without fail, but I promise. I promise that I will lift the class up to a state where we can compete for Class A,” said Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Ichinose looked to her gathered classmates for confirmation. Though they were all puzzled, they nodded to indicate that they would hold off on any conclusions for the moment. From that point on, Ichinose really was her usual self, sounding happy at times and sad at times, as she told them about the events of the past two weeks or so. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Shibata wanted to ask about Ayanokouji the whole time, but in the end, he couldn’t say a single word about it. Perhaps unsurprisingly, he found it hard to ask about something so heavy in front of the other students. He was sure that nothing had really happened between them yet, and held onto that hope as he decided to wait for his moment.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">The warm, gentle times of Ichinose’s class had returned. Though Kanzaki was looking at her coldly, he endured it because he had agreed to wait a few days. Then, when he got up from his seat to go use the restroom, Ichinose followed after him.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Kanzaki-kun?” she asked.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“…What?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Don’t you think you have something to report to me?” she asked.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Report?” he repeated as he stopped to turn around and eye her suspiciously, unsure what she was talking about.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I believe that I am responsible for the results of the End-of-Year Special Exam,” said Ichinose. “But, Kanzaki-kun, do you have any explanation for what happened with you? About whether you fought in a way that was appropriate, as a representative?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You’re saying that there was a problem with the way I fought?” Kanzaki asked. “I fought hard against Horikita, and I lost. It’s frustrating, but that’s the truth.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Did you decide before the exam that you were going to beg your opponent to hand over the win?” she asked.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Uh…” he stammered.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You had given up on winning from the start, Kanzaki-kun,” Ichinose continued. “I wish you would have at least talked to me.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Well, that’s… Did you hear about that from Horikita? Or perhaps—”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It’s not important who I heard it from,” replied Ichinose, cutting him off.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“N-no, I suppose not. It’s just…that was the only plan I could come up with to win. I didn’t care about how it looked. I had no other choice. At the time, I just decided that it was what I had to do, and…”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">He had been making eye contact as he tried to explain himself with dignity, but his breath caught in his throat; he was choking. She looked so different from how she had even mere moments earlier, when she had been listening to what her classmates had to say.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I did ask them to hand over the win… If word got out about that, out in front of Shibata and the other students, then it would definitely lower morale. That’s why I didn’t say anything,” said Kanzaki desperately, trying to make excuses.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Because we couldn’t beat Ayanokouji-kun. Because that was the conclusion you reached, right?” said Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Well, that’s—”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Her strong gaze took a tight hold of Kanzaki’s true feelings, his conscience.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I understand that anxiety,” said Ichinose. “I also understand the pain of not having anyone who understands. It’s okay now, though.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">What do you mean, okay?</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> Kanzaki didn’t have the courage to voice that question.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Forgive me,” he said instead. “It is true that I fought in a way that was not commendable.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“If you’re ever struggling, you can come talk to me any time. I’ll stand by you when you’re feeling discouraged,” said Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">They were kind words, but Kanzaki only felt a chill run up his spine. That was because he was hearing them as a warning, telling him to never do anything selfish again from here on out. Ichinose turned around and then went back to their classmates, and when she sat down in her seat, the look on her face was no different from usual.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Was that…really Ichinose…?” wondered Kanzaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">She was different from when she appeared wasted away and exhausted, right after the exam ended, but also different from when she exhibited moments of confidence before an exam. There was something odd, something he couldn’t quite grasp.</span></p>
