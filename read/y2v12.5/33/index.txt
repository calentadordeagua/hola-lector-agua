So, after politely announcing that enough was enough, I proceeded to forcibly move Ishizaki’s hand away, which allowed Hiyori and I to pull our hands apart. I loosened my grip on Hiyori’s hand so that I could let go, but for some reason, she put a little more force into her grip now than before, when we were being forcefully brought together.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“…Hiyori?” I asked.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Um, may I just say one thing? I thought that I would ask, since trying couldn’t do any harm,” she said as she gazed up at me with what seemed like a determined expression.</span></p>

It seemed like, if I didn’t have to think about anything serious, a transfer to Ryuuen’s class would be the most fun. Not getting involved in the business of class wins and losses, just spending the last year surrounded by good friends. Even Ishizaki and Hiyori standing before me had a good chance of winning. The same was true for Horikita’s class, and hopefully, the two other classes that had been pushed down from the top would be able to do the same. That’s what I wanted.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I wanted to have the fight continue, leaving every class with a chance. What I needed for that was hope. If there was hope, the students would be able to push forward with all their strength until the end. That was precisely why I needed to build things up, to make that a reality. How could I keep them competing?</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">The answer to that would soon be clear…</span></p>

  <p class="P_Chapter_Header"><span>10.2</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">FTER I POLITELY </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">declined the invitations from Ishizaki and Hiyori, the three of us talked for a while rather than just going our separate ways right away. The rejection must’ve been a shock to them, but they didn’t touch on the topic of transferring classes after that, and they seemed to genuinely enjoy just chatting about nothing. They invited me to join them again for tea once Keyaki Mall opened, but I regretfully declined the offer, as I had other business today. We split up and I returned to the dormitory at nine o’clock that morning, but I still hadn’t gotten any word from Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Then, I headed to Keyaki Mall at 10 a.m. as planned, met up with the person I had arranged to meet, and afterward spent some time together with them. By the time I returned to my room, it was past 2 p.m., and I nonchalantly passed the time for the next couple of hours. I didn’t send any additional messages or make any phone calls; I simply continued to wait in my room for a response. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">How was I going to spend this day? The right to choose was Ichinose’s, because this was the day that would determine her fate. I wanted to leave the final move for Ichinose to make, not me. I decided to take care of cleaning my room and doing some light organizing. Then, I prepared dinner and finished eating quietly, alone. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">By the time that curfew rolled around at eight o’clock, there was still no indication of any response coming my way. I saw that my messages were still not read. Even so, a final decision wasn’t made, even as the curfew arrived. The reason being that, while it was forbidden for me to visit the floor where the girls lived, the rules still allowed Ichinose to come and visit me herself. Of course, that wasn’t actually explicitly stated in writing, and in truth, if Ichinose was seen heading to the room of someone of the opposite sex in the middle of the night, she’d receive a warning. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Well, actually, from my time spent dating Karuizawa, I knew that all that stuff was kind of a gray area, as things like matters of prudence or the restrictions placed on men and women were formalities. There were four hours to go until the promised day was over, so I leisurely took a shower and then enjoyed some television. The morning news had been quite interesting, but I still couldn’t get into the late-night variety shows, and honestly, I just couldn’t enjoy them. By the time I finished brushing my teeth, it was past nine o’clock.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Less than three hours to go,” I muttered.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">For students who were early risers, this was the time they’d probably be getting ready for bed. However, the chances that Ichinose was already asleep were about as close to 0 percent as you could possibly get. She was definitely awake and racking her brain. Time passed, and it was getting later and later. By the time 9:30 p.m. came and went, I could hear the soft sound of raindrops hitting my window, but when I gently opened the window to take a look, the rain turned heavy in the blink of an eye.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Come to think of it, I recalled that today’s forecast called for heavy rains to continue through the night, until dawn. Even when I closed the window, the sound of the rain reverberated quite loudly inside my room, and the rainfall was likely to get heavier still. It was now nearly ten o’clock. Everything that I needed to take care of in my room today had been completed. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">So, at last, just two hours left until the day ended, huh?</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I turned on my phone and checked my messages to Ichinose. There was no reply. However, I saw that something vital was different now about the messages I had sent.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">“I’ll be out until three this afternoon, but I want you to come to my room any time you like after that.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">“Get in touch with me once you see this.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I saw that both of those messages, which I had sent to her in succession, were now marked as read. I knew it. Ichinose was awake after all, and she had read my messages. I then waited another ten, then twenty minutes, for her to respond.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">However, she wasn’t getting in touch with me.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Ichinose was free to alter the promised day, but her answer was…not to answer? Well, I supposed that all things considered, it was possible for her to respond that way, but honestly, I had thought she would take some kind of action. Had I simply been expecting too much? If she had chosen silence, that meant that the decision that needed to be made was already provided. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I figured I should conclude that I had given Ichinose enough time to think about it.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">However…</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I slowly got up, lifting my hips off of the bed I had been sitting on. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">I want to check with her directly, in person, after all.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">For the past year, I have been continuously influencing Ichinose for my own sake, so it was only natural for me to want to see the results. It was fine to reject it, but I just wanted to know how she was. That desire naturally welled up in me.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">Even if it means making a compromise on my side at the very last minute…let’s go and find out the answer.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Chapter_Header"><span>10.3</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;">O</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">NCE I MADE THE DECISION </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">to check for myself at 10:30 p.m., I made the perilous journey. Fortunately, it was raining heavily outside, and I couldn’t detect any signs of anyone out and about, not even in the hallways. Making my way from the hallway to the emergency stairwell, I reached just outside of Ichinose’s room. Then, I pressed the doorbell, trying to call for her, as she must have been inside. From the other side of the door, I could hear the sound of the doorbell echoing faintly. However, even after waiting quite a while, there was no answer.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Though she had no way to know that I was the one ringing her bell, I figured that was an indication that she wasn’t meeting with anyone right now, no matter who they were. I proceeded to take my phone out of my pocket and pressed the call button. She must have kept her phone on after she read my messages earlier, because the call went through. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Five rings, six rings…</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Even after ten rings, Ichinose didn’t answer the phone. I pressed the button to end the call and quietly knocked on her door.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It’s me. The day I promised to meet you is almost over. So, I’m here,” I told her.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I told her that with my own voice, not through my phone. Even accounting for the heavy rain outside, it was naturally a substantial risk for me to have spoken aloud here. If other girls discovered that I was paying Ichinose a visit, though there may not have been an immediate uproar, whoever found me would be obligated to report the offender, and I would be held accountable. Which was why I wasn’t going to repeat the act of verbally calling out to her over and over, without end. If Ichinose still didn’t respond even after this, I didn’t intend to pursue it any further.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Even though I most definitely wanted to know the answer, to see it with my own eyes, I just couldn’t take on such an elevated risk. I would just have to accept that this was Ichinose’s answer, then. The other side of the door remained silent, even now.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I’ll wait for just three minutes. If I don’t hear back from you by then, I’ll leave. So don’t worry,” I added.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">I quietly started counting down from 180 seconds. During that time, I cast my gaze at the heavy rain behind me. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Fifty seconds, forty seconds…</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">The remaining time was running out. Then, just when there were only thirty seconds remaining, something happened. My phone vibrated slightly, because I had received a message.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">“Why’d you come?”</span></p>

Ayanokouji had helped him out on several occasions as well, and Kanzaki even recognized him as someone that he could rely on when they were together. However, Ayanokouji had stood against their class as an obvious enemy during the End-of-Year Special Exam. He had defeated Ichinose and struck the class down into despair. Even if Kanzaki had some half-hearted cognizance of companionship, it wasn’t going to lead to anything good. Kanzaki had come to learn that the hard way.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Saved by Ayanokouji, huh?” Kanzaki said. “I hope that’s a lie, but if it’s not, that is a huge problem. Now I doubt that you were even really ‘saved’ at all.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Kanzaki was condemning Ichinose so thoroughly and completely that Shibata and the others couldn’t even be angry with him, only exasperated.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Look, Kanzaki, bruh,” Shibata jumped in. “If Ichinose says that Ayanokouji saved her, let’s just be grateful for that.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You don’t understand,” Kanzaki dismissed his words entirely. “You don’t know how dangerous a man Ayanokouji is.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">At this point, Kanzaki hesitated over whether to reveal all of the information he had. Namely, that Ayanokouji was the son of the respected Ayanokouji Atsuomi, and that Ayanokouji himself was highly skilled; it was just that he wasn’t allowing those around him to see it. Right now, in detail—</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Kanzaki-kun.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“…What?” he asked after a brief silence.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Before we discuss that, I was wondering if we could talk about something else for a minute,” said Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Kanzaki was momentarily thrown off by Ichinose’s words, which seemed to anticipate his thoughts. It wasn’t like she was intimidating him or threatening him at all. It was just that her tone of voice and her expression somehow made him speechless, even if they weren’t any different from her usual.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It was because of my own weakness that I lost the End-of-Year Special Exam. Because, by the nature of the exam, I had to fight equally against my opponent, no matter who they were,” said Ichinose, presenting her friends with a summary of her reflections since the exam had ended. “I lost to Ayanokouji-kun because I lost sight of the essence of competition. I want you all to know what kind of state of mind I was in at the time.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">She tried to find the words to express something that she hadn’t said until now, not even to herself.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I… Since who knows when, I’ve been in love with Ayanokouji-kun,” said Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Ichinose stated her feelings for her classmates to hear, without blushing and without panicking. Shibata was the most surprised of all, his mouth hanging open silently. Ichinose continued, without paying any mind to that or any other reactions.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“At the time, I believed that Ayanokouji-kun was thinking about me, without any doubt. However, as soon as the exam started, those shallow, foolish assumptions of mine were shattered,” she said.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Ayanokouji had proposed to Ichinose that they used their traitor rights, so that no one would be expelled from their classes. By taking advantage of that, Ayanokouji had made Ichinose party to Maezono’s expulsion. Ichinose’s classmates listened expectantly as they heard for the very first time about the battle between representatives. At the same time, they were hearing about Ayanokouji’s true nature, which Kanzaki had wanted them to know. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">An unassuming student who didn’t stand out very much</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">. That was a fabrication. He was a student who was extremely calm and composed, and who could make precise judgments under pressure. Ichinose had wanted everyone to hear about Ayanokouji’s greatness from her, rather than from Kanzaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Is Ayanokouji-kun…really so incredible of a student?” asked Himeno haltingly.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">At that question, Ichinose nodded with a happy squint in her eyes, as though one of her own was being praised. Himeno too had sensed that Ayanokouji was no ordinary student, but she couldn’t fully grasp it yet, and there was a big gap in understanding.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I think that it was only after the exam that I really, truly understood him myself</span><span class="C_Current__And__Properties__And__Times_New_Roman">,</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">” said Ichinose. “While it’s not like there was only one factor that made it possible for Horikita-san’s class to move up to Class A, I think it was something that they definitely wouldn’t have accomplished without Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">That is to say, it wasn’t Horikita’s class. It was Ayanokouji’s class. What she said practically amounted to saying those exact words.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“W-whoa, uh, yeah, man,” Shibata stammered. “For real, so Ayanokouji is that amazing of a dude, huh? Uh, well…I never really had that much contact with the guy, so I guess I never really thought too much ’bout it. Or like, uh, I thought, uh, that, like, your feelings were more like respect for the guy rather than you liking him, though.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">In a bit of a panic, Shibata tried to refute what Ichinose said, suggesting that she simply wasn’t using the right choice of words in expressing her thoughts, or that it was a misunderstanding.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I do have respect for him too, of course,” Ichinose agreed. “But more than that, I love Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Even though Ichinose answered Shibata proudly and openly, she felt herself flush. That was not because her contact with Ayanokouji had been physical, but rather because for a moment, she had been able to touch the surface of Ayanokouji’s heart, which perhaps even Karuizawa hadn’t been able to see. </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Whoa, that’s incredible… I mean, for you of all people to say all that, Ichinose, it’s… Man, how did </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">that</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> happen?” said Watanabe, astonished.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Watanabe couldn’t imagine that her feelings were unreciprocated if she was announcing it like this, and cast a fleeting glance at his own crush, Amikura. Meanwhile, Amikura was happy to see that Ichinose had grown to the point where she could clearly state that she loved someone, and came to the conclusion that something must have happened in the process of Ichinose asking Ayanokouji for advice. Maybe they were in a relationship now, maybe even beyond what she was imagining.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Wait, hold on though. Isn’t…” Shibata hesitated as he tried to line his words up. “Uh…doesn’t Ayanokouji already got a girlfriend?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Shibata-kun… Look, um, it’s kinda hard for me to tell you this, but…” intervened Amikura, not wanting to dash his hopes, but deciding that it would be best to come out and say it immediately. “I heard that Ayanokouji-kun and Karuizawa-san broke up recently.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Shibata flopped down on the desk and started twitching slightly.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“At any rate, you should all understand things well enough now. That there’s no chance for us to win anymore,” declared Kanzaki. He’d been quietly listening until this point, even when the comments turned more toward gossip, but now it felt like he had a chance to make his classmates accept a different strategy. He had to change the course of the conversation to make them comply with an overall change of course for the class. “Now is precisely the time we should be moving in the direction of collecting Private Points.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">He wanted them to give up on Class A as a whole, and shift completely toward accumulating Private Points. Ichinose had once released the funds that she had been pooling on behalf of her classmates, but they had all begun saving again. If they were to increase the percentage of how much they put into their savings to the uppermost limit and all exhibited self-restraint, they could afford a few tickets to Class A.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“So you really think it’d be better for us to just throw away the idea of winning, Kanzaki?” asked Watanabe.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Sorry, but I wasn’t asking you for your opinion right now, Watanabe.” said Kanzaki, having asserted that an optimistic strategy would only delay the inevitable here. “Answer me, Ichinose.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It’s certainly true that if it’s difficult to reach Class A, relying on Private Points is the proper way to do it,” Ichinose replied. “I don’t think it’s a bad idea. In fact, I would even bet that some of the former Class A students have been considering that option several times now.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">When we become third-years, it’ll be a one-on-one battle between Horikita’s class and Ryuuen’s class.</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> More and more students were beginning to accept this situation.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“However, I think that this class of ours can still fight. We can still aim for Class A,” added Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“That’s impossible,” argued Kanzaki. “I cannot imagine that you’ve actually done the job of analyzing the competition, in any way.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Hey, come on, Kanzaki. Even if it’ll be tough to catch up like you’ve been saying, it’s not a terrible thing for us to shoot for it,” Watanabe piped up again despite Kanzaki shutting him out, perhaps inspired by Ichinose’s apparent certainty. “It can make all the difference for your motivation if you got a goal that you can really go for with everything you’ve got, you know?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“That would be inefficient,” countered Kanzaki, unable to accept an argument without evidence. “You’re certainly free to aim, and you’re free to aspire, but that freedom naturally comes at a price.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I don’t get it. What price is there?” asked Watanabe.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I can think of many ways in which there would be a price,” Kanzaki answered. “For example, if we needed to increase our assets in order to win an exam, spending Private Points would be the key to victory in that scenario. It’s not just limited to that kind of thing either. It could be to gather information or to avoid some kind of penalty. Or, what if a student in our class was in danger of being expelled? Would you have another twenty million points ready to go every time something like that happened?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">He was proposing a strategy of not wasting any Private Points, of reducing their consumption of points by ignoring the majority of the competition.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Wait, not only are you giving up on Class A, but you’re even abandoning your friends?!” exclaimed Watanabe.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It’s necessary for everyone in the class to have that approach, not just me,” said Kanzaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Hold on, everyone, that’s…” sputtered Watanabe.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Kanzaki, after having gotten Watanabe to quiet down, took the opportunity to stay on the attack.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Himeno, Hamaguchi,” Kanzaki addressed them directly, hoping to gain allies. “What do you think?” </span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Well…” said Hamaguchi, hesitating. “To be honest, I also feel that everyone should give the idea some consideration, yeah.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Me too, I think…saving Private Points isn’t a bad idea at all,” Himeno added.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“So, how about that?” said Kanzaki. “I’m not the only one. There are at least some students in our class who feel the same way.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Wait, hold up. Are you guys all insane or something?” Shibata pulled his face up off of the desk and looked around. His eyes were wet at the corners, but he just couldn’t let Kanzaki’s suggested approach win out without any resistance. “Don’t talk stupid. You sayin’ that we sacrifice our friends so that one or two people can transfer to Class A and reach the goal? No way, bruh. Absolutely not. Our plan for the whole class winnin’ or losin’ together should </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">not</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> be changed. No way.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I think so too,” said Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Y-yeah…Ichinose…” replied Shibata.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Even though she was speaking up in agreement, seeing her warm smile made Shibata feel like his heart was being gouged out all over again, and so he flopped back down on his desk.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Enough already,” spat Kanzaki. “That’s all just wishful thinking.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You’re right, it certainly is wishful thinking,” said Ichinose without hesitation. “We have been able to fight our way to this point without anyone getting expelled. That is undeniably a strength, but we can’t deny the fact that it’s come at a cost as well, as we’ve continued to lose Class Points in exchange. Regardless, that wishful thinking may also bear fruit.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Ichinose answered with confidence, but of course, Kanzaki saw absolutely nothing of her clear vision. To him, it sounded simply like she was talking hypothetically, about her dreams and ideals.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Once again, I want us to graduate from Class A together, without losing anyone,” said Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You do realize that’s impossible, don’t you?” said Kanzaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“No,” Ichinose replied firmly. “It’s not impossible.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I’m sorry, but I cannot trust you whatsoever,” said Kanzaki. “Yes, I may recognize that you have recovered mentally from the exam, but there is absolutely no way I could be convinced by such a frivolous, careless statement, that we can make it to Class A from here.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Yes, I suppose not. I need to stop thinking that I’ll just resolve it with words, just by saying things like ‘Hey, it’s okay.’ It is true that over these past two years, we’ve fallen from Class B to Class D, after all,” said Ichinose with a nod at Kanzaki to acknowledge that his pointed remark still had a reasonable point. “Regardless, I was hoping that I could ask you to hold on until spring vacation is over.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Can I interpret that as meaning that you’ll prove it to me then?” said Kanzaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Yes. If you’re not satisfied with the answer at that time, then I will proactively consider your proposal, Kanzaki-kun,” said Ichinose.</span></p>

Just a few more days. Time was precious, but Kanzaki decided that it was worth it just for her to accept his conditions.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You will stand by that statement, right?” he asked.</span></p>

I have neither the qualifications nor the right to say that I will absolutely make us graduate from Class A without fail, but I promise. I promise that I will lift the class up to a state where we can compete for Class A,” said Ichinose.</span></p>

Though they were all puzzled, they nodded to indicate that they would hold off on any conclusions for the moment. From that point on, Ichinose really was her usual self, sounding happy at times and sad at times, as she told them about the events of the past two weeks or so. </span></p>

Perhaps unsurprisingly, he found it hard to ask about something so heavy in front of the other students. He was sure that nothing had really happened between them yet, and held onto that hope as he decided to wait for his moment.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Don’t you think you have something to report to me?” she asked.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Report?” he repeated as he stopped to turn around and eye her suspiciously, unsure what she was talking about.</span></p>

About whether you fought in a way that was appropriate, as a representative?”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You’re saying that there was a problem with the way I fought?” Kanzaki asked. “I fought hard against Horikita, and I lost. It’s frustrating, but that’s the truth.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Did you decide before the exam that you were going to beg your opponent to hand over the win?” she asked.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Uh…” he stammered.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“You had given up on winning from the start, Kanzaki-kun,” Ichinose continued. “I wish you would have at least talked to me.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Well, that’s… Did you hear about that from Horikita? Or perhaps—”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“It’s not important who I heard it from,” replied Ichinose, cutting him off.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“N-no, I suppose not. It’s just…that was the only plan I could come up with to win. I didn’t care about how it looked. I had no other choice. At the time, I just decided that it was what I had to do, and…”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">He had been making eye contact as he tried to explain himself with dignity, but his breath caught in his throat; he was choking. She looked so different from how she had even mere moments earlier, when she had been listening to what her classmates had to say.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I did ask them to hand over the win… If word got out about that, out in front of Shibata and the other students, then it would definitely lower morale. That’s why I didn’t say anything,” said Kanzaki desperately, trying to make excuses.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Because we couldn’t beat Ayanokouji-kun. Because that was the conclusion you reached, right?” said Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Well, that’s—”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">Her strong gaze took a tight hold of Kanzaki’s true feelings, his conscience.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“I understand that anxiety,” said Ichinose. “I also understand the pain of not having anyone who understands. It’s okay now, though.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic">What do you mean, okay?</span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"> Kanzaki didn’t have the courage to voice that question.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Forgive me,” he said instead. “It is true that I fought in a way that was not commendable.”</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“If you’re ever struggling, you can come talk to me any time. I’ll stand by you when you’re feeling discouraged,” said Ichinose.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">They were kind words, but Kanzaki only felt a chill run up his spine. That was because he was hearing them as a warning, telling him to never do anything selfish again from here on out. Ichinose turned around and then went back to their classmates, and when she sat down in her seat, the look on her face was no different from usual.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">“Was that…really Ichinose…?” wondered Kanzaki.</span></p>

  <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman">She was different from when she appeared wasted away and exhausted, right after the exam ended, but also different from when she exhibited moments of confidence before an exam.
