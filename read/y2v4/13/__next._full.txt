<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 7:<br/>The Man Called Tsukishiro</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HAT MORNING</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I woke up somewhere along the right edge of area E3. I went to check the map on my tablet. As a result of avoiding the first-years’ assault all day yesterday, I hadn’t set foot in even a single designated area the entire day. Although Sakayanagi had informed me that the first-years had withdrawn in the afternoon, I still didn’t dare head to my designated areas after that. Instead, I just participated in Tasks that appeared along my escape route and scored the bare minimum level of points needed for me to continue. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The one o’clock designated area yesterday had been randomly assigned and was in area F3. The designated area after that was in G3 at three o’clock. I opened the map app and brought up the screenshot that showed my GPS search results as of one o’clock in the afternoon yesterday. At the time, there had been five first-year groups following me around. After that, I had been certain they would let Housen handle the rest, since he had been approaching me with his GPS turned off. I was sure that was the plan.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was also clear from my subsequent search results that after Housen had confronted Ryuuen and they had settled their match, all the groups withdrew and went back to their normal exam activities. However…while Sakayanagi and I were focusing our attention on those enemies, several disparate groups of first-year students had gathered and moved ahead of me in the direction of my designated areas.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I thought there was something suspicious about these groups because as soon as three o’clock in the afternoon rolled around and my fourth designated area for the day was announced, they started moving west and heading toward area F4. The paths were narrow there, and it would’ve been difficult for me to escape if they blocked my way. In order to avoid them, I was forced to take a considerable detour.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I avoided the danger just in case, but that’s going to cost me quite a bit. It’ll put that much more strain on my last day,” I muttered to myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As a result of trying to avoid encounters with those students, I ended up missing my designated areas six consecutive times. I was facing four consecutive penalties. I needed to get out of this situation as soon as possible. If I missed three more areas, then my penalties would cost me eighteen more points. My total score was currently 119 points, but I was far, far from being completely in the clear to avoid expulsion. According to my estimations, the safe zone for me would be around 105 points. If I fell below that mark, then it wouldn’t be surprising at all if I ended up being expelled from school.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was why I had moved about in the middle of the night last night. I had successfully managed to reach the vicinity of area G3, which, again, had been the fourth and final designated area of the previous day. Since we couldn’t check the leaderboards anymore, we’d have to fight our way through the final day while only guessing at our rankings. The rankings as of the night of the twelfth day might not have been entirely dependable, after all. Things seemed like they should be fine since there had been a total of 157 groups, but in reality, many of those groups had likely merged with one another by now. I needed to assume that the number of groups had decreased considerably. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Doubtless, some of those groups’ main goal would be to save their classmates on the final day. If a group that was close to 200 points merged with a group that had fewer points, they would immediately overtake me in the rankings. I couldn’t ignore the impact that doubled score values on the final day would have either. The strategy that the first-year students came up with had slowly but surely pushed me down the path toward expulsion.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was possible that there were still first-year students waiting for me up ahead, but the GPS search function was no longer a practical option for me. My seven o’clock designated area was H3. I couldn’t exactly say that it was a particularly good position even if I was being charitable, considering that it was in the mountains. But since I had no way to predict what was going to happen, I didn’t have any other choice. It was going to take me nearly two hours to get from here to there, even if I took the shortest possible route.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t sit around hemming and hawing. I had no idea if I might find myself forced into a close battle to see if I’d even be able to reach my designated areas today, a day when many students would be tackling Tasks with doubled point values. I could lose even more ground in the rankings by noon. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just when I had packed up my things and set out, I received a call from Sakayanagi on the walkie-talkie.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Good morning, Ayanokouji-kun,”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> she said pleasantly.</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> “It would seem you had quite an ordeal yesterday.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I made it through thanks to you, Sakayanagi,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Will you be all right? With those penalties? Though it does seem as though you’ve moved quite a bit over the course of the night.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She had noticed my movements via the GPS search.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My first designated area is H3,” I told her. “I don’t have much time to spare, but I think I can make it in time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“H3, you say?” </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">she muttered, repeating my designated area with deep interest. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It sounded like there was something on her mind. As I continued moving, Sakayanagi kept the conversation going.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Truthfully, there is one thing that is troubling me,”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> she said</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">. </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose-san seems to have disappeared. She was nowhere to be found this morning</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She disappeared? You think there was an accident?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I believe that she left voluntarily. There’s been something odd about her these past few days.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Come to think of it, I remembered her saying something earlier about Ichinose seeming off.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why are you telling me this, though? I can’t imagine that there’s anything I can do about it that’ll help.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Actually, I performed a GPS search to find Ichinose-san’s location and discovered that she was in E3, the same as you, Ayanokouji-kun. However, she was at the opposite end, near area D3.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if she was in the same area, there was quite a bit of distance between borders, end to end. And besides, I&#160;had already entered F3 by this point.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What was your last designated area yesterday, Sakayanagi?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“D5,”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> she replied.</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> “Ichinose-san was there too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That meant that Ichinose had set out early this morning without telling anyone and somehow made it all the way to area E3. Why?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“This morning, I noticed that our point total had gone down by one,”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Sakayanagi said.</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> “I checked with everyone else in our group and there was no evidence to suggest any of them used the GPS search. It would seem that Ichinose-san was the one who used it. It’s currently unclear whether she intends to reach area E3 or some other area further away, but wouldn’t it be rational to assume that she left to meet with someone?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, you’re right about that,” I agreed. “If she was in your fourth designated area yesterday, then that’s just about the only reason she could have for moving about so early in the morning.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“I thought perhaps she might have left to find you, Ayanokouji-kun, but—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry, but I have no idea why she would,” I told her. “And I haven’t seen Ichinose even once during this entire exam so far. I guess if I wait, then Ichinose might come around to the F3 side, but unfortunately, I’m in kind of a hurry right now. What are you going to do?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Our first designated area of the day is area E6, so that’s where our group ought to be heading. While this means that we will be discarding our chances of getting the Early Bird Bonus, we have no other choice but to ignore it for now. Even if, at worst, Ichinose is eliminated from the exam, it won’t have a significant negative impact on us since it’s the last day.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At seven members, Sakayanagi’s group was impressively large. They had been in fourth place as of the end of the twelfth day, so they were in a prime position to shoot for the winner’s podium. Losing Ichinose now would be a serious blow. Putting it another way, this meant that Ichinose had chosen to do something selfish on the final and most important day of the exam. She was the sort of person who went above and beyond for her friends, more than other people did, which made what she was doing right now totally incomprehensible. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sounds like you’ve got it rough, too,” I told Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Accidents happen,”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> she said.</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> “Well, I suppose even if we leave things be, the special exam is going to be over in just half a day, so I don’t think there will be any problems. If you do happen to see her, though, please ask her what’s going on.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She added that continuing this conversation any further would only be a hindrance to me, so she stopped transmitting then and there.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Where is Ichinose headed…?” I asked myself aloud.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I continued walking, I put the walkie-talkie away in my backpack and took out my tablet. I didn’t need to worry about charging it anymore since it was the final day. I had about 31 percent battery life remaining, so I figured that it would be fine. The map filled up the screen, showing me the designated area that I ought to have been heading toward, as well as various Tasks scattered here and there. Over these past two weeks, Tasks really had appeared everywhere throughout the entire island.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, at least for this final day, I could see that no Tasks had appeared in the northern part of the island, meaning in any of the areas marked 1 through 3. Rather, many Tasks seemed to have been concentrated in the central and southern parts of the island in the areas marked 5 through 10, more specifically in areas A through E. This made sense, considering that this was the final day of the exam and doing things that way would guide people back to the starting area. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Moving quickly to reach my designated areas and to take on Tasks would be a wise choice. I did want to use my GPS search to locate Ichinose, but right now I was in danger of getting expelled. I needed to save every point I could if I was going to increase my chances of survival.</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">7.1</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">M</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">Y SECOND DESIGNATED AREA</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> for the day was revealed to be area I2, in the northeast corner of the uninhabited island. I had managed to stop the penalties from racking up, so it was a safe place for me to go for the time being. Students were basically supposed to walk straight back to the starting point once the exam ended at three o’clock this afternoon, but the school was apparently planning to collect students here and there as needed via a boat that was patrolling the island, depending on how things went at the end. It seemed like the patrol boat would be coming to area J6, which was nearby, at five o’clock.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s the endgame and my designated areas are popping up in some really ridiculous places…” I muttered to myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While the circumstances of the exam were technically the same for everyone, with Tasks concentrated on the south side of the island, my designated area was located at the area furthest northeast. I was tempted to bemoan my luck that I was clearly in a bad Table, but there was nothing I could have done anyway. It would have been easier for me to just accept that and be done with it, but I was beginning to sense that there was something disturbing going on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I hadn’t passed any other students in a while, not a single one. I hadn’t even </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">seen</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> any other students. Though this island was vast, there were many opportunities to spot or overhear other people as long as you kept up with the Basic Movement system. Of course, it wasn’t hard to understand why I hadn’t bumped into any of the students from my Table yesterday, as I hadn’t arrived at my final designated area. What this indicated to me was that most students were already heading down to the southern part of the island, where the Tasks were concentrated. Perhaps I could touch down in I2 and then ignore my final designated area for the day in favor of Tasks. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A narrow river in H3 divided the area. It couldn’t be used as a shortcut, which made that area a pain in the rear as it inevitably forced me to take a detour. The silver lining was that all I needed to do was walk along the river, so there wasn’t any need to worry about getting lost. If I didn’t panic and just made my way southwest along its banks, once I reached the point where I could cross the river, I could head northeast from there. I figured it would be a good idea to just walk along the water until I reached the mountains, and that was exactly how I got to the other side. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once I got near the center of area H3 though, something happened.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kuuuuuun!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was just walking along and listening to the sound of the flowing river when I heard someone calling my name from far away. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T WAS JUST AFTER</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> ten o’clock in the morning on the final day of the exam, and I—that is to say, Horikita Suzune—was heading north along the border of I4 and I3 toward area I2. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How many points was it for a notebook and a pen, anyway…?” I muttered to myself.</span></p>

With that in mind, I decided to give the matter some time and then use the GPS search again.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was still some time before noon, but I wondered how far Ayanokouji-kun might have gone. I felt the desire to use the GPS search welling up inside me, but I firmly reined in that impulse.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My score was good enough to place me conceivably in the top 50 percent. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I followed his line of sight and saw that Shiba had restrained Kiryuuin, holding her from behind. She was about to give up. Just then, Acting Director Tsukishiro raised his hand, giving some kind of signal to someone. The operator of the small boat that had been anchored nearby had something in his hands, and he tried to start coming up on land. It was obvious that this was their final trump card, meant to be used in the unlikely event they were defeated. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But I had a trump card too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, you’re out of time, Acting Director Tsukishiro.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Suddenly, the small boat that Tsukishiro had called, and which had been preparing to come ashore, roared back to life. The engine came on and it departed rapidly, leaving behind the two men on the beach. The reason was probably because the boat’s captain had seen another vessel coming from further out on the water.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…That’s quite a surprise,” said Tsukishiro. “How did you call that boat here? I had already made the arrangements for this meeting, of course. I had to make sure of that on the off chance that you attempted to call upon the school, you would be stopped. Besides, I thought you would have avoided letting the school find out about this.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s simple,” I replied. “Just look at the small boat over there closely, and you’ll see what I mean,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If you took a good, hard look at the approaching boat, you would see Mashima-sensei and Chabashira-sensei aboard. Tsukishiro must have presumed as much, too, after hearing what I’d said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What would happen if someone reported that students from Class 2-A and Class 2-D had collapsed in I2 and were in danger?” I said. “That’s not an issue that could be covered up so easily, after all. I already verified that those students’ respective homeroom teachers would be among those selected to take part in rescue operations should there be trouble, thanks to a previous incident. I&#160;knew that Mashima-sensei and Chabashira-sensei would rush to the scene.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">To put it simply, this was a rule that the school had during this exam. The school administration had figured that it was best for the students’ respective homeroom teachers to be part of the rescue crew because they could identify their students with a single glance. So, if it were reported that students from classes 2-A and 2-D had an issue, the medical crew would have no choice but to allow those homeroom instructors to accompany them, even if they didn’t want to let them come along. And, if a student’s Emergency Alert were to go off, there wouldn’t be any time to check the GPS location of each and every person involved. If the report included a note that a student’s watch was apparently broken, the response crew would be sent to investigate, even if there was no GPS response at the location in the report.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Supposing all of the students’ GPS signatures were checked, would the medical response crew not have come, and the situation not have changed, then?” asked Tsukishiro.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not quite. In fact, if you look at the map right now, you’ll notice that one student from Class 2-A and one student from Class 2-D no longer have a GPS signature. So, if anything, I think it would’ve actually increased the credibility of the report if they had checked.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you were intentionally trying to stall for time from the very beginning,” Tsukishiro said. “That was why you focused on escaping at first, even while at a disadvantage.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Threatening Ichinose in such a half-baked fashion was a mistake,” I told him. “If you’re going to do something, you need to be thorough.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As a result, Tsukishiro had given me the chance to offer help to Sakayanagi before I came here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even so, I hold an esteemed, almost unimpeachable position, you know?” said Tsukishiro, smiling. “There’s no way I could do something so dangerous.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t know whether what he just said was the truth or a lie. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would seem that having rules that require you to wear a wristwatch that gives away your position has quite a few downsides to it in an exam, wouldn’t you say?” he added, seemingly surrendering.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Picking up on Tsukishiro’s air of resignation, Shiba immediately let go of Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Phew…” she sighed. “Thank you for the help, Ayanokouji. I couldn’t even make a dent in this guy. I was so out of my depth that it was almost funny.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kiryuuin got down on one knee, trying to catch her breath and recover. I had watched some of her fight with Shiba, albeit out of the corner of my eye. Although she was on the defensive the whole time, she held her ground well. The fact that she had confirmed that she was clearly outclassed and had worked only to slow down her opponent without overdoing it herself was notable. If Shiba had joined in on the fight with me, alongside Tsukishiro when he was going all out, I don’t know how things would have turned out. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Eventually, the boat came ashore, and Mashima and Chabashira disembarked. That walkie-talkie that I had borrowed from Sakayanagi had come in handy right at the very end.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, does this mean that you’re acknowledging this as my victory?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I can’t not acknowledge it, at present,” he replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There shouldn’t have been any way for Tsukishiro to turn things around anymore, not at this current time and place. If the school investigated the matter, they would find that I was the only one who had their designated area deliberately changed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You are certainly skirting a fine line with your score, but I suppose you should be okay,” Tsukishiro conceded. “Just barely, though. As for me, considering that I have become something of an official, I suppose I wouldn’t be able to avoid objections if you did find yourself in the bottom five groups.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry,” I told him. “I’ve been keeping an eye on staying on the safe side of the line.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see, so that was an unnecessary concern on my part. Well then, I believe I’ll retreat for the time being.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For the time being?” I repeated. “I’d prefer not to see any more schemes like this where you resort to committing acts of violence, thank you very much. I think that, at the very least, it goes against the foundational ideology of this school. Of course, if you’re going to have me tested on my physical strength, officially, and via the school’s rules, then I suppose I would see that as a welcome challenge.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tsukishiro’s smile hadn’t left his face as he looked over at Mashima-sensei and Chabashira-sensei approaching.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please let me ask you one last thing, Acting Director Tsukishiro. Were you really, seriously trying to have me expelled? I’m sure that you were under strong restrictions, but if I were in your position, I would have gone ahead with a more reliable method of doing things.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t believe that the man standing before me was too stupid to have thought of that himself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You are giving me far too much credit,” he said. “I did everything in my power to have you expelled in accordance with the instructions I received from my superiors. But, in the end, that wasn’t what happened, and thus I have fallen before you in defeat.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One thing that I had come to understand from all of this was that I hadn’t yet seen everything Tsukishiro was truly capable of. It wasn’t clear to me whether he was mixing lies and half-truths into what he told me, but I supposed I should assume that he had other aims.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There is one request I’d like to ask of you, myself</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">” he said. “A message to give to Amasawa-san, if you would.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s hear it,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please tell her that because she has continuously disobeyed orders, Amasawa Ichika has been deemed a failure. She no longer has any place to return to. Whether she remains at this school or leaves, she is free to do whatever she wishes.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Was that the truth? Or a lie? I couldn’t tell. Even after he admitted defeat, I didn’t get the sense that he was shaken in the least. Also, even if Amasawa really </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">had</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> abandoned the White Room, I couldn’t imagine that this was going to be the end of it. Only one thing was certain: I didn’t think things with the White Room were resolved, even after everything that happened here. There was still something more. That was the only conclusion I could come to.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please show me how you’ll continue to struggle desperately, up until the very end,” Tsukishiro said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He slowly got to his feet, turning to Mashima and Chabashira with both arms up in the air, like he was showing that he had given up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nothing happened here,” he told them. “Ayanokouji-kun and I were simply having a little chat, nothing more.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you really think that’ll be the end of it?” asked Mashima.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whether or not it will, the matter has been settled. I&#160;have no intention of resisting you two teachers. In fact, I would sincerely hope that you decide to be grateful that I am not resisting.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I glanced over at Mashima-sensei and nodded, signaling to him that it was all right.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, shall we depart?” said Tsukishiro. “The students’ special exam is not yet over, after all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After verifying that the adults were heading toward the boat, I looked over in Kiryuuin’s direction. She must have been worn out after fighting with Shiba as she was down on one knee on the beach, looking out at the ocean.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That was brilliant, Ayanokouji,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no, not at all,” I replied. “You were amazing, Kiryuuin-senpai. Handling Shiba-sensei yourself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“After seeing you in action, I can’t exactly take that as a compliment, not even if we’re being generous. Oh, by the way, don’t worry, I’m not going to tell anyone about this. But I am quite interested in hearing more about you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t expect to be seen by anyone, but fortunately for me, it was Kiryuuin who did. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have a somewhat complicated family situation,” I told her. “That’s all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Family situation, huh? That doesn’t sound like something you can get into that easily, I suppose.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kiryuuin got up, lightly brushed the sand off her backside, and then started walking toward the woods. By the time Kiryuuin and I left I2 together and returned to I3, Nagumo was already gone. In his place, though, were two students that I didn’t expect to run into. They were both just as surprised when they saw my face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now there’s someone I usually don’t see you palling around with, Horikita,” I remarked. “Walking around together with Ibuki? Has hell frozen over? Will mochi start falling from the sky?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Are you all right?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right in what way?” I asked in return.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, um, never mind,” she said. “I just thought that maybe you had a fight with someone or something.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kiryuuin and I briefly exchanged glances and then denied Horikita’s assumptions at almost the exact same time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No. There wasn’t anyone over there,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Then what were you doing?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s been a really, really exhausting two weeks. I was relaxing on the beach, away from people, looking out at the ocean,” I answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re certainly carefree, aren’t you?” said Horikita. “Though I suppose, knowing you, you’ve probably saved up the bare minimum level of points you need.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, Horikita shot a look at me that seemed to say, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“And why is Kiryuuin-senpai here?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I happened to see a student slacking off and I brought him back. We need to take this exam seriously until the last second, after all,” said Kiryuuin.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She lightly patted me on the back and then started walking away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, when the exam’s over, we’ll see each other again on the boat,” she added as she walked away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita, now standing directly next to me, leaned over and spoke in a small voice. “Are you really all right…?” she whispered, trying to make sure of it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right how?” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it’s just… There was something I heard. Well, read. It was on this small piece of paper,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Paper?” I repeated.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Never mind, don’t worry about it,” she said. “It’s nothing. There are still a lot of things I don’t understand, so I’ll come and talk to you after I do some more digging myself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was a little curious since I didn’t understand what she was talking about, but I didn’t want to prolong any conversation relating to I2. I couldn’t tell her about the matter of Tsukishiro, after all.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“More importantly, why are you and Ibuki here?” I&#160;asked. “There isn’t a Task in the vicinity.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki looked like she was about to say something, but Horikita stopped her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ibuki-san challenged me to a contest. We were just checking each other’s scores. Your GPS signature was in a strange location, so I thought I’d come and check on you,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ll say that it ended in a draw,” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…How exactly did you arrive at that conclusion?” said Horikita. “I clearly won, didn’t I?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It was a calculation error,” said Ibuki. “A calculation error.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Calculation error or not, if I’m ahead of you by even one point, it means I’ve won,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t really understand what was happening here, but apparently Horikita and Ibuki had become friends through this test…maybe?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Shortly thereafter, the uninhabited island exam finally came to a close.</span></p>
