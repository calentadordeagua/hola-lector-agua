<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 6:<br/>Each and Every Calculation</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">W</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">E WILL NOW</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> rewind the clock slightly, going back to the ninth day of the uninhabited island exam: the day after Nanase parted ways with Ayanokouji. Although Nanase, Housen, and Amasawa were a group of three, Housen had been working entirely on his own ever since the first day of the exam. He was still lying in his tent when the designated area was announced that morning at seven o’clock. Shortly after eight o’clock, a figure approached and called out to him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good morning, Housen-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s me. It’s Nanase.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I could tell that from your voice,” he said. “Why did ya come here?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why? We’re in a group together. There’s nothing unnatural about us staying in contact,” she said, matter-of-factly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was a serious answer, but Housen laughed scornfully when he heard it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, like you can talk,” he scoffed. “Sure seems like you had a lot of fun together with Ayanokouji. Did it pay off?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It…did not,” Nanase said. “I was not a match for him.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Ha</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. You probably just took ’im head-on, without usin’ a woman’s weapons, didn’t ya?” replied Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A…woman’s weapons?” repeated Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was clear that she didn’t understand what Housen was talking about, but he went on anyway.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You got a big chest, but it’s like you got nothin’ in your head,” he mocked her, exasperated.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Excuse me, but I don’t understand what relationship there is between the size of my chest and my head,” said Nanase. “Not one bit.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whatever. Just forget it. Anyway, so? Did you come here just to give me that report?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Housen proceeded to take out his tablet and used the GPS search without hesitating at all. Not only did he not know who might have been following Nanase, he had also decided that he needed to be wary of whoever might be nearby. However, he didn’t see any signs of anyone that he had been keeping track of anywhere in the vicinity.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The plan that I produced to try and get Ayanokouji-senpai expelled on my own has failed,” Nanase said. “So, I came here thinking that I might be able to get your help, Housen-kun. If you have a plan, please let me hear it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">First, she had gone off on her own, and now she was coming to him to ask for his help. Housen simply couldn’t trust Nanase. Though really, he could never trust anyone in the first place.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Get lost,” he told her. “I’ll handle it myself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I will wait here until you change your mind, then,” said Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Forget about it. Get to the designated area instead. What you can do right now is avoid us gettin’ a penalty,” said Housen, trying to get rid of her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But Nanase showed no signs of budging. Housen ignored her, closed his eyes, and let time pass. After about ten minutes had gone by, Nanase called out to him once more.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Housen-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What, you still don’t wanna go? You’re wastin’ time here, y’know that?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It appears that we have a visitor,” said Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Housen opened his eyes only slightly and saw that there was another silhouette in his view aside from Nanase’s.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“U-um, Housen-kun… It’s me,” said the student, not offering their name.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who’s ‘me’? I don’t know who the hell you’re s’posed to be,” said Housen gruffly, his words coarse.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Uh… I’m…K-Katagiri…from Class C.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t know, don’t care,” replied Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You can talk to me instead,” Nanase said. “What’s the matter?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s… Well, uh, I have something that I need to give to Housen-kun,” said Katagiri.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Something you need to give to him? What in the world is it?” asked Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-well, I’m not supposed to tell anyone but Housen-kun, and—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Housen seemed entirely disinterested as he listened to the student, but he must have reconsidered the situation. He popped his head out of his tent and proceeded to exit it and stand upright, looking down at Katagiri thanks to his massive frame.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If it’s somethin’ borin’, I’m gonna punch your lights out, got it?” said Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“H-here!” Katagiri wailed and held out a walkie-talkie, eyes shut tight, shaking.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That looks like a walkie-talkie,” said Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-you can use this to talk with Utomiya-kun,” explained Katagiri, conveying the message to Housen despite being terrified.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh. What, he wanted to contact me so badly that he was willin’ to send over a nobody like you, huh?” said Housen, swiping the device from Katagiri as if stealing it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What the hell are you thinkin’ anyway, going through all the hassle of sendin’ someone over to try and get in touch with me?” he spoke into the walkie-talkie. “What, you wanna play, Utomiya?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Housen didn’t get a response back. He proceeded to turn on his tablet to verify Utomiya’s location on the map. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I dunno if you just didn’t notice me callin’ you or if you’re ignorin’ me or whatever,” he went on, “but this is your first and last chance. You got that?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After Housen told the person on the other end that this was their final warning, he finally got a response.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“I didn’t want to contact you. It’s just that I can’t avoid it if we’re going to carry out this plan,” </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">said Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Plan, huh? What plan?” said Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you already forget what happened on the sixth day?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh yeah. You said somethin’ ’bout getting together for a secret meeting or whatever. Yeah, sorry, dude, I forgot.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nanase’s expression stiffened a little when she heard that. She didn’t know anything about what happened since she had been accompanying Ayanokouji at the time. Housen shot her a sidelong glance, but he continued to listen to the person on the other end of the walkie-talkie, without moving.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“I took the fact that you’d ignore the meeting into consideration,”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> said Utomiya, flatly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That so? And?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“We’re going to be executing a strategy to save the first-years soon.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Save?” repeated Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After Housen said that last line to Utomiya, he momentarily stopped transmitting so that he wouldn’t be heard. Nanase hurriedly took her tablet out from her backpack and showed him a list of the bottom ten groups in the rankings. At the present moment, there seemed to be four groups of first-year students in danger of being expelled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There are two groups from Class 1-D there too,” said Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Hah,”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Housen scoffed. “I don’t care if garbage like them disappears or not, big deal. Don’t tell me he seriously thinks that I’m gonna do somethin’ to help our classmates, does he?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Stay alert. I think he might be plotting something,” said Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Shut the hell up,” huffed Housen, adding that he already figured as much.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He switched the transmit button back on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know what you’re doin’ or whatever,” he told Utomiya, “but what’s it got to do with me?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nanase intuitively sensed that some form of bargaining had already begun. For the time being, she remained completely silent and listened to their conversation. Their location was made clearly apparent via the GPS search. She figured that, without a doubt, they would have searched the area around Housen before reaching out to talk. Nanase had the impression that whoever was on the other end of the line deliberately chose not to mention that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Because…if we’re going to save people, then you’re someone we absolutely need,” </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">said Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since Utomiya was speaking via the walkie-talkie, they couldn’t see the expression on his face. However, Housen felt like there were some hidden parts to this conversation and that Utomiya was saying things he didn’t really mean. Housen wasn’t so stupid as to not see through that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did someone say that to you?” said Housen. “Well, ain’t that somethin’.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“If you’re gonna turn me down, then just say you refuse. I’m only discussing this with you as a matter of course so I can say I went through the proper procedures here. I thought we could do this successfully without you in the first place.”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, we’re done here. I refuse,” replied Housen curtly, ready to end the transmission.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He held the walkie-talkie firmly in his hand, seeming like he was about to toss it aside any moment. But instead, he just stood there, waiting for a response from Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“…Housen.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though Utomiya was irritated, he called out Housen’s name. Housen responded with silence.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“So, this means we can’t get you to cooperate. Is that it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Considering Utomiya’s nature, he should’ve ended the call immediately when Housen refused. But seeing as he didn’t, Housen took that to mean that there was someone else involved in this request. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait,” he said. “No one said anythin’ about not cooperating.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“What…?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya sounded a little bit flustered on the other end of the line. Housen could guess that he’d been expecting Housen would outright refuse the offer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you come all the way over here, get down on your knees in front of me, and beg for my help, then I’ll give you a hand. Got it?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Screw you. Who in their right mind would grovel to you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Then this whole conversation never happened. You sure you’re okay with that, Tsubaki?” asked Housen, directly addressing the girl listening in on their conversation on Utomiya’s end.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“You noticed? Or did you do a GPS search?”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> asked Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Like I’d bother wastin’ a whole point on somethin’ so obvious,” said Housen. “I’ve known the whole time that she’s a real shady chick.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was a lie on his part. He </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">had</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> used the GPS search earlier and saw that Utomiya and Tsubaki were in the same position, but he passed it off as if it were his own intuition. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Looks like I can’t let Utomiya-kun handle this alone after all, I guess,” </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">said Tsubaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Housen chuckled to himself at that. “What, does that mean you don’t trust Utomiya?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Only when it concerns you, Housen-kun,” Tsubaki replied. “Everyone knows that you two get along like cats and dogs. I’m not willing to let unnecessary emotions cause our negotiations to fall apart.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, so what’d ya mean about all that ‘save the first-years’ stuff?” asked Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“You already know that four of the groups in the bottom ten are first-year students, don’t you?” said Tsubaki. “And on top of that, two of those groups are from Class 1-D. At this rate, when this special exam is over, the damage our grade level will sustain will be significant. And that includes your class too, Housen-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">To someone who held the position of Class 1-D’s leader, this was certainly a grave situation. At least, it should have been. You would expect the leader of that class to be panicking, thinking that they had to do something about it. However, Housen wasn’t just unfazed; it&#160;was like he didn’t even care at all.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So?” he said. “You’re not seriously sayin’ that we’re gonna try and save all the first-years at the bottom of the rankings, are you?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Before I answer, there’s just one thing I want to clear up. It’s safe for us to assume that Nanase-san is on your side, right?” </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">asked Tsubaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was the first time during the conversation that she had mentioned Nanase’s presence. Tsubaki was trying to learn more about the periods of silence and the tactless answers Housen was giving her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“More or less,” said Housen. “She’s just somebody who happens to be a little bit useful out of a class that’s full of garbage.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“I see,”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Tsubaki said. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, never mind. I’ll proceed with what I was going to tell you. And to answer your question, yes, that’s correct. I intend to save everyone, which includes those four groups in the bottom ten right now, and a fifth group that is about to fall into there as well.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You sure talk a big game, but can you actually do it? I&#160;mean, you haven’t exactly done anything noteworthy so far. If you’re usin’ up my valuable time for no reason at all, I’m not gonna show you any mercy. Got it?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Though you say your time is so valuable, it really seems like you’ve been taking it slow,” </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">said Tsubaki. Her words indicated that she had been monitoring Housen since the initial stages of the exam via GPS search.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, how ’bout I beat your little lackey Katagiri half to death and send this kid right back to you? Just for fun?” replied Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The first-year in question cowered, face stiffening in terror. Even the slightest change in Housen’s temperament was enough to make most students tremble in fear.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t get carried away, Housen,”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Utomiya cut in. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“If you lay a hand on Katagiri, I’ll make you pay.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, hold on, Utomiya-kun. Don’t interrupt,”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> said Tsubaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“But—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As Utomiya and Tsubaki discussed things on their end, the call was momentarily put on hold.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What the hell are you doin’? Huh?” sneered Housen, looking over at Katagiri.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Eep!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Katagiri had unintentionally prepared to flee, perhaps because Housen’s smile looked so creepy.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tch. Lame. Hey, just get outta here already,” said Housen, telling the other student to leave.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“B-but…the walkie-talkie…” Katagiri stammered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll hang on to it for now,” said Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Katagiri-kun, I won’t say that you did anything wrong here,” said Nanase, stepping into the conversation. “But I think that you should perhaps just leave it with Housen-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She gave Katagiri a look that also seemed to say, “</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">There’s no telling what’s going to happen if you don’t back down here</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.” Housen’s glare from behind must have been more than Katagiri could bear, because the student turned and fled in terror. Katagiri bolted and nearly toppled over, but ultimately managed to get away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Moron,” huffed Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That was rather heavy-handed,” said Nanase.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s how I do things,” he said. “You knew that already, didn’t ya?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After a brief exchange, Tsubaki got back on the walkie-talkie.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry to have kept you waiting. Can we resume our discussion?”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, fine, whatever. But that Katagiri kid left the walkie-talkie here and went off somewhere.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“You were being threatening, weren’t you?” </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">said Tsubaki without pause, not even needing to guess that’s exactly what Housen did.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even so, Tsubaki did not give Utomiya the sign to go on ahead. She instructed him to stay put. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right… I’ll stay close and watch your fight,” he agreed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As he tried to put enough distance between himself and her so that he wouldn’t be in her way, Tsubaki called over to him. “Hey, Utomiya-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What is it?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You seem like you’re pretty darn strong. Where did you learn to fight? You’re not a delinquent, are you?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not that important. Besides, there’s no need for either of us to pry into each other’s affairs,” said Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s true,” Tsubaki conceded. “But let me ask one thing, just in case… You’re not hiding anything else from me, are you?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hiding anything?” said Utomiya. “No, nothing. My head’s only useful for fighting.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In this case, that’s a good thing,” said Tsubaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When seven o’clock came, the test began for the day. With her walkie-talkie in one hand and her tablet in the other, Tsubaki opened her mouth to begin issuing orders to the groups. Ayanokouji’s destination, C3, was displayed on the tablet she was holding. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is a message to all groups. The enemy’s designated area is C3. Groups in D4, you are on standby. Groups in E6, start moving north and prepare for a pincer attack. Even if you do see him, you are forbidden to make contact until I give you permission to go ahead.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She then quietly ended the transmission.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“After eliminating Ayanokouji-senpai, we’ll take out several second-year and third-year students who are working alone before I get discovered… Who should we go after, I wonder?” she muttered to herself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tsubaki began to organize her final thoughts on who to target.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.2</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">NOTICED SOMETHING UNUSUAL</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> around seven o’clock that morning when the designated area was announced. Mine was C3, by the way. I performed a GPS search just as I had been doing over the past several days as a customary practice, and I started scanning for any rivals who might’ve been racing me to reach the same area. Among the results, I noticed that three prominent first-year students were clustered together. They were labelled as Utomiya, Tsubaki, and Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya and Yagami were in the same group, so there wasn’t anything particularly strange about those two being together. However, Tsubaki being there gave me pause. On top of that, I didn’t see any other members of their respective groups aside from them. But then, I recalled what Nanase had told me the other day and had a hunch about what was happening. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Today was the day that the first-year students were going to launch their attack.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Naturally, the first-year student groups had been scattered all across the island, but I noticed that their positions had changed significantly from when I checked yesterday evening. Quite a few groups had amassed in areas D4 and E6 and were effectively surrounding me. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Should I get moving, then?” I muttered to myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even on a large, uninhabited island like this, it would be difficult to completely avoid bumping into enemies head-on if they were using the GPS search to its limits. The fact that Nanase and I were in the same Table had been known for several days now, so I had to assume that they had determined what designated areas I would be headed toward. In that case, I couldn’t simply continue as normal and head to C3. On the other hand, getting hit with penalties now that we had gotten into the exam’s endgame would be quite risky.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As of yesterday, I had missed two designated areas in a row. If I failed to reach the remaining seven designated areas, how far would I fall in the rankings? I didn’t know if they had intentionally timed their attack to come after I had missed those two areas or if it was just a coincidence, but either way, this was the perfect time for them to strike.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seems like they know how to fight, at the very least,” I remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They had made the right call in deciding not to force things prematurely, holding off on attacking me at night or in the early morning. If they attacked in the middle of the night when visibility was poor and I could slip away, it would’ve been impossible for them to catch me, no matter how many times they used the GPS search. And if they attacked in the early morning, it would have been difficult for them to come up with a solid plan of attack without knowing my next designated area.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Still, they certainly had a fair number of people on this. I had kept in mind the possibility that an exceedingly small number of particularly capable people, such as Housen, might come after me to try something, but this was beyond the scale of my expectations. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Housen’s position had remained unchanged since last night, and he was still in area D4.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If I headed toward my designated area, we’d certainly bump into one another. If I was attacked by the first-years, it was highly likely that the school administrators would advocate for me. But at the same time, I would become established as someone bizarre and unsettling among the entire school, and my goal of trying to lead a normal life at school would be shot.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even the teachers who didn’t know anything about me or the situation might change their impressions of me and think I was unusual. Although the teachers positioned at Task locations guaranteed my safety in those spots, letting myself get chased by a swarm of people would hardly be a wise decision. And, while I did have the option of trying to collaborate with other students, I&#160;thought it best to assume that my enemies were not only the first-year students, but also the third-year students under Nagumo’s banner.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At present, running away from the first-years again and again until they ran out of steam and gave up on pursuing me was one viable option. Ten minutes after I had finished packing up my tent and getting ready, I did another search. It picked up GPS signatures from the younger students who were closing in around me. I could tell that they were approaching—and fast.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What Nanase had told me before was that if they found me, they’d resort to violence. That warning was about to become a reality. Whoever was behind this plan was clearly not afraid of expulsion. In the unlikely event that things went sideways, the ringleader had to be completely prepared to accept responsibility for it. If that was the case, I figured I should do everything and anything possible to avoid careless hostilities. It might even mean I would have to ignore a total of six designated areas, including the ones I had missed yesterday.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Surrounded by rivers and mountains, I was tempted to flee by crossing the mountains, but that wasn’t a wise choice, considering their positioning. It was a far better bet for me to escape to the south, even if it was a little dangerous. Perhaps if I chose to stay far away from my designated areas, my enemies wouldn’t pursue me too much. I took something out of my backpack and started walking.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.3</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">“H</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">OW ARE THINGS GOING</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, Tsubaki-san?” asked Yagami. He was concerned that he still hadn’t heard any reports yet over the walkie-talkie.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was around eight o’clock in the morning. If things were proceeding well, the first-year student groups should have been making contact with Ayanokouji around this time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t panic,” Tsubaki told him. “So far, everything is going according to plan. Things have been going so well that it’s almost scary.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m glad to hear that,” Yagami said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ayanokouji was making a clean detour to avoid being caught by groups of first-year students that were closing the distance between themselves and him. It was unclear exactly how often he was using the search function, but </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">it was obvious that he was using it regularly. If they could get him to waste as many points as possible, that was the best they could hope for. Tsubaki had been willing to resort to violence, but she thought that it would be much better if they could crush Ayanokouji without doing so. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If they could manage to keep making Ayanokouji miss his designated areas, that would open up a path to victory where they wouldn’t even need to make contact. If Ayanokouji couldn’t stand it and tried to force his way through, all they’d have to do was beat him back. Right now, they had surrounded him without fully trapping him, intentionally leaving little paths that were easy for him to escape through. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tsubaki was spending the points that she had accumulated without hesitation, running GPS searches in ten-minute intervals. It wasn’t as if she had been earning points through the twelfth day so that she could win the exam, after all. It was all so that she could use them for this plan, at this very moment. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just after nine o’clock that morning, they confirmed that Ayanokouji had missed his third designated area in a row. His next destination, D2, was displayed on Tsubaki’s tablet. He was currently fleeing to C6, so he’d still have a tough time reaching his designated area even without people standing in his way. Two groups were still on the move to hunt him down. Even without updates coming every ten minutes, his movements were easy to predict. If he continued as he was so far, it was possible that Ayanokouji could pass between areas B4 and C5 and move northward.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Therefore, Tsubaki instructed the remaining three groups to converge in C4. Then, determining that it would be clever to just sit back and keep an eye on the situation for a while, she took a break and stopped searching for an hour. A little after ten o’clock, she checked everyone’s positions again to get a grasp on the situation. Ayanokouji was trying to pass between areas B5 and C5, just as she had guessed that he would. The two groups that were giving chase were just about to enter B5 as well.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We won’t let him get away.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tsubaki instructed the groups that entered C4 to go after Ayanokouji as he came down the mountain. The intention was to get ahead of him and then lead him toward areas B4 and B3. From that point forward, Tsubaki once again started performing searches every ten minutes to get an understanding of everyone’s overall positioning. Then, just as she had predicted, Ayanokouji headed north toward B4 in order to escape the first-year students who had gotten ahead of him. Upon seeing that, the three groups started moving north from C4 in pursuit, to prevent him from escaping.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“May I ask you one question, Tsubaki-san?” asked Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yagami was standing some distance apart from Tsubaki and fiddling around with a tablet, like she was. He turned to look at her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you were to give more detailed instructions, wouldn’t we be able to corner Ayanokouji-senpai?” he said. “It looks to me like progress is a little less than satisfactory.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Irritating…” answered Tsubaki, speaking in such a quiet voice that Yagami was unable to hear her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tsubaki decided to ignore him. After another thirty minutes had passed, though, there was trouble. The three groups that she had ordered to move north from C4 had hardly moved at all. Even if they had encountered some kind of issue while on the move, why would all three groups come to a complete stop? Tsubaki started conducting GPS searches at even shorter intervals now, doing it every five minutes rather than every ten for updates on their positions.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“They really haven’t been moving…” she whispered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The three groups giving chase hadn’t left C4, even though Ayanokouji was trying to cut through to B3 now. At the rate things were going, there was a possibility that Ayanokouji might actually make it to C3. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s wrong? Did something happen?” asked Tsubaki, calling the groups over the walkie-talkie.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She didn’t get a response.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Strange…” she muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tsubaki guessed that this wasn’t the result of a simple accident that had befallen the three groups.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s the matter, Tsubaki-san?” asked Yagami, seeing the clouded expression on her face. He peeked at her tablet without asking. “Did something happen?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Three of the five groups of first-years that we dispatched stopped moving,” she replied. “The three that stopped all have something in common too. They were all in the same position, and groups of second-year students were there as well.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Considering that there were over 400 people on the island for this special exam, it wasn’t especially unusual for many groups to pass one another. Therefore, Tsubaki hadn’t been paying that issue much mind until now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Respond,” said Tsubaki, once again calling to them via walkie-talkie.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But no matter how long she waited, she still didn’t receive an answer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Could it be that they’ve simply suffered some kind of accident?” Yagami suggested. “Many, many groups are constantly on the move in search of designated areas and Tasks on the island. I think that arbitrarily deciding that something is wrong could be dangerous.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even though three groups just so happened to be blocked by second-years?” she rebutted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-well, yes, that does appear true, but…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After another five minutes had passed, Tsubaki, suppressing a desire to be impatient and enduring, refreshed the search results.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It looks like they’ve started moving again, more or less. But they seem quite sluggish, don’t they?” remarked Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Those groups of second-years are sticking close to them, after all,” said Tsubaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Meanwhile, Ayanokouji slipped through B4, descended the mountains into B3, and was heading toward C3. Now that it had come to this, Tsubaki figured that she had no choice but to just let the two other groups that were giving chase handle it. However…she noticed that the two groups chasing Ayanokouji from behind had also stopped moving. Groups of second-year students were sticking to them too, just like the other three groups. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It certainly appears as though we’re being sabotaged by some second-years, but…if that’s the case, then who in the…?” Yagami trailed off. Without asking, he took the liberty of touching Tsubaki’s tablet to check what was going on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey! Don’t butt in,” said Tsubaki, brushing him off.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what?!” sputtered Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m letting you stay here because we’re allies for the time being, but I don’t remember allowing you to touch my things without my permission.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tsubaki’s eyes burned as she glared at him, causing Yagami to take a step back.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN__And__Page_Break"><img alt="" height="100%" id="INTERIORIMAGES_5" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/y2v4/OEBPS/Images/INTERIORIMAGES_5.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand…” he said. “But even so, I would like to share my opinion. Shouldn’t we check to see which second-year students are impeding our progress?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, yeah,” she answered, not needing to be told. She&#160;had been planning on checking for herself anyway.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tsubaki proceeded to use her tablet to look for the second-year students who might’ve been sabotaging their efforts. However, among the five groups, she didn’t see a single student that she had previously flagged for concern.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It appears that the second-year leaders aren’t taking part,” remarked Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And on top of that, it looks like there’s an even distribution of students from all four classes, with no noticeable bias toward any particular one,” added Tsubaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So this isn’t one specific second-year class? They’re moving together, as the will of the entire grade?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tsubaki had come to the same conclusion, but there was something about it that bothered her. She couldn’t imagine that their entire grade would come together to protect Ayanokouji.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So that’s what’s happening…” she muttered. There was one answer that came to her mind from looking at this situation. “Those five groups of second-year students don’t know why they’ve been asked to stop us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You mean that they’re helping Ayanokouji-senpai without even knowing what’s going on?” asked Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sure that the reason they were given could have been anything,” replied Tsubaki. “They could have basically been given a light task. Maybe they were just asked to go and sabotage first-years from reaching designated areas and taking Tasks to protect some second-year student.&#160;Right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After getting a grasp on the current situation, Tsubaki proceeded to look back through the search records from that day. She flipped through screenshots and tracked the locations of these upperclassmen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Their execution is too good,” she said. “They knew about our attack today from the beginning. Our plans were leaked. That’s the only conclusion I can draw.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There are only two days left in the special exam. I don’t think there’s anything strange about our opponents being so wary. Ayanokouji-senpai himself must have known that there was a bounty on his head, so he likely laid the groundwork for this operation in advance,” reasoned Yagami. His words suggested he didn’t find it surprising they had known about the attack. After all, now that they were in the second half of the exam, there were fewer days left for the attack to occur as time went on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“As we’re the ones initiating the attack, we just need to devote our time to carrying it out right now,” Tsubaki told him. “And it’s not as though the second-years can protect Ayanokouji-senpai around the clock, can they? The special exam is still on, after all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With only two days remaining in the exam, this was the time to earn as many points as possible. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s certainly true…” said Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Another thing that bothers me, though, is how easily they got around us,” Tsubaki said. “It’s no simple task to capture five targets when they’re scattered and moving separately.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yagami couldn’t provide an answer to that question. He raised his hand to his lips, deep in thought.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t see why?” Tsubaki asked. “This just proves that there’s a commander hiding in the shadows on their side.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You mean that there’s someone lurking behind the scenes and giving orders, just like you, Tsubaki-san?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She nodded. She pulled up a map of the entire island and looked it over. Right now, somewhere among all the GPS signatures on the screen, there was someone who was looking at the unfolding war efforts, just like she was. And this person was giving precise orders to keep the first-year student groups contained.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Personally, I say that we should consider momentarily suspending our operation,” said Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t tell me you’re thinking about pushing through by force. Are you? That would be dangerous.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not going to do that,” said Tsubaki. “With five groups of students contained right now, there’s no way we could pull off something so daring.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Then why won’t you pause the operation?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Because it would yield the same result either way.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The…same?” repeated Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Everything that was happening now was still within the realm of Tsubaki’s expectations, planned right from the very beginning. In fact, if anything, she felt grateful for the groups that had come forward to sabotage their efforts.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Though we don’t know who is giving the orders on their side, I’ll teach them that the information you can see with your eyes isn’t everything,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What in the world are you going to do?” asked Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Most likely, their commander noticed the five groups of first-year students moving last night,” said Tsubaki.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ayanokouji, who was working alone, would inevitably be eliminated.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">YOUNG MAN</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> of exceptional size and stature among all the students in school ran through the woods at a breakneck pace. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I thought it’d be the kind of cheap little trick that I hate, but it turned out to be pretty convenient for me. I&#160;just decided to put it to good use!” shouted Housen. He&#160;clenched his fists and slammed them together.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s a pretty tall order there, Housen. Don’t you think?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hurry up and get lost,” sneered Housen. “You’re in the way.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The other student had been lying in wait, as if he had foreseen Housen’s arrival. Ayanokouji shared a brief glance with him and then quickly disappeared into the woods. Housen wanted to run after him immediately, but it would have been difficult to ignore this other person standing right in front of him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The hell are </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> doin’ here, Ryuuen?” demanded Housen.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What…? </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Heh</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. Seems like we got intercepted somewhere, huh?”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So it wasn’t a coincidence that those other first-years got caught by you second-years then,” he added.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What? You seriously think that I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">can’t</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">?” Ryuuen sneered.</span></p>

Since there was a clear difference in power and resilience between the two due to differences in&#160;their physiques, Ryuuen aimed for Housen’s jaw.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Can’t imagine there’s anybody out there who could satisfy you though,” argued Ryuuen.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Winnin’ one-on-one isn’t everything nowadays, y’know?” said Ryuuen. “In fact, back when we were in junior high, our opinions of the world weren’t all that different.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He was trying to use logic against Housen to get him riled up. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems like you’d prefer just sneaking around to avoid direct conflicts. What a touching effort,” said Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen’s attempts to shake Housen weren’t meaningless, not completely. Unfortunately, they just weren’t doing enough damage to be effective. Nothing changed the position they were in now; the fact remained that in a fistfight, Housen had an overwhelming advantage. But just then, while Housen still had his hands on him, Ryuuen swung wide with his left hand. He opened his hand before it connected, slapping the dirt he’d been holding right into Housen’s eyes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Agh!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The attack took Housen by surprise, but he defended himself with his free hand while his other was busy wiping away the dust.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yer really in for it now!” shouted Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That so?!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This time, Ryuuen swung his right arm, once again aiming for his opponent’s eyes. He flung some more sand he had been holding onto at him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I told you, yer </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">really</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> in for it!” Housen roared.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though Ryuuen had tossed the sand from his dominant hand, Housen had managed to block it with his arm without much difficulty. Ever since he had picked up Ryuuen earlier, he’d been paying close attention to both of Ryuuen’s clenched fists.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If those’re the kinda cheap tricks a small fry like you’s gotta rely on in a fight, no wonder you never stood a chance against me!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, just as Ryuuen was about to launch his counterattack, Housen quickly hit him again, slamming his fist into the right side of Ryuuen’s face. The punch was a quick jab, more focused on speed than force. Housen followed up with a blow to the left side of Ryuuen’s face, and then switched back to the right, delivering a barrage of blows like a boxer hitting a sandbag.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though the savage beating Ryuuen was getting was leading him to lose consciousness, he shot a quick glare at Housen. The sharp gleam in his eyes caught Housen’s gaze for just a moment and pierced right through him. Housen’s field of vision wavered for a moment as watched Ryuuen fall over onto the ground, like the upperclassman was getting blown away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oof…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen twisted his body as Housen worked him over, delivering a spinning kick just as he was about to fall. The kick just slightly grazed the tip of Housen’s chin. Housen hadn’t intended on letting himself get hit even once. Frustrated, he pressed closer to Ryuuen, grabbing his red bangs with his left hand, holding his head up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Satisfied with your little payback shot there, huh?” he bellowed. “Huh?! I’m gonna kill you!”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“See ya, Ryuuen. You weren’t even fun.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did I say I lost this fight?” Ryuuen asked him. “Even once?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even Housen was beyond exasperated at hearing that. He was taken aback for a moment, but he regained his composure in the next. Although this situation was entirely one-sided with one person heaping abuse on the other, Ryuuen didn’t look dead in the eyes yet. Just as he insisted, Ryuuen hadn’t given up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll admit, you’re tough mentally, at least,” said Housen. “But…you ain’t gonna last forever!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Human beings are creatures that are sensitive to pain. Even the strongest person alive would still feel pain at being hit with blows as powerful as the ones Housen dealt out. It was just a matter of how many hits you could stand. Even if you could endure them, it would be impossible to overcome that overwhelming disparity. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After getting hit by countless attacks from the first-year, Ryuuen’s wristwatch finally upgraded from a Warning Alert to an Emergency Alert. At this rate, if the situation didn’t change in the next five minutes or so, some of the teaching staff and a medical crew would be on their way to his location.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Your body’s being honest, I guess,” Housen told him. “Enough already. Just accept that this situation is hopeless for you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah…” Ryuuen sighed. “The pain is so numbing… It feels good…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He didn’t even glance at his wristwatch. He simply stood up, with an eerie grin on his face. Right then and there, for the first time, Housen realized that Ryuuen’s indomitable will was the real deal. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Dude, what even </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">are</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> you?” Housen demanded. “Why do you keep on tryin’ when you can hardly stand? Being stubborn isn’t getting you anywhere.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen brought his watch up to his ear, as if the piercing, shrill sound of the Emergency Alert was an alarm clock to wake himself up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Stubborn?” Ryuuen repeated. “Hah! Y’know, that way of thinkin’ is just plain wrong.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At that point, Housen thought that Ryuuen would have silenced the Emergency Alert. But in the end, he didn’t. He simply lowered his arms and shoved his hands into his pockets.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This fight ain’t over yet,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You insane? You realize that if you call the kiddie police here, you’re gonna get eliminated, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And in that case, you realize that you’d not only get eliminated, but expelled too, right?” countered Ryuuen. His unspoken question was how exactly the school would judge the situation if they saw what happened here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sure, Housen had received a slight kick to the jaw, but he had essentially suffered no external injuries. The likelihood that the school would interpret this incident as a one-sided act of violence could not be ignored.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What, seriously?” Housen sneered. “There’s no way you can beat me, so you’re just gonna play the victim instead? Lame. You’re so </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">lame</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, Ryuuen.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Depending on how things played out, considering their current positions, the tables seemed to have been turned. Even so, Housen wasn’t that intimidated. He had already decided to use violence to bring Ayanokouji to his knees in the first place; the time to feel any reservations had long since passed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If the kiddie police are so, so scary, then wouldn’t it be better for ya to just turn tail and withdraw?” Ryuuen taunted him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What a load of bull,” said Housen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He had determined that Ryuuen’s strategy was to deliberately keep his Warning Alert on. He moved forward once again, stepping toward Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My GPS has been turned off for a long time,” he said. “If I just beat the ever lovin’ crap outta you before they get here, then there ain’t no problem.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if the school administrators rushed to their location, it would take about thirty minutes for them to arrive.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Ku ku</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. Yeah, I suspected as much,” replied Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen welcomed the other boy to come and try to attack him, even though Housen wasn’t intimidated by his threats. Ryuuen didn’t even remove his hands from his pockets.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you ain’t gonna put up your guard, then just go back to sleep!” shouted Housen. He clenched his right hand into a fist, not wanting to waste any more time. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Likewise, Ryuuen removed both of his fists from his pockets.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t think for one second your cheap little tricks are gonna work on me!” Housen yelled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Housen’s intuition had told him that Ryuuen was clutching something in his hands, but that didn’t stop him at all. He launched his right fist at Ryuuen in another straight punch, fully intending to shatter Ryuuen’s spirit with it. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Seeing the punch coming his way, Ryuuen simply took it head-on, holding his arms up to block, but without opening either of his palms. Housen tried to wrench his arms open to get through his guard, but something happened in that next instant.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Raaaah!!!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Two shadows leapt out from a blind spot amongst the trees and landed right behind Housen, grabbing hold of him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wha—?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was no wonder that Housen was so shocked by these completely unexpected new arrivals. When Housen had used the GPS search just a few minutes prior, he hadn’t picked up any other signatures in the area except for Ayanokouji and Ryuuen. Even if they ran straight for this location after Housen and Ryuuen started fighting, there was no way someone could have gotten here in time. And yet, despite all of that, these two young men were here, holding Housen’s right and left arms. It was almost like they were ghosts.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If it were only Ishizaki, that would be one thing, but Albert was here as well, someone whose physique was in no way inferior to Housen’s. Not even Housen could continue to stand his ground in this situation. Albert held his dominant right arm, while Ishizaki pinned the&#160;left.</span></p>

In the next instant, Ryuuen was back in Housen’s face. He had now dropped his guard and wore an ominous grin. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s simple,” said Ryuuen. “When a watch is busted, it can’t get picked up by the GPS search.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen had instructed Ishizaki and Albert to keep their GPS functions disabled over the past few days and had them accompany him. Housen understood now that when he walked into this fight thinking that it was going to be one-on-one, he had already fallen for Ryuuen’s scheme.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What, you seriously plannin’ on fightin’ me three-on-one, huh? Huh?!” he barked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t bellow so loud, ya ape,” taunted Ryuuen. “Your execution begins now. Got it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen clenched his fists once again and began throwing punches at Housen’s face over and over without an ounce of hesitation. Housen jerked his head to the left and right, and after suffering repeated blows for what seemed like an eternity, he fell to down to his knees. Housen howled, knees trembling, but Ryuuen simply kept on punching without letting up at all.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Eventually, thanks to the beating he had been given, Housen’s knees gave out and he collapsed to the ground. Just as his head dropped into the perfect position, Ryuuen held him in place with both hands and rammed his knee right into Housen’s nose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Urk…!” Housen sputtered incoherently, and he fell to the ground on his back for the first time during their altercation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen signaled to his two classmates with his eyes, and they each pinned down Housen’s arms again, just as they had while Housen was still standing.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Gorillas gotta be handcuffed at all times, after all,” Ryuuen said, brushing back his hair as he straddled Housen. “You really did a number on me, though, huh, Housen?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Lookin’ down on me… You piece of shit!” shouted Housen.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After being smacked around dozens of times, even Housen was reaching his limit. At this point, even if there hadn’t been anyone holding down his arms, it wouldn’t have been so easy for him to defeat Ryuuen anymore.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Remember this…” Housen panted. “Even if you beat me right here, next time we meet, I’m gonna kill you right on the spot.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Eh, I ain’t plannin’ on letting a gorilla get his revenge on me though…” Ryuuen said. “If you’re gonna do somethin’, just make sure you do it good. You got that? Winnin’ ain’t that simple. Even if you beat the crap outta me, if you end up gettin’ expelled ’cause of it, then you lose.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What kinda bull—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Before Housen could finish, Ryuuen swung for him with a straight punch, hitting him right in the cheek and knocking him out cold. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With Housen unconscious, the outcome of their fight had been decided. Ryuuen slowly got back to his feet. Wiping the blood off his fists, he turned to look up at the sky and let out an exhausted sigh.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Phew</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">… That was a bone-breakin’ fight,” he muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Still though, talk about a real crazy dude…” said Ishizaki. “I seriously thought he was a monster or somethin’.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would’ve been stupid to go toe-to-toe with a thing like him,” replied Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Albert nodded his head in agreement.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good work, you two,” said Ryuuen, offering them words of appreciation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“N-nah, dude! I mean, all we did was back you up! Right, Albert?” exclaimed Ishizaki, flustered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Neither Ishizaki nor Albert had any significant, obvious wounds. That was because Ryuuen had decided that if he was going to drag them both into this fight, he needed to avoid letting them get hurt. If more people had gotten needlessly injured, this fight couldn’t be written off as a simple scuffle.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You two should get goin’,” said Ryuuen. “Wouldn’t be surprised if some teachers showed up any minute now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A fair amount of time had passed since his watch had started emitting the Emergency Alert.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, what ’bout you though, Ryuuen-san…?” asked Ishizaki, sheepishly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, this situation is what it is,” Ryuuen replied. “Even if I tried to continue, it ain’t like the school would let me carry on so easily.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just like the unconscious Housen, Ryuuen had also suffered some serious injuries.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll just let myself get eliminated, like Housen,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you sure that’s okay?” asked Ishizaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve already entrusted everythin’ that needs to get done to Katsuragi. Though it has gotten pretty tough to break into the top three.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If Ryuuen had just let Housen go, there was a chance that he would’ve headed after Ayanokouji and found him again. And if Ryuuen disappeared after the fight, having beat up Housen, that would’ve been a problem in itself too. With how things stood, the school would determine that they had a one-on-one fight, and they would both be eliminated. Ryuuen had determined that this plan was the cleanest way of going about the situation, not to mention the only solution, really.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…That’s pretty disappointin’ though,” said Ishizaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As of yesterday, Ryuuen and Katsuragi’s group were in fifth place. Though there was only a slight chance of it, there was a possibility that they could’ve climbed even higher in the rankings. Ishizaki lamented over that fact.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nah, not really,” Ryuuen said with a thin smile. It sounded as though he’d remembered something.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki and Albert exchanged glances, not really understanding the reasoning behind what Ryuuen had said. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll tell ya ’bout it soon enough,” he promised them. “But for now, get goin’.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki and Albert both wanted to make sure that they made it through this exam together with their groups, so they had to avoid getting left behind. The two needed to exchange their watches for new ones and join up with their groups as soon as possible. They ran off toward the starting area as quickly as they could. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once they were gone, Ryuuen sat down on Housen’s unconscious body, using it like a bench.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.7</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">“T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HANKS FOR THE REPORT</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> You can head back to the exam now,” said Tsubaki, quietly ending the transmission on her walkie-talkie.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I take it that means the results were not favorable?” asked Yagami. Based on the look on her face, he guessed that the plan didn’t seem to be going well.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I sent someone to the location where Housen-kun was supposed to have made contact,” Tsubaki told him. “Apparently, the teachers had already collected him and were taking him back to the starting point. From the sounds of it, he had an altercation with someone named Ryuuen from Class 2-B, and both were seriously injured. Well, I already found it suspicious that Ayanokouji-senpai was still on the move anyway.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If Housen had been waiting for a one-on-one match with Ayanokouji, then Ayanokouji’s GPS signature shouldn’t have moved from that spot. It was odd that it hadn’t stayed there.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Admittedly, I do not know very much about that particular person, but I suppose this means that he managed to stop Housen-kun,” said Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">There’s something off about this situation.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Tsubaki pursed her lips into a pout and wondered why the operation had failed. Ayanokouji’s designated areas were C3 and D2, and the first-years had been in the perfect positions to encircle him. However, one could also say that the first-years’ advantage had also given their opponents more time. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This isn’t the end of our attempt to drive Ayanokouji-senpai toward expulsion, is it?” Yagami tried to press Tsubaki for answers. “If we’re going to ensure that we save those in our grade, then we need to crush the other single-person units. If you have a plan to continue the operation, I’d like to hear it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tsubaki simply averted her gaze and muttered, disinterested.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not worth getting involved in any more risky business beyond this,” she said. “Even if we force the issue and manage to help some groups who are failing now, in the long run, people like that are destined to disappear.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Meaning that…we’re going to withdraw?” asked Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t like this,” said Tsubaki. “My strategy might have been doomed to fail from the very beginning.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean by that?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Talk about the bounty on Ayanokouji-senpai’s head has been going around, and he’s been extremely cautious. More importantly, if we can’t even trust our fellow first-year students, then this plan is foolish.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Rather than feeling disheartened, what Tsubaki disliked about failing was the unsettling disturbance that followed. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I should have just done this alone,” she said. “I could kick myself.” She regretted what had happened deeply.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But when Tsubaki lowered her tablet, she noticed something.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh…?” she muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She realized that Utomiya wasn’t there.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s the matter?” asked Yagami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Where’s Utomiya-kun?” asked Tsubaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yagami also acted as though he was only just now noticing that Utomiya wasn’t there. “I thought he was nearby? At least he was about thirty minutes ago…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was when Tsubaki threw herself into a battle with an enemy that couldn’t be seen on the tablet. Sensing that there was something troubling going on, she brought up the screenshot of the map from ten minutes ago and proceeded to look for Utomiya’s last location. He was shown to have been about 400 meters southwest of where they now stood.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you doing…?” she wondered aloud.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was only one other GPS signature near Utomiya’s, and it belonged to a student from Class 2-A named Kitou Hayato. The instant Tsubaki saw the name, she took her walkie-talkie in hand.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.8</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">V</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ISIBILITY WAS POOR</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> among the trees, but a large young man ran through the woods anyway. His destination was the campsite where Tsubaki Sakurako, Yagami Takuya, and Utomiya Riku had been staying. He had been given instructions by Sakayanagi; she had entrusted him with the task of finding out the identity of the enemy leader. As Kitou ran, trying to scan the area for signs of the campsite, he caught sight of a lone figure just ahead. It looked to be another young man. He stood there, watching Kitou, as though he planned on barring his path.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kitou didn’t recall seeing this person’s face before, but he immediately recognized that this was not an ally. There was still some distance between them, so Kitou tried to take a different route, but as soon as the other person noticed him changing direction, they followed suit. Now aware that he was most definitely an enemy, Kitou stopped running and turned directly toward him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You got some business over here?” Although his opponent was an upperclassman, Utomiya forgot to speak politely. His voice was hostile, and there was an aggressive look in his eyes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If I remember right, you’re Kitou Hayato… My senpai, right?” he added. He spoke more calmly now, remembering his manners and managing to be a bit more polite. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though the second-year had originally been working alone, Utomiya remembered removing this upperclassman from his list of targets when Kitou joined up with a group over the course of the exam. However, Utomiya figured that telling him that he had known of him from the very beginning might arouse suspicion, so he addressed him as though he had not really been aware of him until now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m in a hurry right now,” said Kitou, intentionally refusing to engage. He tried to pass, but when he went to move, the first-year grabbed him by the shoulder to stop him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…What?” said Kitou. He glared at Utomiya, irritated.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya simply shot back a sharp look of his own. “Sorry, but I’m not planning on letting you get any farther than this.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What?” repeated Kitou, brow furrowed in puzzlement.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya threw a punch right at Kitou’s face. Kitou calmly avoided it and put some distance between them.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you think you’re doing?” he demanded.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just then, Utomiya rushed in close and moved to grab his upperclassman by the collar.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I told you,” Utomiya growled. “I’m not planning to let you get any farther than this.”</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN__And__Page_Break"><img alt="" height="100%" id="INTERIORIMAGES_7" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/y2v4/OEBPS/Images/INTERIORIMAGES_7.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s your name?” asked Kitou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Utomiya Riku. Class 1-C.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya. He was one of the people that Sakayanagi had ordered him to investigate. Since he had come here to stop Kitou, that ruled him out as the enemy commander. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya, for his own part, had surmised that Kitou must have been sent here on someone else’s orders as well.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who ordered you to come here?” barked Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But Kitou showed no sign that he would give up any names.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if you’re an upperclassman, I’m not going to show you any mercy,” Utomiya warned him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At that, Kitou’s eyes lit up and sharpened. He thrust a thick arm right at Utomiya’s neck. Utomiya didn’t panic at all and calmly kept his distance, evading the attack easily. However, due to his quick evasive maneuver, the walkie-talkie in his pocket fell to the ground near Kitou’s feet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Damn…!” he shouted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if Utomiya rushed and tried to press in close to Kitou again, leaping at him would be a stupid decision, considering the other student’s stance. A tense standoff between the two ensued for a while, but the silence was broken thanks to someone else.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Utomiya-kun? What are you doing?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Tsubaki’s voice could be heard coming from the walkie-talkie laying on the ground near Kitou’s feet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tch…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya clicked his tongue and looked down at the fallen device. Tsubaki seemed to suspect something suspicious going on when she didn’t get a response to her first question, so she spoke once more:</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Weren’t you supposed to be following my orders?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya was still looking for an opportunity to pounce, but Kitou motioned at him, signaling with his hand that he could relax. Kitou bent down and picked up the walkie-talkie at his feet, and, to Utomiya’s total surprise, casually tossed it over to the first-year.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmph. What the… I mean, what are you plotting, senpai?” asked Utomiya, sounding as though all the ill will had left his body.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My objective is complete,” said Kitou, flatly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kitou had determined that there was no longer any need for him to fight, so he picked up his belongings and turned back. He heard Tsubaki’s voice coming from the walkie-talkie, so he had concluded that she was the enemy commander. Kitou turned his back to Utomiya and started to leave, leaving himself completely open.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Utomiya-kun, if you can hear me, then calm down. Fighting with Kitou-senpai right here and now would be a bad idea.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya didn’t answer right away. He could only stare at the device for a while, and as he did, Kitou completely disappeared from view.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…It’s me.” Utomiya finally spoke up now that he was alone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you okay? What happened with Kitou-senpai?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He just left,” Utomiya said. “I watched him go.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Why did you do something so inconsiderate?”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Tsubaki scolded him.</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> “If things went badly, you could’ve been expelled along with him, Utomiya-kun. Don’t you see? Or did you do that to keep the second-years from getting close to me?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, it’s not like that… I’m sorry. It was a selfish decision on my part. I just thought that, even if the plan didn’t work so well this time, it still wasn’t necessary for us to let our opponents get too much information about us. I wanted to stop them from getting close to you, Tsubaki.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I’m not going to criticize you over something that’s already passed. But was this your idea, Utomiya-kun?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was a brief period of silence before Utomiya answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well… Y-yeah, it was mine,” he said. “It was selfish.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Perhaps it was because he sounded upset, but Tsubaki remained silent on the other end of the line for a while before speaking again.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Anyway, for the time being, if you can move, head on back.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Got it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Utomiya ended the transmission and looked down at his tablet. Then, he took the walkie-talkie in hand once more, entered in a different code, and started transmitting again. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I got rid of that pesky second-year insect,” he said. “They should be satisfied. They think that Tsubaki is the commander.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Excellent work. Just as I’d expect from you, Utomiya-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, what about Tsubaki’s plan?” asked Utomiya.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“It failed, just as you wished. Still, I think it was a mediocre plan that was never going to succeed anyway. I probably didn’t even need to go out of my way to make sure Ayanokouji-senpai was warned ahead of time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m ending the transmission.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Taking care not to pointlessly prolong the conversation, Utomiya powered down his walkie-talkie.</span></p>
