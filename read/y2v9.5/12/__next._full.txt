<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em; letter-spacing: 0.60mm;"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.4.1">D</span></span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.5.1">ECEMBER 24:</span></span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.6.1"> the first day of winter vacation. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.6.2">When I woke up that morning, I felt a little strange.</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.7.1">“…I had a weird dream,” I muttered, as I slowly propped myself up in bed by my elbow.</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.8.1">I had been sweating in my sleep, though only a little. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.8.2">Normally, I didn’t really recall what I’d dreamed about when I woke up. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.8.3">Whether they were good dreams or bad dreams, dreams were just that: dreams. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.8.4">They melted away no matter how real they felt at the time, and that was kind of true for everyone. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.8.5">That was the kind of creature humans were. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.8.6">I wouldn’t be surprised if there were exceptions to that out there in the world, but when it came to this, I was typical. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.8.7">Even if I remembered something about them right after I woke up, the memory would slip away in an instant.</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.9.1">“…Was, like, the homeroom instructor a bunny girl…?”</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.10.1">Even as I tried to resist remembering it, it was futile to fight it. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.10.2">I’m sure that if a third party had heard what I just said, they probably would’ve cocked their head in confusion. </span></span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.11.1">What was that about rabbits?</span></span><span class="C_Current__And__Properties__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic"> </span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.12.1">Anyway, I figured that any further attempt to dig deeper into what the dream was about would be a wasted effort.</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.13.1">I let the dream go. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.13.2">Since we didn’t have class today, I took my time getting ready in the morning, letting the time crawl slowly by. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.13.3">There were matching toothbrushes and cups in assorted colors lined up on the washbasin. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.13.4">Even with the distance right now between me and Kei, who I’d been dating for quite a long time, life was getting back to normal. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.13.5">It didn’t mean anything had changed between us. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.13.6">There was something of a cold war between us lately, as lovers, one that had been brought about due to a disagreement.</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.14.1">There hadn’t been any emotional change on my part whatsoever as a result of this event. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.14.2">Of course, I’d instigated it. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.14.3">But I had wondered, if this had happened unexpectedly—if I hadn’t caused it myself—would I have been able to really grab hold of this uneasy feeling I’d been reaching for?</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.15.1">“…I wonder.”</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.16.1">Ultimately, emotional changes were rooted in how indispensable the other party was to you. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.16.2">Without that, why feel anything? </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.16.3">Hypothetically, if there was a situation where one’s life were in jeopardy, there wouldn’t be any need to hesitate to even hurt or abandon a lover if necessary, without a second’s thought. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.16.4">Naturally, the reverse was also true. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.16.5">The other party was entitled to do the same. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.16.6">However, I believed that I had a duty as a significant other, separate from emotion. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.16.7">If people were sharing their time together, if that time was made uncomfortable, it was their joint responsibility.</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.17.1">Furthermore, since this was the most precious time of our lives, we should strive to make it happy, rather than unhappy. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.17.2">Of course, this was an idea based on the morality of human society, or similar. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.17.3">It wasn’t a good idea for me to keep subjecting people to my experiments forever, continuing to burden them with mental and emotional anxiety and stress. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.17.4">However, I hadn’t set this cold war in motion lightly—I had an idea in mind.</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.18.1">The plan had been for us to go shopping for a Christmas present, which we had promised to do before our relationship had deteriorated. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.18.2">It was a standing appointment, as it hadn’t been explicitly crossed out. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.18.3">Originally, the date we’d planned for today was supposed to start this morning. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.18.4">Apparently, it was raining outside, the poor weather continuing from before winter vacation started. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.18.5">It was a little unfortunate, but we couldn’t expect clear skies for Christmas tomorrow, either, as the forecast was already calling for rain for the entire day.</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.19.1">It wasn’t like I had any control over the weather, of course, so there wasn’t anything I could have done about it. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.19.2">There was something else aside from that, though; something I hadn’t planned. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.19.3">I cast a glance at the desktop calendar in my room. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.19.4">A December calendar. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.19.5">There were heart symbols drawn in pink pen, encircling the dates of the 24th and the 25th, but… Something had happened last night, the day our second semester ended. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.19.6">I had tried to contact Kei directly so that we could meet on the 24th, but I couldn’t get through. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.19.7">I waited for a while, then tried sending her a message and waited for her response. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.19.8">But it had remained unread.</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.20.1">After spending close to an hour wondering what to do, I finally got a call back. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.20.2">The first word that I heard come from Kei, after she first let out a cough that sounded somehow both weak and violent, was “influenza.” </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.20.3">Seasonal influenza, or the flu, was very prevalent and could infect people of all ages, causing uncomfortable symptoms. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.20.4">Since the number of infections typically began to increase significantly from late November into December, it wasn’t particularly unusual for people to catch it at this time of year. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.20.5">It seemed that Kei had been unlucky enough to catch it herself and suddenly found herself bedridden. </span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.21.1">Even though Kei was in poor health, she probably would have wanted to keep our promise of meeting on the 24th even if she had to crawl on all fours. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.21.2">However, influenza spread via droplets that were sprayed and scattered around. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.21.3">If she forced herself to go to Keyaki Mall, other people would be infected because of her selfishness. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.21.4">Apparently, Kei had already started to feel like something was off shortly before she had gotten diagnosed. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.21.5">The first thing she did on the phone with me was apologize for not maintaining her health.</span></span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.23.1">It was unclear how soon her flu would pass, but at any rate, at that stage of our conversation, we decided to wrap things up quickly, turning our thoughts toward the year’s end. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.23.2">While it was easy to surmise that there were many things that she had wanted reassurance about, like our relationship, the current situation, and so on, Kei had a high fever right now and was too sick to carry on a proper conversation. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.23.3">So, I told her to focus on rest first, and brought our brief call to a close.</span></span></p>
