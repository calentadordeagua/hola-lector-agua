<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.9.1">If I were hard pressed to describe myself, though, I’d say that I’m more of a minimalist, and I never had much interest in collecting things. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.9.2">And yet things have still turned out like this… Anyway, I wonder if the reason I feel like B-Daman is by far the most interesting is because I’ve grown up into an adult. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.9.3">Personally, I’ve wanted to try out Lego, which I yearned for ever since I was little, but I just can’t take that last step. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.9.4">Maybe because I think that if I try them out, it’ll </span></span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman__And__Italic"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.10.1">really</span></span><span class="C_Current__And__Times_New_Roman"><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.11.1"> be over for me then. </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.11.2">Someone stop me! </span><span xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" class="koboSpan" id="kobo.11.3">(Or give me a push!)</span></span></p>
