<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 5:<br/>A Day of Girl<br/>Troubles and Disaster:<br/>A Devil Smiles Like an Angel</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“Y</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">ou’re gonna do what I say today, Ayanokouji!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Woken by the doorbell, I sighed when I saw my visitor. “What’re you yammering about this early? You sure are energetic, Yamauchi.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry for the bother!” he shouted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Thankfully, Ike and Sudou weren’t with him. But what in the world did he want with me?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What, were you sleeping or something? Dude, you’re acting pretty chill, even though summer vacation is over in a couple days,” Yamauchi said. “Anyway, I decided that today’s gonna be special. Let me in.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was being chill precisely because we only had a couple days of vacation left. Still sleepy, I didn’t exactly follow Yamauchi’s train of thought, but I let him in anyway. I prepared a cup of barley tea for him and set it down.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, am I involved in this special day of yours?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not gonna let you weasel out of this, Ayanokouji. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Remember when I wanted to know Sakura’s phone number?” he pressed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi’s determination was obvious. His eyes were slightly bloodshot.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t just turn him down. The whole situation was kind of my fault, after all. Some time ago, I’d gotten Yamauchi to make a fool of himself by promising him Sakura’s number. That had damaged his perceived market value, especially in Horikita’s eyes. I definitely owed him one. Still, since I’d made that bargain without Sakura’s consent, I wasn’t about to give Yamauchi her number.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I did need to repay my debt to him, though.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, if you’ve come to ask about Sakura’s number, I think that might be kinda difficult.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no. That’s not it. I’ve given up on that.” Yamauchi produced a letter. “I’ve written down all my feelings for Sakura on this paper!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Written down… Wait, this is a love letter?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Right! I’ve written about just how much I love Sakura! Here, read it!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi showed me the note in the unsealed envelope.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Dearest Sakura Airi-sama, you’ve been on my mind for a long time now. Please go out with me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s really…simple. And overly formal at the beginning,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi looked pained. “Even if I write something long, that doesn’t mean it’ll be good, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was probably true. Still, this was too little to go on. I could honestly picture the letter’s recipient—especially Sakura—being uncomfortable.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait. Why is it printed, instead of handwritten?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not really proud of this, but my handwriting sucks. I printed the letter to make it easier to read. I was kind of worried she might not be able to understand it, you know?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi scratched his nose with his index finger, looking a bit proud. I didn’t think that pride was merited.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Besides, even resumes are printed nowadays, right?” he added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you really want to convey your feelings, handwritten is better. Also, why’d you use such a scary-looking font?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was the kind of font you’d choose for something like “A strange demon appears!” It looked as though it was made for casting curses. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s got impact, right? The letter’s got this whole ‘I’ve been thinking of you for a long time’ vibe.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For the sake of argument, I’ll let that go. The problem’s the last part,” I replied, pointing out what he’d written to add to his appeal.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">If you go out with me, I’ll give you all my points every month as tribute!</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s no good.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whaddya mean? Don’t they say that cute girls like men who can support them? If going out with Sakura means I gotta hand over all my points, I’ll do it. That’s how I’ll communicate my passion.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t deny that girls loved financial stability, but it kind of seemed as though Yamauchi was offering to pay Sakura to date him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s fine, dude. Even if she was only after the money, it’d be all right. I want to date her. Is that bad?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I affirmed that it was indeed bad, Yamauchi began to grasp just what I was getting at. “Are you seriously planning on confessing your love to her?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. Starting with the second semester, I’m aiming for my dream school life! I’ve already asked Kikyou-chan to help. I got her to call Sakura.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This was all perfectly normal to Yamauchi, and I found I couldn’t bring myself to rebuke him. I should probably have stopped him, for Sakura’s sake, but at least his methods were direct and honest. I considered offering him a helping hand.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, what should I do? Proofread the letter?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, yeah, but I have one more really important role for you. I want you to deliver the letter to Sakura.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What?” I thought I’d misheard him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I want you to deliver the letter. Look, I’ve been feeling super nervous all morning, all right? The last time I felt this nervous, I won the final match at the Ryougoku Kokugikan Sumo Hall. That’s why I don’t have the confidence to give the letter to Sakura myself,” he explained.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Wait, what final game had he participated in at Kokugikan? I wanted to interrogate that probable lie further. It was honestly a weak statement, and uncharacteristic for Yamauchi. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you say the letter’s the problem, then I’ll rewrite it. Please!” Yamauchi clapped his hands, bowed his head, and begged me to help. “Any past problems between us will all be water under the bridge! If you’re ever in trouble, Ayanokouji, I’ll help you!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you insist, I guess I’ll help,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But what matters is how Sakura feels. Do you understand?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I’m not an idiot. I know my chances aren’t that high.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At least Yamauchi understood that his chances of success were low. In fact, Sakura actively shied away from men, which made his chances </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">despairingly</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> low. Even so, he was determined to try.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand. I’ll deliver your letter. Okay?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji! You’re a lifesaver!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi grabbed my hand and lowered his head in reverence, as if worshipping a god. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">First, I’d need to review the letter carefully. Considering that it was for Sakura, it needed to be gentler if it was going to work. Honestly, this was still rather premature. Confessing his love, when he and Sakura hadn’t even exchanged contact information, was just risky. If Yamauchi wanted to increase his chances of success, he needed to actually talk to her. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then again, maybe Yamauchi had a point. Romance happened spontaneously. People often went from zero to sixty very quickly. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Like Yamauchi, I had zero romantic experience, but I thought that I should at least do something to help.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, that reminds me. I want to add one more thing to the letter. I want to hear Sakura’s response to my confession behind the school building,” added Yamauchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Behind the school building? After Gym Number Two?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, yeah. It’s like, there’s this rumor, y’know? If you confess your feelings there, it’s sure to go well.”</span></p>
 <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sounded similar to the legendary “under the tree” confession. Urban legends seemed to spring up everywhere.</span></p>   
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, that’s the stage you’ve chosen for this whole production?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I mean, it’s not just a rumor. They say, if a student confesses their love, it’s gotta be behind the school. That’s like the golden rule.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t see any connection between telling someone how you felt and the back of the school building. However, I understood his thinking. </span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">5.1</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">t took just under half an hour for me to contact Sakura. How would she react to Kushida’s invitation, I wondered? She probably wouldn’t take this with equanimity. I, on the other hand, was on standby at the agreed-upon spot, waiting for Sakura to arrive. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My phone vibrated in my pocket. I answered it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hello?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“H-how’s it going? Can you see Sakura yet?” Yamauchi asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nope. I mean, she’s probably not going to show up until about ten minutes beforehand, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I see. Damn, I’m so nervous!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi waved from where he stood, some distance away. Even though he didn’t want to be seen, he’d probably gotten curious, and come closer to have a look.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Yamauchi. Should I really hand the letter over for you? I think it would be better if you gave Sakura this yourself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Th-that’s impossible, dude. Whenever I’m really nervous, my hands start to shake. I’ve carried that trauma ever since I was a little kid.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Most people probably shook under extreme stress, though.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand that you don’t want to mess up, but think about it. Does an indirect love letter really have any value?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know when a cute girl asks you to meet her after school, but when you go, a completely different girl from the one you expected shows up, and it’s this plain Jane confessing to you instead? This plan is sort of the reverse. I asked Kushida not to tell Sakura that I’m the one asking her to meet. In other words, when Sakura realizes you’re waiting for her, she’ll be disappointed. But once she learns that it’s really me confessing, my chances will be a lot better when she compares us, you know? So, when you hand over the letter, don’t mention me at all. It’s better for Sakura to think you’re the one confessing,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi didn’t seem to care that he was completely trash-talking me. I had no room to criticize his goals, but he really needed to consider Sakura’s feelings. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Look, I just think getting a confession of love from someone you can’t see is scary,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Th-that’s—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wanted to get him to change his mind. With a confession of love, you had one shot. I figured that even Yamauchi shouldn’t do it in a way that might leave him with regrets.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s still time. I think you should reconsider. That’s why you wrote this letter, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I suppose, but… Ugh, should I confess to Sakura in person?” Finally, the proper conclusion seemed to form inside Yamauchi’s head.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun?” Just then, I heard the faint sound of footsteps, and a voice called out to me. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s Sakura! I’m leaving the rest to you!” whispered Yamauchi. Panicked, he hung up. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, there probably wasn’t much else I could do at this point. All that was left was to hand over Yamauchi’s letter.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is a coincidence, right?” Sakura asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kushida called you here, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-yes. She said that she needed to talk to me about something. She said it was important,” Sakura replied meekly. She looked around, but of course, she could see no one but me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To tell you the truth, I asked Kushida for a favor. She called you out here for me,” I said. Strictly speaking, that wasn’t true, but I couldn’t help it if Sakura got confused.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You, Ayanokouji-kun? I-I see. That’s a relief. I don’t normally talk to Kushida-san, so I was worried I did something to make her angry.” Sakura put her hand to her chest and sighed in relief. She no longer seemed anxious. I decided to be direct.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even so, you’re pretty early. It’s still about half an hour before we were supposed to meet,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was feeling anxious, so I came early.” Sakura still looked flustered. “But it was you, after all, Ayanokouji-kun. I’m really relieved.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When she patted her chest, her expression became calmer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But why get Kushida’s help, Ayanokouji-kun? If you want something, you could just ask me directly.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, um. About that. It’s a complicated situation.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A complicated situation?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">How would I explain? I knew quite enough about the biological differences between men and women through my studies, but I had no practical knowledge whatsoever of romance. Also, the issue wasn’t just our different genders. I had to factor in Sakura’s personality and feelings, too. Even in our modern, effective, intelligent society, this little dance remained a complicated mystery.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Time passed while I considered what to do. The longer I stayed silent, the more guarded Sakura would become.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The truth is, I had Kushida call you here because I wanted to give you this.” I offered her Yamauchi’s letter.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s this?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you read the contents, you’ll understand well enough,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“O-okay.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Feeling something akin to guilt, I averted my eyes. Sakura looked back and forth between me and the letter, as if trying to grasp the situation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A l-letter…behind the school… A boy…” she whispered. Whoa! She thought that </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> was confessing my love. This was bad.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Someone who wants to stay anonymous asked me to give you this. He said that, if you read it, you’d understand. He has bad handwriting, but he really poured his heart and soul into this letter,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, ahhh. Th-this is… Oh my. Oh my. Ahhh!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura’s composure was gone. She stared into space as if looking into the future. I was apprehensive about her reaction if she opened the envelope and read the letter, so I decided it was best that I skedaddled. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, well. I’ve handed the letter over. All that’s left is for you to decide how to respond. You can text or call me if answering directly is too hard,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This being Sakura, she might have trouble saying yes or no. I should help her if I could.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I, I, I, I, I, I, I, I—!” she stammered. “I-I just, well…I can’t! I mean, th-this is a l-love—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, a love letter.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Eek!”</span></p>

<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whoa!” I rushed to support Sakura as she came dangerously close to toppling over. “Are you all right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I could tell she was too warm just by touching her back. This must’ve been really unexpected for her. She was probably trying to figure out who sent the letter.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, um, um!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura’s eyes shot open. She stiffened and stood up straight. Once I was sure she was steady, I let go of her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What about…Horikita-san?! Do you think she’ll be angry?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Horikita?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Why should Horikita get angry? If she saw me deliver a letter in Yamauchi’s place, she’d probably just sigh in exasperation, and say something like, “Oh my, how difficult it must be for you not to get involved in something idiotic.” It certainly wouldn’t make her angry. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Did Sakura still think I was confessing? I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">had</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> made sure to say that I was only the messenger. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Umm.. Ahh…” Sakura’s face continued to redden. She looked as though she might pass out. I couldn’t imagine that the letter was the only thing making her react that way. </span></p>
