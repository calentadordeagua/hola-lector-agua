<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 6:<br/>A Gathering<br/>Between Classes</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">t’s so hot today.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">How many times had I said that this summer? Still, it really was hot, no way around it. Even if saying so out loud made you feel hotter, you couldn’t stop yourself. Just thinking the words didn’t help. Cicadas were probably the only creatures who preferred this sweltering heat.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Heat aside, I’d gotten caught up in yet another unusual incident. If the other guys knew about this situation, they probably wouldn’t be happy with me at all. It was a very nasty problem.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, let’s start from the beginning.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A tree-lined path a short distance from the dormitory led to the school. If you went off that path, you’d find a rest area. That’s where I was. It was a popular spot to sit down and chat. There were several benches and vending machines, the view was great, and many students frequented it in the early spring.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The heat made this a rare off-season period for the rest area. It was completely deserted, which turned it into a perfect spot for a clandestine meeting.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry to have kept you waiting.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I sat on the bench, Karuizawa Kei walked toward me. She shaded her eyes from the blinding sunlight with a hand and looked at the sky.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So hot,” she muttered. Apparently, we were of one mind on the subject. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa’s long ponytail bobbed as she sat down next to me. She wore an unusually casual outfit: jeans and a simple shirt. Even so, the outfit looked coordinated and stylish. I supposed girls had to prioritize fashion no matter how hot it was, which had to be tough.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know you’re busy right now. Sorry for dragging you out like this,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you being sarcastic? I played around too much this summer. I don’t have any points to spend, so I’ve just been hanging out in my room.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you have plans tomorrow?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t really do anything without money. I’ll probably be sleeping.” It sounded like Karuizawa really was indulging herself this summer. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You should get a lot of points next month, though. I mean, after the last test.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">During the exam on the cruise ship, Karuizawa—who’d been the VIP—had worked together with me. We managed to keep her identity secret until the test ended. Because of that, Karuizawa would receive 500,000 points as a reward come September. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I guess. I’ve picked out the clothes and accessories and stuff I want to get. But is it really okay to use all the points like that? Isn’t it better to save some?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You can hold yourself back?” I asked, teasing her a little. She puffed out her cheeks and glared at me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it’s not that simple. When I have points, they seem to last less than a week,” she muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa mimed counting all the things she wanted on her fingers. She was out of fingers in no time. Just how many things did she plan to get anyway?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But, like, even </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> know how important private points are. The school system’s really weird, right? You get, like, this ridiculously huge amount of points during special exams and stuff. Everyone else is wondering about that, too,” she observed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The regular student body was finally starting to suspect that something was up, apparently. I supposed that was natural. If you suddenly received a large sum of money, you’d question the school’s motives. You’d consider the possibility that private points weren’t just meant for use on one’s private desires.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s true. Some students might get as many as 1,000,000 or 2,000,000 points,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. Is it really okay to give high schoolers that much money? It’s definitely not normal.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The points were sort of necessary for us to “survive” at school, which was probably why Karuizawa was unsure how best to use them. For example, if you made a blunder that could result in your expulsion, you could make the problem go away with enough private points. Having a few million points on hand as insurance probably was a good idea. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s no reason to think about it too much. Looking too far ahead can drive you insane. If you retain ten to twenty percent of your monthly points, that should be enough,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">You had to maintain a balance between your wants and needs. For Karuizawa, who’d always been a shopaholic, curbing her desires was hard. Besides, if a former spendthrift suddenly became miserly, students in our class might get suspicious. I didn’t want anyone to connect her change in circumstances to me. I still wanted to avoid notice as much as possible. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I want to ask you for a favor,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What, you’re not even going to apologize first for calling me out here on such a hot day?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Want this?” I handed her a bottle of tea I hadn’t drunk from yet. She hesitated, then reluctantly accepted it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s a little warm,” she muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s the weather’s fault. Nothing I can do.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Some places nearby had recorded temperatures of forty degrees Celsius or higher. Just thinking the number made me feel hot. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa struggled to uncap her drink. “Hmph. Guess I got a loser.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A loser? You don’t usually get prizes in tea bottle caps.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not funny, you know. I’m talking about how hard this is to open,” she grumbled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, it had been a poor joke. I reached over, took the drink, twisted the cap, and returned it. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thanks.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After what’d happened on the boat, the distance between Karuizawa and I had diminished. Before summer vacation, it would’ve been impossible for us to have this kind of conversation. She probably still didn’t trust me, but she showed no sign of that. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa really understood self-control. To protect herself and her status, she could adapt to any environment. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tomorrow’s the last day of summer vacation. One of my friends invited me to make some fun summer memories.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean, summer memories? We don’t have fireworks or a festival or anything like that here, do we?” asked Karuizawa.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The school has a big pool, right? It’s normally reserved for the swim club. Did you know that the restriction was lifted for today?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That pool was even bigger than the one we used during swim class. For the last three days of summer vacation, it was open for communal use. When a huge crowd of students flooded the pool on the first day, the school put additional restrictions in place. Students could only use the pool once during those last three days. Apparently, the second day was really busy, too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah. Now that you mention it, though, I’m not that interested in swimming,” muttered Karuizawa. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She always skipped swim class by claiming she didn’t feel well. Even though it was difficult to cut class due to the point system, the school couldn’t really question a student’s poor physical condition, especially not woman-specific problems. There were girls other than Karuizawa who refused to participate, probably for a variety of reasons. They might feel ill, not want other people to know they couldn’t swim well, or even hate swimming in the first place. Maybe they didn’t want to show that much skin around boys. And so on. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, Karuizawa’s circumstances were different. A while ago, Karuizawa had been viciously bullied by her classmates. As a result, she had a terrible scar on her side, a mark that pained her to this day. If someone saw it, it would definitely cause a stir.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you like swimming?” I asked her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm. Well, I don’t hate it, I guess. I haven’t gone swimming for years now, so I’ve probably forgotten how.” Karuizawa drank her tea and stared ahead as she replied. I could tell that wasn’t how she really felt. “So, what, the guys want to make memories at the pool? You’re obviously just planning to be perverts.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t deny that, actually. That was probably exactly their reasoning.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, what does this have to do with me?” asked Karuizawa.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Before I answer that, let me ask you one thing. Does the school really not know that you were bullied before?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa looked puzzled for a moment, then turned to glare at me. Her polite façade was gone. I returned her stare.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know I don’t want to talk about that, right?” she growled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not reopening old wounds for no reason. I’m asking because it’s relevant,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But…” This had to be a serious topic for Karuizawa. She seemed to come to a decision. “All right. I’m sure you must have a reason for this.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She tried to swallow her anxieties.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I definitely don’t think they know the truth about me being bullied. They might know about my leave of absence from school, or how many days off I took during junior high, but they probably think those were due to me being sick or skipping class, you know? Or they might just think I’m stupid. That’s probably why they put me in Class D.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa sounded self-deprecating, but she was likely right. The school must’ve had a poor impression of her bad attendance record and low grades. The bullying certainly explained her current arrogance. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if the school investigates the matter, they probably won’t find any evidence of the bullying,” she added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You realize that the world is magnificently rotten, don’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah,” Karuizawa muttered. “I suffered for many years. I asked my teachers and classmates for help, but that only made it worse. There was no escape.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Human nature included a strong tendency to fall into vicious cycles. Bullying was deeply rooted in the human psyche. People might not like to confront that truth too closely, but they should be aware of what a pernicious problem it was. There was no simple solution to bullying. If a victim pushed back, it might only result in them being attacked more fiercely the next time. "Why'd you tattle? What're you trying to pull?" the bullies might demand as they increased their attacks.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No matter how often they beat me up, the school ignored the bullying, and wouldn’t do anything. They gave the bullies light warnings, at most. Of course, it got worse, you know?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if a school acknowledged bullying, they usually wanted to deal with it in secret, so as not to risk damaging their reputation. There were schools that would stubbornly refuse to admit the truth even after a bullied student killed themselves, leaving behind a note. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Worse, death might bring no salvation. The bullied student might be insulted even after they died. People could mock them, or share their story on social media for entertainment. What a terrifying era it was, when you could be bullied even after death.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The school, the people who bullied me, even myself… No one admitted the truth. My friends spouted the same lies. That was the only way they could answer, no matter how unjust it was.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa spoke almost as if she were talking about someone else. To her, the past was unchangeable. It wouldn't be strange for someone to cry, looking back on such a painful past, but her eyes were only looking forward.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In truth, this school had probably investigated her case thoroughly. Had they concluded that she was an airhead, didn’t take school seriously, and cut class? If everyone believed that to be true, then the real truth wouldn’t matter. It would be buried forever under all the lies.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But I’m grateful to the people who bullied me, and the school that covered it up,” Karuizawa added. “No one here knows about my past. That’s why I was able to become a new me. I couldn’t have done that if everyone here had heard of the bullying.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She’d turned her situation around by obtaining the exceptionally popular Hirata’s support and protection.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Karuizawa, I think you deserve praise, but I should tell you something first. From now on, you’re forbidden to do anything that supports bullying others.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Are you saying that I’m bullying someone?” </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s head back, then.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As the pool closed, students started to flood into the locker rooms. Which group you belonged to seemed to determine when you headed back. There were groups that left before closing, like Ichinose’s, groups that left just when the closing call came, and groups that remained in the pool until the very last second. I wondered which groups would make it back the fastest.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We quietly watched the other students as they walked away. After some time, the area was deserted, except for some lifeguards.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re still not heading back?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You already know the answer, so why even ask?” Karuizawa lightly patted the spot on her rash guard right above her scar. She looked desperate. Still, it wasn’t like she could go home without changing. She had to wait until she was the last person in the locker room.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would be fine if you just wore a school-issued swimsuit, right?” I asked. No one would notice her scar.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh, swim with one of those on? No way. They’re too lame. I already hate having to wear one during lessons.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, the world of girls was crueler than I thought. Even an unfashionable bathing suit could demote you on the social ladder.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you like swimming?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Well, I don’t hate it,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How about taking a little swim now? There aren’t any students around. The only people here are lifeguards, and they look busy cleaning up.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa pondered the idea. After all, it was better than the crowded locker rooms.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m okay,” she muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I won’t ‘come on.’ I told you, I don’t want to.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if someone sees you, you’ll be fine, as long as you’re wearing the school swimsuit.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not the problem. Why do I have to show you my swimsuit?” she huffed.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol4_5_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_8" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v4.5/OPS/ClassroomElite-Vol4.5_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_8.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, that was what held her back. In that case, I thought perhaps I should use a slightly more aggressive method.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s an order.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa glared at me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You seriously are the worst. I absolutely hate you.” She scowled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You decide whether to obey me or not. So, what’ll it be?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa reluctantly did as instructed, pouting with dissatisfaction. She removed her rash guard and left it on a chair. I inspected her in her swimsuit. Karuizawa stood with her back to me, not turning around.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Maybe this will be the only thing I can wear swimming for the rest of my life,” she muttered. She was still terrified that her scar would draw people’s attention. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I closed the distance between us and grabbed her arm.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what are you—?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I shoved Karuizawa into the pool. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Splash</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">! She crashed into the water. When a lifeguard heard the noise, she shouted at us with a megaphone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’re closed! Please leave right now!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Pwah! What’d you do that for?!” Karuizawa shouted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As she peeked angrily out from the water, I offered her my hand.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you have fun?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Being pushed in isn’t exactly fun, you know.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa took hold of my outstretched hand. Then, without warning, she pulled me into the water. I didn’t resist at all, taking care not to crash into her when I fell. The resulting splash, even bigger than before, would certainly anger the lifeguards. Karuizawa laughed as they rushed over to us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I tried to surface, she held my head down and pushed me deeper underwater. Even though the situation was childish, seeing Karuizawa enjoy something ended up making it worthwhile.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.8</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">fter we finished swimming, I was rather thirsty. The rest of our group must’ve felt the same way, because as dusk fell and we walked back to the dorms, one of Ichinose’s friends said, “Hey, Honami-chan. I think I want ice cream. How about you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. Sounds good,” Ichinose replied. Even after a refreshing swim, the heat was oppressive. “How about we take a little detour before heading back?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There were no objections. We entered a nearby convenience store, and everyone rushed to the ice cream. Horikita had been conflicted over whether she wanted something to drink, but now it looked like she wanted ice cream, too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll have this one! Ultra Choco Monaka!” shouted Ike, pulling out an ice cream three times larger than normal. It was nearly four times the price of a regular ice cream, too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That seemed wasteful, but I supposed if it made him happy, it was probably fine. Sudou and Yamauchi wanted shaved ice, while Ichinose chose a popsicle. Even in a standard convenience store, the group’s individual quirks and tastes were clear. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura, standing behind me, looked around hesitantly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you getting?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, I-I… Wh-what do I pick, I wonder?” she stuttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakura stood on tiptoe and desperately struggled to look inside the ice cream freezer. Honestly, even I could barely see. When Ike and the others finally left, I felt Sakura push against me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s go,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“O-okay,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She really seemed to be struggling to choose, so I stood by her while we made our selections, to help.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What should I do?” she muttered. Her hands shook, as if she were flustered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t like ice cream?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no, I like all kinds. I’ve probably eaten everything here before,” she replied, pointing toward the right half of the case. Horikita, who’d also remained by the freezer, chose her ice cream and went to the register.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hurry up, or you’ll get left behind!” Ike called.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Given how sensitive Sakura was, hearing that only made her more flustered. “Um, umm… Sorry. I’m the type who takes forever deciding things like this.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s no need to panic. He’s just messing around. I haven’t decided yet, either,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you going to have, Ayanokouji-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I shifted my attention from Sakura and looked into the freezer. Honestly, everything looked the same.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think I’ll have this.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I picked up a standard soft-serve ice cream, the kind that coils around and around. Some of the soft serve had chocolate mixed in, but I’d leave that for next time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-well, I’ll have that too, then. It’s delicious,” Sakura replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It felt as though I’d forced her to choose that, but if Sakura was satisfied, then it was probably fine. After we paid and left, everyone gathered in front of the store and began to eat. I opened the package and scooped some ice cream, letting it melt in my mouth.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is…really good,” I said quietly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The sweetness and coldness shivered through my body. This could become a habit. It was honestly revelatory. Who knew ice cream would be this delicious? Eating too much of it could be bad for you, though.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whoa, you sure are enjoying that. It’s almost like it’s your first time eating ice cream,” observed Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Anyone would think it’s delicious. Especially in this heat,” I replied. That should’ve been obvious.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I suppose. It’s just…you’re eating it like it’s something you really love. This is the first time I’ve seen you make a face like that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s because he has a face like a doll. His expression never changes,” said Horikita, interjecting on my behalf. Well, she was sort of like a doll herself in that regard.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita and Ichinose began to chatter happily about the second semester, which was about to start.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Ichinose. Your popsicle looks pretty rough.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Gah! You’re seriously right!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose’s popsicle was melting in the heat. Flustered, Ichinose licked what was dripping and then stuck the popsicle in her mouth.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Mmph, famks bery mug,” she muttered through a mouthful of popsicle.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Was she saying, “Thank you very much”? It seemed that way. Even as it melted, the popsicle did look delicious.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.9</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“W</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">hew! What a long day. I’m beat. This was fun, huh?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. It was nice to talk to Horikita-san and Sakura-san. We should hang out again!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The Class B girls seemed satisfied with how they’d spent their last day of vacation. Sakura, who looked a little more relaxed, even managed a small grin. On the other hand, Ike, Yamauchi, and even Sudou looked anxious. After quickly saying their farewells, they rushed into the elevator.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ll come hang out in your room later, Ayanokouji.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They left it at that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder what’s up with them? I thought they were in high spirits today,” said Kushida.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“They seemed especially strange. Perhaps a certain someone knows what happened, though,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They both glanced at me, but I refrained from commenting, for my own reasons.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, then. See you at school, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“See you tomorrow.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After parting with Kushida and Sakura, only Horikita and I remained in the lobby. I thought she’d stayed behind to avoid Kushida, but even when another elevator arrived, Horikita didn’t get on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re not going up?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What about you? Would you like to take a little walk?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita and I walked along the tree-lined path while looking at the sky, now dyed the color of sunset.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Surprisingly, I had fun today. I suppose a day off every once in a while isn’t so bad.” That was an unexpected statement, coming from Horikita. She spoke slowly, her still-wet hair fluttering behind her. “We start the second semester tomorrow. I’m sure even bigger challenges await.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, probably.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This wasn’t an ordinary school. Countless unknown hardships—like the survival test on the island, or the test of deception on the cruise ship—undoubtedly lay in wait for us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve thought a lot during summer vacation. About the things I’ve done, and the things I was able to do,” Horikita continued.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And what do you think now?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s a secret. If I told you, you’d laugh.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She dodged the question, although I didn’t know why. Maybe she was embarrassed by what she’d been about to say.</span></p>
