<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 4:<br/>Dangers Lurk in<br/>Everyday Life</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">t all began at 6:00 P.M. on a certain day. A text message from the school informed us that the entire dormitory wouldn’t have water for quite a while, due to trouble with the water department. I turned the faucet to confirm, and nothing came out.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It sounded as though the repair work wouldn’t finish until the early morning. In the meantime, the school was taking the necessary steps to provide for its students. It would hand out two liters or more of water in the cafeteria as needed, although the text warned us that the cafeteria was expected to be crowded as a result. The convenience stores were temporarily unavailable, but the Keyaki Mall would provide free drinking water. However, we were prohibited from bottling the water and taking it home. That really wasn’t an issue for me, though.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No, my big problem would be the toilet. Even though there was water in the tank, we had to be cautious, since we could only flush once.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The tea in the refrigerator should have been enough for one cup, which could get me through the day. As for dinner, I’d cook something without using any water. I’d just started prepping dinner when my phone suddenly rang. The moment I went to answer it, it stopped after two rings.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I saw that Horikita Suzune had called. It was unusual for her to reach out. Even if Horikita had some business with me, she generally texted. Curious, I decided to call her back. However, no matter how many times the phone rang, Horikita didn’t answer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I did think it was strange, but I decided to give up. I placed my phone on the table and went back to making dinner. Since I already had rice on hand, I settled on fried rice as a simple option. Once I added the egg, the finishing touches were all that remained. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Suddenly, my phone rang again.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">By the time I turned off the burner and went to my phone, the ringing stopped. Another call from Horikita. I tried calling back again, but she didn’t answer. This was getting suspicious. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Maybe Horikita just happened to become busy right after she tried calling? Considering her personality, it was hard to imagine that was the case. She was the type of person who called only when calm. Even if something had happened, ending the call twice in a row, and not picking up afterward, was strange. I concluded that Horikita had probably been caught off guard by something unexpected.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, right,” I muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Exasperated at myself for reading too deeply into Horikita’s call, I decided to stop cooking for a bit and send her a text.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Hey, looks like you tried calling me twice. Did you want something?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The read receipt instantly popped up, but no reply came. I waited for quite a while, but there was nothing.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I’m cooking right now, so I might not respond right away. Just text me, and I’ll get back to you.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once again, the read receipt popped up, but she didn’t reply. I went back to my dinner.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">4.1</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">B</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">y the time I finished eating, I still hadn’t heard from Horikita. As I drank the last of my barley tea, I started feeling like something was wrong.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She couldn’t be… She’s not really in danger, is she?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Could she have collapsed somewhere? This whole thing was atypical of Horikita, without a doubt. Was her phone malfunctioning? If so, she could just have contacted the school.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If I knew someone close enough to Horikita to go to her room and check on her, this would have been over quickly. Sadly, I couldn’t think of anyone who fit the bill.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Are you okay? </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I texted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had to find out what was going on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The read notification didn’t appear. The situation had changed. Maybe her phone’s battery had run out, or her phone had automatically shut off. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What other possibilities existed, though? The fact that Horikita called in the first place still weighed on me. Why? It was also strange that she hadn’t said what was going on.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Logically, my first thought was that Horikita had some business with me, but had been interrupted by something else. A teacher might’ve called her, or a classmate. But that theory was very shaky. It was hard to imagine someone from the school calling Horikita in the middle of the summer, especially at night. She didn’t have any friends who’d contact her that way.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Maybe some accident had stopped the call. Or she just fell asleep and forgot to call me back. That might have been it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That doesn’t fit.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita was a gifted and focused student. I couldn’t see her forgetting to reply. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m worried.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In the end, my options were limited, but I was too concerned to just let things be. For the time being, I decided to try calling Horikita again. I dialed her number. On the fourth try, I finally managed to connect.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hello?” Horikita didn’t seem surprised. If anything, she actually sounded tired.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey. Sorry for calling a bunch of times. I was worried, since you tried calling me. Were you sleeping?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I wasn’t. I’m sorry for not replying.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t hear any hint of panic, nor did I get the feeling she’d had an accident.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m kind of in the middle of something right now,” she continued.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I heard a metallic </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">thunk</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> over the phone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What was that?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nothing. Nothing to worry about. Goodbye.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She abruptly ended the call. Well, at least I managed to get in touch with her, and she said that everything was fine. I decided to forget about this for the time being.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">4.2</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> thought that was that, but around 9:00 P.M., my phone lit up. A new message.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Are you awake?</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> read Horikita’s text.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I’m awake.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I’d like to talk to you. Do you have time? </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was roughly two hours after we’d last talked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I’ll call you.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita picked up on the first ring.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s up?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s something I wanted to ask you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As before, Horikita sounded tired. She paused before speaking again.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s say that there was a turtle,” she began.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita launched into a completely crazy story.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s an extremely smart and talented turtle. If I accidentally hit it, and flipped it onto its back, that would be terrible, wouldn’t you agree? It couldn’t right itself under its own steam.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose. But actually, in most cases, turtles can extend their necks and use their legs to flip over. The only turtles that absolutely can’t right themselves are giant tortoises and sea turtles,” I explained.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…………”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita fell silent at my unnecessary explanation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This would have been easier if you’d just assumed that turtles can’t get up on their own and listened to me,” she said after a moment.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yeah. That made sense.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. So, they can’t get up on their own. Is something wrong with that?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In such a situation, what would you do?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’d probably flip the turtle over. It’s not that much trouble.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wouldn’t have any reason to save the turtle, but I wouldn’t have any reason to abandon it, either. I thought I might as well extend a helping hand. However, I wondered what exactly Horikita was getting at. Maybe she was in trouble, like the turtle that couldn’t get up on its own? </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t get the impression that she was panicking, though. She seemed calm. That probably meant this wasn’t a pressing issue.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, what’s wrong?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I’m not upset or anything,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it sure </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">sounds </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">like that’s where this is headed.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was just talking about a turtle on its back. It had nothing to do with me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. Then why are we talking about a turtle?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I just…wanted to talk to you about a turtle that flipped over,” she insisted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Okay, this was getting weird.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This isn’t like you,” I said. “Well, I guess asking for help isn’t like you, either, but… You called me because you don’t have anyone else to go to, right? If that’s the case, then just come out and say why.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita paused.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you say that you can’t prevent wanting to help people, then perhaps I can’t go to you for advice,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? It’s fine. Talk to me,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita finally opened up. “I’m just having a little trouble.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At least she’d finally admitted it. “Where are you now?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m in my room.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait. Are there bugs?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If that were the case, I understood why Horikita didn’t want to talk about it. I was probably right on the money, although the dormitories were generally kept clean, and Horikita lived on an upper floor—actually, that made the likelihood of bugs rather low.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not it. I could deal with a bug myself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How would you deal with it? Detergent? Hot water? Slippers?” No matter how good my powers of deductive reasoning were, I couldn’t imagine Horikita’s current situation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I’m in trouble because… No, it’s fine after all. I’ll take care of it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You say you’ll take care of it yourself, but it’s been more than two hours, and you haven’t done anything yet, right?” I shot back. If she’d called with this problem earlier, then she’d been struggling for quite some time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well. Well… It’s true that I’m just about at my physical limit. I’ll tell you everything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Finally. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Instead of explaining, however, Horikita suddenly made a request. “Could you come to my room?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She sounded both embarrassed and disgusted.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now? But it’s already past nine,” I protested.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand, but…to deal with this, you need to be here.” Horikita spoke as though she was frustrated or in pain.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I might get in trouble for going to the girls’ floor at this time of night,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know, but I can’t do this unless you’re here,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that, Horikita abruptly hung up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is kinda scary. But I guess I have to go,” I muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Grabbing only my phone and room key, I hurried out. Wouldn’t want to keep her waiting.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">4.3</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> didn’t want to run into any other girls, so I waited until no one else was using the elevator. Sneaking around like that was pathetic, but hey. That was me. I managed to reach the thirteenth floor undetected. When I got to Horikita’s room, I rang the doorbell. After waiting a while, I tried to slowly open the door myself. It wasn’t locked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita’s place was a one-bedroom and kitchen model, but I couldn’t see into the bedroom area. It was hidden behind a closed door. There was no sign of Horikita in the kitchen or hallway. Like me, she’d barely decorated since moving in.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re alone, right?” I heard her voice from behind the bedroom door.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re being way too loud,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s okay. Even if someone came in right now, I’d conk them with my right hand,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What in the world was </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> supposed to mean? Cautiously, I entered Horikita’s bedroom. She had her back to me, so I couldn’t see her expression. The room was decorated simply. Nothing seemed particularly strange.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, I’m here. What’s the problem?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Once you see, you’ll understand.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita slowly stood and turned to face me. Two radically different emotions coursed through me at the exact same time.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. So. That’s it, huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s it, yes.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I felt some secondhand embarrassment as I looked at her right hand, which was completely stuck inside a small water bottle. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How do I say this? This is completely unlike you. Don’t tell me you were just playing around.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t be dumb.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is like when you challenge yourself to use your fingers to pick up a single piece of corn, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That must’ve irritated her, because she swung her right arm at me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-It’s just a joke.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s no point telling an unfunny joke. You failed.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It wasn’t funny because I was teasing you, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This water bottle got stuck because I tried to wash my hand. Can you help me get it off?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, that was what happened. I grabbed the water bottle and pulled, but only ended up pulling Horikita toward me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on. If you don’t pull back yourself, you’re gonna stay stuck. At least give it a little elbow grease,” I said. If she didn't offer a resisting force, I'd get nowhere.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I already know that. It’s just that I’m tired. Let’s get this over with quickly,” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After trying to free herself on her own for more than two hours, Horikita was exhausted. I grabbed the water bottle again, put a little more strength into it, and pulled. Horikita pulled back as I did so, enduring the pain. However, her arm remained stuck.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s no use. At this rate, the bottle will never come off,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. I suppose I expected as much.” Horikita appeared to have resigned herself to being trapped. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Guess we have to rub your arm down with soap and slowly pull the bottle off. Let’s head to the kitchen,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you forget that there’s a water outage right now?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was right. We wouldn’t have water until twelve. The only usable water was in the toilet, but Horikita probably wouldn’t like that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll go to the cafeteria.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We were low on options, but as long as I could get some water, we could remove the bottle. I left Horikita’s room and headed straight for the cafeteria. However, an unfortunate surprise awaited me there.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m very sorry. So many students came that we’re all out,” said the cafeteria lady. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, those who’d needed water for dinner had taken all of it. Well then, I’d just go buy some at the vending machine. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita said nothing, continuing to hide behind my back.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita seems to be acting differently than usual. Is something the matter?” asked Kushida. She kept moving toward us, overshooting her room.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita raised her voice. “Nothing’s wrong!” she replied curtly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her bluntness worked. Kushida stopped.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Well, if something’s troubling you, please tell me. Okay? Maezono-san was in trouble earlier because she had no water. I have more than enough,” said Kushida.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kushida had what we wanted most of all. If Horikita could only ask for her help, she’d get water quite easily.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, Horikita pushed the water bottle into my back like the muzzle of a gun. She didn’t want Kushida’s help. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well then, good night to you both,” Kushida said sweetly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, ’night.”</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">4.5</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">t took us quite a while to get to the first floor from the thirteenth. We were worried that the lobby might be crowded, but fortunately, no one was around.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We can go right now,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I made my way toward the exit, Horikita trailing closely behind me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, several male and female students emerged from the darkness, chatting as they walked. They didn’t appear to be Class D students, but from Horikita’s perspective, it didn’t make any difference who they were. We couldn’t get out of the dormitory in time. Horikita turned on her heel and started to head back, as if returning to her room.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At this rate, they’re going to see us,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The students drew nearer, their presence hard to ignore. Flustered, Horikita and I opened the door to the west emergency stairs. How unlucky could we be? I heard a voice directly above us. It sounded like a male student on the third or fourth floor, heading downstairs. Students who lived on the lower levels often didn’t use the elevators.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No longer able to go upstairs, Horikita and I were forced to return to the lobby.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We have no choice now but to use the elevator!” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that okay? You’ll be seen on the monitors,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll have to use you as cover. Since we know the camera’s position, we should be able to do that,” replied Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That suggestion was bizarre, but not impossible. I would rather have avoided it, but since we lacked an escape route, there was no other choice. We hurried to the left elevator and got on. I positioned myself directly in front of the camera, and Horikita stood behind me, making sure to hide her arm. She was like a ghost clinging to my back.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I hoped observers wouldn’t notice anything. At any rate, we needed to get away from the first floor. I pushed a button at random.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, we’re safe for now, but…this is just the beginning,” I muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s give up. I can’t go outside like this. I’ll just endure this bottle until the water comes back,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That had to be a tough pill for her to swallow, but if it was what she wanted, we just needed to return to the thirteenth floor. I cancelled the request for a random floor, and pushed the button for the thirteenth. Hopefully our trials were over for tonight.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, just as we relaxed, the elevator suddenly slowed. I was having terrible luck with elevators lately. At least it wasn’t breaking down, and I hadn’t pressed the wrong button.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We had stopped on the fifth floor. That meant that someone had pressed the call button. No matter who got on, they’d see Horikita in this bizarre state. She might’ve gone undetected in a big crowd of people, but fate continued to be cruel, and the elevator doors opened to reveal a single male student.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Unbelievable. Of all the people we could have encountered…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kouenji Rokusuke, a Class D student, waltzed into the elevator with his usual haughty air. He went straight to the mirrored wall, not even giving us a passing glance. As he gazed at his reflection, he pulled out a comb he always carried, and started to style his hair.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Elevator boy. Top floor,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita appeared stunned by Kouenji’s incredible display of narcissism. I wanted to say a bunch of things right back at him, but it was probably best to just stay quiet. I pressed the button for the top floor, the elevator doors closed, and we started our ascent once again.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kouenji showed no sign of paying attention to us. I would’ve thought that he’d spare us a glance, considering that we were his classmates, but at least that meant he probably wouldn’t notice the water bottle. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While Horikita hovered in the camera’s blind spot, the elevator passed the tenth floor. I wondered what business Kouenji had on the top floor, but couldn’t ask. Maybe he had no real reason for going.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When the elevator doors slowly opened, Horikita and I stepped out simultaneously. Kouenji never once moved his eyes from the mirror. Things had ended without incident.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita rushed straight to her room.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s impossible. Walking around outside is completely out of the question,” she muttered as she went inside. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was about to follow her when my phone vibrated.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Sorry for the late reply. I was researching something, so I didn’t notice your text, </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">read the message from Sakura.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sakura-san?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah.” I texted Sakura back.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">You wanted water, right? Of course that’s okay. Would one bottle be enough? </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">said her message.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">That’s plenty, thank you. Can I come get it now?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Sure. I’ll wait for you,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Sakura replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was difficult to hold a conversation with Sakura in person, but when we texted, it went smoothly. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good news, Horikita. Apparently, Sakura has water. She just gave me the okay to borrow some, so I’ll be going now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you. However, please don’t tell Sakura-san about me,” Horikita replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, since you won’t be stuck like this anymore, how about we take a commemorative photo?” I joked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita seemed to be about to start swinging the water bottle at me, so I ran into the hall. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What a terrifying woman. Considering her physical prowess, if she hit me on the head with that bottle, I’d probably die,” I muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If a high school girl with her arm stuck in a water bottle killed me, my name would live on in infamy.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-before: always;"/>
<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">4.6</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“T</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">here—it’s off,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After a long, arduous struggle, we’d finally succeeded in freeing Horikita from the water bottle. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Honestly, this day was a complete disaster,” she muttered. If my hand had been trapped in a water bottle, I would’ve felt the same. “Ayanokouji-kun. Please don’t speak a word of this to anyone.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Before you go issuing warnings, isn’t there something else you’d like to say first?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It wasn’t sincere, but at least it sounded like an approximation of gratitude. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have to say, getting trapped in a water bottle? That’s just so unlike you, Horikita.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Shut up,” she snapped. "I don't go looking for trouble."</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I decided that I’d outstayed my welcome, so I headed back to my own room.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Really, though, was it even possible for someone’s arm to get stuck in a water bottle? I took a bottle out of a box, rinsed it, and then inserted my hand as a test. It was an exact fit. The bottle felt surprisingly snug.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Rocket punch! Eh, just kidding.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I gave in to silliness for a moment. When I tried to get my hand out of the water bottle, though…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I think I’m trapped!”</span></p>
