<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 5:<br/>Everyone’s Growth</span></span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HE DAYS SPENT</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> on the luxury cruise ship for our summer vacation continued to prove to be a valuable experience, and our trip had already passed the halfway mark. As the students enjoyed their remaining time on the ship to the absolute fullest, they might have loosened their purse strings much more than ever before. While this might have all sounded shocking to those students who were planning to move up in the class hierarchy, it wasn’t like splurging a little during a period of rest and relaxation was a totally bad thing by any means. It relieved the fatigue that had built up within us, and at the same time, provided a sense of euphoria and happiness.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Still, that was a very charitable way of putting it, and I supposed it might have sounded like I was just making excuses I was using my meager Private Points too. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I changed into my swimsuit. Once I opened the door leading out of the changing room, an expansive pool stretched out before my eyes. This luxury cruise ship had a large pool which was open to anyone, but, in addition, there was another pool on board as well. That one was called the private pool, and, as the name suggested, you could reserve it for private use.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The reservation fee wasn’t cheap, at 20,000 yen for sixty minutes. But the time spent with close friends was more than worth the money. Moreover, up to a maximum of forty people could use that pool at a time. If you were to rent out the pool as a whole class, then it would only come to 500 points per person. The private pool was unexpectedly popular with the students and was almost always fully booked during its operating hours, which ran from eight in the morning to eight in the evening.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Also, it was difficult to swim freely in the large pool as it was often packed with lots of people. On the other hand, the private pool offered plenty of space, enough that you could enjoy yourself without any inconveniences.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whoa, it’s huge, isn’t it?” marveled Akito, having arrived at the pool slightly after I did.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This pool was roughly the same size as the large pool that could be used freely, without reservations, but it looked so big now that it felt like the scale of its size changed once you reserved it for yourself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Where’s Keisei?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He said he’ll come after he finishes using the bathroom. The girls are still getting ready, unsurprisingly enough.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t need to bother asking to confirm that the girls didn’t change into their swimsuits in as short a time as guys did. Akito reached over and picked up a menu that had been left beside some pool chairs.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Dang… It’s more expensive than the other place,” he remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The prices of the drinks at the private pool were considerably higher than at the free pool. Almost double the cost, actually. That might not have been entirely surprising, considering the number of orders there compared to the number of people staffed to prepare them, but it was still rough. This was yet another example of the relentless exploitation we were suffering under. The fact that we were prohibited from bringing our own food and beverages was another indicator that they had thought this out very well.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just then, the door to the changing rooms cracked open. Akito and I turned to look at almost the exact same time, but we still couldn’t see any signs of anyone physically coming through yet. Instead, we heard a number of voices.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on Airi, what the heck are you doing? Hurry up!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“B-b-b-b-b-but! But! Th-this is so embarrassing, Haruka-chan!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s embarrassing about it? You’ve posted all kinds of embarrassing pictures online, so this is totally fine, isn’t&#160;it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“B-but it’s not like people were looking at me </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">directly</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, if you ask me, those pictures are </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">way</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> worse. Come on, let’s go.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah! Wait, hold on!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Haruka and Airi were going back and forth in an indescribable conversation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, dude. It’s like, sometimes </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">not</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> seeing is pretty great too, huh,” said Akito.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was a rather unexpected remark from him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What?” he asked, turning to me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was just thinking, wow, you think about that stuff too, Akito.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, listen man… It’s normal for guys, right?” he said. “I mean, it’s not like I casually say stuff like that on a daily basis, like Ike and those kinda guys. You’re the same way,&#160;right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Akito was looking at me with a somewhat dumbfounded expression in his eyes, and at the same time, there was this feeling in the air like he wasn’t going to allow me to deny it. It wasn’t as though I was explicitly trying to read between the lines here. But I did understand that Akito summoned courage to say what he did. Since I knew it wouldn’t be a good idea to bluntly deny it, I admitted it, openly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, yeah,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Akito chuckled a bit, as if he were relieved. “If the girls heard us, they’d probably call us idiots or somethin’.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Akito was usually quite calm and composed with somewhat of a poker face, but it was obvious from how much he was talking right now that he was feeling anxious. However, it sounded like Haruka and Airi were still going back and forth, and they weren’t quite coming out&#160;yet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But it’s so embarrassing!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, listen here! I feel the same way, you know!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, H-H-Haruka-chan… This is a very daring outfit though, isn’t it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You promised me that you’d show it to everyone if you wore it!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Eek!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Waiting for the girls, Akito and I were left in a state of limbo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Daring, she says,” remarked Akito.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sounds like it,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Anticipation filled the air, accompanied by a feeling of embarrassment. Where should we look and what should we say to the girls when they come out?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, no, I can’t do this! A-at least let me go borrow something to put on over it!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Absolutely not! Come on, you are not running away!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ughhh, but this kind of swimsuit is just so embarrassing, Haruka-chan!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it’s embarrassing for me too, okay? Besides, I&#160;have no choice but to do this along with you!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I never asked you to do that!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We were eagerly, impatiently awaiting their arrival, but from the sounds of things, Haruka’s attempts to wrangle Airi were going to continue for a little while longer yet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Ayanokouji,” Akito said. “What do you think about Airi?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Akito had been looking in the direction of the girls earlier, but I realized now that he was looking over at me. I knew he wasn’t just asking me some random, off-the-cuff question. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In what way?” I immediately understood what he was trying to get at, but I deliberately decided to play dumb.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I mean, mixed-gender groups can be a little complicated, right?” he said. “I mean, someone could end up getting a crush on somebody else or something. That wouldn’t be so weird.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was becoming more difficult for me to respond to that question.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What about you?” I asked, turning the question around. When I did so, Akito had a somewhat conflicted look on his face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, I see,” he replied. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After a brief period of silence, Akito spoke once more. “I’d probably be lying if I said I didn’t have any feelings at all.” With that answer, he didn’t deny there was someone. He admitted it. “But if it might break this group apart, I&#160;don’t think I’d push it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That meant that he was going to leave those feelings to smolder deep down in his heart of hearts. I couldn’t determine right now if he was talking about Haruka or Airi, but… I wondered what the right thing was for me to say. Unlike with math, it wasn’t like you could find a clear, quantifiable answer for something like this.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kiyotaka, you—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kyaaaah!!!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just as Akito was about to say something, the half-opened door swung wide with great force. Airi came rushing out, like she had been falling forward. Akito and I exchanged looks once again as we heard loud shouting coming from their direction.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“H-hey! That was so mean, Haruka-chan, pushing me like that!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that’s because you weren’t getting out here fast enough!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After saying that, Haruka stepped out of the changing room, appearing after Airi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“H-hey, hey…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Akito stared, dumbstruck. It went without saying that I was similarly astonished. How should I put this? Both of them were wearing incredibly daring swimsuits. If this were not a private pool, I’m sure they would have attracted many stares from men and women alike. Haruka immediately raised her gaze, looking over at us. Somehow, I got the feeling like it was somewhat criminal for us to stare at them like we were, and Akito and I both averted our eyes at the exact same time. We both randomly turned to look in another direction. However, something else must have caught Akito’s curiosity, because he said something to me as we kept our gazes fixed elsewhere. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Airi really makes a different impression, huh?” he remarked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had really wished that he wouldn’t talk to me about that right here, but I figured that Akito must have been in a painful situation too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. She has this sense of purity,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, that’s it,” he agreed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As Akito and I were expressing our thoughts about Airi, Haruka had a clearly disappointed look on her face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And I’m common? Ordinary?” she huffed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t say that,” I said. “It’s just…I’m too surprised to say anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I would have really loved it if Haruka caught on to the part where my vocabulary was rapidly declining.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m…gonna swim for a bit,” announced Akito.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Perhaps the two girls were too stimulating for him because Akito quickly turned his back to us and jumped into the pool without even doing any warm-up stretches or anything. Splashing around, he swam through the pool, all alone. I knew how it felt to be overcome with the feeling of wanting to escape. It was precisely because of the environment we were in, a private pool, which we rarely got to experience, that when faced with the overwhelming destructive power of these two in front of us, it became impossible for us to escape.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Running away like he did by diving into the pool and swimming away so that he could rid himself of his various worldly desires had been the correct choice. That being said, if two men suddenly poured all their energy into swimming, the vibe would obviously become awkward. I&#160;had no other choice but to continue facing them, acting as a shield. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">What should I do…?</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I casually glanced over at the two of them and noticed that Airi seemed fidgety and nervous. She was blushing. Upon seeing this, Haruka merrily went around behind Airi, and grabbed both of her shoulders tight.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Eep!” she squeaked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sooo? Come on Kiyopon, whaddaya think of the new-and-improved Airi?” she asked.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun. Hello.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We exchanged brief, casual greetings, and now I was standing directly in front of her. With the kind of mood that was hanging in the air, I didn’t feel like cutting straight to the chase, so I decided to make a little small talk first.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you still using that strategy? With you being a bank for everyone’s Private Points?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was a question unrelated to the main topic of what we were going to talk about, but Ichinose didn’t show even a hint of displeasure.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes,” she replied. “We decided that there wasn’t any harm in sticking with it. It’s easy. We can save up as much as we can, and if they’re no longer needed, I can just return everyone’s points to them.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She had said it was easy, but it was a strategy that could only continue to work because Ichinose was a trustworthy person. Like she herself had said, it wasn’t a bad idea to just keep the points saved as long as they were able to. If students’ balances were automatically diminishing, then it might cause some inconvenience, but as long as she promised they’d get back what they gave, it was a good plan. Their class would be able to move large sums when the need arose. The fact that it was the one and only unique advantage given to Ichinose was another major factor.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But the strategy of pooling your points is just something so you’re prepared for emergencies,” I said. “That alone can’t be enough, can it?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If it was something that we only just started, that would be a different story. But in this case, it’s just a continuation of what we were doing already.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Meaning that they weren’t readying any new strategies, they were only maintaining the status quo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you think we’re lacking in, Ayanokouji-kun?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What your class is lacking in, Ichinose?” I repeated.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I feel like we can’t really see that ourselves, so… I&#160;was wondering how our class appears from your perspective, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I talked to some of your classmates during the uninhabited island exam,” I said. “Based on what I heard, and from being at the post-exam party, my impression is that there are many students in your class with good-natured personalities.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was something she likely understood without me needing to say it aloud, but it was an integral part of the issue. Since they essentially didn’t like conflict, they couldn’t aggressively go for Class Points.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think it might be important for you to be a little more assertive. I’m not telling you to break the rules or engage in foul play or anything, but I think it’s important to stand strong in the face of other people playing rough.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Playing rough… Okay. Yes, you’re right. We must get it together more, or we won’t be able to fight,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She didn’t have any concrete solutions in mind just yet. The only thing I could tell now, which was painfully clear, was that she was desperately trying her hardest to forge ahead into the darkness of uncertainty.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“About the uninhabited island exam before. I mean, about my answer…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-yes… Yes, that’s right, that is why we got together to talk, isn’t it?” she replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I quietly brought my face close to Ichinose’s ear. Though I knew that no one else was around, as I opened my mouth, I spoke in such a quiet voice that it would be difficult for anyone to hear without really concentrating on listening to us. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And that’s when it happened.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, ho, what are you and Honami talking about, all alone in a place like this?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose was surprised by the sight of the newcomer. It was none other than the student council president, Nagumo. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In a panic, she hurriedly tried to pull away from me, but he had most definitely seen what was happening moments earlier when Ichinose and I were so close to one another. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Had I been followed? No, I wouldn’t have done something so stupid as allow myself to be trailed without noticing. In that case, had he been tracking Ichinose since the very beginning? Perhaps not. It was more likely the result of the surveillance Nagumo had me under, with countless eyes watching me. No matter how much I moved around to avoid being seen, it was virtually impossible to completely escape the eyes of all the third-years on this cruise ship. It was reasonable to assume that several of them must have seen me while I was on my way here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, there hadn’t been any signs that Nagumo was trying to contact me over the past few days. Then, he had appeared before me at the time I had hoped to avoid him most, almost as if by design.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Great work on the exam, Student Council President,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The mood had been immediately disrupted, so Ichinose hurriedly struggled to return to normal mode. Still, it wasn’t like she was able to completely dispel how shaken and bewildered she felt. But even if she had managed to perfectly smooth things over, it would’ve been meaningless to Nagumo right now anyway.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seemed like you two had a meeting on the final day on the island too,” he asked. “Are you having yet another secret rendezvous here?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“U-um…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose suddenly found herself again having to deal with what happened during the exam and was at a loss for words. Back then, she had inadvertently confessed her romantic feelings for me. It wouldn’t be so easy for her to just talk her way out of this. I was about to interject, but Nagumo kept me silent with a wave of his hand. He was strongly pressuring me not to interrupt him right now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, whatever, it’s no big deal,” he said. “However…if Honami, a fellow member of my student council, might be reduced to tears, then as the student council president, I can’t just let that be, can I?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I knew it. So that was what this was all about. I&#160;had been able to guess this would’ve happened since I had&#160;fully understood that Kiriyama was on Nagumo’s side. As&#160;Nagumo came even closer to us, he went over to stand next to Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Reduced…to tears?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This might just be a misunderstanding on my part, but this is about Karuizawa, no?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He was deliberate and used few words, speaking slowly so that she would understand him well. He wanted to make sure that what he was saying wouldn’t be misunderstood.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She blinked. “Karuizawa-san?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Naturally, Ichinose wouldn’t be able to understand why Kei’s name had been brought up at a time like this.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It seems you might have only told your closest friends about this, but word is that you’ve been dating Karuizawa for a long while now. Isn’t that true, Ayanokouji?” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Dating Karuizawa.” Even though Ichinose heard those words, she might not have understood what they meant right away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, is this the first you’ve heard of it?” said Nagumo. “It seemed like you and Ayanokouji are so close, I had thought you would’ve already known.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was a slight pause, and then Nagumo continued speaking.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heavens, don’t tell me you were thinking of two-timing. Were you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t respond to Nagumo’s one-sided attacks. There was no longer any point in me trying to tell Ichinose that I was dating Kei at this point. If anything, it was obvious that doing so would just be rubbing salt on the wound.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that…true?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Ayanokouji, since Honami is asking, how about you answer her?” Nagumo said. “Or perhaps I’m misunderstanding something, and you and Karuizawa have nothing going on? In that case, you can deny it, and I’ll give you a heartfelt apology. How does that sound?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kiriyama had seen Kei and me together, but that alone wasn’t enough to give a clear indication that we were dating. In other words, that meant there wasn’t a zero percent chance that he just made an assumption about my relationship with Kei and was trying to trick me into revealing the truth. But right now, I didn’t have the option of responding with </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“No, that’s not true.”</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If I were to say that and then later it came out that I really was dating her, my lie would be exposed. Besides, it would be better for me to assume that Nagumo would have gotten more evidence for his assumptions before he mentioned it in the first place.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Actually, I haven’t officially told </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">anyone</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> yet,” I said. “How in the world did you get that information?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“……!” Ichinose squeaked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I could see Ichinose’s obvious shock at my admission. Without a doubt, Nagumo must have realized that Ichinose’s feelings were for me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems like you realize I wasn’t just jumping on mere gossip and speculation, eh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo flashed me a happy, toothy grin as he said that, but he wasn’t revealing the source of his information nor how he had verified it. What Kiryuuin had said to me before, about how Nagumo might be the type of opponent I wasn’t good at dealing with, sat fresh in my mind.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t intend to comment on your love life,” he added. “However, as I had said before, Honami is a member of the student council. And it’s quite possible she might be president in the future. I have to protect her.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can understand quite well that the relationship Ichinose and I have might appear unnatural in your eyes, Student Council President. However, don’t you think it was premature for you to step in at this stage?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You certainly might be right,” said Nagumo. “It would be one thing if you were going out with Honami and pulling the wool over her eyes, but that doesn’t seem to be the case here from what I can see. I guess you two might have been having a totally unrelated discussion. Still, with the two of you secretly meeting up in a deserted place like this just before dinner, it’s kind of hard </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">not</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> to suspect as much, dontcha think? And I’m sure your girlfriend would be saddened if she saw this situation too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re right, it might create unnecessary misunderstandings,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I did what came naturally, as president of…no, actually, simply as another person on the student council.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo shot Ichinose one final look, giving her a knowing wink, before approaching me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Introduce your girlfriend to me sometime. I’d like to see her face at least once,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, Nagumo patted me on the shoulder, and whispered something directly into my ear. “You’re free to think whatever you want about my methods. But I haven’t even gotten started yet, y’know?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Haven’t started what?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You can slip a single lie amongst a hundred truths and no one will notice. You need to make a decision before it’s too late. If you want to fight me, then come find me. I’ll welcome you anytime. If you get down on your knees before me, I’ll be your opponent.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This meant that unless I agreed to fight Nagumo, this relentless surveillance and harassment would continue indefinitely. He meant to drag me out into the arena by force.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“See ya later,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He left, leaving only those words behind. It still hadn’t started yet, huh? Only Nagumo possessed this kind of overwhelming surveillance and information network. All of the third-year students were at his beck and call, operating as his eyes and ears. For the students who lived on the school campus, that was as good as saying that everything about their lives was exposed. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, there was that other thing he said, about a hundred truths and one lie.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Right now, he was only saying things that were true, but that likely meant he was going to start mixing in lies. To an outside observer, what Nagumo was doing was simply a form of harassment. I couldn’t help but call his behavior childish. However, he had inflicted more psychological damage on me than anyone else I had fought so far. He didn’t care one bit that he was antagonizing his classmates in his own grade level by fixating on me like this.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Did he think he wouldn’t lose the trust of his followers for something like this? Or did he not care about that from the beginning, as long as they were bound by his rules? At any rate, it was clear that Nagumo was prepared to do whatever it took. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Now that he had left, though, all that was left was silence. That somewhat lighthearted atmosphere from when Ichinose and I had met up was gone. The air was heavy, and everything was quiet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">A-ah ha ha</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. I guess we, um, got interrupted there for a minute,” Ichinose said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, well… Why did you invite me out here, again?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s about the uninhabited island, when—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ahhh! Oh, uh, yeah, that, huh? That’s, um… Well, I mean… So…” She started speaking in such a loud voice, but gradually, it withered, growing quieter and quieter. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Could you…maybe just forget about it?” she asked, struggling to get the words out. She was trying her best to keep a smile on her face the whole time. “I’m sorry for not knowing anything. I just got all excited and carried away and selfishly said, um, something pretty weird, and…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Like Nagumo himself said earlier, I hadn’t told anyone about it,” I said. “There was no way for you to know.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-yes, I suppose so. Right? Yes, that’s true, but…I really was an idiot though! I, I mean, Ayanokouji-kun, you’re so kind… You’re amazing, you’re cool… There’s no way you wouldn’t have a girlfriend…” she said quietly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Despite Ichinose’s firm determination to never let her smile falter, her eyes were clearly growing misty, beginning to fill with an abundance of tears. She was trying her absolute hardest to keep the tears from flowing while struggling to maintain her composure, pretending like nothing was wrong. </span></p>

<div style="page-break-after: always;"></div>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What did a person feel inside when they fell in love with someone who has feelings for someone else? That wasn’t something that you could understand through television, books, or hearing about it from other people. It was something I was able to personally experience, though, right here and now, today.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Goodbye,” said Ichinose, managing to squeak out just that one word, before leaving.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As she walked away, I didn’t say a word, nor did I reach out my hand to her. I silently watched her back as she walked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nagumo, huh,” I muttered. “I might have made a much more troublesome enemy than I thought.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Things had turned out a little differently than I planned, but the path I was on remained the same. Although I was annoyed by what was happening with all these unfavorable situations piling up one after another, I&#160;couldn’t help but feel a strange sensation welling up from deep within me.</span></p>
