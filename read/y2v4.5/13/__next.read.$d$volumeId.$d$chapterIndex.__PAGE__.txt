<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T WAS THE SAME DAY</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> that the teachers had gotten together to drink.
