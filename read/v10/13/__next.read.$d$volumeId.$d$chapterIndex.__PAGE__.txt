<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 7:<br/>Other Classes’ Ideas</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">O</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">N THE SURFACE</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Class D had seemed no different than usual all through this exam. That was because about ninety percent of the class had been unified in opinion ever since the supplementary special exam was announced. And that hadn’t changed as of Friday, the day before the exam.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen Kakeru would be expelled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A great many of Class D’s students had made up their minds about that without even needing to discuss it, or to conspire beforehand. Ryuuen had been a dictator, and no matter how much you tried to sugarcoat it, his leadership hadn’t yielded good results. In fact, they’d been demoted from their position as Class C, and were now at the bottom.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">More importantly, though, many students had suffered under the violence and intimidation of his rule. He&#160;took advantage of the weak-willed, creating a situation where they couldn’t talk back to him. He was the root of all evil. If Ryuuen weren’t around, then they at least wouldn’t have been demoted to Class D, even if they couldn’t rise to Class B, either—or so many students thought.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">By the time we made it to the third day of the special exam period, many of the students in Class D had already decided to write Ryuuen’s name for one of their criticism votes. The remaining two votes should be scattered around so they weren’t concentrated on one person. That was the only way to ensure that Ryuuen would get expelled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki didn’t really want Ryuuen to be expelled. But matters were complicated by the fact that he was being held up by his peers as a savior—the one who’d supposedly knocked Ryuuen off his pedestal. This left him playing the role of the class’s leader, forced to gather criticism votes against Ryuuen. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen understood both what Ishizaki was going through and what stance his class was going to take as soon as he heard the rules of the exam. He made the decision not to resist being expelled. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Which was precisely why he intended to enjoy what little time he had left until the supplementary special exam ended. He also needed to think about where he’d go and what he’d do after leaving school. There was no greater waste of his time right now than to just sit around in the classroom, so Ryuuen immediately left when class ended. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki watched him go, quietly thinking about how to kill time after class. Ryuuen often invited her to join him, but he hadn’t been doing so of late. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, a figure appeared before Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, don’t you look sad. What, do you hate the idea of Ryuuen getting expelled that badly?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Sigh</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">… You again? Do you enjoy messing with me that much?” Ibuki replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not really. I’m talking to you because I’ve been worried, is all. Since Ryuuen-kun’s been gone, it seems like you’ve become more and more invisible in class, you know?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The speaker—the one currently provoking Ibuki—was her classmate: Manabe Shiho. She was the person the rest of the girls in class followed, and she and Ibuki had never gotten along ever since they started school here. They’d clashed more than a few times, but since Ibuki had Ryuuen’s support, Manabe couldn’t really complain about her out loud. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It had to have made her really upset, deep down. What she was doing now, provoking Ibuki, was her way of finally releasing all the anger she’d been holding onto.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re going to use a criticism vote on me, aren’t you, Ibuki-san?” asked Manabe.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Dunno.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Go ahead. I’m going to vote for you. It’d be mutual.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Is that so?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki’s unenthusiastic reply irritated Manabe. She wanted to see Ibuki angrier and more upset.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Aren’t you just relieved that you’re not going to be expelled, Ibuki-san? Even if a few people give Ryuuen praise votes, it still looks like he’s going to get thirty or more criticism votes,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Manabe might have been acting tough because Ryuuen wasn’t around, but she was far from the only one doing so. The supplementary special exam was tomorrow. Once it started, there was nothing more they could do.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki rose from his seat.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey Ibuki, come with me for a sec,” he said, approaching the two girls, who were glaring at one another.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Sure, whatever.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though Ibuki looked depressed, she took Ishizaki’s suggestion and left the classroom with him. If it meant being able to get away from Manabe, then going with him was a good idea.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You can go ahead and act all cool and composed, fine. But once Ryuuen-kun is expelled, you’re next,” Manabe said, a strongly worded parting shot. It was almost as if she was implying Ibuki was another person the class needed to be concerned about.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, so where are we going?” asked Ibuki, once she and Ishizaki were in the hall and out of Manabe’s sight.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nowhere, really. I just wanted to chat with you a bit, is all… about the private points Ryuuen-san has. What happened to ’em?” asked Ishizaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean what happened to them? He’s still got them,” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You still haven’t collected them? The exam’s tomorrow, remember? If he gets expelled, they’re all gone,” said Ishizaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, and who was the one who got all worked up and said we weren’t going to take those points, earlier?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that’s… At the time, I wasn’t really thinkin’ much about private points and all, and…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you want to get those points so badly, why don’t you just go beg him for them in person? Collect them yourself?” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t do that,” said Ishizaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki knew that already, which was precisely why she’d said something so cruel.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Look, as far as the rest of our class is concerned, you’re the guy who took Ryuuen down. So if they catch you talking to him, they’ll get suspicious. And then they’ll wonder if you’re going to betray them,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was exactly what Ishizaki wanted, though, considering his desire to stop Ryuuen’s expulsion. But if he did that, he’d risk getting expelled himself next time. More importantly, the truth that Ishizaki was only pretending to be the person who’d brought Ryuuen down would be exposed. There was no way he could go through with it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He wanted to save Ryuuen, but he also wanted to save himself. He was stuck between those two conflicting desires.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I… Ugh, damn it! What do I do…?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The best thing is for Ryuuen to get expelled. You know that too, don’t you?” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that really what’s best? Do you really think we can win at all in the future without Ryuuen-san?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He had us put him up on a pedestal, even though he never really produced results. And his behavior was totally incomprehensible. Honestly, the future wasn’t looking bright with him around, anyway,” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s definitely a gamble, having him around. But without him, Class A might as well be a pipe dream for us,” said Ishizaki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was Sakayanagi in Class A, who wielded such complete and total power that even Ryuuen was wary of&#160;her. Then there was Ichinose in Class B, with superior team unity at her disposal and consistently good grades. And there was Ayanokouji in Class C, who not only had the kind of physical strength capable of overwhelming Ryuuen, but also possessed unfathomable ingenuity.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The difference in power between their class and the others was clear. Ishizaki was solidly convinced that if they intended to compete with such monsters, Class D needed a monster of their own to match them. Ryuuen Kakeru wasn’t the student they should be losing right now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I do admit that Ryuuen’s not exactly normal,” replied Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She had her own thoughts on the matter. Strangely enough, her opinion of Ryuuen hadn’t been affected by his defeat at Ayanokouji’s hands. There was something that only Ryuuen possessed, something that Sakayanagi and Ichinose didn’t. It might even be something that could reach Ayanokouji. She found herself thinking about that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Damn it…” huffed Ishizaki, irritated. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki gave Ishizaki a sidelong glance, wondering what she could do, herself. Even Ishizaki, a hothead, was trying his hardest to push through this exam. And here Ibuki was, thinking about saving herself by letting Ryuuen fall. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was right. Ibuki didn’t have the kind of leeway Ishizaki did.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was very aware that she was, without a doubt, someone the rest of her class hated. In fact, if Ryuuen disappeared, Ibuki would be the next target. Manabe’s parting words hadn’t been mere harassment. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Still, if Ibuki just stayed quiet, she’d survive this time. She might even find a different path in the days ahead. That was the main thing stopping her from doing anything.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki thought back to what </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">guy had said. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“This exam isn’t easy enough that you can save someone just because you want to</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That guy understood what Ibuki was feeling, the way she thought. That was why she hadn’t seriously considered doing this. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Ishizaki.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What…?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t want Ryuuen to get expelled. Is that really how you feel, deep down?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Yeah. I ain’t lyin’.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was absolutely no way anyone would get more criticism votes than Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t want to admit it, but I feel the same way. Just remember this, though. When Ryuuen’s gone, I’m the next to go,” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once Ryuuen was out of the picture, she was next. She laid out that truth for Ishizaki to see.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m going to go meet with Ryuuen tonight and recover those private points. I’m probably the only one who can,” she added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That way, they’d keep those points around to be used in Class D’s best interests. They would inherit Ryuuen’s regrets and use them to sustain the class.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So there’s really no other way, is there…?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s just about all we can do,” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She had come to a decision. She would take all the remaining private points from Ryuuen. If it was for the&#160;good of Class D, then they were assets that needed to be seized.</span></p>

<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">7.1</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">BUKI PAID RYUUEN</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> a visit in the middle of the night and without asking him first. The dry sound of her fist knocking lightly against his door resounded through the cold hallway. Moments later, the door opened. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, it’s you, huh?” Ryuuen answered the door half-naked, wearing only a pair of boxers.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…What are you doing?” asked Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If it was something indecent, would that scare you off?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’d just kick you square in the balls and head back to my room.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh heh. I just got out of the bath. Come on in,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">His hair was definitely still wet, so it looked like he was telling the truth about having just finished bathing. Though still wary of Ryuuen’s teasing and his word games, Ibuki entered his room. It was the first time she’d been here this whole year. There were more knick-knacks scattered around the room than she had expected. It felt different from </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> guy’s room.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re not here because you wanted to spend the night with me before I get expelled, are you?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki had no intention of drawing this out by playing games with Ryuuen. She cut straight to the heart of the matter. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Your private points. Give them to me. All of them,” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Didn’t you turn me down before? Said you didn’t need ’em?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Drying his hair with a bath towel, Ryuuen took a plastic bottle from the refrigerator. He opened the cap and started pouring the contents of the bottle down his throat without offering any to Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You have no way out of this exam. Which means that money is going to go to waste,” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, that’s right. If I get booted out while holding onto those points, they’ll just vanish into thin air,” said Ryuuen. His secret contract with Class A would be null and void, leaving Class D with nothing.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So I’ll take them and put them to good use,” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s pretty audacious of you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is what you wanted, isn’t it? If you didn’t want to hand them over, you might’ve wound up squandering them all before the end. But there’s no sign you did anything of the sort. It’s like you’ve been telling us to come and get them from you,” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen had been keeping pretty quiet over the past few days. It was clear he’d only used a few hundred or a few thousand points, at most. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh heh. Well now, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> is interesting. Okay, fine, take ’em. They’re useless to me anyway,” said Ryuuen, smiling at Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He took out his cell phone and started fiddling with it. A minute later, all of Ryuuen’s assets were transferred over to Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, I’ve got them. That means our business is settled, Ryuuen,” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just as she moved to put her phone back into her pocket, Ryuuen grabbed her arm. Then, he slammed Ibuki against the wall.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, what’re you doing?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki immediately tried to kick him, but he grabbed her leg with his other arm and stopped her without much effort.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know, I never disliked your belligerent personality,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki looked back at him with hostility, as if ready to retaliate. But Ryuuen quickly smiled and let go of her. It&#160;was his way of giving her a final farewell.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re strong. But if you ask me, you leave yourself wide open. You can’t win against Suzune,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s none of your business,” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“See ya later, Ibuki.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen looked away from Ibuki, disinterested. He walked over to the front door, as if he were trying to get her to leave. There was a short silence as Ibuki put on her shoes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you have fun, being here at this school?” asked Ibuki, with her back still to Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nothing.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If you knew what Ryuuen was normally like, then you knew he couldn’t be satisfied by this. He was going to quietly leave the school, still dissatisfied. When Ibuki stood up and opened the door, a cold wind blew in.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Goodbye,” she said, leaving behind those parting words as she closed the door behind her. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki stood in an empty hallway in the middle of the night. A huge number of private points was reflected in her balance on her phone’s screen, but all she felt was empty. She exited the screen. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She immediately placed a call as she began walking down the hallway. The person she was calling might have already been asleep, in which case she planned to leave a voicemail. But the person on the other end picked up just before the second ring.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s me. I’ve collected all of Ryuuen’s private points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Once she’d given her report to the person she was supposed to contact, Ibuki’s work was done. Over the phone, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> guy had said he wanted to meet with her in person.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure. That’s fine, I guess…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was already out, anyway. Agreeing to his request, Ibuki headed toward his room.</span></p>

<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">7.2</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T WAS FRIDAY</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> the day before the supplementary special exam, and the students of Class B were still hanging around in the classroom after school. Every single student was there. Not a single one was missing. The person standing up at the podium wasn’t their homeroom instructor, Hoshinomiya, but rather, Ichinose Honami.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hello, everyone. Thank you all for carrying on as usual this week. I’m honestly really grateful to you for indulging my selfish request,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After the supplementary special exam was announced, Ichinose had said just one thing to her classmates.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sure this will inconvenience you all for a while, but… Are you okay with this plan?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Amazi—I knew you could do it, Ichinose! Heck yeah, we’re all for it!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her classmates were all on board. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This was exactly why Ichinose Honami would never let anyone get expelled. This was the reason for her resolve to protect her friends.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Four million, forty-three thousand and nineteen points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Welp, I can cover that. That’s keeping the strain on my account to a minimum, really. Still, there’s no getting around the fact I’ll be at a considerable disadvantage in the upcoming exams.” </span></p>

Ichinose was painfully aware of how fortunate she was to be offered this.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She let out another heavy sigh. If she persevered, she could save her classmates. If that was the best and only option available, then…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But even so, even at the very last possible moment, her heart was telling her to pump the brakes. She had an ominous premonition that she would cease to be herself if she accepted such a condition. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Having found her resolve once more, she smiled quietly to herself.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ve helped me many, many times before today, and I am grateful for that,” said Katsuragi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Katsuragi-san…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But, after I’ve been expelled, follow Sakayanagi. If you foolishly oppose her, then you will be her next target, Yahiko.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was precisely because Katsuragi knew that that he’d wanted to avoid letting Totsuka clash with Sakayanagi earlier.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Those are my final instructions for you,” said Katsuragi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…D-damn…!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">His face contorted with frustration, Totsuka could only manage to nod in response.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">7.5</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">“S</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HALL WE BE GOING,</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Masumi-san?” said Sakayanagi after class, that same day, calling over to Kamuro as she stood up from her seat.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Sure,” replied Kamuro.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve heard there’s a new drink being served at the café in Keyaki Mall. How about we go get one on our way?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This weekend, one of their classmates would be expelled. A student whom Sakayanagi had nominated herself, no less. And yet, even so, she was acting the same as usual.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What is it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Nothing.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kamuro changed her mind, deciding it would be pointless to even ask. Sakayanagi’s cold-blooded decision </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">might seem inhuman, but Kamuro was a similar kind of person, which was precisely why she felt it might be absurd for her to point that out. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A phone call broke the silence between the two of them. Sakayanagi took her phone out of her pocket and answered it with a thin, happy smile on her lips. “Good day to you, Yamauchi-kun. I was hoping that I would get a call from you about now.”</span></p>
<div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><img alt="" height="100%" id="INTERIORIMAGES_7" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v10/OEBPS/Images/INTERIORIMAGES_7.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></p>
<div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ve sure got strange taste in guys…” muttered Kamuro.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Seeing Sakayanagi talking to Yamauchi wasn’t exactly an unusual sight these days. They would call each other on the phone almost daily, chatting away about trivial things.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Today? Not at all, I don’t mind. Let’s meet. I’m afraid that I do have a minor prior engagement first, so I’ll have to meet you after that,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Based on the content of their conversation, Kamuro immediately understood that this was yet another lovey-dovey phone call from Yamauchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m walking right now, though, so I’ll have to get back to you later,” she added, ending her call just a few seconds later. “Well, then. It’s been decided that I’ll be meeting with Yamauchi-kun tonight.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems like you’ve been talking to him a lot lately. What are you planning?” asked Kamuro.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He’s caught my interest,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Caught your interest? Meaning you like him?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Would it be strange if I did?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kamuro pictured Yamauchi and just shook her head. “You’re joking, right?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I’m joking.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Jeez…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m training him. To see if I can use him to spy on Class C,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Training him…? It can’t be that simple, can it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Actually, in his case, it is that simple. Also, since a rather interesting exam has just been announced, I was thinking of using him as a test subject,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her words were half-true, half-lies. Even though Kamuro was part of her entourage, Sakayanagi didn’t trust her completely, so she hid what she needed to when she spoke to her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s meet with him today. You’ll have some idea of what my goals are, after that.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi smiled happily as she imagined what was about to happen.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">7.6</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HAT NIGHT</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Sakayanagi and Kamuro met up with Yamauchi at the Keyaki Mall. They used the karaoke room as their meeting spot, so they wouldn’t be seen by anyone else.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, uh… I see Kamuro-chan is with you again today,” said Yamauchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry. I’m still kind of shy about going out on a date with just the two of us…” replied Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“N-No, it’s fine! Totally fine! Seriously, I’m just happy to be able to even go on a date like this!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi, who wanted desperately to be liked, did his best to put on a smile. In truth, if it’d just been the two of them alone tonight, he would’ve confessed his feelings and asked her out. He wanted to make things official, be her boyfriend for real—but he pushed that down, trying as hard as he could to just grin and bear it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yamauchi-kun. Will you be all right in this special exam?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you’re going to be all right, then it’s fine. I was just…” said Sakayanagi, intentionally leaving a pause before speaking again. “If you do get expelled, we won’t be able to meet like this anymore, Yamauchi-kun. That’s…the last thing I want to happen.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though Sakayanagi’s cute and innocent act made Kamuro feel sick to her stomach, she didn’t let it show on her face. This was, at best, Sakayanagi just toying with him. Besides, Kamuro thought, if she let every single thing Sakayanagi did get to her, she’d have a breakdown before long.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I don’t want that, either!” shouted Yamauchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So we both feel the same?” Sakayanagi patted her chest lightly and breathed a sigh of relief. “If there’s something troubling you, you can talk to me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s true that you and I are technically enemies, Yamauchi-kun. But this test is a different matter. There’s no reason to compete with the other classes, is there?” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, you’ve got a point there…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It might be possible for us to cooperate, instead.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Cooperate…?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was an idea that had crossed Yamauchi’s mind, as well.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is just an example, but…I could use my praise vote for you, Yamauchi-kun,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hearing that, Yamauchi gulped. People wanted as many praise votes as they could get from the other classes. Students at real risk of expulsion needed them so desperately they could practically taste it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-you’re seriously going to help me?” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you are in trouble, I will gladly cooperate,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Although Yamauchi looked calm and composed on the outside, internally, he was jumping for joy. Her kind words left him elated. He’d never had such a warm conversation with a girl in his life. It would have been mortifying for him if Sakayanagi found out how inexperienced he was when it came to love.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I… To tell you the truth, people in my class seem like they’re kinda jealous of me. So, uh, well, I’m worried that those people are gonna use their criticism votes on me,” said Yamauchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Jealous?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Because I’m the only one who can meet with you like this, Sakayanagi-chan.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. I have absolutely no interest in other boys.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He couldn’t possibly, under any circumstance, bring himself to say he was a potential candidate for expulsion because his grades were bad. Yamauchi wanted to make himself look good, so that Sakayanagi would like him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But I understand. So I’m going to teach you a secret plan to save you, Yamauchi-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“S-Secret plan?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. Please find and persuade potential allies to join you. Roughly half of the people in your class. Then, target one specific person and have that person expelled,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait… But, if I do that, then they might come after me…!” said Yamauchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, you’re right. Anyone would be afraid of being seen as a ringleader, in this situation. After all, if you end up carelessly hurting the wrong person as a result, you might end up getting criticism votes instead.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi nodded.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Which is why I’ll help you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“H-how?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have about 20 allies in Class A who follow me. I&#160;will call on all of them to use their praise votes on you, Yamauchi-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Besides, I’m sure you have more than few classmates who are going to give you praise votes too, right, Yamauchi-kun? If you include those people, then supposing even if you do get thirty criticism votes or more, we’ll be able to offset those votes. It is unlikely that you will be expelled,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A-Are you being serious?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course. But, even if you do gather 20 votes, we cannot say for certain that you will be absolutely safe. Which is precisely why I’d like you to take the lead and go after one specific student,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Who?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, that is a good question… Obviously, we cannot eliminate anyone who would be useful to Class C. Masumi-san, can you think of any suitable candidates?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…What about someone like Ayanokouji?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun, hm? I’ve heard that name before, but…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, uh, well, he’s the kinda guy who just blends into the background. How do I explain it…?” said Yamauchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s quite all right. No need for details. It sounds as though he might be the perfect candidate. You’re not particularly close with him though, are you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no, not at all! We’re just classmates!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, let’s make him the sacrifice,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi’s desire to save himself clashed with his inability to sacrifice one of his classmates. But it went without saying that his desire to protect himself was far, far stronger.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think it would be truly painful to cut ties with a classmate, no matter what kind of relationship you have. So let’s not dwell on that too much, hm? I think we’ve chosen an appropriate target. All we need to do is follow through,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Her smile seemed to say that if it wouldn’t hurt as much if they did that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Next week on Monday, after the exam is over, would you meet with me? Just the two of us? There’s something I’d like to tell you then, Yamauchi-kun. Something very important,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wha—?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi had delivered the final coup de grâce, ensuring that Yamauchi was hooked. His delusional fantasies ran wild, interpreting what she said as a sign of a confession of love. Yamauchi would do whatever he could to prevent his expulsion, no matter the cost, if it could turn those fantasies into reality. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">More importantly, if he didn’t successfully carry out the strategy Sakayanagi had proposed, it might sour her opinion of him. That thought, too, had occurred to him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Then let’s begin by identifying the people who seem to be close to Ayanokouji-kun. It would be best if we can get him expelled quietly, without him hearing anything about it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“G-got it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“However, before that, a word of warning, Yamauchi-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Warning…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please do not tell anyone that I and the other students in Class A will be using our praise votes on you. If we carelessly let that slip, then we run the risk of you being resented by your classmates.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh yeah, that’s true…” It was clear as day that the other students would grow jealous and hostile if they found out Yamauchi was the only one home safe. “Got it. I promise I won’t say anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But… u-um, well…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What is it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it’s not like I’m, you know, doubting you at all or anything, but… Are you really going to use your praise votes on me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you saying you’d like it in writing, then?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s just that I’m really worried, and…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi’s anxiety was to be expected. A verbal promise wasn’t iron-clad. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you think I’ll betray you, Yamauchi-kun? There’d be no benefit to me, even if I did that. But if you’re saying you can’t believe me, then…let’s just pretend this conversation didn’t happen. If you can’t trust my word on this, then I suppose I need to reconsider meeting with you next week, too,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-wait! I believe you, I do!” As Sakayanagi attempted to pull out, Yamauchi frantically tried to keep things together. “I’m sorry for doubting you…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s all right. I understand you’re feeling anxious.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi, smiling gently, gave Yamauchi one final warning.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And also… Yamauchi-kun, if you ever happen to do something like secretly record our conversations or take pictures on the sly, our relationship will be over that very instant. You and I will become enemies.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“D-don’t worry. I’d never do anything like that!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good. Well then, Masumi-san, please perform a body search.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you please.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Fine.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Despite sounding reluctant, Kamuro proceeded to pat Yamauchi-kun down.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Things are getting quite interesting,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This was nothing more than a game to her. As far as she was concerned, the outcome had already been decided long ago.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After Yamauchi left, Sakayanagi stayed behind in the karaoke room with Kamuro.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re still not headed back?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was now a little after eight o’clock. Students could only come in until nine. Stores were going to close soon.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you think of my strategy, Masumi-san?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do I think…?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun is no ordinary person. You understand that too, don’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I know that you’re extremely interested in him,” said Kamuro.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But that’s not all, right? You’ve seen him up close before, Masumi-san. I’m sure you sensed it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kamuro couldn’t put her finger on it, exactly. The impression she had of him was of someone enigmatic, but also unpleasant.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t you think he’s strong?” asked Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…How strong?” asked Kamuro in return.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The likes of Katsuragi-kun, Ryuuen-kun, and Ichinose-san are no match for him,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Wait, then what about you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who can say? I have to wonder.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…You sound like you’re serious. I can’t believe you’re saying this.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kamuro was shocked. She’d been sure Sakayanagi would immediately say she could beat Ayanokouji.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can beat him, of course. But it’s also true that I can’t see the full extent of his abilities. Well… No, I suppose that’s not quite right. Maybe there’s a part of me that </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">wants </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ayanokouji-kun to be an opponent even I can’t hope to compete with.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A mysterious feeling, that. One she’d never felt before.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I hope to see him get serious before he is expelled by my hand,” she added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was what she wished for, from the bottom of her heart.</span></p>

<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">7.7</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">S</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">AKAYANAGI AND YAMAUCHI</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> came to their agreement on Tuesday. Starting the next day, Sakayanagi continued to receive reports from Yamauchi. While moving around chess pieces that she had placed on the board before her in her room, she kindly instructed Yamauchi on what to do and how to make it through this exam.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. That many people will be using their criticism votes on Ayanokouji-kun then, hm?” asked Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Twenty-one people, in total. Yamauchi had gathered more supporters than Sakayanagi had expected. She was impressed. Things probably wouldn’t have gone so well if he’d been acting alone, though.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yamauchi-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-What is it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It looks as though asking Kushida-san to act as the intermediary was the correct decision after all,” said Sakayanagi. Kushida was the kind of person who kept her classmates in mind when choosing her next move.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, yeah. It’s just like you said, Sakayanagi-chan.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi had determined that if Kushida couldn’t easily turn down Yamauchi if he went to help. More importantly, she’d also learned a few interesting tidbits of information regarding Kushida.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When you asked her to help you, did you cry your eyes out to persuade her?” asked Sakayanagi. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I didn’t do anything that uncool!” shouted Yamauchi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi and Kamuro had a conversation with just their eyes, both of them concluding that yes, Yamauchi had probably begun bawling to convince Kushida to help him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would appear that your negotiation skills are exemplary, then,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, yeah…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At any rate, I’ll contact you again tomorrow regarding who you should draw in.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Got it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Crucially, tomorrow was Thursday. Sakayanagi decided the real question, now, was how Yamauchi could expand his reach to win more of his classmates to his side. When she ended the call, Kamuro spoke up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you really think Kushida would help get someone kicked out of school?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If someone comes crying to her, there’s no way she won’t try to help them. And you have to be a good talker if you’re going to draw in this many students. This student, Kushida-san, appears to have quite the silver tongue.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi, holding her queen, looked at Kamuro.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you think will happen next?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“At this rate, Ayanokouji will get enough criticism votes to be expelled… But, if he’s as powerful a foe as you say, won’t he try and do something about it?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if he doesn’t know that he’s being targeted?” asked Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He doesn’t know the method, though.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He is always wary. Even if he doesn’t know that he’s being targeted now, considering the nature of the exam, he won’t rule out the possibility of getting some criticism votes. Given that, he’ll think of countermeasures ahead of time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…What kind of countermeasures?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Like proving in front of everyone that there’s a student who’s holding the class back. It could be for any reason, but the more incompetent the student, the more immediately effective the method would be.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi could imagine a scenario that might play out in Class C a little further into the future.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Take for example, Yamauchi-kun. He’s working with me to eliminate a friend, Ayanokouji-kun. If something like that were to come to light, then he’d be the ideal candidate for such a countermeasure.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you’re saying that it doesn’t matter to you whether it’s Ayanokouji or Yamauchi who gets expelled.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi took the opposing king with her free hand.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No. You need to save the king for last,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She controlled every single move made in this game.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">7.8</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T WAS FRIDAY NIGHT</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> the day before the exam, and Sakayanagi was in the karaoke room, preparing for tomorrow’s test.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s the situation?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was a total of four people there, including Sakayanagi. The other three were Kamuro, Hashimoto, and Kitou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would appear everything was brought into the open today. Horikita-san got wind of what was going on and revealed that I was working with Yamauchi-kun. I wonder where in the world this leak could have been?” said Sakayanagi, grabbing a single French fry and popping it into her mouth.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One of the students watching spoke up. “Sakayanagi, the leak came from Karuizawa. I said it before, didn’t&#160;I? I said that if you really wanted to make sure that Ayanokouji would get kicked outta school, Karuizawa shouldn’t be brought into Yamauchi’s group.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hashimoto Masayoshi. He was one of Sakayanagi’s close followers, and a student who had developed suspicions about Ayanokouji completely on his own. While in the process of tailing Ayanokouji, he’d seen him meet with Karuizawa in secret. And so, Hashimoto had advised Sakayanagi not to include Karuizawa in Yamauchi’s group. At first, Sakayanagi had agreed. But on Thursday, she changed her mind. And now, the truth had gotten out.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wouldn’t the best strategy be to make sure Ayanokouji didn’t know he was being targeted until the exam was over? Didn’t I say that?” said Hashimoto.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I remember your advice quite well. You said that Ayanokouji-kun and Karuizawa-san might have some kind of unusual relationship. In other words, if she found out, chances were high that word would inevitably reach Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was exactly why Sakayanagi had decided to postpone putting Karuizawa in Yamauchi’s group. She purposefully waited until Tuesday and Wednesday had passed, and then did so on Thursday. Given what had happened today, she could conclude it was highly likely Karuizawa had leaked information to Ayanokouji.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You made a bad move, huh, Sakayanagi?” said Kamuro, who had been listening to the conversation so far.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hashimoto offered his analysis of why Sakayanagi had made such a poor move.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If we managed to rope in Karuizawa, the leader of the girls in her class, we could have rained down criticism votes on Ayanokouji from all sides. It’s possible we could’ve exceeded our goal of 20 votes and gotten closer to 30. You got a little greedy,” he said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I knew they were going to have a class trial. It was only a matter of time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But if this hadn’t come to light, Yamauchi might have had a way out.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Having heard all their theories, Sakayanagi couldn’t help but be amused.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Herbivores will make one final show of resistance when they know they’re about to fall prey to a predator. But that’s exactly why I find it interesting. Don’t you want to see what he’ll do with the time he has left? How he’ll struggle?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that why you deliberately gave this information to Karuizawa? Because you wanted to see that?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It also let me confirm that your tip was correct.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But Ayanokouji consulted with Horikita about it, and in the process, she revealed that information to their classmates. Now we don’t know what’s going on. Even if Yamauchi doesn’t get expelled because of the praise votes he’ll be getting from us, there’s no way that Ayanokouji will be getting expelled, either. I don’t even know </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">who’s</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> going to get expelled anymore,” said Hashimoto.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Also, wasn’t it a mistake to limit things to spoken promises, rather than written contracts, when it came&#160;to people casting criticism votes for Ayanokouji? I have to&#160;wonder how many are going to change their minds after learning what came out today…” said Kamuro.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Criticism votes for Ayanokouji would fall dramatically, while criticism votes for Yamauchi would increase. However, Yamauchi would be getting 20 praise votes from Class A, so he’d make it out of this. Given that, it was hard to tell who’d be getting the most criticism votes in the end.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hearing Hashimoto and Kamuro’s analyses, Sakayanagi smiled. She could already see the outcome, she thought. The outcome that still wasn’t visible to Kamuro, Hashimoto, Yamauchi, or the others. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She took out her cell phone, which had been turned off. When she turned it back on, she found innumerable missed calls and messages from Yamauchi. He was wondering where the many praise votes from Class A would be going to. He wondered if they were really going to vote for him. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, it was only to be expected that he’d be this anxious.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, there’s something I forgot to tell you, everyone. Something very important regarding Yamauchi-kun,” said Sakayanagi, who then proceeded to tell everyone what she had apparently not felt bad about forgetting whatsoever. </span></p>
