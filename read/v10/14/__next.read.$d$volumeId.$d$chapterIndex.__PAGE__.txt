<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 8:<br/>The Expelled Students</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">S</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ATURDAY MORNING</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> had come. Finally, it was the day of the exam. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It seemed nearly every class had come to a consensus. For Class A, it would be Katsuragi. For Class D, it would be Ryuuen Kakeru. And Class B still believed no one would be expelled from their class. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, there was the possibility that none of these students would be expelled. Or that all of them would. No one could know for sure until the results were announced. Even if you tried to eliminate someone, your plans could be foiled by that person getting praise votes from the other classes. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">All that mattered was what happened now, from this moment onward. It wasn’t like I was 100% in the clear myself, either. There were no absolute guarantees in this exam.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We still gathered in the classroom at the usual time, but the exam didn’t start until nine. It was now around eight-thirty. I wondered if the school had scheduled the test slightly later out of consideration—or if it was for a much worse reason. A trick to keep the students mired in doubt, jumping at shadows until the very end.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, in the end, you really didn’t do anything?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m asking if you just stayed on the sidelines, even though you’re in danger.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do I look like I’ve done anything?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…On the surface, no, I can’t see that you’ve done anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, there’s your answer. I didn’t do anything this time. If anything, you’re the one who saved me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Then it won’t be funny if you get expelled after all&#160;that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t think I’d find it funny even if I got expelled despite fighting back, like you did.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This might be the last conversation that we would have, as desk neighbors.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose,” replied Horikita shortly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I thought we were all just going to quietly start the exam. At least, that was what I thought…but at the very last minute, the situation had changed yet again.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Everyone, please listen to me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was Hirata. He’d had a shouting match with Horikita the other day, but it wasn’t like he had actually </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">done</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> anything. He’d just said he’d be voting for Horikita. Of course, some of the students who worshipped Hirata might go along with what he did, but that probably wouldn’t become a deciding factor. Horikita’s standing within Class C was relatively high. Her blunt and unreserved manner might come off as prickly, but at the same time, people felt like they could count on her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“After listening to what Horikita-san said yesterday, as well as what everyone else had said, I’ve come to one conclusion. And that’s, well…the main focus of this exam is who to use our criticism votes on, right?” said Hirata, calm and collected.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He still has something to say?” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Looks like it,” I replied. If not, he wouldn’t be bringing this up at the last minute like this.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is pointless. He doesn’t have a plan. All he can do is talk and try and delay the inevitable.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wasn’t not so sure about that. I could see something like a kind of determination in Hirata’s eyes.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“First of all, I would like to apologize for yesterday, when I said that I would use my criticism vote on you, Horikita-san.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just as I thought, Hirata had bowed to Horikita, apologizing to her for his rudeness.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s no need to apologize. What in the world are you trying to do here?” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve just decided that you are necessary to this class,” said Hirata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Can I interpret that to mean you’ve thought of someone who is unnecessary?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I have,” replied Hirata definitively, causing Horikita to swallow her words and stumble a bit.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Can you tell us who it is you’ve thought of?” she&#160;asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, I’ll tell you now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata slowly rose from his seat and went over to stand behind the podium. Just like how Horikita had done yesterday. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I love this class. I think all of you are necessary. No matter what anyone says, I won’t change my mind on that. However, I know that won’t solve the problem here,” said Hirata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After much agonizing, this was the answer Hirata had arrived at. I doubted it had changed from what I heard yesterday.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I would like you…to use your criticism votes on me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">…or he was going to say what I’d thought he might end up saying.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Th-there’s no way we could do that!” shouted Mii-chan. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Other girls spoke up afterward in quick succession, saying similar things.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t mind if I get expelled. I’m prepared to do at least that much right now. I intend to.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Think about what you’re saying… Have you lost your mind?” snapped Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It would’ve been fine if she’d let Hirata say whatever he wanted to. And yet, Horikita unconsciously raised her voice at him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You plan on sacrificing yourself because you can’t pick someone else to expel?” she demanded.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You said it yourself, Horikita-san. You said that if a student wanted to get expelled, then discussion would be over,” said Hirata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, I’m volunteering,” said Hirata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There isn’t a single person in this class who seriously wants you to be expelled. You act as the class mediator, you resolve conflicts. This is absolutely ridiculous.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even so, I don’t mind.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was safe to say that Class C had already become a total mess. At this point, anyone might get kicked out without it being total surprise. The issue was no longer one of who would get criticism votes, but of who would get praise votes. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Without Hirata, the hurdles in our path would get significantly higher. Passing the special exams to come would probably be much harder. We risked losing one of the central figures of our class.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s no way I’m voting against Hirata-kun! No way!” shouted Shinohara, speaking up for Hirata, along with several other girls.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Each time they spoke up to defend him had to hurt him even more.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s nothing to be gained by defending me. I’ve already come to hate all of you,” said Hirata. He had the same tone of voice as usual, but his words were harsh. “So, please, just make this easier on me.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I… I’ll vote for you, Hirata!” shouted Yamauchi. “If Hirata’s asking, then I think we should all do like he says!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. So this is Yamauchi-kun’s last stand…” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yamauchi had probably gone to Hirata sometime yesterday. He must have begged and pleaded for help, telling Hirata that he didn’t want to get expelled. That might have be one of the reasons why Hirata had steeled his resolve to accept expulsion himself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, after a long period of silence, Chabashira came into the classroom.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We will now begin the class poll. Students will head over to the voting room in the order that their names are called.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, we weren’t all going to be voting at the same time in the classroom. I supposed it wasn’t necessarily impossible to sneak a peek at people’s votes if we did it that way. It sounded like this meant the school was doing what it could to keep the vote anonymous. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Well, I wonder what the results will be…?</span></p>

<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">8.1</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">E</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">VERYONE IN CLASS A</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> calmly waited for the results to be announced on Saturday. The question of who was going to be expelled had been settled right when the supplementary exam had been announced, with no one raising any objections. When the bell rang, signaling that the poll results were going to be announced, Mashima strode into the classroom. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He was calm and collected, as always. No matter what lay ahead today, it seemed he wasn’t thinking too hard about it—or rather, that he was </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">trying</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> not to think about it. This was his fourth year teaching at the Advanced Nurturing High School. He had seen many students get expelled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I will now announce the results of the supplementary special exam. First, the student with the most praise votes is…Sakayanagi. You’ve come in first place, with thirty-six votes,” said Mashima.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I never imagined that you would all pick me. Thank you very much,” said Sakayanagi diplomatically, paying the class an empty compliment for politeness’ sake. She had gotten praise votes from almost every single person in class.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Next… I’ll announce the student who had received the most criticism votes in class. I’m sure that you’re already aware of this, but the student whose name is called will be expelled. Afterward, that student will be asked to pack their things and come with me to the faculty lounge.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was no commotion whatsoever. No one made the slightest sound. The students of Class A just quietly, solemnly waited for the name of the student who would be expelled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The student in last place, with the most criticism votes, a total of thirty-six, is…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was a brief moment of silence. And then…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Totsuka Yahiko.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A name was called. The name that Mashima spoke clearly reverberated throughout the dead-silent classroom.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is ridiculous! What is the meaning of this?!” shouted Katsuragi immediately, raising his voice and standing up from his seat.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“K-Katsuragi-san… Wh-Why…?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Totsuka looked at Katsuragi in complete disbelief. The results showed that Totsuka had gotten the overwhelming majority of criticism votes. A total of thirty-six, ensuring his expulsion. Mashima went on to announce how many praise and criticism votes the rest of the students in class had received. Katsuragi had come in just before Totsuka in terms of number of criticism votes, with a total of thirty.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What is the meaning of this, sensei? I’m the one who should have been expelled, not—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The results are correct,” answered Mashima calmly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One girl opened her mouth to speak, as if she trying to explain how such an incomprehensible situation had come about.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Katsuragi-kun, it would seem you got some praise votes. I’m so glad.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When Katsuragi heard that, he understood what had happened. This wasn’t some kind of slip-up. This was exactly as planned.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, Sakayanagi! Wasn’t </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> the one who was supposed to have been expelled?!” shouted Katsuragi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Expelled? You, Katsuragi-kun? You were never the target from the beginning,” said Sakayanagi flatly and decisively.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Enough with the jokes. You definitely said it yourself! You talked about getting rid of me!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come to think of it, yes, I suppose I did, didn’t I? Well, what I said about getting rid of you… That was a lie.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There wasn’t even the slightest trace of guilt in Sakayanagi’s gentle, broad smile.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why…? Why?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The answer is quite simple. It’s because Totsuka-kun does nothing to benefit Class A. On the other hand, you, Katsuragi-kun, are quick-witted and your athleticism is nothing to scoff at. That, combined with your calm, composed attitude makes you quite useful, in your own way. This exam is meant to dispose of unnecessary people. Only an idiot would eliminate someone of superior stock.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Grr!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That wasn’t Sakayanagi’s only goal, either. Totsuka wasn’t the only student who’d been following Katsuragi from the start. His expulsion would probably have a significant impact on Class A in the sense that it would serve as an example, showing traitors would be punished without mercy. It planted the idea in people’s minds that if they were to cooperate with Katsuragi, they, too, would be punished straight away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why would you do something like this? In such a roundabout way…?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Isn’t it only natural to try avoid risk as much as possible? The other classes had quite a few praise votes at their disposal in this exam. If Totsuka-kun had collected praise votes from them on his own, then I can’t imagine we would’ve been able to get him expelled, no matter how hard we tried,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was impossible to completely rule out the possibility that the other classes wouldn’t try to save Totsuka on a whim. Openly naming Katsuragi the target meant no one was going to waste their praise votes on Totsuka.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you for all of your hard work, Totsuka-kun. Please take care, even after you’ve left this school.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“U-ugh, da… Damn it! Damn it…!” shouted Totsuka, hunching forward, looking as though he were about to collapse on the spot.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Katsuragi was unable to go over and say anything to him. Normally, Totsuka would have probably been overjoyed to find that Katsuragi wasn’t going to be expelled. However, now that he was going to be expelled himself, that no longer mattered anymore. If anything, he felt resentful, wondering why it was him and not Katsuragi. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If Katsuragi had been expelled, Totsuka Yahiko could have remained in Class A. While he wouldn’t have liked it, he could have followed Sakayanagi, graduated, and had a successful life. Although he felt bad about it, he had begun to vaguely envision that future for himself. But this unforeseen attack had taken everything from him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We probably can’t…save him by using twenty million points, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. Unfortunately, even if you add up all of our points, we can’t reach that number.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Totsuka, there…is no way to overturn this decision,” said the homeroom teacher, Mashima, also hiding the pain he felt.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…………”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Totsuka was at a loss for words. All he could do was slowly nod.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come with me to the faculty lounge for the time being. We’ll collect your things later,” said Mashima, urging Totsuka to leave the classroom out of consideration for his feelings. It would only hurt him more to stay in the classroom after his expulsion had been decided.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“By the way, Mashima-sensei… I would like to ask you just one question,” asked Sakayanagi, stopping Mashima just as he was about to leave the classroom with Totsuka.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What is it, Sakayanagi?” replied Mashima, instructing Totsuka to go ahead and wait for him in the hallway.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“While it is sad that Totsuka-kun was sacrificed in this exam… It has already been decided who will be expelled from the other classes, correct?” asked Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tentatively. As soon as the results are confirmed, they will be posted on the bulletin board on the first floor.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, isn’t it possible that Katsuragi-kun might still be affected, depending on the results?” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you saying, Sakayanagi?” said Katsuragi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m just trying to confirm something.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">For a few moments, both Mashima and Katsuragi didn’t seem to understand what Sakayanagi was saying. Mashima hadn’t considered the possibility that she might be referencing one particular issue. After seeing the unsettling smile on her face, however, it occurred to him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…No matter who gets expelled, there will be no such repercussions, no. The thing you’re thinking of isn’t going to happen, and even if it did, you probably couldn’t get someone expelled quite that easily,” said Mashima.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, I suppose you’re absolutely right. Thank you very much.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After Mashima left the classroom, Katsuragi quietly approached Sakayanagi. Hashimoto and Kitou hurriedly stood up and blocked his path to Sakayanagi, probably to keep anything violent from happening in the unlikely chance that things got heated. But before Katsuragi could even say a word, Sakayanagi went first.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would be entirely unreasonable for you to hold a grudge against me, Katsuragi-kun. This is an exam where someone had to be expelled. Whether it was you or Totsuka-kun, we must come to grips with the result. The fact is that it was the students of Class A who voted. Not anyone else,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I understand.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Katsuragi had never planned to do anything violent in the first place. He was only planning on airing his displeasure, but Sakayanagi had stopped him from even doing that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s good. I would much prefer if you didn’t fall into despair and drag Class A down. However, on the off chance that you do anything to harm Class A, then…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I already told you that I understand. Don’t go after any more students.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m glad we could conclude this conversation so quickly,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If Katsuragi had bared his teeth at Sakayanagi in resentment over Totsuka’s expulsion, then she would have someone else from Class A eliminated. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Back when we had our talk about wanting to postpone our contest, I was about ninety percent certain that you were being truthful. But I couldn’t trust you completely. So, I made a couple of moves myself, just to be safe,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know. However, you do agree this doesn’t mean that I’ve broken our promise, hm?” she replied. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You didn’t do anything to impede me. Besides, you didn’t lie.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Setting aside the mental strain of recent events, the end result was that I’d gotten an overwhelming number of praise votes. I hadn’t even the slightest reason to hold a grudge against Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you ever so much,” said Sakayanagi, showing her gratitude by bowing lightly. “By the way… Totsuka-kun should have gotten a total of thirty-eight criticism votes, but he only got thirty-six. Did you give him a praise vote?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wasn’t entirely sure. But when you talked about having Katsuragi expelled, I thought you might be bluffing,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If that was true, I felt it was more likely that she was going after Yahiko, Katsuragi’s follower. So I voted for him, even though it was only one vote and it wasn’t going to change anything.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wonderful. I knew you were the opponent I needed to defeat, after all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So? Was this all just because you wanted to mess with me?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well…I’d be lying if I said that wasn’t part of it. But there’s a reason why I wished to postpone our contest. I&#160;mentioned something along these lines a short time ago, but this supplementary special exam was undoubtedly arranged by a certain someone just so that they could get you expelled. In fact, this certain someone sent me an e-mail, specifically asking me to have you expelled,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“An e-mail?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I’m sure the sender was the person who had my father suspended from his position. It would seem they originally intended to only allow students to cast criticism votes for students from other classes. But that would’ve been far too outrageous of an exam, wouldn’t you say?” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If that rule had been put in place, it wouldn’t matter what kind of student you were. If classes colluded, they could get anyone expelled.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It would’ve been an entirely ridiculous exam. One where people could even take down the likes of Sakayanagi and Ichinose, if they wanted to.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. The current staff were vehemently opposed, and it seems they were able to prevent that rule from being put in place. At any rate, cooperating with this individual to get you expelled that way would be the dullest possible thing. I decided to allocate every praise vote from Class A to you, in order to protect you no matter what. That way, even if someone was acting in secret to get you expelled, it wouldn’t work,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, why Yamauchi? Did he just happen to be the one you picked to use?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you not recall? When we were at the school camp, he bumped into me. He was quite rude,” she answered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Come to think of it, yeah. That had happened.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It was retribution for that,” she added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So that was all it took for Sakayanagi to make him a target, huh? Well, I supposed that was more than enough reason for her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“However, I only created the opportunity. In the end, he was eliminated because he was unnecessary to your class.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, that’s true.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if Sakayanagi hadn’t gotten involved in this test, the end result would probably have been almost the same.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So there you have it. That’s the main reason why I asked we not have our contest this time around. Now, while I would love for my father to be reinstated to his position as soon as possible, and for school management to be returned to a state of normalcy…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was no one else in the special building. It had only been the two of us. But suddenly, a figure appeared, casting a shadow in the space between Sakayanagi and me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, hello.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was a man clad in a suit.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is my first time at this school. Do you happen to know where the faculty lounge is?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My, my. I must say, if you’re looking for the faculty lounge, then you’re looking in entirely the wrong place. Oh, by the way—please excuse my rudeness, but who are you?” asked Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My name is Tsukishiro. I’m the new acting director,” he replied, giving us a gentle smile and a kind, polite wave.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He was probably in his forties, about the same age as Sakayanagi’s father. A young director.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh heh. Is that so? Well, I must say, our new acting director must be </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">quite</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> directionally challenged, if he just so happened to get lost and accidentally came all the way here. Or perhaps… I wonder if he spotted us on the security cameras and came here to check on us? This is the place Ayanokouji-kun and I used to hold our secret meetings during the exam. If the director was always watching, it would be rather easy for him to come pay us a visit,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As Sakayanagi spoke, I recalled the strange look she’d given the cameras earlier. If someone had been watching us meet here, she might have done that to lure them out. That had been Sakayanagi’s plan. And it would appear that this person had fallen for it. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Director Tsukishiro avoided her implications with a smile.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re a girl who says very interesting things. Well, I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">have</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> heard this is an exceedingly delightful school. I&#160;wonder if all of the students are like you, hm? If you’ll excuse me…” He moved forward, as if he intended to walk between us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you’re looking for the faculty lounge, shouldn’t you to turn around and go the other way? You’re in the wrong building.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As Sakayanagi gave him that polite advice, Tsukishiro, still smiling, kicked her cane out of her hands. The unexpected action naturally left Sakayanagi unable to react, and she started to fall.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whoa!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I rushed over and grabbed her to keep her from falling. In the next instant, a large arm came swinging right at me. Since I was holding Sakayanagi, unable to dodge, I took the hit, doing my best to absorb the impact. I set Sakayanagi down on the floor and he followed immediately with another swing, catching me by the neck and pinning me against the wall with monstrous force.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re not as good as the rumors say, Ayanokouji Kiyotaka-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He was pressing so hard on my throat that I couldn’t make a sound. His strength was almost impossible to comprehend, given how he looked. It seemed it was going to be difficult for me to shake him off.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Well, you’ve gone and done something quite contemptible, haven’t you, Acting Director?” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was sure you were given orders. To have him expelled.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, that e-mail was from one of your people, hm? I&#160;suppose it’s understandable that you’d want to rely upon someone like me, since school officials can’t just blatantly have a student expelled,” she replied with a smile, while slowly getting back up. “Thank you for helping me, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t possibly have told Sakayanagi, with her disability, to dodge the attack. She might have done more than just fall. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t you think there might be a problem with the acting director getting violent with students?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s no need to worry. The surveillance cameras have already been rigged to show dummy footage.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Meaning, no matter what happened, there would be no record of it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now then, I have a message from your father. He says, ‘I’m not playing these childish games anymore. Come home at once.’ How about you blink twice for yes?” said Tsukishiro.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He didn’t let me speak, and didn’t even give me the option of saying no. This was certainly something </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> man would do. I didn’t answer, but simply remained silent.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So you have no intention of leaving this school voluntarily, huh,” muttered the acting director, sounding bored. “Aren’t you going to at least </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">try</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> to resist? Show me that you’re not just some normal kid.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He started applying more force on my throat. This wasn’t an opponent an ordinary student could handle. He was a well-trained foe. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You have more than just observational skills, right? I&#160;want to see what you can do,” he goaded me once again. But I still wasn’t showing the slightest sign of resistance. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Finally, realizing that I had no intention of fighting back, Tsukishiro let me go. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I officially start work at this school in April. I sincerely hope you’re looking forward to it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And with those parting words, he left the special building.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You made a wise choice, Ayanokouji-kun,” said Sakayanagi, praising my decision to not resist.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He’s the acting director. Had I carelessly decided to fight back, I don’t know what he might have used against me,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He’d said the security cameras were showing dummy footage, but there was no guarantee he wasn’t still recording what was happening here. It would’ve been checkmate if he had cut the footage to just show me being violent with the acting director.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you all right?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry. I’m used to this kind of thing. More importantly, Sakayanagi?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes? What is it?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s officially have our contest in the next exam.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi’s eyes widened in apparent surprise. “I&#160;never thought you’d say that directly to my face.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If Tsukishiro’s going to be involved come April, we’re not going to have time for me to deal with you much longer. I’d like to make things explicitly clear. I want to end this.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I do not mind. There won’t need to be a second or third time. I will happily be your opponent,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The final exam of our first year was about to begin. And I was going to bring an end to the confrontation Sakayanagi was hoping for.</span></p>
