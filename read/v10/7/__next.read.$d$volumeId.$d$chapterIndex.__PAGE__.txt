<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 1 - Prologue:<br/>Hirata Yousuke’s Soliloquy</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">M</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">Y FRIENDS IN MY CLASS</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> are extremely important to me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">…Well, what I mean is, it’s the </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">class</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> that’s important to me. I’m well aware how bizarrely backward that statement sounds, but in order to protect my beloved friends, I will protect the class. If I can protect the class, I can protect my friends.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There are dozens of students in this class. There are as many perspectives present as there are people, and they clash over the smallest of things. But that’s why I have to protect them. Before I even realized it, protecting the class had become my purpose. My challenge. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But in truth…that wasn’t who I really was.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I hadn’t always been the kind of person a class rallied around. If anything, I was a shadow. If I had to describe it in terms of what Class C was like now, I’d say I was a lot like Ayanokouji-kun. That’s why I sometimes compare him to my old self. But I’ve changed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After it</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">happened, I suppose there was no way for me to </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">not</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> change…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I was little, I had a really close friend. A friend who was in the same class as me all the way from kindergarten to junior high. Unbeknownst to me, that friend was being bullied. He then attempted to take his own life, and it was only by chance that he lived. He could easily have died. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That day. From that day, my destiny began to change. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I did what I could do to stop bullying…and I failed. Though we kept the class together, we did so in the wrong way. The in-fighting stopped, but so did the smiles. And now history was about to repeat itself in front of me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I couldn’t make the same mistake again. That was the one thing I was sure of. The only way I could protect the class.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That—</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I saw the surprised faces of my classmates in front of me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita… Just shut up for a second.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My words were rude, coarse, and stupid. My voice was filled with neither anger nor sadness. My classmates, Horikita-san included, looked me at strangely.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It didn’t matter, though.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Now that things had gotten to this point, it didn’t matter anymore.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At the end of the worst special exam, I… I…</span></p>
