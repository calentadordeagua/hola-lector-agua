<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 4:<br/>The Difficulty of Saving</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">W</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HEN I WOKE</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> the next morning, I decided to check my phone. Sure enough, the Ayanokouji Group had been chatting away at great length while I was asleep. I&#160;suppose the supplementary special exam had only just been announced yesterday, so it wasn’t surprising that the conversation revolved around that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“They’re really letting their anxiety drive them up the wall, huh?” I said to myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was plain to see from the chat that Airi in particular was worried. It would be extremely troublesome if someone from our group did become the rest of the class’s target. While I was uncertain about how much it would involve me, it would be difficult to deal with. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though I was planning on laying groundwork focused on Hirata and Kei, there were no guarantees. Even if you practically threatened someone, or made a deal with them, people could still change who they were giving their criticism votes to at the very last minute. There was no ironclad method of avoiding expulsion if criticism votes were concentrated on you. Everyone would be at a certain degree of risk.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I scrolled through the messages in the chat, I noticed that Keisei had made a rather interesting suggestion. I started reading from the beginning of his message.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Keisei: </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“How about we have one person from the group go to school early for the next three days, starting tomorrow, to gather info?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Akito: </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Since our group’s pretty small, that’s probably a good idea. I’m on board with Keisei’s suggestion.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Haruka: </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, that’s probs a good plan. I am pretty curious about what people are talking about.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Airi: </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“I agree.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Haruka: “</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Well, I’m heading out early tomorrow anyway, so lemme handle it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Everyone had come to a unanimous decision. They’d mentioned my name in the chat, but then decided to go ahead and get my approval after the fact, since I’d been too slow to read the messages in time. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see,” I said to myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I couldn’t imagine information would fall into our laps that easily, it was better than doing nothing. It was a pretty easy strategy to pull off, and it might give us results. Given the fact that these messages were from yesterday, Haruka was probably already in the classroom around now. Judging from what was said in the conversation, I assumed the other members of the group were going to head to school early on other days, so it’d probably be fine even if I did nothing.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The vote was coming up in three days. That meant we should have a solid idea of who to focus our criticism votes on by today at the latest. In the meantime, though, it would be a lucky break for the Ayanokouji Group if we could secure some information in the mornings. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I waited for Kei to report back on the girls’ movements, I decided I would try and get some information about the guys from either Hirata, or Horikita, who was using Sudou. It was important to know as much as I could at this early stage.</span></p>

<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">4.1</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HAT SAID</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">—you really did get used to things with time. Before I even realized it, I’d spent an entire year in this dorm.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It doesn’t feel like time is passing the way it used to,” I&#160;said to myself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">How you perceive the passage of time depends on whether or not you’re having fun. When I’d first encountered that concept, long ago, I honestly didn’t understand it at all. Before I started high school, the passage of time felt rigid. Not a single second was out of place. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But now, things felt different. Obviously, the days were still passing at the same speed that they had been in years past. And yet, the thought that I’d be graduating in just two years made me feel like time really had gone by in a flash. It was odd.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good morning, Ayanokouji-kun!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I heard Ichinose call out to me from behind. Perhaps she tended to head out of the dorm around the same time as me in the mornings. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I turned and responded to her. “Oh, hey. Morning, Ichinose.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But for some reason, Ichinose stiffened up a little bit in that instant.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She didn’t get any closer. She just stood there, completely still, her hand still up in the air after waving to me.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s up?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I said that, Ichinose finally started to approach, as if she had just been released from a spell—though her movements were still somewhat stiff.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh well, uh, it sure is cold today, huh?” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah,” I replied. Whenever we spoke, you could see our breath form white clouds in the air.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you have plans to walk to school with anyone?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nope, not at all. I usually walk by myself in the mornings.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, in that case… Do you mind if I come with you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There probably wasn’t a single person out there, guy or girl, who would turn Ichinose down if she asked them that question. I nodded, accepting her offer.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“………”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“………”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Whenever it was just the two of us, Ichinose was usually be the one to strike up conversation. But now, we were both totally silent. Ichinose was walking slightly behind me, and the only sounds we could hear were our footsteps.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I decided to try talking about the exam. “This upcoming exam’s gotta be pretty tough for your class, huh, Ichinose?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Compared to the other classes, Class B was overwhelmingly unified, with solid teamwork and a strong sense of camaraderie. It had to be extremely distressing for them to have to choose one of their own to remove. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah… Well, yeah. You’re right about that. I think this is probably the most difficult exam we’ve had so far,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose’s downcast expression made that point abundantly clear. Only she, the person that the rest of her class rallied around, was absolutely, positively safe. Her situation wasn’t quite like Hirata or Kushida’s, either. She was probably the only student guaranteed to pass this exam. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was precisely why it was difficult for her to decide who to cut from the class. It might be best if she stayed on the sidelines for this one, not getting involved in discussing either praise or criticism votes. If was possible she intended that to be her strategy, but…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if this is a really tricky exam, I have to do something, don’t I?” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re probably right.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Yeah. I think I have to do something,” said Ichinose, now walking beside me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I saw a faint smile on her profile.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, you’re not…thinking of dropping out yourself, are you, Ichinose?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Oh, no way. I would never say something like that, y’know?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though she denied it, I could see in her eyes that she was a little upset. She looked like she was prepared to make that choice if it came to it. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just for the record, I’m sure that your classmates wouldn’t use their criticism votes on you so easily,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I didn’t say I was going to drop out, but if that’s what you think Ayanokouji-kun…you might have a point.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s written all over your face. You’re an open book.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“R-Really?” replied Ichinose, flustered and trying to confirm if what I said was true. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Was that a natural response? Or was it intentional? It&#160;seemed more the former.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Sigh</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">… Well, please just keep this between us, okay?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you willing to sacrifice yourself to save someone else?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, not exactly. I think it’s more like…I have to fight and face the risks myself.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Fight and face the risks herself, huh? In other words, she had no intention of taking the easy way out and staying on the sidelines.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t get it. Is this like you offering some kind of tribute to the student who’s going to get expelled?” I&#160;asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though a parting tribute from Ichinose was a lot better than one from anyone else, it still probably wasn’t any student’s ideal outcome. I couldn’t exactly imagine someone just leaving the school with a smile on their face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t really talk any further right now. This is something that I don’t want other people to hear, and besides, you’re a student of Class C, Ayanokouji-kun. No matter what kind of exam it is, there are some times where we can’t really collaborate, y’know?” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, that’s certainly true.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At best, all we could really do was discuss praise votes with one another. A praise vote from Ichinose would give you a slight advantage in the test. That being said, Ichinose wasn’t the kind of student who really needed praise votes herself, in the first place. I doubted she’d sell her vote for points or anything, either.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And so, I wasn’t going to make any suggestions. Even if I did buy her vote, it wouldn’t really be anything more than a good luck charm.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Still, I have to say, the school really is terrible, isn’t it? I&#160;mean, forcing someone to get expelled? Even if you manage to get praise votes from kids in other classes, someone’s going to have to get kicked out in the end,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">None of us were exactly welcoming this exam with open arms. Someone was going to be forced out just as our first year came to an end.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you going to be okay, Ayanokouji-kun?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I’m not so sure about that… I mean, I’m not really that essential of a student in my class,” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, if you’re all right with me helping, I might able to do something,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Meaning what?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, since I have a praise vote to use for someone from another class, I could use it on you, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She’d brought up the topic of praise votes—which I had decided not to mention, earlier—of her own accord.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s just one vote, so it might not be worth very much, but…” she added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m really grateful for the offer, but I have to decline. I think your vote would be wasted on someone like me,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not true. If anything, it’s probably the most appropriate use of a vote in this exam. You’re supposed to use a vote to praise someone from another class. So, yes, I think you’re definitely the one who deserves it, Ayanokouji-kun, since you’re the one who saved me,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What she’d just said was difficult for me to respond to. “All right. Well then, if the time comes, I might ask you for help,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. I’ll keep that in mind,” she replied with a smile.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“God morning, Honami,” someone called out to Ichinose from behind us.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good morning to you, Asahina-senpai,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You look as bright as ever today. Wait a minute, aren’t you two from separate classes? You must get along quite well, hm?” said Asahina.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Uh, well, yes. He’s a good friend…” said Ichinose, somewhat bashfully, with a smile on her face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh? A friend, hm?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose could have answered in a more normal manner… it would probably have been less misleading.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, anyway, I’d like to borrow Ayanokouji-kun for a little bit. Is that okay?” asked Asahina, approaching the two of us as if hoping that Ichinose would keep going so that she could talk to me alone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. Well then, Ayanokouji-kun, I’ll be heading off,” said Ichinose. Showing no signs of displeasure, she bowed and did just as Asahina had asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry, Honami. See you later.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no, no problem. See you later!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t sense anything odd from their short conversation. If anything, it seemed like the two of them had a solid upperclassman and underclassman relationship.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She’s a good girl, isn’t she? Cute. Bright. Even the second-years have nothing bad to say about Honami,” said Asahina.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. I think Ichinose’s really popular with both the first-year guys and girls, too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Maybe you’re the one who’s won her heart though,” said Asahina. It seemed Ichinose’s somewhat odd behavior moments ago hadn’t been lost on her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nah, there’s no way.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Putting aside the matter of Ichinose, who was in my grade level, I wanted to keep my conversation with Asahina as brief as possible. If I was seen with someone under Nagumo’s control, then people would probably get the wrong idea. If she had something she wanted to talk to me about, I’d prefer she get right to it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you have some business with me, I’d like to hear it,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, you’re all business, huh? Well, whatevs. You and Honami were chatting away so happily, so I just wanted to run something by you real quick,” said Asahina.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She’d been smiling cheerfully this whole time. But now, the smile disappeared from her face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I heard about the test you freshman are about to take. Someone’s gonna be forced out of school, right?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, seems that way.” It seemed the test had already become a topic of conversation among the second-years.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know how Honami’s the kind of person who puts friends first, right? Or like, how her personality won’t let her simply accept someone from Class B being expelled?” asked Asahina.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, you’re right about that. People aren’t talking about it, but I think they’re concerned about what’s gonna happen with Class B.” I chose my words to be bland and nondescript, while still conveying my thoughts clearly.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Then, how do you think Honami is gonna tackle this exam?” asked Asahina, looking at me with prying eyes. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It seemed she was testing me, perhaps trying to get something out of me, rather than simply asking out of curiosity. Would giving her an absurd, roundabout answer be counterproductive, then?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Assuming that she’s operating with the goal of preventing anyone from getting expelled, then… Well, Class B has saved up a considerable number of private points. So she just has to gather the rest of the points she needs to save the person about to be expelled. Something like that, right?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ding ding ding. Well, I guess that’s really the only possible answer, huh?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If you began with the assumption that she wasn’t going to let anyone get expelled, then I supposed anyone would arrive at this conclusion. But this wasn’t something just anyone could pull off. Coming up with twenty million points was extremely difficult. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And it looks like she went to Miyabi to ask him for help. When she did, do you know how he responded?” asked Asahina.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m guessing he agreed to help almost immediately?” I replied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Correct.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Based on what had happened so far, it didn’t seem like things could have gone any other way.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let me just get this out of the way. There’s no way he’d just lend her private points that easily, is there?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No matter how many private points Class B already had, the amount they were lacking must be significant. They had to be millions of points short.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Totally </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">not. I mean, if it were tens of thousands of points, then that would be a different story. In that case, there’d be room for consideration. But no one can just give away </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">millions </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">of points,” said Asahina, without hesitation. “The third-year students and us second-years have gotta be prepared for the special exams ahead. And since we never know ’til the very last minute whether or not private points will come into play, it’s not like we can afford to hand them over to freshmen.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">All true. That was precisely why Chabashira talked about it the way she did. You might manage to get a few paltry points out of the upperclassmen, but it was almost certainly impossible to get them to hand over tens or hundreds of thousands of points. Sure, you could offer to pay them back with interest, but that was hardly attractive to a third-year who was about to graduate. Even if you got a second-year student to lend you some points, it still seemed impossible to secure such a large sum.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess that if there’s anyone out there who could handle something like this, it would probably be Student Council President Nagumo,” I reasoned.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah…”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hirata, you haven’t been sleeping well at night precisely because you know that’s true, right?” I said, interrupting him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s…”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata’s tone, which had sounded amicable until that point, now contained slight hints of anger. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Sigh</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">… I’m sorry. I was acting a little out of character back there.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry about it. Kouenji’s excuses are a problem.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata forced a smile, and slightly lowered his head.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, what he said got to you too, huh?” he said. “…I understand it’s not realistic to hope no one will ever get expelled. I gave up on that idea long ago, despite everything I’ve been saying out loud.” </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please, come in.”</span></p>

It was a bit glum, a bit bland, but neat and orderly, like Hirata himself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please sit. Would you care for some coffee or something?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure, if it’s not a bother.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was usually the one who had guests over, so this was something of a new experience for me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, picking up where we left off…” Hirata addressed me over his shoulder as he prepared the coffee. “I wonder if there’s really no way to save everyone in class?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I dunno. Maybe it’s just that I can’t think of anything.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I gave him the same kind of answer I did earlier. Even though he knew the truth, Hirata was still looking for some path to salvation. I’d hoped my answer would make him feel better, but it seemed to have had the opposite effect.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you can’t come up with anything, I can’t imagine that anyone else will,” said Hirata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re giving me way too much credit.” When did Hirata develop such a high opinion of me?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ever since that matter with Karuizawa-san, I’ve considered you the most reliable person in our class,” said Hirata, as if he had just read my mind.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry to say this, but I’m really not.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The coffee finished brewing and he handed me a cup.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s the truth. Though you are quite humble, so I figured you wouldn’t admit that yourself,” said Hirata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It would be a waste of time to argue. No matter how I tried to deny it, Hirata wouldn’t listen. It seemed smarter to just change the subject.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata had guessed what I was thinking. “This test requires someone to be expelled. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand your concern, but we have no other choice. We just have to wait for the answer to come next&#160;week.”</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not really.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My words could be interpreted as cowardly neutral, but they were honest. You can base this on whether you personally like them, or on whether they’ve contributed to the class,” I added.</span></p>

If things continued this way, he’d be heading into the special exam completely defenseless. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s okay. I think you should start by coming to your own conclusions,” I replied.</span></p>

Thank you for this,” he said, managing to squeeze out words of gratitude. It seemed our conversation had reached a stopping point for now.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm? What is it?”</span></p>

I was interested in whether or not he had a romantic partner because of a conversation I’d had with Mii-chan, a classmate of ours. She’d come to me before the year-end exam, asking for advice because she had a crush on Hirata, and I was wondering if anything had happened between them.</span></p>

They didn’t even have to do anything for girls to fawn over them. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think dating someone is just too much for me right now. I’m not qualified,” said Hirata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you’re</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> not qualified, Hirata, then dating would be just a pipe dream for someone like me.” Besides, you didn’t need “qualifications” when it came to romance.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m just not good enough for something like that,” said Hirata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The more capable someone was, the humbler they were. The more incompetent they were, the more arrogant. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In the end, the conversation ended without Hirata and me delving deeper into anything.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, this is about our conversation this morning, huh? Well, I’ve been…considering how to come up with a way to get through this exam without anyone getting expelled,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Have you come up with any solid ideas?” I asked, as I turned around.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, that question was merely a kind formality. We both already knew quite well that the only option was to spend the twenty million points.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Unfortunately, no, I still haven’t come up with anything… There isn’t much time left, so I’m kind of starting to panic,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Neither her words nor behavior gave the impression she was hiding anything. This was how she really felt. That said, I remembered being very impressed with Ichinose’s surprisingly good poker face back during the exam on the cruise ship.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was thinking that you might have gone to President Nagumo for help or something,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Help?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What I’d just said might have made someone panic if they weren’t ready for it, but Ichinose seemed the same as always. What I was about to say next, however, was probably going to get past her poker face. After the water in the kettle came to a boil, I poured a cup of cocoa and handed it to Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This supplementary exam is different from what we’ve had so far. We can’t make it through without someone being forced out of school. However, there is one sole path out of that: saving up twenty million points. No matter how many points Class B has saved up already, there’s no way you’ve accumulated twenty million. Which makes the cooperation of a third party absolutely essential,” I&#160;told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose’s gaze shifted down to her cocoa. She blew on it gently to cool it down.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Asahina-senpai already knew that, too. But I didn’t think she’d talked to you about it, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since she’d guessed how I’d found out right away, she must have come to the conclusion that she couldn’t hide anything.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, I’m guessing you also heard about the condition he has in exchange for lending me the number of points we need?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I gave her a gentle nod. Ichinose had a pained smile on her face.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It really is stupid, isn’t it? In a lot of ways,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The idea of lending out points in exchange for entering into a relationship. The fact that she was seriously considering his condition. That was probably what she meant by a “lot of ways.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In case you were wondering, Nagumo-senpai forbade me from telling anyone about the deal. He said that if word got out, he’d just act like it never happened. But if&#160;Asahina-senpai is the one who told you, then I suppose it’s okay,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wouldn’t worry about that part,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But this doesn’t concern you, does it, Ayanokouji-kun…?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s true.” This was Class B’s problem. This was Ichinose’s decision. “How many points are you short?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“A little over four million, give or take,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So by starting a relationship with Nagumo, she’d make up those four million points and no one would get expelled.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s one hell of a deal,” I told her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. It’d be impossible for someone like me to both go out with Nagumo-senpai and borrow points from him. When you think about it, I suppose it’s natural that he’d ask for something in return,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As I listened to her speak, I started to understand what was going through her head. There was absolutely no way she’d let someone from Class B get expelled. To keep that from happening, she was prepared to sacrifice herself. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s probably the only way I can save everyone in Class B,” she added.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No matter what I said to Ichinose right now, there was no way I could help her. Realistically speaking, private points were the only solution to her problem. And four million points wasn’t a sum I could come up with, even if I were in her position right now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you… worried about me, by any chance?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It might be a bit presumptuous, but yeah.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh no, not at all. Actually, it makes me really happy.” Despite saying that, there was still a dark cloud hanging over her. Ichinose slowly brought the cocoa to her mouth now that it had cooled down slightly. “But I really do think I might be in trouble… Honestly, if I hadn’t talked to you, Ayanokouji-kun, I might have been able to make a decision more readily. What do you think, Ayanokouji-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“About the deal?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. What does it look like I’m trying to do here, from your point of view?” she asked, her eyes meeting mine.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I answered her directly. “I think this is a tactic that only you could use to prevent anyone in your class from getting expelled, Ichinose. The option of saving up enough private points is available to you because you have a connection with President Nagumo thanks to being on the student council. This—the option of getting to twenty million points by accepting his condition—is a valid choice.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And you’re not going to hold me in contempt for it?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, there’s no need for that. But to be honest, I can’t really determine if spending twenty million points to save a classmate is worth it.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I see.” Ichinose took another slow sip of her cocoa, still looking me in the eyes. “Hey, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun… Maybe you’re actually a really amazing person?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hearing her call me amazing left me a little bewildered. All I did was tell her what I’d heard from Asahina.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What makes you think I’m an amazing person? Sorry, it’s just something I don’t see myself as at all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If that’s true, it just makes you even more amazing. After all, Ayanokouji-kun, you…” Ichinose stopped in the middle of her sentence.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s up?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, nothing. It’s all right.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was almost as if even </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">she</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> didn’t even understand what she wanted to say. It was like her mouth was moving ahead of her thoughts.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…What is this, I wonder…?” muttered Ichinose quietly, seeming to direct the question at herself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though I’d been a bit forceful in arranging this meeting, I was glad I’d invited her over. I was once again cognizant of the fact that, no matter what, Ichinose would act in Class B’s best interests. She would probably agonize over this for a long time, then come to a decision. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A decision about whether or not to begin a relationship with Nagumo Miyabi.</span></p>
