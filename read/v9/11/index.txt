      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 5:<br/>Ichinose’s Secret, Kamuro’s Secret</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ODAY WAS FRIDAY.</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Four days since Kanzaki had made contact with Hashimoto. The rumors about Ichinose
            had spread further with each passing day, to the point where it was no exaggeration
            to say the whole school knew of them. However, Ichinose had yet to report anything
            to the school. She seemed to pay the rumors no mind, going about her business as if
            nothing had changed. She stood firm in the face of the harassment. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">People were beginning to speak up in support of her, saying things like “Rumors are
            just rumors, after all.” These were all red herrings. They were nothing but lies.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Rumors didn’t last forever, though.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The plan to defame Ichinose had ended in failure. She’d made it through the ordeal
            by maintaining her silence. As more and more people began to see it that way, their
            attention shifted to studying in earnest for the year-end final exams. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But something else happened during that time. Something that stoked the rumor mill
            again and caused fresh gossip to spread. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I made it back to the dorm after class on Friday, I witnessed a large group of
            people gathered in the lobby. This was a familiar spectacle.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Déjà vu, huh?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Coincidentally, Katsuragi was standing in the same spot as last time. The difference
            this time was that Yahiko was standing next to him. Since there didn’t seem to be
            anyone else around who I could talk to, I decided to approach Katsuragi and call out
            to him.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s all this fuss about?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems like there was a letter put in people’s mailboxes. It’s similar to the previous
            incident,” muttered Katsuragi, crossing his arms in apparent disapproval. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Didn’t you get one too?” said Yahiko, directing his question at me at me a slight
            nod.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll go check.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I went over to my mailbox, turned the dial of the combination lock, and checked the
            contents. I saw a piece of paper that had been carefully folded into fourths and placed
            inside. Just like before. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If this really was the same as last time, then it should be a printout. Of course,
            when a piece of paper was folded this many times, it made it harder to tell whether
            it was handwritten or printed. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I slowly unfolded the paper.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Ichinose Honami is a criminal.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was all it said. But this time around, the name of the sender wasn’t included.
            It was also typed in a standard font, making it look exceedingly simple. Since I couldn’t
            imagine this being printed at the convenience store, the sender had probably used
            a printer that they’d purchased themselves.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The sentence brought to mind those rumors that had just begun to die down. But the
            message was different this time. It simply said that she was a “criminal.” No mention
            of </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">what</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> crimes she’d committed.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sure Ichinose must be exasperated by this prank.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But won’t saying it so blatantly cause a lot of problems? I mean, doing something
            this malicious more than once. That’s going to cause trouble, isn’t it?” asked Yahiko.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The situation is certainly completely different from last time. The last letter merely
            hinted at the possibility that Ichinose had amassed points illegally. Although she
            was determined to have done nothing fraudulent, the school did recognize that she
            had a large number of points, so they made an unprecedented announcement recognizing
            their legitimacy. This time around, however, the contents of the letter are clearly
            designed to defame Ichinose. If we report this to the school and ask them to take
            action, there’s a possibility they can identify the sender.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow. Whoever sent it sure is an idiot, then.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I’m not so sure I would say that.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why’s that?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think the culprit would be well aware of something as simple as that.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh…? Wait, you wouldn’t happen to know who’s been spreading those rumors, would
            you, Katsuragi-san?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nothing more than a hunch is all.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though Sakayanagi had told me of her plans in advance, she publicly denied the
            truth of her involvement. It was possible Hashimoto had been working alone on this,
            or that he was acting on the orders of the second- or third-years. It was also possible
            that an entirely different person was the source of the rumors.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, Katsuragi said he had a hunch about who the source might be. That meant Sakayanagi
            was obviously the clear candidate, right?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whether or not the school takes action will likely depend on the affected person:
            Ichinose.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The person who’d sent these letters was convinced Ichinose wouldn’t report anything
            to the school, just like with the rumors of last time. They were confident that, no
            matter what they did, Ichinose would remain silent. If she took no action in response
            to the rumors or this letter, then the school couldn’t act in response to her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While all this went on, Ichinose had arrived at the dorm. No—it looked like she’d
            been contacted by her friends in Class B and then hurried back to the dorm. A friend
            handed her one of the letters right away, and she began to read it. Katsuragi, I,
            and the other ten or so students present watched her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…………”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose didn’t say anything. She just stood there, staring at the paper. It took
            no more than a second to read the statement on the paper, but she stared at the words,
            seeming to read them over and over, for dozens of seconds.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…This was put in the mailboxes?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah… It’s awful, isn’t it? It’s probably in every first-year student’s…” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One of the girls from Class B, Asakura Mako, approached Ichinose and hugged her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, look, there’s really no need to stand for this anymore. Why don’t we talk to
            the teachers? This is unforgivable.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, that’s right. If we talk to the teachers, I’m sure they can find out who’s
            behind this!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Until now, there had just been unseen rumors. But this was different. This was a physical
            piece of evidence—clear proof that someone was attacking Ichinose with malicious intent.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry. Something like this doesn’t bother me,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“B-but we have to do something! If we don’t, these horrible rumors about you will
            just keep spreading, Honami-chan!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was no surprise that Ichinose’s classmates were desperately trying to persuade
            her to act. Even if nine out of ten people dismissed the rumors, that one person who
            believed them would be all it took to make Ichinose Honami’s reputation slowly deteriorate.
            Ichinose was unhesitatingly committed to keeping her silence, but those around her
            felt differently. They were all looking for a way to help her, to prove that she was
            innocent, and to punish the culprit. Their attempts, however, would only back Ichinose
            further up against the wall.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry, everyone. For making you all worry about me. But really, please don’t
            worry about this,” said Ichinose, smiling at the girls of Class B. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was almost no doubt that these letters had been distributed in the middle of
            the night, while everyone slept. Since no one really checked their mail first thing
            in the morning, the letters weren’t discovered until after students returned to the
            dorms after class. All that remained, after that, was to wait for someone to find
            the letter and tell Ichinose about it.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was one girl who carefully observed the upset girls from Class B. Katsuragi
            was glaring at her with a sharp gleam in his eyes. It was Kamuro Masumi, from first-year
            Class A. She was usually seen with Sakayanagi, but today, she seemed to be alone.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is something up with Kamuro?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No… It’s nothing.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Katsuragi didn’t give an answer. He tossed the letter away in a nearby trash bin and
            then pressed the button for the elevator. His face remained stern from the time he
            called the elevator until he and Yahiko finally boarded it, after it had come down
            from the first floor. As I watched the elevator go back up, I decided to return to
            my room.</span></p>

      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">5.1</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">M</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">Y ROOM WAS</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> on the fourth floor of the dormitory: Room 401. When I got onto the elevator, Kamuro
            got on at the same time. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What floor?” I asked her from my position right in front of the elevator buttons,
            but she didn’t answer. Instead, she stood there in silence until the door closed.
            The elevator quietly began to move, bringing us to the fourth floor, where I disembarked.
            Kamuro exited right after me, like she was following me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A simple coincidence? She was probably on her way to meet up with some guy. I walked
            to my door, and a second later, called out to Kamuro.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Was there something you wanted?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have something to talk to you about.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I would have preferred it if you had said something sooner.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What, do you have plans?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No. Is there any problem if we stand here and talk?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I catch cold pretty easily. If you don’t mind, can you invite me in?” she asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">If you don’t mind</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, she said, but the request sounded almost like a threat. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure, I don’t mind…” I replied, unlocking the door and heading inside. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kamuro ran her eyes across my room, her expression never changing. “Such a plain room.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s the first thing you say after forcing your way inside my room?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How did I force my way in? You gave me permission to enter, didn’t you?” replied
            Kamuro, sitting down on my bed.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it was the way you got permission… Never mind. So what is it?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Get me something to drink. This is going to be a bit of a long story.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Wow, okay. She was pretty brazen. “Okay. I’ll go make some tea or coffee.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t have cocoa?” Unexpectedly, she requested a third option.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I do. Okay, cocoa it is, then.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I broached the subject of the talk she’d requested while I prepared the cocoa. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So what did you want to talk about? If you’re worried about the cold, we could’ve
            talked in the lobby.” The lobby was heated, after all.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No one will bother us here. This is the best place to talk.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you want to talk about then?” I asked again. To be honest, I wasn’t interested,
            and didn’t particularly want to hear it.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you getting defensive now?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would be strange if I didn’t. A girl whom I’m not close to—who’s an enemy student

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, you sure are quite different from Yamauchi,” said Kamuro, still staring at me.

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nope.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. Then I won’t touch on that subject anymore. Whatever. It doesn’t matter.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While Kamuro might be hiding a trick up her sleeve, like recording our conversation
            with her phone or a tape recorder, it was also true that her position was somewhat
            unique. Since Sakayanagi already knew about me, Kamuro didn’t need to choose her words

            The reason she wasn’t doing so right now was because she didn’t want to draw attention

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That letter earlier, about Ichinose,” Kamuro asked. “What do you think about it?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean, what do I think?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Exactly what I said. Do you believe that she’s a criminal, like the letter said?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who knows? I’m not interested, anyway.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if you’re not interested, you must have an opinion on the matter. Do you think
            Ichinose is a good person or a bad person?” asked Kamuro.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You can’t say someone’s a bad person just because they’re a criminal, or that they’re
            a good person just because they aren’t.” Good and bad were abstract concepts. How
            people defined them could vary greatly, depending on perspectives, positions, and
            relationships.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“………”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kamuro stared at me, completely unamused. She didn’t seem to want to let the conversation
            go in that direction at all. There was probably no way for me to avoid getting the
            heart of the matter any longer.</span></p>

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think it’s connected to those rumors someone’s been spreading,” I said.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose so. I did hear about those rumors that have been going around.”</span></p>

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. But that doesn’t solve the problem. Ultimately, if the person who’s spreading
            these rumors knows the real truth and keeps at it, they can get more and more specific
            with the rumors, without actually saying it themselves. When they do that, there’s
            a high probability that Ichinose won’t be able to hide it anymore.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The water came to a boil, and I poured it into a cup. Then, I put a cup of cocoa on
            the table. Kamuro didn’t go to drink it right away.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not going to drink it?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have a sensitive tongue.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered how true that was. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s just as you’ve guessed. Right now, Ichinose is being targeted by a student who
            knows the truth that she wants to keep hidden.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And how do you know that?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know why. Sakayanagi said it right in front of you, after all.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I did remember that, of course. However, Kamuro didn’t have any reason to tell me
            that herself. Was this also one of Sakayanagi’s strategies? </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just for your information, Sakayanagi doesn’t know that I’m here talking to you right
            now. She’d probably be angry if she knew.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, this means that you’re betraying Sakayanagi?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, it does.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry, but I can’t really believe that.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I figured. So I’ll tell you the truth that Ichinose has been hiding. Besides, I’m
            sure that tomorrow or the day after, everyone will know it anyway.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So then, that means you can prove to me what you’re saying is true then, huh?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But before I tell you, there’s something else. About why I’m being pushed around
            by Sakayanagi. That’s something I need to tell you about.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Your own personal story?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know you’re not interested, but just listen.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If it didn’t matter whether or not I was interested. If all I had to do was listen,
            then I’ll just listen. If I didn’t do that, then she probably wasn’t going to leave.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">5.2</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">S</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">AKAYANAGI HAD MADE CONTACT</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> with me a week after the entrance ceremony. I’d stopped at a convenience store on
            my way back to my dorm, and finishing up my business there, had just left the store
            when one of my female classmates called out to me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please wait a moment.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I stopped in my tracks. “What do you want?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it hasn’t been long since we started school here. I was just thinking I’d like
            to speak with you a bit, Kamuro-san.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You remember my name, huh?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I made sure to memorize the names and faces of my classmates,” the girl replied as
            she walked towards me. She moved at a slow pace. The cane that she gripped with one
            hand made it clear her legs weren’t in the best shape. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If I’d remembered correctly, her name was…Sakayanagi Arisu, I think. Her physical
            handicap made her stand out. I made no real effort to memorize my classmates’ names,
            but for some reason, hers stuck in my memory.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Would you mind if we walked back to the dormitory together?” she asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I should’ve declined—but I didn’t. It wasn’t because her legs were bad. There was
            just something about the mood, in the moment, that made it difficult for me to turn
            her down.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure, if you want.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you very much.” Sakayanagi smiled happily. She sped up a bit to match my pace,
            walking beside me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not going to help you if you push yourself too hard and fall over,” I told her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry. My cane and I are quite well acquainted. We’ve been together a long
            time.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So she said, but…she was still moving pretty slowly. I intentionally released a heavy
            sigh. Which didn’t seem to bother Sakayanagi at all. She might look frail at first
            glance, but apparently, she was quite bold on the inside.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“By the way…what were you doing at the convenience store, earlier?” she asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It doesn’t look like you purchased anything.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What does it matter? There wasn’t anything I wanted.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I tried to bring the conversation to a close, but Sakayanagi grabbed hold of my arm.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You shoplifted, didn’t you?” she said, meeting my gaze squarely. Her eyes were sparkling,
            almost as if she’d found herself a fun new toy. “I’m assuming you already checked
            out the place several times, so you knew the positioning of the cameras. Was this
            your first time shoplifting at this school? How many times have you done it before?”
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re really that certain I stole something?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. It seems you aren’t taking me very seriously, but yes, I am quite confident
            that you did. If I weren’t, I wouldn’t have asked you if you had shoplifted.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose you have a point.” She’d called out to me precisely because she’d seen
            me there. “So even if I did steal something, then what? You gonna rat me out to the
            school or something?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm, let’s see. While it would be a simple enough matter for me to report this incident,
            please listen to what I have to say first.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi kept going, ignoring my scowl. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Your execution was superb. What surprised me most of all, though, was how cool and
            composed you were. Most people would also buy something cheap, like gum or candy,
            to assuage their guilt. You did nothing of the sort, and I get the feeling you never
            have. More proof that the act of shoplifting has become something of a routine for
            you.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was right on the money. Just from watching me do it once, she’d inferred that
            I’d done it many times before. But so what? What did that matter? I had no intention
            of drawing out this conversation. No matter how good my execution had been, the fact
            remained that she’d seen me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do whatever you want,” I told her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I reached into my bag and pulled out the can of beer I’d stolen from the convenience
            store. People under twenty weren’t allowed to buy alcohol. The shops only stocked
            it for the teachers and other staff living on campus. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just hurry up and contact them already,” I added.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol9_INTERIORIMAGES_5" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v9/OPS/ClassroomElite-Vol9_INTERIORIMAGES_5.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What Sakayanagi said next, however, was a complete non sequitur. “Do you often drink

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? …No. I’m not really interested in booze.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So shoplifting isn’t something you do to make your daily life easier. Rather, you
            do it just for the thrill, right?” said Sakayanagi, analyzing the situation. “For
            the feelings of guilt?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, I get it, you can see right through me. So why don’t you just hurry up and
            hand me over to the school already?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you sure that’s what you want? If the school finds out you shoplifted, then suspension
            is quite likely, don’t you think?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s only been one week since we started school. There are still so many things,
            both fun and not so fun, to look forward to, aren’t there?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you’re not going to contact the school, I’ll do it myself.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I moved to take out my phone, but she stopped my hand.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I quite like you, Kamuro Masumi-san. You’re going to be my very first friend,” said
            Sakayanagi, urging me to put my phone away.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you saying?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In exchange for keeping your secret, I’d like you to help me with a few things, please.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That isn’t what I’d call friendship.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, really?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Besides, do you think I’ll just obediently do as you say?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s certainly true that even if I report you, the penalties the school inflicts
            on you would likely be minimal. But the fact that you, Kamuro Masumi, are a shoplifter
            would become known to all. And that would make it hard for you to ever shoplift again,
            hm?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re saying you won’t just overlook me shoplifting this time, but you also don’t
            care if I do it again?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whatever you do is entirely up to you. I will do nothing to influence your actions.

            criminal acts are wrong, my words would have no lasting impact. Am I wrong?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that’s…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Regardless…I think that if you follow me, your life will be anything but boring.
            Perhaps I can help you scratch this itch that currently only shoplifting assuages,

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was my first encounter with Sakayanagi Arisu.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">5.3</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“…A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">H, MAN,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I’m exhausted. It’s been a long time since I talked that much.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Having finished her story, Kamuro gazes at me with the same look in her eyes that
            she had since we started the conversation. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So basically, I’m a habitual shoplifter.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even recently?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sakayanagi has been working me to the bone. I haven’t had the time to shoplift.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Despite saying that, Kamuro didn’t sound entirely dissatisfied. She’d probably never
            been </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">needed</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> by anyone before, which had led to the darkness she kept within in her heart. The
            fact that Sakayanagi now needed her kept her from continuing to commit crimes.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If that was the case, Sakayanagi was making good use of her. If Kamuro kept shoplifting,
            sooner or later, she’d be caught. She might have flown under the radar if she were
            doing it off campus, but the school provided her a very limited territory to work
            in. If on-campus store owners noticed continuing inconsistencies in their inventory,
            then they’d quickly figure out the truth. And if that happened, Class A would take
            some significant damage.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sakayanagi said something at the school camp about you and Ichinose sharing the same
            secret,” I told her. In other words, if I assumed Kamuro was telling me the truth,
            it meant Ichinose has a history of shoplifting too. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Exactly.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So what exactly do you hope to achieve by telling me the truth about your past?”
            Depending on the circumstances, I might be able to use this to investigate her past.
            If that happened, Kamuro would be the only one losing out.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t particularly like Sakayanagi or Ichinose or anything. But it’s just that,
            well, the truth about Ichinose shoplifting really was a shock to me, honestly. She’s
            so popular. You’d think she would be satisfied with everything she has, but she’s
            actually the same as me.” Kamuro chuckled self-deprecatingly. “Stop Sakayanagi. You
            can do it, can’t you?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In other words, you’re asking me to save Ichinose?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. If things keep going this way, Ichinose is going to be crushed, and I don’t
            mean physically. I mean her heart.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was difficult to verify the truth of Kamuro’s story. A store owner could detect
            monetary discrepancies or missing items by thoroughly analyzing their inventory, but
            that wouldn’t tell them the cause of the missing items. The loss could be the result
            of an employee’s processing error.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kamuro had said that she’d shoplifted when she started school here, but she obviously
            didn’t just steal the same items over and over again. I could hardly ask the store
            to show me their surveillance camera footage. The only thing I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">could</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> do was leak the fact of Kamuro’s shoplifting to the school and to the convenience
            store employees, but irrespective of the truth of her story, that posed far too much
            of a personal risk to me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if everything she said was true, I wasn’t inclined to take her words at face
            value. While it was probably true that she was dissatisfied with Sakayanagi, that
            was hardly enough incentive to make her come looking to me, a complete unknown, for
            help. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What was the point of this sequence of events, then? Realistically speaking, should
            I assume this was all happening at Sakayanagi’s command? She might be using Ichinose
            to set up a direct confrontation with me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You think that I’m lying?” asked Kamuro, breaking the silence after a long period
            of consideration.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To be honest, there’s no guarantee what you said is true,” I told her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, based on what she’d told me, I’d come to the conclusion that it almost
            certainly </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">was </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">true. Even so, her close connection with Sakayanagi made me wary of admitting that.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I see. In that case, how about I prove it to you?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Can you prove it?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Probably.” Kamuro took out her student ID and handed it to me. “Okay, then. Wait
            for me, and don’t lock your door.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that, she left the room. Wait a minute… Was she really planning on stealing something
            right now to prove she was a shoplifter?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I idly examined Kamuro’s student ID while I waited. About 10 minutes later, she returned.
            She took something out from under her clothes and showed it to me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, hey…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It seemed I’d guessed correctly.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I thought about taking some gum or something, but figured a beer would give my story
            more credibility,” said Kamuro.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">True enough. Anyone could buy gum. She could have just purchased it in advance and
            pretended to have stolen it. Alcohol was a different story. Even if she borrowed another
            student’s ID, she couldn’t have purchased an alcoholic beverage. It was impossible
            for students to purchase age-restricted goods.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Additionally, it was extremely unlikely that a teacher or one of the employees working
            on campus could have gotten it for her. There was no mistaking the fact that this
            was stolen merchandise. Had she done this in order to gain my trust?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Get it now?” Kamuro moved to put the can of beer away, but I reached out my hand.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just in case, I want to verify that it’s the real deal. It could be fake.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Moron. You really think I could fake something like this?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kamuro seemed reluctant for a moment but then quickly handed over the can. It was
            ice-cold, which made me think that it had just come from the convenience store. I
            gently looked over the can, rotating it slowly. It was most certainly a real alcoholic
            beverage.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you really want, you can just have it, you know?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No thanks.” In the unlikely event something like this was discovered in my room,
            it would spell trouble for me. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I suppose,” Kamuro took the can back. She tapped lightly on it with her hands
            and repeatedly tossed it in the air and caught it, over and over. “So, anyway, do
            you believe me?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You showed me the real thing. I can’t not believe you now.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m glad to hear that.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So why me?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re the only person in this school who I can come to. You should know that much,”
            said Kamuro.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I picked up the cup of cocoa I had prepared for Kamuro, since I was sure she wasn’t
            going to drink it. Ten minutes had passed without her taking a sip, and it had gotten
            cold. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I stand to gain nothing from this,” I told her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Probably not, no,” said Kamuro. She stood up, seemingly satisfied. “I’m looking forward
            to seeing how this ends.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As if trying to bring the conversation to a close, she moved to leave the room.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait a second.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…What?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You forgot your student ID.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kamuro grabbed it with her free hand—her other still holding the can of beer—and left.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">All things considered, this really raised some troublesome questions for me. Was ignoring
            everything going on with Ichinose the best plan of action, after all?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well… I suppose I can’t know that for sure, can I?” I said aloud.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In fact, it might be best to take advantage of this opportunity. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I grabbed my student ID and my phone, left my room, and made my way to the convenience
            store. While I was on the way there, I got a call from Horikita’s brother. I’d thought
            I finally had a chance to relax, now that my guest had left, but…</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, this was a call from a rather unexpected person. I doubted he was calling just
            to chat.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There are a few things I’d like to talk to you about,” said Horikita when I answered
            the call.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is this urgent?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Depending on how things play out, it might be too late. It’s about my sister.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…About your sister?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This was unexpected too. Horikita’s brother wouldn’t be talking about his sister unless
            there was something serious going on.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kushida Kikyou contacted Nagumo Miyabi.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?” I was surprised, but also impressed by the speed at which the news had reached
            Horikita’s brother. “And here I thought you were surrounded by enemies on all sides.
            You’ve acquired some good information. Who did you get it from?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“From Kiriyama. It’s clear from what happened at the school camp that my relationship
            with Nagumo has fractured significantly. I’m almost certain he’ll be launching an
            attack before long. I have no choice but to make a move myself,” said Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Vice President Kiriyama, huh. While I thought things over in silence, Horikita continued
            speaking.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t trust him at all, do you?” he asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s because I don’t know Kiriyama as well as you do,” I told him.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s all right. You always err on the side of caution.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As the man serving as student council president, Horikita always approached others
            with a certain degree of trust, whether it be Kiriyama or Nagumo. Even if he had his
            suspicions, he gave them the benefit of the doubt until he was actually betrayed.
            That was something I could never do.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So what’s up?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She asked Nagumo for help in getting Suzune expelled. Quite a bold move.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see she’s acting out with no regard for appearances or consequences, then.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kushida, after losing her wager with Horikita, had said she wouldn’t do anything to
            interfere in the future. This proved she had no intention of honoring her promise.
            She’d tried to use Ryuuen to her advantage in the past, and now she’d approached Nagumo.
            Assuming she’d seen what Nagumo did at the school camp, it wasn’t a surprising thing
            for her to do, really.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, Kushida had to realize what this meant. That every time she pushed against
            Horikita like this, she was also driving herself further into the corner. But as they
            said, no pain, no gain. You could really sense her determination.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">To be honest, while it might have been premature for her to reach out to Ryuuen when
            she did, it wasn’t a bad idea for her to rub elbows with Nagumo. Working with a senior
            one year above her meant that once he graduated and was out of the picture, there
            would be no one around who knew what had happened. But that only held true if Nagumo
            was someone she could trust.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I anticipate Nagumo, or someone close to him, will strike Suzune in the near future,”
            said Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So what do you want me to do? Protect your little sister?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If Suzune ends up getting expelled from school, that will be her own fault. However,
            Kushida also named you as someone who has caused her trouble.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo might not have been too interested in me, but if my name kept coming up like
            this, then it was going to stick in his memory whether he wanted it to or not. If
            I didn’t do something to sever this connection sooner rather than later, the problems
            it caused would just keep mounting. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is it possible that Nagumo and Hashimoto have been in contact?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why are you asking that?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I thought I noticed a slight change in Hashimoto’s behavior between the start of
            the school camp and the end. I wasn’t sure of what I was seeing, but when I saw him
            the other day, I became certain it wasn’t just my imagination. I suspect someone told
            Hashimoto something about me during our time at the school camp,” I answered.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The number of students who’d taken notice of me and could pass that information onto
            Hashimoto were extremely limited, to say the least.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s just as you suspected. Nagumo warned Hashimoto about you during the school camp.
            That being said, Hashimoto still probably hasn’t arrived at the conclusion that you
            are the student manipulating Suzune.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see.” Which meant he was sniffing around to try to ascertain the truth of the matter
            himself.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I didn’t think I’d really need to come right out and ask you this, but are you displeased?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No. Even if you’d told me beforehand, it wouldn’t change the current situation,”
            I replied.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita muttered an “I suppose so” in response.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t care if a student from Sakayanagi’s faction distrusted me. No matter how
            hard they tried to investigate me, as long as I did nothing, they’d come up empty.
            Even if they did come up with a strategy of their own, the moment they shared it with
            Sakayanagi, that would be the end of it. In that sense, she was easier to deal with
            Ryuuen or Nagumo. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, Nagumo was at the center of all of this. Which meant just standing by and
            observing might cause me problems down the line too.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve given you information. What you do with it is your decision,” said Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose so.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The call was disconnected.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In a school like this, information of the sort that the elder Horikita had just given
            me was useful in more than a few ways. Not a day passed in this school without someone
            enacting schemes to entrap someone else, which made Horikita’s brother a useful source
            of information. While he wasn’t as dexterous as Nagumo, and his information network
            wasn’t as extensive, he was far more credible and accurate.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Regardless, it seemed the first sparks were already starting to fly. I probably needed
            to move fast if I was going to stop this from becoming a full-blown fire.</span></p>
