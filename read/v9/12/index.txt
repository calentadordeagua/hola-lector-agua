      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 6:<br/>Rumors Running Rampant</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HE WEEKEND WAS OVER,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> and Monday was here. I took my morning shower and then brushed my teeth while drying
            my hair off with a towel. My plan was to take things slower than usual, lingering
            in my room as long as possible without actually being late to class. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d slept with my phone turned off last night. I turned it back on now and saw some
            messages immediately light up my screen.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Kiyotaka-kun, do you have a little time this morning? Can I come to your room?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was a message from Airi, apparently sent right after I stepped in the shower. I
            saw a missed call from Kei too, but I’d just call her back later. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Sorry, I was taking a shower and didn’t see your message. I don’t really have time
            now. Can we meet at school? </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I texted back. Less than a second later, I saw that she’d read it. Was that just a
            coincidence? Or was she waiting for me to respond?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">That’s okay, don’t worry about it. I’ll talk to you later, </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">replied Airi, making me think it must not have been an urgent matter. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I decided to focus on getting ready for now. I was out of time to laze about, so I
            finished getting dressed and headed over to take the elevator to the lobby. It was
            generally packed full of students on their way to school in the morning, making it
            slow to respond, but given how close I was cutting it, it shouldn’t be too busy now.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I pushed the button to summon the elevator, then took out my phone and sent a message
            to Kei. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">What did you want? If it’s possible, I’d like to meet you sometime today this evening
            or later tonight to talk.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The message was marked as read almost immediately.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kei texted back. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I wasn’t calling for any reason in particular, so no worries. Anyway, I’m okay with
            meeting up, but can we do it earlier? I have plans to hang out with my friends tonight.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">How about five o’clock?</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I suggested.</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;"> Six would be okay too.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Okay, let’s do five then, please. What is it about?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Let’s talk when we meet.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The elevator came down from the upper floors just as I sent that last reply. Hirata
            was the only one inside.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, good morning, Ayanokouji-kun,” he said.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is a rare sight, Hirata. You’re cutting it pretty close today too, huh?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata was an honors student, so he almost always made it to class with plenty of
            time to spare. It was pretty unusual for him to be leaving the dormitory late, let
            alone at the last possible minute, like this.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I had really planned on leaving earlier, but…” said Hirata, a bitter smile
            on his face, looking somewhat conflicted.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was vague. “But?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As we made it to the lobby and got off the elevator, I saw several girls awaiting
            us. Not girls from just one class either, but girls from all four—from A to D. I wondered
            for a moment why they were all gathered here like this, but then immediately realized
            what was going on. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good morning, Hirata-kun!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, good morning.” While Hirata wore a charming smile, he still seemed a little out
            of sorts.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This…is a valentine, for you!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">All six girls presented him with chocolates at the same time. I had to imagine this
            precise scenario had been repeated many times this morning, forcing Hirata to keep
            going back to his room to drop off chocolates. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I said a hasty goodbye and hurried off to class. I could’ve waited for him, but I
            was overwhelmed by the pressure I was feeling from the girls, who were emanating a
            clear vibe of “You’re in the way.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was right. It was Valentine’s Day, wasn’t it?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve never gotten chocolates before…” I muttered to myself without meaning to. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I kind of wanted to be given chocolates before I did something like get a girlfriend,
            which surprised me, to tell the truth. I hadn’t thought I was capable of wanting such
            things, even faintly.</span></p>

      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.1</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">WASN’T THE ONLY GUY</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> who was excited about Valentine’s Day. As soon as I arrived at Class C, I felt the
            classroom enveloped in a strange atmosphere. Many of the guys were clustered together
            in one place. Today was the culmination of an entire year of hard work. Just like
            Christmas, this was an event that put a spotlight on lovers. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, you showed up, huh, Ayanokouji? Come over here for a sec,” said Sudou.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I went over to him.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did you get any chocolate?” he asked, his face tense, like he was really pressing
            me for an answer. He was almost glaring at me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let me translate that for you. It seems like he’s actually asking, ‘Did you get any
            chocolate </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">from Horikita?’</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">” said Ike, with a grin.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t go running your mouth, idiot. Ain’t got nothin’ to do with this,” said Sudou,
            not smiling at all. If anything, his eyes were filled with this almost demonic energy,
            like he was saying, ‘Well?’</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I didn’t get any. There’s no way I would,” I answered.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Really?” asked Sudou.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sudou nodded a couple times in response, then released me from his piercing glare.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I get why Ken’s all panicky. I mean, Ayanokouji’s </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">thing </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">is a total monster, after all,” said Ike, drawing the outline of something like a
            plastic bottle in the air with his hand as he spoke.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Okay Ayanokouji, don’t think you’ve won just because you’ve got </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">that</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, okay?” said Sudou.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I don’t think that at all…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d been occasionally getting comments like that since the school camp, and it was
            honestly beginning to annoy me. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come to think of it, how are you doing, Kanji? Things going well with Shinohara?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“H-huh? Why you bringin’ up Shinohara?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, dude, enough already. Just be straight with us. Everyone already knows already.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“E-everyone… knows?” said Ike, directing his question at me for some reason. I more
            or less got where the conversation was going, so I nodded lightly in response.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugghhh!” Ike immediately crouched down, his face turning red.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“See? Even a total recluse like Ayanokouji knows about it. So did you get any chocolates?”
            asked Sudou.</span></p>

            his usual partner in crime, to razz him and be resentful, but he was nowhere to be
            seen.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I didn’t get anythin’…” replied Ike.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Guess you and I are the same, then,” said Sudou. He gave Ike a sympathetic pat on

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-well, it doesn’t matter anyway. ’Cause I got some from Kushida-chan,” said Ike,
            proudly showing off a box of chocolates with a pink ribbon on it.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Uh, okay, dude, you say that, but didn’t every guy in class get some? I did too,”

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m still grateful, but yeah, I guess it really is just obligation chocolate at best.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d never expected Kushida would give chocolate to all the first-year boys. I wondered
            how she’d done it? Well, I supposed it wasn’t that usual, given this was Kushida we
            were talking about. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The air was charged with the guys’ overwhelming enthusiasm. I couldn’t help but feel
            like it was precisely this kind of childish behavior that made the girls keep their
            distance, but then again, my classmates had so little experience in the realm of love.
            This was inevitable, even if their desperation changed nothing. Your chances of getting
            chocolate depended on how you’d behaved in the days leading up to this, not how desperate
            you got now that the moment was here.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At least, that was the conclusion I came to as I watched a girl from Class B give
            Akito some chocolate. </span></p>

      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.2</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">OMORROW, ON THE 15TH,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> we’ll be having a comprehensive practice test covering all subjects, just as the
            schedule indicated. However, as I had said before, this will have no bearing on your
            grades. The purpose of this test is to, at most, measure your current abilities. In
            addition, it will serve as good practice for the year-end exams. Many of the questions
            on the practice test will be similar to what you’ll see on the year-end test, though
            of course, they won’t be exactly the same. Don’t get careless just because you got
            promoted to Class C,” said Chabashira.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That much-appreciated explanation marked the end of today’s lessons. I decided to
            say a few words to my neighbor as she was getting ready to leave.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How are things with Kushida lately?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean?” replied Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I mean, are things going well?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know. I’m doing my utmost to come up with ways to improve our relationship.
            Are you considering helping me?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m just asking is all.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kushida-san has been changing, little by little,” said Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean, changing?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m going to go have tea with her at Keyaki Mall later today. Normally, she would
            have turned me down without a second thought,” said Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, things were going better than I had thought—if only on the surface.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So does that mean that your hopes are being realized?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If we talk to one another, we may be able to come to some mutual understanding.”
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That would be good. Well, see ya.” With that brief response, I got up from my seat.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Okay, what was that about?” said Horikita, shooting me a slightly contemptuous look.
            She rose from her seat too.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, Suzune. Uh, um… When would be a good time to get some help with my studies?”
            asked Sudou.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“My, you’re being quite proactive, Sudou-kun,” said Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I guess. I don’t wanna get expelled, after all,” said Sudou nervously. I was
            willing to bet his real goal was to get some Valentine’s Day chocolate from Horikita.
            “Any time works for me today. So?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However…</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Your club still hasn’t gone on break yet, has it? I can help you study after the
            practice test. It won’t be too late,” replied Horikita, dashing Sudou’s hopes to the
            ground as I left the classroom. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Someone shouted my name, the sound echoing throughout the hall. Well…I say “shouted’”
            but the actual volume of the words was quite low.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kiyotaka-kun!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s up, Airi?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is it really true that you’re not meeting with the group today?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wasn’t planning to, no.” The Ayanokouji Group had invited me to hang out, but I’d
            declined. I still had a problem that needed to be dealt with at the moment.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I think it’ll be okay even if you come later. Do you think you could still come?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm… I might not be free until sometime after six. Is that okay?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah! I think everyone should still be together by then!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right. We’ll, I’ll get in touch later then, okay?” I answered.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That brief comment was enough to change Airi’s stiff expression to a broad smile.
            I parted ways with her and got moving again. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I made it to Class B, the classroom was strangely quiet. There were only a few
            students I really wanted to talk to—Kanzaki would have been my first choice, but Sumida
            or Moriyama, who’d been with me at the school camp, would do as well. Unfortunately,
            all three had left the classroom by the time I arrived. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I could probably have picked someone at random, but I wasn’t going to do that. I turned
            around and left. On my way out, I overheard a shred of conversation between some Class
            B girls.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey… Do you think the reason Honami-chan is absent today is because…?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, there’s no way.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, Ichinose was absent, huh? As I walked away from Class B, I wondered if it was
            mere coincidence, or if it had something to do with what had happened the other day.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">How had Sakayanagi come to know Ichinose’s secret in the first place? Sure, there
            were conversational techniques like cold reading and hot reading that could be used
            to extract a person’s secrets, but I couldn’t imagine Ichinose willingly revealing
            her past as a shoplifter. Her refusal to comment on the rumors, even now, was proof
            of that. Would she really have let herself be coaxed into telling one of her greatest
            enemies, a member of Class A, such a secret? I mean, Ike and Yamauchi were one thing,
            but Ichinose was quite clever. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did she just succumb to Sakayanagi trying to wheedle it out of her…?” I asked myself
            aloud.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Or was there someone else who knew Ichinose’s secret? But even Kanzaki, whom she probably
            trusted the most out of anyone in Class B, didn’t seem to know it. I couldn’t imagine
            her close friends knew it either, judging by their reactions. So maybe the teaching
            staff at school, or…the student council, which Ichinose was a part of.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If Nagumo betrayed Ichinose and chose to side with Sakayanagi, then it’s possible,
            I suppose,” I reasoned.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This was a theory based on several assumptions, though. And besides, unless everything
            Kamuro had said was true, nothing could be substantiated. The only person who could
            overturn the premise of this assumption would be Ichinose Honami herself. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though this school could be called huge, in the eyes of the rest of the world, the
            campus was actually a rather confined space. If you met with someone in secret, there
            was always the danger of being seen. That meant you usually had to meet either early
            in the morning or in the middle of the night to avoid detection.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t know Ichinose Honami’s room number, but that was easily fixed—I could just
            call the dormitory management office and ask for it. From the school’s point of view,
            there was no reason to keep student room numbers secret. If you said that you were
            a student, and that you were trying to get in touch with each other, then the school
            should cooperate with you.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I called to confirm her room number while I walked and managed to get it right away.
            As I did so, I ignored Hashimoto, who was watching me from a distance. He’d been tailing
            me frequently in the afternoons and evenings of late, and he wasn’t too shabby at
            it either. I could tell he’d had plenty of experience tailing people. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">On the surface, it might seem like there was no merit to me doing something like visiting
            Ichinose while I was being watched. In fact, the opposite was true. It was precisely
            </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">because</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I was being watched that the action was worthwhile. Besides, I wanted to confirm
            something with Ichinose.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I hurried back to the dorm and went to her floor. Unfortunately, there were several
            girls standing in front of her room when I arrived, all people whom Ichinose was particularly
            close to. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I quickly turned back around and got back on the elevator, deciding to call it a day
            for now.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">6.3</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">F</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">IVE O’CLOCK CAME,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> and I asked Kei to meet me at a spot that was a little ways away from the dorms.
            It wasn’t exactly a well-populated location, but it wasn’t as though no one ever came
            there either. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, it’s so cold! Why are we meeting in a place like this, anyway? There are lots
            of other options, aren’t there?” she complained.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, we can’t exactly meet in the lobby, can we? If we meet out in the open, people
            will start whispering about us, and that would be bad for you, wouldn’t it?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I suppose, but…doesn’t meeting secretly like this actually make us look even
            more suspicious? If anyone sees us, there’ll definitely be all kinds of rumors popping
            up…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry about it.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know, I kinda get the feeling that you’re not being very cautious. But that’s
            fine, I guess.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was all right. After all, it would be a long wait for the guy who’d been following
            me. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Still, it’s way too cold out. I wish summer would hurry up and come already,” said
            Kei.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Aren’t you going to just end up saying you wish winter would hurry up and come once
            summer does roll around?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kei pondered that for a bit, then sniffed. “That’s just how a maiden’s heart works,”
            she answered, pouting a bit. “Come to think of it, I wonder if there’s going to be
            a special exam this month?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, we only just got done with the school camp. It wouldn’t surprise me if there’s
            no special exam this month.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So you think we can breathe easy for a bit?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you going to be all right for the year-end exam? It’ll probably be pretty difficult.”
            When I said that, Kei’s posture grew stiff.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh…? Seriously?” she said.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She’d managed to somehow squeak past in the exams we’ve had so far, but she couldn’t
            afford to get careless with her studies.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Help me study,” said Kei.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ask Hirata… Actually, while I suppose that’s not impossible, it might be pretty difficult,
            huh?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kei certainly was audacious enough to ask Hirata to tutor her if she felt like it,
            even after they’d just broken up. But she didn’t seem to be too keen on the idea.
            She was staring at me. The easiest solution would be to have Keisei tutor her, but
            that wasn’t realistic. If I tossed her into the midst of our group without warning,
            it would have repercussions.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would have to be in the middle of the night. Is that okay?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s way better than getting expelled.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well put. “All right, then. I’ll go ahead and put together a schedule.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thanks.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even if we got through the year-end exam, though, there’d be new problems on the horizon
            soon after. We could probably expect to see a massive special exam around the start
            of March. If we made it safely through that, we’d have completed our first year of
            school. The school would keep us fighting to the bitter end, so we couldn’t afford
            to let our guards down.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, anyway, what did you need from me?” asked Kei, seeming a little fidgety and nervous
            for some reason.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Something up?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, not really. It’s just…I was thinking it seemed like you really wanted to meet
            with me today, for some reason.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I just had something I wanted to confirm. It would’ve been fine if we didn’t get
            to it today.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmph,” she scoffed, shooting me a look of suspicion. Deciding to pay it no mind,
            I focused on the topic at hand.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Any idea whose number this might be?” I asked, showing her the unregistered phone
            number that I had gotten a call from the other day. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Who is that from? What, are you getting calls from strangers or something?”
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems that way.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kei brought up her contacts and manually entered the number via the keypad. If the
            number was registered in her contacts, then the person’s name and other information
            should show up.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Doesn’t seem like anything’s coming up.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I do have more contacts in my phone than the average girl, but I don’t know most
            of the seniors,” said Kei.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d hoped she’d get a hit after checking her contacts, giving me a new lead to investigate.
            But I supposed the odds of that happening were slim, after all.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why not just try calling the number back directly?” she asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve tried doing that several times, but whoever it is has their phone off.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm…? If it’s important, do you want me to look into it for you?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure. That was why I called you out here today, anyway. But don’t do anything careless,”
            I told her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Got it,” answered Kei, nodding, taking note of the number. “Is that all?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yep. See ya.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I tried to bring our conversation to a close, but Kei hurriedly moved to stop me,
            looking flustered.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, uh, by the way, um… there’s something I kind of wanted to talk to you about.
            Can I ask you a question?” A strange question, as it turned out. “What day is today?
            Okay, 5, 4, 3—”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…That’s a much easier question than I expected. Actually, it’s so easy that I’m worried
            I’m going to give you the wrong answer after all,” I replied.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t overthink it. Just give me a straight answer.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Valen—”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, that is correct,” she replied, cutting me off.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I felt my head being lightly bonked by a box.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re giving me this?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I got it for Yousuke-kun, but there’s no need to give it to him anymore.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For Hirata, huh?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What? You don’t like it?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, it’s not that. I was just thinking that you must have prepared this quite a long
            while in advance,” I replied. Kei had decided to break it off with Hirata already
            well over a month ago now.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I prepare for things very thoroughly! Even though I decided I was going to break
            up with him, I thought it might still be useful to have, okay? Well, I guess it’s
            not like I should expect someone like you, a complete beginner to the world of romance,
            to understand.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I supposed she had a point.</span></p>

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was just thinking that maybe you chose today to meet because you were hoping to
            get some chocolate from me,” she added.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry. Didn’t think about that at all,” I replied.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kei wore a look of irritation for a moment but then quickly recovered and changed
            the topic of conversation, like she was trying to avoid something. “By the way, did
            you get anything from other girls?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nope, didn’t get anything.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d decided beforehand to tell her that regardless of whether I actually received
            anything or not.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ha, too bad, so sad. Guess you’re a guy with zero prospects, then,” she replied,
            switching to making fun of me at the drop of a hat.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you sure you want to give this to me, then? If you give it to me, that means
            I no longer have zero prospects, right?” I replied.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That just makes it even more pathetic. That means you’ve gotta come to me for salvation.”
            She was really condescending to me now. “Oh, by the way, feel free to return the favor
            by paying me back a thousand times over, if you like.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, that was just extremely absurd.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“By the way, um—”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kei tried to change the subject yet again, but the words seemed to get stuck in her
            throat when she met my gaze. We stood close together, gazing into each other’s eyes,
            until I slowly shifted my line of sight in the direction of the dormitory.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I’ll head back to my room then,” said Kei.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay. See you.”</span></p>
