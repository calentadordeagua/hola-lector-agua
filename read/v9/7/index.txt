      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 1 - Prologue:<br/>Ichinose Honami’s Soliloquy</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">’VE NEVER REALLY THOUGHT</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> of myself as a good person, never thought of myself as a bad person. I suppose I’ve
            managed to become an honest person, just like my mother would’ve wanted.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Things were fine in elementary school and in junior high. I had lots of friends, guys
            and girls—everybody. I had a little trouble handling sports, sure, but I pushed myself
            just like I did with my studies. By my third year of junior high, I’d managed to become
            student council president, something I’d always aspired to. I’d even managed to get
            into a private high school as a scholarship student.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A nice school life.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A nice home life.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But I…I made a mistake. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Something that never should’ve happened. An unforgivable “mistake” that I never should’ve
            made.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The angry look on my ailing mother’s face as she lay sick in bed. Her tears. The look
            of heartbreak on my little sister’s face, hurt and distant as she was, retreating
            into herself, shutting herself away… I can’t ever forget it. It comes to me even now.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My trembling fingers.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My trembling body. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The creeping blackness spreading across my heart.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I tossed my third-year of junior high in the trash. For about half a year, I shut
            myself away.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But…one day, that all came to an end.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I learned about this school, I knew I needed to put an end to it. I’d …bring
            smiles back to the faces of my mother and little sister again. I wouldn’t run away
            from my own “sin.” No, I’d face it head-on.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Or so I swore.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But…</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I enrolled in this school with a dream, but an ordeal awaited me. When I found the
            letter, I completely froze, and all around me, my classmates turned to watch curiously.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I read the letter over and over. But no matter how many times I read it, the words
            refused to change.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Ichinose Honami is a criminal.”</span></p>

      <p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol9_INTERIORIMAGES_" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v9/OPS/ClassroomElite-Vol9_INTERIORIMAGES_.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">1.1</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">L</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">ONG, LONG BEFORE THAT INCIDENT,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> she’d been in the student council office on a day when school was off, feeling incredibly
            nervous.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ichinose Honami from first-year, Class B, right?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes,” she managed to squeeze out. Ichinose Honami faced Student Council Vice President
            Nagumo, her nerves clear on her face.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This was a special one-on-one interview. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What did the Student Council President say to you?” he asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He said that now still wasn’t the time…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose had wished to join the student council right from the start. She’d come knocking
            at their door right after enrolling, but Student Council President Horikita had rejected
            her request after interviewing her, leaving her dejected. However, Vice President
            Nagumo reached out to her as soon as he heard what happened.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Why? Three reasons: First, she belonged to Class B, just like him. Second, her grades
            were excellent. Finally, she was quite attractive, which Nagumo always appreciated
            from the fairer sex.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And Ichinose easily met that last and most important requirement. The first two reasons
            were, after all, just icing on the cake. She had aesthetic value to him, which made
            her a valuable piece of property to be kept by his side. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I heard that you were on the student council when you were in junior high,” said
            Nagumo. “The president, in fact. Is that right?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. That’s why I wanted to join the one here.” Ichinose told the truth. But also
            a lie.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, your homeroom teacher, Hoshinomiya-sensei, told me. It sounds like you had excellent
            scores on your entrance exam, too,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you very much,” she said humbly, though she didn’t meet Nagumo’s eyes.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You are, honestly, quite exceptional.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, but…Student Council President Horikita didn’t acknowledge any of it,” said Ichinose
            with a bitter smile. Shame nipped at her, shame that she hadn’t managed to secure
            the position she knew she deserved. Even so, that faint hint of a smile remained.
            She wouldn’t be able to make a good impression if she came in here looking all down
            in the dumps.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, President Horikita’s quite strict. Frankly, he probably passed on you because
            you weren’t placed in Class A. He’s a real stickler for position, you see.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see,” said Ichinose. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, Nagumo was lying.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At first glance, Horikita Manabu </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">looked</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> like the sort who would fixate and fuss about position. In truth, he was the very
            opposite. He looked deep within a person and saw the core of who they were. He knew
            the exceptional when he witnessed it, whether they were in Class D or Class A. But
            to Ichinose, still stung by his rejection, what Nagumo said felt like the truth.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose sighed. “I guess I have no choice but to get to Class A if I want to join
            the student council.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know about that. Even if you do make it to Class A, President Horikita may
            never acknowledge you. The fact is, Ichinose, that you were never considered a thoroughbred
            from the moment you entered this school. No matter how hard you work from here on,
            President Horikita will never accept a student who was placed in Class B,” said Nagumo.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With one cruel statement, the faint smile on Ichinose’s face vanished.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“B-but, you’re in Class B, Nagumo-senpai. And you’re vice president, so—”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo immediately dashed whatever faint hopes she had left.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In my case, there were two reasons. You see, I joined the student council before
            Horikita-senpai became president. Back then, it was a different third-year student
            who served as president. Horikita-senpai was the Vice President, and the sole person
            on the student council to object to my appointment. He fought until the bitter end.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose’s expression grew sadder. Seeing that filled Nagumo’s heart with joy. He
            decided that he definitely needed her on the student council, to toy with as his personal
            plaything.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The other thing,” he continued, “is that I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">know </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">myself. I know my high potential. Rightfully, I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">should </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">have been placed in Class A. When I expressed my desire to join the student council,
            I confessed the full truth behind why they’d assigned me to Class B. I told them everything
            right away.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Confessed…?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Indeed. I had proven that in terms of ability, I wasn’t willing to play second fiddle
            to Class A. That’s what led to my current position.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nagumo-senpai,” said Ichinose, “what…were those things you confessed?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo bit back a smile. “Now, now, Ichinose. Aren’t you the one being questioned?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“M-Me?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not quite convinced of something. See…normally, it would’ve been perfectly reasonable
            for you to be placed in Class A. Your grades are excellent, and your communication
            skills are nothing to scoff at. And if you take into account your track record as
            student council president…well, why would you wind up in Class B? There must be some
            reason.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose couldn’t hide her embarrassment at his deduction, but this was an inevitable
            outcome. Nagumo had made his conclusions based on information he’d obtained from Ichinose’s
            homeroom teacher, Hoshinomiya-sensei, ahead of time.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tell me,” he continued, “here and now, what you think the reason is. If you can convince
            me that you are a student worthy of Class A, then I shall take responsibility for
            bringing you onto the student council.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that… possible?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“President Horikita’s authority is absolute, certainly. But what will happen after
            Horikita-senpai graduates? If first-years don’t join the student council, it will
            be impossible for us to train future members. Imagine the trouble that would cause
            for the next student council president—namely, me. Do you understand?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I…suppose I do, yes…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Someone who can’t seize this chance isn’t qualified to join the student council.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose’s secret gnawed at her from deep inside. Memories of spending half of her
            third year of junior high cooped up in her room came rushing back. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What I say here—”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Will be confidential. Your secrets are ours to keep, and ours alone.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The past that she’d thought she could live with. The past that she’d thought she could
            conceal forever. But how could she move forward without trusting others? She’d lost
            that trust…so maybe confiding in people would help her to trust again. Would help
            others to trust her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I… I—”</span></p>
