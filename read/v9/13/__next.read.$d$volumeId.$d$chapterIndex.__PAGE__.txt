      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 7:<br/>Ambiguous Things</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">F</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">OR HASHIMOTO MASAYOSHI,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> the question of who to follow was trivial. Actually, it wasn’t an exaggeration to
            say that he didn’t care at all. It didn’t matter to him whether Sakayanagi or Katsuragi
            led Class A; he would simply use whoever served him best. That was all there was to
            it. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While he’d been fortunate enough to start out in Class A, he had considered the possibility
            of dropping down to Class B or Class C along the way. The important thing for him
            was to get into a position where he could turn the tables on his foes, whoever they
            were, and pull off a late-stage victory in the end. That was precisely why he’d made
            contact with Ryuuen Kakeru at an early stage in his rise to power, sensing his potential.
            Ryuuen was a person of exceptional talent, capable of defeating both Sakayanagi and
            Ichinose. Hashimoto recognized his unsettling power. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If necessary, Hashimoto wouldn’t hesitate to leak information about Class A to Ryuuen.
            Of course, he was currently conducting reconnaissance on Sakayanagi’s behalf, at most.
            However, if Ryuuen ever did surpass the others, Hashimoto was prepared to betray Sakayanagi.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He’d also set his sights on Ichinose from Class B, in the same fashion. But Ichinose
            wasn’t like Ryuuen and Sakayanagi. You couldn’t engage her with shady, underhanded
            tactics. And so, rather than trying to force the issue, Hashimoto chose instead to
            remove the obstacles in the way of his objective. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He’d made contact with a girl in Class B who was close to Ichinose. He wasn’t able
            to get her to go so far as to betray Ichinose, but the connection had been established
            at least, and he went on to establish similar relationships with people from each
            of the four classes. There was simply no such thing as having too much insurance against
            unexpected events. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And today, he was trying to make preliminary arrangements in preparation for one such
            “unexpected event.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“U-um, Hashimoto-kun, do you have a minute?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One of Hashimoto’s classmates, a girl named Motodoi Chikako, called out to him in
            the hallway after class. Like Hashimoto, she was in the tennis club. Apparently, she’d
            run after him after he left the classroom. She looked nervous and fidgety. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hashimoto immediately understood what was happening without her saying a word. Today
            was February 14th. He’d already experienced this sort of thing many times. Of course,
            even though he understood what was going on, he didn’t let it show on his face. Nor
            did he say a word.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s up, Motodoi? Something you wanna talk to me about?” he asked gently.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At that, Motodoi seemed to gather the courage to come out with it. “Here, for you.
            Chocolate. Because it’s Valentine’s Day today,” she replied, handing some chocolate
            to Hashimoto, which he immediately accepted.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thanks, Motodoi. This makes me really happy.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I’m glad!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hashimoto had noticed some time ago that Motodoi had been looking at him fondly for
            a while now. The chocolate she’d just given him was undoubtedly an expression of romantic
            feelings. While he felt confident that she’d say yes if he asked her to go out with
            him, he felt nothing whatsoever for Motodoi in return.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">For better or worse, he thought of her only as someone who wasn’t worth using. He
            had already determined that there was absolutely zero benefit to going out with her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You should drop by the club every now and again,” she added.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry. Guess I’ve been skipping all the time lately, huh?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seriously! Our senpai are so totally exasperated.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll remember that. Anyway, I’ll make sure to thank you properly next month,” Hashimoto
            said.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“O-okay.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Motodoi blushed, nodded, and then ran off, as if trying to escape her embarrassment.
            There was absolutely no chance that he’d go out with her, but Hashimoto still left
            the option open by hinting to her about it. Something might change in the future,
            after all.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He sped up his pace a bit as he made his way to first-year Class C, trying to make
            up for lost time. There was currently one person occupying far more of his attention
            than Motodoi, and it was a boy from Class C named Ayanokouji Kiyotaka.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why can’t I stop thinking about him?” murmured Hashimoto. A part of him couldn’t
            help but wonder.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Prior to the school camp, Ayanokouji hadn’t even been on Hashimoto’s radar. He’d barely
            known what he looked like. He remembered that Ayanokouji ran a fierce race against
            the former student council president during the sports festival, but that was it,
            and Hashimoto wasn’t about to drastically change his evaluation of someone just because
            they were a fast runner. More importantly, he didn’t pay Ayanokouji much mind because
            both Sakayanagi and Ryuuen, who had keen instincts when it came to sussing out other
            powerful people, didn’t seem to be paying Ayanokouji any extra attention.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, something had happened recently to make him change his mind about Ayanokouji.
            A puzzling remark made by the student council president, Nagumo Miyabi. Miyabi had
            made a mysterious claim that Horikita Manabu held Ayanokouji in higher regard than
            he did anyone else. Hashimoto tried to dismiss the idea as nothing more than a joke,
            but he couldn’t do it.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In retrospect, the signs were all there. Why did Ayanokouji and the former student
            council president directly confront one another during the sports festival relay?
            What if that wasn’t mere coincidence, but intentional? What if there was a reason
            they’d wanted to make a big show of competing against one another? Those questions
            began to swirl around in Hashimoto’s head.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Additionally, he remained unsure about the matter of Ishizaki and the others supposedly
            overthrowing Ryuuen. Class C had been Class D, the lowest-ranked class by far, back
            in the spring. But they began steadily closing the gap between them and the upper-level
            classes. What if Ayanokouji had something to do with it…?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What if he surpasses both Sakayanagi and Ryuuen…?” wondered Hashimoto.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As of this moment, he couldn’t really imagine that happening. All he had to go on
            was mere suspicions; a series of paranoid delusions that might have gone too far.
            He was missing a crucial part of the puzzle. Nagumo’s words might have been nothing
            more than an unfounded joke; what happened at the sports festival relay might just
            have been a product of Hashimoto’s imagination. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Which was why he was taking action to discover the truth.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While acting under Sakayanagi’s orders to spread rumors about Ichinose, Hashimoto
            had recently found himself with some free time to spare. He’d spent it following Ayanokouji
            around to try to find out what he was up to. Now, he arrived at Class C, only to find
            Ayanokouji was already gone.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You never waste any time, huh, Ayanokouji?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Perhaps because his circle of friends was rather limited, Ayanokouji hardly ever stayed
            back in the classroom after classes ended for the day. Was he with that close group
            of friends of his again today, Hashimoto wondered, the one including Miyake and Yukimura
            and the others? But Yukimura and Sakura were still in the classroom, so he eliminated
            that possibility. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yo, Hirata.” Just standing there idly and observing another class would draw attention
            to himself, so Hashimoto quickly called out to Hirata, who hadn’t yet left to go to
            his club.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, hey there, Hashimoto-kun. What’s up?” replied Hirata.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just came by to check and see whether you got yourself a new girlfriend.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I’m not really thinking about jumping into another relationship right now.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So you’re still in the process of mending your broken heart, eh?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ha ha… Something like that, I suppose,” said Hirata.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ll have to tell me more about it sometime, then. Oh, by the way, I’ve been trying
            to get contact info for the guys I was with back at the school camp. I was thinking
            I’d ask Ayanokouji next, but it looks like he’s already left,” said Hashimoto.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You didn’t bump into him? I think he only just left a minute or two ago…” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just a hair too late, then. Hashimoto, quickly determining that he might still be
            able to catch up to Ayanokouji, thanked Hirata and immediately stepped back out into
            the hallway. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was almost time for the year-end exams. Even he couldn’t afford to spend every
            day doing nothing but chasing after Ayanokouji. He wanted to come to a definite conclusion,
            and he wanted to do so quickly, so he could shift his attention to prepping for the
            exams and be ready to take them in top form. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Man, hope I get something soon,” he muttered to himself.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If there was a chance, he would take it. And so he continued chasing after Ayanokouji.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As luck would have it, Ayanokouji was by the entrance, fiddling with his phone. Was
            he waiting to meet with someone? Or was he just killing time? Either way, it seemed
            like Hashimoto’s luck was holding.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ayanokouji was almost incessantly fiddling with his phone to communicate with people.
            It was unclear whether he was talking to Miyake and the other people in that group,
            or if he was communicating with someone who Hashimoto didn’t know. The only thing
            he did know for sure was that Ayanokouji was an extremely easy person for him to track.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He’d been tailing several students so far: Katsuragi, Ryuuen, Kanzaki, and sometimes
            even Ichinose. None of them were all that easy to follow. If he got a bead on them
            once in two days, he considered himself lucky. And sometimes, when they gave him the
            slip, he’d go almost a whole week without knowing what they were up to.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, Ayanokouji’s daily routine was ironclad, and his circle of friends was extremely
            small. This made it incredibly easy to anticipate where he was going to be. On top
            of that, Ayanokouji seemed to have no awareness whatsoever of what was going on around
            him. He never paid attention to anything behind him, and he never gave any indication
            of having a keen sense of intuition, not even that he had </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">accidentally</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> detected Hashimoto following him.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even so, Hashimoto wasn’t so careless as to let his guard down. He followed Ayanokouji
            while keeping sufficient distance between them, just to be completely safe. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then, his phone rang. It was one of his classmates, Shimizu Naoki.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, what’s up, Naoki?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well… To tell you the truth, it’s about what happened this morning… Seriously, dude,
            I give up. This sucks.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know, dude. Hey, it’s probably best just to forget about it, all right? We just
            have a lot of people who like to talk in our class is all.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A minor problem had cropped up that morning in Class A, which Hashimoto belonged to.
            Apparently, the girls of Class A had found out that Shimizu had confessed his feelings
            to one of their classmates, a girl named Nishikawa. And that she’d turned him down.
            Nishikawa had probably inadvertently let the fact of Shimizu’s confession slip out
            to her friends, and it had spread. It happened all the time.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Dude, you’re never going to get anyone to go out with you if you worry about each
            and every little thing, you know?” said Hashimoto.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-yeah, I guess so, but… Seriously dude, I just can’t forgive Nishikawa for this.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, as much as I’d love to stick around and listen to you complain, I’m kind of
            in the middle of something right now.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, really? Sorry, man.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hashimoto promised that he’d call him back that night and then ended the call.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s just what happens when you ask someone out without first making sure you’ve
            met all the conditions for success,” he muttered after he hung up. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Resolving to console Shimizu later, Hashimoto followed Ayanokouji back to the dormitory.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If he’s heading straight back, then I guess I’m not getting anything today either,
            huh?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The worst part of tailing Ayanokouji was probably the almost complete lack of variety.
            However, the elevator had passed the fourth floor, the location of Ayanokouji’s room.
            It continued to ascend for a while. Hashimoto watched the monitor, observing Ayanokouji
            getting off alone on a floor where the girls were residing.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If I remember correctly… That’s Ichinose’s floor, isn’t it?” he said aloud.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It might be a coincidence. Ayanokouji might be meeting some other girl. But given
            what had been happening lately, Hashimoto couldn’t help but connect the dots to Ichinose,
            even if he didn’t want to. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Since it’s Ichinose, though, I guess it’s possible he’s just paying a simple visit…?”
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While Ayanokouji had a small circle of friends, Ichinose was highly popular, liked
            by students across all grade levels. It wouldn’t be surprising for her to be friends
            with Ayanokouji. Also, she was pretty cute. Plenty of students might be making a point
            of visiting her, maybe hoping to spark something by doing so.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Regardless, Ayanokouji got back on the elevator soon after. This time, he got off
            at the fourth floor, which was where his room was. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What…?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hashimoto struggled to understand what in the world Ayanokouji was doing. On the monitor,
            he saw several girls from Class B entering the elevator from the floor Ichinose resided
            on. Hashimoto deduced that they’d come to visit her before Ayanokouji, causing him
            to decide to turn back when he bumped into them. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just in case, Hashimoto quickly took the elevator up to the fourth floor. But Ayanokouji
            was gone, almost certainly back in his room.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In the end, I got nothing today either, huh,” he muttered.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After pondering whether or not to call it quits for now, he decided to mull over the
            situation for a while in the lobby. It was still early. There was still a good chance
            that Ayanokouji would make contact with Ichinose later, or that he might be making
            plans to go out and see someone else. If Ayanokouji hopped on the elevator, Hashimoto
            would be able to see whether he was headed up or down on the monitor. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">His decision to hang around a bit longer paid off just about an hour later. Ayanokouji
            got on the elevator and began descending toward a lower floor. Additionally, he was
            still in his uniform.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is he heading back to the school building?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It made no sense for him to be heading back out now, after going through all the trouble
            of heading back to the dorms. If he were headed to the convenience store and didn’t
            want to bother changing his clothes, that might explain it…but Ayanokouji had his
            school bag with him. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hashimoto immediately got up off the sofa and hid near the emergency stairs.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“All right, here’s hoping something interesting is about to happen,” he said.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As if Hashimoto’s wish had just been granted, Ayanokouji exited the lobby and walked
            toward a relatively isolated area of campus. This pretty much put paid to the possibility
            of him heading to the school building or the convenience store. So was he meeting
            with someone? But…the place he was headed really wasn’t well suited to casual hangouts.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">All things considered, Hashimoto was sure he was up to something, and that he was
            meeting someone. If it turned out to be the former student council president Horikita,
            or Ryuuen, then things would be heating up for sure. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">His expectations, however, were overturned quite unexpectedly.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whoa, whoa, are you serious…?” he murmured aloud.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The person who turned up to meet Ayanokouji was none other than Karuizawa Kei, from
            first-year Class C. She was quite the hot topic, even in Class A, after recently breaking
            up with Hirata. Hashimoto had had almost no contact with her himself, so he couldn’t
            control his surprise at her unexpected appearance. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Disappointment washed over him. His expectations had been totally shattered.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This had nothing to do with the “hidden side” of Ayanokouji that he’d been trying
            to discover. This was simply a romantic affair. Hashimoto tried automatically to arrive
            at a different interpretation for the scene before him, but no matter how he looked
            at it, he couldn’t help but feel like the two of them shared something more than a
            regular friendship. He’d witnessed Hirata and Karuizawa on dates several times before,
            but never sensed much intimacy between them or gotten the feeling they were lovers.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I don’t get it. Why Ayanokouji?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Which of them was romantically interested in the other? Or were they both interested
            in each other? Hashimoto tried to hazard a guess, but came up blank. Romance was never
            logical, anyway. After all, if he were to objectively compare Hirata and Ayanokouji,
            80 percent of girls would probably choose Hirata. The remaining 20 percent might choose
            Ayanokouji as an alternative to having nothing at all. In short…</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The person who Ayanokouji has been in frequent contact with is Karuizawa…?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hashimoto quickly ditched that idea. It was just his imagination. Just something he
            had selfishly latched onto to explain the current situation. He needed to investigate
            further to know the truth. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, he couldn’t hear the details of their conversation. It wasn’t like he could
            just casually stroll up close to them either, since this was a place students didn’t
            often frequent.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What should I do…?” he mused aloud. No matter how hard he tried, he was at a loss.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then the scene took an unexpected turn.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Chocolates, huh?” said Hashimoto.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Karuizawa handed Ayanokouji something she had been holding. Today was February 14th.

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And one more thing… It seems like similar rumors were posted on the Class B bulletin
            board.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Meaning they must’ve noticed what was written about us too.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems that way.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita hurriedly logged into the app and checked the Class B bulletin board. Much
            like with Class C, there were four rumors posted on the boards, each naming a different
            student. There were similar messages on Class D’s bulletin board, too.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Conveniently enough, it looks like the students of Class A were the only ones who
            had no rumors posted about them. Can I have a moment of your time after class today?
            I’d like to check in with Ichinose, get some more information, and discuss how to
            respond to these posts.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure,” I agreed.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For the time being, let’s focus on the practice test. It’s a valuable opportunity
            to gauge the difficulty level of the year-end exam, and to get an idea of where our
            class is at.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But Horikita wasn’t one of the people targeted by the rumors. Unlike her, the victims
            weren’t going to be able to concentrate on the test quite so easily. When Kei and
            her friends arrived at the classroom, they huddled together and started whispering
            among themselves. Then, they looked over at me. They looked at me as if I were human
            trash. I could tell what was going on even if I couldn’t hear what they were saying.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Does it really look like Ayanokouji-kun has a crush on Karuizawa-san?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, what do you think about him anyway? Huh, Karuizawa-san?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was sure the conversation they were having right now went something like that. And,
            without a doubt, I bet that Kei was responding to them with things like “He’s disgusting”
            or “He’s the worst.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Didn’t you say that you were fine?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…It kinda bothers me a little.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I would’ve kept watching them talking, but I didn’t want to actually overhear what
            they were saying, so I decided to stop. The real issue was the students other than
            me who’d been singled out by name and were currently being discussed.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">7.4</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HE PRACTICE TEST</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> had begun, despite the chilly traces of awkwardness and ill will still lingering
            in the air. This was an important phase of the end of the school year. The content
            of the practice test was, even comparatively speaking, much more difficult than what
            we’d seen on tests so far. Extremely so. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, it was also true that students who’d gotten through the exams we’ve had so
            far should be able to handle what was on this one without needing to panic. On the
            other hand, the students who’d barely managed to squeak by probably needed to really
            put their noses to the grindstone after this practice test. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d been invited to join the Ayanokouji Group for a study session, but I decided to
            let them know they could start without me, since I was going to be accompanying Horikita
            today. Kanzaki didn’t want to draw any attention to himself, so we decided to meet
            up at the Keyaki Mall after school, after the practice tests were over. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Following Horikita’s lead, we went over to where Kanzaki was waiting for us. He was
            by the southern entrance to the mall. This was the farthest point from the school
            building, so students didn’t usually come by here. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wasn’t particularly invested in the whole class conflict thing, but that didn’t
            mean that I wasn’t worried about Ichinose, as a friend. Besides, more information
            wasn’t a bad thing. Not to mention the fact that Hashimoto had been tailing me lately,
            and for some time now. If I made contact with Class B, the shadow of Class A would
            inevitably loom closer…but that was exactly what I was hoping for. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In fact, Hashimoto had followed me all the way here, keeping an appropriate level
            of distance all the while. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Two days off in a row. And on top of that, you still haven’t been able to get in
            touch with Ichinose-san yourself?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not that she isn’t responding at all; it’s that her responses are slow. All
            I’ve gotten from her was a notification that she’s sick with a cold.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kanzaki seemed on edge. I imagined he’d been under a lot of stress lately. Ichinose
            must have told him not to worry, but I supposed he could hardly sit there and take
            it. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose’s health issues were probably one reason why she was reluctant to meet with
            any of her classmates right now. I was willing to bet another was that she really
            didn’t want to hear about or discuss the rumors any further.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What did your homeroom teacher say about it?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The same as always. She only said that Ichinose was taking the day off because she
            was out sick with a cold.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Their homeroom teacher had probably gotten the same message from Ichinose that everyone
            else had. Kanzaki looked depressed because he doubted whether Ichinose was really
            absent from class because she had a cold. After all, she’d been the subject of an
            ongoing scandal lately. Of course, he suspected that was the real reason for her absence.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How about we go to visit her? I think we could clear this all up if we just went
            to see her in person,” said Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Apparently, some girls from our class went to visit her. It doesn’t sound like they
            were able to see her face-to-face,” replied Kanzaki.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita, realizing that the situation wasn’t looking good, pondered the matter deeply.
            “Well, on the bright side, Ichinose is academically gifted. Even if she doesn’t take
            the practice test, she’ll probably do just fine,” she reasoned.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was possible for students who were sick, like Ichinose, to get the test questions
            at a later date, or they could ask other students about what problems were on the
            test.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’re not worried about that either. We’re only concerned about Ichinose’s mental
            well-being,” said Kanzaki.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As Horikita and Kanzaki continued to try to come up with a plan, several shadows approached
            us. It seemed like Hashimoto had already reported on this secret meeting.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, it sounds like Ichinose-san is absent from class again today, huh? Next week
            is the year-end exam. If she were absent for an extended period of time…why, perhaps
            all the way to the day of the exam… Well, she might be in a lot of trouble, hm?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Sakayanagi.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi and her underlings had appeared before us. Well, before Kanzaki, rather.
            We saw Kamuro and Hashimoto with her, and also a boy named Kitou. So these were the
            key members of Sakayanagi’s faction, huh?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Now then, what in the world could you be discussing with these Class-C fellows?”
            asked Sakayanagi.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It has nothing to do with you.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, my, it doesn’t seem like we’re very welcome here.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you want to be welcomed, then you should stop spreading weird rumors. Before you
            do something that you can’t take back.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi and her classmates looked at one another and chuckled. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What in the world are you talking about?” she asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Class B’s unity won’t be shaken, no matter how many rumors you spread.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m afraid I don’t know what kind of situation your class is in. But I look forward
            to finding out what comes next.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was guessing she’d only come here to see for herself how things were proceeding.
            She seemed to conclude that her plans were showing immediate results, for she departed
            right after that exchange with Kanzaki. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry about her, Kanzaki-kun,” said Horikita. “This is all part of Sakayanagi-san’s
            strategy.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Unfortunately, Kanzaki’s concern for his companions, and his humble, reserved nature
            meant his suffering wouldn’t be ending anytime soon. </span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">7.5</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">C</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">LASS WAS OVER,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> but it wasn’t like the rumors were going to stop spreading. I made it back to the
            dorm and was relaxing when I got a call from Kei.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, w-w-w-wait a minute! What’s going on, Kiyotaka?!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean, ‘What’s going on?’” I already knew, but I figured I’d anyway.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, come on, you know what! Kiyotaka, there’s…well, um, there’s a-a rumor going around
            that you have a c-crush on me! Did you not know?!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry about the rumors.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“N-no, no way, no way, no way! It’s not like I can just </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">not</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> worry about it just because it’s a rumor! How did this even happen?!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was shouting so loudly that there was a shrill ringing in my ears. I moved my
            phone away for a moment and hit the button to lower the volume.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose it might be Hashimoto who started the rumor,” I said. “Or maybe some other
            students saw us together.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Aahhh!” Kei let out a quiet scream.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I mean, this is fine though, isn’t it? It would’ve been really bad for you
            if things were reversed.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“R-reversed?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If the rumor went ‘Kei has a crush on Kiyotaka,’ then that’d be bad news for you,
            wouldn’t it? I think if that were the case, then people would be even more suspicious
            of you than they are of me, since you just broke up with Hirata,” I told her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I-I suppose so, but…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry. Things like rumors always fade quickly.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Still, I suppose it might be easier for us to make contact moving forward, thanks
            to this rumor. If I try to talk to you, people will just assume it’s because of what
            the rumor says, and that’ll be that.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It all came down to perspective. I had no plans to start having conversations in conspicuous
            locations, but it could be something of an insurance policy, circumstances depending.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, no, no, no, no, no, no!” Kei repeated the word many more times than before. “If
            the two of us are together, people will look at us weird! They absolutely, definitely
            will look at us weird!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Was repeating yourself like that becoming some kind of trend? What a bizarre speech
            affectation. Anyway, this meant that Hashimoto, who’d been following me, would also
            have this information planted in his head.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry about it.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if you tell me not to worry about it………” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A long period of silence followed, after which she apparently decided it was just
            too difficult after all. “It’s just impossible. I can’t do it!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kei continued to grumble at me about a variety of topics for a while but then eventually
            seemed to give up, and she ended the call.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">7.6</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HINGS WERE BEGINNING</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> to move at a dizzying pace. Even though there was no special exam, and we should
            be concentrating only on the written exam that was going to be held in late February,
            these were tumultuous days. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">On Friday, February 18th—three days after the practice test—students from each class
            except for B had gathered at a spot some distance from the school building. Hirata
            had done his best to keep the new rumors from spreading at this critical juncture,
            but his efforts had been in vain. Class A, the only class with no rumors about them
            posted on the bulletin boards, had already gotten wind of the information.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yo, Ishizaki. So what the heck did you wanna talk about with me, anyway?” asked Hashimoto,
            seeming the same as always.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do I want to talk about? You already know what this is about, Hashimoto! And
            what are you thinkin’, bringin’ Kitou along with you? I told ya to come alone, didn’t
            I?” said Ishizaki.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, you brought Albert with you, didn’t you? Just taking precautions.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The air was tense, like everyone was on tenterhooks. Looking at the current situation,
            it was hard to imagine the two of them had been rooming together just a little while
            ago at the school camp, but the reasons why things had gotten to be like this were
            plain to see.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We just came to talk today. Isn’t that right, Ishizaki-kun?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki and Albert weren’t the only Class-D students present. Hiyori and Ibuki were
            there too. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, as long as they don’t make a scene, then I suppose everything will be fine.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hiyori’s worries were understandable. Given the people who were present, it was hard
            to imagine </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">nothing</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> happening. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But what about those other people? I didn’t know you invited other people out here
            aside from us,” said Ishizaki.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hashimoto looked over at us and let out an exasperated sigh. “Dunno about them. Aren’t
            you the ones who called them?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Apparently, both Class D and Class A were uncomfortable about having us Class-C students
            present.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol9_INTERIORIMAGES_7" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v9/OPS/ClassroomElite-Vol9_INTERIORIMAGES_7.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s just like you said, Ayanokouji,” said Akito, who stood beside me, along with
            the rest of the Ayanokouji Group. We’d all met up at the café for a study session
            a little while ago.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I just remembered that time when Kanzaki and Hashimoto were going at it. And then
            I just ‘happened’ to see you guys heading away from campus, so I thought that maybe…
            Well.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As soon as I’d told Akito that I had sensed something was wrong, he immediately followed
            me here. The only thing I hadn’t planned on was Haruka, Airi, and Keisei coming along
            too. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s way more people here than there was last time. Things might get pretty rough…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, come on, why do we keep getting into these kinds of dangerous situations all
            the time?” said Haruka, apparently exasperated.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, whatever. It doesn’t matter who called them here. So let’s hear what you have
            to say, Shiina-chan.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s about the rumors. You Class-A people are the ones who’ve been starting them,
            aren’t you?” said Hiyori, perhaps deciding this would result in a fistfight if she
            left it to Ishizaki to handle the conversation.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, hey, why are you asking us something like that?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s friggin’—”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please leave this to me, Ishizaki-kun,” said Hiyori, gently stopping Ishizaki from
            angrily shouting back. “I happened to overhear Kanzaki-kun saying that he saw you
            spreading rumors about Ichinose-san.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, he’s a blabbermouth, huh? Or maybe you happened to hear that from those two
            over there?” replied Hashimoto. He was referring to me and Akito, since we’d heard
            Hashimoto’s conversation with Kanzaki the other day.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please answer the question, Hashimoto-kun.” Hiyori continued to press Hashimoto without
            even looking in our direction.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Well, Ayanokouji and Miyake know, so I’ll just come out and say it, I suppose. I

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hashimoto didn’t admit the truth, of course.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s a rather convenient excuse. Do you really think something like that is still
            going to fly?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Excuse? It’s the truth. I guess if it’s wrong to repeat rumors ’cause it amuses you,
            then I’m the bad guy. Still, it’s strange, isn’t it? That Class D, who should have
            nothing to do with this, would just swoop into this whole situation?” said Hashimoto.
            He continued talking, jovial as ever, but with a sharp glint in his eye. “Is it possible
            that…it’s actually you Class-D folks who are the ones spreading those rumors?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ve got to be friggin’ kidding me. We already know that Sakayanagi is the one
            spreading these rumors!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t be so quick to assume that. Sure, it’s true that our leader is the aggressive
            type. There are times when she, against her better judgment, says things that’ll provoke

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Used Kamuro-san?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Before I could seek to clarify the facts myself, she told me everything. Only me.
            About the fact that Ichinose shoplifted in the past.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She acted entirely on her own,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, that’s not true.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How can you be so sure of that?” Apparently, she wanted to hear my reasoning.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She handed over a can of beer as proof that she shoplifted. But she hadn’t stolen

            her you would dispose of it. Which means she re-procured that can from you before
            meeting me and presented me with it. Or contacted you right after she left my room
            and got it then.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">By that point, the fact of Kamuro contacting me and telling me about Ichinose’s past
            was well within the range of what I had expected.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why would you think I’d take such roundabout action?” Sakayanagi asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To lure me out, probably?” I countered.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh heh heh. I suppose I should respond by saying that’s just what I’d expect from
            you, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It would have been easy for me to sit on the sidelines and watch events unfold. Actually,
            that’s exactly what I had planned to do.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The person who made me stray from those plans was none other than Sakayanagi herself.

            Of course, she’d done the latter in an extremely roundabout way.</span></p>

            as a result. However, I was hoping that if I simply left the possibility open for
            you to intervene, then you would take hold of it. I estimated the odds were fifty-fifty
            that you would…but it would seem things turned out exactly as I hoped it would.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In other words, she was saying Ichinose’s existence didn’t matter to her at all. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please have a little contest with me, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And if I say no?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“While you might try to say it wouldn’t significantly hurt you, I’d expose you as
            the mastermind leading Class C. And I’m sure that you understand quite well that that
            wouldn’t be something you could dismiss as a mere rumor.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was sure Sakayanagi would calmly and unhesitatingly proceed to make my secrets known
            to all, even if the school had ostensibly prohibited her from spreading rumors.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So what do you say? Will you accept?” she asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How would we win this? You’re Class A. I’m Class C. The difference is obvious.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I don’t know exactly what the next exam will cover, but how about we compare
            our rankings? If you win, then I promise that from this point on, I will not speak
            a word to anyone about your past.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While that wasn’t a bad offer, there was no guarantee that she was going to honor
            her word. And I had absolutely no intention of keeping any written or audio records
            of this arrangement.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t believe me, hm? But you have no choice but to believe me. If you don’t,
            then your past will be exposed for all to see. That would make it rather hard for
            you to live an ordinary life, wouldn’t it?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do whatever you want. If that does happen, though, you’ll never, ever get the chance
            to fight me.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh heh. Yes, I suppose that’s what you would say, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi knew I wouldn’t agree to compete with her so easily. Which was exactly
            why she had yet to tell anyone about my past.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, then, what if I wager my future at this school? If I lose, I’ll drop out. Furthermore,
            I don’t mind if you have my father, the principal, be a witness to act as guarantor.”
            Sakayanagi radiated absolute confidence that she would undoubtedly win against me.
            “Of course, even if you lose to me, there’s no need for you to leave the school. I
            don’t intend to ask you to wager anything significant, either. I will, however, publicly
            announce that you are the mastermind leading Class C. That is all. If you won’t accept
            some degree of risk, then you might simply withdraw from our little contest.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If those are your conditions, then I accept.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you very much, Ayanokouji-kun. It seems my boring life here at this school
            is finally at an end.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With a broad, satisfied grin on her face, Sakayanagi withdrew. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I decided to call the person at the center of recent events; the one who’d been looming
            in the background the whole time. It wasn’t Horikita, or Kei. And not Horikita’s brother
            either.</span></p>
