      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 9:<br/>Return</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T LONG LAST</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, the day of the year-end exam had come. We’d each done what we could to prepare,
            based on the practice test we had taken. According to Horikita, Sudou, Ike, and Yamauchi
            were all expected to perform quite well. Apparently, they’d done everything possible
            to cram during the week before the test. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then there was Akito, Haruka, Airi, and Kei. The students in my group had all done
            quite well at raising their personal bars. The other students, meanwhile, had been
            getting help from Hirata. There were no signs of any students really struggling, so
            as long as everyone made sure to keep an eye on their health, the whole class should
            make it through this exam just fine.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just then, I heard the sound of footsteps. Someone was hurrying up behind me, trying
            to catch up, and then slowing down to match my pace.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good morning, Ayanokouji-kun!” Ichinose approached me, a broad smile on her face.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good morning, Ichinose.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Today’s finally the day, huh? The year-end exam. Did you study hard?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“More or less, yeah. But I guess… I don’t even need to ask, in your case.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t even need to think about it. Class B was far more coordinated than our class,
            and I was sure they’d were much better prepared for the test. Even Ichinose, who’d
            been absent until just the other day, probably had nothing to worry about as far as
            academics.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You were really cool yesterday, Ichinose. Cool enough to charm even a guy like me,”
            I told her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“R-really…? I suppose it’s just like Sakayanagi-san said, though… that was just me
            being shameless.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose hadn’t </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">really</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> committed a crime in the first place, though. Everything had been resolved after
            her mother took appropriate measures to handle it. It was a non-issue. She’d been
            carrying a completely unnecessary burden. Nothing more.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s all thanks to you that I was able to get back on my feet, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t be a shoulder to lean on and worry about you like the students of Class B
            can, though. I just thought I’d try to listen, is all. It’s nothing you need to thank
            me for.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, that’s not true… If you weren’t there, Ayanokouji-kun, I think I would definitely
            have ended up self-destructing and falling apart, just like last time. In that sense,
            Sakayanagi-san completely defeated me this time.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Sakayanagi had manipulated Ichinose with devastating success, driving her to the brink
            of self-destruction. It was certainly true there was no telling what would have happened
            had I not gotten involved. But I wanted to make something clear.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t want you to feel like you owe me too much, though. I was nothing more than
            the catalyst. In the end, you’re the only one who was able to overcome your own past,”
            I told her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Yeah, I suppose you’re right. I can’t ever undo what I did. No matter how much time
            passes, there may never come a day where I feel like my sins have truly disappeared.
            But…from this point onward, I can face life head-on. I’m sure of it.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Everything should be all right now. No matter who tried to blame her, Ichinose would
            be able to stand her ground. She’d grown strong—stronger than anyone else. I expected
            she’d grow into an even more formidable rival in the future. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even so, there were no guarantees in life.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you’re ever feeling like you’re about to lose sight of yourself, you can talk
            to me again.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh…?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When that time comes… Yeah. I should be able to at least listen.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose stopped and stood completely still. “Is it okay for me to depend on you…?”
            she asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you’re okay with someone like me.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really?” she asked again.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Yeah, really.” I nodded, though I was somewhat confused by the question.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Th-thank you…” she said quietly. A rather unusual reaction from Ichinose, who was
            usually so upbeat. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Then she quickly shook her head, maybe because she, too, realized her reaction had
            been out-of-character.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“B-but… won’t you come to regret it someday?” she asked, gazing deeply at me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm, I guess you’ve got a point. I mean, our class might get stuck in B. If you and
            your class graduate from A, then my classmates might end up blaming me for it.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“R-right?” she answered, scratching her cheek with a wry smile.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, if that time comes, just don’t tell Horikita.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">“…</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">heh heh. Yeah, okay. I’ll make sure to do that.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose, walking alongside me once more, stretched her shoulders. She looked bright
            and cheery again, as if she’d just been reborn. Well, then. All that remained was
            to get through the year-end exam. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was still quietly looking over at me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What is it?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“H-huh?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ve been staring at me for a while now. If there’s something you want to say,
            I’m all ears.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, to tell you the truth, I… Ah! Sorry, Ayanokouji-kun. Can you please wait a
            little bit?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just as Ichinose was about to say something, her attention shifted to another student
            in front of us. A brief glance in the direction of that student, and the people surrounding
            them, made it obvious what was up.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry. I’m going to head over there for just a sec,” said Ichinose, leaving my
            side and running to catch up to the student in front of us. “Good morning, Nagumo-senpai.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, Honami. You’re looking cheerful this morning.” Nagumo might have been surprised
            by the sight of Ichinose’s restored smile. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Because that’s just the way I am.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t you resent me, Honami?” he asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Resent you?” replied Ichinose, tilting her head to the side in apparent confusion.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Immediately afterward, though, she seemed to understand the meaning behind his question.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t resent you at all, Nagumo-senpai. All I am is grateful to you. Really, thank
            you so much for bringing me onto the student council. I’ll do my best from here on
            out, so I’m looking forward to working with you.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Well, it would seem like you’re going to exceed my expectations.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nagumo looked over at me for just an instant, then immediately turned around and walked
            away. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It wasn’t difficult to intuit what he was saying with that look. He had wanted to
            break Ichinose Honami down and build her back up with his own hands. He had intended
            to completely tame her and make her his pawn. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The look he’d just given me conveyed his displeasure that I’d thrown off his plans—which
            meant he understood that I’d been involved in this incident in some capacity. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose bowed to Nagumo and then came back over to me once again.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey!” she called, her voice noticeably loud. She opened her mouth wide and continued
            speaking. “Um, hey, so, listen.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While she spoke, she reached into her school bag. Then she froze.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What is it?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Umm, well, it’s… Th-this is odd. It’s just, well, I had planned to just be like ‘Here
            you go!’ but…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose seemed to hesitate, still digging around inside her bag, but then seemingly
            made up her mind and took something out. She handed it over to me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is a little bit late, but…will you accept these Valentine’s chocolates from
            me? It’s just, well, I’ve never given anyone chocolates like this before, and… This
            is the only way that I can express my gratitude, so…” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t have to force yourself to pay me back by giving me something, you know?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The 14th had already passed, but I certainly wasn’t upset to be given more chocolate
            by a girl. That said, I hadn’t done what I had for the purposes of getting chocolate
            from her. She didn’t need to feel obligated to give me any.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I-I’m not forcing myself, though! Y-you don’t want it?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I do… Thank you.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The chocolates might start to melt if we stayed out too long. Not to mention people
            would notice us. And so, I gratefully accepted Ichinose’s gift.</span></p>
