      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 4:<br/>Unchanging Intent</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HAT THURSDAY,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I spied Ichinose while heading back to the dorms. She was usually surrounded by a
            gaggle of students, both guys and girls, but she seemed to be alone right now, which
            was rare. For some reason, I didn’t sense her usual bubbliness from her either. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered if she was alone because she was keeping her distance from her friends
            right now, rather than by mere coincidence. She was currently the person with the
            most concentrated attention focused on them in our school, regardless of grade level,
            and anyone she carelessly got involved with might take collateral damage themselves.
            I wouldn’t be surprised if she’d made the decision to stay away from her friends as
            a result.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I thought back to Kanzaki’s and Hashimoto’s conversation from the other day. Should
            I try calling out to her? </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I considered doing so but then sensed someone behind me and decided to refrain. I
            took out my cell phone and turned on the camera, switching from the rear-facing lens
            to the front-facing one and using the image on my screen to casually examine what
            was behind me. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Two students on their way back to the first-years’ dorm, just like me. One was Hashimoto.
            He seemed to be walking normally, but after what happened the other day, I couldn’t
            assume it was a coincidence. Was he following me? </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I was trying to ascertain that, however, the other student approached me without
            hesitation. I closed the camera app and returned my phone to my pocket as she drew
            near. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“U-um, excuse me, Ayanokouji-kun. Do you have a minute…?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was my classmate, Wang Mei-Yu. People tended to call her Mii-chan because her name
            was difficult to pronounce, though I found it a little embarrassing to call her that,
            even in my head. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you… have a little time right now? I was hoping I could talk to you about something,”
            she said.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She wanted to talk to me? We’d barely had any contact at all so far. In fact, this
            was practically the very first time she’d ever spoken directly to me. It didn’t seem
            like there was anyone else around whom she could be addressing, though…</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose continued getting further away, without noticing me. At this point, running
            on over and chasing after her would just look strange.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry, you’re probably busy, aren’t you…?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I was just heading back to the dorms. It’s okay.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Mii-chan brightened when I said that, releasing a sigh of relief. Hashimoto passed
            by us and continued in the direction of the dorms, not looking in my direction and
            not calling out to me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So… You said you wanted to talk to me?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, talking here will be a little awkward…” Mii-chan looked around the surrounding
            area, fidgeting. Apparently, this wasn’t a topic for casual conversation.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay.” Of course, it wasn’t like I could go, “Oh, since the dorms are so close, how
            about you come to my room?” And going to Mii-chan’s room myself seemed even more far-fetched.
            “What do you want to do, then?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I left the decision to Mii-chan, who thought it over, then suggested, “Would…the café
            be okay? We might be a little late heading back after, though.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If that was what she wanted, I had no reason to decline. She said we’d be a little
            late, but it was only a difference of about five or ten minutes on foot, which wasn’t
            a big deal. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In accordance with Mii-chan’s suggestion, therefore, we headed on over to the Keyaki
            Mall Café. That being said, we didn’t know each other very well. We walked some distance
            apart from one another, rather than close together.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">4.1</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HE CAFÉ WAS ALWAYS PACKED,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> and today was no exception. Even I, who lacked common sense about the details of
            normal high school life, understood why. It was extremely popular, especially with
            girls, and owned by a major company in the outside world. Their drinks were pricey—too
            pricey for your average high school student to indulge in more than a few times a
            month, unless they worked a part-time job. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But students at this school received allowances based on their class points, meaning
            many could afford to patronize the café as often as they liked, as long as they weren’t
            in dire straits or anything. As a result, the café was inevitably packed, day in and
            day out.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We still managed to find open seats, though, and sat down, facing one another. Mii-chan
            stared at the drink she’d ordered, making no attempt to look me in the eye. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She reminded me of Airi. If I inadvertently put pressure on her, it would probably
            make it even harder for her to speak her mind. I decided against attempting to break
            the ice. To give her room to marshal her thoughts, I said I was going to get some
            sugar.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I went over to the counter, where I took one sugar packet. Without letting my eyes
            wander around the room, I confirmed that Hashimoto had also found his way to the café.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I doubted he’d had a sudden craving for a caffeine fix. He was following me, without
            a doubt. Had Sakayanagi told him to keep an eye on me? No, that didn’t seem right.
            Sakayanagi didn’t want word about me to get around. And if she did want to tail me,
            she’d use her puppet, Kamuro. Assuming Sakayanagi understood what kind of person Hashimoto
            was, she’d know he wasn’t the right choice for tasks like these. She’d want to avoid
            carelessly giving Hashimoto information about me, only for him to leak that information
            to a third party.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Was he following me of his own accord, then? I didn’t recall doing anything during
            the school camp to warrant that. I should have come across as nothing more than just
            another member of the group. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen, Ishizaki and Albert, and Ibuki. I wondered if they’d been talking to anyone,
            but then nixed that possibility. Well, I wasn’t going to solve this mystery right
            now. It did seem like it was going to become a problem I’d have to deal with before
            long, though.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I decided to ignore it for now in favor of continuing my conversation with Mii-chan.
            It had been about a minute since I’d left my seat across from her, which I now retook.
            Almost immediately after, Mii-chan broke the silence.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um… Well, it’s about Hirata-kun.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">About Hirata-kun, huh?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I was hoping you could tell me some things…” she continued.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’re not really all that close,” I replied almost immediately, as if taking precaution.
            But Mii-chan looked at me with surprise.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Then why did Hirata-kun tell me you’re the most reliable person in class, Ayanokouji-kun?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Is that so?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. He said that you’re the most dependable person in class. He really spoke highly
            of you.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">While I was honestly happy to be praised by Hirata, if that kind of talk got around,
            it would spell trouble. That said, I could understand why Hirata had given Mii-chan
            my name. There were plenty of students you could count on, but if you restricted your
            search to Class C, well, things got a little complicated. If you further narrowed
            your criteria to only include guys, then it wasn’t so odd that Hirata had named me.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Still…she wanted to talk about Hirata, huh? In light of the earlier conversation with
            Haruka, I could guess what this was about.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, um, Hirata-kun and Karuizawa-san, well… They broke up recently. Did you know
            that?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, of course.” I pretended to have no idea why she was bringing that up.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-well, um…” Mii-chan faltered a few times, then finally got to the heart of the
            matter. “…D-do you know if there’s anyone that Hirata-kun likes right now?”</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol9_INTERIORIMAGES_3" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v9/OPS/ClassroomElite-Vol9_INTERIORIMAGES_3.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yep, there it was. What was the correct course of action in a scenario like this?
            I mulled it over for a moment, then decided it would be best to just give her an honest
            answer.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t think so.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“R-really?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t say for absolutely certain, of course. But as far as I know, no, there isn’t
            anyone. Besides, he only just got dumped by Karuizawa. It’s probably too soon for
            him to have feelings for someone else,” I replied. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s certainly true,” replied Mii-chan, seeming to grow calmer.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is it okay if I ask you one thing, just out of curiosity?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“S-sure.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“When did you start liking Hirata?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huuuuuh?!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Was that an odd question? Mii-chan’s face had gone bright red. She looked completely
            flustered.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-wh-wh-why are you asking me that?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, if you don’t want to answer, you don’t have to—”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Right after the entrance ceremony, I think?” she replied, cutting me off.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, she’d told me anyway, huh.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m a little clumsy…” She’d fallen in love with Hirata the very first time she’d
            met him, Mii-chan went on to candidly explain. “…I guess. That’s what it feels like,
            anyway.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that so?” There was a lot I didn’t understand, but I was sure of one thing: she’d
            been charmed by Hirata’s gentleness.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But—” Mii-chan had blushed as she spoke about meeting Hirata, but now her expression
            grew cloudy, like she’d snapped back to reality. “I… I don’t think someone like me
            could be Hirata-kun’s girlfriend…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why?” I asked, wondering how she could be so sure of that.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Because there are too many rivals out there… And besides, I’ve never been in love
            before or anything…” she replied.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So despite overflowing with feelings of love, she didn’t have the courage to make
            a move? I didn’t want to believe the absence of romantic experience was necessarily
            a handicap, but I couldn’t say for certain that it had no effect at all.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, Mii-chan… Wait, it’s probably not okay for me to be calling you Mii-chan, is
            it?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, no, it’s completely fine. Everyone calls me that. Even though both my parents
            are Chinese, they seem to like my Japanese nickname, so they call me Mii-chan too.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In other words, she wasn’t half-Japanese. “Are you studying abroad?” I asked. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Um, well, when I was in my first year of junior high, my father came to Japan on
            business,” she answered.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So she’d moved here with her family? “Did you encounter any obstacles? Like dealing
            with a language barrier, for example.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It was really at first. I was more worried about whether I could make friends than
            learning the language… But lots of people spoke good English at the junior high I
            enrolled in, so we got along pretty well,” she explained.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That reminded me—I’d heard Mii-chan was good at English. On top of that, she’d also
            perfectly mastered Japanese in her three years of junior high. I’d heard Chinese students
            had to keep their noses to the grindstone to make it in a society that was far more
            competitive than Japan’s. It was probably precisely because she’d received such a
            rigorous education that Mii-chan had been able to smoothly integrate into Japan. All
            that remained was for her to improve her communication skills, like Airi.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder if someone like me even has a chance…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t want to make any irresponsible statements, but I think you should have a
            decent chance. Don’t you?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not lying about that. But…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“B-but?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Though this might make her more anxious, I should probably tell her about a rather
            difficult part of this equation. “Hirata’s a good guy, right?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t you think that’s exactly why, the next time he goes out with someone, he’ll
            be really careful? Right? He might even feel responsible for what happened, like he
            wasn’t able to make Karuizawa happy or something.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see…” she replied, nodding. “You’re right. And I don’t think…I could confess my
            feelings to him right away either.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You might be concerned about the competition out there, but if you get impatient
            and confess your feelings too soon, there’s a high chance you’ll be rejected,” I told her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I advised her to take things slow and steady. She was going to have to ask Hirata
            how he felt before she could be sure of anything, but I couldn’t picture him pointlessly
            rushing into dating another girl right now. It was far more likely that he would reject
            the majority of the girls who happened to confess their romantic feelings to him right
            now. In that sense, Mii-chan had a better chance of winning if she waited.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I think I might have been mistaken about you, Ayanokouji-kun.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Mistaken?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it’s just, you don’t normally talk all that much. Or at all, really. You kind
            of give off this scary vibe. But after meeting with you face-to-face, and talking
            like this, I feel like you’re super easy to talk to. It feels like you really care
            about my problems, I guess…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Despite her compliments, the truth was that I wasn’t </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">really</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> listening to her. It would be more accurate to say I was subconsciously analyzing
            our conversation, carefully scrutinizing it for information that might be helpful
            to me later, or something that I could use. If I came off like I really cared about
            her problems, then that was a convenient bonus, no more. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Should I go a little bit further? It seemed this might be a good opportunity to ask
            her about a number of different things.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh? Mii-chan and… Ayanokouji-kun, is that you?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just as I opened my mouth, Shiina Hiyori, from first-year Class D, appeared at our
            table. I closed my mouth without a word. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hello, Hiyori-chan,” said Mii-chan. Judging by the way they referred to each other,
            they were close.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are the two of you here on a date, by any chance?” asked Hiyori.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“N-n-no, that’s not it at all, Hiyori-chan!” said Mii-chan, panicked, quickly rising
            to her feet. She waved her arms before her to signal her rejection of the idea, a
            gesture so expansive that her entire body shook with the motion. The fact that she
            would deny it so excessively kind of stung.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, would it be all right if I joined you?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course, that’s fine with me. …Is it okay with you, Ayanokouji-kun?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Thank you very much.” Hiyori sat down next to Mii-chan, wearing a wide and happy
            smile. “It’s rather unusual to see the two of you together. What were you talking
            about?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“U-Um, well…” Mii-chan struggled to admit that we were talking about her crush.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m interested in China, so I was asking her about it,” I replied.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In…China?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. It’s a country I’d like to visit sometime, and so I thought I’d talk to Mii-chan,
            a Chinese person.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I shot a look over at Mii-chan, basically saying “Right?” with my eyes. She nodded
            quickly several times in response.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“China is really nice, isn’t it? I’m also really interested in it, especially things
            like the Great Wall.” Hiyori put her hands together in front of her, still smiling.
            Surprisingly enough, it seemed this was a topic she was really into.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose you can’t really avoid mentioning the Great Wall when you talk of China.
            But personally, I’d like to visit the Ancient City of Ping Yao,” I replied.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ping Yao?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It seemed Hiyori hadn’t heard of that before. On the other hand, Mii-chan’s eyes widened
            in surprise at hearing me mention it.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow. It’s a World Heritage Site, but still…I’m impressed that you know it…” she said.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I just happened to hear about it is all.”</span></p>

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. We’re reading buddies.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, you’re not wrong about that, I guess.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Reading buddies…?” repeated Mii-chan. She looked puzzled for a moment, like she didn’t

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She probably hadn’t had any friends outside her own class before the school camp.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I think so too. I think there’s more to life here at school than just antagonizing

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That said, the school was still keeping parts of its agenda hidden from us. If not,

            a half-hearted acquaintanceship could do more harm than good. </span></p>

      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">4.2</span></p>

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HANK YOU</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> for everything today, Ayanokouji-kun,” said Mii-chan.</span></p>

            Mii-chan to scratch her cheek with her finger, appearing embarrassed. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, w-well, I suppose,” she replied.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll head on up after taking a look at my mail,” I said aloud, turning my back toward
            Mii-chan and Hiyori as they were about to get on the elevator.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I checked the contents of my mailbox once or twice a week, as I was sure many other
            students did. A lot of what arrived in our mailboxes was from the school, but people
            sometimes received packages via personal </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">correspondence. There were also times when things students had mail-ordered were routed
            to them through the school. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But I wasn’t checking my mailbox for anything so ordinary. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nothing today either, huh?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had started checking my mail regularly ever since my father came to visit the school,
            anticipating he might try to contact me. Since I saw nothing of note, I headed back
            to the elevator. When I got there, I saw Hiyori there waiting for me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you have a minute?” she asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We headed over to stand by the sofa in the lobby, a short distance from the elevator.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There was something I had wanted to ask you earlier, but since you were with Mii-chan…”
            said Hiyori, trailing off. She briefly scanned our surroundings to see if anyone was
            around, and then spoke again. “Have you heard anything about Ichinose-san?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Meaning what? If you’re referring to those bizarre rumors, then yeah, I’ve heard
            about those.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Exactly that. Do you happen to know who’s spreading those rumors?” asked Hiyori.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No… I’m afraid I don’t.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It would have been easy to name Sakayanagi or Hashimoto, but I thought I’d avoid doing
            that. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To be honest, I really hate seeing Ichinose-san being tormented like this. She treats
            even students like me, who don’t have many friends, the same as she would anyone else,”
            said Hiyori.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If I’d remembered correctly, Hiyori and Ichinose had been in the same group at the
            school camp. I supposed she might feel a stronger bond with Ichinose than with other
            students after eating the same meals and sleeping in the same room. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji-kun.” There was a kind of determination in Hiyori’s eyes. “I dislike hurting
            others. However, I believe that it’s sometimes necessary to fight in order to protect
            your friends.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose that’s right. You can’t possibly save everyone.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even though Ichinose-san is a mutual enemy of ours, there must be a way to help her.
            I don’t have a plan yet, but… Will you help me?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Help, huh? In that case, you should talk to Horikita.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita-san?” Hiyori didn’t seem thrilled by the prospect.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose Class C might also be willing to help Ichinose,” I added. If that happened,
            it would mean Classes D, C, and B were joining forces against Class A. But Hiyori
            wasn’t jumping for joy at this prospect either.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So you won’t do anything yourself, Ayanokouji-kun?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have no influence whatsoever over Class C.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that so?” she replied, tilting her head to the side with a puzzled look on her
            face.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For the girls, it’s Horikita. For the guys, it’s Hirata. You’ll just have to talk
            to one of them about it,” I answered.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see…” said Hiyori. Her shoulders slumped, seemingly in disappointment.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Dissatisfied?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No…Well, it’s just that I barely know Horikita-san or Hirata-kun, So I thought that
            if I went to you, Ayanokouji-kun, then…” She slumped her shoulders even further, looking
            despondent. I was surprised by the sight.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry. If there’s nothing I can do, then there’s nothing I can do.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, no, it’s okay… It was a selfish idea on my part. I just came up to you and asked
            without considering how you might feel,” she replied, bowing to me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, do you want me to put in a word in with them on your behalf, for the time being?”
            I asked. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really? You would do that for me?” Even though that was exactly what Hiyori had just
            said she wanted, she seemed to have changed her mind. “I’m sorry, but…on second thought,
            let’s just meet another time. If we act carelessly, it might make the rumors spread
            even further and cause more trouble for Ichinose-san.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. Yeah, you might be right about that.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As of this moment, there was no telling what the people targeting Ichinose might do
            next. Moving incautiously might do more harm than good. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Not to mention the possibility that the rumors about Ichinose were closer to the truth
            than many might think…</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">4.3</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">FTER RETURNING TO MY ROOM,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I received a text from Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Do you have a minute now?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t respond, but when she saw the read receipt on her text, she sent another
            right away.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Since the message I just sent was marked as read, I’m just going to go ahead and keep
            talking. Ichinose-san is coming to my room tonight. Are you coming too?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Now that was unexpected. I’d planned to just read her messages, but now decided to
            actually respond. </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">What led to this?</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I texted back. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">We have an alliance with Class B. It’s only natural that we’d want to lend a hand
            when the situation calls for it. But in this case, we’re not getting the full story.
            So I was thinking we’d hear it from the person in question,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> replied Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In other words, she’d contacted Ichinose and asked if they could meet in person, huh?
            A bold move. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It would be easy for me to refuse. Horikita would probably tell me what they talked
            about if I asked her later. That being said, Horikita might not get the whole story
            out of Ichinose. Even Kanzaki didn’t know everything about her, despite how close
            they were. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If that was the case, would I get closer to the truth if I met with Ichinose directly
            and asked her myself? Unfortunately, I couldn’t do so without getting involved in
            whatever happened next.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">What should I do? After giving it some thought, I sent Horikita a short response.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">What time?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Seven.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was a little late. I’d have to be careful to not be seen by other students.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Got it. I’ll let you know before I head over.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">And so, I decided to meet Ichinose together with Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">4.4</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> </span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">SPENT THE REST</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> of the time leisurely hanging out in my room. At five minutes to seven, I left my
            room and walked over to Horikita’s. At almost the exact moment I got off the elevator,
            I spied Ichinose.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, good evening, Ayanokouji-kun,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I responded to her with a light wave.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry for the bother,” I told her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Aha ha! No no, I’m the one being a bother.” With those words, Ichinose took the lead
            and rang the bell to Horikita’s room. We heard the lock click open at once.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please come in.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We planned to meet at seven; there was nothing odd about us arriving at the same time.
            Horikita invited us in without comment, and I went ahead and sat down on the floor.
            I had visited her room before, and it didn’t seem to have changed much since the last
            time. It was much like mine: simple and austere.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry to call you over like this on a weeknight, Ichinose-san.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re doing this out of consideration for me, though. There’s no need to be sorry,”
            Ichinose said. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Seeing her face-to-face like this, she seemed like the same old Ichinose she’d always
            been. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But…if this goes on too late, it’ll affect us tomorrow, so I don’t plan to draw out
            this conversation. So I’m sure you’ve heard some worrisome rumors going around about
            me.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. Do you know who is spreading those rumors?” asked Horikita, getting straight
            to the point. I listened, curious to see if Ichinose would answer the question honestly.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t have any hard evidence. But if I had to guess, I’d peg Sakayanagi.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A much more direct answer than I’d expected. I doubted Ichinose would have named a
            specific person if she was less than halfway certain; she wasn’t the type to suspect
            others without good reason. This made something very clear to me: if nothing else,
            Ichinose was aware of the identity of the person who was spreading rumors about her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sakayanagi-san… What makes you so sure that she’s the one?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To put it simply, it’s because she declared war on us. Is that not enough to convince
            you?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita probably already knew how aggressive Sakayanagi could be. Considering the
            fact that she’d been willing to deepen the rift within her own class to bring down
            Katsuragi, one could easily imagine her taking aim at Class B’s leader, Ichinose,
            in order to defeat them.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No. That is enough.” It was precisely because Horikita thought the same way that
            I did that she saw no need to pry further. “So, she’s circulating baseless rumors
            about you to damage your character?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm… How do I put this?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you not going to deny the rumors?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry, Horikita-san. I can’t answer that question. You and Ayanokouji-kun are
            my friends, but you belong to a different class. Even if we say we’re allies now,
            we’re still destined to compete with each other someday, right?” said Ichinose.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Direct as Ichinose’s first answer had been, it seemed she was declining to answer
            this question. Well, that was only to be expected. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t intend to force an answer from you. But you do know that silence can be interpreted
            as you saying the rumors are true, right?” said Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Everyone is free to interpret the rumors that way, including you, Horikita-san. But
            I have absolutely no intention of overreacting to this situation. Sakayanagi’s strategy
            is to get Class B all riled up. I think the best possible countermeasure is to stay
            silent,” replied Ichinose, smiling, looking the same as she always did.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Harassment of this sort happened everywhere, all the time. There was no foolproof
            way of dealing with it: you could react or stay silent, but at the end of the day,
            the peanut gallery would cause as much of a ruckus as they wanted. But perhaps it
            was precisely because of the rampant speculation she knew people would engage in that
            Ichinose had chosen not to react, but rather to simply wait for things to pass.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The reason I wanted to meet with you today, Horikita-san, was to talk to you about
            this whole thing. I don’t want you to get dragged into it. Even if I stay silent,
            it’ll take longer for the situation to blow over if people around me make a fuss.
            More importantly, though, there’s no need for Class C to end up in Sakayanagi’s crosshairs
            just because they tried to help me. I’ll be just fine,” said Ichinose, giving us a
            vigorous nod, her smile not fading at all.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I know that you have a strong heart. Regardless of their veracity, anyone would
            be affected by having such vile rumors about them do the rounds. But despite that,
            you aren’t thinking about yourself. You’re showing consideration for the people around
            you,” said Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m really not that great,” said Ichinose, sounding a little bashful now. “You and
            your class should carry on as you have been, Horikita-san. I’ll clean up my own mess.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Saying that, she quickly stood up. It seemed she’d only come here to caution Horikita
            not to get involved.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you know about Kanzaki and the others?” I asked. My input was probably unnecessary,
            but I thought I’d try something.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Kanzaki-kun?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He confronted Hashimoto from Class A the other day, asking him point-blank to stop
            spreading the rumors. Well, I guess it might have gone a bit beyond simply asking.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see… Kanzaki-kun is really kind. I told him he didn’t need to do anything.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not just Kanzaki-kun, though. I’m sure more of your classmates are trying to
            take action for your sake,” said Horikita. Though this was the first time she’d heard
            mention of what Kanzaki did, I was willing to bet she was right on the money.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll have a talk with my classmates again. Is it okay if we call it a night and end
            our conversation here?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you really okay with this?” Horikita stopped Ichinose, seeking confirmation yet
            again, just in case.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course,” answered Ichinose, without any hesitation. “Thank you for worrying about
            me. And Ayanokouji-kun, thank you too, for coming out so late.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s nothing. I just tagged along.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ichinose bid us goodnight and then left. Horikita didn’t stop her this time.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder if we really shouldn’t do anything?” she said aloud.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who knows?” I replied. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Based on what we’d just seen, Ichinose seemed no different than usual. If I had to
            describe it, I wouldn’t say she was acting tough, but more that she was trying not
            to think about the situation. That was my impression, anyway.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you think I should do?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You want my opinion?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. I want your honest opinion,” she replied without hesitation.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, I say do nothing.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And your reasoning?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If Sakayanagi is the source of the rumors, as Ichinose said, then getting involved
            might cause her to set her sights on Class C.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. But, what if Ichinose-san is defeated by Sakayanagi-san? Wouldn’t Sakayanagi
            make Class C her next target, anyway?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A natural conclusion. “It’s certainly true we’ll be targeted sooner or later. But
            when that time comes, the pesky leader of Class B will have already been dealt with.
            That’ll work to our advantage.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…So, you’re saying you don’t care about whatever happens to Ichinose-san? You’re
            quite cold-blooded.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Cold-blooded? Isn’t that also how you acted at first? Helping a classmate is one
            thing, but Ichinose is from another class. She’s an opponent we’ll have to face. Her
            defeat would be a welcome development for us. There’s no reason for us to lament it.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She’s currently our ally. Until Sakayanagi-san and the rest of Class A falls, and
            things come down to a one-on-one battle with Class B—”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s being pretty idealistic though, isn’t it?” I said, interrupting her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Class A being conveniently demoted to C, while Ichinose and Class C rose to A and
            B respectively, ending in us duking it out? That was nothing more than a pipe dream.
            While I wasn’t sure how much she could really rely on other people in this situation,
            Ichinose was turning down people’s offers to help her. If Horikita and Ichinose had
            had this conversation weeks ago, I was sure Horikita would have been satisfied and
            stopped offering help at a much earlier stage. How and why had she come to think the
            way she did now? </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, I supposed I could hazard a guess. She </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">had</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> been striving to improve her relationship with Kushida, after all.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You should leave things alone,” I told her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I suppose…” Horikita didn’t try to argue any further, which told me that she knew,
            deep down, that this was the right thing to do. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We’d been able to express to Ichinose that we were concerned about her, as a partner
            in our alliance, and that we were prepared to help. That was a good thing. Class C’s
            best plan of action from here was to keep a low profile and ingratiate itself with
            the other classes, slowly closing in while they were busy battling each other. Here
            and now, it was important that we not seek to help them.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d given Horikita my opinion because she asked for it. It was ultimately up to her
            to decide what Class C did next, though I did think she probably wouldn’t get further
            involved in what was going on with Class B. After all, she didn’t have a way to really
            help improve the situation without getting in the way of Ichinose’s plan.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll head back too. A guy shouldn’t be sticking around too long in a girl’s room,”
            I told her. If I stuck around past eight o’clock, there might be trouble.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose…” replied Horikita, lost in thought, without looking my way.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita had begun to change, little by little. However, the changes she was going
            through were a little extreme. She was showing an increasing tendency to lose sight
            of her goals and be influenced by her surroundings. I imagined she’d keep struggling,
            both for her own sake and for others, for the foreseeable future. But would she move
            past that and finally become her true self? That was the most important thing.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After I left the room, I saw Ichinose standing in front of the elevator. While I wondered
            whether she had been waiting for me to come over, she waved at me with a broad smile
            on her face.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, over here!” she said in a quiet voice, calling me over. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I got onto the elevator, feeling like she was urging me on. She pushed the button
            for the first floor, sending us down to the lobby.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you mind coming with me for a minute?” asked Ichinose.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t mind, no, but… Where are we going?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm, would it be okay if we headed outside for a bit?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We got to the lobby. Even though there wasn’t anyone else around, we headed outside.
            The sun had set, and it was now completely dark. In that darkness, Ichinose and I
            walked over to one of the rest areas on the path toward the school building. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know that it’s cold outside, but…I don’t want to attract any attention.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I understand. Are you okay, Ichinose?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m fine. Ah… Um, well, how do I say this… I’m really sorry.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had wondered what she was going to say, but the first thing that came out of Ichinose’s
            mouth was an apology.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Why are you apologizing?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess since I’ve been causing problems for you and Horikita-san, Ayanokouji-kun,
            and the rest of Class C. I’ve caused you a lot of unnecessary worry because of these
            rumors. Please don’t pay them any mind,” said Ichinose.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s what you said to Kanzaki and the others, isn’t it?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s the best answer I have, I think. This is the stance I’m going to take until
            the rumors have gone away.” Ichinose looked at me with determination in her eyes.
            If she’d addressed them this way, it was no wonder Kanzaki and the rest of her supporters
            in Class B had had no choice but to listen. “Well, that’s all I had wanted to say…
            It really is cold, isn’t it? Let’s head on back.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We’d talked for just a moment. Ichinose had urged me to head inside first, so I made
            it back to the dorm before her.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">4.5</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HE DAILY LIVES</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> of the people around me had gotten quite hectic. I hadn’t even done anything particularly
            proactively myself, and I kept getting swept up in the things going on around me.
            Despite the ups and downs, maybe this </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">was</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> the kind of normal, everyday life that I’d wanted to have? </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had a hunch I was going to arrive at an answer—soon.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But then something strange happened. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The phone I’d placed near my bedside vibrated quietly. The clock showed that it was
            just past one in the morning. I checked to see who would be calling me at such an
            unusual time, but it was an unregistered number. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It shouldn’t be possible for an outside number to contact me. The phones the school
            had provided us were only able to make and receive calls from school numbers, and
            there seemed to be no way to change those settings—a countermeasure put in place to
            prevent students from inadvertently making contact with the outside world. It wasn’t
            a particularly unusual feature for a phone to have. There were similar parental controls
            you could activate when giving a phone to a small child, for instance. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In other words, the call was coming from someone on the school campus whose number
            hadn’t been registered in my phone. I couldn’t determine if it was from a student
            or from a teacher.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…Hello?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Still slightly sleepy, I cautiously answered the phone, pressing it to my left ear.
            No one spoke on the other end. The only sound that reached my ears was the faint sound
            of someone breathing. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">We both waited in silence for about 30 seconds, as I waited for the other person to
            speak up.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you’re not going to say anything, I’m hanging up,” I said, warning the person
            on the other side that I had enough of waiting in silence.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ayanokouji Kiyotaka.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The person on the other side said my name. It was a male voice that I didn’t remember
            ever hearing before, but it didn’t sound like an adult. In which case, it was highly
            likely they were a student.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And you are?” I asked in return.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The person went quiet again. And then, they hung up.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Calling me just to say my name.” I couldn’t dismiss this as a simple wrong number.
            “So you’ve made your move, huh…?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The identity of the caller was a trivial concern. I began to see that man’s strategy.
            He’d begun moving against me. But why do this, and let me know he was coming? If his
            goal was to get me kicked out of school, then a surprise attack made more sense. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Deliberately doing something like this was a threat. A threat that he meant to crush
            me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Was there anything at all that lay beyond that man’s power…? </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Regardless, there was no changing what had just begun.</span></p>
