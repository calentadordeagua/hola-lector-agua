      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 3:<br/>Changing Relationships</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">N UNUSUAL SPECTACLE</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> presented itself early one morning in Class C. A circle of girls seemed to have formed
            around Karuizawa Kei, all making so much noise that it bordered on total pandemonium.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re awfully late today, Ayanokouji-kun,” said Horikita Suzune, my neighbor. We
            had only about five minutes left until the morning bell rang to signal the start of
            class.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I overslept.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Hm</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">,” replied Horikita, disinterested. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Compared to our totally emotionless conversation, Kei’s group was practically on fire.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It sounds like Karuizawa-san broke up with Hirata-kun,” said Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that why they’re all riled up today? A well-known couple broke it off?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“They were polite enough to talk so loud the whole class could hear. So the information’s
            been drilled into my head, whether or not I wanted it.” She let out a heavy sigh,
            sounding annoyed. “You seem to be close with Hirata-kun and Karuizawa-san. Didn’t
            you know?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not like I knew anything. It’s their private business.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It hadn’t seemed like she’d broached the topic yet back at the school camp, but I
            guess she’d gone ahead and done it after all. They’d been a well-known couple all
            through the whole school, so the news had really made some waves. Any third party
            hearing about it had to have been shocked. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Still, all this really meant was that Kei’s and Hirata’s connection had ostensibly
            severed. It didn’t necessarily mean Kei had lost her position as the queen bee of
            the girls–though that might happen if someone else won Hirata’s heart and became his
            one true love. Even then, I couldn’t see Kei being pushed out of her position. If
            the other girls tried to disrespect her, Hirata himself would be the first person
            to put a stop to it. If not, there would have been no point in him going so far to
            save Kei as entering into a fake relationship with her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So who dumped who?” I asked Horikita. I didn’t know the answer to that myself, so
            there was nothing for Horikita to be suspicious about.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems like Karuizawa-san was the one who did the dumping.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s unexpected. She always seemed like the sort who’d want to date a good guy,
            like as a status thing.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose. That’s what I thought, at the very least…” Horikita eyed me for a moment
            like she was suspicious of me, but looked away a moment later. She couldn’t read my
            poker face, and the fact that she’d looked away was proof she was beginning to understand
            that, too. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Still…Kei broke up with Hirata, huh? Made sense, considering she’d started the whole
            fake relationship in the first place, and it didn’t </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">really </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">matter who dumped who. It was probably Hirata who’d suggested that this arrangement
            would be better for Kei. If he’d been the one to dump Kei, it might’ve posed a danger
            to her status, suggesting there was something wrong with her. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Regardless, just looking around me made it obvious what a huge shock their breakup
            had been for Class C. What I really found incredible, though, was how openly the girls
            were discussing romantic affairs.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh, what? You broke up with him, and you don’t even have a new boyfriend lined up,
            Karuizawa-san?!” asked Shinohara, her words carrying loud and clear across the room.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though Ike, Sudou, and the other guys were chatting amongst themselves, they
            were clearly listening in on the girls’ conversation. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know. I just think I need to step things up and move on. It would be easy
            to just let Yousuke-kun pamper me, but I’ve got a lot I want to think about on my
            own, you know?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The catastrophic breakup of </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">the</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> couple obviously had an impact on Class C, but it might’ve had an impact on the other
            classes, too. If nothing else, the girls would certainly be fighting over who got
            Hirata next.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I wonder how they can even think about things like romance. Considering the rules
            of this school, you’d think there’d be no guarantee what tomorrow brings.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Maybe it’s exactly because there’s no guarantees that they’re enjoying the present
            as much as they can.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have no reason to deny that, as long as they’re not robbing someone else of their
            future…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata Yousuke, the other subject of this conversation, sat surrounded by guys and
            girls from our class, wearing his usual gentle expression. Even though he’d supposedly
            just been dumped by his girlfriend, he didn’t look miserable at all. The clearest
            proof of that was that Ike and Sudou weren’t heading on over to tease him.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No… It might be more accurate to say they’d graduated from that sort of behavior.
            They did look interested in the conversation, but didn’t seem to be whispering gossip.
            Actually, if anything, it was Horikita and I who were having a tasteless conversation.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After all the special exams and the training camp, those immature guys were starting
            to change, little by little. Of course, it wasn’t as though everyone was maturing
            at the same rate.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yo, Hirata! I heard you got dumped by Karuizawa? Don’t stress over it, dude! It’s
            all good!” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d thought they were all capable of reading the room, but apparently, Yamauchi was
            the exception. He approached Hirata, all smiles, smacking him on the shoulder. The
            sight clearly made Ike and Sudou a bit uncomfortable, because they approached, flanking
            Yamauchi and grabbing him from the sides.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, what’s the big deal? Come on, dudes, let’s all console Hirata together!” said
            Yamauchi. “Even pretty boys can get dumped!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not cool, man,” said Sudou. “Knock it off.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? But we’re talking about the big heartthrob getting dumped. Extra spicy, extra
            rare!” Yamauchi just argued back, ignoring Sudou’s attempt to chastise him.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sorry, Hirata. I’ll get him outta here,” said Sudou.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s okay,” said Hirata. “It’s the truth, isn’t it?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He would have been well within his rights to look displeased, but he didn’t seem to
            mind at all.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">From her seat beside me, Horikita spoke up suddenly. “That reminds me…what do you
            know about Ichinose-san? I’ve been hearing some things about her that sound like slander
            lately.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">A question about Class B, out of the blue? Hmm.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Maybe someone’s spreading rumors because they’re jealous of her? She’s popular. Or
            maybe it’s part of a strategy to bring down Class B. What do these slanderous rumors
            say?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m…” Horikita glanced around. “a little hesitant to say them out loud.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She passed me her notebook under the desk. Scrawled on the page was a whole list of
            things. Things like “She has a history of violent outbursts,” “She’s engaged in paid
            dating,” “She’s committed theft and robbery,” and “She has a history of drug usage.”
            Even the delinquents over there hadn’t checked off all the items on this list. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Those are some terrible rumors,” I said.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She doesn’t seem like that kind of person at all, though…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, even if someone is starting rumors, I suppose they can’t be charged with a
            crime.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not true. Regardless of whether or not the defamatory statements are true
            or false, if they’re spread publicly, and to enough people… it’s defamation of character.
            You could sue,” said Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Sure, out in the real world.” But this was high school. This was a self-contained
            space filled with students who were minors. It wasn’t like these rumors were posted
            on the internet for the whole world to see.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re saying that it doesn’t count as a crime at all, then?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Well, even if it couldn’t be punished through legal action, the school might still
            choose to issue some form of punishment at its own discretion. But it would probably
            be difficult to pinpoint precisely who had started the rumors. You could simply claim
            that you’d heard it from someone else, and that would be the end of it. The school’s
            attempts to investigate would only go so far, and in the end, the problem would remain
            unresolved. All the school could really do was warn students not to thoughtlessly
            spread more rumors.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At any rate, it was clear that someone had been working to crush Ichinose for a while
            now. It was most likely Sakayanagi pulling the strings here, but barely anyone seemed
            to realize that. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What has Ichinose been doing in response?” I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No idea. It’s not like we’re close. And if I carelessly step forward, suspicion might
            fall on us.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, it’s true that watching closely from the sidelines is the wisest option,” I
            told her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Still…I have to wonder if a strategy this cliched will really work against Ichinose-san,”
            mused Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you mean?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No matter how bad the rumors are, the damage they can do is limited. Even I know
            what kind of reputation Ichinose-san has in this school. This kind of harassment is
            too vicious to be about something as everyday as envy, like you suggested.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’re saying the culprit messed up?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose. But, as the saying goes, ‘where there’s smoke, there’s fire,’” she replied.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you’re saying that Ichinose has a history of violence, or did drugs, or something?”
            I asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if the rumors are mostly baseless, maybe there’s one in the mix that’s true?”
            said Horikita, before adding, “Well, it’s extremely unlikely, of course, but…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hmm. I guess there was no way to know yet whether everything was lies or nothing more
            than rumors, just as Horikita said. And Sakayanagi had hinted that there might be
            some truth to the rumors, even if just a shred of it.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well… it’s not like we’ll come up with answers just sitting here thinking about it.
            Enough about that. It seems the school has released updated class rankings based on
            the results of the school camp . Care to take a look?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Meh, I don’t really—”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, I’m aware you don’t really care. But you really should know, just in case.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Fine, fine.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I flipped through the pages of the notebook she’d forced me to look at.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">3.1</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HE UPROAR SURROUNDING</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Hirata and Kei’s breakup that morning was still fresh in everyone’s minds when Class
            C was rocked by yet more romantic drama. Classes had come to an end for the day, and
            some students were heading off to their clubs, while others went back to their dorms.
            In the midst of all that came an extremely unexpected visitor.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please excuse me,” said the visitor. “Would Yamauchi Haruki-kun happen to be here?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The students still in the classroom turned toward Yamauchi in unison, shock plain
            on their faces. As for Yamauchi, he’d probably been planning to head back to the dorm
            with Ike and play a game, because he’d just cracked open a strategy guide.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh. Yeah, that’s me,” said Yamauchi, “but…what do you want?” He usually got excited
            when he saw a cute girl, but this time? He seemed completely thrown.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The visitor was Sakayanagi, leader of the first-year Class A. And here she was, asking
            for Yamauchi by name. “Would you mind giving me a moment of your time?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“O-of course! Uh, I don’t mind. I’m free.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hm. But you know, I’m afraid that this isn’t quite the place for this conversation.
            Why don’t you meet me in the hallway by the stairs?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Perhaps she was concerned about the looks she was getting from other students. Sakayanagi
            disappeared into the hall, her eyes cast downward. Silence fell upon Class C once
            more.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, no, no, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">no</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">! This can’t be happening!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was Ike—standing next to Yamauchi—who broke the silence. If Sudou were here, there
            probably would’ve been an even greater uproar, but he’d already left for basketball
            practice. Everyone in the room, including Yamauchi himself, struggled to wrap their
            heads around Sakayanagi’s bold entrance and invitation.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Suddenly, as if moving purely on instinct, Yamauchi snatched up his bag. “Sorry! Got
            a little something to take care of!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-yeah, sure…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But just as he was about to bolt from the classroom, Horikita blocked the exit, as
            if telling him to stop and think. “Wait, Yamauchi-kun—”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wh-what is it, Horikita?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Maybe she’s trying to bring Class C down.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? Why do ya think that?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I could say that the fact that </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you’re</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> the one being invited out is abnormal, in and of itself.” Horikita’s face was serious,
            and her words were as pointed as knives. This was taking bluntness a bit too far.
            You couldn’t blame someone for thinking she was insulting them.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But, on the contrary, Yamauchi was rather positive about the whole thing.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know that whole thing where you, like…fall in love with the transfer student
            after crashing into her at the street corner, and she falls over all cute, and she’s
            holding toast corners in her mouth? Yeah. You know, right?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Toast…corners?” repeated Horikita. She furrowed her brow, confused. Who </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">wouldn’t </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">be confused, with just Yamauchi’s words to go on? But I’d seen him crash into Sakayanagi
            back at the school camp, so I figured that was the incident he was referring to.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m going. Because Sakayanagi-chan is waiting for me.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nothing Horikita could say was going to stop him. Yamauchi began walking off.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you plan to do if this does happen to be a trap?” she asked.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nah, no way.” He wasn’t even considering the possibility. “I’m definitely our class’s
            ultimate weapon, but that’s why it’s all going to be fine. If it’s a trap—and that’s
            a one-in-a-million chance—I’ll just handle it, okay?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He’d “handle it,” huh? Handle it how? I bet he didn’t even have a plan.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“…I understand,” said Horikita. “I can’t stop you. But please, don’t get careless
            and leak any valuable information related to our class’s internal affairs.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t worry ’bout it. I know.” And with that, Yamauchi left the classroom with a
            huge grin on his face. Some of the students, Ike included amongst them, hurriedly
            followed.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We should go too,” said Haruka, glancing not just at me, but at Keisei and Airi,
            who’d been sitting with her. I couldn’t think of a reason </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">not </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">to go, so I nodded and got up from my seat.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Out in the hallway, we immediately ran into Ike and a few of the boys.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah, stop, stop! Over here!” The Professor called out to us before we could move on.
            “Those two are talking over there, right now.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? What’s up with the way he’s talking?” Haruka muttered, noticing the Professor
            no longer spoke in the bizarre, archaic way that he used to.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Seems like he got straightened out at the school camp,” I answered, offering an explanation
            for the Professor’s newly serious manner of speaking.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It just feels like…I don’t know, he’s just less interesting. Ehh, who cares,” said
            Haruka, almost immediately losing interest. We all focused our attention on Yamauchi
            and Sakayanagi.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Umm, so. Uh. What did you wanna…talk about?” asked Yamauchi nervously. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As for Sakayanagi, she was running her left hand through her hair, looking somewhat
            embarrassed. If I had to analyze this situation from a psychological perspective,
            I’d say the hair thing was probably unconscious, meant to make her more attractive
            to the member of the opposite sex whom she was interested in.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol9_INTERIORIMAGES_2" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v9/OPS/ClassroomElite-Vol9_INTERIORIMAGES_2.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait,” muttered Ike, sounding frustrated, “maybe Sakayanagi seriously does have a
            thing for Haruki?” He’d probably instinctively sensed that from Sakayanagi’s expressions
            and gestures.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In this particular instance, however, I should probably assume that Sakayanagi was
            deliberately trying to give off that impression. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In contrast to my calm, collected analysis—</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, no, this is way too stupid,” Haruka spat. She looked sick to her stomach. “She’s
            playing him. No way in hell that she could like Yamauchi-kun.” A woman’s intuition,
            maybe?</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Y-yeah, I think you’re right,” said Airi, perhaps because she could sense the same
            thing.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on! Men are </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">so</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> simple. How could someone be fooled by that obvious act?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is she…</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">really</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> acting?” asked Keisei uncertainly. I guess I wouldn’t have noticed either if I hadn’t
            looked carefully…</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Definitely</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> an act,” said Haruka, with absolute certainty.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Maybe she’s trying to get information about Class C, just like Horikita-san said?”
            said Ike.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know, seems a bit obvious,” said Keisei. “You’d think there’s a better way
            to do that. If she’d met up with Yamauchi in secret, it wouldn’t put us on our guard
            like we are now. It would’ve made things way easier for her.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I suppose that’s true…” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Keisei was exactly right. Even if she were planning to lure Yamauchi into a trap,
            there were plenty of ways to make contact with him without raising our suspicions.
            Deliberately making the entirety of Class C aware of her actions would do her more
            harm than good, implicating her in any problems that arose as a result. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So maybe she actually did have a thing for Yamauchi, just like Keisei and Ike had
            said. That would make more sense. But then again, Sakayanagi was aggressive and bold
            in general, so it really could be either one. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To tell you the truth,” said Sakayanagi, “I’ve been wanting to talk to you for a
            while now.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“F-f-f-for real?” sputtered Yamauchi. “Like, for real for real?” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I don’t exactly have the kind of free time to lie about something like this,
            do I?” she replied.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I watched them carefully as they talked, conducting my own analysis.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m afraid I can’t quite calm myself down here, Yamauchi. Maybe we should…go somewhere
            else?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I see! Yeah, okay. Sure, let’s!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, then, come along now, please.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The two of them walked side by side, Yamauchi trying to match Sakayanagi’s slow pace.
            It seemed like he </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">was</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> capable of showing the bare minimum of consideration. The other students watched
            the pair walk away—they probably knew that trying to follow any longer would be difficult.</span></p>
      
      <div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
      
      <p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">3.2</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">A</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">LL OF THE MEMBERS</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> of the Ayanokouji Group had assembled in the café except for Akito, who’d headed
            to his club activities.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Haruka kicked things off at once. “Okay, so what do you think is </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">really</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> going on with that whole farce earlier? What’re Yamauchi-kun and Sakayanagi-san </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">doing</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Can we really say that it was a farce for certain though?” asked Keisei.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s…of course it was! Anyone would know. Right, Airi?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, um.” Airi blushed. “I suppose. I think maybe it </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">could</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? But come on, it was so </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">obviously </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">put on, right? As a show? Right?” said Haruka.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah, I suppose that the gestures she made did make it look one way… but like as
            Keisei-kun said, do you really think she’d come right out and do something bad by
            coming to visit Class C?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on,” said Haruka, “use your head. That’s part of the play; she wants to get
            us all mixed up with reverse psychology.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hmm. So Haruka thought that by being so overt, Sakayanagi would make us think that
            it was way too obvious to be a trap in the first place. It was possible.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you think, Kiyopon? Yukimuu? Do you seriously think there could be…romance?”
            asked Haruka.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is a topic that I’m not very well versed in,” said Keisei. “I’ll pass on further
            questions, thank you.” He clearly didn’t want to talk about romance any further. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, now Haruka and Airi turned their gazes toward me.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“To be honest,” I said, “Yamauchi and Sakayanagi haven’t really interacted at all
            until now. This is way too sudden for something so basic to turn into romance, don’t
            you think?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Quite the level-headed opinion, Kiyopon,” said Haruka. “But falling in love doesn’t
            always follow the same pattern or pace. People like Hirata-kun are one thing, I guess,
            but I doubt Yamauchi-kun is the kind who needs a while to fall for someone.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In the end, the conversation stalled out due to our lack of further information. Eventually,
            the topic changed from Yamauchi and Sakayanagi’s romance to what was going on within
            Class C.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, speaking of Hirata-kun,” said Airi, “didn’t he break up with Karuizawa-san?”
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t really say I’m surprised,” said Haruka. “Honestly, it seemed like they’d
            break up for sure someday.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“R-really, Haruka?” sputtered Airi.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I guess if you look at it as the leader of the boys going out with the leader of
            the girls, then they make sense as a couple. But they’re not really a good match for
            one another, y’know? It’s just like, how do I put it…? Hirata-kun seems like he’d
            like a cute, gentle girl.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But I think that Karuizawa-san is really cute,” said Airi. “Don’t you think so, Kiyotaka-kun?”
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was asking me? It was a difficult question. Perhaps she asked me that because
            she specifically wanted to hear </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">my</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> thoughts on that matter.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Dunno,” I said. “I’ve never really paid much attention to Karuizawa.” </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I didn’t know how Airi felt about that, but it was the only answer I could give.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmm, well, I </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">suppose</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> that’s true.” said Haruka. “Anyhow, Karuizawa-san aside, the problem is that Hirata-kun
            has become a free agent.” Thankfully, she was shifting the topic of conversation back
            to Hirata. “It seems like there are quite a few girls in class who like Hirata-kun.
            I wonder what’ll happen.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really?” asked Airi.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? You mean you never noticed? I mean…for example, Mii-chan definitely has a thing
            for him.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well… now that you mention it, she certainly does look over at Hirata-kun now and
            again.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I know, right?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Keisei took out his notebook, perhaps because he was getting fed up with all this
            talk of romance. “I’d like to study for a bit, I think.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, that’s right, the year-end exam’s coming up soon… Well, that’s a depressing thought,”
            said Haruka.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ll need to put together some study guides for you guys too,” said Keisei.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Haruka let out a little laugh before lowering her head down on the table, like she
            was trying to make a full bowing motion. Chabashira hadn’t given us any special instructions
            about the year-end exam, so it was probably going to be your standard written test.
            If a student got a failing grade, they’ll be expelled at once. That was my guess,
            anyway.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So when are we going to start our study group?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s see,” said Keisei. “Hmm…how about after we’ve finished with the practice test
            on the 15th? That’ll leave us about ten days until the final. If we focus on questions
            that have cropped up in the past and the trends we’ve been seeing in class, we should
            be good.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, ho!” Just like that, Haruka’s mood seemed to lift, probably because this meant
            she could put off studying a little longer. “Just what I’d expect from you, Yukimuu!
            A perfect plan. Completely agree, let’s roll with it.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The last special exam of the school year will probably be held after the year-end
            final. In March, I expect,” said Keisei.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The last special exam of the year…” Airi echoed. “Wow, so our first year is already
            almost over, huh?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ve certainly been through a lot,” said Haruka, “but it’s really gone by so fast,
            hasn’t it?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Airi and Haruka both reflected back on the year for a moment…but Keisei brought them
            out of their reverie at once. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s way too early to be reminiscing. If you fail the year-end final exam, you’ll
            get expelled. Not to mention what’s in the special exam, too…” He probably didn’t
            want them to lose focus.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Soon enough, Keisei was nose-deep in studying…and Haruka noticed something.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I followed her line of sight, spotting Ichinose with what looked like several guys
            and girls, all Class B students. Unlike our own gathering, they all looked stiff and tense.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If I had to guess, they were trying to come up with ways to protect Ichinose from
            the defamation and slander currently directed her way. Ichinose herself, however,
            probably didn’t want anything of the sort. She was behaving exactly the same as she
            always did: chatting with her friends, engaging cheerfully with people everywhere
            she went.</span></p>

      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Again,” huh? So he’d noticed Kanzaki tailing him some time ago. Although it didn’t
            seem like he really cared about being followed, probably because he didn’t see it
            as a real threat. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Was it Sakayanagi who told you to spread those rumors?” asked Kanzaki.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Uh, no?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, then who? The only other person who could give out orders to Class A would
            be Katsuragi.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who knows? I’ve just been hanging out with other students. I picked up the rumors
            somewhere along the way. Even if you say that the rumors came from Class A, what am
            I supposed to do with that? Maybe it’s Ryuuen’s handiwork. Maybe he’s just been pretending
            to be in retirement?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After hearing that, Kanzaki changed his approach. “So you took stories without knowing
            whether or not they were actually true, accepted them at face value, and just…spread
            them around?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s just how people work, isn’t it? It doesn’t matter if they’re true or made-up;
            if the rumor is interesting, people are going to talk. Besides, us guys are nothing
            compared to the girls in that department, eh?” Hashimoto directed his gaze over at
            Haruka and Airi.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well… I certainly do like rumors, but…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“And sadly, the juicier the rumors, the more exciting they become. Try to think this
            through a little more objectively, Kanzaki. Ichinose neither confirmed nor denied
            the rumors. And she’s not asking anyone to help her, either. Don’t you think that’s
            strange? If they’re a complete fabrication, then you’d think she’d be asking for help
            finding their source, right?” said Hashimoto.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ichinose has an extreme aversion to conflict,” said Kanzaki. “I believe she could
            find it in her heart to show compassion to everyone…even those who spread vicious
            rumors about her.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Since she wasn’t taking a stance of her own on the subject, Kanzaki couldn’t do anything
            but choose to believe in her.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“God, you Class-B people…”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In any case, Hashimoto’s words and behavior had led me to one conclusion: the rumors
            being circulated about Ichinose…weren’t all lies, after all.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I put aside my position as a student for a moment, examining this case from a social
            perspective. Ichinose could probably sue the person(s) who’d started the rumor for
            defamation of character. Regardless of whether the rumors were true, they’d damaged
            her reputation, which gave her grounds to sue. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">…Unless, of course, the rumors involved facts that were already public knowledge.
            If this </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">was</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> Sakayanagi’s handiwork, then it was all going according to plan. Ichinose’s silence
            was proof it was working as intended.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">After giving Kanzaki a pat on the shoulder, Hashimoto thrust his hands into his pocket
            and tried to walk off.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We aren’t done talking,” said Kanzaki.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, man, isn’t this enough? Even if we continue this conversation, it’s not
            like we’re going to see eye-to-eye,” said Hashimoto.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He gave Haruka and Airi a gentle wave, then headed off in the direction of the school
            building. Something was off, here. My gut told me Hashimoto was acting differently
            around me than he had when we were at the school camp. </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was nothing more than a hunch, though. I couldn’t put a finger on what, specifically,
            had changed.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Please excuse me.” Kanzaki offered us a slight bow, and then walked back toward the
            dorms rather than the school building.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wow, okay,” said Haruka. “That right there? That was </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">awesome.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What on earth,” said Akito, “was </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">awesome</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> about that?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Haruka stuck out her tongue. “Come on! It’s like, you know, we were on the verge of
            things getting exciting. Besides, even in the off chance that we got attacked, you
            could’ve totally taken the dude, right, Miyacchi?” She made a few quick jabbing motions
            in the air to demonstrate.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I hear you used to be a delinquent?” I asked, following the flow of the conversation.
            </span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Akito let out a heavy sigh. “Don’t tell people that, Haruka. I don’t really want it
            getting around.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come on, what’s the big deal?” she asked. “You’re different now, anyway. You were
            really strong then, huh?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I…I wasn’t some famous delinquent or anything, okay? Someone else was top dog at
            the junior high I went to. That guy was way stronger than me.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh. Was it a pretty rough school?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well yeah. That’s just how people were in my hometown. I mean, the adults, too. And
            when they had kids, they’d raise ’em to be the same. Oh, by the way, Ryuuen from Class
            D? He used to go to a junior high next to mine,” said Akito.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whoa, seriously?!”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yep. We ran into each other a few times when fights broke out between the schools.
            I don’t think the guy really paid me any mind, though.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Akito probably knew how to handle those kinds of situations because he’d had experience
            brawling.</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay,” he said, “the conversation ends here. And don’t spread this outside the group,
            okay?”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Okay, okay, I get it,” said Haruka. “How about we head back to the café? Yukimuu
            is waitin’ for us.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Gotcha.”</span></p>
      
      <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At the end of the day, this was Akito’s business, after all. If I was sure of anything,
            it was that it was best not to dig any deeper.</span></p>
