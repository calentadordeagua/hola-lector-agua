<p id="auto_bookmark_toc_top" class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 4:<br/>Insanity</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">O</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">NE DAY,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> shortly before winter vacation, a storm hit Class D. It happened just after the end of homeroom. Our classroom door swung open, and Ryuuen and his fellow Class C students strode inside. The entire class immediately erupted into chaos.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Chabashira-sensei glanced at Ryuuen and the others, but left without a word. It would’ve been one thing if a brawl broke out right then and there, but there was no issue with students from another class coming to visit. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen and his classmates had observed Class D from a distance so far. Since they hadn’t gotten the answers they’d been looking for, they’d finally decided to take a direct approach. Or perhaps they were acting on a strategy I couldn’t yet understand. In any case, they’d clearly decided to confront us. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita, who was packing up her things to leave, stopped and stared at the Class C group consisting of Ryuuen, Ishizaki, and Yamada Albert. Komiya and Kondou were there, too. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With all these opponents gathered together in force, the mood was tense.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, what’s goin’ on? This is Class D.” Sudou was the first to react. He was usually quick to pick a fight, but seemed more defensive this time. More importantly, he probably felt he needed to protect Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He stood and approached Ryuuen; Hirata panicked and hurried to stand between them, probably afraid that they were about to get violent. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Do you have some business with our class, Ryuuen-kun?” Hirata asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In response, Ryuuen exaggeratedly raised his hands. “Is there any reason I can’t visit my classmates? That </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">does</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> happen at this school, doesn’t it? Visiting a pal. Why’re you all shaking?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The statement sounded like a provocation, but Hirata remained cool and composed. “That’s normally true, yes. But this school isn’t normal, is it? Besides, you’ve never visited Class D before.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ve been too estranged. I thought we should be a little more assertive about buddying up,” said Ryuuen. He placed his hand on a nearby girl’s desk, showing off his white teeth in a grin. “Man, you guys sure did great in the Paper Shuffle. Class C lost because of your brilliance. Well, the results still aren’t out, but word is you guys might be Class C next semester. That’s huge.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh. Guess the only thing big about you is your head, you incompetent monkey. Time for </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">to get a taste of being Class D,” said Sudou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hirata grabbed Sudou by the shoulder to reign him in, clearly panicked. “It’s because we’ve been working hard.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Working hard, huh?” said Ryuuen. “I mean, the concept of hard work seems completely foreign to Sudou, yet he’s still here. I thought he’d be the first to get kicked out.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">do</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> remember my name,” said Sudou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen and Sudou stared each other down. The air between them was electric. Several classmates who’d been heading out stood frozen in place. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Could you tell us why you’re really here?” asked Hirata. Perhaps he wanted to bring the situation under control as soon as possible, and was therefore trying to keep Ryuuen from dragging it out. Or perhaps he was just being as straightforward as always.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m giving you a fair warning, Class D,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Warning? What do you mean?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have no intention of explaining things to an idiot. Or are you just pretending not to understand?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It might’ve seemed as if he was trying to provoke Hirata, but in fact, Ryuuen barely even looked at him. He was busy scanning the entire classroom. He might have aimed his statement at me, or Keisei, or even Akito. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ultimately, his gaze landed on a rather unexpected person. The individual in question didn’t even realize Ryuuen was staring at him—or perhaps he just didn’t care. In fact, he was heading back to the dorms.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kouenji got up and nonchalantly left the classroom, apparently unaffected by Ryuuen’s presence. Ryuuen gave a quiet chuckle and signaled his comrades to follow him. They left immediately, and as the door closed behind them, the tension in our classroom dissipated at once and was replaced by an uproar. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, hey, that Ryuuen dude looked like he was gonna do something crazy! This is unreal!” Ike exclaimed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“They’re plannin’ to do something to Kouenji,” said Yamauchi. “Right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Indeed. Kouenji Rokusuke, Class D’s resident enfant terrible. Conspiracy theories flew every which way, starting with Ike and Yamauchi. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Unusually, Kushida stayed out of it. She’d stopped getting as actively involved with class issues, perhaps due to her defeat at Horikita’s hand. Even now, though she was murmuring to some other girls about Ryuuen and his crew, she wasn’t actively contributing to the conversation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is really bad, isn’t it?” asked Horikita, while I was deep in thought. Even she, who wanted to avoid entanglement with Class C as far as possible, couldn’t leave this situation alone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Maybe,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It did seem as though Ryuuen had some business with Kouenji, but that puzzled me. me think. Kouenji was weird, sure, but even to an outside observer, the possibility of him being a big mover and shaker in Class D had to be low. There had to be a reason why Ryuuen was trying to make contact with him in such a blatant way, while simultaneously scoping out a number of other people.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Should we go check it out, Kiyotaka?” asked Akito.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So many people are around, though. There’s not much Class C can try.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“They may have something planned.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose… I guess even though lots of people are watching, that’s no guarantee Ryuuen won’t try something.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This was odd. Even from an outside perspective, the probability that Kouenji was Class D’s secret savior was low. In the unlikely event that Class C assaulted Kouenji, other members of Class D might get in trouble for jumping into the fray. Then again, if we held back when we could’ve helped, we’d regret it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">When I went into the hallway with Akito, Keisei trailed me. “I’ll come, too. Safety in numbers,” he reasoned.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita lagged slightly behind us, and Sudou followed her. Hirata also came along, looking worried. This was shaping up to be a real storm. I asked Keisei and Akito to wait, then went to talk to Hirata.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wouldn’t it be better if you stayed behind, Hirata? If you come, other students might follow. If people like Ike and Yamauchi join in, it’ll add fuel to the fire,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s true…but will Kouenji-kun be all right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Horikita’s coming. Keisei and Akito are too. In the worst-case scenario, if it looks like things are getting violent, I’ll call you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Keisei? Huh. Okay. Just don’t do anything rash, sound good?” Hirata looked puzzled at the way I referred to Yukimura, but went back inside the classroom.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That was the right decision, Kiyotaka.” Keisei nodded. “Besides, Hirata’s better suited to calming the rest of the class down.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The next problem was figuring out where Kouenji and the others went. Even Ryuuen and his goons couldn’t start trouble in the school building. If they were going to try something, it would probably be outside, but I had no idea where Kouenji might’ve gone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Where does Kouenji normally go after class?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No idea.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know, either.” Akito and Keisei tilted their heads to the side, apparently clueless. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Does anyone know anything about Kouenji?” Practically none of us ever spoke to him at length.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He usually heads straight back to the dorms.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How do you know?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see him fairly often. Let’s head to the entrance for now.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If his shoes were still there, we could confirm that he was still in the school building. In that case, we probably still had enough time to deal with this. We hurried there, keeping pace with one another.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Could be a serious fight,” said Sudou to Horikita, clenching his fists.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t be funny. Violence between Classes D and C is nothing to laugh about. More importantly, why are you following me?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, ’cuz I’m worried about you, Suzune, you know? There are rumors that Ryuuen will even hit girls.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not so soft that I need your protection,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t say that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita was as bullish as ever, and Sudou’s chivalry was misguided. A girl could easily take down a boy if she had the requisite martial arts skill. However, Sudou was Sudou, and the idea that Horikita was strong enough to hold her own probably hadn’t occurred to him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Besides, though I may be worrying needlessly, what about your club?” asked Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Not to worry. Still got a little time left until practice. Let’s hurry up and find Kouenji.” Sudou was going nowhere.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For crying out loud. I don’t want a troublemaker tailing me around,” she muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Still, if Horikita got hurt taking these Class C guys on by herself, Sudou would go nuclear. If the school caught him picking fights again, he was done for. Letting him come along might’ve been the best way for us to keep an eye on him.</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">4.1</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">L</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">EAVING THE SCHOOL BUILDING,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> we took the tree-lined path back to the dormitories. Since class had just ended, barely anyone was around, but we saw a bunch of Class C guys on the path ahead. Ibuki was with them, though she hadn’t accompanied Ryuuen when he visited our classroom. Some distance in front was Kouenji, walking alone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It seemed Class C really planned to attack him. As Ryuuen drew closer, he ordered Ishizaki to block Kouenji’s path.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s just like Suzune predicted. Let’s stop ’em,” said Sudou, looking to Horikita for orders.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s wait and see what happens. We still don’t know what Ryuuen-kun’s after,” reasoned Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">As Ryuuen himself said, it was well within the rules to simply talk to someone from another class. We approached slowly, observing the situation.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey, Kouenji. Lemme pick your brain, eh?” Ryuuen called.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’re you doing? I don’t recall behaving in any way that’d warrant you flagging me down.” Because Ishizaki was in the way, I couldn’t see Kouenji’s face. But his tone of voice was arrogant as usual.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t get to decide that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hmph. You certainly don’t, either,” replied Kouenji. He looked at Ryuuen and his goons without the slightest hint of anxiety. </span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, why bring this up now, after keeping it quiet all this time? It looked like Sakayanagi was trying to show up Ryuuen. He’d accused her of being stupid, and she’d made a fool of him in response by showing she’d known about the contract all along. Maybe?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I’m not the one who’s gonna be in trouble if those details leak. You will,” said Ryuuen. “Other classes will find out we’ve been getting twenty thousand monthly points from you, won’t they?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you wanted to tell everyone, you would’ve done so already. Besides, it was Katsuragi-kun who suggested the contract in the first place,” Sakayanagi replied definitively. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She hadn’t been present on the island, and could avoid taking responsibility for the decision. It was even possible that she instructed her followers in Class A to let Katsuragi go ahead, but we couldn’t know. What mattered was that Katsuragi was keeping a low profile right now, while Sakayanagi appeared to be in control of the class.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ugh. So, Class C’s basically got a guaranteed monthly allowance?” grumbled Sudou.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t let them fool you, Sudou-kun. Class C could’ve obtained those class points themselves, but they wasted them. They gained nothing,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is that really true, Suzune?” asked Ryuuen. “It’s no different than getting two hundred class points the real way, back on the island. Besides, our private point income is going to continue indefinitely. We’ll keep earning those points until Class A loses their standing.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wrong. It seems similar, but it’s not. All you’re getting is private points. They have nothing to do with class points.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In that regard, Horikita was correct. However, eight hundred thousand private points flowing from Class A to Class C every month was significant. Even if Class C continued to lose class points from here on, they were guaranteed an allowance. Though Sakayanagi’s faction was coming for them, Katsuragi’s faction had given Class C a free ride.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are we finished here? I have no intention of denying you your fun, but I’d like for you to get out of my way. I’ve wasted enough time listening to your meaningless drivel,” said Kouenji.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, Kouenji. You haven’t answered yet.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Kouenji looked at the sky, as if trying to remember. “Something about a clever person in Class D, was it? To be honest, I hadn’t even considered it. At any rate, it’s probably better if I don’t tell you, right? You’re doing whatever it takes to hunt this person down, even if it means putting yourself at risk. I don’t wish to rob you of your fun. As for me, I don’t care for anyone in this school. I’m simply enjoying the halcyon days of my youth, romancing beautiful women and basking in my own&#160;beauty.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you’re saying you won’t help your own class?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I haven’t, and I won’t. I said that all along. From my perspective, Class A and Class D are the same. All of you people are so dreadfully boring.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ryuuen-san, this jerk’s looked down on us for the last time! Let’s teach him a lesson!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki raised his fists, but the person who spoke up to stop him wasn’t Ryuuen. It was Sakayanagi, who had simply sat on the sidelines and smiled at us so far. Kouenji had said something she couldn’t overlook.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s something you said that interests me,” she began. “Dragon Boy-san aside—”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Whoa!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen rushed Sakayanagi and launched a kick. Hashimoto hurried between them in a panic, blocking Ryuuen’s foot with his left arm. He went flying, landing hard on the concrete, but if he hadn’t intervened, Ryuuen probably would’ve kicked Sakayanagi in the face. A white-gloved Class A boy, Kitou, immediately took a fighting stance.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Oh, did I hurt your feelings?” asked Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I told you I’d kill you if you called me that again.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Stop this at once. What you just did is horrible,” Horikita started, but Sakayanagi stopped her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Is there a problem with what just happened, Hashimoto-kun?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No. I fell over.” Hashimoto slowly got back up, dusting himself off. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There you have it, Horikita-san.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tch. Both you and Ryuuen-kun are lunatics,” Horikita said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even in the face of a deliberate act of violence, the Class A students led by Sakayanagi showed no signs of discontent. If anything, they looked entirely ready to trade blows.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m sorry, Ryuuen-kun. I’ve teased you too much,” said Sakayanagi. She focused back on Kouenji. “To return to my original question—what do you mean when you say that everyone here, myself included, is boring?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“For crying out loud…” muttered Horikita. Her exasperation was understandable. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Did I really hurt your feelings that much, little girl?” Kouenji pointed at Sakayanagi, approaching the bench where she sat.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Tsk. Little girl, hmm? That’s a </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">wonderful</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> nickname,” Sakayanagi replied. Ryuuen snorted in amusement, as if that was payback for Kouenji calling him Dragon Boy. “Kouenji-san, was it? Your English could use some work. I’m not a little girl.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not for you to decide. Calling you a little girl is appropriate, considering your age and physique,” replied Kouenji.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That is precisely where you’re mistaken. The appropriate usage of the words ‘little girl’ would be to describe girls of elementary school age. You cannot alter the rules of the world at will,” said Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Being a slave to common practice isn’t my style,” said Kouenji. He brushed his fingers through his hair.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Knock it off, Kouenji,” said Kitou, stepping forward. He motioned as if to remove his white gloves, which I initially thought he wore to protect his hands from the cold. Apparently, that wasn’t it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The heck is </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">with</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> that guy? Will a demon appear if he takes those gloves off or something?” Sudou muttered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What?” I asked, surprised.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You don’t know? It’s from this old manga that used to be real popular. There’s this dude, and when he takes off his white glove, a demon shows up to fight devils,” said Sudou. I’d never heard of it in my life, but then again, I’d never read any manga.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I have no business with Class A. Leave now,” commanded Kouenji.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Allow me to correct this boy’s behavior,” said Kitou to Sakayanagi.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ha ha! Well, I don’t mind you fighting over me. Unfortunately, my tastes in both men and women run toward older people,” said Kouenji. He was toying with both Sakayanagi and Ryuuen, the representatives of their respective classes. Maybe lunacy was a kind of power after all, much like both violence and lies.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’ve concluded our business. Now get lost,” snapped Ryuuen. Dealing with something like Kouenji had to be exhausting, even for him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Very well, then. See you,” said Kouenji. He turned on his heel and walked away.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The storm we’d feared would break might just have been Kouenji, not Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With that source of controversy gone, everyone grew quiet. Sudou had collected most of the broken mirror’s shards, and it seemed that the tension had dissipated for&#160;now.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, show’s over,” said Sakayanagi. “Shall we head back?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Better be on your guard for the third semester, Sakayanagi,” warned Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course. If you’re so certain you’ve defeated Class D, I’ll be your opponent anytime,” Sakayanagi shot back as she left with her entourage in tow.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Should we head back too, Horikita?” I asked. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yeah. I can’t stand being here a moment longer. Although…I must say, Ryuuen-kun seemed less interested in Kouenji-kun than I anticipated,” mused Horikita. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen’s actions clearly still puzzled her, and it seemed Class C shared some of the same doubts. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Should we really let him go that easily?” asked one of Ryuuen’s flunkies.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If he were the one, I wouldn’t have let him leave,” answered Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“He seems super suspicious to me, though. He could’ve been lying.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“His way of thinking doesn’t match mine. Whoever X is, he and I think the same way. Besides, does he even look like the type to join forces with Horikita?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s hard to imagine, true. But then why’d you target Kouenji?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen stopped watching Kouenji’s retreating back and turned a creepy grin on us. “Yo. What do </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">guys think about Kouenji?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You’ve been muttering a bunch of crap for a while now,” grumbled Sudou, clenching his fists. “I don’t get what you’re sayin’ at all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Idiots should stay out of this,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What—?!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Horikita stopped Sudou in his tracks with a look and a wave of her hand. “Your actions are bizarre and incomprehensible, Ryuuen-kun,” she said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen seemed to take that as a compliment. “In that case, I must be doing something right. I narrowed down my suspect list quite a bit today, Suzune. Down to that weird creep lurking behind you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m not listening to anything you have to say. This is a waste of time. In future, stop approaching my classmates,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m free to approach or not approach whomever I want. It’s not against the rules.” Ironic, considering how often he broke the rules. “Anyway, the show will be over soon. Can’t wait for the grand finale.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen glanced briefly at Sakayanagi’s retreating form in the distance, then left.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Finally. Let’s go. We should tell Hirata-kun about this,” said Horikita.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What’s Ryuuen’s deal? You think he’s up to something?” Sudou groused.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Who knows? I doubt anyone truly understands how his mind works.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">It sounds like Ryuuen’s preparations are nearly complete,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I decided as I watched him and his goons walk off. It wasn’t a pleasant thought.</span></p>
