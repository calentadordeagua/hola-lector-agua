<p id="auto_bookmark_toc_top" class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 1 - Prologue:<br/>Ryuuen Kakeru’s Soliloquy</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T WAS SOON AFTER STARTING</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> elementary school that I realized I was abnormal. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I found a large snake on a class field trip. I wasn’t afraid of being bitten. Some students watched with great interest from a safe distance, some were scared, and still others couldn’t care less. Most, however, wanted the snake to die. Even the adults panicked, crying out for someone to help.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">So, I grabbed a big rock and bashed in the snake’s head. I might have gotten bit, but I wasn’t afraid of that.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My classmates screamed, and the teachers were in a panic. I didn’t really care. I wasn’t trying to be a hero by destroying the snake everyone feared; I just didn’t see the need to be afraid of it. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I learned something about myself that day. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The moment an enemy succumbs, I feel a huge rush of adrenaline. Fear and pleasure are two sides of the same coin to me; violence rules the world, and the effectiveness of your own particular brand of violence determines what you accomplish. The snake’s death was my first unshakeable victory, and the sight of its flattened corpse gave me such pleasure. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The fact remains, however, that people are hostile toward those they sense are different from them. I’ve had many enemies since that day, both internal and external. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even so, I was never afraid. All I thought about was how to get revenge, and how to turn the tables on my enemies. Eventually, they would all bow to me. Those who possess an unparalleled capacity for violence are truly the elite. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was just one problem, and it grew as I did. It became increasingly difficult for me to feel pleasure. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I was bored. Bored because no one could fulfill my dearest wish—to be overthrown.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Perhaps I’d only meet my match in death.</span></p>
