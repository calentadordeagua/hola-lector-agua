<p id="auto_bookmark_toc_top" class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 5:<br/>Time to Settle Things</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">“T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HIS BRINGS US</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> to the end of homeroom,” said Sakagami-sensei, Class C’s teacher. “I’d like to remind everyone to please be on your best behavior, even during winter vacation, and to make good use of your time. That’s all.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I took out my phone as I listened to his gracious, meaningless words. Today was the day of the closing ceremony—the final day of the second semester. Our classes finished early, leaving us free by afternoon. There were no club activities today, either. The school encouraged students to head back early, so hardly anyone would be left in the building.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">In other words, it was finally time to strike.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I eliminated everyone I can, but that still leaves about ten possible candidates,” I muttered to myself. Some were people I’d never even spoken to, but it couldn’t be helped. I would’ve preferred to do this without using Karuizawa, but X had eluded me so far.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, I guess this just means I get to have some more fun.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">To be honest, there was a particular person I’d had my eye on all along, but I couldn’t jump to conclusions yet. If anything, it would be all the more fun to be able to confront X with a clear head and no doubts.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d taken certain steps after the Paper Shuffle. I mobilized every Class C student and assigned them targets to spy on. Of course, it wasn’t like tailing people was likely to get me much closer to discovering X’s identity. I excluded the weaklings, narrowing my list of suspects to delinquents like Sudou and Miyake, and conservative people like Hirata. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The Class D punks had noticed they were being tailed—except for Sudou, who was so dumb and oblivious I’d had to have my people provoke him directly. But being noticed was fine by me. I wanted them constantly aware that I was watching. I wanted X to spend their days trembling with fear, terrified of having their identity discovered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yet they’d continued to use Suzune as a cover, stubbornly keeping themselves hidden. Which meant I had to tighten the noose.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">One more thing. Though I’d all but told X I’d target Karuizawa, I had yet to make a move. I was sure they’d been on edge these past two weeks or so, probably wondering how and when I’d contact Karuizawa, how I’d extract information from her. Chances were, they were asking her daily whether anything out of the ordinary had happened. The uncertainty would take it out of them. It would confuse them. They’d be exhausted, paranoid, and jumping at shadows, constantly imagining me breathing down their neck. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Which made today the best day for me to strike. Now, I was ready to corner them.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even though it’d only been a few minutes since dismissal, more than half the class had left. The clock on the wall seemed as if it was ticking slower than normal. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh heh…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My heart raced. It’d been years since I felt this kind of exhilaration. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki had asked why I risked so much just to identify X. She called it meaningless. She was right that I didn’t care about anything past discovering X’s identity, at least. Ibuki thought I’d feel disappointed once I found out who&#160;X was. That might be true of normal people, but fighting Class D had led me to understand that X thought and moved like me. I’d never met another person like myself before. That fascination drove me onward. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wondered how I would feel when I came face-to-face with X, and what my next goal would be. X had entertained me for so long. The thought of meeting them made my pulse quicken, almost like first love.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I would do anything, use any means, to find them. The text I’d sent to X this morning was already marked as read. They knew what was going to happen today. What would they do, then? What kind of plan would they devise?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ryuuen-kun,” said Shiina Hiyori, who sat right beside&#160;me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Everyone seems really restless today, don’t they?” she asked, surveying the room. The remaining students all drew nearer to me. “What are you planning?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m going to confront my entertaining quarry of these past several months. You wanna come?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I must decline. I don’t quite see how that would be fun.” Shiina paused, then said, “Do you really need to hunt them down?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Nothing. I suppose that’s for you to decide, Ryuuen-kun, as our class’s leader.” She stood. “I’ll be in the library. If there’s any trouble, please contact me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s not like you’ll be of any use, though, is it?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose you’re right. Well, have a nice winter&#160;vacation.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She said that calmly, without a trace of fear, then left. Hiyori was clever, but she hated conflict, which made her useless as a pawn. People who obediently followed me around were much easier to use.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I finished my final preparations, then gathered my people.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s time, isn’t it, Ryuuen-san?” said Ishizaki, looking restless.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Let’s make the most of today,” I told him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I handed Ishizaki a bag with certain indispensable items in it. Ibuki and Albert stood up, too. I didn’t need large numbers for this. I needed people who were tight-lipped, because we were about to do something that would shock this proper, well-behaved school to its core.</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">5.1</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">HIRTY MINUTES AFTER</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> homeroom ended, winter vacation had begun. The campus was practically deserted. As with summer vacation, the students stampeded back to their dorms, leaving barely anyone to witness our bold movements. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, where are we going? Cut the crap and tell us,” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d said nothing of my current strategy to anyone, to eliminate the possibility of spies like Manabe and her friends. Ibuki and the rest only knew that I’d ordered Ishizaki and the other guys to keep an eye on Miyake and a few more people. Therefore, they didn’t know the real reason I’d pressed Kouenji. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had no doubt that X would do whatever necessary to keep their identity hidden. So, I’d hidden my plans as much as possible to improve my chances of cornering them.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Concerned, Ibuki?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You make me nervous, dragging me into all your stupid schemes.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki might have been curious about my true intentions as well, because he drew in closer, too.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You remember Karuizawa, right?” I said. “She’s the reason Manabe and her friends were strong-armed into spying on us for Class D.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That loud girl from Class D, right?” Ibuki had infiltrated Class D on the deserted island, so she knew its students quite well.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I sent Karuizawa a message today, asking her to come to the rooftop. Karuizawa knows I was the one who sent it, too. I got her email address from another girl in her class.” I avoided saying the traitor’s name. It wasn’t necessary for me to reveal Kushida Kikyou’s part in this yet.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? The rooftop? No way Karuizawa will come if </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">you</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> ask her.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She’ll definitely show. I told her that, if she doesn’t, I’ll expose her past.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">If that pathetic bullying story went public, it would cause an uproar. Her social life would be dead in the water. She had no choice but to bite the bullet and turn up.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if Karuizawa </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">does</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> show, do you think she’d tell you X’s identity?” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I suppose she wouldn’t let that slip, normally…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Normally? No.” X had almost certainly promised to protect Karuizawa from all her enemies. “But I sent X a message, too. I told them I was meeting Karuizawa today, and that I was going to learn their identity from her. That I’d use any means necessary to get what I wanted. That way, I’m not just threatening Karuizawa, but also threatening X at the same time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But…you sent a threatening message to Karuizawa, right? What if she tattles to the school? If X puts the idea in her head, she might.” Ibuki glared at me, as if demanding to know why I hadn’t thought that far ahead.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She won’t do that. If she does, I’ll expose her right away. No matter what she tries, Karuizawa can’t turn the tables on us.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The only countermeasure at their disposal would be if X showed up to confront me directly instead of Karuizawa.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The worst-case scenario,” I said, “will be if X shows up instead of Karuizawa. Regardless, it’s going to be fun to see what Karuizawa does.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I can’t imagine it’s worth the risk.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s not true at all. Crushing Karuizawa means taking one of X’s most valuable pawns off the board. It seems they’ve been using her in various ingenious ways.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How do you know that? I mean, I understand that X threatened Manabe and her friends to protect Karuizawa, but…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Even I had only recently realized that Karuizawa was one of X’s pawn. I had arrived at that conclusion precisely because of several incomprehensible things that happened during the Paper Shuffle. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh. Well, eyes forward, Ibuki. X aside, you’ll definitely get to see Karuizawa terrified by the threat of me exposing how she was bullied in the past,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If Karuizawa </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">does </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">show…what are you going to do if you can’t extract X’s identity from her?” asked Ishizaki. Both he and Ibuki seemed concerned.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“According to Manabe and her friends, Karuizawa was bullied quite viciously in the past. People who’ve been through such traumatic experiences tend to lose control of themselves when placed in a similar scenario. Why don’t we go ahead and reproduce those memories for her? Let’s put on a show. We’ll reenact her past until she tells us X’s identity.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No way. This is insane!” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is extreme, Ryuuen-san. A bunch of us ganging up on and bullying a girl? I mean, there are cameras on the rooftop!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m fully aware of that. I have a plan.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I began ascending the stairs to the roof. Halfway up, I stopped and looked back at Ibuki and Ishizaki, who were lagging a couple steps behind.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you don’t like it, run,” I told them.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I won’t run. I’ll follow you, Ryuuen-san.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What about you, Ibuki?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It all depends on your plan. If I think it’s dangerous, I’ll leave.” She’d been curious about X’s identity for some time now, too. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I had Ibuki, Albert, and Ishizaki wait near the rooftop door, taking the bag from Ishizaki. I removed the necessary tools, then handed the bag back.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This is…”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I opened the door. It was rare for a school rooftop to be accessible year-round. The reason this one did was because it had both proper fencing and a surveillance camera. Any dangerous activities would be recorded. Students knew this, which was why they behaved themselves up here. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, the roof was also a secluded spot. With its cafes and mall, the campus had many much more popular hangouts. I was probably the only one eccentric enough to often come all the way up here.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">There was also a limit to the number of cameras the school could install. There was only one surveillance camera on this particular roof, positioned above the door, which was the only real place a camera could be installed here. The one camera was more than enough to capture nearly the entire rooftop in its field of view, leaving almost no blind spots. The flipside of this, of course, was that if that one camera stopped functioning, the rooftop would be unmonitored.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It was the same kind of camera as the ones in the school building: vandalism-proof, with a strong polycarbonate lens and tough steel body that would be tough to break. But violence wasn’t the only way to disable a camera.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I stood directly underneath it and looked into the lens. I took the can of black spray paint I’d brought with me, pointed it at the lens, and sprayed. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There. Safe from prying eyes,” I said.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I’d done my research. Of the hundreds of cameras installed throughout the school, only a limited number displayed footage in real time, and this wasn’t one of them. The school authorities wouldn’t notice what was happening up here in time to stop it. I’d painted a different surveillance camera before, to test my theory, and my only punishment when I reported what I’d done to Sakagami was paying to have it cleaned.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The school was bound to be on low alert today, when most of the students had left. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Albert, remain on standby down the stairs. When Karuizawa comes, let her pass. If anyone unexpected pays us a visit, like one of the teachers, call me right away.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Albert nodded and went back downstairs. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You painted over the camera?” asked Ibuki. “Isn’t that a punishable offense?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Just a simple prank. Nothing to get worked up over.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“We’d better hope Karuizawa shows up.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“She’ll come. This is a matter of life and death for her.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">All that remained was to wait.</span></p>

<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">5.2</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 3.00em;">I</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">T WAS ALMOST TWO</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> in the afternoon, just shy of the time I’d set for Karuizawa, when the rooftop door opened to admit a lone student. The leading lady of today’s show appeared, stiffening in the cold air. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh. I knew you’d come, Karuizawa.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I turned off my phone and slipped it into my pocket. Ibuki and Ishizaki faced Karuizawa, both looking a little nervous.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The message you sent,” said Karuizawa. “What’s it supposed to mean?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s really no need to ask me that. You’re here precisely because you understand what it meant.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My message had read, </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Manabe and her friends told me everything about your past. Come to the rooftop after class, alone. If you talk to anyone, word of your past will spread through the entire school.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Just mentioning Manabe and her friends was enough to guarantee Karuizawa would understand what the message meant. She had no choice </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">but</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> to understand it. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’m guessing you told no one, as I instructed? Well, it’s not like you had a choice. You can hardly let just </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">anyone</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> know about your past, after all.” Karuizawa might’ve panicked and alerted X, considering that X alone probably knew her secret, but I didn’t really care. As I told Ibuki and the others, I’d sent X a message myself. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Today, I’d pass judgment on Karuizawa. And then, I’d hound her for X’s identity. Whether or not she’d asked for help, it didn’t matter. The end result would be the&#160;same.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You came alone after all, huh?” I asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You told me to, didn’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh. Suppose so.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">It wasn’t like X, who’d done so much to hide their identity in the first place, was going to carelessly show up here. And Karuizawa couldn’t ask for help from anyone other than X, because if she did, then her past would be exposed. The same applied to X, who wanted to keep their identity hidden. In other words, both of their options were severely limited.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Look. I really don’t, like, have a single clue what you’re talking about, but it’s cold out. I wanna finish this quick.” Karuizawa rubbed her arms, as if she had a chance of pretending she didn’t know what was going on. She was a poor actress.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“In that case, why come here? You should’ve just ignored me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, that’s…because I didn’t want any lies spreading around.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">She was trying her hardest to appear calm, but I easily called her bluff. “Lies, huh? Everyone here knows the truth… that you were bullied before you started high school.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ah…” When confronted with that, her behavior changed.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It was a stroke of luck that Manabe and her friends found out. If you want to blame anyone, blame yourself for not handling them better.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What do you want? What do you gain by threatening me?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What would you do if I told you I’m just killing time?” Though I still had plenty of leverage, Karuizawa was already out of options.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you do anything to me…I-I’ll tell the school,” she stammered.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You came here alone because you know you can’t do&#160;that.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Aren’t you getting carried away, Ryuuen? She might still have something up her sleeve,” Ibuki said. She still seemed to suspect something was up, since Karuizawa had come to the rooftop alone. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Karuizawa’s powerless to do anything but rely on X. No need to be so cautious. <p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“K…?” Ryuuen repeated the noise back to me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“K…t…” Slowly, very slowly, I managed to squeeze more sounds out of my throat. This was it. If I did this, I’d be free.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“One more time.” Ryuuen put his face closer to mine.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“K-knock…it…off. No matter how many times…” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The words were coming out now, but they weren’t </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">those </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">words. I’d never had any intention of telling him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Because I…wouldn’t…</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No matter how many times you ask me…I’ll never, ever tell you…creep.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At that, Ryuuen’s smile vanished. It felt like a single ray of light pierced the cloudy sky. My words hadn’t changed at all, but with those words, I’d made my choice.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even if I lose everything I built at this school starting tomorrow… No matter how much you put me through…” These weren’t Ryuuen’s words or Kiyotaka’s. They were </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">mine</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">. And they were something I could believe in, no matter what. “I’ll never give you the name.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Warmth flooded my chest.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you certain, Karuizawa?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Yes. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I’m okay with this.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">I might come to regret it.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">But, I’m okay with it…!</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Even though X was just using you, why you’re still protecting him?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wanted to ask myself the same question. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t know why.” But there was one thing I did know. “I want to stand tall until the very end!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My vision, which had grown hazy, suddenly sharpened once more.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I see. That’s too bad, Karuizawa. I don’t want to do this, but you’ve left me no choice. Still, you have my respect. Despite all your trauma, and the one person you could rely on betraying you, you didn’t sell him out. I&#160;honestly admire you.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">This is fine</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">This is fine</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I kept telling myself that. I was going to be broken, now, but somehow, I couldn’t help but feel proud of myself. Even though he’d betrayed me, I hadn’t betrayed him. I’d protected him. If I could somehow help him find the peace he sought, that wouldn’t be so bad, would it? It even made me pretty cool. Right?</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">My life had never been very interesting, except when I worked with Kiyotaka. Helping him out with all his different schemes wasn’t so bad. In fact, it was exciting. I had fun. I felt like I was playing the role of supporting actress, aiding the hero from the wings. I didn’t understand a lot of what we were doing, but somehow, it was extraordinarily fun.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Besides, no matter what happened, the truth was that he’d saved me. That was why I had no regrets.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">But, well…deep down, I still hoped he’d come and save me. Those fleeting feelings within me were…real, I guess. Ah, I was such an idiot. I’d only ever been dancing in the palm of his hand. Guess you reap what you sow, huh? </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">First Yousuke-kun, then Kiyotaka, had protected me. I was a girl who couldn’t do anything on her own.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Somehow, underneath the cold winter sky, I felt glad.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Goodbye, fake me.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">Welcome home to the cold, empty Karuizawa of the past.</span></p>
