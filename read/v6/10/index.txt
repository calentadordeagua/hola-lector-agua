<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 4:<br/>Class C Makes Its Move</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">M</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">EANWHILE</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">, in a different classroom, the atmosphere was quite cold and strained. The assembled Class C students appeared greatly intimidated by the person standing at the podium before them.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’ve been many unnatural goings-on, wouldn’t you say?” he asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen Kakeru was Class C’s leader and dictator. Yamada Albert, Ishizaki, and other students adept in martial arts stood next to him. They were willing to dispense punishment with their fists, should someone oppose him.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“There’s no way any of this could have been a coincidence.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He spoke as if he were talking to himself, but his ambiguous words seemed to be directed at someone.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“First there was that test on the uninhabited island, and then the sports festival. Someone lurking in Class D knows how to think like me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Someone thinks like you, Ryuuen-san? I can’t imagine someone like that in Class D, though,” blurted Ishizaki. To him, Ryuuen was a strange, incomprehensible being who commanded both respect and contempt. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen smiled at Ishizaki. “I thought so, too. But not anymore.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, are the events on the island and at the sports festival connected somehow?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes, exactly. But don’t worry. I’m starting to see how this person handles things. Listen up, all of you! From here on out, we’re going to hit Class D with everything we’ve got. We’ll put Classes A and B aside for the time being. I’ll smoke out the person working in Class D’s shadows,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><img alt="" height="100%" id="ClassroomElite-Vol6_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_5" src="/hola-lector-agua/images/books/v6/OPS/ClassroomElite-Vol6_INTERIORILLUSTRATIONS_5.jpg" loading="lazy" decoding="async" style=" top: 0px; vertical-align: top; height: 100%;"/></p>
<div style="page-break-after: always;"/>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting__And__Page_Break"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No one objected to his plan. Even if they wanted to, they couldn’t. They’d already made a deal with the devil himself.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ryuuen-san, is there really anyone competent in Class D, besides Horikita or Hirata?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Yes. And someone in Class C can expose the puppet master’s true identity.” Ryuuen turned away from Ishizaki and gazed upon the rest of the class once more.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What are you trying to say, Ryuuen?” asked Ibuki, crossing her arms. She stood near the window, her posture defiant.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Heh. Ibuki, can’t you even listen quietly?” asked Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I don’t have that luxury. Besides, you’re just trying to intimidate the entire class. I gain nothing from silence, right?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know, someone with no authority shouldn’t be talking. Besides, you made a rather disgraceful blunder, didn’t you?” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“That’s…” She was forced to swallow her words.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki’s failure in the sports festival had been significant. Ryuuen had wanted nothing more than to crush Horikita. Ibuki had requested to challenge Horikita directly, but had been narrowly defeated. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Like you, then?” said Ibuki, glaring at Ryuuen. This was her only opportunity for rebuttal. “I mean, you also failed to crush Horikita in the sports festival, or to get the private points you expected. You’re the same as me.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“The same as you? Don’t make me laugh. My strategy was perfect,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, how do you explain the results? You never explain anything. You seriously expect to convince us that Class D has some master strategist?” said Ibuki.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Hearing Ibuki’s comeback, the other students trembled in fear. They wanted to avoid incurring Ryuuen’s wrath. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, Ryuuen only smiled thinly. “Don’t you think that a perfect strategy is meaningless if someone leaks information?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Leaks information?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Class D’s success is due to the maneuverings of a mysterious person I’ll refer to as ‘X.’ X has an ally in Class C—</span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">my </span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">class. There’s a spy among us,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Everyone looked slightly confused. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ibuki’s eyes widened in shock. “Wait, are you serious?” she asked.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“It’s true. It seems that my control over you isn’t strong enough. It’s extremely unfortunate,” said Ryuuen. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He smiled as if he enjoyed the possibility of being spied upon. Everyone in the room started praying that this would be over quickly. They all yearned to scurry back to their dorms or rush off to their club activities.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“This nonsense is coming to an end right this instant,” said Ryuuen. He slammed the top of the podium, silencing everyone. Without hesitation, he declared, “First, I’ll ask directly. The person who betrayed me, raise your hand.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Of course, none of his classmates put up their hands. Some averted their eyes and feigned ignorance, while others looked around to see whether anyone came forward. Some stayed completely still to avoid drawing attention to themselves. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If you just step forward now, I’ll forgive you,” said Ryuuen. The continuing silence seemed to delight him. “Yes. I knew that a spy would plan to stay hidden. Don’t come forward. In fact, stay hidden at all costs.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">No one could have imagined that he would tell their class spy to </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">stay</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">concealed</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Wait, what? You’re not seriously going to accept that Class C has a traitor, are you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Shut up, Ibuki. Don’t get in the way of my fun. I’ll bury you if you do.” Ryuuen had been all smiles before, but his face twisted as he glared at Ibuki. He was completely serious.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen treated men and women equally. If he determined someone to be a nuisance, he would do whatever it took to remove them, regardless of their gender.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I’ve actually tried not to get too violent so far. You may think I’m lying, but it’s true. I’ve taken it easy on my opponents.” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">He slammed the podium twice. BAM! BAM! It was the class’s death knell.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But maybe that was foolish. I mean, now we have a traitor among us.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen struck the podium once more. BAM! The sound reverberated through the room. The timid students shook. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, we’re going to play a little game. A silly, childish little game where we try to find the spy. For most students, there’s really no need to be afraid. This shouldn’t take long at all; just thirty minutes,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Despite his insistence that everyone should have fun, it was obvious that everyone was terrified. Even Ibuki started to feel apprehensive.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“First things first. Take your phones out, and place them on your desks. I’ll come by and check personally. Are there any idiots who didn’t bring their phones? They should step forward immediately. One of them is the culprit,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The students immediately put their phones on their desks.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Great job listening,” he told them. “That helps a lot.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki went around the room and collected them, attaching a label with the owner’s name to each phone. The labels seemed to have been prepared ahead of time. Ibuki handed her phone over to Ishizaki, looking dissatisfied.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Ryuuen-san, I collected everyone’s phones,” said Ishizaki. “Ours too.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Good work. Well, I think it’s time for a thorough investigation, don’t you?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“What should we check? The call histories?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Come now, as if someone trying to hide their identity would make an incriminating call! Look at the email histories. Then look at the texts. Read all of them, even if it’s just a minor conversation. The traitor might have used an alias.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“W-wait a minute! There are lots of really private messages on my phone, though!” shouted one girl, her desire not to have Ryuuen see her personal information outweighing the risk of him suspecting her.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You really don’t want me to read what’s on your phone, Nishino?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course I don’t! Even if it’s just you, Ryuuen-kun!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Don’t be ridiculous, Nishino. You gave Ryuuen your phone on the cruise ship, didn’t you?” said Ishizaki. “Why are you so—?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Th-this is different from back then! All he did on the cruise ship was check the email I received from the school!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen listened to Nishino’s appeal with cold indifference. During our summer vacation, Ryuuen had gone through all of his classmates’ phones on the cruise ship. However, as Nishino had said, he didn’t go through any private information; he only confirmed the correspondence they had received from the school. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">This situation seemed similar, but it really wasn’t. If someone were to go through and read through their entire phones, word of people’s crushes or who disliked who would spread like wildfire. This kind of private information had to be kept under wraps.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“You know that this will make you a prime suspect, Nishino?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-I will obey you, Ryuuen-kun, but there are some things I can’t accept!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nishino wasn’t the type to argue, but in this case, it looked like she was dead serious. She’d loudly announced that she had something on her phone that she didn’t want other people to see.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Are you the spy, Nishino?” asked Oda, who was just one of the students beginning to suspect Nishino.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“No, I’m not the spy!” </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But the fact that you’re hiding something is pretty suspicious.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I just want to protect my privacy!”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen showed no interest at all in the current conversation. He grabbed one of the phones. “This is your phone, Nishino?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Hey!” Nishino panicked, sure that he’d start reading her messages. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">However, Ryuuen handed Nishino’s phone to Ishizaki. “Give this back to her.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I-Is that okay? You didn’t check the contents.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I told you to give it to her.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ishizaki quietly returned Nishino’s phone. She and the other students shook.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Really, it’s not such a mystery. I confirmed your innocence, so I returned your phone. That’s only natural, right? If it’s not the traitor’s phone, then looking at it would be a waste of time and effort,” said Ryuuen. He disregarded the bewildered Nishino, then continued on with no change in his affect.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“If anyone here thinks that what I’m doing is unacceptable, like Nishino said, raise your hand. However, prepare to come under even more suspicion than she did.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Nishino didn’t have her phone inspected, but she had been deemed innocent. Anyone else who followed probably wouldn’t be so fortunate. Basically, Ryuuen was telling them to choose between being becoming a suspect or keeping their privacy. Faced with those two options, four girls and two boys raised their hands, despite their fear.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Six people are standing up to Ryuuen-san. The spy is definitely among them, I’m sure of it! Nomura-san, you were the last one to raise your hand. You’re not jumping on the bandwagon to save your own skin, are you?” said Ishizaki.</span></p>

<p class="P_TEXTBODY_CENTERALIGN_PAGEBREAK"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.27em;">4.1</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"> </p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">R</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">YUUEN CHECKED</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> everyone’s phones himself. They could’ve sent each other handwritten letters, sure, but those are way too old-fashioned and inefficient. Therefore, I’m certain that X used email or similar tactics to remain anonymous,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Of course I didn’t. That was just for show.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Huh? But you said you’d know the spy’s identity if you looked at our phones.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Apply some common sense here. If you were the spy, would you intentionally leave a suspicious email on your phone?”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Well, no, I wouldn’t. That’s why I thought that checking our phones was a waste of time.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“Right. It was obvious how investigating everyone’s phones would turn out. It wouldn’t have been odd for someone to destroy evidence. Even if we suppose that the spy didn’t think to do that, X would have probably instructed them to do so. The spy is someone who thought that they could look innocent just by giving me their phone. Therefore, those who didn’t show me their phones are innocent, whereas the spy wouldn’t have taken that chance,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">Ryuuen turned from Manabe Shiho, fixing his sharp gaze upon her friends, Yabu Nanami and Yamashita Saki.</span></p>

<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“But, Ryuuen-shi, shouldn’t we be especially suspicious of Ayanokouji-shi?” Kaneda, fully prepared to earn Ryuuen’s ire, dared to speak up. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“I agree,” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">With regard to Ayanokouji, he had felt something was fishy from the beginning, primarily because of Ayanokouji’s connection to Horikita Suzune. However, if Ayanokouji were the mastermind, and had been close to Horikita Suzune from the beginning, he would never have let her be hurt.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“So, you’re saying that the real mastermind’s hiding right under our noses? I find that hard to swallow.”</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">The situation felt hopeless.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">“How about we try using him?” said Ryuuen.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">They’d come this far; it made sense to go further. Ryuuen made his next move and sent a text message.</span></p>
