<p class="P__STAR__STAR__STAR__page_break" id="auto_bookmark_toc_top"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 1.17em;">Chapter 1 - Prologue:<br/>Sudou Ken’s Soliloquy</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting">&#160;</p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-weight: bold; font-size: 2.40em;">T</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">O BE COMPLETELY HONEST</span><span style=" font-size: 0.80em;">,</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> I ain’t really a capable person. I know that much without anybody having to tell me. After my mom abandoned us to go into the nightclub business, I decided to get stronger. I remember that my old man’s back was always hunched. It made me sick to my stomach that he spent each day toiling away as a janitor. Being the idiot I am, I decided to give up on studyin’ and go into sports.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">At first, I liked sports you could play by yourself, like tennis and ping-pong, but I didn’t really stick with those. I was great at ’em, but I knew I’d never become a top-tier athlete. Then I tried basketball.</span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">I wasn’t a team player or nothin’, but somehow, I got seriously into playing basketball. And I just kept getting better. I was even recommended to a high school with one of the best basketball teams in the entire country. But I ended up getting in some trouble, and there was violence involved, so my recommendation got pulled. That was when I really understood the painful truth: I was trash, born from trash. </span></p>
<p class="P_Prose_Formatting"><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;">That was why I chose this school. A dream school that didn’t cost any money and could guarantee even </span><span style=" font-style: italic; font-size: 1.00em;">my</span><span style=" font-size: 1.00em;"> future.</span></p>
